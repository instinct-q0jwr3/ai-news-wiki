Summary

Short note from feed metadata only: Bloomberg (Bailey Lipschultz) reads Anthropic's S-1 and reports third-quarter revenue up 12x year over year to nearly $17 billion, a net loss of $23.3 billion (up from $12.1 billion in Q3 2025), and operating expenses up 292 percent to $17.5 billion.
