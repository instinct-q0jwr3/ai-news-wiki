The AI-collapse pre-mortem

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

bert-hubert

Summary

An essential part of being able to say ‘I told you so’ is in fact having told you so.

In April 2023, I wrote an article titled AI: Guaranteed to disrupt our economies.

In this piece I also announced I was going to make a fool of myself by making some AI predictions.

Highlights

  • In retrospect this was all spot on, except for perhaps missing the sheer magnitude of the madness that was about to ensue.
  • In what follows, I am not claiming AI is ‘good’ or ’ethical’.
  • But for completeness, I am no fan of what people are doing with LLMs, how AI is burning through far too much energy, and the copyright issues also annoy me.
  • The tl;dr: even if the AI bubble bursts, LLMs can do some amazing things, despite not being overly useful in a business sense while also often being very wrong.
  • Meanwhile, the other parts of AI have already led to a Nobel prize, and are doing astounding things for translation, transcription and image analysis.
  • The AI bubble collapsing has no bearing on that this is some very impressive technology (even if LLMs are not generally useful yet, frequently very wrong and worrying on many fronts).
  • So take the claims of LLMs being useless with a grain of salt.
  • I’d like to get my word in first, in this AI-collapse pre-mortem.
