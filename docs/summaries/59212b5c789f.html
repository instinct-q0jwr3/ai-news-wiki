Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, its "strongest model with state-of-the-art cybersecurity" capabilities, ranked #1 on Artificial Analysis' new Intelligence Index

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, two weeks after Argon, with state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities. It ranks #1 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 at 59, and leads SEC-bench Pro (88.2%) for vulnerability finding, ExploitGym (54.8%) for exploitation and CWE-bench (71.0%) for patching, with gains in coding and agentic tasks and reduced misalignment and fabrication in live internal coding use.

The rollout is deliberately staged: Google is in the US government's voluntary pre-release testing process, ships first to a subset of cyber defenders this week, then to trusted partners through its Fairwind Program, iterating on guardrails before general developer and consumer availability.
