Meta AI creates campaign material for Flávio Bolsonaro and blocks Lula content

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high

Summary

Folha tests found that Meta AI in WhatsApp refuses to make campaign material for Brazil's President Lula but does so for Senator Flávio Bolsonaro: a 'Lula 13' poster was blocked while 'Flávio 22' was generated. When asked, the chatbot blamed an automatic safety filter.

Correspondents abroad could make Lula posters, but Brazilian phone numbers were blocked even from overseas. Meta says it temporarily banned candidate-related content to comply with Brazil's election rules.

Highlights

  • Meta AI blocked Lula campaign posters but made Bolsonaro ones.
  • Blocking followed Brazilian phone numbers, not location.
