Filing: Anthropic's IPO could give it an up to $1.405T valuation on a fully diluted basis, up from the $965B valuation during its $65B Series H announced in May

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports the amended filing prices Anthropic's IPO at $70 to $78 per share for 825 million shares, a valuation up to $1.405 trillion fully diluted, up from $965 billion in May. Sources say pricing is planned for Nov. 18 with trading on Nov. 19 and about 20% going to retail, far above the usual 5% to 10%. At the high end proceeds would be up to $64.35 billion. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC will buy $26 billion of stock in a private placement at the IPO price. The pitch lands after a Democratic midterm sweep and ballot measures that could slow the AI buildout.
