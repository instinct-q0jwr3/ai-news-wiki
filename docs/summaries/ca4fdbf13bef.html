Introducing Exa 3.0

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Exa releases Exa 3.0, which it calls its largest search-quality improvement ever, ranking first on every benchmark it tested across coding, finance, deep research, and company intelligence, driven by three upgrades to its retrieval stack that benefit every search type. Alongside it launches Exa Lite, a cost-sensitive search type at $4 per 1,000 requests running on the same index as the $7 Exa Auto, together setting a new price-quality Pareto frontier. Dynamic Highlights, previously a research preview, is now generally available at no charge, selecting tokens across all results with 95% fewer tokens than full page text. Exa's independent crawler and index track over 2 trillion URLs and serve 100 billion pages, newly expanded around personal AI assistant demand.

Highlights

  • Claims first place on every benchmark tested: coding, finance, deep research, company intelligence
  • Quality gains come from three retrieval-stack improvements across all search types
  • Exa Lite launches at $4/1k requests vs $7/1k for Auto, a new Pareto frontier
  • Dynamic Highlights GA at no charge, using 95% fewer tokens than full page text
  • Independent index: 2T+ URLs tracked, 100B pages served
  • New data partnerships target personal AI assistant use cases
  • Live Q&A with Exa's product and R&D heads on 21 October
