LLM Policies: Progress At All Costs

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

lobsters

Summary

A post on LLM Policies argues that the fights over AI contributions in GNOME and KDE are really about the raison d'etre of FLOSS projects, not workflow nitpicking. KDE ignited a flame war with an LLM-friendly draft policy that was deleted after causing bans and a 'Some of you may die, but that is a sacrifice I am willing to make' attitude.

GNOME has proposed nothing official, but teams including gnome-calendar, loupe, libadwaita, gnome-software and Circle already have strong policies, and an informal draft now circulating would ban all LLM contributions to GNOME projects and infrastructure.

Highlights

  • Essay: LLM contribution policies are about the raison d'etre of FLOSS.
  • KDE's LLM-friendly draft was deleted after a flame war and bans.
  • Several GNOME teams already have strong AI policies.
  • An informal draft would ban all LLM contributions to GNOME.
  • Author: the debate is not about workflows but purpose.
