Meta says users will be able to video chat with Muse, agents will get their own email addresses, and Muse will get computer use on Mac and smart glasses support

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

techmeme meta muse agentic-systems compute-buildout

Summary

Meta is expanding Muse on several fronts at once: users will be able to hold live video conversations with their Muse avatar, communicate with the agent over new email addresses, and the Muse Mac app is gaining the ability to use your computer. A new Muse Realtime Avatar model will drive how the avatar moves, and its voice will be customizable the way its appearance already is.

The push follows a strong launch - Muse topped the App Store charts and was reportedly downloaded more than ChatGPT on mobile in its first 12 days - and Meta says Muse will come to its smart glasses in the coming months, letting wearers talk to the agent about what they are looking at.

Highlights

  • Live video conversations with the Muse avatar are coming, driven by a new Muse Realtime Avatar model.
  • Muse Mac app gains computer-use ability; Muse also gets email addresses.
  • Avatar voice customization joins existing appearance customization.
  • Muse topped the App Store and reportedly out-downloaded ChatGPT on mobile in its first 12 days.
  • Muse heads to Meta smart glasses in the coming months.
