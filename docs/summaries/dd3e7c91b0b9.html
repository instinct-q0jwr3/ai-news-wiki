Amazon snaps up Decart's AI optimization tech for $7.5B as it prepares to sell Trainium chips

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports Amazon's agreement to acquire Decart's optimization-stack technology together with its founders and engineering and research talent. It attributes a $7.5 billion price to Bloomberg and places the transaction against Amazon's plans to sell Trainium chips and make workloads easier to run on its hardware.

The article describes acquisition of a substantial part of Decart, not necessarily every product or the whole corporate entity. The amount is sourced reporting, and the expected efficiency and competitive benefits are not independently benchmarked. An agreement and its strategic rationale are distinct from confirmed completion, integration success or a guaranteed customer cost reduction.
