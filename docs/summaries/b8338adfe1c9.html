Ramp Glass

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

Ramp launches Glass, a shared workspace where employees get the AI setups its most AI-forward staff built. Ramp's argument is that giving everyone the best models and tokens does not make a company AI-native, since in one May week it burned $1.5 million in tokens, and that good workflows otherwise live in one person's chat history and vanish. Basis: the opening of the announcement.

Highlights

  • Ramp Glass spreads its power users' AI setups to everyone.
  • Ramp spent $1.5M on tokens in one week in May.
  • Pitch: workflows should be shared, not lost in individual chats.
