Update on October 2026 security incident

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Fireworks' updated incident notice says the breach went beyond secrets used in evaluation jobs to account and billing information and, for a limited set of accounts, secrets, datasets and incomplete models. A third-party cloud provider hosting its R&D machines was compromised on October 23 and the attacker reached development machines on October 24, running an unauthorized workload. Fireworks links it to a financially motivated group selling LLM access ('LLMJacking') and says services that are zero data retention by default were not hit. Unit 42 assisted and the investigation continues.

Highlights

  • Impact extends to billing info, datasets, incomplete models
  • Entry via a third-party cloud provider on Oct 23
  • Group sells LLM access (LLMJacking)
  • ZDR-by-default services not affected
  • Law enforcement and Unit 42 involved
