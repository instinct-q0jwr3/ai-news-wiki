Consumer AI Fatigue Is Overwhelming, Local Businesses Should Reconsider Use

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high

Summary

An opinion piece by James Parks argues that consumer fatigue with AI is overwhelming and local businesses should reconsider using it. He moves from harmless early ChatGPT fun to harms: fake AI-generated texts that put a woman in jail for two days, and wrongful arrests from facial recognition.

His conclusion is that pushback and responsible use are needed, and that businesses risk alienating customers who see AI as replacing human creativity. It is an op-ed, so the cases are cited anecdotally rather than as research.

Highlights

  • Opinion: AI fatigue among consumers is a reason for local businesses to rethink use.
  • Cites a 2026 case of fake AI-generated texts leading to jail time.
  • Cites wrongful arrests from facial recognition, including a 2021 Oklahoma case.
  • Argues for pushback and responsible use.
  • Resident News Network states the views are the author's alone.
