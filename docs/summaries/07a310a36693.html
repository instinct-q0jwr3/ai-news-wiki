Stepfun Step 5 Preview (LLM): On AA Pareto frontier

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Artificial Analysis has benchmarked StepFun's Step 5 Preview, a proprietary reasoning model released in September 2026, and places it among the leading models on its Intelligence Index with a score of 44, well above the comparable-model median of 24. It supports text and image input with a 1M-token context window and runs faster than average at 100 output tokens per second.

The trade-offs are clear: the model is very verbose, burning 160M tokens across the Intelligence Index versus a 92M median, but it is priced aggressively at $1.00 per million input tokens (median $1.88) and $2.70 per million output tokens (median $10.00), making the full evaluation cost $922.84. The index covers ten evaluations including Terminal-Bench 4.0, SciCode, Humanity's Last Exam and GDPval-AA.
