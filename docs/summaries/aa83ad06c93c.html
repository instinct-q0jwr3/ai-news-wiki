Introducing touring_test: A Cucumber Extension For Agentic Usability Testing

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

works-on-my-machine agentic-systems ai-safety-incidents

Summary

It's like BDD, but the user is a hallucination Scott Werner Nov 01, 2025 8 2 Share We’ve been lying to ourselves about our tests for years.

We’ve been telling ourselves we’re “testing what users do” while secretly giving our tests superpowers no user has ever possessed.

The ability to click things that are technically visible but practically invisible.

Highlights

  • My tests have been pretending to be users the way a GPS pretends to be a navigator.
  • Basically accomplishing the same goal, but missing something fundamental about the lived experience of trying to navigate downtown Pittsburgh’s highways at full speed.
  • And then one night, I wrote this Cucumber step after seeing Gemini’s Computer Use model get released and I’ve been thinking about the implications ever since.
  • What it does is send an instruction to the computer use agent.
  • The agent looks at a screenshot of your website and attempts to do what you asked.
  • What We’re Actually Testing When you hardcode click_button(‘Logout’) in your test, you’re testing that a button with that exact label exists.
  • Your selenium tests will never tell you that your logout button is too small.
  • Yes, like the Turing test, but for touring your application.
