AI expansion puts pressure on Singapore office rents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme openai anthropic

Summary

AI heavyweights including OpenAI and Anthropic are putting pressure on office rents in Singapore as they expand in response to government overtures, the Financial Times reports. Both companies are taking more space in the city's already squeezed prime property market.

Highlights

  • OpenAI and Anthropic are expanding offices in Singapore.
  • Their growth is pressuring rents in an already tight prime market.
  • The expansion responds to Singapore government overtures.
