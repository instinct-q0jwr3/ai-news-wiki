Aina, which raised $5.5M, launches an "AI ring" with a screen, joystick, and mic to let users talk to their AI agents, take notes, control Apple TV, and more

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports hardware startup Aina launched a $399 'AI ring' with a screen, joystick, mic and speaker to talk to agents, take notes, set reminders, take calls and control things like Apple TV. Aina raised $5.5M in July and previously made Mirage, a three-key Mac controller whose keys mapped to app actions such as muting in Google Meet. The ring is pitched as a controller for AI and non-AI apps. Notes can also be dictated on a Mac with Wispr Flow.
