Nasscom President Rajesh Nambiar warns AI could make it harder for fresh Indian engineering graduates to find jobs, urging an immediate CS curriculum overhaul

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Rajesh Nambiar, president of India's Nasscom, told Nikkei Asia that recent college graduates could struggle to land technology jobs as companies automate entry-level coding and software testing with AI.

He says India's tech education needs a rapid overhaul, because the threat to the traditional business model means new skills will be required. The piece is an interview from Nikkei's editor's picks; only the opening was readable in the cache.

Highlights

  • Nasscom's president warns entry-level tech jobs are at risk from AI.
  • Coding and software testing are named as automation targets.
  • He calls for a rapid overhaul of India's tech education.
  • New skills will be needed as the business model shifts.
