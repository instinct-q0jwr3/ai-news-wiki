Personal Computing 2.0

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Josh Albrecht of Imbue argues for 'Personal Computing 2.0'. He remembers 1995 computers as doing what you told them, with no subscriptions, notifications or ads, then describes how the web was overgrown by spam and enshittification.

His proposal is that AI can bring back computers that serve their owners, building what you imagine without login screens and upsells. It is a manifesto-style piece from a company with a stake in the idea.

Highlights

  • Nostalgic case for computers that obey their owner.
  • Blames web enshittification: ads, pop-ups, upsells.
  • Argues AI could restore personal control.
  • Written by Imbue, which sells this vision.
