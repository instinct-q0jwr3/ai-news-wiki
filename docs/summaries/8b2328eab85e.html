Meet Helix World: A Real-Time Audio-Visual World Model

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Noiz AI and HKUST introduce Helix World, a real-time audio-visual world model. From a single image you move with WASD and look with arrow keys, and it generates the next video and matching stereo sound together at 24 fps on a single NVIDIA H800. The sound field turns when the camera turns. It scores 79.9 on the WBench navigation split, close to the best silent world models, at a real-time factor of 0.77, and the team releases an audio-visual benchmark, HelixBench.

Highlights

  • Generates explorable video and spatial stereo audio together from one image.
  • 24 fps on a single H800, faster than real time (RTF 0.77).
  • WBench navigation score 79.9, near top silent world models.
  • Releases HelixBench for audio-visual evaluation.
