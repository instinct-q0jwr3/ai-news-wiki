Three xAI founding members launch Epochal to build AI agents that achieve "superhuman prediction"; source: co-founder Guodong Zhang personally invested $100M

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

An accessible Bloomberg opening introduces Epochal, founded by three early xAI members to pursue prediction of future events. The company describes an initial time-portal product that lets agents browse historical versions of the internet to practice forecasting without later information.

The full article was not accessible. The feed's reported $100 million personal investment by Guodong Zhang is not independently confirmed by the opening, and superhuman prediction is a goal rather than a demonstrated result. Historical simulations require controls against future-data leakage and overfitting; no predictive benchmark or investment outcome was verified.
