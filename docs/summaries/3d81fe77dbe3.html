OpenAI disbands its standalone robotics team, moving its staff to research and Applied units, saying models like GPT-6 Astra can be directly applied to robotics

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Fortune reports that OpenAI dissolved its standalone robotics team and moved researchers into its main research organization while applied robotics and hardware staff joined Applied. A company spokesperson portrays the change as integration around general-purpose models, not abandonment of robotics.

The article connects the reorganization to a broader bet that language, code and vision models can also control physical devices. That strategy does not establish safe robot performance, and a planned future demonstration remains a target rather than a delivered product.

Highlights

  • Company confirmed the reorganization
  • Research and Applied receive different staff
  • Robotics remains a stated long-term goal
  • General models motivate the strategy
  • Future demonstration is not a completed launch
