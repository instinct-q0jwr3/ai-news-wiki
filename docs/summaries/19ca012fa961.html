George Hotz’s opinion on AI coding

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

George Hotz announces that tinygrad is hiring - and along the way gives his verdict on AI coding. The company's bounty program, once a good hiring signal, is dead: people who barely understand a bounty now paste it into Claude Code and submit the PR, so the signal is gone. AI companies promise anyone who pays can be a software developer, and for a 'slop app for your slop brand' that's true - but that isn't software development, it 'looks a lot more like a slightly more flexible WordPress.' tinygrad encourages AI as tooling, but 'there's nobody now that we would hire because AI exists who we wouldn't have hired before and vice versa.'

The replacement hiring process is radical transparency: the company coordinates in public on Discord and GitHub, weekly meetings are open to anyone, and the way to get hired is simply to start contributing - 'we don't want features, we want deletion... deep bug fixes and regression tests.' Hotz frames the mission as a 10-year attack on big-tech rent seeking: commoditize the petaflop and there's nothing left to rent-seek on. Logistics: remote/hybrid from San Diego and Hong Kong, one hour-long meeting a week plus a three-sentence sprint summary every other week, $75k-150k plus 0.1-0.5% equity. 'If you read this, you already know how to apply.'

Highlights

  • The bounty program is dead - AI killed its hiring signal: paste bounty into Claude Code, submit PR, learn nothing
  • Hotz on AI-for-everyone coding: fine for slop apps, but 'a slightly more flexible WordPress' isn't software development
  • tinygrad encourages AI as tooling; it changes nothing about who they'd hire
  • Hiring process: just start contributing - they want deletion, deep bug fixes, regression tests, not features
  • The mission: commoditize the petaflop to cut the ground from big-tech rent seeking - a 10-year attack
  • Logistics: remote/hybrid SD and Hong Kong, 1h weekly meeting, 3-sentence biweekly summary, $75k-150k + 0.1-0.5% equity
