Community condemn Bedminster AI mural as 'insult' to artists

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

A community in Bedminster, Bristol - an area renowned for its street art scene and host to Upfest, Europe's largest street art festival - has condemned an AI-generated mural installed as a vinyl sticker on the side of the Jolly Colliers pub. The mural depicts hot air balloons and Bristol landmarks like the Clifton Suspension Bridge, but critics say it is riddled with glaring mistakes, including invented buildings.

A letter taped to the building called the piece a disrespectful affront to a city full of creatives, accusing the owners of laziness, environmental damage and contributing to the theft of artists' work. Nina Raines of the Bristol Mural Collective said artificial renderings undermine the city's reputation as a creative hub and that local artists would willingly have made a temporary mural. Punch Pubs & Co., which owns the pub, said the mural is a temporary solution and that it will work with local artists to replace it.
