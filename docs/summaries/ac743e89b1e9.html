GLM-5.3's open release has yet to produce major attacks despite Anthropic's warnings about its Mythos-level cyber risk, undercutting calls to ban open models

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Nathan Lambert argues that GLM-5.3's open release has so far produced no major attacks despite Anthropic's warnings about its Mythos-level cyber risk, which undercuts calls to ban open models. He maps the debate: frontier labs and national-security voices call open weights an untenable risk, moderate Western voices (himself included) say they are needed for defence, and Chinese firms keep releasing strong open models. He warns that bans would hurt US competitiveness and may raise long-term cyber risk. Basis: the first part of a long piece was read.
