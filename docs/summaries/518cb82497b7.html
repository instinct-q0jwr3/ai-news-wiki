Nscale faces investor pushback on its ~$30B IPO valuation, with some urging a lower valuation of $16B to $19B, as Microsoft exits a UK data center deal

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Basis: Techmeme's feed summary of a Bloomberg article; the Bloomberg source fetch failed. Bloomberg reports that Nscale, the AI cloud/data-center firm, is facing investor pushback against the roughly $30 billion valuation it is targeting for its IPO, with some investors urging a lower $16-19 billion range. Adding pressure, Microsoft has exited a UK data center deal involving the company. The pushback is an early sign of public-market discipline meeting the AI infrastructure valuation surge - neocloud economics are being stress-tested before the first big listings.
