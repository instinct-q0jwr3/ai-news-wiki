Introducing Clef-omni with full multimodality, plus a faster Clef and a cheaper Clef-flash

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Cloudflare extends its Clef family of open-weight decision models: Clef-omni adds audio and video input alongside text and image, Clef itself is now faster, and Clef-flash got a price cut that makes it cheaper than Jev. The launch comes a week after the original Clef and Clef-flash release.

Cloudflare stresses the pace: the Clef story went from a Friday-evening decision to enter the decision-model space, to weekend training, to a Thursday launch - evidence, it says, that even as a newcomer to models it can ship performant open-weight releases quickly, with more to come.

Highlights

  • Clef is faster; Clef-flash is cheaper than Jev after a price cut.

  • Origin story: decided Friday evening, trained over the weekend, launched Thursday.
