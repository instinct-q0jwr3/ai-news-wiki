Show HN: Premortem – AI agents that red-team your startup idea

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Premortem is a web tool where you paste a startup idea and six AI agents try to tear it apart, covering market, tech, competition and unit economics, among other angles. A seventh agent writes the memo.

The page claims over 2,400 ideas red-teamed and about 90 seconds per memo. Basis: only the landing page was available, so those figures are the site's own claims and there is no evidence about quality.

Highlights

  • Six agents critique an idea; a seventh synthesizes a memo.
  • Claims 2,400+ ideas processed and under 90 seconds per memo.
  • Show HN launch; claims unverified.
