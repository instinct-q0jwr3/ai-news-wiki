The UK plans to invest £100M to create the AI Biosecurity Lab to boost defenses against biological threats, supported by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-24 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high

Summary

The UK will invest £100 million to create the AI Biosecurity Lab (AIBL), a 'world first' national institute to strengthen defenses against emerging biological threats, announced as part of Wednesday's Budget with support from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind. The chancellor's package also pledges millions more across initiatives targeting cyber, biological, and AI-enabled threats.

John Healey framed the investment inside a 'distinctively British approach to AI': one that 'doesn't leave workers behind and defends our national interest' while spreading AI-enabled growth across the country. The AIBL gives the UK's AI-security institutional build-out a biosecurity arm alongside AISI, with frontier labs formally attached from the start.
