No AI Slop: A Manifesto

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

The No AI Slop manifesto asks software practitioners to judge AI-assisted work by outcomes and understanding rather than volume or speed. Its stated values put the author’s own thinking ahead of accepting a model’s first draft and favor fixing the process that produces poor work over repeatedly patching its output.

The accessible page attributes the statement to AIE Code Summit participants and invites community signatures. It is a statement of professional values, not evidence that a particular development method improves productivity.

Highlights

  • Outcomes take priority over output volume
  • Understanding matters more than speed
  • Human thinking remains part of authorship
  • System improvements beat repeated patches
  • The manifesto is normative, not an evaluation
