Google launches Genie 4, a world model powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash designed to generate interactive AI simulations, initially as a preview to trusted testers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

The Verge covers Google's launch of Genie 4, a world model that generates interactive AI simulations. Four main updates: more realistic worlds, real-time updates via natural language prompts, generated audio, and Gemini 3.8 Flash under the hood. Simulations now run about two minutes, double Genie 3's one minute.

Google pitches pre-visualization for filmmakers, game prototyping and robot training scenarios, but warns that details and object permanence drift as simulations lengthen. Genie 4 launches as a research preview for trusted testers, with Google AI Ultra subscribers next and a waitlist open for Project Genie.

Highlights

  • Genie 4 world model: interactive simulations with generated audio
  • Powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash; ~2-minute simulations, up from 1
  • Real-time world edits via natural language
  • Trusted-tester preview first; Google AI Ultra access 'coming soon'
