'AI' is Not a Tool

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high

Summary

'AI is Not a Tool' picks apart the word everyone reaches for. A tool, the author argues, is something you wield: held in the hand, transmitting power, under continuous control - an implement with which you do something, not something that does things for you. Shirt pockets and drive-thru windows are useful but not tools; reserving 'tool' for 'anything useful' empties the word of meaning.

The payoff: tool use builds, rewards and requires expertise, technique and sensory feedback. Whatever AI is - and the AI titans have their own inverted usage where people are the tools - calling it a tool misdescribes the relationship and hides what is actually changing.

Highlights

  • Argument: AI is not a tool, and the word choice matters.
  • A tool is wielded - hand, power, continuous control, expertise.
  • Useful is not enough: pockets and drive-thrus are useful but not tools.
  • Tools do things with you; AI does things for you.
  • Barb: for AI titans, 'tool' means anyone exploitable - VCs, politicians, devs.
