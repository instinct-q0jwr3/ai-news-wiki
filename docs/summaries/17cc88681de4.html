How the AI boom is boosting paint maker PPG's sales: its specialized gray coatings serve critical functional needs and total $3M to $8M per data center, per JLL

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

The New York Times reports that the AI data-center boom is lifting paint maker PPG: its specialized gray coatings resist fire, rust, and static electricity on data-center exteriors, and JLL estimates the coatings run $3-8 million per data center. Tech companies are painting facilities gray over gray concrete and steel, turning an unlikely supplier into an AI-boom beneficiary.
