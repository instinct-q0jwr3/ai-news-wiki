Australian healthcare AI startup Heidi raised $340M, including a $100M Series C led by Blackbird at a $900M valuation, taking its total funding to $430M+

Summary

Australian healthcare AI startup Heidi raised $340 million, including a $100 million Series C led by Blackbird at a $900 million valuation, Bloomberg reports, doubling its valuation and taking total funding past $430 million. Heidi helps doctors automate clinical notes and other administrative work.

Highlights

  • Heidi raised $340M, including a $100M Series C led by Blackbird.
  • The round values the company at $900M, doubling its prior valuation.
  • Total funding exceeds $430M for the clinical-notes automation startup.
