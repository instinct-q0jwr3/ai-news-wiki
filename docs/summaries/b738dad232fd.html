Flow, a hardware development platform for AI agents, raised a $50M Series B led by Valor's Antonio Gracias and Atreides' Gavin Baker at a $750M valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

Flow Engineering, a three-year-old San Francisco startup that builds AI agents for hardware design, raised a $50 million Series B at a $750 million valuation. Antonio Gracias of Valar Equity Partners and Gavin Baker of Atreides Management co-led. Sequoia, which led the Series A last October, took part, and former Sequoia partner Roelof Botha invested personally and joined the board.

Flow's agents automatically align CAD drawings with product requirements, simulation results and other tests. TechCrunch lists Anduril, Rivian, Joby Aviation, GM PPU, RV Tech and Stoke Space as customers.
