Timestral: Mistral's time-series foundation models

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Mistral released Timestral, its first family of time-series foundation models - four sizes from 5M to 209M parameters, with the largest ranking first on all three main public benchmarks (GIFT-Eval, TIME, fev-bench) while using only two-thirds of the previous state of the art's parameter count. The models deliver probabilistic, multivariate forecasts, support past-only and known-future covariates, and run efficiently on CPUs.

The recipe combines synthetic pre-training, covariate-aware attention and long-context learning, with domain fine-tuning available through Mistral Forge. Mistral is targeting the enterprise bread and butter - capacity planning, energy, supply chains, predictive maintenance, finance - where forecasting drives decisions and zero-shot accuracy on unseen series is the actual requirement.

Strategically it's a notable lane choice: while the frontier labs fight over general reasoning, Mistral is building a small-model specialist family for a data type every industrial company has and general LLMs handle poorly.
