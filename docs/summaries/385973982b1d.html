Summary

The Biological Computing Co. partners with AWS to sell its neuron-derived AI video model.

“Our partnership with AWS takes neuron-derived AI optimization to commercial scale,” said Alex Ksendzovsky, TBC’s chief executive and co-founder.

“Nature solved the computing efficiency problem billions of years ago.

Highlights

  • TBC’s insight is that we can learn from the original computer—the human brain—to make AI faster, more efficient, and more economical,” said Jason Bennett, Vice President and Global Head of Startups and Venture Capital at AWS.
  • “Compute is becoming one of the biggest constraints on AI,” said Jon Pomeraniec, co-founder and COO of TBC.
  • “We need more infrastructure, but we also need to make every unit of compute dramatically more productive.
  • Lower inference costs mean more companies can afford to build, scale and put powerful AI to work.” The Biological Computing Co. (TBC), a San Francisco startup that grows living neurons to improve AI models, has partnered with Amazon Web…
