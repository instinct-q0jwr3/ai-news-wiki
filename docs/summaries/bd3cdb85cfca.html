Modal Labs is in funding talks at a ~$15B valuation, up from $4.65B in May; Baseten is in funding talks at a $26B valuation, up from $13B in June

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

A Bloomberg report, per its sources: Modal Labs is in funding talks at a valuation around $15 billion - up from $4.65 billion in May - and fellow AI-inference startup Baseten is also in raise talks. The body is paywalled and was not captured, so this summary rests on the headline.

On that basis: the inference-infrastructure tier is re-rating violently as AI workloads shift from training to serving. A 3x markup in four months for Modal signals how scarce investors believe this capacity is. Round sizes and investors are unknown from the capture.
