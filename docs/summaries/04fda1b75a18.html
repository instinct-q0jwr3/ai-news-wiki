Returning from vacation? The government can search your phone without a warrant

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

Writer and immigration advocate Thomas Kennedy sued the federal government after a Customs and Border Protection agent at Miami International Airport took his phone for about 45 minutes on re-entry. He says the agent searched his bags, asked about his books and magazines and threatened to keep the phone indefinitely if he refused. He believes data was copied and is asking the court to order its deletion.

Kennedy works with groups including the Florida Immigration Coalition and Amnesty International and thinks he was targeted for that work, since he was asked repeatedly whether he was an immigration attorney. The article frames it as a case study in warrantless border phone searches. Based on the first part of the article.
