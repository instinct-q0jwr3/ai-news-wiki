Chip design software leader Synopsys says it is exploring partnerships with Chinese AI labs to develop AI-powered chip design tools for the Chinese market

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Synopsys, the world's leading chip design software maker, is exploring partnerships with large language model builders in China to help Chinese companies develop chips faster with an AI version of its tools, saying it will work within Washington's tightening export controls, Nikkei reports.

The move comes as Synopsys forecasts $11.15 billion in FY27 revenue, with OpenAI and Amazon deals driving its AI push. The China partnerships would test how far a US EDA leader can go under the current control regime.
