Offline survival assistant – local LLM and Kiwix, runs from a USB stick

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-07 · confidence: high

Summary

An open-source offline survival assistant that works without internet on Windows, Linux and macOS from a laptop or USB drive. Questions search the kit's guides (BM25) and Kiwix archives, then a local llama.cpp model answers with numbered sources, or shows relevant passages if no model is present. A preparer downloads the model (1-5 GB), runtime and Kiwix libraries once, online. Health advice follows WHO, Red Cross and CDC guidance, with a disclaimer that it does not replace emergency services.

Highlights

  • Local LLM plus Kiwix, fully offline
  • Answers cite guide and Kiwix sources
  • One-time online preparation of the kit
  • Disclaimer: not a substitute for emergency services
