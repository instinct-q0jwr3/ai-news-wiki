It's enough - vibecoding tag crossed from useless to harmful

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-18 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high

Summary

A Lobsters meta post argues the 'vibecoding' tag has crossed from useless to harmful. The trajectory: it lost its original meaning as LLM-assisted engineering culture matured, then got silly as general LLM posts picked it up, then useless as anything mentioning an LLM-built project got tagged - and now, the author says, it's actively harmful because tagging valuable members' posts with it drives them away.

The quoted debate shows the split: half the site wants a filterable tag for AI-content fatigue, while others point at real harm - projects embracing LLM-written code becoming impossible to read or backport, with Debian reportedly giving up and shipping a feature update of rsync because nobody understood the generated patches well enough to backport fixes.

Highlights

  • Meta post: vibecoding tag went from useless to harmful on Lobsters
  • Tag creep: from its original meaning to any post mentioning LLM-built projects
  • Claim: tagging valuable members' posts pushes them away
  • Counterpoint in thread: AI-content fatigue makes a filter useful
  • Concrete harm cited: rsync's LLM patches unbackportable, Debian shipped a feature update
  • Debate over whether tags should follow content or provenance
