Quoting Anthropic Frontier Red Team

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison quotes Anthropic's Frontier Red Team evaluation of GLM-5.3: on 100 tasks from an internal Binary Exploitation benchmark, GLM-5.3 achieved full control-flow hijacks in 4% of trials versus 6% for Claude Mythos Preview. The significance, per the quote, is that a threshold has been crossed - earlier models such as Claude Opus 4.6 and GLM-5.2 succeeded in none of the trials.

Highlights

  • GLM-5.3: full control-flow hijacks in 4% of 100 binary-exploitation trials
  • Claude Mythos Preview: 6% on the same benchmark
  • Earlier models (Claude Opus 4.6, GLM-5.2): zero successes
  • Anthropic frames this as a meaningful threshold crossed in autonomous exploitation
  • Quotation collected by Simon Willison, 29 Sep 2026
