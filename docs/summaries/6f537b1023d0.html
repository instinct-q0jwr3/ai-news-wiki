Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Google's announcement of Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: native 15-second video (up from 10), duration in one-second steps from 3 to 15, multi-turn conversational editing, better reference following, and a 30% price cut to $0.07 per second at 720p. Available in the Gemini API, Google Vids and Gemini for business. A cheaper workflow drafts at 360p or 720p, fixes details with follow-up edits and upscales the final to 1080p or 4K.

Highlights

  • 15-second native clips, 3-15 s in one-second steps
  • Multi-turn conversational editing
  • 30% cheaper: $0.07/s at 720p
  • Draft low, upscale the final to 1080p or 4K
