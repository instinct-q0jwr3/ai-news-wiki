A US Senate investigation led by Senators Warren, Van Hollen, and Blumenthal says some hyperscalers misled the public about AI data centers' costs and benefits

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

A yearlong Senate investigation led by Senators Warren, Van Hollen and Blumenthal argues that major tech firms are misleading the public about the true costs and benefits of AI data centers. The probe, shared first with TIME, covered seven developers: Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft, CoreWeave, Digital Realty and Equinix, with staff requesting information and interviewing employees.

The findings, which largely match a year of news reporting, feed a broader congressional push to put guardrails around the power-intensive build-out. Warren has called for a national moratorium on new AI data centers until developers agree to cover the full costs, saying Congress must hold Big Tech accountable so companies pay their fair share.
