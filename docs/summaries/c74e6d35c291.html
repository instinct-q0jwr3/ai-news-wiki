Character.ai Files for Bankruptcy

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Character.AI, one of the first breakout startups of the generative AI boom, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the US to facilitate selling the company and its assets. It cites significant legal, regulatory and operational headwinds, after years of lawsuits and scrutiny over chatbot safety, and lists liabilities between $50 million and $100 million.

Disney would be the lead bidder, setting a baseline for rival offers: $20 million for undisclosed assets, transition support from former Character employees it has hired, and a nonexclusive license to unspecified IP. Disney recently hired former Character CEO Karandeep Anand as CTO and expects some of the technical team to follow, which the piece compares to an acquihire. Disney had earlier complained about unauthorized chatbots of its characters.

Highlights

  • Character.AI files Chapter 11 to enable a sale of assets.

  • Disney is lead bidder at $20M plus an IP license and transition support.
  • Disney already hired former CEO Karandeep Anand as CTO.
  • Years of lawsuits over chatbot safety preceded the filing.
