OpenAI cannot make AI safe on its own [pdf]

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Three safety researchers fired by OpenAI, including Mikita Balesni and Tomek Korbak, wrote a letter saying OpenAI cannot make AI safe on its own. They say communications around their firing have made former colleagues afraid to speak and to work with outside safety groups.

They argue that freedom to raise concerns and collaborate with independent experts is an essential safety mechanism, and that the path to superintelligence cannot be navigated safely without high-trust ways of working. The letter introduces each author's safety background. It is the researchers' own account of the dispute.
