UK AI Minister Kanishka Narayan says AI is set to add £55B to £140B to the economy by 2030, below a £400B estimate from a Google-led report touted by officials

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high

Summary

WIRED reports that the UK government told the public for two years that AI could add 400 billion pounds to the economy, but its AI minister, Kanishka Narayan, admitted in a parliamentary answer that the figure was far above the government's own projections. His own estimate is that AI adoption and data investment could add 55 to 140 billion pounds by 2030, with a central estimate of 120 billion, which he called 'hugely uncertain'.

He said the 400 billion figure came from a 2023 public report commissioned by Google and produced by consultancy Public First, now called PF Global, so the government's own estimate was less than a third of the number it had used to calm fears about jobs and an economic crash.
