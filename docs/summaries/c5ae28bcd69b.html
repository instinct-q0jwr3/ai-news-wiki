Tell HN: Google Search to start locking down search behind a login wall

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

A Hacker News poster reports seeing Google Search require login on some shared-network IP addresses after earlier CAPTCHA challenges. The report describes inconsistent behavior across connections rather than a verified policy applying to all signed-out users.

In the thread the poster agrees that the original title was too categorical. This is evidence of a user-observed access challenge, not confirmation that Google is putting every search behind a login wall or that AI scraping is the proven cause.

Highlights

  • Observation concerns selected IP addresses
  • Behavior differs across connections
  • Poster accepts a less categorical framing
  • No general policy announcement is supplied
  • Cause remains speculative
