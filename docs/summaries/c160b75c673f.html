Can we predict the jobs robots will do?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

A paper asks whether we can predict which jobs robots will do. Since many jobs are physical, AI's economic impact depends partly on robotics. The authors build a measure of job exposure to robots by using Claude to assess how well present-day robot capabilities match job tasks, arguing that listing current capabilities gives insight into the coming years. Based on the abstract.

Highlights

  • Measures job exposure to robots
  • Uses Claude to rate task-capability fit
  • Argues enumerating today's capabilities helps forecasting
  • Physical jobs matter for AI's economic impact
  • Basis: abstract
