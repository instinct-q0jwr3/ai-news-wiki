LLMs and Programming in the first days of 2024

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

antirez openai google meta mistral deepseek small-specialist-models ai-safety-incidents labor-displacement

Summary

antirez's early-2024 testimony as an individual programmer using LLMs daily - explicitly not a retrospective on 2023. Since ChatGPT, and later with locally-running models, he made extensive use of the technology to accelerate code-writing and, just as much, to avoid wasting mental energy on the parts of programming not worth the effort: hunting documentation for peculiar APIs, boilerplate, one-off glue.

He includes a concrete example from his own Redis work: a hashing-implementation question answered by DeepSeek Coder running on his MacBook M1 Max - the model flagged poor distribution from appending hash_id, suggested XOR mixing, and produced improved C code. 'Not bad for a model running on my MacBook' - an early data point that local models were already useful for real systems questions.
