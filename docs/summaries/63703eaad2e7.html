New York-based Melius, which provides AI tools for generating ad campaigns, images, and videos, raised $25M, including a $20M Series A and a $5M seed

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports Melius, a New York AI platform for ad campaigns, images and video, raised $25 million: a $20 million Series A led by CRV and a $5 million seed round. It claims over $1 million annualised revenue within two months of leaving stealth in July, and its co-founder admits the first product, a performance-marketing tool, did not take off at first.
