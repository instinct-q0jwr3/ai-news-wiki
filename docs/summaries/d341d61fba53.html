Microsoft unveils its third-gen in-house AI chip, the Maia 300, built on TSMC's N2P process node and aimed at serving as its "primary AI inference system"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Microsoft unveiled the Maia 300, its third-generation in-house AI chip built on TSMC's N2P process, designed as the 'primary AI inference system' for Microsoft's products and services - including the latest reasoning models and agentic tasks. Production rollout starts in 2027.

Microsoft's Scott Guthrie claims Maia 300 will deliver 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU-based systems, and up to 7x the rack-scale token throughput of Maia 200 in a full 96-accelerator configuration. The endgame: scaling the Azure Maia family to multi-gigawatt deployments so Microsoft relies mainly on its own silicon instead of Nvidia and AMD.
