SuperMe, which generates AI-powered chats with simulated profiles of prominent industry experts to let users ask questions, raised $20M

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

SuperMe raised $20 million led by Kirsten Green at Forerunner, with Greylock Partners participating, to scale a network of AI profiles built from the knowledge of real experts. Ask a career or work question and the platform assembles a group chat of the AI profiles best suited to answer, drawing on what prominent operators actually know. Founders Casey Winters (ex-Grubhub, Pinterest, Eventbrite) and Ludo Antonov (ex-Pinterest, Lyft, Whatnot) started the company in 2024 after connecting while Winters advised Whatnot.

Winters' framing: Silicon Valley runs on hidden networks of who is genuinely best at niche skills, and SuperMe's bet is that those networks can be made transparent - and the insights locked in experts' heads made available on demand - as AI companions take over the role of tapping your network for advice.
