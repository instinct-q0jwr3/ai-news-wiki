Hark debuts an AI agent a year before its first devices

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

The Next Web reports that Hark has released Hark Pro on the web and mobile ahead of hardware planned for 2027. The company pitches an agent for errands such as groceries, bookings and bill payment, with a cloud memory system, an encrypted credential vault and up to six simultaneous browsers. Its listed plans are free, $20 and $100 monthly.

The article compares that software-first launch with Meta's Muse and OpenAI's forthcoming device, while pointing out unresolved European availability and payment-authentication questions. It explicitly corrects an earlier browser-count claim from 36 to six. The described automation and vault properties are company claims, not an independent test.

Highlights

  • Software arrives before Hark's first devices.
  • Web, iOS and Android are the reported surfaces.
  • The corrected browser count is six.
  • The article lists three subscription tiers.
  • European availability is not established.
  • Agent-mediated payment still raises authentication questions.
