Introducing harness adapters: adapt your favorite model to your harness to improve performance and cost

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Hugging Face describes reinforcement-learning LoRA adapters trained inside particular coding harnesses for Qwen3.8-27B. The post reports gains in task success and fewer tokens or tool calls under the tested harnesses, alongside a merged adapter. Its pipeline validates task graders with wrong-answer controls and filters for tasks the base model sometimes solves, rather than treating every available task as useful training data.

These are the authors' experiments on a particular model, task mixture and harness versions. The reported pi improvement from about 39% to 55% pass@1 and token reduction from 2.9K to 1.5K do not guarantee gains after a harness changes. Fewer blocked tool calls during training do not by themselves distinguish safer behavior from avoidance of a detector. The recipe was read, not executed, and no adapters, dependencies or compute jobs were installed or launched.

Highlights

  • Adapters are trained within pi, Hermes and OpenCode harnesses
  • Authors report task-success and efficiency gains
  • Grader validation includes known-wrong controls
  • Task difficulty filtering seeks a useful RL learning signal
  • Reported gains are model, task and harness dependent
  • Fewer detector blocks do not alone establish general safety
  • No training or installation was performed
