the US restores the UK AISI's access to the latest US models for safety testing, after ordering OpenAI and Anthropic to stop sharing models last month

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high

Summary

The US has restored the UK AI Safety Institute's access to the latest US models for safety testing, per Telegraph sources, reversing last month's order telling OpenAI and Anthropic to stop sharing models with the UK regulator. The block had cut UK regulators off from reviewing frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google DeepMind.
