Every, a media company that provides consulting services to help companies become AI-native, raised a $17.5M Series A at a $110M post-money valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Fortune reports Every raising a $17.5 million Series A at a $110 million post-money valuation, led by IA Ventures with other investors. It describes a business combining AI-focused writing, consulting, software and events, with founder Dan Shipper saying he retains control and sought a trusted investor rather than running a broad fundraising process.

Subscriber counts, staffing and control arrangements are reported through the interview rather than audited accounts or independently inspected legal terms. The financing is not proof that venture-backed media economics have been solved, and Shipper's optimism about AI augmenting human work is a viewpoint, not an employment forecast established by this round.

Highlights

  • Reported round is $17.5 million
  • Valuation is post-money rather than annual revenue
  • Business mixes media, services, software and events
  • Founder describes retaining control
  • Subscriber figures remain reported company context
  • No audited profitability or future outcome is established
