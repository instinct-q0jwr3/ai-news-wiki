Meta adds an FDA-cleared hearing enhancement feature to supported Meta Glasses for Meta One subscribers or a one-time cost of $149, rolling out later this year

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme meta

Summary

Meta is adding an FDA-cleared hearing enhancement feature to supported Meta Glasses, aimed at users with mild to moderate hearing loss who might otherwise use a hearing aid. It can be switched on or off with a voice command ('Hey Meta, start Hearing Enhancement'), requires no prescription, and can be set up at home.

Pricing is a subscription or a one-time $149, with a 30-day free trial, and the software is HSA/FSA eligible. The feature goes beyond the existing Conversation Focus mode for noisy environments, and arrives alongside improvements to the glasses' Detailed Responses feature based on feedback from the blind and low-vision community, plus new customizable audio and controls.

Highlights

  • FDA-cleared hearing enhancement for supported Meta Glasses, targeting mild-to-moderate hearing loss.
  • Voice-activated ('Hey Meta, start Hearing Enhancement'); no prescription; home setup.
  • Pricing: subscription or $149 one-time, 30-day trial, HSA/FSA eligible.
  • Distinct from the existing Conversation Focus noise mode.
  • Ships with accessibility-driven Detailed Responses improvements and new audio controls.
