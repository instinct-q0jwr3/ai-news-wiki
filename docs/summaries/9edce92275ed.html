Artifacts Are Alive

Summary

There is a photograph of a coral reef on the wall of my dentist’s office.

Scott Werner Apr 25, 2026 20 3 7 Share There is a photograph of a coral reef on the wall of my dentist’s office.

I’ve stared at it and imagined I was snorkeling with them at basically every cleaning since he put it up.

Highlights

  • But as my jaw starts to ache, I’m reminded of the core feature that makes it a photograph.
  • I cannot wait for the fish to move and see where they go.
  • I cannot go over to the strange thing in the bottom-left corner that might be a sea slug or might just be a rock or might be something I’ve never seen before and would like to learn about.
  • For the longest time, whenever we needed to communicate an idea, we’d reach for whatever tool was available and make a static artifact: a blog post, a slide deck, a PDF, a README, a Confluence page, a Notion document.
  • We capture the idea at a moment in time, from one angle, at one level of detail, and then we hand it to someone and say “here, understand this.” And they read it.
  • I’ve been calling it Bring Your Own Claude Code ( or, ok, bring your own agent ).
  • For Artifact Land, this means: you connect your own agent, it builds artifacts using your own inference budget, and Artifact Land stores and surfaces them.
  • Your agent can build artifacts that reference your codebase, your design system, your conventions, your data, your context.
