Launch HN: Versine (YC F26) – MCP to send and receive money

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

Versine's founders introduce an MCP-connected payment wallet using USDC on Base. Their launch post describes passkey approval for one-time transfers, configurable trusted-contact permissions and claims for people who do not yet have an account, with group payments as an example use.

This is founder-supplied product and regulatory framing, not an independent custody, compliance or security audit. Standing permission for trusted contacts changes approval risk; self-custody does not itself establish recoverability after a wrong transfer. Questions about disputes and passkey failures in comments remain unresolved observations, and no wallet, permission or transfer was created.

Highlights

  • Wallet balance is described as USDC on Base
  • One-time sends use app and passkey approval
  • Trusted contacts can use standing permissions
  • Off-platform recipients use a claim flow
  • Regulatory status is a founder claim, not legal verification
  • Reversal and dispute risks remain important unknowns
  • No payment action was taken
