Crypto and AI super PACs spent $250M in 2026 midterms, but lost 9/12 competitive races called so far, spending $74.7M on losing candidates and $28.3M on winners

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Molly White's election-night tally says crypto and AI super PACs spent $250 million in the midterms but mostly lost where it mattered. Most spending went to safe seats, but nearly three-quarters went to competitive races, and in the twelve competitive races called so far the side they backed lost nine. They spent $74.7 million on losing sides versus $28.3 million on winners.

Examples: Sherrod Brown won Ohio despite more than $64 million of crypto spending against him, the most ever on a single race; Annie Andrews beat Darline Graham in South Carolina despite about $4.1 million of AI money; in Wisconsin's District 1 the incumbent subcommittee chair lost despite $1.9 million; and in Michigan and Iowa Democrats won over crypto and AI-group spending. The author says the PACs' leverage depends on lawmakers fearing them and there is now less reason to. A longer postmortem is promised next week.
