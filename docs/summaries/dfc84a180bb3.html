NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe and Granary v2: Intelligent Multilingual Transcription You Can Prompt and Adapt

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weight, promptable speech model that folds transcription, speaker diarization, translation and audio question answering into a single checkpoint. It transcribes 40 languages, translates 31 of them to or from English, attributes speakers in 13, and handles recordings up to an hour long with up to eight speakers. Because it is promptable, developers can steer the output with natural language - formatting, domain vocabulary, accent adaptation - without retraining. It tops the Hugging Face Open ASR Leaderboard for English among open-weight models, posts the lowest error rate on David AI's 21-language diarization leaderboard and ranks first in VoiceArena's 14-system comparison.

The release comes with the scaffolding to customize it: Granary v2, a multilingual dataset with a reusable curation pipeline, plus NeMo Speech fine-tuning tools. In a medical case study, fine-tuning cut word error rate on held-out speakers by 45.8 percent relative to the best prompt alone. Quantized versions are coming for local runs on RTX Spark, DGX Spark or Jetson AGX Thor, scaling up to Blackwell data-center GPUs.

Highlights

  • Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe: open-weight, promptable speech model on Hugging Face and ModelScope
  • One checkpoint: transcription (40 languages), translation (31), diarization (13), audio QA
  • Up to one-hour recordings and eight speakers
  • #1 open-weight English model on HF Open ASR Leaderboard; best diarization on David AI's 21-language board
  • Natural-language prompting steers format, vocabulary and accents
  • Granary v2 dataset plus NeMo fine-tuning; medical fine-tune cut WER 45.8% vs best prompt
  • Quantized local versions coming for RTX/DGX Spark and Jetson Thor
