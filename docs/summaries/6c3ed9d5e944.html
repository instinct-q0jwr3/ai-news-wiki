AI startup acquisitions by other AI startups hit 195 through Sept. 29, up 14% from all of 2025, but number of buyers grew just 2%; OpenAI was the most active

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Crunchbase's review finds that AI startups are increasingly buying one another to acquire teams, enter markets and fill product gaps. It counts 195 acquisitions by venture-backed AI companies through September 29, already 14% above all of 2025, while the number of buyers rose only 2%. The implication is more repeat buying by an existing set of acquirers, rather than a comparable expansion in the buyer population.

OpenAI leads the activity, and legal AI, customer-service and coding companies also make repeated purchases. The article links the pattern to pressure for rapid growth and the speed advantage of buying a ready-made team or product. Financial totals are much less clear than deal counts: prices were disclosed for only 12 of the 195 transactions, so the dataset cannot establish total acquisition spending.

Highlights

  • 195 deals are counted through September 29, not the full year.
  • Deal count is 14% above all of 2025.
  • Buyer count rose only 2%.
  • Repeat acquirers drive much of the increase.
  • OpenAI is the most active buyer in the review.
  • Only 12 deal prices are disclosed; total spending remains uncertain.
