Recursion into madness

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

lcamtuf

Summary

lcamtuf, a security person, wants to know how models fail - and recursion is his lab: agent loops feed models their own output, and it's the mechanic behind both model-collapse doomerism and self-improvement gospel. His demo: ask Gemini to 'boldly rewrite' a Raymond Chandler passage, then feed it only its previous output, round after round.

The prose degrades on a tour through kitsch horror - mirrors 'weaponized the brutal glare', Mrs. Regan's legs become 'stiletto blades' - until around iteration 20 the model steps back from the ledge and the output stabilizes at iteration 31 as cycling tepid menace ('venom wrapped in high fashion'). The lesson: open-ended rewrites don't diverge forever; they thrash, then settle into 'peak LLMese' - a stable point far from the source. Image pipelines, with their per-step noise, collapse much faster: a few edits in, pure chaos.

Highlights

  • Recursive self-consumption is the core of agent loops, model collapse and self-improvement claims
  • Experiment: 31 rounds of Gemini rewriting one Chandler passage
  • Output drifts into bizarre horror metaphors, then stabilizes far from the source
  • Rewrites converge to 'peak LLMese' rather than diverging forever
  • Image editing collapses faster: a few iterations to chaos from accumulated noise
