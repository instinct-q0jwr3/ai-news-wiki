Business map startup Veridion raises a $20M Series A

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Veridion, a business-intelligence startup founded in 2019, raised a $20 million round for its 'live business graph' - a continuously updated digital representation of around 640 million businesses worldwide. The platform analyzes billions of digital signals from company websites, public registries, regulatory filings, product catalogues, social profiles and news sources, pitching itself against traditional market-intelligence providers whose data is updated quarterly or annually.

The company is targeting banks, investors, insurers and other organizations that need current pictures of commercial risk as geopolitics, supply chains, extreme weather and economic conditions shift by the hour. In its own words: 'Information updated quarterly or annually is no longer intelligence. It's history.'
