DAPO

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-21 · confidence: high

tldr-ai

Summary

DAPO (Decoupled Clip and Dynamic Sampling Policy Optimization) is a fully open-sourced system for large-scale LLM reinforcement learning: algorithm, code infrastructure and dataset, built on the verl framework. The release is framed as giving the broader research community practical access to scalable RL rather than keeping it inside labs.

Headline result: 50 points on AIME 2024 from the Qwen2.5-32B base model, outperforming the previous state of the art DeepSeek-R1-Zero-Qwen-32B with 50% of the training steps. The reported training dynamics emphasize stability: steady response-length growth enabling exploration of more complex reasoning, a stable rising reward signal, and controlled entropy increase.
