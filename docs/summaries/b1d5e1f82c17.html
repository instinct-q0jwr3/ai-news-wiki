SemiAnalysis estimates ~90% of Anthropic's business comes from agentic AI, while sources say nearly 25% of its revenue in 2025 came from just two clients

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high

Summary

The Financial Times reports that SemiAnalysis estimates about 90% of Anthropic's business comes from agentic AI, and that sources say nearly 25% of Anthropic's 2025 revenue came from just two clients. Powerful AI tools burn through budgets, which is prompting a rethink of how the technology is priced before frontier-lab IPOs. Based on the Techmeme excerpt; the FT article was not retrieved.
