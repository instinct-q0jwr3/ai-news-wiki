Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Sister Daniela Sold $561M in Stock During IPO

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

The filing interpretation is Forbes' reporting; the original SEC forms were not independently inspected here. The transactions are described as part of the IPO, not a fresh decision after subsequent price declines.
