Chan Karunamuni on the Duo’s Open/Close Translucent Transition

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-24 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple human interface designer Chan Karunamuni, cited by Daring Fireball, describes the open/close transition he designed for the newly announced iPhone Duo. The animation aims to make the device's two displays feel like one seamless surface by perceptually bending the focal plane rather than the content, so opening the phone feels like opening a window onto a single view.

Highlights

  • Apple's iPhone Duo ships with an open/close transition designed by HI designer Chan Karunamuni.
  • The design bends the focal plane perceptually, not the content, so two displays read as one seamless view.
  • The stated goal: opening the device should feel like opening a window onto a singular view.
