Reddit plans to require users to log in to fully access the site in response to AI scraping and will test it in select markets outside the US this quarter

Summary

Axios reports Reddit plans to require users to log in to fully access the site, a direct response to AI scraping, and will test the change in select markets outside the US this quarter.
