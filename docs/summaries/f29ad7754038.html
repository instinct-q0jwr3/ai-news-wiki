AI training data startup Micro1 raised $100M+ at a $4B valuation, up from $500M in September 2025

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme xai

Summary

AI training-data startup Micro1 raised more than $100 million at a $4 billion valuation, Forbes reports, up from a $500 million valuation in September 2025. Ali Ansari's company began last year as a recruiting business with $7 million in ARR and now generates over $500 million in ARR; two xAI cofounders invested in the latest round.

Highlights

  • Micro1 raised $100M+ at a $4B valuation, up from $500M a year earlier.
  • The company went from $7M ARR as a recruiting business to over $500M ARR.
  • Two xAI cofounders joined the latest round.
