It remains “very difficult [to find] actual, materially measurable financial impacts” from spending on AI, per new study

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

Gary Marcus cites a new study reported by The Information finding it is 'very difficult' to find materially measurable financial impacts from AI spending, and contrasts that minimal documented ROI with trillions invested. He adds that Character.AI has filed for bankruptcy and that CoreWeave bonds have jumped to about 15%, which he reads as bankers having had enough.

Highlights

  • New study finds little measurable financial impact from AI spending.
  • Marcus: minimal ROI against trillions invested.
  • Character.AI bankrupt; CoreWeave bond yields about 15%.
