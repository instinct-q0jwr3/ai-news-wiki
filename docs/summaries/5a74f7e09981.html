Cross-border ecommerce lender Qupital raises a $300M Series C

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Hong Kong-based Qupital, which offers cross-border ecommerce financing to SMEs, raised a $300 million Series C led by Asia-based asset manager M Capital, with additional asset-backed securities commitments from Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group (MUFG) and Quester Capital. The capital will broaden its financing reach across China, the US, Japan and Southeast Asia and keep funding its proprietary AI risk engine.

Qupital underwrites merchants automatically from live sales and operational data on marketplaces including Amazon, TikTok Shop, TMall and JD.com. The company says it has been profitable for the past two years, expects profit margins to climb beyond 45% within 12 months, and is weighing capital-markets options including a possible IPO, additional fundraising and strategic acquisitions. Co-founder Andy Chan framed the round against a 'trillion-dollar liquidity gap' in cross-border ecommerce; CEO Winston Wong pointed at agentic and social commerce as the next demand wave.
