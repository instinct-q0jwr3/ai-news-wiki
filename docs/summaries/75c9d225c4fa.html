How Claude is uplifting biomolecular modeling

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-20 · confidence: high

tldr-ai anthropic

Summary

In this post, we share how Claude made the open-source models that scientists use to predict and design biomolecules faster and more memory-efficient. Claude, working within Claude Science, optimized more than 30 of these models in just under four weeks, speeding them up roughly 4x on average.

Recently, we shared results demonstrating Claude’s abilities to design de novo protein binders through expert-level orchestration of open-source protein design and structure prediction models.

Highlights

  • It also created a low-memory mode that enables the accurate prediction of biomolecular systems larger than 10,000 tokens (amino acids, nucleotides, and atoms from small molecules and ions) on a single NVIDIA GPU node.
  • We are open-sourcing all of the optimized code and announcing a protein design competition co-sponsored with Adaptyv Bio, backed by up to $1 million in Claude credits and wet lab validation for over 5,000 designs.
  • We allowed Claude to spend up to $10,000 per target on the AI infrastructure platform Modal, roughly equivalent to 2,500 NVIDIA H100 GPU hours.
  • As an early result of these optimizations, Claude Mythos 5.1 accelerated seven open-source biology models, enabling them to run up to 2.5 times faster.

  • Claude also improved the memory utilization of these models, making it possible to predict biomolecular systems of unprecedented sizes.
