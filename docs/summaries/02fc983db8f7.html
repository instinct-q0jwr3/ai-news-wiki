GPT-Rosalind-6 System Card Addendum

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-06 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI's system card addendum for GPT-Rosalind-6 describes a frontier reasoning model aimed at biology, drug discovery and translational medicine - same weights as flagship GPT-6 Astra, but with fewer refusals on dual-use biological queries, gated behind trusted access: qualified scientists, institutes, companies and government partners whose proposed uses, security controls and governance are reviewed before access is granted. It still refuses biological weaponization and keeps refusals on other safety topics.

Under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, Rosalind-6 is treated as High capability in the Biological and Chemical domain - below Critical - while inheriting Astra's assessments elsewhere, including Critical in Cybersecurity and below High in AI Self-Improvement. The document is a marker of where deployment policy is heading: capability tiers unlocked per domain, for vetted users, with the general-purpose model keeping its guardrails and the trusted tier getting a longer leash. The tension with this week's Anthropic work - where the safety classifiers themselves are being stress-tested - is the field's live argument.

Highlights

  • GPT-Rosalind-6: Astra's weights tuned for biology, drug discovery and translational medicine
  • Fewer refusals on dual-use biological queries - but only via trusted, reviewed access
  • Still refuses weaponization; other safety refusals intact
  • Preparedness: High in Bio/Chem (below Critical); Critical in Cybersecurity; below High in self-improvement
  • The emerging deployment pattern: domain-specific capability tiers for vetted users
