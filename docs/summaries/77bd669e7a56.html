HP reports Q4 revenue up 9.3% YoY to $16B, above $15.3B est., gives "prudent" fiscal 2027 guidance above estimates, and says AI PCs reached ~60% of shipments

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

An accessible Bloomberg opening says HP forecasts adjusted annual earnings of $3.15 to $3.45 per share against an average analyst estimate of $3.18. The report interprets this as better management of memory-chip costs and says shares rose in extended trading; the feed separately reports quarterly revenue and AI-PC shipment share.

Only the opening was readable, not HP's full earnings release or accounting reconciliation. The $16 billion revenue, 9.3% growth and roughly 60% AI-PC share remain feed-level claims here. Adjusted earnings guidance is not a guaranteed outcome or the same measure as GAAP profit, and after-hours reaction is not a later closing price.
