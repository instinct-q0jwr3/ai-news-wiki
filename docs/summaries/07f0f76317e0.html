Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Orchestrator

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Google Antigravity adds Hydrogen and a dynamic Orchestrator option that pairs it with Carbon. The design can move from planning to execution and back when a task encounters difficulty, rather than locking the whole task to a model chosen at its start.

Google reports near-Carbon success with 24% less quota and 19% faster completion on its stated terminal benchmark. The Antigravity announcement describes Hydrogen rolling out to paid Pro and Ultra users, Orchestrator to Ultra, and enterprise access through Gemini for business. Those are launch access terms and measured claims, not an assurance of the same savings on all projects.

Highlights

  • Dynamic switching within one trajectory
  • Carbon and Hydrogen serve different task needs
  • Vendor reports quota and completion-time reductions
  • Hydrogen and Orchestrator have different plan access
  • Enterprise access is separately described
