DeepSeek raised ~$15B in its latest fundraising round, one of China's largest ever, with CATL and Tencent among the biggest contributors

Summary

DeepSeek has raised roughly $15 billion (about 100 billion yuan) in its latest fundraising round, one of China's largest ever, with battery giant CATL and Tencent among the biggest contributors, Bloomberg reports citing sources. The round would cement DeepSeek's position as China's flagship AI lab with backing from national industrial champions.
