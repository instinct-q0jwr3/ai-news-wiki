Show HN: Your Spreadsheet Is Your Database

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Spreadsheet to App describes importing an Excel workbook into Google Sheets and building an interface that reads and writes the imported tables. The accessible page is a brief product pitch.

It does not establish faithful formula conversion, access controls, concurrent-write behavior or suitable backups. Keeping data in a sheet may simplify an initial workflow without making it equivalent to a transactional database.

Highlights

  • Pitch converts workbook data into a sheet-backed app
  • Interface is described as reading and writing
  • Formula fidelity was not checked
  • Access and concurrency limits are unknown
  • Spreadsheet storage is not automatically database equivalence
