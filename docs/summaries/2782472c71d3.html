Booking Holdings launches Vayo, a travel startup that uses AI agents to track booking confirmations, price drops, and more, with a planned $100M investment

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high

Summary

Booking Holdings launches Vayo, a Seattle travel startup led by Mike Fridgen, betting that travel-specific agents beat general assistants. Booking plans to invest up to $100 million. Agents read the user's inbox for confirmations, watch for price drops and openings, and flag knock-on effects of delays. Restaurant booking works now, flights and hotels in weeks; a free private beta waitlist opens (GeekWire).

Highlights

  • Booking plans up to $100M for Vayo.
  • Agents track confirmations, price drops and delays from the inbox.
  • Restaurant reservations now; flights and hotels soon.
