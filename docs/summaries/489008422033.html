[AINews] Google admits AI Mode hurts Search revenue; -15% $500B drop worst since 2004 IPO. AI Bear Market coming?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Latent.Space's AINews breaks its own rule against financial stories for the Google earnings moment: Chief Business Officer Philipp Schindler acknowledged that 'the strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode' - the company admitting, in effect, that its AI answers monetize worse than the search results they replace. The newsletter frames it as the old featured-snippet tension turned inward: a good enough summary obviates reading the source, and now the source being obviated is Google's own ad inventory.

Combined with other weak earnings it set off steep declines across tech, against a backdrop of souring AI sentiment and OpenAI raising at $1.25T rather than $1.4T - 'not the sort of thing you want in the lead up to one of the biggest tech IPOs of all time.'

Highlights

  • Google's Schindler: AI Mode query growth hurt revenue because AI Mode monetizes lower
  • The summary-versus-source tension turns inward on Google's own ad inventory
  • Alphabet's drop combined with weak results across tech into a broad selloff
  • Context: OpenAI raising at $1.25T not $1.4T ahead of a giant IPO window
