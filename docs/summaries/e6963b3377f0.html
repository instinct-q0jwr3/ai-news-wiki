Dell, Jera, and UK-based Rhaelm partner to build a $15B, 400MW off-grid AI data center and gas power plant near Tokyo, set to begin operations around 2028

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

The Financial Times reports that Dell, Jera and UK-based Rhaelm will build a $15 billion, 400-megawatt off-grid AI data center with its own gas power plant near Tokyo, due to start around 2028. It would be among Asia's largest such projects outside China. Based on the Techmeme excerpt; the FT article was paywalled.

Highlights

  • $15B, 400MW off-grid AI data center near Tokyo
  • Partners: Dell, Jera, Rhaelm
  • Dedicated gas power plant
  • Operations around 2028
  • One of Asia's largest outside China
