EliseAI, which provides AI tools for health care and housing industries, raised $350M at a $4B valuation, up from $2.2B after raising $250M in August 2025

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

EliseAI, which automates back-office work for landlords and health systems, has raised $350 million at a $4 billion valuation - nearly double the $2.2B it commanded 13 months ago. The round was co-led by existing investors a16z and Bessemer with new investor Ontario Teachers', plus Sapphire and Navitas, and was entirely primary capital with no secondary sales, CEO Minna Song told Fortune. Song credits execution over any single catalyst: expanded products and penetration across housing and healthcare. The funds go to product development and expansion. It is the fourth time a16z and Bessemer have backed the company since 2023.
