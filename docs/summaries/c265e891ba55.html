Show HN: Fakeipedia – Wikipedia for worlds that don't exist

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high

Summary

Basis: the project's own Show HN post; the live page returned almost no readable text. Fakeipedia is an automatic world-building platform: give it an imagined world and it generates a whole fictional wiki you can explore endlessly by following red links, in the style of Wikipedia for places that don't exist.

The creator says the idea came from experimenting with worldbuilding in Gemini, but release waited until a model judged creative enough - Argon - could write articles worth reading. Generating an article carries a nominal charge of $0.90.

Highlights

  • Generates an entire explorable fictional wiki from an imagined world premise
  • Navigation mimics Wikipedia, including red links to not-yet-written articles
  • Built on a model the author calls Argon, chosen for creative writing quality
  • Article generation costs a nominal $0.90
