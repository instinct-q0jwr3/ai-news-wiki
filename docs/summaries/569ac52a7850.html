Prominent art therapist quoted by Vice, Forbes and others is AI-generated

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Press Gazette reports that 'Dr Eleni Nicolaou', an art therapist quoted more than 30 times by outlets including Forbes, Vice, Glamour, Tom's Guide and Yahoo, appears not to exist. Her profile photograph and much of her output show signs of being AI-generated, and she was removed from the journalist response service Qwoted after her details proved false.

Nicolaou purported to be an expert in residence for paint-by-numbers seller Davincified, and all of her Facebook and LinkedIn profiles link back to that company; there is no sign of her purported Cyprus practice online. She did not respond to verification requests. The case lands amid growing concern about synthetic expert sources entering news coverage through response services.
