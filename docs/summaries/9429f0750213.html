Apple Introduces $50 AI Usage Packs in Logic Pro

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple updated Logic Pro with a $49.99 one-time Intelligence Usage Pack, its first charge for extended Apple Intelligence use. The packs extend features that hit usage limits because they rely on cloud models from OpenAI or Google Gemini.

Highlights

  • First paid Apple Intelligence add-on
  • $49.99 one-time
  • Covers cloud-model-based features that have limits
