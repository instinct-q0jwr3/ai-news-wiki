Building strong safety evaluations

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic argues that safety evaluations must measure appropriate behavior in context rather than treating refusal as universally desirable. Its guidance combines domain expertise, realistic multi-turn scenarios, validated graders, preregistered analysis and uncertainty reporting.

The examples show why observational score differences do not identify causes, why configuration changes can alter results, and why apparent percentage-point gaps may be sampling noise. The article is a methods proposal informed by the company’s own evaluations, not proof that its models are safe across untested settings.

Highlights

  • Construct validity comes before a headline score
  • Multi-turn context can change the right response
  • Grader failures can silently distort results
  • Causal claims need controlled comparisons
  • Intervals and configuration support interpretation
