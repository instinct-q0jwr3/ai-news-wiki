Summary

A short story: Ivan, an engineer, discovers Claude and automates his email, calendar, documents and research over days without sleep. Each module works and each takes something from him; his girlfriend stops texting, his thoughts fray at 4 AM, and he feels both a cheat code and a creeping dread. A fictional sketch of automation compulsion in the Claude era.
