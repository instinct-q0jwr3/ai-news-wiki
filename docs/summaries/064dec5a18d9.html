Oracle adds ChatGPT and Codex to its app stack and expands Oracle Cloud Infrastructure lineup with Google TPUs, OpenAI's Jalapeño chip, and Cerebras hardware

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports Oracle is adding ChatGPT and Codex to its application stack, announced at Oracle AI World in Las Vegas where Sam Altman joined co-CEO Clay Magouyrk on stage - one day after Anthropic's S-1 filing. Oracle's cloud president Mahesh Thiagarajan said his team watched OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model train at Oracle's Stargate campus in Abilene, Texas.

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure is also expanding its hardware lineup: Google's TPUs will run on another hyperscaler's cloud for the first time, alongside OpenAI's Jalapeño chip and Cerebras hardware. Thiagarajan said this is no pivot away from Nvidia - its chips remain 'the best, most fungible' - but chip scarcity makes accelerator choice valuable.

Highlights

  • ChatGPT and Codex embedded in Oracle's app stack
  • Google TPUs run on a rival hyperscaler's cloud for the first time
  • OCI also adds OpenAI's Jalapeño chip and Cerebras
  • GPT-6 Astra trained at Oracle's Abilene Stargate campus
