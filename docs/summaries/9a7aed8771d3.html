Just say no to Anthropic’s IPO

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Gary Marcus argues against buying Anthropic's IPO, citing a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, nearly $900 billion in future liabilities and a history of losses apart from one subsidized quarter. He quotes critics such as Ed Zitron on slowing Q3 revenue growth and passages in the amended S-1 that admit costs and losses will continue and that growth will not stay at comparable rates. It is an opinion piece built on quoted posts.

Highlights

  • Opinion: avoid the IPO
  • Cites about $895B in commitments and past losses
  • Quotes S-1 language on continued losses
  • Relies on other commentators, such as Zitron
