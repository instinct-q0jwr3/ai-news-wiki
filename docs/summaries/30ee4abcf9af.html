The Recommendation Engine for Commodity Traders — Anekha Sokhal, Moshi

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

On the Latent Space podcast from AI Engineer New York, Moshi CEO Anekha Sokhal describes building the tool she wished she'd had as an energy-derivatives trader at JPMorgan: a recommendation engine that connects the flood of market information to what actually matters for a trader's book. She pitches Moshi as what you might get if Bloomberg, Claude, and Netflix had a baby - market data, an interface for working with it, and recommendations personalized to the trader.

The conversation with swyx ranges across trusted sources, document extraction, and the build-versus-buy question for models, with Sokhal arguing that useful AI has to fit the way people actually work rather than demanding a new workflow. She also touches on the pressure on AI engineers who are told 'you are the team,' and shares what customer conversations at the conference taught her - why meeting users comes before hiring.
