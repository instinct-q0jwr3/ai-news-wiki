Thiel's leaked Rome lectures cast EA and AI "safetyism" as Antichrist forerunners, Bill Gates as "Mr. Hyde", and Eliezer Yudkowsky as "more Luddite than Greta"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

The Verge reports on leaked recordings of Peter Thiel's Rome lectures, in which he casts effective altruism and AI 'safetyism' as forerunners of the Antichrist, Bill Gates as 'Mr. Hyde', and Eliezer Yudkowsky as 'more Luddite than Greta'. In the transcript, Thiel works through a Cold War II framework: because the Antichrist 'talks about Armageddon nonstop', fear of sleepwalking into Armageddon pushes the world toward peace at any price, which he reads through 1 Thessalonians 5:3 ('peace and safety') as the unjust peace, against Matthew 24:6's 'see that ye be not troubled'. He also argues Pope Benedict privately read the church's decline during his papacy as a sign of the end times, citing Ratzinger's cryptic 1959 dissertation footnotes on Bonaventure.

  • Framework: fear of Armageddon drives 'peace at any price', the unjust peace
