Threat of AI share crash puts world financial stability at risk, says IMF

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

The IMF's twice-yearly Global Financial Stability Report names a sharp fall in AI share prices as one of three salient risks to world financial stability, alongside rising government borrowing costs and a major cyber-attack. It points to sky-high valuations, heavy borrowing to fund AI investment and the interconnection between big AI companies.

The Fund says the consequences of an AI correction could be global. Asian markets would be most exposed, the UK and euro area somewhat insulated, though investors holding US assets would be hit. Deputy director Athanasios Vamvakidis says the rest of the world's exposure to US assets, especially AI, is the highest ever, which supports growth through wealth effects today but would spread losses in a correction.

Highlights

  • IMF lists an AI share crash among three 'salient risks' to stability.
  • Cited drivers: high valuations, rising debt and interlinked AI companies.
  • Asia most exposed; UK and euro area potentially more insulated.
  • Global exposure to US AI assets is the highest it has ever been.
