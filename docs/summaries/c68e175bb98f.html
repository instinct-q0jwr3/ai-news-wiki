Google SWE points 48 AI agents at NASA Lidar, finds unmapped Maya pyramid in 72h

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

A Google software engineer traveling in Peru pointed 48 AI agents at NASA lidar data and, in under 72 hours, went from a random thought to an official reply from Mexico's INAH about a Maya site that, as far as anyone can tell, was unreported. The diary-format post shows the workflow: agents check which known-looking villages are absent from the literature and archives, then advise contacting INAH and help write the report.

The author is not an archaeologist and presents it as one story of what agent workflows now let an amateur do, with the discovery still to be confirmed by INAH.

Highlights

  • 72 hours from idea to a reply from Mexico's INAH
  • Agents searched literature and archives, drafted the report
  • Amateur author; discovery awaits INAH confirmation
