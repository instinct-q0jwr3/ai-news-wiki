AI chip startup Tenstorrent raised a $1.5B Series E led by Trace Capital and Innovation X at a $6.2B valuation, up from $2.6B after raising $700M in 2024

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Basis: Techmeme's feed summary of a Bloomberg article; the Bloomberg source fetch failed. AI chip startup Tenstorrent has raised a $1.5 billion Series E led by Trace Capital and Innovation X at a $6.2 billion valuation - up sharply from the $2.6 billion valuation at which it raised $700 million in 2024. Bloomberg notes the raise follows takeover interest in the company earlier this year. Tenstorrent, led by veteran chip designer Jim Keller, remains one of the best-funded independent challengers to Nvidia's accelerator dominance.
