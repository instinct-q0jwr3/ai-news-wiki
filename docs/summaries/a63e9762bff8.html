Nvidia verbally told cloud compute clients to sell 51%+ of their services to non-Chinese companies, imposing new limits on China's access to AI chips

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

The Financial Times reports Nvidia has verbally told cloud compute clients to sell at least 51% of their services to non-Chinese companies - a self-policing quota that further restricts China's access to AI chips through the cloud rental channel. Nvidia is effectively enforcing export controls on its own customers, without a written rule, as Washington's restrictions keep leaking through the services layer.

Highlights

  • FT: Nvidia verbally instructs cloud clients to sell 51%+ of services to non-Chinese customers
  • Export controls extend to the rental channel - enforced by the vendor, verbally, without a written rule
