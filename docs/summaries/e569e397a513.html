Nicolas Cage says he won't work with Amazon again over AI dispute

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

The LA Times reports that Nicolas Cage said at New York Comic Con that he refused to sign an AI waiver for the Amazon-backed series Spider-Noir and does not expect to work with the studio again. He linked it to Amazon's reported 50 billion dollar investment in OpenAI and called himself 'not an AI-friendly actor'. Amazon had not responded to a request for comment.

The article places this in the wider Hollywood dispute over AI, noting recent protections won by SAG-AFTRA and the Writers Guild. It adds that Spider-Noir was cancelled after one season despite strong viewing and Emmy nominations, and that Cage announced he is joining HBO's True Detective.

Highlights

  • Cage says he was asked to sign an AI waiver for Spider-Noir and declined.
  • He ties Amazon to its reported 50 billion dollar OpenAI investment.
  • Amazon had not commented when the article ran.
  • Unions recently secured AI protections in new contracts.
  • Spider-Noir was cancelled after one season; Cage joins True Detective.
