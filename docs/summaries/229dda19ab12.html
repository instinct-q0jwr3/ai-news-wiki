Jay Clayton, director of national intelligence and AI czar, will also be acting national cyber director pending Kirsten A. Davies' Senate confirmation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Axios reports that Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton will also serve temporarily as acting national cyber director while Kirsten Davies awaits Senate confirmation. The article cites a White House official, says Davies will assist as a senior adviser in the interim, and includes Clayton's statement about supporting a transition. It places the arrangement alongside his existing AI-policy role and the government's response to recent infrastructure disruptions.

The appointment and transition are reported by Axios, not checked against an official personnel notice here; confirmation remains a future condition rather than a completed event. The article reports administration statements that recent widespread outages were unrelated technical problems and upgrades, not evidence of coordinated attacks. That account does not settle the separate reported attacks on named agencies or establish one cause for every incident.

Highlights

  • Axios reports a temporary additional cyber-policy role for Clayton
  • Davies is described as awaiting Senate confirmation
  • The article cites a White House official and a Clayton statement
  • Davies is reported to advise during the interim
  • The transition is conditional, not a completed confirmation
  • Official outage explanations differ from proof of coordinated attacks
  • No official appointment record or independent forensics was inspected
