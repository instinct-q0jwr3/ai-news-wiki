Anthropic prices initial public offering

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic announces an IPO price of eighty-three dollars per share and a larger offering than the share count used in earlier coverage. The announcement distinguishes newly issued company shares from selling-stockholder shares, whose proceeds do not go to the company.

Trading and closing are described as expected events subject to conditions, not completed outcomes. An underwriter option could increase the share count. The announcement does not establish future market value or returns.

Highlights

  • Primary announcement sets an offering price
  • Company and selling-holder shares differ
  • Earlier share-count estimates are superseded
  • Trading and closing remain expected
  • Underwriter option is additional potential issuance
