AI less regulated than milk, Microsoft president says in warning over safety

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

The Guardian's version of the Brad Smith story: Microsoft's president warns AI is less regulated than milk and washing machines, urging tougher rules including legal obligations to victims of rogue AI agents and 'kill switches'. 'Right now, your washing machine is subject to more safety rules than AI,' he told the BBC. 'Every carton of milk that you see in the grocery store is subject to safety rules and inspection.'

Smith called AI both 'a tool and a weapon' that should be regulated urgently, joining growing calls from experts and politicians for binding safety rules on the technology.
