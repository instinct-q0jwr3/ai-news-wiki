type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Beat’s landing page describes drafting reviews and follow-ups across workplace tools, with a person approving before changes are posted. It advertises beta availability and later paid plans.

The page’s claim that prompt injection cannot cause actions relies on its stated draft-review boundary and was not independently tested. Human review can reduce some risks but is not proof that every malicious draft will be caught. No accounts were connected.
