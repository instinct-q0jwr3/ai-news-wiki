Moonshot unveils Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 3T-class open-weight model with a 1M-token context window, and K3.1 Pro Highspeed, with full weights to be released by Nov. 4

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Moonshot's announcement of Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model in the K3.1 series and its most capable 3T-class open-weight model, with a 1-million-token context window. It delivers substantial improvements across coding, 3D creation, financial research, and other agentic capabilities, and ships alongside K3.1 Pro Highspeed (2-3x faster). Both are available today on Kimi.ai, Kimi Work, Kimi Code, and the Kimi API, with full weights by November 4.

The technical story is post-training at scale: K3.1 Pro is a post-trained version of K3 with substantially larger post-training compute, and Moonshot says enabling large-scale joint RL across hundreds of downstream domains required optimizing every layer of the stack - the RL framework, long-context inference, training efficiency, sandbox infrastructure, and rollout scheduling. The K3.1 series is Moonshot's bid to stay at the open-weight frontier against the proprietary leaders.
