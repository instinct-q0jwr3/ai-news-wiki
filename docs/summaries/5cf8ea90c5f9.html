Vacate a drone restriction that criminalized recording immigration agents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

hacker-news agentic-systems

Summary

The EFF joined the ACLU, the National Press Photographers Association and others in an amicus brief urging the D.C. Circuit to vacate an FAA drone flight restriction in Levine v. FAA, arguing it violated the First Amendment right to record law enforcement. The restriction effectively criminalized drone recording of DHS officers - including ICE and CBP agents - and their 'mobile assets', even from more than half a mile away.

The FAA rescinded the restriction in April after a drone operator sued in March, but the brief argues the court should still rule: pilots remain punishable for violations while it was in effect, and the rescission looks like an attempt to dodge judicial review rather than 'a true change of heart'. The brief stresses drones capture perspectives otherwise impossible for oversight and newsgathering.
