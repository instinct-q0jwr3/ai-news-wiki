MiMo-V2.6-Pro ties Grok 4.7 (xHigh) and beats GLM-5.3 (max) on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index, making it the benchmark's top-scoring open-weight model

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro ties Grok 4.7 (xHigh) and beats GLM-5.3 (max) on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index, making it the benchmark's top-scoring open-weight model, VentureBeat reports. That puts Xiaomi's new flagship ahead of proprietary models including xAI's Grok 4.6 and Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash; a cheaper V2.6 Flash variant debuted alongside it.

Highlights

  • MiMo-V2.6-Pro is the top open-weight model on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index.
