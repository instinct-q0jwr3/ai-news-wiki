Samsung expects capex to rise ~60% to ~$63B this year, with HBM capacity sold out through 2027 and 10 long-term supply deals secured with key customers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Bloomberg reports Samsung expects capital expenditure to rise about 60 percent to roughly $63 billion this year, with HBM memory capacity sold out through 2027 and ten long-term supply deals secured with key customers. The spending hike follows a chip profit that missed estimates but was lifted by resurgent AI demand.
