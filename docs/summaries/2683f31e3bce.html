Launch HN: Coverage Cat (YC S22) – Umbrella insurance via your personal agent

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

hacker-news openai google microsoft muse agentic-systems ai-coding-agents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Coverage Cat, a licensed insurance brokerage (YC S22), launched with a pitch for AI-guided intake paired with a human brokerage team: compare home, umbrella, auto and renters coverage side by side with 'straight pricing, no sold leads'. The company says its agents surface the best policies for users even when Coverage Cat makes no commission on them.

The service is live in California, Florida, New York, Texas and Washington. Notably for the agentic crowd, it exposes the same shopping flow to developers through an Agent API, with agents directed to a /.well-known/mcp.json endpoint.
