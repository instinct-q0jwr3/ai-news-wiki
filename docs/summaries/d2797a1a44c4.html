YC's Garry Tan uses AI agents so much, he spends up to $200K a year on tokens

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

YC CEO Garry Tan told Walter Isaacson's podcast he spends roughly $100,000 to $200,000 a year on AI tokens. He has released 23 Claude Code skills called gstack. His agent has read his email, Dropbox and Apple Notes, including 20 years of therapy notes, and he likens it to a Jiminy Cricket that runs ahead and helps him see around corners.

His advice is not to treat agents like traditional software: people who over-index on repeatability and safety give up what makes agents valuable. YC also runs an internal agent, Stray Light, with a personality and a 'company brain' holding context on every founder it has backed and all applications. Tan thinks personal agents go mainstream only if cost falls, and admits his spending is 'a crazy privilege'.
