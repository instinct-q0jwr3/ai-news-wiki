Pointing AI at archives found a forgotten meteorite, lost rhinos, and more

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Inspired by historian Benjamin Breen's use of Opus 5.5 to find a 1615 eyewitness account of dodos in Dutch East India Company records, a software engineer pointed AI agents at millions of digitized historical records and surfaced a forgotten meteorite report (an 1850s letter describing a four-pound iron-heavy stone fallen near Cokurrgaum), three lost rhinos, and unrecorded volcano eruptions.

The method extends Breen's: ask a Deep Research assistant which open historical questions are most likely solvable with existing data, then run many searches in parallel, chaining several AI models to filter results and automate the tedious manual steps. The author's motivation is partly technical but partly civic - adding even one new data point to a niche field, he argues, is an honorable contribution anyone with a home AI lab can now make.

Highlights
