Mark Carney, Emmanuel Macron, and other Western leaders are pushing to establish a global supervisory regime and "technology stability" body to govern AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

A Wall Street Journal report that Mark Carney, Emmanuel Macron and other Western leaders are pushing to establish a global supervisory body for AI - coordinated through what the piece calls a leaders' group chat. The body is paywalled and was not captured, so this summary rests on the headline.

On that basis: after national frameworks and voluntary commitments, heads of government are attempting top-down international AI coordination - with the usual questions of who joins, who enforces, and whether the US and China sit at the table. Detail is unknown from the capture.

  • Would be the first attempt at standing international AI governance with executive backing
