OpenAI announces ChatGPT Wallet, which lets its dots AI agents make purchases on behalf of users, available to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US who are over 18

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet launch for eligible US adult Pro subscribers using dots agents. It describes per-purchase approval and transaction-scoped payment tokens supplied by card networks rather than exposing the user's ordinary card details to the agent.

Those limits distinguish a payment mechanism from permission to spend freely. The report describes partnerships and an initial rollout, not universal merchant compatibility or a guarantee that every purchase flow succeeds. Approval still concerns the actual item, destination and total.

Highlights

  • Launch is restricted by plan, age and country
  • Each purchase needs approval
  • Network tokens scope payment to a transaction
  • Ordinary card details are described as hidden from agents
  • Initial rollout does not imply universal coverage
