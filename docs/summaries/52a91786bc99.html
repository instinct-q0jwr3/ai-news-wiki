AuxoAI, which helps large businesses build and deploy AI systems, raised a $20.7M Series A led by NTTVC to accelerate growth and scale

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

AuxoAI, an enterprise engineering and consulting company that helps large businesses build and deploy AI systems, has raised a $20.7 million Series A led by NTTVC to scale its consulting model. Over three-plus years working with Global 2000 enterprises, the company has built a practice around embedding on-site engineers and strategy teams inside clients to understand their culture, data, workflows, and customer engagement before building tailored AI solutions.

Its platform centerpiece is Damia, described as a forward-deployed-engineer workbench that turns captured business knowledge and processes into reusable agent capabilities, applications, and licenses. The thesis is that the knowledge consultants accumulate while working with a customer should be preserved in software rather than walked out the door, so AI agents built around a company's processes keep improving. The raise funds growth and scaling of this FDE-plus-platform approach, a model in the mold of Palantir-style forward deployment now being productized for the agent era.
