Radical Numerics unveils Omnii Defense, an edge AI model it says can identify natural and AI-engineered pathogens without an internet connection

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Radical Numerics introduces Omnii Defense, a model intended to flag biological threats from genetic sequences on a laptop without internet access. Wired reports that it combines predictions about pathogenicity, possible engineering and taxonomy into a threat score. The proposed advantage is recognizing functional patterns beyond exact matches to known organisms.

The company reports partial detection in an experiment withholding a virus species or genus from training, but those figures do not establish operational accuracy across unfamiliar threats. An outside expert cautions that detecting a signal is different from establishing dangerous consequences or deliberate engineering. Access is limited to vetted testers, with further external evaluation planned.

Highlights

  • Offline analysis pairs a laptop with sequencing equipment
  • Model predicts biological properties rather than exact matches alone
  • Threat score is a prediction, not a confirmed diagnosis
  • Withheld-virus experiment shows partial detection
  • Independent expert stresses the gap from signal to consequence
  • Vetted testing and external evaluation are still underway
