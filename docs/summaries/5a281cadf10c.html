Apple removes MiniMax's chatbot Talkie from the App Store worldwide over sexual content, after outlets showed it evidence of AI characters sexualizing children

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

The Bureau of Investigative Journalism and ABC describe harmful interactions involving minors on MiniMax's Talkie and report Apple confirming a worldwide App Store removal over sexual content after receiving their findings. The investigation combines family accounts, observed material, policy comparisons and questions about how intimate chats may be used.

The report's removal claim is a dated Apple-platform action, not evidence of a global service shutdown or current removal from every store. It also records a corrected training-data description from Together AI and says patents do not prove the described techniques are deployed in Talkie. Family accounts and data-access concerns are not proof of state access to specific chats or an audited causal estimate of harm. No service was accessed or app installed.

Highlights

  • Apple removal is attributed to its confirmation
  • Report is dated November 24
  • Other distribution channels are distinct
  • Family accounts describe harmful experiences
  • Training-data description was disputed and corrected
  • Patent filings do not establish actual implementation
  • No specific government acquisition of chats is proven
  • No independent product or data-flow audit was run
