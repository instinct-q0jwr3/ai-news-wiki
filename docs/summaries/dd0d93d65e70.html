Apple Documents Extra Apple Intelligence Usage Limits Per iCloud+ Paid Tier

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-21 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple's support page describes daily limits for server-backed Apple Intelligence and Siri AI features in its 2027 software releases, with increased access through eligible iCloud+ plans starting at version 27.3. The baseline belongs to each device and feature category; the extra allowance is pooled across the account's devices and any participating family members rather than multiplied independently for everyone.

The table lists up-to multipliers of 2, 5, 10 and 20 for the 200 GB, 2 TB, 6 TB and 12 TB plans. These are relative limits, not disclosed request counts or a promise of unlimited use. Regional exclusions are material: the page says increased access is unavailable in the EU and several other markets. This is documentation of a future rollout, not evidence that these benefits are available on a particular account today.

Highlights

  • Documentation concerns 2027 releases and version 27.3
  • 50 GB retains the standard device allowance
  • Higher-plan multipliers are up-to values
  • Extra allowance is shared across devices and family
  • Limits reset daily without rollover
  • Basic Siri requests remain possible after advanced requests pause
  • EU exclusion prevents assuming increased access in Spain
  • No plan was purchased or account eligibility tested
