Muru

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-22 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison's link blog highlights Muru, a new AI-assisted server written in Rust by Graham Dumpleton, the author of mod_wsgi, which aims to run ASGI and WSGI Python applications in development and production. Willison notes that Dumpleton has been publishing an extraordinarily detailed account of the project as he works, as he did with wrapture, and quotes Dumpleton's own framing: the AI does much of the surface work, but deciding what is not good enough remains his call, informed by twenty years of experience. The linked posts span roughly three weeks of public development notes.

This is a short link post endorsing a development process, not a review, benchmark or security assessment of the server. The quoted passage is the author's own account of how he divides work with the model, not a measured evaluation of AI-assisted output quality, and Muru's readiness for production workloads was not tested by the author of the post or in this pass.
