Maybe it's time for lots of little indie AIs to take over

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

anil-dash openai anthropic ai-policy-regulation labor-displacement

Summary

Anil Dash, quoted in The Guardian, makes the case for 'lots of little indie AIs' rather than a ChatGPT killer: many small, responsible players instead of a few giants - mammals after the comet. He frames this against a charged backdrop: Trump administration AI policymakers suspended access to Anthropic's newest product, and Dash notes those policymakers hold webs of investments in competing players (including the about-to-IPO SpaceX), making honest risk judgment impossible.

He also points to the cultural pushback against AI - strongest among people born this century - while usage data shows even younger cohorts use some AI tools, a pattern he compares to social media's trajectory.
