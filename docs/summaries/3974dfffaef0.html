What Frontier Labs Buy: A Data Deep Dive with Handshake AI — Garrett Lord & Team

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Latent Space's first deep dive with an AI data company features Handshake AI, whose training-data business went from launch to $1 billion in gross annualized revenue in about a year. The episode asks what frontier labs are buying at that scale and shows live demos of the reinforcement learning environments Handshake builds.

In finance, an agent must find its task in a bank's email, identify governing files and deliver a workbook; it fails when it must apply a tax rule to a specific line and instead subtracts a goodwill figure. In bio, the VIALS benchmark tests reading scientific images such as tumor slides. The point is where models still trail humans.

Highlights

  • Handshake AI training business reached $1B annualized in about a year
  • Labs buy RL environments and expert evals
  • Finance demo: model explains a rule but misapplies it in code
  • VIALS benchmark tests interpreting scientific images
  • Shows evals where models are far from human level
