Scott Aaronson on recent complexity theory AI achievements

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Scott Aaronson reacts to a wave of claimed complexity-theory results, especially OpenAI's matrix-multiplication lower bound, and describes a change in his personal expectations about AI-assisted mathematics. He distinguishes his confidence in the labs' formal artifacts from his much lower confidence in specific cryptographic rumors.

This is expert commentary and a first-person reaction, not an independent proof audit of every cited manuscript. Aaronson says in a comment that he has not read far into the recent papers. His working presumption of correctness and prediction that P versus NP may be near remain his judgments; they do not establish a Boolean separation, a broken cryptosystem or universal verification.
