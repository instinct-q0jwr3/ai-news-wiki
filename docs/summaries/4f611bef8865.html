Pluralistic: The death of history's most successful publisher (20 Oct 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow's Pluralistic entry eulogizes 'history's most successful publisher', which, by his definition of publishing as the act of making something public, identifying a work and an audience and connecting them, is Google. He draws on a lifetime in and around publishing, from his first novel editor Patrick Nielsen Hayden onward, to argue that Google's scorching 'main character syndrome' has broken that connective function. The full argument sits below the linklog fold of the 20 October 2026 edition.
