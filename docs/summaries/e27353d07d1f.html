Q&A with Signal's Moxie Marlinspike on AI and cryptography, his privacy-focused AI chat service Confer, surveilling AI models rather than users, and more

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

In a Conversations with Tyler interview, Moxie Marlinspike argues that conversational interfaces imply a privacy that underlying AI services may not provide, and describes his private chat service Confer. He favors monitoring model behavior rather than building systems that monitor every person's activity, while acknowledging that safety and privacy are difficult to reconcile.

The transcript is dated November 25 and says it was recorded October 10, so it is not a live reaction to every later mathematical or security report. Marlinspike explicitly does not know whether AI will break cryptography and offers possible ranges of outcomes, not evidence of a deployed break. His claims about Confer's privacy and a hoped-for defensive equilibrium remain interview statements, not a cryptographic audit, service test or forecast established here.
