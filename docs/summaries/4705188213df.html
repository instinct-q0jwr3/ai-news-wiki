How much do tokens really cost?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Hadley Wickham tests the suspicion that AI tokens are unsustainably priced and frontier labs are subsidizing them with VC money. His sense is that it is mostly not true, and he builds the case with an open model, Kimi K3, which he has used heavily for months and still prefers to Opus 5.5 for cost effectiveness even though Opus feels a bit smarter.

Because Kimi K3 is open, many providers sell it, so market prices reveal cost. He lists OpenRouter prices with a blended column assuming a 20:1 input to output ratio and 95% of input from cache: roughly $0.59 per million tokens at the cheapest providers and about $1.13 at big clouds such as Bedrock, Together and Moonshot AI itself. The comparison to self-hosting comes later in the post, which was only partly read. The implied point is that competitive third-party pricing for a near-frontier model suggests token prices are not a bait-and-switch.

Highlights

  • Wickham doubts that token prices are mostly subsidized and uses Kimi K3 as a test
  • Open-model pricing across providers gives a market price signal
  • Blended price assumes 20:1 input:output and 95% cached input: ~$0.59/M cheapest, ~$1.13/M at major clouds
  • Self-hosting comparison not read
