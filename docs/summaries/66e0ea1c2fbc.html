OpenRouter removes Together AI, Phala, and Novita over data policy violations; Novita says a misconfiguration sent some MiniMax requests to MiniMax official API

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high

Summary

BleepingComputer reports OpenRouter's removal of Together AI, Phala and Novita after data-handling concerns, alongside precautionary withdrawal of ZDR eligibility from 125 endpoints. It describes Dragos' seeded prompts and canary credentials, then summarizes separate provider explanations involving a repository credential, a compromised server and a routing misconfiguration.

The report distinguishes Atlas Cloud's voluntary withdrawal from the three removals and says OpenRouter denied a breach of its own systems. Provider accounts and Dragos' observations are attributed, not independently audited here; not all precautionarily delisted endpoints are shown to have leaked. This coverage complements the earlier primary incident summary rather than reopening a superseded extraction gap. No private prompts, credentials or accounts were accessed or rotated.

Highlights

  • Three provider removals are distinct from Atlas Cloud withdrawal
  • 125 ZDR eligibility changes include precautionary action
  • Dragos describes seeded prompts and controlled canary systems
  • Providers offer different incident explanations
  • OpenRouter denies compromise of its own systems
  • No independent incident audit was performed
  • No private account or credential changes were made
