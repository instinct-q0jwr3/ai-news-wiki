Tell HN: HN is being scraped for emails to scam

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

A Hacker News contributor reports receiving a fake AWS payment email at an address they say was used only on HN. They interpret that as evidence that public contact details are being collected for scams; replies describe similar use of site-specific addresses.

This is a community anecdote, not a verified campaign investigation or proof of an HN security breach. Publicly posted email addresses can explain the reported exposure without access to private account records.

Highlights

  • Poster reports a site-specific email alias
  • Message allegedly impersonated an AWS bill
  • Public harvesting is the proposed explanation
  • No breach is demonstrated
  • Replies provide anecdotes rather than campaign attribution
