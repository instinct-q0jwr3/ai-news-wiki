Pluralistic: Amazon achieves enshittification inception (04 Sep 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

pluralistic google apple amazon agentic-systems small-specialist-models compute-buildout ai-policy-regulation labor-displacement ai-price-war

Summary

Cory Doctorow reads Amazon's balance sheet as proof of the Enshittocene: the company converted its two biggest cost centers into profit centers - logistics (more than fully subsidized by the fees of the independent sellers who compete with Amazon) and AWS (competitors' rent gives Amazon its own computing for free, at a profit).

But both are eclipsed by Amazon's most profitable line: payola - the 'advertising' toll sellers must pay to be seen at all. The 'inception' of the title: the fraud economy built on top of that payola is itself being defrauded - cheating on your fraud, a hell of a thing.

Highlights

  • Amazon turned its two biggest cost centers (logistics, AWS) into profit centers
  • Both are funded substantially by Amazon's own competitors' payments
  • Its most profitable business is neither: it's payola ('advertising')
  • 'Enshittification inception': the fraud on the platform is itself being defrauded
  • 'Cheating on your fraud is a hell of a thing'
