Read This Before You Buy That TV Streaming Stick

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Krebs reports on a Bitsight investigation showing that cheap H96 TV streaming sticks - long suspected of renting out their owners' internet connections - are also conscripted into a large ad-fraud operation. Researcher Pedro Falé registered an expired domain once used for the devices' telemetry and found tens of thousands of TV boxes reporting in, nearly all of them claiming to be mobile phones from Samsung, Vivo, Huawei and Xiaomi.

The trail leads to Zhejiang Fengwo IoT Technology, a mainland China company whose two apps ship on every device, with monetization collected through Hong Kong and Singapore shell identities. The sticks spoof mobile profiles to click ads on AI-generated news sites operated by the same group - sites that only display ads to visitors matching the spoofed profiles. Fraud routines are assembled by low-skilled operators dragging blocks in Google's Blockly visual editor, exported as JavaScript and pushed to selected devices, and a fusion of three vision-and-reasoning systems lets the bots spot and click ads like humans. The group's own site advertises more than 120,000 'AI digital humans' for rent.

Highlights

  • Bitsight's Pedro Falé hijacked an expired telemetry domain and saw tens of thousands of H96 TV sticks reporting as mobile phones
  • The same two apps sit on every device, built by Zhejiang Fengwo IoT Technology (Fengwo Group), with patents matching their behavior
  • Monetization flows through Hong Kong, Singapore and single-person shell companies back to mainland China
  • The sticks act as a captive traffic source, clicking ads on AI-generated websites run by the same group
  • The sham sites (finance, health, gaming, food blogs) show ads only to visitors matching the spoofed mobile profiles
  • Fraud tasks are built in Google's Blockly editor so low-skilled operators can assemble them without understanding the code
  • Selected devices are pushed modules to silently open browsers, visit pages, manage tabs and click ads
  • Three fused vision-and-reasoning systems let the bots identify and click ads the way a human would
  • Fengwo's site claims 120,000+ rentable 'AI digital humans' for companionship, customer service and design
