Anthropic cuts Sonnet 5.5 cache read price from $0.20 to $0.10 and adds monthly API credits: $100 for Max 5x, $200 for Max 20x, up to $500 shared by Team users

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

VentureBeat reports Anthropic is cutting Sonnet 5.5 cache read prices from $0.20 to $0.10 per million tokens and adding monthly API credits for subscribers: $100 for Max 5x, $200 for Max 20x, and up to $500 shared among Team users.

The credits are tied to the Haiku 5.5 launch, which starts at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output, matching OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna pricing according to the headline. This is a Techmeme excerpt; the full article was not read.
