What Happened in Fall 2026: AI and P vs. NP

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

In a Communications of the ACM blog essay, Emanuele Viola interprets reported AI-assisted advances in theoretical computer science as support for his longstanding belief that P equals NP. The piece recounts claimed results about 3SUM, integer multiplication and the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and argues that historical underestimation of efficient algorithms should make researchers reconsider their expectations. It explicitly says that the AI results it discusses did not prove P equals NP.

This is an author's interpretation and account of reported results, not independent verification of their correctness, acceptance or AI contribution. The referenced manuscripts and proofs were not checked here. In particular, falsifying a stronger conjecture such as ETH would not by itself resolve P versus NP, and a narrative about a transformed research community does not supply that missing implication. The publication venue and detailed references identify a source for further checking; they are not substitutes for inspecting the mathematical claims.

Highlights

  • The essay discusses claimed 3SUM, multiplication and ETH results

  • The account of AI's contribution remains attributed
