An analysis of 10M job ads in China between 2021 and 2025 finds a double-digit decline in listings for roles more exposed to AI than for less-exposed positions

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

A Bloomberg excerpt indexed by Techmeme describes an analysis of Chinese job advertisements and a relative decline in listings for occupations classified as more AI-exposed. The full analysis was inaccessible. Advertisement counts are not employment totals, and exposure classifications and relative changes alone do not identify AI as the cause.

Highlights

  • Evidence is an indexed analysis excerpt
  • Full methodology was inaccessible
  • Job listings differ from people employed
  • Exposure categories require validation
  • Association does not establish causation
  • No occupation-specific forecast is inferred
