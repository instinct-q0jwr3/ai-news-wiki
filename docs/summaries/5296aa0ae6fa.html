NVIDIA's Video Generation with Matching 3D Motion

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

NVIDIA Research presents Joints, Camera, Action! (JCA), which jointly generates video and synchronized 3D motion for rigs of any topology from a text prompt. An input image grounds the motion in its surroundings so it can be registered within the 3D scene, and a few chosen keyframes give control over the motion trajectory. JCA also converts in both directions: rig motion to video and video to rig motion.

The motivation is that video-generation models now depict remarkably realistic motion, but that motion is trapped in pixels - unusable for animating a specific 3D asset or replaying animation inside a scene. JCA extracts the underlying articulated motion alongside the video, turning generative video into a source of actual animation data for games, film, and simulation.
