Premium: How Much Money Does AI Need?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

ed-zitron

Summary

Ed Zitron's 7,000-word premium essay 'How Much Money Does AI Need?' opens with a meta-apology for its length - specificity is his method - and then takes aim at AI boosterism's relationship with numbers: hundreds of billions and trillions get declared, and the boosters assume nobody would say such things in bad faith and that the industry will certainly deliver.

His anchor point: we're barely a few years from Mark Zuckerberg burning $80 billion on the metaverse - 'what will one day be known as the second-worst misallocation of capital', with the implication of what the first-worst will be.
