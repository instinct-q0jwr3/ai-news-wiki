Bringing the magic: Google breaks open a bottleneck in quantum computing

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high

Summary

Google Quantum AI says it has shown 'magic state cultivation' working on its Willow processor. Magic states are a key resource for fault-tolerant quantum computing, and the post presents the result as opening a major bottleneck.

The report is Google's own, from researchers Emma Rosenfeld and Craig Gidney.
