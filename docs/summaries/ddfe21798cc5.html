Inkling 2 Small

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Thinking Machines introduces Inkling 2 Small, its second-generation model: a Mixture-of-Experts with 594B total and 17B active parameters, image input and variable effort. It scores 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, far above its predecessors, with gains in agentic coding, vision, knowledge work and reasoning, from a more efficient pre-training and one large unified RL run over hundreds of thousands of long-horizon coding tasks, including an agentic grader that judged the approach rather than only passing tests.

It launches with closed weights on Tinker, with Apache 2.0 weights promised next week after external safety testing and red-teaming. The post says it follows instructions better, considers changes more carefully and verifies its work unprompted. Benchmarks are the company's own.

Highlights

  • 594B total / 17B active MoE with image input
  • Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score of 31
  • Closed weights on Tinker now; Apache 2.0 weights promised next week after safety testing
  • Trained with RL on hundreds of thousands of long-horizon coding tasks
