A year as a Research Engineer working on RSI at Meta

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

A Meta research engineer describes his first year on a team working on recursive self-improvement (RSI), without a academic background; he started in technical support. His view is that data science skills carry over, titles matter little across research scientists and engineers, and about 90% of the work is code that makes research reproducible, with the rest ideas and tests.

He explains RSI as teaching AI agents to train the next generation of models: one route updates the foundation model, another improves the prompts, tools, memory or code around it. The post is personal career reflection with a short technical frame, not a result.

Highlights

  • Year-in-review of an RSI research engineer at Meta
  • RSI: agents that help train the next models
  • Two routes: update the model or the scaffold
  • About 90% of the time is engineering
  • Data science background is enough to enter the field
