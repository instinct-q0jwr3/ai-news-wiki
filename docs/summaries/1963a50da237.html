The GNOME LLM Policy That I Want

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

lobsters ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Responding to KDE's controversial proposal for an official AI/LLM policy, a GNOME community member argues such policies should shape social norms, not micro-manage developer workflows. His proposed GNOME policy is uncompromising: LLMs cannot be used to create or modify anything submitted to GNOME or hosted on its infrastructure, contributors may be asked to prove compliance, and circumvention can bring a ban. The rationale is humanist: GNOME is framed as a collective that finds joy in reaching beyond individual limitations, not merely software that ships every six months.

Highlights

  • A proposed GNOME LLM policy prompted by KDE's controversial AI-policy debate.
  • Total ban: no LLM-created or LLM-modified submissions to GNOME or its infrastructure.
  • Enforcement via proof requests and bans for circumvention.
  • Frames the policy as norm-setting and humanist, not workflow micro-management.
