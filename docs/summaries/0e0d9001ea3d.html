What Is Reasoning

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

armin-ronacher openai deepseek small-specialist-models ai-safety-incidents

Summary

A few weeks ago a paper was shared that showed how to extract reasoning traces from closed-weight models.

Twitter seems full of half-truths and confusion about how this works, so perhaps this helps some to understand what is happening.

Hiding Traces Reasoning traces are usually hidden from us.

Highlights

  • Together with online discussions about tricking models into leaking them, it made me investigate it more out of curiosity.
  • This is probably a good reason to separate them from what is normally shown to users.
  • The industry has done a good job at making reasoning traces sound special and exotic, but they really are just text: the model is trained to emit its thinking into a scratchpad as part of its response, before its final answer.
  • The markers are special tokens, but the reasoning between them uses “the same text” as the final answer (just that GPT chain-of-thought text sounds really funny).
  • Reasoning Effort How much budget goes to reasoning?
  • Earlier APIs exposed reasoning token budgets, making it seem like a property of the sampling process.
  • In reality, reasoning effort is baked into the system prompt.
  • I think closed GPT models call reasoning effort “juice,” since you can ask most models how much juice they have.
