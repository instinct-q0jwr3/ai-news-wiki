Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu says the company plans to train a 5T- to 10T-parameter AI model, as it lays out a sweeping push across AI models, chips, and data centers

Summary

Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu says the company plans to train an AI model with 5 trillion to 10 trillion parameters, Reuters reports, part of a sweeping push across AI models, chips and data centers. The model would be up to four times larger than the company's current flagship.
