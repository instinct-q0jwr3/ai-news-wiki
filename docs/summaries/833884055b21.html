Pilgrim, whose device combines air sampling and genomic sequencing to detect biological threats, raised a $25M seed led by Buckley at a $150M valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high

Summary

Pilgrim, a startup building hardware that combines air sampling with genomic sequencing to detect biological threats, raised a $25M seed round led by Buckley Ventures at a $150M valuation. The striking detail is the cap table: Logan Graham, head of Anthropic's frontier red team, and Sholto Douglas of its technical staff invested personally, alongside Coinbase co-founder Fred Ehrsam; earlier backers include Figma CEO Dylan Field and Peter Thiel.

The Wall Street Journal frames the round against growing fear that AI systems could enable novel biological threats - the people paid to red-team frontier models are personally funding early-warning infrastructure against the same class of risk. Pilgrim is based in Redwood City, California; CEO Jake Adler.

Highlights
