On Amazon's earnings call, Andy Jassy says outside AI shopping agents are "still not great, nor more convenient than coming to a retailer directly"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

On Amazon's earnings call, Andy Jassy told investors not to worry about outside shopping agents invading its store: few customers are buying through them because the experience is, in his words, still not great and no more convenient than coming to a retailer directly. The comments land a month after Amazon blocked Meta's Muse assistant from its store on terms-of-use grounds - and while Meta has signed up Shopify, Walmart and Best Buy as Muse-friendly partners, Amazon is pointedly not among them. The stakes are advertising: agents that bypass the storefront bypass the ads that drive Amazon's profit, though Jassy said outside agents were not behind the quarter's weaker-than-expected ad performance.

Jassy's counter is Amazon's own agent: more than 400 million customers used Alexa for shopping in the last year, interactions grew 570 percent year over year, and US customers using the shopping agent spend 50 percent more per order. Meanwhile Meta says Muse has passed 10 million users and briefly unseated ChatGPT as the top free app on the App Store.

Highlights

  • Amazon blocked Meta's Muse from its store in September; Shopify, Walmart and Best Buy said yes
  • Agents bypass storefront ads - Amazon's profit engine
  • Jassy: outside agents not to blame for the quarter's weak ad numbers
  • Alexa for shopping: 400M users in a year, interactions +570% YoY, agent users spend 50% more per order
  • Meta claims 10M+ Muse users; Muse briefly topped the free App Store chart
