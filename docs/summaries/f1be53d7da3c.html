Coxon Day

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

The New Critic's Rufus Knuppel writes 'Coxon Day', an essay from inside Berkeley's Constellation on the day AI safety went mainstream. The piece opens with a blunt five-step account of the frontier recipe, train on all text, expand context, go multimodal, RL for math and coding, then recursively self-improve, and a manifesto: preserve human agency and the intrinsic value of being human as most tasks become AI-performable, prevent extreme concentration of power when a few people with a large computer can do what took thousands of experts, and keep humans in control. Its author asserts no lab has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to keep scaling at maximum speed, expects voluntary slowdowns until shared safety bars exist, and wants international coordination on AI development to become a top government priority.
