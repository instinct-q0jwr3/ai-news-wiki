USPTO and Copyright Office were surprised by the DOJ's pro-OpenAI brief

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme openai microsoft amazon ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Axios reports that the USPTO and the US Copyright Office were caught off guard by a Department of Justice statement of interest backing OpenAI and Microsoft in the New York Times' copyright infringement lawsuit against them. The DOJ's brief supports the two companies' position in the dispute, apparently without prior coordination with the agencies that oversee patents and copyright policy.

Highlights

  • The DOJ filed a statement of interest supporting OpenAI and Microsoft in the NYT copyright lawsuit.
  • The USPTO and US Copyright Office were surprised by the brief, per Axios' sources.
  • The case centers on the New York Times' copyright infringement claims over AI training.
