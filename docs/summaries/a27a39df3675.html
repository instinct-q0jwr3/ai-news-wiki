Show HN: Doom or Bloom, map your AI worldview with Jev

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news typesafe

Summary

Doom or Bloom is a Show HN interactive that maps AI worldviews on a spectrum from civilizational doom to bloom, built with Jev. It places named public figures along the axis - from Eliezer Yudkowsky, Nate Soares, Daniel Kokotajlo and Ed Zitron on the doom side to Marc Andreessen, Jensen Huang and Donald Trump on the bloom side, with a wide middle of researchers, CEOs and commentators in between.

The roster spans the field's loudest voices: Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio, Dario Amodei, Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, Ilya Sutskever, Yann LeCun, Fei-Fei Li, Gary Marcus, Scott Alexander, Dwarkesh Patel and dozens more.

Highlights

  • Interactive map of AI worldviews from doom to bloom.
  • Built with Jev; a Show HN project.
  • Doom side: Yudkowsky, Soares, Kokotajlo, Zitron.
  • Bloom side: Andreessen, Huang, Trump.
  • Maps dozens of researchers, CEOs and commentators in between.
