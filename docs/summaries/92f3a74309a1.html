Google updates Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, an animated AI persona that lip-syncs and shows different facial expressions, for Gemini Enterprise customers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme google gemini

Summary

Google updated Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, an animated AI persona that lip-syncs and shows different facial expressions during real-time conversations. It can also pull up information on-screen while it talks.

Google says Live Avatar transitions between the 97 languages it supports 'without degrading video fidelity or introducing visual drift'; a demo video shows it switching between English and Japanese with mouth animation matching the speech. For now, Live Avatar is available only to Gemini Enterprise customers.

Highlights

  • Gemini 3.8 Live adds Live Avatar: an animated persona that lip-syncs and emotes.
  • It can display information on-screen while talking.
  • Supports transitions across 97 languages without visual drift.
  • Demo shows English-Japanese switching with matched mouth animation.
  • Enterprise-only at launch.
