Kākāpō Party

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-27 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison wanted a celebratory closing slide for his keynote at the WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America, tied to the record-breaking kakapo breeding season of 2026. Having heard that Claude Opus 5.5 was good at pixel-art animation, he gave it three kakapo photos and asked for an HTML5 canvas animation of at least twenty pixel-art kakapos jumping with confetti - and got a result he calls pretty great.

He then pushed the experiment further: he asked the model to make a 15-second video of the resulting page, which required loading the file in a browser and clicking several times to trigger the confetti effect. The post is a small but telling STAR-moment demo of agentic multimodality: a model producing interactive art, then operating a browser to record its own creation.
