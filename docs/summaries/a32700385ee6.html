Anthropic Buys $3.4 Billion in Gas Generators

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic is spending $3.4 billion on Caterpillar natural gas generators originally ordered by supplier OnePWR, per the Financial Times, for up to 1.7 gigawatts of power. It is unusual for a company that has preferred renting compute, such as its $1.25 billion a month deal with xAI through May 2029, and it shows how acute the power shortage is.

Highlights

  • $3.4B for Caterpillar generators, up to 1.7 GW
  • Originally ordered by OnePWR
  • Departs from its rent-not-buy approach
  • xAI deal: $1.25B a month through May 2029
