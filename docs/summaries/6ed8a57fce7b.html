Apple: ‘Strengthening App Review Guidelines Against Nonconsensual Intimate Imagery and Bait-And-Switch Apps’

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple updated its App Review Guidelines to tighten rules on nonconsensual intimate imagery and on bait-and-switch apps. Pornographic material was already banned, and the update adds explicit action against apps that generate such content and against apps that hide undocumented features.

Highlights

  • App Review rules target nonconsensual intimate imagery.
  • Also bans hidden or undocumented app features.
