How surging demand from AI hyperscalers has quintupled flash memory prices and shifted market and negotiating power from Apple to suppliers like Kioxia

Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports that surging demand from AI hyperscalers has quintupled flash memory prices and shifted negotiating power from Apple to suppliers such as Kioxia. The iPhone maker spent years bullying flash-storage makers into steep discounts until hyperscalers bid up prices and absorbed the supply.
