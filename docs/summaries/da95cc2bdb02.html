Releasing Personal Agent Protocol 1.0

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high

Summary

Sierra announces version 1.0 of Personal Agent Protocol, also called Poppy, as a way for personal agents to discover company interfaces and authenticate with OAuth. The announcement describes a commerce extension connecting discovery and permissions with UCP shopping functions.

Claims about partner participation and automatic Shopify publication come from Sierra’s announcement, not an independent deployment census. A protocol’s permission mechanism does not itself grant any agent access to a customer account or approval to transact.

Highlights

  • Protocol concerns discovery and authorized access
  • Existing standards underpin interfaces
  • Commerce extension connects with UCP
  • Adoption claims are vendor-reported
  • Implementing a protocol is distinct from customer consent
