Dreamteam, which uses AI agents for CRM tasks like data capture and research, raised $53M across seed and Series A led by Together Fund and Accel, respectively

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-17 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports Dreamteam raised $53 million across seed and Series A rounds for an AI-centered CRM. The company combines customer-interaction data with agents that research prospects, prepare meetings, draft follow-ups and monitor deal risks, with sales teams described as controlling outgoing messages.

Customer adoption and replacement of incumbent systems are attributed to the founders and cited customers. The article distinguishes the product’s ambitions from the difficulty of displacing established systems of record; forecasts and revenue targets are not achieved results.

Highlights

  • Funding spans two rounds
  • Shared interaction history supports agent work
  • Salespeople retain reported send control
  • Adoption metrics are attributed
  • Replacing incumbent CRMs remains a business challenge
