AI inference-chip startup Etched is in early talks to raise funding at a $40B-$50B valuation, up from $21B in August

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports AI chip startup Etched, which raised $700 million at a $21 billion valuation only months ago, is getting offers at double that or more. Bids range from $40 billion from top investors to $50 billion from lesser-known backers, per a source, and talks are early. Etched builds full systems on its own chips and says it has $1 billion in orders, including from lead investor Jane Street. A raise like the last could give it about 3.5 years of runway.

Highlights

  • Offers of $40B to $50B vs. a $21B valuation a few months ago.
  • Talks are early; terms may change.
  • Jane Street led the last round and is a customer.
  • $1B in orders claimed; seen as a possible Nvidia challenger.
