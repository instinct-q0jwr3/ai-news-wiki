Last night an AI agent performed brain surgery on a mouse

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-03 · confidence: high

Summary

A tweet thread from Gabriel Ocana-Santero, and the claim is extraordinary: an AI agent performed brain surgery on a mouse. The setup: 19 gene therapy vectors in a spreadsheet, a mouse model of epilepsy, access to a motorized stereotaxic rig with cameras, and one goal - reduce seizures. The agent picked the drug, picked the brain region, derived the injection coordinates from three papers, then drove the stereotaxic arms itself and injected 250 nanolitres while watching the entry site through the rig camera for bleeding.

Two details carry the weight. A licensed veterinarian gated every irreversible step and held a physical kill switch - the human-in-the-loop design was deliberate, not decorative. And the thread promises 'the moment it refused to let me override it,' which will do the discourse no end of work either way. The author wrote it on the spot after 18 hours coding and coordinating, promising more cohesive thoughts soon; treat it as a striking claim with a viral denominator (1.6M views) rather than a peer-reviewed result, but the direction of travel - agents running physical lab protocols end to end - is the same one the frontier-lab reporting describes.
