Pluralistic: Digital sewer socialism (08 Aug 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow profiles 'public interest technology' - the growing movement of hackers, from CS students to veterans, who want to turn their expertise into public goods. The movement swelled as Big Tech saturated its markets and switched from making products people like to locking users in and extracting from them - the era when, in Jeff Hammerbacher's words, the best minds of a generation went to work making people click ads.

His frame, 'digital sewer socialism', is deliberately unglamorous: the historical model is the sanitary engineers who built sewers - technical experts whose unglamorous infrastructure work saved more lives than miracle cures. Public-interest technologists' equivalent today is keeping essential digital infrastructure honest, public and working, and Doctorow maps where that work actually happens (and how to join it).
