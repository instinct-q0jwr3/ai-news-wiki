Automatically detecting AI text in my browser

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

lobsters

Summary

A builder's survey of AI-assisted side projects answers the skeptic's question - if LLMs are so good at code, where is the tsunami of AI-generated apps? - by arguing code was never the only bottleneck to shipping, and by listing a year of personal projects built with AI. One observation: ReAct agents can see massive sudden improvements just by swapping the underlying model, a reason to invest in agent scaffolding early and ride better models as they arrive.

One of the projects is automated AI-text detection in the browser. The author calls the niche underserved - Pangram does an excellent job but needs competition - and predicts that within a few years every major social network will scan new posts and comments for AI content to tag or remove it.
