GitHub Octoverse 2026

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

GitHub's Octoverse 2026, announced by EVP Jay Parikh at Universe, quantifies the agentic engineering wave: merged pull requests tripled in under a year to 220 million per month, commits more than tripled to 7.38 billion per month (mostly in the second half), and the new-repository rate doubled to 30.3 million per month.

New cuts this year include industry breakdowns via a fine-tuned multilingual embedding model sorting repos into NAICS sectors, country rankings adjusted for VPN bias using LLM-extracted profile locations, and a breakout count of repositories mentioning interoperability or reverse engineering - the decompilation wave showing up in GitHub's own telemetry.

Highlights

  • Merged PRs tripled to 220M/month; commits to 7.38B/month
  • New repo creation doubled to 30.3M/month
  • Most growth landed in the second half of 2026
  • Report now tracks interoperability and reverse-engineering repos
