Research Firm Calls Anthropic's IPO 'Unprecedented Test of Investor Gullibity'

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high

Summary

New Constructs calls Anthropic's upcoming IPO the 'most ridiculous IPO of 2026' and an 'unprecedented test of investor gullibility'. Against a potential $2 trillion Nasdaq market cap, the independent research firm values Anthropic at $150 billion, arguing that to justify the target it would need to book double Nvidia's trailing-year profit - and Nvidia made over $190 billion in the past four quarters while Anthropic's 2025 revenue was $4.6 billion against a $42 billion net loss, per a leaked prospectus cited by Reuters.

The firm's structural point: since open-source models arrived, closed models have struggled to generate profits, and Anthropic's mounting operating losses plus open competition lead New Constructs to conclude 'we don't think Anthropic has a viable business'. Founder David Trainer has a bearish track record that includes calling WeWork the most ridiculous IPO of 2019, six weeks before its collapse.
