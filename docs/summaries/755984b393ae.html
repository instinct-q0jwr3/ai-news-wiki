Hello Deep Learning: Intro

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

bert-hubert

Summary

The intro to Bert Hubert's 'Hello Deep Learning' series is a confession and a motivation: he spent years dismissing deep learning as overhyped nonsense with fake demos, and was only half right - the demos were often fake, but staggering things were happening underneath, which he ignored.

His turning point: Andrej Karpathy's 2015 'The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks', whose self-contained examples prove something remarkable was discovered. The intro sets up the series (skippable - chapter 1 is where the machine learning begins) and frames his conviction that deep learning and generative AI will have huge impact, good or bad.

Highlights

  • Hubert admits he wrongly derided deep learning as overhyped for years
  • Karpathy's 2015 RNN essay is what changed his mind - self-contained proof of something remarkable
  • The series starts from his catch-up journey; chapter 1 is where the ML begins
  • His premise: deep learning and generative AI will have huge impact, for good or ill
