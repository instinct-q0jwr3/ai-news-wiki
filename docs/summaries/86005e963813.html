OpenAI publishes a preprint that claims to prove that the matrix multiplication exponent (ω) is exactly 9/4, addressing a major open problem in mathematics (alphaXiv)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

alphaXiv presents OpenAI's matrix-multiplication manuscript with an abstract claiming exponent nine fourths over every field and an explicitly labeled AI-generated overview. That overview explains a contradiction argument involving tensor-network compression and list-distinctness lower bounds, while keeping circuit cost separate from hardware runtime.

This is another presentation of the same preprint, not independent mathematical confirmation. An AI overview is a guide to the manuscript rather than expert certification; neither it nor its citation markers prove the argument correct. Exponent equality is not automatically a fixed-constant Theta(n^2.25) implementation, and no proof checker, algorithm or benchmark was run here.
