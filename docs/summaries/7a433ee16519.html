Amazon agrees to acquire much of startup Decart, including the Decart Optimization Stack and its founding team and AI researchers, a source says for ~$7.5B

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Bloomberg reports an agreement for Amazon to acquire much of Decart, including its optimization-stack technology and founding research and engineering team. The accessible opening attributes the technology/team scope to a company post; the feed attributes an approximately $7.5 billion price to a source.

This is a reported agreement rather than verified closing or necessarily an acquisition of every company asset. The full price terms, liabilities and retained business were not inspected. It updates the earlier Information headline about a deal nearing agreement, not a second transaction. No independent company filing or regulatory approval was checked.
