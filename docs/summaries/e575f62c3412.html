OpenAI suspected the three fired researchers of leaking internal model designs to AI safety researcher Ryan Greenblatt, including via disappearing Signal chats

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI suspected three fired researchers of leaking internal model designs to AI safety researcher Ryan Greenblatt, including via disappearing Signal chats, the Wall Street Journal reports. The company fired the three safety researchers for mishandling sensitive information; they had been fighting to keep the information flowing to outside safety scrutiny.
