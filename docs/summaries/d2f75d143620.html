OpenAI testing new "Aurora" GPT-Image model on Arena

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

TestingCatalog reports that three unknown OpenAI model references - aurora-red-alpha, aurora-green-alpha and aurora-blue-alpha - briefly appeared on OpenAI's APIs on October 18 before being removed in what looks like a mistaken rollout. Around the same time, three unnamed models showed up on Design Arena, the image-model comparison arena, suggesting all three are image-generation models now in public arena testing. The pattern points to OpenAI readying a new GPT-Image release, possibly a three-variant family, under the codename Aurora.

Highlights

  • Three 'aurora-*-alpha' model references appeared on OpenAI's APIs on Oct 18, then were removed
  • Three unknown models simultaneously appeared on Design Arena
  • All three are believed to be image-generation models in arena testing
  • Suggests a GPT-Image successor (possibly three variants) codenamed Aurora
  • Sourced from TestingCatalog and community spotters; unconfirmed by OpenAI
