A $12B DeepSeek Raise Is Reportedly Close, With Tencent And CATL Among Backers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Yellow reports, citing unnamed people familiar with the matter, that DeepSeek is near an outside financing round of at least 80 billion yuan, approximately $12 billion. That would exceed its original 50 billion yuan target, with Tencent and CATL described as major backers and the final amount potentially higher.

The report links the raise to preparations for a possible Shanghai STAR Market listing and says the round could close in October. DeepSeek has not announced it, and the cited companies did not immediately comment. The amounts, completion and listing timetable therefore remain reported plans rather than a closed financing or confirmed IPO.

Highlights

  • The reported raise exceeds the original target.
  • Tencent and CATL are named as major backers.
  • The story relies on unnamed sources.
  • DeepSeek has not announced completion.
  • A possible listing is not a confirmed IPO timetable.
