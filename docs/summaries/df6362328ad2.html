Your Free Tier is Someone Else's Twenty-Minute Side Project Scott Werner Mar 07, 2026 23 3 3 Share So try explaining freemium to someone who’s never heard of it.

The free tier existed in the gap between your need and your ability.

You could use a constrained version but you couldn’t really make your own version, so the constraint was real, and the funnel worked.
