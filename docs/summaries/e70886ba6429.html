Ask HN: Dark mode for HN please? (2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

A 2026 Ask HN revives the perennial request: a native dark mode for Hacker News. The poster points to the 2020 thread as proof of how much bikeshedding the question generates - a hundred solutions, Parkinson's Law of Triviality in action, and a detour into alligator metabolism - while arguing the usual defenses (workarounds, third-party apps) are janky: one iOS app flashes the page white between loads. The underlying complaint is that a site that should look like top still ships only a blinding default two decades into dark-mode ubiquity.
