Summary

A report from The Information, per its sources: DensityAI, an AI chip startup founded by former members of Tesla's Dojo team, is in funding talks at a valuation near $10 billion. The body is paywalled and was not captured, so this summary rests on the headline.

On that basis: another data point in the rush to fund would-be Nvidia challengers, with the Dojo pedigree as the draw. Round size, investors, and the chip's architecture are unknown from the capture.
