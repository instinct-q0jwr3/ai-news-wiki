Ex-OpenAI researcher finds Blackwell signatures in open-weight models

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Will Depue’s post describes checkpoint forensics that matches numerical traces in published model weights to GPU initialization patterns. He reports Blackwell matches for six Chinese models and physical-hardware checks for two, presenting the result as evidence about the hardware used to initialize those weights.

The post also estimates training-cluster size and cost, which are further inferences rather than direct observations. A hardware signature does not establish chip location, procurement route or export-law violations, and the post’s claim of outside verification is attributed to its author rather than independently confirmed here.

Highlights

  • Weight initialization supplies the proposed fingerprint
  • Six model matches are reported
  • Two physical-chip checks are described
  • Cluster costs are estimates
  • Location and legality are not established
