Dessa - the Conversation Model trained only on speech

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Dessa is a speech model trained from scratch on nothing but human conversation - no text, no pretrained components - and its makers claim it is the first AI you can hold a realistic live conversation with that learned entirely from sound. The pitch is a critique of the standard voice pipeline: converting speech to text throws away emotion, nuance, pauses and cadence, leaving the world's most powerful AIs unable to hear how you feel, which the team calls ridiculous, experience-degrading and potentially dangerous.

The result is a full-duplex model that the team says talks like a person. Beyond the product, Dessa is framed as a new research category: how efficiently do speech-only models learn language and facts compared to text, how efficiently can they pick up new languages, and what behaviors emerge that text training never produces.
