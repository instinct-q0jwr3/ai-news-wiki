OpenAI says it will make its AI models available on Google Cloud's model garden and Antigravity coding tool in the coming weeks, after launching on AWS in April

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports OpenAI will bring its models to Google Cloud's model garden and the Antigravity coding tool in the coming weeks, completing its presence on all three top clouds after the AWS launch in April, which followed Microsoft losing its exclusive license on OpenAI's IP less than six months ago. The move is about enterprise distribution as OpenAI builds beyond its consumer roots and courts investors at a $1.4 trillion valuation ahead of a targeted IPO. Google's cloud, meanwhile, is on a run: annualized growth accelerated to about 82% in Q2 from 63% in Q1, versus 37% for Amazon and 42% for Microsoft, with all three reporting next week. Ramp data shows most business AI spending still goes to OpenAI and Anthropic.
