Theatre Review: FrankenTacit ★★☆☆☆

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Terence Eden’s two-star review of FrankenTacit describes an audiovisual performance in which a human and two AI participants build sound against generated imagery. The reviewer finds the questions about creators and digital reflections more compelling than the visible stage interaction, which he describes as static and difficult for newcomers to interpret.

A multiplayer-Tetris sequencer is singled out as a strong moment, while the limited visual palette and repetitive sounds disappoint him. These are one critic’s artistic judgments, with a supplied ticket disclosed, rather than an evaluation of the AI systems’ technical ability.
