Highlights

  • Quail: a query-aware inference layer for AI-SQL workloads
  • Over 1 billion tokens/minute per H100 on a heavy multi-join query, >10x a vLLM baseline
  • Under 6 cents per billion tokens on Modal
  • The workload: SQL queries with LLM predicates (AI.IF) generating millions of long, simple sequences
  • Small open-weights models suffice; agentic-optimized engines waste that fact
  • 1.84x geomean speedup over vLLM on their released AI-SQL benchmark
  • Karpathy: this regime (no thinking, single-token, low-latency) had large latent demand and was under-invested
