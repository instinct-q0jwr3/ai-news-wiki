Apple is now offering additional AI access via one-time $50 "intelligence usage packs" when monthly usage caps are met, starting in its Creator Studio apps

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple launched its first paid Apple Intelligence add-on: a one-time $49.99 'intelligence usage pack' in Logic Pro, available when you reach your monthly limit. Unused usage rolls over and expires after 12 months, does not support Family Sharing and works only for the buying iCloud account. 9to5Mac says Apple has long said expanded usage would eventually cost money, and more apps will follow.

Highlights

  • First paid Apple Intelligence usage pack, $49.99 one-time
  • Used only after the monthly limit
  • Rolls over, expires after 12 months, no Family Sharing
  • Tied to Apple Creator Studio apps, more coming
