Apple Announces Record $114.5B Fourth-Quarter Results

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Jason Snell's traditional chart barrage for Apple's record fourth quarter: revenue of $114.5 billion, up 12% year on year, with iPhone up 17.5%, Mac up 23.7% and Services up 9.8%. The soft spots are Wearables, down 7.9%, and iPad, down 10.4%.

The AI angle is what Apple isn't: in a week when every other tech balance sheet is a story about compute commitments and capex, Apple's record quarter is a hardware-and-services story. Snell's piece pairs with his earnings-call transcript and analysis over at Six Colors; the charts are the quick visual read on where the growth actually came from.

Highlights

  • Apple posts a record $114.5B Q4 (+12% YoY): iPhone +17.5%, Mac +23.7%, Services +9.8%
  • Wearables (-7.9%) and iPad (-10.4%) are the declining lines
  • A record Big Tech quarter with no compute-commitment story attached - the counterpoint to the week's AI-capex news
