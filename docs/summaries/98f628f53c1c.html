MiniMax-M3.1-Flash Artificial Analysis Performance and Price Analysis

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Artificial Analysis scores MiniMax-M3.1-Flash at 42 on its Intelligence Index - well above the 13 median for comparable models - with pricing that leads its class: $0.15 per million input and $0.60 per million output tokens, about $0.22 per evaluation task. The model supports text and image input with a 1M-token context window and runs faster than average at around 120 tokens per second. The familiar caveat for this class: it is very verbose, burning 180M tokens across the evaluation suite versus a 99M median, which eats part of the price advantage in real workloads.
