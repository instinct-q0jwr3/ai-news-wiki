The Tower Keeps Rising

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

Summary

I feel that some vibecoded software changes somewhat randomly and unexpectedly.

That made me think about Bruegel’s “The Tower of Babel” which shows an already quite chaotic depiction of the Tower of Babel.

The story is one of pride and ambition and ultimately why people no longer speak the same language.

Highlights

  • The text begins with a technology upgrade: And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly.
  • And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter.
  • They use it for a civilizational project: let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven But when God assesses the situation the bricks are not what concern him: the people is one, and they have all one language,…
  • 1 They get their power through coordination which they have because they share a language.
  • With AI-assisted programming we should get better tools which lets us build more ambitious software.

  • The tower does not fall, it just keeps rising.
