OpenAI cuts GPT-6.2 usage: Astra hits limits 40% faster, Sol 25% faster

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

A post from the account thsottiaux says an erroneous launch discount on GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage has been corrected, so the same work consumes more of a plan allowance. It describes users reaching limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than the previous week, no retroactive charges and a promised banked reset for Go, Plus, Pro and Business.

These are attributed statements from a retrieved social post, not a live check of any subscriber account, reset balance or contractual plan terms. The percentages describe reaching usage limits sooner rather than a directly inspected change in token price or model speed. The account says other models were unaffected and usage should still compare favorably with earlier generations; no such comparative workload was reproduced here.
