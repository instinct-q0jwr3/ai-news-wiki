Premium: How Has AI Changed The Economy?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Ed Zitron examines how AI has changed the economy. Goldman Sachs estimates US AI investment will reach 1.9% of GDP in 2026, a share last exceeded by the 19th century railroad build-out. He argues the data is mostly data center construction and GPU sales, not revenue from companies renting AI.

He criticises a WSJ and Brookings projection for 2025 to 2032 because six of eight years are estimates, and warns spending may end up lower. It is an opinionated newsletter post.

Highlights

  • AI investment about 1.9% of US GDP in 2026 per Goldman Sachs.
  • Last higher share: the railroad boom.
  • Mostly construction and GPU sales, not customer revenue.
  • Disputes projections that rest on future estimates.
