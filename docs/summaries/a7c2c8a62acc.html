The Browser Company says it will shut down its Arc browser on December 22, launched in 2023, shifting attention to its AI-focused Dia browser, launched in 2025

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

The Browser Company will sunset Arc on December 22, ending the innovative browser it launched in 2023. Feature development stopped last year when the company moved to its AI-focused Dia browser, but Arc had been maintained; now the shutdown runs in stages: new Arc accounts are closed starting today, Browse for Me and other AI features die on November 23 along with sign-in and tab sync on mobile, and on December 22 the browser stops working on macOS and Windows, accounts deactivate, and Arc Search leaves Google Play.

The company published an FAQ for importing data into Dia, the replacement it launched in 2025. Arc's arc - darling of browser design, stalled roadmap, AI pivot, shutdown - is now complete, and its user base gets two months to migrate.

Highlights

  • The Browser Company will shut down Arc on December 22
  • New accounts closed immediately; mobile AI features and sync end November 23
  • December 22: macOS/Windows apps stop working, accounts deactivate, Arc Search pulled from Google Play
  • Users directed to Dia, the AI-focused replacement launched in 2025, with an import FAQ
  • Feature development on Arc had already ended last year
