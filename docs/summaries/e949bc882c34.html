Speaking in Singapore, Broadcom CEO Hock Tan and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan warn that access to capital and rising borrowing costs could become AI's main bottleneck

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high

Summary

Speaking at the DBS Asia Future Forward Conference in Singapore, Broadcom CEO Hock Tan and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan warned that capital - not chips, memory or electricity - could become the biggest constraint on the AI boom. '[What] I think might be the biggest constraint is capital,' said Hock Tan, as financing grows harder for the infrastructure buildout.

Lip-Bu Tan pointed to the rising cost of capital and the longer-run question of whether companies investing heavily in AI will generate sufficient returns: 'people want to see the ROI'. Their remarks mark a shift in the bottleneck narrative from supply shortages to balance sheets.

Highlights

  • Broadcom and Intel CEOs: capital may constrain AI more than chips or power.
  • Hock Tan: financing could be the biggest obstacle to AI expansion.
  • Lip-Bu Tan: rising cost of capital, and investors will want ROI.
  • Remarks at DBS Asia Future Forward Conference in Singapore.
