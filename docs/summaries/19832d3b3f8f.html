Clef: Open-source decision models, and new RL fine-tuning platform

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Clef announces open-source decision models and an RL fine-tuning platform, riding the buzz around TypeSafe AI's Jev System One. The post explains decision models as a different idea from classifiers: they produce bounded, structured outputs cheaply, quickly and consistently, can sit inside a workflow wherever a decision is needed, and handle new categories without retraining. It contrasts them with LLMs, which are largely non-deterministic. Based on the opening of the post.

Highlights

  • Clef releases open-source decision models
  • Adds an RL fine-tuning platform
  • Decision models give bounded, structured, consistent outputs
  • New categories need no retraining
  • Positioned against non-deterministic LLMs
