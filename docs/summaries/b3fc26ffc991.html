Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic plan to launch an AI safety standards body without government oversight in late 2026 or 2027, and are considering CEOs

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

A report from The Information, per its sources: Google, OpenAI and Anthropic plan to launch an AI safety standards body that operates without government participation. The body is paywalled and was not captured, so this summary rests on the headline.

On that basis: the frontier labs are moving to write their own safety standards - self-regulation ahead of, or instead of, public rules. Membership beyond the three, the body's remit, and how binding its standards would be are unknown from the capture.

Highlights

  • Google, OpenAI and Anthropic plan a joint AI safety standards body, per The Information's sources
  • Notably without government involvement - industry self-regulation
  • Paywalled; remit, membership and enforcement unknown
