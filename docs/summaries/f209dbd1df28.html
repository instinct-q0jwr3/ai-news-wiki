HP, Asus, and Acer are shifting some PC production from Thailand and Vietnam back to China amid rising component costs; Dell is mulling a similar move

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Nikkei reports HP, Asus and Acer are shifting some PC production from Thailand and Vietnam back to China to combat soaring component costs, reversing part of their 'China+1' diversification; Dell is considering a similar move. A new generation of Chinese contract manufacturers is reaping the benefits of the reversal.

Highlights

  • HP, Asus, Acer moving some PC production back to China
  • Driver: rising component costs squeezing margins
  • Partial reversal of the 'China+1' diversification strategy
  • Dell mulling the same shift
