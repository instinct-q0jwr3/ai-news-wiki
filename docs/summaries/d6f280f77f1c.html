America Turned on Data Centers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

A Mother Jones podcast episode, Stress Test, argues that data centers have become one of the most toxic issues in the US midterms. Correspondent Tim Murphy says opposition is roughly three to one and that left and right both dislike the AI building boom, so politicians are changing positions.

The page cites his earlier investigation into hyperscale data centers and the communities they land on, and mentions Flock cameras as another AI-era technology reshaping political fortunes. It uses Texas, where Governor Abbott promoted cheap power for building, as an example of the reversal. This is an advocacy-leaning outlet, so the opposition figure is the guest's characterization.

Highlights

  • Data centers described as a top midterm issue
  • Claimed three-to-one voter opposition
  • Both parties' politicians shifting positions
  • Flock cameras also cited as a political flashpoint
  • Texas used as an example of the reversal
  • Framing is from Mother Jones and its guest
