AI is an information gateway. Audiences still don't want it making their news

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

A Nieman Lab piece on new research with Rasmus Kleis Nielsen and Richard Fletcher finds generative AI is becoming a major information gateway: on average 24% of respondents across nine countries saw an AI-made-content label in the last week and 20% saw an AI news summary. But audiences still do not want AI making their news, so AI mediates access to journalism rather than replacing it.

Highlights

  • AI is a growing information gateway, used and incidental
  • 24% saw AI-content labels in the past week; 20% AI summaries
  • Audiences still reject AI as news producer
  • Report with Nielsen and Fletcher, nine countries
