A federal appeals court upholds DOD's Anthropic blacklisting, finding Claude's integration with DOD systems is "a statutorily covered national-security risk"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme anthropic google ai-safety-incidents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

A federal appeals court panel in Washington upheld the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic on Friday, dealing the company a blow in its months-long battle with the Trump administration. The 2-1 decision rejected Anthropic's argument that the Department of Defense ban on its Claude models was arbitrary, unauthorized and unconstitutional.

Judge Gregory Katsas wrote for the D.C. Circuit majority that the Department 'had ample support for its conclusion that the continued integration of Claude into the Department's information systems, by the Department or its contractors, presented a statutorily covered national-security risk.'
