A federal appeals court upholds DOD's Anthropic blacklisting, finding Claude's integration with DOD systems is "a statutorily covered national-security risk"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme anthropic google ai-safety-incidents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

A federal appeals court upheld the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, a 2-1 decision that rejects the company's argument that the ban on its Claude models was arbitrary, unauthorized and unconstitutional. Writing for the D.C. Circuit majority, Judge Gregory Katsas (joined by Judge Neomi Rao) said the Defense Department had ample support for concluding that continued integration of Claude into its information systems presented a statutorily covered national-security risk; Judge Karen LeCraft Henderson dissented.

The designation, issued in March after negotiations over military use of Claude spiraled, bars the US military and its contractors from using Anthropic's models. The ruling is a major blow in Anthropic's months-long fight with the Trump administration - both majority judges were Trump appointees.
