Stripe Link data shows agentic transactions increased by 38x and volume of payments by 20x in September, with retail leading in both, and a ~$100 average spend

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high

Summary

Upstarts Media reports fresh Stripe Link data quantifying agentic commerce: agentic transactions through Link grew 38x and payment volume 20x in September, with retail leading both measures and average spend around $100. Link now powers about 200 AI agents - consumer assistants like Instinct, Muse and Grok Bot plus work-focused agents like Skydive and Town and a long tail of niche apps - while Anthropic and OpenAI use Link but not yet for agentic commerce. Dan Hill, Stripe's head of consumer product for Link, says his team is in Slack channels with each agent builder, fielding capability requests in daily stand-ups to keep pace with launches. Link's consumer base of 300 million users across merchants like Airbnb gives the agent channel a ready-made payment rail.
