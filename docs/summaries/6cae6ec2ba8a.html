The Claude Delusion

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

hacker-news anthropic

Summary

Cory Doctorow's essay argues that attributing mind and intention to chatbot output is a delusion we are slowly curing. He opens with atheism and sunsets: a religious person sees an intentional creation, an atheist sees beauty empty of purpose, and an artist's sunset sits between, made by a mind talking to itself. AI-generated prose, he argues, lacks the trace-elements of human uniqueness, so many digits past the decimal that the model always rounds them off, and the more we encounter smoothed-out AI texture, the less we hallucinate intentionality. Most of us are becoming 'AI atheists'.

Two barriers remain. First, most people cannot tell which parts of an unfamiliar activity require intention, so any competent performance implies an intender. Second, a small but important group, billionaires to whom nearly everyone is not a real person, sees chatbots as equal or superior to humans because they do not think most humans are fully people either. He warns that chatbot personhood is incompatible with rights for nature and resembles corporate personhood, and closes: chatbots are marvels of mathematics, and that is enough; it may be impossible to build an AI that does not hallucinate, but it is both possible and necessary to stop hallucinating about AI.

Highlights

  • Doctorow: treating chatbot output as intentional speech from a mind is a delusion ('The Claude Delusion').
  • Sunset analogy: religious vs atheist vs artist's sunset, three grades of perceived intentionality.
  • AI prose lacks 'trace-elements of uniqueness', rounded off before the model starts doing math.
  • Claims most users are slowly becoming 'AI atheists' as AI texture gets familiar.
  • Barrier 1: people assume any competent performance of an unfamiliar task implies an intender.
  • Barrier 2: billionaire solipsists equate chatbots with humans because they devalue most humans.
  • Chatbot personhood likened to corporate personhood and incompatible with rights for nature.
  • Close: chatbots are marvels of mathematics; stop hallucinating about AI.
