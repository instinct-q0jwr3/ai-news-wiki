Reach Security, which develops AI-powered exposure management tools for securing enterprise systems, raised a $50M+ Series B led by Top Tier Capital Partners

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

Reach Security has raised more than $50M in a Series B led by Top Tier Capital Partners, with Ballistic Ventures, In-Q-Tel, M12 and Microsoft's Venture Fund participating, and launched Reach Everywhere, extending its security control intelligence into every environment where defensive decisions are made. The capital goes to product development, integrations, overseas expansion and US enterprise and federal growth, after a year in which customer count doubled.

The company frames its timing around two data points: VulnCheck found under 1.3% of vulnerabilities are actually exploited in the wild, so the problem is reaction time rather than discovery, and CrowdStrike recorded an 89% jump in AI-enabled adversary activity with breakout times down to 27 seconds. Reach's AI reviews and adjusts each security control independently, concentrating operations teams on exposures that remain genuinely reachable and closing attack routes by reconfiguring products the organisation already owns - no extra staff required.

Highlights

  • $50M+ Series B led by Top Tier Capital Partners
  • Backed by Ballistic Ventures, In-Q-Tel, M12 and Microsoft's Venture Fund
  • Launched Reach Everywhere alongside the raise
  • Customers include Autodesk, Insurity, MPS and Ensemble Health Partners; count doubled in a year
  • Premise: under 1.3% of vulnerabilities are exploited, so reaction time is the real problem
  • Cites 89% jump in AI-enabled adversary activity, 27-second breakout times
  • AI reconfigures existing security products to close reachable attack paths
