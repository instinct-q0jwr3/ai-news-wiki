Summary

Corridor, an AI-native health-benefits broker, raised a $16 million seed round led by Bain Capital Ventures, CEO Nikhil Aggarwal told Axios Pro exclusively. The full details sit behind the Axios Pro paywall; the disclosed terms confirm the round size, the lead investor and Corridor's positioning as an AI-native broker in the health benefits market.
