Vantora raises $100M+ to build AI-native startups for corporates

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

UP.Labs, the four-year-old venture lab that builds startups solving problems for corporate customers like Alaska Airlines and Porsche, has rebranded as Vantora and raised a $100 million investment from Silversmith Capital Partners. The firm still builds startups with corporate partners who invest in the ventures and serve as first customers - but founder and CEO John Kuolt says it is moving toward a 'proprietary M&A pipeline': corporate partners can now fold the startups Vantora builds into their core businesses and keep them to themselves.

The shift came out of missed upside: Kuolt recounts that an AI idea developed with partner J.B. Hunt had to be shelved because the company would never let it be sold to the wider market. Large industrial firms, he argues, increasingly want to own the intelligence layer - sovereignty over AI retrofitted into their hardware and operations - rather than rely on third parties, and Vantora's new structure lets them buy that ownership outright. New corporate customers include unnamed industrial manufacturers and oil and gas companies.
