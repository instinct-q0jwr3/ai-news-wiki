Salience Labs, which develops specialized all-optical circuit switching tech for AI data centers, raised a $125M Series B led by Bullhound Capital

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high

Summary

Salience Labs raised a $125M Series B led by Bullhound Capital with Intel Capital and AMD Ventures, SBI, Hudson River Trading and the British Business Bank. It makes all-optical circuit switching for AI data centers, avoiding the optical-electrical-optical conversion used by electronic switches (SiliconANGLE).

Highlights

  • $125M Series B led by Bullhound.
  • Intel Capital and AMD Ventures participate.
  • All-optical switching avoids electrical conversion in data centers.
