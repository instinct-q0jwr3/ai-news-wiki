FrontierCode 2.0

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Cognition launched FrontierCode 2.0, its benchmark for agentic software engineering, arguing existing coding benchmarks saturate quickly and miss where models actually fail: inferring user intent, respecting unstated constraints, and anticipating long-term consequences. Elite open-source maintainers grade solutions on mergeability rubrics; solutions failing blocking criteria score zero.

The Pareto frontier runs from Opus 5.5 at the high end to Luna, Haiku and GPT-6.1 Sol at the low-cost end, with Sol notably strong for its price. The benchmark is far from saturated - the top model scores only 45% - and open-source models, especially western OSS, show significant room to improve.

Highlights

  • Targets intent inference, unstated constraints, long-term consequences

  • GPT-6.1 Sol leads on cost-efficiency; OSS lags
