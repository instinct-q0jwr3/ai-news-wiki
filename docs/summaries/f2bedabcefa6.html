Letter to Anthropic Re: Planned Initial Public Offering

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

New York City Comptroller, writing as investment adviser and trustee of the city's $325B pension funds, urges Dario Amodei to pause Anthropic's planned IPO until the company fully and transparently addresses the AI safety, governance and development-pace concerns it has itself raised. The letter calls the recent 'morally binding' accord Anthropic and five other companies signed with the White House insufficient.

The argument is fiduciary as much as ethical: preparing a public offering diverts resources and attention from safety work, and the letter points to Anthropic's own unusually direct public warnings about frontier-model capability as the standard the company should be held to before going public.
