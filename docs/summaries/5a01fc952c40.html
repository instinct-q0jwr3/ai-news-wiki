Tencent Launches Hy4, Joining the Ranks of the World's Leading Open-Source AI Models

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

Tencent announced Hy4, its latest large model, which it says ranks among leading open-source models. Weights and system cards come in the coming days, with a focus on cyber and bio safety. New: visual understanding (image processing, charts, math reasoning), better instruction following and long context, stronger long-horizon agent tasks, and first-time computer use across command lines and GUIs. Rankings are Tencent's claim.

Highlights

  • Weights and system cards promised within days
