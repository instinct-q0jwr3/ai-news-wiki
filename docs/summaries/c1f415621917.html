Jev in 25 Lines of Python

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news typesafe

Summary

A skeptical counter to the Jev hype: the author reimplements the idea behind decision-type models in 25 lines of Python. The script loads a small GGUF model (Qwen3-0.6B via llama-cpp-python), prompts it to choose among labeled options (e.g. Legitimate / Spam / Phishing for an email), then reads the logits of the label tokens, converts them to log-probabilities and picks the argmax. The point: constrained-choice 'decision' inference is a logit-comparison trick any developer can reproduce, not a new paradigm.

Highlights

  • Reimplements Jev-style typed decision inference in 25 lines of Python.
  • Uses llama-cpp-python with Qwen3-0.6B-GGUF, n_ctx=512.
  • Compares logits of the label tokens (A/B/C) and takes the argmax after log-softmax.
  • Framed as a direct rebuttal to claims that Jev is 'the next frontier of LLMs'.
