Meta quietly launches Style Sketch, an AI fashion app that helps users put together outfit ideas using clothes they own, in Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Meta has quietly launched Style Sketch, an AI fashion app, in Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand on iOS and Android. Spotted by social media consultant Matt Navarra, the app connects to Instagram and Facebook to build a virtual closet from photos you have already posted - or from items you add by photo, video or camera roll - and suggests outfit combinations matched to your style, including weather-based looks and trip packing plans.

The standout feature is virtual try-on on a 'realistic model matched to your body shape and skin tone'. Meta did not respond to TechCrunch's questions about wider availability. The launch extends Meta's quiet run of AI consumer experiments into wardrobe territory already occupied by several specialist apps.

Highlights

  • Meta soft-launches Style Sketch in Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand
  • Builds a virtual closet from your Instagram/Facebook photos or manual uploads
  • Suggests outfits matched to style, weather and trip packing
  • Virtual try-on on a model matched to body shape and skin tone
  • Spotted by Matt Navarra; Meta silent on wider launch plans
  • Second Meta AI consumer app this week after Waddle World
