OpenAI says users can message Dots through ChatGPT, Slack, and Microsoft Teams and join a waitlist to text via iMessage or RCS messaging on Android

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high

Summary

Dots are OpenAI's new always-on agents, announced at DevDay 2026 and powered by GPT-6 Astra. Unlike single-turn chat, a Dot keeps working: it crawls the web, pulls context from connected apps, learns preferences, and messages the user through ChatGPT, Slack, or Microsoft Teams. The canned demos: a Dot notices the user's calendar runs through dinner and proactively messages two Grubhub options with prices; another collaborates on launching a website. The agents are personalized as cute nameable blobs and take multi-step projects.
