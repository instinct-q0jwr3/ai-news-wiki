Many longtime Etsy sellers say they are leaving after Etsy updated its terms to say Etsy and its third-party partners can use sellers' content for AI training

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high

Summary

A Techmeme excerpt of a New York Times report says some longtime Etsy sellers are leaving after terms changed to permit AI-training uses of seller content by Etsy and partners. The original article and the current terms were inaccessible in this pass.

The excerpt establishes the reported dispute, not the complete legal scope, opt-out rules or proof that any particular seller’s work has already trained a model. No account or content settings were changed.

Highlights

  • Evidence is an indexed report excerpt
  • Sellers object to reported training terms
  • Current legal terms were not inspected

  • Opt-out details were unavailable
