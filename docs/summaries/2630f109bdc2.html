[AINews] Anthropic ends Coding Interviews: "Hunger beats tenure"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Latent Space's AINews gives the headline to Felix Rieseberg's 'Hunger beats tenure' post on Anthropic ending coding interviews, and extends his advice for junior engineers: pick business impact over backlog, choose a radical idea, build it over a weekend instead of lobbying through PRDs, focus on one good idea per prototype, and lean into the fresh perspective that newcomers have on user-facing work.

The same issue notes major version bumps from Google Genie, Artificial Analysis and FrontierCode, plus 'impressive math verification' news, as mostly incremental annual improvements.

  • One good idea per prototype; fresh eyes as an asset

  • Frames Anthropic's interview change as a path forward for juniors
