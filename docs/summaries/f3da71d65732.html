Rein Security, which develops runtime tools to secure enterprise AI agents and stop adversarial AI agents, raised a $25M Series A, taking total funding to $35M

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Rein Security, an Israeli startup founded in 2024 by Matan Bar-Efrat and Netanel Rubin, raised a $25 million Series A co-led by Glilot Capital and Sienna Venture Capital, bringing its total funding to $35 million. The company builds runtime tools to secure enterprise AI agents and to defend against adversarial AI agents, and says revenue has grown eightfold since January, with its platform now securing thousands of agents executing millions of actions.

The raise lands as organizations deploy agents with ever broader access to business systems, data and workflows - a class of risk traditional security approaches were not designed to detect. Rein frames the problem as two-sided: companies must secure the agents they build while also defending against AI-powered attacks. The 31-person company has offices in Tel Aviv and New York.
