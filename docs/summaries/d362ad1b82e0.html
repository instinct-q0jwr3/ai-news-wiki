Gwern's May 2021 newsletter: site changelog plus a dense ML link roundup. Also noted: a 'Choose Your Own Adventure AI Dungeon' proposal and Decision Transformers ('preference learning as simple as possible'). A snapshot of the scaling era just before GPT-class models went mainstream, from the OPML backfill.
