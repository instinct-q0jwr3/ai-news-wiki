Chinese state media: DeepSeek and MiniMax completed registration and won approval to open-source and deploy AI models overseas under proposed export controls

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Chinese state media report that DeepSeek and MiniMax have completed registration and won approval to open-source and deploy AI models overseas under China's proposed export controls - part of a wave of homegrown AI technologies Beijing is clearing for global release, per Bloomberg.
