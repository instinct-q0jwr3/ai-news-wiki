Baselayer, which uses AI to help financial institutions verify businesses and assess fraud risk, raised a $35M Series A led by M13, for ~$40M in total funding

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Baselayer, an AI-powered startup that helps financial institutions verify businesses and assess fraud risk, raised a $35M Series A led by M13 with Picus Capital, Torch Capital, Afore Capital and Socure's Matt Thompson participating, taking total funding to about $40M since 2023. The new money expands its identity technology to AI agents.

Baselayer combines business identity, credit and fraud data for KYB verification, fraud detection and risk management, selling directly and through resellers. Co-founder and CEO Jonathan Awad says more than 2,000 financial institutions - over 20% of such institutions in the US - use it to onboard, underwrite and open accounts for merchants, alongside Fortune 500 customers; the valuation was not disclosed.
