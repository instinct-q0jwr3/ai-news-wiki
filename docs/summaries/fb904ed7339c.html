Show HN: Vellum, the best diagram editor you'll ever use

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

hacker-news anthropic external-evaluation

Summary

I love Excalidraw, I use it all the time for work to quickly draw up a proposal for a customer.

But its annoying I have to re-draw the same thing, but better in Draw.io / Visio when it comes to writing the formal documentation later.

Vellum is designed to give you the speed of a whiteboard, but the architecture-diagram-finish in quality.

Highlights

  • It uses YAML to store everything, and even has a 'graph' section for those of you who like the diagram-as-code elements of mermaid... but vellum allows full authoring of layout without the restrictions of Mermaid.
  • Been working on it a while, but decided to make it source available here: https://github.com/blueprintr-io/vellum-editor Proud to say its not purely vibe coded slop, but I used Claude to help with some of the more challenging geometry…
  • Vellum .s3. .amazonaws.com — a third origin, neither this SPA's nor the API's — so 'self' and https://blueprintr.io do not cover them.
  • 2026-09-04: their absence blocked every save of a diagram containing a new image.
  • Scoped to S3 rather than reusing img-src's broad *.amazonaws.com — connect-src grants read access to responses, so it earns a tighter leash.
  • NOTE THE REGION COUPLING: eu-west-1 is pinned across this deployment; moving the bucket's region without editing this line reintroduces the bug silently. -->
