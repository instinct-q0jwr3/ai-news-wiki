Mercury 2.5 LLM hits 770 tokens per second

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Artificial Analysis' model page for Mercury 2.5, released September 2026, puts its headline number at 770 output tokens per second - notably fast for its class. The proprietary model scores 12 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, below the comparable median of 13, but generated only 35M tokens during evaluation versus a median of 85M, making it fairly concise.

Pricing is $0.25 per 1M input tokens and $0.75 per 1M output tokens, with an average Intelligence Index evaluation cost of $0.06 per task. The model is text-only with a 260k-token context window, and the page covers the reasoning variant, with a non-reasoning variant also available.

Highlights

  • Mercury 2.5 runs at 770 output tokens per second, well above its class median.
  • Scores 12 on the Intelligence Index vs a 13 median - below average intelligence, well priced.
  • Concise: 35M tokens generated in evaluation vs an 85M median.
  • Pricing: $0.25/1M input, $0.75/1M output; $0.06 per task on the Index.
  • Text-only, 260k context window; page covers the reasoning variant.
