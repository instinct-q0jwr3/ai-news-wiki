TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

Google announced two more systems in its eighth-generation TPU line: TPU 8tx for secure, confidential inference, and TPU 8i Max for very large context windows and the highest compute density per rack. With the earlier 8t (training) and 8i (real-time reasoning), that makes four TPU systems in one year, a first for Google.

Vahdat says the move is driven by customer feedback: agent-scale workloads need accelerators that reason over huge contexts, and regulated customers want specialized encryption. He stresses that a new system, which combines compute, networking, storage and packaging around a chip, can be delivered faster than a new chip. Google says TPUs now have nearly 100 customers, including frontier labs and trading firms, and that it is easing access through PyTorch support.
