TSMC reports Q3 revenue up 50.9% YoY to $46.36B, net income up 68.7% to $23.7B, both above est., marking a sixth straight quarterly profit record on AI demand

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

TSMC reported record third-quarter results: revenue up 50.9% to $46.36B and net income up 68.7% to $23.7B, both above estimates, a sixth straight record profit quarter. Operating margin reached 58.7%, above guidance.

It guides fourth-quarter revenue to $52.3B-$54.3B, about 58% higher than a year earlier at the midpoint, driven by AI chip demand.

Highlights

  • Q3 revenue $46.36B, net income $23.7B, both beat.
  • Q4 guidance $52.3B-$54.3B.
