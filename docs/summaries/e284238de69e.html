A coalition of 42 AGs opposes Character.AI's plan to auction off data from 21M+ US accounts, 2.1M+ registered to minors, saying the sale lacks user protections

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports a coalition of forty-two attorneys general opposing a proposed sale of Character.ai user data during bankruptcy proceedings. It attributes to officials a dataset exceeding twenty-one million US accounts, including more than two million registered to minors, and concerns about private chats, IP addresses and earlier privacy promises.

The article describes a proposed auction and legal objections, not a completed sale or a court ruling that resolves the dispute. It distinguishes this data transaction from a separate proposed Disney intellectual-property license that does not include chats. Allegations about inadequate protections remain attributed to the officials; the underlying court filings, buyer plans and ultimate approval were not independently inspected here.

Highlights

  • Officials oppose a proposed bankruptcy data transaction
  • Account counts are attributed to the coalition
  • Private chats and other identifiers are at issue
  • Auction is reported scheduled for December 1
  • Buyer's intended use is unclear
  • Separate Disney IP license does not include chats
  • No final court approval or completed sale is established
