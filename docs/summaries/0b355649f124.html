Numeral, a provider of AI-powered tech to automate sales tax compliance workflows in over 90 countries, raised a $100M Series C led by Insight Partners

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-27 · updated: 2026-09-28 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Numeral, whose platform uses AI to automate sales tax compliance, has closed a $100M Series C led by Insight Partners, with Salesforce Ventures, Geodesic, Benchmark, Mayfield, FCVC, Y Combinator and Uncork participating. The pitch: tax obligations keep widening across states and countries - California, for example, is bringing prewritten software and SaaS into sales-tax scope from 2027 - and companies cannot track the changes by hand.

The company says transaction volume grew 327% year-over-year and expects its engine to handle more than 80 million transactions, with customers broadening across software, manufacturing, distribution and wholesale. The new capital goes to faster product development, a wider offering for those sectors, and hiring across engineering, sales, marketing and product.
