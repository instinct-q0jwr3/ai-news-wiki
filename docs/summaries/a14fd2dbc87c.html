AP: Avoid language that gives [AI] human characteristics

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

The AP Stylebook's guidance on AI language: artificial intelligence systems do not think, feel, want or understand, so avoid language that gives them human characteristics - anthropomorphizing, the habit of ascribing human traits, emotions or behaviors to non-human things.

The recommended alternative is functional description: explain what a system does, how well it performs, who built it and who could be affected by it. The guidance was posted alongside AP coverage of OpenAI flagging concerning model behavior.
