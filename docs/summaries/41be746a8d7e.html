Show HN: Pointless but mostly-exact clone of Hacker News

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high

Summary

HN Simulator is a pointless but mostly-exact clone of the Hacker News front page, down to the 'new | past | comments | lists | submit' chrome - except every story, points count, username and comment tally is invented. The simulated front page riffs on the real news cycle: 'Claude Haiku 5.5' at 316 points, 'GPT-6 and Intelligent UI for everyone', 'Shipping JPEG XL in Chrome' at 400, a Commodore 64 keycap font, even a fake Nobel chemistry prize.

It works as both parody and mirror: the fake rankings are plausible enough that the joke is how predictable the real front page has become.

Highlights

  • A mostly-exact visual clone of the Hacker News front page with fully simulated content.
  • Fake stories parody the real cycle: Haiku 5.5, GPT-6, JPEG XL in Chrome, a keycap font.
  • Points, usernames and comment counts are all invented but plausible.
  • The joke lands because the real front page is this predictable.
