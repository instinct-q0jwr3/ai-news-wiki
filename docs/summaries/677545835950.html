Hinton: We're creating alien beings. I'm willing to be selfish and choose humans

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

In a wide-ranging interview, Geoffrey Hinton repeats his warning that AI is an existential threat and dismisses the idea that the industry can regulate itself. He also attacks Donald Trump's use of the word superintelligence.

He frames the larger challenge as accepting that humans are not the only intelligent beings, and says he would choose humans. The interview follows a month in which other senior researchers joined his alarm.

Highlights

  • Hinton says industry self-regulation is not credible.
  • He says we are creating alien intelligences but would side with humans.
