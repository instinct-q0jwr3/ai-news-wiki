Meta announces $95 billion stock offering to fund AI expansion

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Meta announced plans to raise $95 billion to fund AI infrastructure and its superintelligence push: $35 billion through underwritten public equity offerings plus $60 billion via an at-the-market program. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom expressed interest in buying up to $5 billion of shares. The stock fell 1.6% on the news.

The raise follows a Q3 where revenue jumped 26% but investors punished negative free cash flow and a capex forecast raised to $135-145 billion. Meta is building four superclusters of over 1 gigawatt each and plans to nearly double infrastructure capacity in 2026. The move lands amid a broader funding wave: Alphabet's $85 billion raise, SpaceX's $40 billion debt financing, and Anthropic's IPO preparations. Goldman Sachs estimates AI capex of about $7.6 trillion from 2026 through 2031.

Highlights

  • $95B total: $35B underwritten equity plus $60B at-the-market program
  • Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom interested in up to $5B of shares
  • Full-year capex forecast raised to $135-145B; four 1GW+ superclusters
  • Goldman estimates $7.6T AI capex across the industry 2026-2031
