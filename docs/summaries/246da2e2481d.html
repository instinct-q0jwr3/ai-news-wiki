Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news anthropic

Summary

Anthropic introduced a new life sciences research group and laboratory focused on fundamental biology research with Claude: exploring DNA datasets to identify uncharacterized protein families, generating hypotheses at scale and testing them experimentally. As an early result, the company says Claude discovered a novel enzyme system with properties reminiscent of CRISPR - RNA-repeat arrays associated with reverse transcriptases - with only high-level direction from its scientists.

The post frames the effort in the lineage of restriction enzymes, Taq polymerase and CRISPR itself, all of which began as oddities noticed in nature's molecular diversity. Anthropic formed the group in spring 2026 to test whether general AI models can systematize and accelerate such discoveries, betting on a way of doing biology in which agents collaborate with humans at every step.

Highlights

  • Claude identified RNA-repeat arrays associated with reverse transcriptases, a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like properties.
  • Anthropic is standing up a dedicated life sciences research group and wet lab.
