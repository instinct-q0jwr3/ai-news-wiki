US law enforcement and intel agency docs: since 2021, the FBI, DHS, and NYPD have viewed AI as both a facilitator of violent extremism and a target for attacks

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

The Guardian reports on previously unreported US law enforcement and intelligence documents showing that since 2021 the FBI, DHS and NYPD have treated AI as both a tool and a target of would-be terrorists. One example is the May 2025 Palm Springs fertility clinic car bombing, where the suspect used a generative AI tool that failed to catch his request for bomb-making instructions. The documents also warn about anti-AI extremist threats. Basis: the opening of the article.

Highlights

  • Documents show agencies see AI as a facilitator of violent extremism and a target of it.
  • Palm Springs 2025 bombing involved an AI tool that missed a bomb-making request.
  • Warnings include anti-tech extremist threats.
