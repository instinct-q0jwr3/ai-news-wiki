A proposal: how to hire junior engineers in the age of AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-03 · confidence: high

Summary

Junior-engineer hiring has collapsed - Stanford's 'Canaries in the Coal Mine' work puts employment for developers aged 22-25 nearly 20% below its late-2022 peak while senior hiring keeps growing, and Anthropic says juniors have fallen from 27% to 9% of its engineering hires since 2025. Laurie Voss's new post argues the industry's real problem is that it deleted the job juniors were trained for and never built a way to interview for the new one.

The old filter is dead: coding interviews measured programming, and programming is now the part agents do. Companies are improvising - one Anthropic team dropped coding interviews entirely, Canva made an AI-assisted round mandatory, Meta swapped one onsite round, Google is piloting code-comprehension rounds - but nobody claims a working answer, so many firms simply only hire seniors, which Voss calls totally unsustainable. The altruistic argument (train juniors or the industry dies) fails under capitalism because every manager prefers that some other company pay for the training.

His selfish counter-argument: juniors are cheap and available while seniors are 'extremely employed,' and 71% of software-job-posting growth from May 2025 to May 2026 was for senior roles - a bidding war for a fixed pool. Shopify (internships up from ~100 to over 1,000 a year) and Cloudflare (1,111 interns targeted for 2026) show one paid-trial path, but startups can't afford it. The post's core proposal is an interview format for 'product engineers' - selecting for the judgment the new job actually requires rather than the syntax it no longer does. It's the most concrete attempt yet to answer the question his quarter-million-view talk left open.

Highlights

  • Junior developer employment (22-25) is down ~20% from its 2022 peak; Anthropic's junior share of engineering hires fell from 27% to 9% since 2025
  • Coding interviews are dead as a filter; Canva, Meta, Google and an Anthropic team are all improvising replacements with no settled answer
  • Voss's selfish case for hiring juniors: seniors are scarce and ever-pricier (71% of posting growth), juniors are cheap and available
  • Paid-trial models exist (Shopify 1,000+ interns, Cloudflare 1,111) but don't scale to startups; the post proposes a product-engineer interview format
