Apple unveils the fourth-gen Apple TV 4K, with Siri AI, an A19 chip, 128GB of storage, far-field mics, Wi-Fi 7, and Bluetooth 6, shipping November 13 for $299

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-17 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple introduced the fourth-gen Apple TV 4K at its Welcome Home event: A19 chip, 128GB, far-field microphones for hands-free Siri, Find My in the remote and surround sound with up to four HomePods. It is the first model with Siri AI and costs $299, more than twice the previous generation's launch price (The Verge).

Highlights

  • Fourth-gen Apple TV 4K at $299 with an A19 chip and 128GB.
  • Far-field mics for hands-free Siri; remote has Find My.
  • First device with Siri AI; pairs up to four HomePods for surround.
