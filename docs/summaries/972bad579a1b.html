Finnish neocloud startup Verda raised $189M led by Emergence at a $1B+ valuation and aims to raise up to $1.5B in equity and debt in 2026 and up to $10B in 2027

Summary

Finnish neocloud startup Verda raised $189 million led by Emergence at a valuation above $1 billion, Bloomberg reports. The company aims to raise up to $1.5 billion in equity and debt in 2026 and up to $10 billion in 2027, underscoring investor appetite for AI infrastructure.
