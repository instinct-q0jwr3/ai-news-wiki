Dallas-based Disruptive raised $10B to invest in AI, defense, and software, doubling its AUM, and pivots from single-deal SPVs to a discretionary fund structure

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Bloomberg reports Dallas-based Disruptive raised $10 billion to invest in AI, defense and software, doubling its assets under management and pivoting from single-deal SPVs to a discretionary fund structure. The 15-year-old firm plans to back about 10 later-stage companies with $1 billion each.
