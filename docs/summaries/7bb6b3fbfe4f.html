Show HN: I built a tool that prices a prebuilt PC part by part

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

PC Deal Check is a Show HN tool that grades a prebuilt PC's asking price by pricing its parts. You paste a retailer link or upload a photo of the price and specs, AI identifies the main components, and a pricing engine compares the price to market-based part estimates to grade the deal. The example: a PowerSpec G531 at $1,399.99 graded A against a parts estimate of about $1,569. It is free for US visitors with US retailer listings.

Highlights

  • Grades prebuilt PC prices against part-by-part estimates
  • AI reads the listing; a custom engine does the pricing
  • Example: $1,399.99 PowerSpec G531 got an A vs ~$1,569 in parts
