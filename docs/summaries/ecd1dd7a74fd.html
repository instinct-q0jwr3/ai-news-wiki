SpaceX closes $40 billion debt deal for AI chips, Elon Musk says

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Elon Musk says SpaceX has closed $40 billion in debt financing to buy GPUs for its AI systems. In posts on X, he described the company as investment grade, pointing to revenue from Starlink and the launch business, and said the financing is primarily callable debt for chips expected to arrive in the first and second quarters of 2027. A regulatory filing on Monday confirmed a binding commitment with an Apollo-managed entity for roughly $40 billion in GPU financing.

The deal underscores the AI boom's enormous capital requirements and SpaceX's aggressive expansion into AI compute, even as Musk simultaneously warns that debt markets are shutting for other AI data-center borrowers and predicts consolidation among neoclouds.
