SAP is preparing a plan to eliminate at least 10,000 positions, or 9% of its staff globally, to transform its software business for the AI era

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

A Bloomberg opening and its machine-generated takeaways say SAP is preparing a possible restructuring involving at least ten thousand positions, around nine percent of its workforce, citing unnamed people familiar with discussions. The report says the number is not finalized and may change after labor negotiations.

Only the opening and takeaways were accessible, not the full report, company response or employee consultation documents. A plan under discussion is not ten thousand completed layoffs, and an AI-transformation framing does not prove each role is being directly replaced by a model. Scope, timing, geography and final terms remain unresolved here.

Highlights

  • People familiar with discussions are unnamed
