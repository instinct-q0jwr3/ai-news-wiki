I'm with Linus

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high

Summary

A short opinion post siding with Linus Torvalds' settled position on AI: it is a tool one can embrace for efficiency or oppose to retain inefficiency. The author, a Linux user since 1999, frames the current anti-AI backlash as Luddism and a symptom of a polarized Western society. His bottom line: as long as you understand the tool and its output, the choice of tool matters less than the understanding.

Highlights

  • Brief opinion piece endorsing Torvalds' pragmatic stance on AI
  • Frames the anti-AI mood as Luddism and polarization
  • Argument: AI is a tool to embrace for efficiency, not fear
  • Condition attached: you must understand the tool and its output
