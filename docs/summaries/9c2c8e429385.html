Proton's CEO Cancels AI 'Re-Interviews'

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Proton CEO Andy Yen on Friday cancelled a series of nearly company-wide 're-interviews' that would have required employees to explain how they use AI - with the clear possibility that using it too little could get them fired - according to six current employees and audio of a two-hour all-hands meeting obtained by 404 Media. The cancellation came a day after 404 Media published its investigation into Yen's aggressive AI push at the privacy company.

The reporting details the cultural clash: when a security-team employee urged taking a few days to pentest AI-written code before shipping, Yen replied in Slack, 'That's bullshit and loser thinking. Of course it's possible to be fast and secure,' adding 'stop lovingly handcrafting all our code and use AI more.' The cancelled re-interviews are unlikely to settle the bigger concern among Proton's users - that the company is deprioritizing security - and many have spent the last 24 hours calling for Yen's resignation.
