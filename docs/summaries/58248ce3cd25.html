China's GDP is set to grow 4.5% YoY in Q3, up from Q2's three-year-low growth rate of 4.3%, as AI-driven exports helped offset weak domestic spending

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

The BBC reports China's GDP grew 4.5% year-on-year in Q3 2026, rebounding from Q2's three-year low of 4.3% as AI-driven exports helped offset weak domestic spending. The figure sits at the bottom of Beijing's 4.5-5% annual target, which analysts say leaves policymakers room to act. China's National Bureau of Statistics described the economy as sustaining steady development 'against pressure' with new growth drivers, and the trade surplus is on track to pass last year's record $1.2tn. High-tech sales led the export strength. The outlook is clouded by global trade tensions and uncertainty over AI investments, while weak spending at home remains the structural gap exports are papering over.
