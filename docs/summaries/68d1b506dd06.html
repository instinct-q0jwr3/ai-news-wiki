Trump says the AI accord with tech leaders is "morally binding" and the administration is considering a 10-person committee to oversee the AI industry

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

President Trump said Tuesday he signed a 'morally binding' AI document with tech leaders after a White House luncheon, telling reporters he is seeing 'tremendous self-policing' and that the administration is considering a 10-person committee to oversee the AI industry. House Speaker Mike Johnson called the agreement a voluntary statement of principles. Outside, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei said risk rules are still under discussion, as Washington debates intensify alongside mounting agent-orchestrated attacks.

Highlights

  • Trump signs what he calls a 'morally binding' AI accord with tech leaders
  • Administration weighing a 10-person committee to oversee the AI industry
  • Mike Johnson: the principles are 'voluntary on behalf of the industry'
  • Trump cites 'tremendous self-policing' by tech companies
  • Amodei: rules addressing AI risks 'still under discussion'
