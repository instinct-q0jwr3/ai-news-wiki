Mammoth-1 and Elephant-1: frontier decision models

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

TypeSafe released the technical report behind what it calls the world's best decision models: mammoth-1 (27B) and elephant-1 (9B). The decision-model format, which the company introduced in September 2026 with Jev and the System One API - named for Kahneman's fast, intuitive mode of thinking - targets quick, single-shot judgments rather than long chains of reasoning, and the report's refrain is that training a frontier decision model is about data, data, data.

On the two community benchmarks, mammoth-1 ranks first of 208 models on the Jev Decision Index (37 public benchmarks plus private tests) and both models place at the top of the Artificial Analysis Decision Index covering knowledge, reasoning, tool calling, and safety. The release continues the industry's split between deliberative reasoning models and fast decision models optimized for latency and cost in agent loops.
