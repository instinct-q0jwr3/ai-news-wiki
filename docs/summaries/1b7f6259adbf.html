Ando, which is building a team messaging platform for both humans and AI agents, comes out of stealth with $20M in pre-seed and seed funding

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme agentic-systems

Summary

TechCrunch profiles Ando, a startup building a team messaging platform for both humans and AI agents. Founder Sara Du kept hitting the problem while helping companies build MCP servers in 2025: people wanted to use AI agents from within Slack, but moving messages back and forth and getting agents the right context burned through token budgets.

Digging in, Du concluded the issue was deeper than tooling: Slack and Teams were built for humans, not for agents - so Ando is rebuilding the communication layer itself for mixed human-agent teams.
