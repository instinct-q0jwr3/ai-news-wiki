Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Google's own blog introduces Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar: by natively coupling its live dialogue capabilities with low-latency streaming video, Live Avatar brings near real-time visual presence to Gemini's conversational AI, pitched at enterprises and their users.

Building on last week's Gemini 3.8 Live launch, the September 24 post is by research scientist Shuo-yiin Chang and software engineer CJ Zheng on behalf of the Gemini Audio Team.
