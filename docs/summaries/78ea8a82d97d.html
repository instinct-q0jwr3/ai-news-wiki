[AINews] Claude Haiku 5.5 — better than GPT-6 Luna at the same pricing

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic shipped Claude Haiku 5.5, its first Haiku update in about a year, priced to match OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna - and cut prices on Sonnet 5.5 and its subscription plans the same day. Anthropic calls it the cheapest, fastest and most capable small model it has released, costing about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5 on average, and scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, up 26 points year over year.

Haiku 5.5 is the first Haiku with Anthropic's effort settings and adaptive thinking, with tiered pricing introduced. It is live on the Claude Platform and in Claude Code, positioned as a subagent paired with Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 for high-volume work like summaries, compactions and database queries. The Latent Space AINews issue also tracks the pre-launch leaks: posters had the model's Claude Code appearance and Luna-matching pricing before the official announcement.

Highlights

  • Claude Haiku 5.5 released, priced to match GPT-6 Luna.
  • ~75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5 on average; scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis index (+26 YoY).
  • First Haiku with effort settings and adaptive thinking; tiered pricing added.
  • Same-day price cuts on Sonnet 5.5 and Anthropic subscription plans.
  • Positioned as a subagent for summaries, compactions and database queries in Claude Code.
  • Launch details leaked ahead of time via Claude Code updates.
