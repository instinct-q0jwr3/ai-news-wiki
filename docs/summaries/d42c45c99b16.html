Our Project Suncatcher prototype satellite is in orbit

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Google says its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite, built with Planet, launched on SpaceX's Transporter-18 rideshare and is operating as expected. It is a first step in researching whether space could host scalable machine learning infrastructure.

Over the coming weeks Google will gather data on how its TPUs handle spaceflight stress, radiation and thermal extremes. A peer-reviewed paper is available.

Highlights

  • Prototype satellite launched on Transporter-18 with Planet.
  • Contact confirmed; operating as expected.
  • Will test TPUs under radiation and thermal stress.
  • Research moonshot toward space-based ML infrastructure.
