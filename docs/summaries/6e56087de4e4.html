Moondream is joining Prime Intellect

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Prime Intellect acquired Moondream; founders Vik Korrapati and Jay Allen join to lead inference and speed up multimodal work in vision and computer use. Moondream had shipped open-weight vision-language models with millions of monthly downloads and was an early customer.
