Tencent signed a ~$7B, five-year deal with Oracle this year for access to ~100K advanced AI chips unavailable in China via Southeast Asian data centers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

The Financial Times reports, citing sources, that Tencent signed a roughly $7 billion, five-year deal with Oracle this year to access about 100,000 advanced AI chips that are unavailable in China, through Oracle's Southeast Asian data centers. It comes as the WeChat owner races ByteDance and Alibaba. Based on the Techmeme excerpt.
