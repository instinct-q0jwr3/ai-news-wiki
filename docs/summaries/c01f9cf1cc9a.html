Arda, which develops AI software and agents to accelerate assembly line programming and manufacturing automation, raised $230M at a $1.5B valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC exclusively interviews Bob McGrew, OpenAI's former chief research officer, whose previously stealth startup Arda has raised $230 million at a $1.5 billion valuation in a round led by Altimeter Capital, Accel, and Khosla Ventures. Arda builds AI software and agents to accelerate assembly-line programming and manufacturing automation; McGrew pitches it as doing for manufacturing engineers what Claude or Cursor does for programmers, and says its models are already deployed with Anduril. The wedge is changeover time: retraining a human on a new design takes a day, but reworking the automation takes three to six months, and Arda's product aims to collapse that gap. McGrew recounts staying at OpenAI through the November 2023 Altman firing weekend to ship the reasoning product he called the last key breakthrough before AGI. Co-founders include Augustus Odena and former Palantir engineers Alex Mark and Jakob Frick; Altimeter's Apoorv Agrawal frames the bet against a $16 trillion global manufacturing industry, $3 trillion in the US. Robotics is next on the roadmap.
