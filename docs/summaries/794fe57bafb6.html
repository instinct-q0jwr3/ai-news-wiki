At the unBoxed conference, Amazon moves AI from being a feature to playing a more central role in ad-buying and brings previously separate ad workflows together

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

At its unBoxed conference in San Francisco, Amazon pushed AI from a feature into the centre of ad buying, renaming its unified platform Amazon Ads Agent and launching Full-Funnel Campaigns, which combine sponsored ads, display, video and streaming TV in a single campaign: advertisers supply products, creative and budget while Amazon's AI executes and continuously optimizes toward outcomes like new-to-brand customers. DVA+ consolidates Sponsored Display, Sponsored TV and programmatic display, video and audio buying.

Digiday notes the launches come while government allegations about auction practices still dog parts of Amazon's media operations, and observers advise caution about taking the claims at face value.
