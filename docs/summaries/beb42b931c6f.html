What Comes After AGI?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high

Summary

A DeepMind Institute essay maps possible routes beyond human-level AGI, including cheaper and more numerous agents, stronger individual models and coordinated groups. The examined passages explicitly state that efficiency extrapolations are conditional and that scaling observed trends cannot confidently predict what AGI or ASI requires.

The authors also acknowledge missing capabilities and coordination problems that adding more agents may not solve. Their scenarios are research arguments, not a demonstrated arrival of superintelligence or a guaranteed timetable. This summary covers the examined opening and scaling discussion, not the full accompanying report.

Highlights

  • Essay defines a demanding general capability target
  • Compute extrapolations are hypothetical
  • More copies need not fix missing capabilities
  • Observed scaling laws have limited domains
  • Group coordination can show diminishing returns
  • No guaranteed ASI date is established
