Apple says the HomeView will not be available in the EU because Siri AI is currently unavailable on iOS there and it is "working constructively with the EU"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple says its new HomeView smart home hub will not be available in the EU because Siri AI is currently unavailable on iOS there. Apple is 'working constructively with the EU' but gave no timeline. It costs $299 in the US and launches November 13 in eight countries (MacRumors).
