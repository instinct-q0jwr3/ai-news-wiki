Microsoft launches MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming, a model for low-latency, real-time transcripts, and two new voice models, MAI-Voice-2.1 and MAI-Voice-2.1-Flash

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Microsoft AI launched MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming, a real-time speech-to-text model, plus two voice models, MAI-Voice-2.1 and a faster Flash variant. The transcriber supports 60 languages with continuous language detection and produces first partial hypotheses in just over 100 ms, then revises them and commits a stable transcript.

Microsoft says it ranks first for accuracy on Artificial Analysis and sits on the accuracy-versus-latency frontier, with words appearing about twice as fast as its closest competitor in internal tests. Streaming is priced at an introductory $0.54 per hour of audio through year end. MAI-Voice-2.1 covers 23 languages and 26 locales with one voice speaking each with a native accent.

Highlights

  • MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming: 60 languages, partials in about 100 ms.
  • Claims first place for accuracy on Artificial Analysis; speed claims come from Microsoft's internal evaluations.
  • Introductory price $0.54 per hour of audio until the end of the year.
  • MAI-Voice-2.1 supports 23 languages and 26 locales with a single consistent voice.
  • MAI-Voice-2.1-Flash is the low-latency variant.
