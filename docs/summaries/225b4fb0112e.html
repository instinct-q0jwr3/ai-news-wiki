Nvidia, Oracle, CoreWeave, and other AI stocks fell after a report that OpenAI's annualized revenue at September's end was $20B less than previously reported

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports that Nvidia, Oracle, CoreWeave and other AI stocks fell on Thursday after the market learned more about OpenAI's revenue. OpenAI told investors it reached about $50 billion in annualized revenue at the end of September, lower than the $68 billion figure widely reported late last month.

A source says the $68 billion figure included gross revenue from OpenAI's partners, which allows a more direct comparison with Anthropic. The Financial Times first reported the $50 billion figure.
