Who gets to decide if something is written by AI?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Sean Goedecke argues that arguments about AI-text detection are also arguments about who has authority to judge authorship: communities or software providers. He defends detector use while acknowledging that people who distrust the technology industry may reasonably distrust industry-controlled arbiters too.

This is an opinion essay, not an independent accuracy study of Pangram or proof of AI use by any named writer. Claims about detector reliability, privacy and the insufficiency of ordinary investigation remain the author's views; neither automated labels nor stylistic suspicion alone establish individual misconduct. Related-post previews about watermarking are separate content, not an announcement verified here. No writing sample was submitted to a detector or used to accuse anyone.
