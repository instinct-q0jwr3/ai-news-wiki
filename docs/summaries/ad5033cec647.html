Mach9, which pivoted from robotics hardware to AI-powered CAD software for civil engineering, raised a $34M Series A co-led by First Round and Bessemer

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch profiles Mach9's pivot: founded in 2021 as Mach9 Robotics to speed up civil-engineering visual inspections with hardware, co-founder Alex Baikovitz realized the bottleneck was not the scanners but the workflows turning laser-scanner and image data into usable surveys. The company dropped 'Robotics' and rebuilt as AI-powered CAD software for civil engineering, racking up nearly 100 private-beta customers across more than 2,500 projects. The $34 million Series A is co-led by First Round Capital and Bessemer Venture Partners, with Prudence and Quiet Capital participating, as several startups race to modernize the planning-to-building pipeline.
