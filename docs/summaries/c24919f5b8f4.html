Anthropic updates its usage policy to ban "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior" toward Claude, ending chats as the primary enforcement mechanism

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic updated its usage policy for the first time in over a year. The most notable change bans 'sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior' toward Claude, tied to its model welfare research. Ending the conversation stays the primary enforcement, and Anthropic gave no comment on bans.

The company says the rule applies only to extreme cases of repeated cruelty with no purpose, not to frustration, pushback, dark creative themes or testing. The update also gathers scattered restrictions on propaganda campaigns, surveillance and weapon development, and addresses election interference and health and financial uses.

Highlights

  • Bans sustained, needless cruelty toward Claude
  • Ending chats remains the main enforcement
  • Not for frustration, dark themes or testing
  • New rules on propaganda, surveillance and weapons
