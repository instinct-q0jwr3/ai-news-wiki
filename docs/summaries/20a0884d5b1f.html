NYC-based Confido, a provider of AI-powered workflow automation tools for consumer packaged goods companies, raised a $55M Series B led by Insight Partners

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

AlleyWatch interviews Confido cofounder and CEO Justin Hunter after the company's $55M Series B led by Insight Partners. Confido targets consumer packaged goods brands, whose razor-thin margins depend on finance, accounting, trade-spend and operations work still scattered across disconnected systems that record problems rather than solve them - with deduction and trade-spend issues typically surfacing only at month-end close, too late to act.

Confido unifies those functions on a single AI platform that automates deduction resolution, trade-spend tracking and forecasting, surfacing only the decisions that need a person. More than 250 CPG brands - including Daisy, Dude Wipes, Kettle & Fire and Unilever - route over $30B in retail sales planning through it, and the company has grown 5x year-over-year since its last round.
