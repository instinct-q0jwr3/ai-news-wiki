Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark says "I now think it's more likely we'll see full RSI become possible in 2027", earlier than his previous prediction of 2028

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high

Summary

In Import AI, Jack Clark covers a new benchmark, TasteVal from P-Zero Research, that measures AI research taste. Models appear to improve on it faster since the end of 2025, and one result puts a model at a 2.30x compute multiplier versus the human baseline (95% CI 1.15 to 4.37x) at roughly one thirtieth of the cost per attempt.

Clark ties this to recursive self-improvement: he had flagged creativity as the key variable and now sees creative math breakthroughs plus signs of AI research skill. He says he now thinks full RSI is more likely to become possible in 2027 rather than 2028. The issue also covers a visual-reasoning benchmark, Humanity's Sixth Sense, and Reflection's Beam, a 501B MoE with 23B active parameters that trails the best Chinese models. Basis: the opening and the RSI section of a long newsletter.

Highlights

  • Clark moves his estimate for full recursive self-improvement from 2028 to 2027.
  • TasteVal measures research taste; one model reaches a 2.30x compute multiplier at about 1/30 the cost.
  • Research taste appears to rise faster since late 2025.
  • Humanity's Sixth Sense benchmark shows gaps in intuitive visual reasoning.
  • Reflection's Beam: 501B MoE, 23B active, behind top Chinese models.
