Geely's AI fast charging 'heals' EV batteries for 20% more cycle life

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Geely says its new AI-powered fast charging system can reverse some of the battery damage caused by fast charging, using micro-pulse currents to add up to 20% more cycle life.

Charging a car in under 5 minutes is great, but not if it wears the battery out years early.

Geely claims that its ‘Smart Charging’ addresses that and it is using something that sounds like sorcery.

Highlights

  • Earlier this week, we reported that Geely one-upped BYD with a 2.2 MW charger capable of 4-minute EV charges .
  • The full announcement came on Wednesday in Ningbo, and the fifth-generation Geely Smart Charging Station is rated at up to 2,250 kW peak.
  • In testing, the battery used in the Lynk & Co 10 and Zeekr 001 went from 10% to 70% in 4 minutes 30 seconds, and from 10% to 97% in 8 minutes 40 seconds.
  • Its 1,500 kW Flash Charging network, now at over 10,000 stations , takes Blade Battery 2.0 from 10% to 70% in 5 minutes and to 97% in 9 minutes.
  • The main way fast charging hurts a lithium-ion cell is lithium plating.
  • Geely’s answer is what it calls “lithium-ion pulse restoration technology.” The system sends micro-pulse currents through the cells to “reactivate” the lithium ions that accumulated at the negative electrodes during repeated fast charging.
  • Combined with AI-managed charging, Geely claims this increases battery cycle life by 20%.
  • Geely says it developed a low-rate charging strategy that “gradually” fixes degradation from repeated fast charging while you’re plugged in at home overnight.
