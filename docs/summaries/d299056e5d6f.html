Geely's AI fast charging 'heals' EV batteries for 20% more cycle life

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Geely's fifth-generation Smart Charging Station peaks at 2,250 kW - one-upping BYD's 1,500 kW Flash Charging network - and in real-world tests took the Lynk & Co 10 and Zeekr 001 from 10% to 70% in 4 minutes 30 seconds and to 97% in 8 minutes 40. But Geely spent most of its presentation on battery damage: fast charging causes lithium plating, where ions pile up on the anode surface instead of slotting in, permanently draining capacity.

Its answer is 'lithium-ion pulse restoration': micro-pulse currents that claim to reactivate plated lithium and add up to 20% more cycle life (a best-case figure), plus a low-rate home-charging mode that gradually repairs fast-charging wear overnight. The orchestration brain, Xingrui PowerMind (co-developed with AI startup StepFun), forecasts battery temperature 30 seconds ahead instead of reacting when the pack is already hot, holds an average of 55C during charging with a five-point liquid-cooling chain, and gives every car and battery its own profile. New batteries - a 12C-peak Golden Battery and a 6C Next-Gen Ultra Short Blade for cheaper models - would bring 10-minute-class charging downmarket. Electrek notes Tesla has simply throttled aging packs since 2017, and most US EVs still charge below 350 kW.

Highlights

  • Geely's 2.25MW charger edges BYD: 10-70% in 4:30 and 10-97% in 8:40 on Lynk & Co 10 / Zeekr 001
  • Fast charging's main damage is lithium plating - ions stuck on the anode surface stop cycling and capacity fades
  • 'Pulse restoration' sends micro-pulse currents to reactivate plated lithium, claiming up to 20% more cycle life (best case)
  • A home charging mode slowly repairs fast-charging degradation overnight - fast on trips, heal at home
  • Xingrui PowerMind (with StepFun) coordinates car, battery, charger, cloud, grid and station storage
  • It predicts battery temperature 30 seconds ahead rather than throttling after the pack is hot
  • Industry-first five-point liquid cooling covers storage battery, pile, cable, port and pack
  • Next-Gen Ultra Short Blade Battery (laser-welded tabs, 10% less heat, 6C) targets affordable models
  • Contrast: Tesla has capped Supercharging on high-DCFC packs since 2017; most US EVs top out below 350 kW
