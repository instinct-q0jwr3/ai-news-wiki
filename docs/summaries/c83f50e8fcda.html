GM plans to switch from the NYSE to Nasdaq on November 17 as part of its push under Tesla veteran Sterling Anderson to use AI to speed up its development cycle

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

The Financial Times reports GM plans to move its listing from the NYSE to the Nasdaq on November 17, part of a push under Tesla veteran Sterling Anderson to use AI to speed up its development cycle. The defection is another blow for the Big Board as its tech-focused rival attracts some of the world's most valuable companies.
