Summary

TechCrunch reports a $20 million seed round for Supertake, a waitlisted beta translating users' market theses into stock portfolios through AI agents and brokerage connections. The founders describe trading constraints, asset lists, drift limits and monitoring, while separating current stock portfolios from hoped-for derivatives or prediction-market expansion.

Funding and founder anecdotes about their own assets do not demonstrate performance, suitability or regulatory compliance. Claimed controls on deposits, withdrawals and credentials do not eliminate market losses or establish that the service is safe. The article is product reporting, not investment advice. No brokerage account was connected, funds moved, waitlist joined or trade authorized.
