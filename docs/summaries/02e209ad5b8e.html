Anthropic's corporate structure

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

A LessWrong post describes Anthropic's current, pre-IPO corporate structure, focusing on who controls the business. The board has seven seats: two elected by common shareholders (Dario and Daniela Amodei), one by voting preferred shareholders, and four by the Long Term Benefit Trust, one of which is vacant.

The CEO breaks ties. The author says Anthropic amended its certificate of incorporation recently ahead of an IPO and expects another amendment just before it closes, likely more interesting. The post is a reference piece, not news, and the seat counts and details are the author's reading of the filings.
