Chicago schools to Google – we'd rather lose Search than be forced to accept AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

TechSpot reports that Chicago Public Schools is considering alternative search services if it cannot offer Google Search without AI features. The dispute follows Google describing Search and AI as integrated services and the district seeking ways to suppress AI Overviews for students.

The report describes a conditional response and public protests, not a completed migration away from Google. Its underlying issue is whether schools can choose ordinary search without accepting generative features, a product-bundling and educational-control question rather than a judgment that every classroom use of AI is equivalent.

Highlights

  • District seeks search without AI Overviews
  • Alternative search is a conditional option
  • Report describes protests and policy disagreement
  • No completed migration is established
  • Control over bundled features is the central issue
