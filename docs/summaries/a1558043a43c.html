Asian chipmakers extend Wall Street losses as US Fed rate hike fears and AI funding worries mount; SK Hynix fell 10%+ on the KRX, while Samsung fell ~10%

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

Tech hardware stocks sold off further as Asian and European chipmakers followed Wall Street down. Reasons given are fears of more Fed rate hikes, worries about financing AI investment and rising competition.

SK Hynix fell over 10%, Samsung 8.7%, SoftBank 7.9% and Advantest nearly 14%, while TSMC slipped only 1.8%.

Highlights

  • SK Hynix down over 10%, Samsung 8.7%, Advantest nearly 14%.
  • Rate-hike fears and AI financing worries drive the selloff.
