You don't need long context

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

An arXiv paper argues long context is less needed than assumed. Later tokens cost more to generate than early ones, up to 7x on open-weight frontier models. With frequent compaction, models lose little or no accuracy on long-horizon and long-input tasks, and do not collapse on software engineering even taking one action per context. The authors propose 'semantic state space models' and question the need for long-context models.

Highlights

  • Late tokens can cost up to 7x more
  • Frequent compaction keeps accuracy on long tasks
  • Works even with one action per context
  • Proposes semantic state space models
