Mistral releases Mistral Large 4, dubbed "le Chonk", a 1T-parameter open-weight model for general agentic capabilities, trained on 4,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Mistral launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed le Chonk, a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight model aimed at general agentic work and trained on 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs at what it calls a fraction of rivals' cost. It claims parity with top closed models and competitiveness with open models three times its size. Mistral also pitches security: co-founder Guillaume Lample says open weights let companies build cyber defences without fearing the model will be withdrawn. Mistral's docs list 1.05T total and 49B active parameters.

Highlights

  • Public preview of a 1T-parameter open-weight model.
  • Trained on 4,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs at lower cost, per Mistral.
  • 49B active of 1.05T total parameters, per the docs.
  • Security pitch: open weights cannot be sundowned.
