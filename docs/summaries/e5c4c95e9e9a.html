LFM2.5-24B-A2B

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-24B-A2B, a 24B reasoning model with 2B active parameters for fast, private on-device agents and the last model in the LFM2.5 family, which spans 230M to 24B. It is competitive with Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on five of eight agentic and tool-use benchmarks despite about half the size.

Highlights

  • 24B total, 2B active parameters, aimed at on-device agents.
  • Final model of the LFM2.5 family.
  • Matches Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on five of eight agentic benchmarks.
