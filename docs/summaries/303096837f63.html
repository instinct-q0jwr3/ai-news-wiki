Show HN: Pulse Local, a Mac budgeting app whose AI models run on your machine

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Pulse Local is a Mac budgeting app in beta for Apple Silicon whose chat answers from your own figures using AI models that run entirely on your machine, so neither the ledger nor your questions leave the device. It contrasts with apps that send questions to a hosted model and rely on a privacy policy promise.

Highlights

  • Local-first budgeting app with on-device AI models.
  • Questions never leave the machine.
  • Mac, Apple Silicon, beta.
