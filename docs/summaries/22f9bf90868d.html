Data Processing Incidents on Inference Providers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-22 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenRouter has published a standing announcement on data-processing incidents at inference providers, dated 22 November 2026. It says security researchers reported that certain providers had data compromised or showed evidence that their data policies were not upheld, that OpenRouter is collaborating with the researchers and providers, and that the findings did not show any breach or unauthorized access of OpenRouter itself. The post thanks the Dragos report, says affected users have been notified whether or not a provider posted publicly, and maintains a live list of provider security announcements while encouraging every provider to publish an incident write-up.

This is the routing layer's own response to the canary investigation already covered from Dragos and Ars Technica, not a new experiment or independent finding. The fetched text did not include the specific action items the post says it lists, so the concrete measures could not be itemized here. The statement that no breach of OpenRouter occurred is the company's own account, and exposure of prompts passing through a provider remains separate from compromise of OpenRouter's systems. In the live announcement, OpenRouter says it off-boarded three providers, removed 125 endpoints from ZDR eligibility, including precautionary removals, and notified potentially affected customers. It says ongoing checks will examine forwarding paths and that restoring eligibility requires assurances and validation. These are the company's reported actions, not an independent verification of their completeness.
