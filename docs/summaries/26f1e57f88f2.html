Fireworks AI security breach impacts Notion and their customers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Notion says a breach at its vendor Fireworks AI may have exposed some Notion AI chat transcripts. It estimates under 1% of workspaces are affected. The chats came from the Insights feature, were 'unattributable' (no link to a user, workspace or company) and contained no tool-call results; no other workspace data is affected.

Fireworks first warned of incidents earlier this month and Notion shut off its connections. On November 6 Fireworks said attackers may have reached customer data, and on November 8 confirmed the dataset that included the chats. Notion disabled Insights and stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks. Affected workspaces will be contacted.

Highlights

  • Under 1% of Notion workspaces may be affected
  • Exposed: unattributable Notion AI chats from Insights
  • No workspace, user or tool-result data tied to them
  • Timeline: Nov 6 notice, Nov 8 confirmation
  • Notion cut Fireworks and disabled Insights
