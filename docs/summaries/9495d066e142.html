China's export curbs on critical metals have disrupted supplies of micro drill bits made mainly from tungsten carbide, used in PCBs and chip substrates

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Nikkei Asia reports that China's export controls on critical metals have disrupted supplies of micro drill bits, made mainly from tungsten carbide, which drill tiny holes in printed circuit boards and chip substrates.
