Pluralistic: Enshittifiers groom us for scammers (18 Nov 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow argues that chaotic official security procedures make scams harder to distinguish from legitimate requests. The essay connects breaches, weakened support and inconsistent authentication flows with a public trained to expect strange demands, then calls for better maintained institutions rather than impossible vigilance from every individual.

This is an argumentative essay built around reported examples and personal experience, not a measured causal study of fraud rates. This summary covers the examined main-argument passages; linked allegations, legal claims and individual accounts were not independently verified. Its descriptions are not instructions to weaken security or change authentication tools.

Highlights

  • Essay connects institutional disorder with plausible impersonation
  • Author criticizes changing authentication flows
  • Personal banking experience is one example
  • Better support is proposed as an anti-fraud measure
  • Causal claims are argument rather than experimental evidence
