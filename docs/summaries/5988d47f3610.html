Zombie stats: Where the numbers AI quotes come from

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

An investigation asked ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google's AI Overviews 200 everyday questions that call for a statistic - a share, rate, average, count or cost stated as fact - and then followed the citations all the way down. The opening example: the claim that 44 percent of salespeople give up after one follow-up, a number whose trail says a lot about where AI-quoted statistics actually come from.

Highlights

  • 200 numeric questions posed to five AI assistants
  • Method: follow each citation chain to its origin
  • Example: the '44% of salespeople give up after one follow-up' statistic
  • Basis: article introduction; the fetched page yielded only the opening section (534 chars)
