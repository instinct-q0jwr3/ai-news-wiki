A closer look at Apple's Siri home hub; Fitness+ staff cuts

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme apple labor-displacement

Summary

Mark Gurman's Power On newsletter details Apple's upcoming Siri-powered home hub, codenamed J490, now being tested widely in employees' homes ahead of release. The device matches the 'intelligent personal hub' vision new CEO John Ternus laid out in his first keynote: pervasive connectivity, an advanced display, camera and microphones, and an interface that mashes up iOS, tvOS and watchOS, syncing with an iPhone for setup.

The roughly square screen comes in stand (like a HomePod mini chopped in half) or wall-mount versions - closest to an Amazon Echo Show but with a smaller display. A headline feature is facial recognition through the front camera, personalizing apps, notes, calendar, news, music and podcasts for whoever is looking at it - notably without a Face ID-level depth sensor. Apple imagines users asking Siri to surface content across their devices and accounts, like a podcast someone sent them weeks ago. The same issue also covers job cuts at Fitness+ while Apple ponders the service's future, the fate of the iPhone Duo Pencil, and an imminent Apple Pay launch in India.

Highlights

  • Apple's home hub (J490) is in wide employee-home testing ahead of release
  • New CEO John Ternus framed the 'intelligent personal hub' vision in his first keynote
  • Interface blends iOS, tvOS and watchOS; pairs with an iPhone for setup
  • Stand and wall-mount versions; closest analog is a smaller-screened Amazon Echo Show
  • Front-camera facial recognition personalizes content per viewer - without a depth sensor
  • Siri on the hub reaches across the user's devices and accounts (e.g. 'play the podcast someone sent me')
  • Also in the newsletter: Fitness+ job cuts, the iPhone Duo Pencil's fate, Apple Pay India launch imminent
