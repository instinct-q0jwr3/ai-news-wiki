Anthropic tests Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 ahead of the release

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

tldr-ai anthropic

Summary

Testing Catalog reports that Anthropic appears to be quietly A/B testing Claude Fable 5.2 in the wild, weeks after launching Fable 5.1: users on X shared outputs from standard prompts that seemed far more advanced than typical Claude results, including a ship-shooter game with a video demo produced 'one shot' in pure JS. Anthropic has not commented.

The report also says Anthropic is testing Opus 5.5 ahead of release. Users sharing the alleged outputs noted the model added unprompted polish - in one clip, every animated animal got its own customized snack.
