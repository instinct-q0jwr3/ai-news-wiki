Q&A with Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar on the Iran school strike and AI targeting, the DOD dropping Anthropic, AI liability, effective altruism, and more

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

In a Face the Nation interview, Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar fields CBS polling showing 50% of Americans think AI will create more problems than it solves and 45% want government restrictions. His answer: the burden is on the tech industry to show real benefits - job growth and prosperity, not travel scheduling - and he rejects the 'no guardrails' framing while arguing an unseen force drives the safety narrative, which he describes as a millenarian doomsday cult.

The wide-ranging transcript also covers the Iran school strike and AI targeting, the DOD dropping Anthropic, AI liability, and effective altruism - Sankar defending Palantir's role at the intersection of AI and state power.
