Avicena, which links AI chips with microLEDs instead of lasers, raised a $100M+ Series C led by Intervest, with participation from SK Hynix, Samsung, and AMD

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

The Next Web reports Avicena raising more than $100 million in a Series C led by InterVest and naming Kevin Pope chief executive as it prepares its first product for next year. Its LightBundle approach distributes optical data across many microLED channels rather than a few fast laser channels.

Reported evaluation-kit shipments differ from volume product availability. Energy and memory-interconnect advantages remain vendor and investor claims rather than an independent measured comparison, and a memory-shortage forecast is not a certainty. The funding amount does not establish valuation, revenue or commercial success. No component was purchased or tested.
