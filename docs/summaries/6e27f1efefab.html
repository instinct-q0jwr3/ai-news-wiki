Who wants to crowdsource LLM scans of articles submitted to Lobste.rs?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-08 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

A Lobsters thread proposes a shared index of Pangram AI-detection scans of articles posted to the site, so people do not each pay for their own scans. The author has a prototype publishing his own results and could add contributors with their own API keys.

Commenters dispute whether the scans can be trusted. One says detectors are better than a coin toss but prone to false positives, especially on pre-LLM text. Another replies that Pangram's technical report documents methodology and false positives are rare, though the rate counts light AI wording tweaks as true positives. A third objects on principle to having writing approved by a machine.

Highlights

  • Idea: crowdsource and centralize Pangram scans of submitted articles.
  • Cost is the motivation; scanning is expensive.
  • Disputed: false-positive rate and methodology.
  • Rare false positives are claimed, but AI-tweaked wording counts as positive.
  • Some object to machine style approval on principle.
