Meta’s Muse Logomark, Designed by Jessica Hische

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

daring-fireball meta amazon muse agentic-systems

Summary

Bill McCool, writing at Dieline: This past Wednesday, lettering artist, designer, and author Jessica Hische took to Threads to announce that she had worked alongside Meta’s team to create the icon and logotype for their agentic AI, Muse.

Acknowledging that people would be “mad because it’s AI and Meta,” Hische sought to explain at least why she had taken the gig in the first place.

Obviously, she took some flak for working with Meta.
