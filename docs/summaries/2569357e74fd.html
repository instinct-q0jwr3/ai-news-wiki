Apple adds a beta AI assistant built on Apple and OpenAI models to Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Logic Pro, plus MCP support to Keynote, Pages, and Numbers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple is adding a beta AI assistant built on Apple and OpenAI models to Keynote, Pages, Numbers and Logic Pro. It drafts and restructures text in Pages, organizes data and suggests charts in Numbers, and designs and refines slides in Keynote; Logic Pro's version can set up a vocal track from a single prompt.

Keynote, Pages and Numbers also gain Model Context Protocol support on macOS 27, letting third-party AI agents plug into the apps. The assistant requires Apple Intelligence-compatible devices on iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or macOS 27. Related updates: Pixelmator Pro gets a Generate Fill feature, and Final Cut Pro gains MotionVFX templates and a built-in Content Hub; Creator's Studio updates are free to subscribers in November.

Highlights

  • Beta AI assistant across Keynote, Pages, Numbers and Logic Pro
  • Built on Apple and OpenAI models; needs iOS/iPadOS/macOS 27
  • MCP support lets third-party agents plug into the iWork apps
  • Pixelmator Pro Generate Fill and Final Cut Pro Content Hub also announced
