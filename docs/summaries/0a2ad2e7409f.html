AI Crawler Index

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Mojodojo's AI Crawler Index reports bot traffic on its own site. AI crawlers made 12,650 of 54,295 counted bot hits (23.3%), against 26.1% for search engines, so AI bots made 0.89 hits per search-engine hit.

Training crawlers account for 16.3% of hits, AI search 5.2% and live assistant fetches 996 pages. Of hits claiming to be a verified bot, 13.2% came from the wrong IP, and 396 spoofed hits were removed from the shares. This is one site's data, so it does not generalize.

Highlights

  • AI crawlers are 23.3% of bot hits on this site
  • Training bots 16.3%, AI search 5.2%
  • 996 live fetches by assistants answering questions
  • 13.2% of verifiable bot claims came from the wrong IP
  • Single-site data
