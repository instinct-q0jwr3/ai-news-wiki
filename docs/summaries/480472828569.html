Summary

Bert Hubert's Chatcontrol 2025 brief, in its Esperanto translation (one of a dozen community translations he links and hosts). Same content as the English original: led by Denmark, many EU states want WhatsApp/Signal to scan all photos and links with AI, reporting 'doubtful' images plus location and phone number to a new EU Centre, Europol and local police - with Signal threatening to leave the EU and governments exempting their own police and military.
