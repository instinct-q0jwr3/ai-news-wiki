NVIDIA's Long-Video and World-Action Research

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

NVIDIA's NVlabs published a repository of long-video generation and world-action modeling research. Each project (Long-WAM, LongLive-Plug, LongLive 2.0, LongLive 1.0) has its own code, docs and weights and can be fetched alone with a sparse checkout.

The README lists public checkpoints and speed figures, with LongLive 2.0 timings on a single GB200 and NVFP4 runs that also quantize the KV cache. LongLive-Plug adds adapters for Wan and MiniMax-H3 few-step and guidance-free setups.

Highlights

  • NVIDIA long-video and world-action research repo
  • Four self-contained projects
  • Public checkpoints released
  • LongLive 2.0 timed on one GB200, with NVFP4
  • Adapters for Wan and MiniMax-H3
