Google DeepMind says four Gemini 4 Argon agents beat Minecraft without any human help in 27 hours and 14 minutes, costing $1,620 at Argon's planned 2027 price

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high

Summary

The Decoder reports that four agents running on Google's Gemini 4 Argon have beaten Minecraft for the first time without any human help, killing the Ender Dragon in a fully autonomous run of 27 hours and 14 minutes. The DeepMind team dropped the agents into a random world with no assigned roles, strategies or game-specific tools; the agents split tasks themselves, warned each other about threats, recovered items from dead teammates and logged mistakes in a shared memory. Along the way they wrote 2,783 JavaScript programs, exchanged 229 chat messages and died 605 times. At Argon's planned 2027 standard price, the run cost $1,620. Minecraft is the latest in a line of games that needed purpose-built AI systems a few years ago and now fall to general agentic models.
