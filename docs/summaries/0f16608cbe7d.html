Don't build tools for AI agents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

seangoedecke agentic-systems

Summary

Sean Goedecke on two adjacent debates. First, the Anthropic researcher's resignation tweet that revived AI-apocalypse talk - 'the people building AI earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade' - and the skeptical readings: PR for regulation, self-promotion, stock-pumping, or proof-by-absurdity ('if you believed it you'd be bombing datacenters, not posting').

Second, his title argument: lots of people now say we should stop building software for humans and start building for AI agents. It sort of makes sense - his own agents already use Datadog more than he does, purely by moving faster through more things. The essay pushes back on where that logic ends.
