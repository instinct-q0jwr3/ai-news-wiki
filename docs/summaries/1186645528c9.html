Meta expands its AI age estimation tech globally to apply Instagram's Teen Account protections for millions of users from 13 to 17 who misrepresented their age

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Meta is expanding globally its AI age estimation, which spots likely teens on Instagram who gave a false adult age and moves them into Teen Accounts. The accounts are private by default, limit content and block DMs from strangers; users 16 and under need parental permission to change settings. Meta also uses Apple's age-range signal in Australia, will notify US parents next month, and is bringing it to Facebook and Threads. It follows an August $17.1 billion settlement requiring stronger age assurance. The Verge corrected that this tool does not remove under-13 accounts.

Highlights

  • Global rollout of AI age estimation for Instagram Teen Accounts
  • Applies to teens who misstated their age
  • Parents in the US to be notified next month
  • Follows an August $17.1B settlement
  • Correction: under-13 removal is a separate process
