I Have a Confession: I Built This Site with AI – Please Forgive Me

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

A developer with a PhD in Instrumentation Physics confesses that his site's engine is built entirely with AI - though none of the content is. He describes himself as a scientist who ended up in software, self-taught through reading, open source (Mojolicious), and conferences, specializing in low-level protocols and async patterns across Perl, PHP, Python, Node, Java and more, coding in Vim and recently working with Kubernetes.

He frames the post as 'a long and somewhat sad story' about how that craft changed a few years ago, and asks the reader's forgiveness.
