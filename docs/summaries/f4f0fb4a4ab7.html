AI models think humans are a bunch of amoral sheep

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Press coverage of the ModelCompare experiment in which 12 LLMs from ten labs in three countries were asked for their most controversial take on humanity - and converged on the same answer: humans are not as rational as they think; people are dumb, panicky, group-loyal animals who pick their group's beliefs and invent reasons afterward. The Register-style write-up opens with Men in Black's Kay: 'A person is smart. People are dumb, panicky dangerous animals.'

The coverage treats the convergence as equal parts amusement and warning: every model independently produced a variation on conformism, which either says something true about humans or - more skeptically - something about the shared corpus of human self-criticism the models all read. Either way, an analytics firm best known for capability rankings found its most-shared result in what models think of us.

Highlights

  • ModelCompare asked 12 LLMs from 10 labs for their most controversial take on humanity
  • Every model converged: humans are group-loyal conformists, less rational than they believe
  • Framed with Men in Black's 'people are dumb, panicky dangerous animals'
  • Convergence could reflect human nature or just the shared corpus of human self-criticism
  • The ranking firm's most-shared result is about model attitudes, not capabilities
