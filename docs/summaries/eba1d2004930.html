Show HN: Koi.rest – watch some fish and regain your balance

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

In a Show HN post, developer Paul introduces Koi.rest, a virtual zen garden where visitors watch koi fish to regain their balance. He explains the personal context: he has ADHD, has been unemployed since the beginning of August, and the stress of the past year made him want to build the calming project he had been postponing.

The twist is the process: he does not know JavaScript and says he lacked the capacity to learn it right now, so after letting 'perfect be the enemy of good' for a while, he used AI to make the thing he really wanted - tweaking, adding, removing, drawing, researching, questioning and testing without being the one writing the code.
