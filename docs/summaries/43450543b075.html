Bessent: US and China agree to set up an AI dialogue

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme external-evaluation

Summary

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says the United States proposed an AI incident notification mechanism to China, and both sides agreed to set up an AI dialogue ahead of the Trump-Xi summit, the Financial Times reports. Bessent said the two sides are working towards a 'shared vision' after talks with his Chinese counterpart.

Highlights

  • The US proposed an AI incident notification mechanism to China.
  • Both sides agreed to establish an AI dialogue ahead of the Trump-Xi summit.
  • Bessent described progress towards a 'shared vision' after talks with his counterpart.
