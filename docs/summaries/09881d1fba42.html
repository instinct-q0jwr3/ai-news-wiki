Jev was built for agents, here's how we're using it in computer use instead

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high

Summary

Kyle Jeong explains Jev, TypeSafe's class of 'System One' AI models built for fast, structured decisions that software can consume directly: state in, typed answers with probabilities out. TypeSafe claims Jev is 20-200x faster and 40-400x cheaper than LLMs, positioning it as a frontier-intelligence function call rather than a chat model.

Jev exposes three primitives - Choice, Score and Noul - and the article walks through a customer-support example where one call routes a message to the right department and detects an explicit refund request. Jeong's goal is to teach what Jev is, why it exists, and how to introduce it into production systems.
