Meta, Walmart, Stripe, and others publish the Personal Agent Protocol to standardize and secure interactions between AI bots and businesses

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

A month after Meta's Muse personal agent became a viral hit topping the App Store, Meta, Walmart, Stripe, Sierra and others are publishing the Personal Agent Protocol - an open standard for how AI agents interact with businesses. Bret Taylor, Sierra co-founder and OpenAI chairman, is leading the effort: businesses need to know when they're dealing with a personal agent versus a person, and to block random bots not acting on anyone's behalf. 'It is kind of chaos until such a standard exists.'

Taylor compares the design to Login with Google or Facebook, which he worked on at Facebook: authentication plus visibility into what agents actually do. The backdrop includes Amazon blocking Meta's agents over scraping worries - and separately blocking and suing Perplexity for allegedly concealing its agents to keep scraping.
