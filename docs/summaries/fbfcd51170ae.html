NJ-based AidenAI, which helps enterprises integrate AI into apps, data infrastructure, and business workflows, raised a $32M Series A led by A91 Partners

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

Inc42 reports AidenAI raising $32 million in Series A financing led by A91 Partners. The New Jersey-based company combines enterprise AI integration with software modernization through its AI Builder Platform and AiDAP, with much of its workforce in Hyderabad. It says it serves more than thirty enterprise clients across six countries.

The funding is reported as a completed round, but customer outcomes remain company and investor claims rather than an independent deployment audit. Building a workforce of more than one thousand frontier AI engineers through an academy is a plan, not the current verified headcount. The article's market growth figures are projections and do not establish the startup's future revenue or ability to standardize across clients.

Highlights

  • Reported round is $32 million Series A
  • A91 Partners leads the financing
  • Integration targets existing enterprise applications and infrastructure
  • AiDAP is positioned for legacy modernization
  • Client and geographic reach are attributed claims
  • Thousand-engineer academy target is prospective
  • No audited customer return or revenue forecast is established
