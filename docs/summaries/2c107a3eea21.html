Katie Notopoulos on Alexandr Wang’s ‘Faux-Hallmark Pap’

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

Katie Notopoulos quote-posts Alexandr Wang's launch essay 'Why We're Building Muse' and lets it have it: 'This makes me feel insane!' The essay's greeting-card humanism - 'Here's to the wanting. Here's to the dreaming. Here's to everyone,' and its claims about helping people spend more time with family or open a bakery - is, for her, 'faux-Hallmark pap.'

Her kicker is the memorable part: anyone who believes for a second that the pitch is about family time and bakeries doesn't need to wait for AI to kill them, being 'stupid enough to drown from looking up at the rain.' It's a snapshot of the reception scale-AI marketing now gets from tech press veterans: the loftier the sentiment, the louder the gag reflex.
