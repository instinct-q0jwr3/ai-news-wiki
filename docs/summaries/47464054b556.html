Partnering with Accenture on Embedded Evaluation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high

hacker-news external-evaluation

Summary

Anthropic is partnering with Accenture, through its specialist AI business Faculty, on independent evaluation of frontier AI, a concrete step toward the commitment in CEO Dario Amodei's 'We Must Pace the Frontier' essay to embed evaluators inside the company. The scope covers evaluating and red-teaming models, conducting alignment assessments and testing model safeguards, and both companies expect to invest at least $1 billion each in this capacity over the next five years.

Embedded evaluation is framed as a new category: evaluators work inside the AI company with employee-level access, watching models take shape in training, following build and deployment decisions, and reporting incidents to give the public a more informed account of risks. Anthropic stresses the evaluators do not reduce its own accountability, acknowledges there are no standards or settled funding model yet (it will fund Accenture directly for now), and says the partnership is non-exclusive, with more evaluators to be announced and talks underway with METR and other nonprofits.

Highlights

  • Accenture partnership, led by its Faculty AI business, covers red-teaming, alignment assessments and safeguard testing.
  • Both Anthropic and Accenture expect to invest at least $1 billion each in evaluation capacity over five years.
  • Embedded evaluators get access comparable to an employee's: training visibility, decision processes, direct employee contact.
  • Anthropic will fund Accenture's work directly; it argues long-term funding should be pooled or governmental (per its June Advanced AI Framework).
  • Non-exclusive: Anthropic will announce more evaluators in coming weeks; Accenture will work with other AI developers.
  • Anthropic is also in dialogue with METR and other nonprofit evaluators on pilots using their own funding.
  • Framed as delivering on the 'We Must Pace the Frontier' commitment to embed evaluators within Anthropic.
