The US DOJ indicts three foreign nationals for allegedly using AI to impersonate CEOs and attorneys at major international law firms in a $129M+ fraud scheme

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high

Summary

NBC News reports the US DOJ indicted three foreign nationals for a scheme that used AI to impersonate corporate executives and attorneys at major international law firms and steal over $129 million. The indictment in the Southern District of New York says it ran from July 2024 to February 2026, using AI to imitate voices. Jacob Soall was arrested in Spain in June, Sharon Hazan in Spain in August and Guy Sheetrit in the UK in September. These are allegations.

Highlights

  • Three charged over an alleged $129M+ AI impersonation fraud
  • Ran July 2024 to February 2026 per the indictment
  • Arrests in Spain (June, August) and the UK (September)
  • Allegations, not convictions
