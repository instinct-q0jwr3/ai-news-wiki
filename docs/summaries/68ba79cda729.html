Outmarket, which uses AI to help insurance agencies and brokers automate their tedious paperwork, raised a $34.5M Series B, a source says at a $335M valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports that Outmarket, founded in late 2023 by former Ethos product lead Vishal Sankhla (ex-Facebook, ex-Uber), raised a $34.5 million Series B at a reported $335 million valuation. Outmarket uses AI to automate paperwork for insurance agencies and brokers, focusing on commercial insurance, where policies require deep customization; Sankhla notes 95% of insurance is still sold through human agents through highly manual processes.

Highlights

  • Outmarket raises $34.5M Series B at a reported $335M valuation
  • AI automation for insurance agency and broker paperwork
  • Focus: commercial insurance (general liability, workers' comp, D&O)
  • Founder Vishal Sankhla, ex-Ethos/Facebook/Uber
  • Sankhla: 95% of insurance still sold via human agents, mostly manual
