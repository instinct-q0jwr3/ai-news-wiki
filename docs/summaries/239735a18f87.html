Launch HN: Kino AI (YC S23) – AI media manager and video editor

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

Kino lets you make great videos with your agents and your teammates.

Storage of footage and assets, a rich video editor, and review links for teammates are all bundled into one.

See a basic overview at https://kino.ai/walkthrough-video and our launch video (made entirely with Kino!) at https://kino.ai/launch-video Opus 5.5 can edit videos and make great motion graphics now.

Highlights

  • Your colleagues and Codex / Claude Code can work on the same timeline with review notes and footage all in one place.
  • This was hard to get right, as we found ourselves building a what-you-see-is-what-you-get editor for React, which we then turned into a video editor.
  • For our launch video I told Claude-with-Kino to find a macOS Leopard install disk and reskin our whole video to match.
  • To make this code-first editor work with video footage, everything gets transcribed, embedded, described, and face-detected.
  • This local scrubbing plus custom pro media decoders means you can drag a 250GB ProRes clip from your new iPhone right into your Kino timeline.
