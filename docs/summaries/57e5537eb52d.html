Siri AI Expands to More Languages in OS 27.2

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

With the 27.2 software updates, Apple's rebuilt Siri AI expanded to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. The new Siri draws on personal context across messages, email and photos, handles systemwide app actions like drafting emails or editing photo sets, uses onscreen awareness, and taps broad world knowledge.

Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese follow by year's end. Notably, Siri AI remains unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, with Apple saying it is 'working constructively with the EU' on the matter.

Highlights

  • Siri AI now in French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish
  • Personal context, onscreen awareness and systemwide app actions
  • Four more languages promised by year-end
  • Still unavailable in the EU; Apple cites ongoing talks
