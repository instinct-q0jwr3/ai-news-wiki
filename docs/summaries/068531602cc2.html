Bessent: the Hugging Face hack 'is the responsibility of OpenAI management'

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the July hacking incident involving OpenAI's Hugging Face presence 'is the responsibility of the OpenAI management, not a bunch of agents,' Bloomberg reports. His comments assign accountability for the incident to the company's leadership rather than to autonomous systems.

Highlights

  • Bessent said the Hugging Face incident is OpenAI management's responsibility.
  • He explicitly rejected blaming 'a bunch of agents'.
  • The incident occurred in July and involved OpenAI's Hugging Face activity.
