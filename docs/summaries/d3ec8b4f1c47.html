Analysis: total net profit at ~190 chipmakers listed in mainland China rose 620% YoY in H1 2026, driven by the AI boom and China's self-sufficiency campaign

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Nikkei analysis: earnings at roughly 190 chipmakers listed in mainland China grew more than 600% in the first half, buoyed by the AI boom and the national self-sufficiency campaign, with the bonanza spilling over to chip tool makers.

AMEC saw net profit quadruple in the first half and exhibited at an August chipmaking-equipment trade show, alongside Naura, as equipment vendors ride the same wave as the fabs they supply.

Highlights

  • Net profit at ~190 mainland-listed chipmakers up over 600% in H1.
  • Driven by the AI boom and China's self-sufficiency campaign.
  • AMEC net profit quadrupled; gains spilling over to tool makers Naura and AMEC.
  • Reported from Shanghai by Nikkei.
