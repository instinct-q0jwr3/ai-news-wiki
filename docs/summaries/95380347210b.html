Apple Begins Opening Siri AI to Developers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple Developer: Starting today with the iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, macOS 27.3, and visionOS 27.3 betas, a new Developer Preview enables any developer to integrate their app with Siri AI, giving businesses and developers new ways to reach…

With this early Developer Preview, your app can work directly with Siri AI, giving you a first look at new ways to reach and serve your customers.

This preview provides early access before first shipping to customers in a few months; and with submission to the App Store opening up in the spring, developers have time to explore, build, and help shape their experiences before launch.
