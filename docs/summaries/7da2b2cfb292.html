BREAKING: Secret US AI evaluation framework has been partly revealed

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

marcus-on-ai external-evaluation

Summary

Gary Marcus: while Trump proclaims violent opposition to regulating AI, his administration is in fact running a secret framework for evaluating which 'frontier' models may be released - and almost nobody knows what's in it. Protect Democracy sued under FOIA, and to Marcus's astonishment the government responded promptly with 132 pages.

The pages set the tone with their first one: nearly everything is redacted. The only real revelations are that OSTP director Michael Kratsios and US CTO Ethan Klein are in the conversation. Marcus's dry conclusion: US policy on screening AI 'remains opaque for another day' - while Protect Democracy's counsel vows to keep litigating, arguing that secret criteria known only to a few powerful actors are fertile ground for corruption and autocratic abuse.

  • Only names revealed: OSTP's Michael Kratsios and CTO Ethan Klein
