Anthropic's S-1 implies a $40T+ annual TAM based on knowledge work's economic value in 2025, topping SpaceX's $28.5T estimate, without explicitly naming TAM

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

The Wall Street Journal calculates that Anthropic's S-1 implies an annual total addressable market above $40 trillion, based on the economic value of knowledge work in 2025 - topping even SpaceX's $28.5 trillion estimate from earlier this year - though the filing never names the figure as TAM.
