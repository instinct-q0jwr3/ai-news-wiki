Microsoft Plugs Nearly 1,000 Security Holes

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

krebs-on-security microsoft ai-safety-incidents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Krebs on Security: Microsoft's September Patch Tuesday fixes at least 974 vulnerabilities - by far its largest batch ever, obliterating July's record of 570 and pushing 2026 past 2,600 CVEs, more than double the previous record year. Two zero-days are being actively exploited (both privilege escalation), 113 bugs are critical, including a wormable DNS flaw (CVE-2026-69730) and a 9.8 CVSS Windows Shell RCE (CVE-2026-69829).

Microsoft credits AI-assisted discovery for the accelerating find rate, and it's not alone - Adobe, Cisco, Google, Mozilla and Oracle all report the same, with Google moving to security updates every two weeks. Experts' caveats: Tenable's Satnam Narang says AI is 'creating larger haystacks, not finding more needles' - organizations must prioritize by actual reachability; and Fortra's Tyler Reguly warns the human side (testing and deploying) is now the bottleneck, telling CISOs to budget dinners for weekend patching teams.
