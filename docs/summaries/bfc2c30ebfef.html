Put a price on breakthroughs

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Alex Wang argues that neolabs, the new AI startups pursuing big research bets, are being asked to pull off two moonshots at once: a research breakthrough and a venture-scale business, each alone a 1-in-100 outcome, making the pair a 1-in-10,000 ask. His alternative borrows from how pharma handles risky R&D.

The diagnosis: frontier labs like OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind are locked in a model-quality race that pushes resources toward proven scaling work, while senior researchers who want to pursue discontinuous new paradigms leave to found neolabs. Putting a price on breakthroughs, he argues, would let frontier labs, neolabs and investors all come out ahead.

Highlights

  • Neolabs face a 1-in-10,000 double moonshot
  • Frontier labs' race favors proven scaling over new paradigms
  • Senior researchers leave to found neolabs for big bets
  • A pharma-style model prices breakthroughs to share risk
  • Proposal aims to serve labs, neolabs and investors at once
