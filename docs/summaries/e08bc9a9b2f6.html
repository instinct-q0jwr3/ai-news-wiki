Filing: AMD will pay ~$2.9B in cash and stock for AI chip startup Taalas, with $1.4B dependent on unspecified conditions; the deal was announced in August

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-04 · confidence: high

Summary

AMD disclosed in a filing that it will pay about US$2.9 billion in cash and stock for Toronto AI-chip startup Taalas, with $1.4 billion contingent on unspecified conditions. The deal, announced in August, has not closed and needs regulatory approval. The Globe and Mail calls it one of the largest takeovers of a Canadian tech company, second only to the $4.75 billion Ecolab-CoolIT Systems deal in March and above Nasdaq's $2.75 billion Verafin purchase in 2020.
