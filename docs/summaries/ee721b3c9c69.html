AI: Guaranteed to disrupt our economies

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Bert Hubert (English version of a Dutch post) sets out simple, malice-free scenarios in which AI still disrupts economies horribly. After mocking the prediction business - 'anyone making ChatGPT forecasts is now making a total fool of themselves' - he makes his own: no evil plans needed from humans or AI for things to go badly wrong.

He sidesteps the 'truly intelligent' debate and the error-rate argument to focus on structural economic disruption from systems that merely work as advertised.

Highlights

  • He mocks AI forecasting, including his own, citing how bad humans are at prediction
