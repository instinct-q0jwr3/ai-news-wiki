Enterprise voice AI tools provider Deepgram acquires Daily, which has raised $62M+ and develops open-source voice AI framework Pipecat, for an undisclosed sum

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-01 · updated: 2026-11-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Voice AI consolidation continues: Deepgram is acquiring Daily, the maker of Pipecat, one of the most-used open-source voice AI frameworks. Pipecat orchestrates the real-time pipeline - voice and video streams, speech-to-text, LLMs, text-to-speech - with latency as the core metric, and Daily sells hosted call infrastructure underneath it. The deal amount is undisclosed, but the strategic terms are public: Pipecat stays open source and vendor-neutral, a commitment Daily co-founder Kwindla Kramer says was decisive in choosing Deepgram over other suitors. Both companies came through the same YC batch in 2016.

The shape of the combined company: Deepgram brings speech models and fresh unicorn status from a $130 million Series C earlier this year; Daily brings over $62 million raised from a cap table including Tiger Global, Nat Friedman and Kevin Weil, plus the real-time infrastructure know-how. Kramer frames the open-source commitment as both personal and commercial - sitting vendor-neutral in the middle of a fast-growing ecosystem is worth more than locking the framework down. The acquisition is one of several recent moves as voice AI players consolidate ahead of the next platform fight.

Highlights

  • Deepgram acquires Daily, maker of the Pipecat open-source voice AI framework
  • Pipecat orchestrates STT, LLM and TTS over real-time voice/video streams with low latency
  • Terms undisclosed; Pipecat stays open source and vendor-neutral - the deciding condition for Daily's founders
  • Daily raised $62M+ (Tiger Global, Nat Friedman, Kevin Weil); Deepgram hit unicorn status with a $130M Series C this year
  • Both companies are from the same YC 2016 batch
  • Part of a broader voice AI consolidation wave
