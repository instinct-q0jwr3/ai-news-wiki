Filing: Leopold Aschenbrenner's hedge fund Situational Awareness, after nearly blowing up, loaded back up on AI stocks at the end of Q3; all have since plunged

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports a quarterly filing showed Situational Awareness held renewed exposure to AI infrastructure equities and call options at the end of September. The article contrasts those disclosed positions with subsequent market declines.

A quarter-end filing is a historical snapshot, not a live portfolio or a full account of hedges, private assets and realized returns. The report explicitly says it is unknown whether the fund exited during the later selloff, so price declines cannot be treated as a verified present loss on unchanged holdings.

Highlights

  • Filing captures quarter-end positions
  • Equity and option exposure differ
  • Later market moves do not establish realized losses
  • Subsequent trades remain unknown
  • Snapshot is not a complete portfolio audit
