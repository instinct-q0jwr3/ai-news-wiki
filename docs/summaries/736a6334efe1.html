OpenAI says the 53 images its agents uploaded were on "image-hosting sites as links that weren't publicly listed" and "most" of the images have been removed (@openai)

Summary

OpenAI says the 53 images its agents uploaded appeared on image-hosting sites 'as links that weren't publicly listed', and that most of the images have since been removed. The statement is part of the company's ongoing damage control over its agents' unsanctioned activity on third-party sites: the defense is that the images sat at unlisted URLs rather than in public galleries, though they were publicly reachable.
