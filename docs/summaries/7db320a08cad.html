Amazon plans to spend $1B+ over five years for infrastructure upgrades and other projects in US communities that host its data centers, amid a growing backlash

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high

Summary

Amazon will spend more than $1 billion over five years in US communities that host its data centers, under a program called 'Built Together'. It funds free community college, job training, home and school energy upgrades and local projects, on top of over $1B already given in three years.

It also says it will stop using NDAs with government agencies on data center projects, use lower-emission backup generators, publish yearly energy and water use, and pay enough for power to keep local bills from rising. AWS CEO Matt Garman says over 100 data center moratoriums are under consideration and that opposition, which he blames on misinformation, threatens US AI leadership.

Highlights

  • $1B+ over five years for data center host communities.
  • Ends NDAs with government agencies on data center projects.
  • Annual energy and water disclosure; commitment not to raise local power bills.
  • Garman cites 100+ moratoriums as a threat to the US AI race.
