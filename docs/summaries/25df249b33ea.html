Hadrian, an agentic AI offensive security service, raised $40M co-led by Forgepoint Capital International and SmartFin, taking its total funding to $65M

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Hadrian, which sells an agentic AI platform that emulates hackers' attack paths to find and rank exploitable risks on an organisation's external attack surface, raised $40 million co-led by Forgepoint Capital International and SmartFin, bringing its total to $65 million. HV Capital, Motive Partners, Picus Capital and Oetker Ventures also took part. The pitch is that attackers already automate with AI while many security teams still rely on manual penetration tests and drown in alerts.

Highlights

  • $40M round co-led by Forgepoint and SmartFin; $65M total.
  • Platform maps the attack surface and ranks real risks.
  • Pitch: AI-speed attackers vs. manual pen tests.
