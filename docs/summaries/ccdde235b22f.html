The US stood alone in dismissing AI safety concerns at the UN, leaving nations to set nonbinding rules without buy-in from the home of the biggest AI companies

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme ai-safety-incidents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

The US stood alone at the UN General Assembly in dismissing AI safety concerns, leaving other nations to weigh going ahead without it. The White House line, repeated in New York and in meetings with Xi Jinping in Washington this week: US companies lead in AI, they plan to keep it that way, and Washington rejects international attempts to rein the technology in - Trump called to leave AI 'exactly where it is'.

That contrasts with Xi, who said responsible AI development is necessary, and with other Chinese officials who this week backed a global governance framework. Without US support, any such agreement will be difficult to execute.
