Show HN: Kapso – Iterative learning from enterprise data

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

We're Alireza and Majid building Leeroo ( https://leeroo.com/ ).

We’re now focusing on building AI systems for enterprise data.

Today, we're open-sourcing Kapso ( https://github.com/Leeroo-AI/kapso ), an engine that runs experiments, deploys the best solution, and learns from every run.

Highlights

  • Previously we’ve built AI systems for products with millions of users, including at LinkedIn and Meta.
  • The nature of data work is iterative: investigate, form hypotheses, experiment, and improve based on what you learn.
  • This bottom-up process is the only way to deliver meaningful outcomes quickly in large, messy enterprise data environments.
  • Consultants gathering context in meetings or teams defining a business ontology upfront to fit into the data has no chance of execution, in quarters and sometimes years.
  • An AI-native system should automate this process, compounding learning to each new investigation and implementation.
  • For instance, one of our enterprise projects was moving a company off its old data estate onto a lakehouse with more than 30 source-system families, thousands of tables and transformations, and hundreds of BI reports.
  • No individual or AI agent knew how that data estate was connected and operated.
  • The iterative work goes beyond understanding how data and systems fit together, and the business operations.
