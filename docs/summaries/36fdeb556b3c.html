London-based Scarlet, which certifies AI and software for medical devices, raised a $50M Series B led by Headline to support its US and Asia-Pacific expansion

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Scarlet, a London-based certification body specialising in AI and software medical devices, has raised a $50M Series B led by Headline, with Notion Capital joining and Atomico, Creandum, Kindred Capital and Entrepreneurs First participating. Headline partner Nancy Xiao Mclaughlin joins the board. The money funds expansion into the US and Asia-Pacific.

The company attacks a real bottleneck: an AI diagnostic model can be trained in weeks and then wait 12 to 18 months for expert assessment, plus six to eight more for approval of a minor update. Scarlet says it is the only one of the roughly 52 organisations designated under the EU Medical Device Regulation that specialises in software and AI, and it also holds UK MHRA designation and US FDA Third Party Review Organisation status. Founders James Dewar and Jamie Cox started it in 2021 after building AI medical devices at Babylon Health, and spent the first three years winning regulatory designations before issuing any certificates. Customers upload technical documentation in any format to its AI-enabled platform.
