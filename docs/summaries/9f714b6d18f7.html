Summary

Simon Willison quotes a striking anonymous account ('voxium') from inside a big company where everything - specs, code, tests, PRDs, tickets, reports - is produced by Claude Code. Nobody on the team likes it. Management keeps asking why shipping is slow when 'pushing code is not a bottleneck'; meanwhile people work 12-13 hour days 'just to press enter', and nobody reads anything. From L1 to L7, every engineer does the same job: talk to Claude.

It's a vivid field report of agentic coding adopted as mandate rather than tool: output is up, ownership and understanding are gone, and the org has confused keystroke throughput with engineering.
