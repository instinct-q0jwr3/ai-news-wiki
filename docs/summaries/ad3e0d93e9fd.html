Survey of Anthropic staffers and US adults: 39% of US adults vs. 91% of staffers expect AI will be able to make a Nobel-level discovery by 2030, and more

Summary

A survey of Anthropic staff and US adults compares expectations about AI. Only 39% of US adults against 91% of staffers expect AI to be a major economic force, in a study that also asks how fast recursive self-improvement could speed progress.
