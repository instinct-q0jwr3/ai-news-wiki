Pluralistic: Economic probabilities for our grandchildren (03 Oct 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow argues, in a long post, that post-war prosperity rested on industrial capacity, political freedom and pent-up demand. Citing Piketty, he says wealth pools with the already-wealthy until a collapse destroys capital and impoverishes oligarchs, which let ordinary people gain afterwards.

He uses that to question what economic outcomes to expect for our grandchildren, including from AI. The summary covers the opening argument; the post is long.

Highlights

  • Post-war boom came from capacity, freedom and demand.
  • Piketty: wealth concentrates until collapse destroys it.
  • Frames future economic outcomes, including AI's, by that history.
  • Opinion post; summary covers the start.
