Meaning After an AI Eucatastrophe

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

A playful philosophical essay asks what meaning looks like after an AI 'eucatastrophe' - the good kind of catastrophe, where machines do the work and humans are left with the question of what to do instead. Its answer borrows from the Etsy economy fantasy: a future where everyone makes beautiful handcrafted things - drawing pterodactyls, crochet - and buys them from each other.

The piece then wanders deliberately into the absurd and the meditative: sharpening the senses, making holes in habits, being alone for a month, regarding yourself as a genial criminal, being a witch at odd times of the day - playfully. Underneath the games is a serious claim: opening awareness frees you from domination by your own mental contents, and that freedom, not productivity, may be the point of the post-AI future.

Highlights

  • The essay imagines meaning after a 'eucatastrophe' - the benevolent AI upending
  • One candidate future: an Etsy economy of everyone making and buying handcrafted things
  • It prescribes play: sharpen the senses, break habits, be a witch at odd hours
  • The serious core: opening awareness frees you from domination by your own mental contents
  • Meaning after AI is framed as a practice, not a job description
