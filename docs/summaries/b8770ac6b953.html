An interview with Cognition CISO Alex Stamos on how AI is upending cybersecurity, AI-powered red teaming, vulnerabilities in legacy codebases, and more

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high

Summary

On The Verge's Decoder podcast, Nilay Patel talks with Alex Stamos, chief security officer at Cognition, about how AI is changing cybersecurity. Stamos argues the multibillion-dollar security industry as it existed is overdue for disruption. Criminal groups and state actors such as Russia and North Korea use AI to find and exploit vulnerabilities at the same time, which gives the moment a frontier feel like the 1990s.

He is not a long-run doomer but expects a 'dark age' while defenders adapt. The conversation also covers AI-assisted red teaming, weaknesses in legacy codebases and how AI misinformation and slop might be handled on social networks. The page shown was the episode introduction, so the detail comes from the host's summary.

Highlights

  • Stamos: the old security industry is overdue for upheaval
  • AI helps attackers detect and exploit flaws at once
  • Both private and state actors are active
  • Expects a 'dark age' before adaptation, not a permanent one
  • Topics: red teaming, legacy code, social-media slop
