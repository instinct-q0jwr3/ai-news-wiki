Siri AI Class-Action Lawsuit Settlement Website Is Now Live

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

daring-fireball apple amazon ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Joe Rossignol, MacRumors: In May, Apple agreed to pay $250 million to settle a U.S. class action lawsuit over Siri AI’s delayed launch, and the settlement website is now live.

Apple will pay an estimated $25 per eligible iPhone to eligible customers, who can submit a claim via the settlement website between September 21 and December 21 of this year.

Apple denies all of the allegations made in the lawsuit.
