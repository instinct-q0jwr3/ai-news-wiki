A Jev that can ask Opus for help gives Opus accuracy at 4x lower cost

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Hightouch tested whether LLMs and decision models (Jev and Luna) could grade identity-resolution results, the task of mapping records to real people. Both did acceptably out of the box (88.8-97.3% accuracy), and a hybrid where Jev can ask Opus for help reached frontier accuracy at over 4x lower cost. The post explains why grading is hard: no answer key exists on real data, over-merging identities is the worst failure, heuristics like emails-per-identity help, and teams spend months checking by hand. Vendor test.

Highlights

  • Out-of-box accuracy 88.8-97.3%
  • Jev plus Opus hybrid matches frontier accuracy at >4x lower cost
  • Over-merging is the main failure mode
  • No answer key on real data, so grading needs sampling and review
