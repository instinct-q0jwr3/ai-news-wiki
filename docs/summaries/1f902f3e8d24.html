Amsterdam police arrested between 150 and 200 protesters for trespassing and violence at a Microsoft data center construction site owned by Pure Data Centers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Amsterdam police arrested 150 to 200 protesters at a Microsoft data center construction site on suspicion of trespassing, per Tom's Hardware. Police said tools for breaking in were seized, and some people entered the site with violence; some items thought stolen and vandalism were found. It continues an action from the previous Friday and is the first of several by the 'Stop Dirty Data Centers' coalition planned across Europe, including Dublin and Warsaw. NOS named the group as Fossielvrij. Police text was machine translated.
