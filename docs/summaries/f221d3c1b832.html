Dungeons and Dragons maker will pass AI settlement money to authors

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

The Seattle Times reports that Wizards of the Coast will pass its portion of the Bartz v. Anthropic settlement to authors of covered works written under work-for-hire contracts. The article combines a company statement, author interviews and an account of roughly four hundred covered works at about three thousand dollars each.

The resulting approximately $1.2 million total is the publication's arithmetic, which the company declined to confirm, not a separately announced exact fund. Reported letters and the beginning of installments do not prove every author has received payment. The court history and contractual eligibility are reported rather than independently legally audited, and the company makes no blanket commitment about future lawsuits. No claim, payment or copyright permission was submitted.

Highlights

  • Work-for-hire ownership makes this different from an ordinary author split
  • Company decision and author reactions are directly reported
  • Approximate total is not company-confirmed
  • Letters and installments differ from completed payment to everyone
  • Future litigation policy remains unstated
  • No legal or payment action occurred
