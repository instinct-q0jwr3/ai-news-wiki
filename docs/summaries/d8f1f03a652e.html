Wiley agrees to 50-50 split with Dummies authors in Anthropic settlement

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Victoria Strauss of Writer Beware reports on Bluesky that Wiley will split Anthropic copyright-settlement money 50-50 with authors of its For Dummies series. This follows outcry over the larger share Wiley had claimed.

Strauss notes payment will still go through literary agencies and not directly to authors. The post is short; the settlement terms and the original percentage claimed are not given in it.

Highlights

  • Wiley changes For Dummies split to the default 50/50
  • Followed author criticism of Wiley's claimed share
  • Payments still routed via literary agencies
  • Related to the Anthropic copyright settlement
  • Short social post, no figures on the earlier share
