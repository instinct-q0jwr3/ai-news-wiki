Highlights

  • Modulate raised $25M led by Future Ventures, with Hyperplane and Lakestar participating
  • Runs 100+ small models: signal extraction (emotion, tone, synthetic voice) plus analysis/detection (intent, scams, rule violations)
  • Targets transcription, deepfake and AI-music detection, and voice-agent policy enforcement in regulated industries
  • Founded 2017 by MIT physics undergrads; pivoted from gaming voice modulation to moderation to AI-audio detection
  • Small models mean no specialized hardware and lower compute bills, per the CEO
  • PitchBook: $41M raised at a $170M valuation before this round
