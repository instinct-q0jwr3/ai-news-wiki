Why Muse May Never Need Ads

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Ben Thompson-style analysis in MBI Deep Dives argues Meta's Muse may never need ads. Citing DoorDash, it says users arriving from AI chatbots converted better and filled bigger baskets, while early agent traffic had baskets two-thirds smaller. These figures are DoorDash's own.

Meta says Muse does not share conversations or VM data with its ad systems, though browsing as the user can influence ads.

Highlights

  • Argues commerce commissions may replace ads for Muse.
  • Chatbot referrals converted higher; agent orders had smaller baskets.
  • Nearly half of chatbot restaurant orders went to new places.
  • Meta says Muse conversations stay out of ad systems.
