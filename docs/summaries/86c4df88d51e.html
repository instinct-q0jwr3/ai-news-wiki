OpenRouter: the share of business spending between OpenAI and Anthropic models was roughly even in September, vs. Anthropic commanding a 75% share in January

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports, citing OpenRouter data, that business spending was split roughly evenly between OpenAI and Anthropic models in September, against a 75% Anthropic share in January. The piece frames it as a heating price war and notes both companies need to show sustainable businesses ahead of planned IPOs.
