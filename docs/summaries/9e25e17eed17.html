Tony Fadell on why the first wave of AI gadgets failed — and what comes next

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

At a TechCrunch event, Tony Fadell, co-creator of the iPhone and founder of Nest, explains why the first wave of AI gadgets failed: they solved no real need and were interesting technology for geeks. Companies asked him for help and he declined.

He argues that promising an assistant misses that under 0.01% of people have ever had a human assistant, so most consumers do not know how they would use or trust one. It is an opinion from a hardware veteran, not data.

Highlights

  • Fadell says early AI gadgets met no real need
  • Companies sought his help; he declined
  • Under 0.01% of people ever had a human assistant, in his estimate
  • Consumers do not know how to use an assistant
  • Opinion from an experienced hardware founder
