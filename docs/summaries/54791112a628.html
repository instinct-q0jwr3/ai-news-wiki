What's the deal with all the random weekly quota resets for agents lately?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

max-woolf agentic-systems

Summary

Max Woolf complains about getting free stuff: OpenAI and Anthropic's surprise weekly-quota resets for Codex and Claude Code. After GPT-5.6 Sol's release, OpenAI reset Codex weekly quotas six times in two weeks, unannounced - users discover them by finding their quota back at 100%, sometimes hours before their natural reset.

The annoyance is real economics: a reset with his quota at 50% 'wastes' $12 of the $100 plan he upgraded to; resets unset his weekly timer, forcing a throwaway prompt; and the random-reward schedule that should give dopamine instead produces deficit - urgent idea-generation to burn quota before it resets again. His cynical read: resets aren't serendipity, they're retention - keeping power users from drifting to Grok 4.5, Muse Spark 1.1 or Kimi K3 when their quota runs out. If it continues, his rational move is downgrading back to $20.

Highlights
