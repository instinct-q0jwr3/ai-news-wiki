10 Takeaways from Tibo Sottiaux

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high

Summary

Lenny Rachitsky's takeaways from OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux sketch where product thinking is heading. First: most internet actions will soon be taken by agents, and most people have not priced that in - Notion's MCP server launch flooded its systems and forced a rethink of its economics, and every product team will have to decide whether to build for agents. Second: the model picker should die - Sottiaux himself finds it fatiguing, and Dots ships without one.

Third: build as if models will be ten times better in a year; his own forecast ran too slow. Fourth: the era of intricate agent loops, graph architectures and fine-tuned prompts is ending. Fifth: while Dots got the DevDay attention, he bets the plugin ecosystem is the sleeper hit - 'Sign in with ChatGPT' launched with 16 partners, plugins share revenue when subscribers spend tokens with them, and ChatGPT recommends plugins based on retention and quality.

Highlights

  • Most internet actions will soon be taken by agents - Notion's MCP launch strained its systems.
  • The model picker is likely going away; Dots ships without one.
  • Build as if models will be 10x better in a year - even Sottiaux's forecasts ran slow.
  • Loops, graphs and fine-tuned prompts are not the long-term answer.
  • Sleeper hit prediction: the ChatGPT plugin ecosystem with revenue sharing and in-chat recommendations.
