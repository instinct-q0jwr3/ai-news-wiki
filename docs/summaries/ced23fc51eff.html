Ask HN: What do you run on a $5 VPS that's worth keeping online 24/7?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

This Ask HN thread asks when a cheap, always-on server earns its maintenance cost compared with running tasks on a laptop. The author starts from DHH's AI-shed idea but asks for practical uses that remain useful over years, rather than a list of things one could install.

Replies describe personal websites, VPNs, media and photo hosting, private Git services and remote access. The thread supplies individual experiences, not a benchmark or a demonstrated AI workload; its central tradeoff is persistent availability and control versus the continuing work of operating a server.

Highlights

  • The question concerns roughly $5-10 monthly servers.
  • An always-on machine remains available when a laptop is off.
  • VPN and remote-access services are recurring examples.
  • Personal hosting trades provider dependence for maintenance.
  • The AI connection is the motivation, not a tested application.
