Game decompilation site is closing because AI has 'broken the community aspect'

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high

Summary

One of the biggest game decompilation sites is shutting down, blaming AI for breaking the community aspect of the scene. Fan decompilation projects reverse-engineer games into readable code - technically legal, since they use no leaked content or copyrighted assets and require players to supply their own legally-sourced ROMs - and enable higher frame rates, widescreen support, dual-stick controls and other mods. The community has split over whether AI-assisted decompilation is legitimate, and the site's maintainers say the arrival of AI-generated contributions has eroded the collaborative culture that made the projects worth running.

Highlights

  • Decompilations are legal: no leaked assets, users supply their own ROMs
  • They enable frame-rate, widescreen, control and mod improvements

  • Maintainers: AI contributions broke the community aspect
