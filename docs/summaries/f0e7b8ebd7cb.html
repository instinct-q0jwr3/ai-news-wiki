Anthropic cuts its coordinated vulnerability disclosure window from 90 to 30 days for high- and critical-severity issues, in response to AI-driven bug discovery

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic says its coordinated disclosure policy will give maintainers 30 days for human-reviewed high- and critical-severity findings and 45 days for other findings reported from January 1, 2027. Existing reports retain their deadlines, with a separate transition described for overdue serious findings.

The update also shortens nonresponse escalation and post-patch detail publication periods. Active exploitation retains a seven-day window, and hard-to-fix cases may receive negotiated timelines. This is Anthropic’s disclosure policy, not a new legal deadline for every maintainer or a rule covering unreviewed automated findings.

Highlights

  • New windows start with reports from January 2027
  • High and critical issues receive 30 days
  • Other covered issues receive 45 days
  • Active exploitation and difficult remediation have distinct treatment
  • Unreviewed automated findings are excluded
