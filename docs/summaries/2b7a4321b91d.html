OpenAI says Plus and Pro subscribers can use their existing plan allowance with 16 launch partners, including Vercel and Devin, via Sign in with ChatGPT

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI says Plus and Pro subscribers can use their existing plan allowances inside third-party AI and coding platforms - Amp, Devin, Lovable, and OpenClaw - plus developer and productivity platforms including Notion and Vercel. The move turns the ChatGPT subscription into portable compute credit across the ecosystem rather than a single-app entitlement.
