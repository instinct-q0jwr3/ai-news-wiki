Toshiba plans to double HDD production capacity for AI data centers within FY2027 from its 2025 level, targeting a 30% share by storage capacity, up from ~10%

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-04 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Nikkei reports Toshiba plans to double its hard disk drive production capacity, from the 2025 level, within fiscal 2027 to serve AI data centers as storage demand rises. It will expand its Philippine plant and raise per-drive capacity, its first major investment in the HDD business in about five years. Basis: only the opening of the article was available.

Highlights

  • Toshiba to double HDD capacity by fiscal 2027.
  • Philippine plant expansion, first big HDD investment in about five years.
  • Driven by AI data center storage demand.
  • Basis: opening only.
