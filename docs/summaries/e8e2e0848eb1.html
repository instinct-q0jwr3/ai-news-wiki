US-China AI Gap Hits 3%, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Now Leads on Agentic Coding Benchmarks

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

TechTimes reports a narrowing US-China model-performance gap, citing a Bloomberg Intelligence note and an October 4 LiveBench snapshot. It gives DeepSeek V4.1 Flash an overall score of 81.1 versus Anthropic's 83.4, framing the relative difference as about 3%, and describes a stronger agentic-coding score.

The article also mixes those reported independent leaderboard figures with DeepSeek's self-reported DeepSWE results and discusses data-jurisdiction risk for API users. These are distinct claims with different evidence. A leaderboard snapshot is not a general measure of national AI capability, and the legal interpretation and unvalidated coding figures should not be treated as settled by the benchmark.

Highlights

  • The 3% framing comes from an overall-score comparison.
  • Overall and agentic-coding scores measure different things.
  • Self-reported DeepSWE results are separately identified.
  • The figures refer to a dated snapshot.
  • Data-jurisdiction questions are separate from model scores.
