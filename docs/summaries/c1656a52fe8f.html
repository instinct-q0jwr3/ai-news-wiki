General Medicine, an online storefront for medical care founded by PillPack founders TJ Parker and Elliot Cohen, raised a $120M Series B led by a16z

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-11 · confidence: high

Summary

General Medicine, an online storefront for medical care founded by PillPack founders TJ Parker and Elliot Cohen, has raised a $120 million Series B led by a16z. The thesis: AI makes healthcare advice easy to access, but turning advice into actual care remains the hard part, and few companies have built the marketplace and fulfillment layer required.

The startup connects recommendations to real care, whether filling a prescription, ordering a lab test, scheduling a telehealth visit or booking an in-person appointment. Consumers need a healthcare store, Parker argues.

Highlights

  • $120M Series B led by a16z
  • From the PillPack founders
  • Connects AI health advice to actual care delivery
  • Prescriptions, labs, telehealth and appointments in one store
  • Bets the gap is fulfillment, not advice
