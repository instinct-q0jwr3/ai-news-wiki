Battle Drones - a vibe coded RTS with audio-based multiplayer

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison documents Battle Drones, his most ambitious vibe-coded game yet: a mobile-friendly Command & Conquer-style RTS where drone armies fight over territory and resources, built through a sequence of escalating prompts and a dynamic workflow script orchestrating many subagents at once. He iterated on fun systematically: openers (Blitz, Industry, Air Wing), call-down powers (Barrage, EMP, Drop Pod, Sky Hammer), objectives and salvage, combat juice, adaptive music, an 8-mission Ops campaign, Last Stand survival, heroes, mutators, and a Daily Op. The balance claims are data-backed: across 1,200 AI-vs-AI test matches on Normal, games undecided after 25 minutes fell from 14% to 0.9%, and comeback wins from 800+ scrap behind rose from 24% to 46%. Later prompts ported it to three.js with 3D models and designed maps with air-only island uplinks to force combined-arms play.

Highlights

  • Vibe-coded RTS built through iterative prompts and a subagent-orchestrating workflow script
  • Mobile-friendly Command & Conquer style with flying and ground drones
  • Openers, call-down powers, salvage, heroes, mutators, Daily Op, 8-mission campaign
  • Balance tuned via 1,200 AI-vs-AI matches: stalemates 14% to 0.9%, comebacks 24% to 46%
  • Later ported to three.js with 3D models
  • Map design exploits Condor-only island uplinks to force combined arms
  • Willison: unlike his earlier experiments, this one is actually fun
