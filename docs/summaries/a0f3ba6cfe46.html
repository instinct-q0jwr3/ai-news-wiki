ChatGPT is adding real cartoonists' signatures to fake New Yorker cartoons

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Nieman Lab reports that ChatGPT is imitating New Yorker-style cartoons and also adding real cartoonists' signatures to fake images. A viral cartoon of Dolly Parton and Tim Curry at heaven's gates carried the pen name BLOPER, Brendan Loper's signature, but he did not draw it. A Facebook fan had only asked ChatGPT for a New Yorker-style cartoon. Loper says people wrote asking if it was his. The piece looks at false attribution of AI images to real artists.

Highlights

  • ChatGPT put a real cartoonist's signature on a fake cartoon.
  • Brendan Loper never drew the viral Parton-Curry image.
  • The prompt only asked for 'a New Yorker-style cartoon'.
  • Basis: first part of the article.
