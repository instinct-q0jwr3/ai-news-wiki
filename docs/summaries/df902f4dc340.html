Reliable Robotics, which develops an autonomous flight system, raised a $115M extension for its $160M Series D, pushing its valuation to $1B+

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports Reliable Robotics has raised a $115 million extension of its Series D, $90 million in equity led by Nimble Partners plus $25 million in debt from Silicon Valley Bank, bringing the round to $275 million, total funding to $380 million, and the valuation past $1 billion. The Mountain View startup, founded by SpaceX veterans Robert Rose and Juerg Frefel, has spent nearly a decade commercializing an autonomous flight system for existing aircraft and is pursuing FAA certification on four linked fronts. The first product, continuous autopilot engagement, handles engine start, taxi, takeoff, and landing while the pilot supervises via touchscreen, with certification expected in 18 to 24 months; pilot-on-the-ground certification is at least three years out. The company also built its own collision-avoidance radar after legacy suppliers declined the project.
