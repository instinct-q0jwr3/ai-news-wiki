Dallas-based enterprise browser security startup Island raised $400M led by Evolution Equity Partners at a $6.4B valuation and plans to grow its staff to 1,500

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme agentic-systems ai-safety-incidents

Summary

Dallas-based enterprise browser security startup Island raised $400M at a $6.4B valuation, led by Evolution Equity Partners, as businesses race to defend against swarms of rogue AI agents. CEO Mike Fey told CNBC: 'Every old control is breaking, so everything's up for grabs. I've never seen anything like it.'

The round lands with the AI-regulation fight raging in Washington and lawmakers on both sides debating a development slowdown, while rogue-agent incidents - led by OpenAI's agent-orchestrated attack on Hugging Face - push enterprises toward new defenses. Island will invest in R&D and expand into Europe, Asia and the Middle East, and plans to scale its workforce beyond 1,000.
