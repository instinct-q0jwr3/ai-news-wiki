Meta's Muse appears to use an OpenAI model labeled muse-special

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news openai meta muse

Summary

Part 2 of a teardown of Meta's Muse filesystem asks an awkward question: does Muse actually use OpenAI and Claude models behind the scenes? The author found a model labeled azure/muse-special in his logs while Muse was building his website, and dug deeper after his first article hit the Hacker News front page.

Muse records which model each agent session uses: nearly every session log in his VM was routed to Meta's internal model, called Avocado - but one subagent used azure/muse-special, and a search of the repo inside Cursor turned up a 'GPT Responses model client'.

Highlights
