Alibaba's T-Head unveils the Zhenwu V900 AI accelerator, which it says triples its predecessor's performance and can scale to clusters of up to 500,000 units

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme alibaba nvidia

Summary

Alibaba's T-Head unit has unveiled the Zhenwu V900 AI accelerator, which it describes as China's most powerful AI chip, saying it triples its predecessor's performance and can scale to clusters of up to 500,000 units. Bloomberg reports the chip is meant to compete with Nvidia and to drive 20GW of data centers by 2032.
