OpenAI now lets you text your Dot agent

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI is rolling out texting for its Dot personal agents, in preview for US Pro users. From ChatGPT you enable texting on a Dot's profile, and from then on you can hand it tasks by SMS-style message - text, photos or voice notes - over iMessage or RCS. The Dot can also initiate contact, texting you proactively with updates on whatever you asked it to watch. OpenAI says it partnered with Linq for the messaging layer and ran extensive penetration testing; its pitch is that only you can text your Dot and your Dot can only text you.

The strategic detail is memory: whatever a Dot writes to its memory is shared across ChatGPT and the texting channel, so context carries over between conversations and surfaces. The launch lands a month after Dot's debut and into a suddenly crowded personal-agent market.

Highlights

  • Arrives a month after Dot's debut as the personal-agent race heats up
