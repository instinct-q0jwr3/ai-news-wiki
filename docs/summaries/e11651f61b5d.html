How the Fed Is Quietly Inflating the AI Bubble While Hiking Your Electric Bill

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-18 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high

Summary

Matt Stoller-style argument that the Federal Reserve is quietly inflating the AI bubble while rate policy raises ordinary costs. The peg is a WSJ profile of Fed official Beth Hammack, a 30-year Goldman Sachs veteran turned monetary hawk, whose stated concern is that businesses have too much pricing power, driving wage growth - a view the author reads as the Fed's accustomed role of punishing ordinary people under the veil of technocratic inflation-fighting.

The piece connects cheap-money era financing of AI data centers to rising electricity bills for households, arguing the Fed's framework ignores how its own policies feed the asset bubble it later fights with rate hikes.

  • Peg: WSJ profile of Fed hawk Beth Hammack, ex-Goldman Sachs
  • Hammack's worry: business pricing power driving wage growth
  • Author: suppressing wages is the Fed's accustomed technocratic role
  • Links AI data center financing to rising electricity bills
