Meta Launches an Enterprise AI Platform

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

Mark Zuckerberg announces Meta Enterprise Platform, 'the next major pillar' of the business: bringing Meta's full AI stack - the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API, Muse Code, and more - to businesses and developers. The stated thesis is that superintelligence creates new opportunities and Meta's strengths (advanced models, leading agents, infrastructure scale, years of business relationships) are few companies' match. Chirantan 'CJ' Desai joins from MongoDB as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer, reporting directly to Zuckerberg.

Highlights

  • Zuckerberg launches Meta Enterprise Platform as a 'next major pillar'
  • Brings Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API, and Muse Code to businesses
  • CJ Desai (ex-MongoDB CEO) joins as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer reporting to Zuckerberg
  • Thesis: superintelligence creates opportunities Meta's scale is built to serve
