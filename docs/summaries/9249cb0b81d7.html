Sensor Tower: Muse hit 902K+ downloads in six days

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high

techmeme meta agentic-systems

Summary

Meta's new AI agent app Muse was downloaded more than 902,000 times in the six days after its September 8 launch, according to Sensor Tower data cited by Bloomberg, outpacing Meta AI's 773,000 downloads in the same post-launch period. Meta shares jumped more than 12% as the app rose to the top of mobile app charts and drew strong early reviews.

Highlights

  • Muse saw 902K+ downloads in its first six days, per Sensor Tower.
  • That beats Meta AI's 773K downloads in its own first six days.
  • META stock jumped 12%+ as Muse topped app charts.
