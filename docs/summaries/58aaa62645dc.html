Tom the Dancing Bug’s Inner Hive, and Harvey Richards, the Greatest Lawyer in the History of Fiction

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

A brief plug, via Daring Fireball, for the Inner Hive, the subscription membership that funds Ruben Bolling's Tom the Dancing Bug comic. Members get each week's strip at least a day before it appears anywhere online, plus extras like commentary, process images, bonus comics and puzzles. The post also offers a no-strings 'Friend of Tom the Dancing Bug' donation option for readers who want to support the strip without subscribing. It is a small example of the direct reader-support model sustaining independent cartoonists and writers outside of ad-driven media.

Highlights

  • Members receive each week's comic at least a day before public release
