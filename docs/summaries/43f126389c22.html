Grant Sanderson – AI and the future of math

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

dwarkesh-podcast

Summary

Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown) joins Dwarkesh Patel to argue that mathematics is the leading edge of AI progress: AI is advancing faster in math than elsewhere, so math concretely previews what progress will look like in other fields. Even within math, though, the landscape is jagged - some areas yield easily, others resist.

Their conversation ranges over what history's big conceptual breakthroughs look like versus what AIs currently do, whether AI on net increases or decreases human understanding of mathematics, how large the 'overhang' is from AIs systematically connecting ideas already in the literature, and Sanderson's advice to students entering the fields being transformed fastest.

Highlights

  • Sanderson: math is where AI progress is most visible, a preview for other fields
  • Progress within math is 'jagged' - easy in some areas, resistant in others
  • Open question: do AIs increase or decrease human understanding of the field?
  • The 'overhang': AIs systematically connecting ideas already in the literature
  • Discussion contrasts historical conceptual breakthroughs with current AI behavior
  • Sanderson gives advice to aspiring mathematicians and coders in AI-transformed fields
