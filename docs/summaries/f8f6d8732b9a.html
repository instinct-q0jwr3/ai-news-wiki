Mistlabs, which develops Ambi, an ambient AI app for iPhone, Apple Watch, and Mac that learns about users from conversations, raised a $26M pre-Series A

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Mistlabs has taken its Ambi ambient AI system out of beta on iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac, backed by a $26M pre-Series A. Ambi's pitch is software that acts without being asked: CEO Peter Mo argues most AI platforms complete tasks, while Ambi tries to understand, reason and act in real time - sometimes 'before the user even thinks to ask.'

Ambi learns about a person from the conversations it hears across their Apple devices, sharpening its read on how they work over time, and its state follows the user between Mac, phone and watch. A Routine feature hands a recurring job to several agents at once; in one example, agents fact-check a competitor meeting live and have a comparison and draft proposal waiting on the user's MacBook before the meeting ends. Next, Mistlabs wants Ambi agents to negotiate with each other when two users sit in the same meeting, surfacing overlapping priorities and likely sticking points.
