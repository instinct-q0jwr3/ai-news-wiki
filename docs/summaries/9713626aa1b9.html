Ask HN: How do you interview devs in a post-AI world?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-20 · confidence: high

hacker-news anthropic agentic-systems

Summary

Since the rise of AI coding assistants, about 80% of the dev candidates that I interview tell me that they aren't writing much code themselves anymore - they are directing agents instead.

I still want to know that devs on my team can actually write code themselves, and have some idea of what they are doing when it comes to system design.
