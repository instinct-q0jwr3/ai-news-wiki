Adzuna: UK entry-level job postings mentioning AI are up ~3x since early 2025; Indeed says one in six vacancies in AI-exposed fields now mentions AI, up 2x YoY

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Bloomberg reports, citing Adzuna, that UK entry-level job postings mentioning AI are up about 3x since early 2025, and Indeed says one in six vacancies in AI-exposed fields now mentions AI, double last year. AI skills are no longer optional for young Britons seeking a first job.
