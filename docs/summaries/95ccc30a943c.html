SpaceX agrees to a ~$40B equipment finance lease with Apollo to fund purchases of Nvidia GPUs for its AI initiatives, set to close before the end of 2026

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high

SpaceX has agreed to a roughly $40 billion equipment-finance lease with Apollo Global Management to fund purchases of Nvidia GPUs for its AI initiatives, with the deal set to close before the end of 2026. It is one of the largest chip-financing arrangements yet and a sign of how far private credit is reaching into the AI build-out.
