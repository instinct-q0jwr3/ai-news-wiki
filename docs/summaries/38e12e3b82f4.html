The Hackintosh Reborn

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high

Summary

The author, who wrote a hackintosh guide a decade ago, says AI has revived the community. After Apple moved to ARM and macOS 27 dropped x86, he used a spare ThinkPad on macOS 26 and built an iOS app in a day with Xcode's Claude Code and Codex integrations, without writing code. He says hackintosh and 'hackios' activity has exploded in recent weeks thanks to AI-assisted reverse engineering, and lists projects like macOS 27 on a Snapdragon laptop, macOS 27.2 beta on an RK3588 board and iOS 17 on a Switch OLED.

Highlights

  • macOS 27 dropped x86, but AI tooling revived the hackintosh scene
  • Claude Code with Ghidra credited for reverse engineering gains
  • Examples: Snapdragon laptop, RK3588 board, Switch OLED
  • Author built an iOS app in a day with AI help
