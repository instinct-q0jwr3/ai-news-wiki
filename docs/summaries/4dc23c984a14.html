The AI sell-off compared to well-known stock market crashes

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Kadoa puts the AI sell-off next to past crashes. The S&P 500 is only 6% below its record, a mild correction, but tech fell harder: the Nasdaq is down 11% and the PHLX chip index 36% from its June 22 record, as deep as in the Covid fall (35%). The page tracks daily closes against the 56 falls of 6% or more since 1928 and the 16 past 15%, and shows years to recover. Recovery times are price-only and are worst cases for someone who bought at the peak.

Highlights

  • S&P 500 about 6% below its record
  • Nasdaq down 11%; chip stocks down 36%, like Covid
  • Compared with all 56 falls of 6%+ since 1928
  • Price-only recovery times; worst case for peak buyers
