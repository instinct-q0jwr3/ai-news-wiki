What is the actual percentage of people For and Against AI?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

An Ask HN thread asks what share of people are actually for or against AI. The author says a web search gives no satisfying answer, and that it feels like skepticism is growing, though that may just reflect the people around him.

The excerpted replies are opinions, not data. One says development should have been iterative and that the race for supremacy and rent from the technology makes the question moot because the deal is done. Another points out it is not binary, since even pro-AI people reject some uses. The thread offers no survey numbers.

Highlights

  • Ask HN: what percentage of people are for or against AI?
  • No polling data in the thread
  • One reply: the race is done and it will not disappear
  • Another: attitudes depend on the specific use, not a single side
  • Anecdotal discussion only
