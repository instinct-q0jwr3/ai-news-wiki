Huawei told some clients that a large share of its upcoming flagship AI chip, Ascend 960DT, will run largely on HBM made by Samsung and SK Hynix

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

The Information reports that Huawei has told some clients a large share of its upcoming flagship AI chip, the Ascend 960DT, will run largely on high-bandwidth memory made by Samsung and SK Hynix - an illustration of how porous US restrictions on AI-chip shipments to China remain, since the memory inside China's flagship silicon still comes from allied-country suppliers.
