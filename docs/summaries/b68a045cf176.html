AI stock selloffs fuel fears Big Tech's best quarters are over

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high

Summary

The LA Times, in an Oct. 30 piece, reports that top tech stocks were hammered as fears grew that massive AI spending will not pay off soon. The Nasdaq 100 fell roughly 4%, wiping over $1 trillion, and the S&P 500 dropped over 2% on Friday afternoon. Latest quarterly results confirmed worries: AI profits are far off and more spending is needed.

Companies have yet to show enough customers, and some are realizing AI could pull customers from more profitable business lines. Analysts quote a market 'a lot more sensitive than it might normally be' with investors selling first. Strategists predict earnings growth deceleration. The article also links the data-center boom to the midterm elections.

Highlights

  • Nasdaq 100 down ~4%, over $1T lost; S&P 500 down 2%+
  • Investors doubt AI spending will pay off soon
  • AI may cannibalize profitable lines, per Dan Ives
  • Dated Oct. 30
