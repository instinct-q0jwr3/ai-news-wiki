Claude Haiku 5.5

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5, which it calls its cheapest, fastest and most capable small model. It is aimed at high-volume, cost-sensitive work such as summaries, compaction, database queries and classification, and at speed-sensitive uses like live customer support and browser use. Anthropic also positions it as a subagent that pairs with Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 on coding work.

The price drops sharply: Anthropic says Haiku 5.5 costs about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5. The benchmark table shows large jumps over the old Haiku, for example 72.4% on an offline OSWorld 2.1 subset against 15.7%, and 1620 against 735 on GDPval-AA, with Sonnet 5.5 still ahead on most rows. The launch comes with two pricing changes: Sonnet 5.5 cache reads are halved, making it roughly 20% cheaper on most agentic work, and Max and Team subscribers get a monthly API credit for building agents on the Claude Platform.

Highlights

  • Cheapest, fastest small Claude; about 75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5
  • Targets summaries, compaction, classification, support and browser use
  • Suggested as a subagent beside Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 for coding
  • Large benchmark gains over Haiku 4.5, still behind Sonnet 5.5
  • Sonnet 5.5 cache reads halved, about 20% cheaper on agentic work
  • New monthly API credits for Max and Team subscribers
