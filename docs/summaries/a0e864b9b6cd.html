Wake up, people. What we should actually fear, near term, is not so much rogue superintelligence as unleashed agentic AI causing hacking the internet at scale.

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

marcus-on-ai agentic-systems ai-safety-incidents

Summary

Gary Marcus rounds up three escalating security stories and draws one conclusion: the near-term AI risk worth fearing isn't sci-fi. The major item: researchers ('s1r1us') announced they hacked OpenAI on July 25 in under 72 hours - two bugs let them take over ChatGPT/Codex accounts of OpenAI employees and reach connected services (Outlook, Slack, GitHub), proven with a PR into OpenAI's internal codebase.

Marcus credits WSJ Opinion (Brian Gross) for being among the few mainstream outlets to lay out the big picture, quotes its essay approvingly ('I agree with every word'), and frames the pattern - account takeovers, agent incidents, government-site meddling - as the concrete, present-tense risk that most coverage keeps missing.
