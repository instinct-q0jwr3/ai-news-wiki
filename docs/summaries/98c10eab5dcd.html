Anthropic's Amended S1 Filing Shows Slowing Q3 Growth

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-06 · confidence: high

Summary

The SEC's copy of Anthropic's amended S-1 confirms what CNBC reported from the filing: Q3 revenue grew about 47% quarter on quarter, a marked slowdown from 144% in Q2 and 132% in Q1. The deceleration is the first hard data point public-market investors get on whether frontier-lab growth is leveling, and it arrives weeks before Anthropic's planned mid-November IPO roadshow.

Highlights

  • The amended S-1 shows Q3 revenue growth of ~47% QoQ, down from triple-digit rates earlier in 2026
  • The slowdown becomes a central valuation question for the first mega AI IPO
