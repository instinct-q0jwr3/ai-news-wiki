Summary

Gwern's April 2021 newsletter: a collation of that month's links and site changes. On the ML research side he flags Set Transformer and Perceiver - early attempts to slim or generalize attention - and pauses on the question of whether attention was 'getting too much attention'. His answer: no. If the marginal return on Transformer research had fallen below rival directions, those neglected directions would be invading Transformer topics; instead the reverse kept happening, with whole application areas still untouched.

Also noted: Z-IL, showing predictive coding can perform exact backpropagation on any neural network, plus the usual mix of links and site changelog entries.
