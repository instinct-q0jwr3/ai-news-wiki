Meta brings its Muse AI assistant directly into Instagram DMs

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch reports Muse beginning a rollout inside Instagram DMs, where users can share posts and ask for context or follow-up actions without switching apps. It also describes organizing saved content into Collections and turning posts into itineraries or lists.

The reported DM rollout covers iOS and Android in the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore; the saved-post organization launch is narrower, covering the US and Canada. This is not evidence of availability in Spain or on every account. Meta's statements about approval, forgetting and undo are product claims, not a guarantee that every external effect can be reversed. No account integration or real action was tested.

Highlights

  • DM conversations can appear as side chats in Muse
  • DM rollout and saved-post features have different markets
  • Spain is not among the reported launch countries
  • Examples include post context and organizing bookmarks
  • Company describes user approval controls
  • Undo claims were not independently tested
  • No messages, bookings or purchases were made
