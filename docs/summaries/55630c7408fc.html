A finance benchmark asks agents to finish the whole assignment

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

tldr-ai agentic-systems external-evaluation

Summary

DAYJOB: Finance is a new benchmark measuring long-horizon finance agents across corporate finance, banking, credit, investing and real assets. It is built around the messy reality of knowledge work: realistic requests, complex environments, and end-to-end professional judgment - asking agents to finish the whole assignment rather than answer a question.

A sample task: evaluate a 2026 expansion proposal using attached materials and recommend whether the company should proceed and how to finance it, then summarize the work in an exportable text file. (The reference answer: proceed with Nashville only, financed with incremental senior debt in early FY2026.) The environments ship off the shelf alongside new Frontier data and RL environments.
