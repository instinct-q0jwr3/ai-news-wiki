Analysis: Nvidia's 2025 figures to the US government undercounted chips sold to Megaspeed via partners by 50K; Nvidia did not give Bloomberg an exact figure

Bloomberg reports via Techmeme that Nvidia's 2025 figures to the US government undercounted chips sold via partners to Megaspeed by 50,000, and that its general counsel intervened in a US probe of suspected chip smuggling by Megaspeed. Nvidia gave no exact number.
