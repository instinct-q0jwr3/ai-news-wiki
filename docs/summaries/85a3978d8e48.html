AI is mass decompiling games. That's both awesome and scary.

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

A post argues AI that can decompile games and software means anyone can reuse any program, so companies can steal from competitors and paid apps can be open-sourced against the owner's will, while it is also a big win for preservation since old games can be ported and enhanced. It follows an indie developer using AI to decompile a game. Basis: the excerpt.

Highlights

  • AI decompilation works on any software, not just old games.
  • Risks: copying competitors, open-sourcing paid software.
  • Benefit: preservation and porting of old games.
