Slovenia says 44,000 domain names with the .si suffix were registered in September, up from ~2,000 in August, as Trump renamed AI "super intelligence"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high

Summary

Slovenia's registry says 44,000 .si domains were registered in September, against under 2,000 in August, a rise above 2,100%. The BBC links it to Trump's call to rename AI as 'super intelligence', sharing the initials SI with Slovenia's domain. The registry calls the scale unprecedented for the previous 12 months.

Highlights

  • 44,000 .si registrations in September vs under 2,000 in August.
  • Possible cause: Trump's push to call AI 'super intelligence'.
  • Registry calls it unprecedented.
