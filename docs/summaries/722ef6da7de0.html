OpenAI acquires Odessia, travel startup created by Sonder founder

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI acquired Odessia, the AI travel agent founded by Sonder founder Francis Davidson, who joins OpenAI to work on the future of travel. Terms undisclosed; looks like an acqui-hire. Odessia was a year old, had raised $6M led by Sequoia, launched five months ago, and has already shut down; Davidson says early users planned millions of dollars of travel. OpenAI confirmed the hire but announced no travel product.

Highlights

  • OpenAI acqui-hires Odessia's founder
  • $6M pre-seed led by Sequoia; product already shut
  • No OpenAI travel product announced
