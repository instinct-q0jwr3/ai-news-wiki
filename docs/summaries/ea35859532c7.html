Anthropic raises its five-hour usage limits by 20% on Pro, Max, and Team plans, and gives subscription users a rate limit reset

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme anthropic

Summary

ZDNET's hands-on coverage of Claude Opus 5.5, less than two months after Opus 5: the workhorse delivers Fable 5.1 performance for most work at about 40% lower running cost, generates output more than 30% faster, and tokens are priced 20% below Opus 5 - with Box reporting Opus 5.5 used a third of the tokens Opus 5 did and was 40% less verbose without losing accuracy in their evaluations.

For subscribers, Anthropic is raising its five-hour usage limits by 20% on Pro, Max and Team plans, and Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are promised in the coming weeks. The announcement also merges Claude chat and Cowork into one product.

Highlights

  • Opus 5.5: Fable 5.1-level performance for most work at ~40% lower running cost.
  • 30%+ faster output; token prices 20% below Opus 5.
  • Box: a third of Opus 5's tokens and 40% less verbosity at equal accuracy in evaluations.
  • Five-hour usage limits raised 20% on Pro, Max and Team.
  • Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 coming; Claude chat and Cowork merge into one product.
