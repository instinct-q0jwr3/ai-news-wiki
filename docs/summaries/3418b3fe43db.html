FrontierPhysics Shows Wide Gaps Across Physics Fields

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Artificial Analysis reports a FrontierPhysics leaderboard using fifty-four private research tasks across nine fields, excluding two GPU tasks from the original set. Scores require numeric checks plus model-based manuscript review, with Fable 5.2 leading its reported run at 32%.

A score is not the fraction of papers accepted by human journals: failed runs receive zero and passing runs receive a rubric share awarded by AI reviewers. Nearly half of Neon runs passing numeric checks failed review, illustrating why a computational result is not sufficient on its own. Configuration, costs and private tasks limit generalization; no task or judging process was independently reproduced.

Highlights

  • Fifty-four of fifty-six tasks are used
  • Two GPU tasks are excluded
  • Numeric validation and AI review are separate gates
  • Rubric-weighted score differs from pure pass rate
  • Reported lead is configuration-specific
  • No human journal acceptance is established
  • Private tasks constrain independent replication
