Nvidia commits $1B over five years to build out the US' "capacity for super intelligence R&D" in fields like quantum computing, healthcare, and energy security

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Nvidia announced $1 billion over five years to support US scientific research and development in AI, with focus areas including quantum computing, healthcare and energy security. The Register notes it is roughly one sixtieth of the company's quarterly profit and fits its long-running push to merge AI with high-performance computing.

The commitment came at a Washington event, as Nvidia prepares to supply at least seven AI-optimized supercomputers to the US government.

Highlights

  • Nvidia commits $1B over five years
  • Aimed at US science: quantum, healthcare, energy security
  • About 1/60th of quarterly profit
  • At least seven AI supercomputers planned for the US
