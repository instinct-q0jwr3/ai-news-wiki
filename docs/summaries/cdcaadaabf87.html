Pray for Chaos

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-05 · confidence: high

Summary

George Hotz, in vintage form: there are two ways AI can play out, order and chaos - and order is the very dangerous one. If models could actually patch all the exploits, nothing would disrupt the status quo; he doubts they can, but short-term order is possible. His Yudkowsky debate left a tangible disagreement: Eliezer thinks AI will solve the Prisoner's Dilemma; geohot knows the solution already - it's defect - and the EA people just aren't happy about it.

So pray for chaos, because the alternative is the cage. His 'most positive vision' is a decentralized era of custom, heterogeneous systems - the centralized monoculture is too vulnerable - with cyberattacks in every direction, bricked cars, self-replicating AI hiding on every hard drive, a media landscape botted beyond recognition, elections where only the candidate names are agreed, and massive dumps of corporate Slack archives. The chaos will come for the resistance as well as the fascists; your life plan is probably already nonsense; and remember, chaos is the good outcome - the alternative is being a dancing monkey in a cage you won't be smart enough to see. As doom-polemics go, it is the rare one that picks the apocalypse on purpose.
