Introducing Bonsai 2 27B: Near-Lossless Compression in a 9x Smaller Footprint

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high

tldr-ai

Summary

Prism ML has released Ternary Bonsai 2 27B, a compressed version of Qwen3.8 27B that uses ternary {-1, 0, +1} weights with FP16 group-wise scaling, 1.76 effective bits per weight, for a total footprint of 5.9 GB. The model retains 98.2% of the full-precision model's aggregate benchmark performance while being more than 9x smaller, supports a 262K-token context window and multimodal input, and ships under Apache 2.0.

The pitch is intelligence density for local deployment: 143 tokens/second on an RTX 5090 and 46.8 on Apple's M5 Max, at 0.714 mWh per token on an RTX 4090, which Prism says is 40% more energy-efficient than a full-precision 8B model. Retention improving from 95% (first Bonsai generation) to over 98% strengthens the case that low-bit models can be practically lossless, widening where 27B-class capability can run.

Highlights

  • Ternary Bonsai 2 27B: 1.76 effective bits per weight, 5.9 GB total footprint, based on Qwen3.8 27B.
  • Retains 98.2% of the full-precision model's aggregate benchmark score (83.9), up from 95% retention in the first Bonsai generation.
  • More than 9x smaller than the full-precision counterpart; 262K-token context window; multimodal text-and-image input.
  • Throughput: up to 143 tokens/s on RTX 5090, 46.8 tokens/s on M5 Max.
  • Energy: 0.714 mWh/token on RTX 4090, claimed 40% more efficient than an 8B model at full precision.
  • Released under Apache 2.0, with CUDA and MLX (Mac, iPhone, iPad) support via custom low-bit kernels.
  • Retention is strongest in compounding-error domains: coding agents, tool use, multimodal workflows, long-horizon tasks.
  • Prism ML is a Caltech spin-out backed by Khosla Ventures, Cerberus, Google and Samsung.
