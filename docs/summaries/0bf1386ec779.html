European rights court bars Romanian lawyer for 2 years over AI citations

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high

Summary

The European Court of Human Rights announces that its President excluded a Romanian lawyer from representing or assisting applicants before that Court for two years. It describes repeated substantive errors, including nonexistent cases, invented paragraphs and real cases used contrary to their meaning, after the lawyer acknowledged using AI.

This is a restriction on practice before that Court, not an announcement that the lawyer has lost a domestic license everywhere. The release distinguishes a pattern of unverified substantive errors from isolated citation mistakes; it does not establish a blanket prohibition on every use of AI in legal drafting.

Highlights

  • Primary court announcement describes a two-year exclusion
  • Scope is representation before the ECHR
  • Errors include invented and misapplied authorities
  • Lawyer acknowledged using AI tools
  • Decision addresses professional verification duties
  • No universal domestic disbarment is announced
