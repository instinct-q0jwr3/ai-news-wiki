Grok 4.7

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

hacker-news xai

Summary

SpaceXAI released Grok 4.7, pitched as its most capable model for coding and knowledge work, served at the same price and speed as Grok 4.6. It uses a new, larger base model trained with a longer reinforcement learning run on a harder mix of tasks weighted toward problems that take many hours to complete, and it was trained to natively understand the Grok Bot harness for conversational and knowledge work. On CursorBench 4.0, which stresses longer-running coding tasks, the company claims frontier price-performance.

The release leans on a new safeguard stack: SpaceXAI says Grok 4.7 is the strongest model it has tested on refusals and jailbreak resistance, tops LatchBio's biosafety benchmark at 62.4%, and scores highest on its internal HackerBench v0.3 for risky cyber tasks, allowing only 3.3% of risky dual-use prompts through while rarely blocking legitimate security work. On professional knowledge work benchmarks GDPval and AA Briefcase, which simulate tasks done by lawyers, nurses and financial analysts, it improves on Grok 4.6 and performs comparably to other frontier models.
