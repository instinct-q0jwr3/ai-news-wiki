Jobless tech workers are being left out of San Francisco's AI boom

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Bloomberg reports on the workers the AI boom is leaving behind: laid-off tech employees in San Francisco who are struggling to find new roles even as the industry's valuations and spending hit records. The piece captures the bifurcation inside the boom itself - capital and compute concentrate at a handful of labs and hyperscalers while the broader tech labor market stays soft.

Highlights

  • Bloomberg profiles jobless SF tech workers excluded from the AI boom
  • The split: record AI spending coexisting with a weak hiring market for ordinary tech roles
