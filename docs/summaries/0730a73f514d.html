Google's Self-Improvements in LLMs

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

A Google arXiv paper reformulates on-policy self-distillation as a self-training framework that needs no external teacher, ground-truth answers or human-curated problem sets. Guided only by coarse, high-level topic descriptions, the model synthesizes its own curriculum of queries and privileged reasoning traces.

The authors report consistent self-improvement across text and multimodal domains, including standard language benchmarks and Visual Question Answering, while retaining capabilities in domains the model was not self-trained on - evidence, they argue, that foundation models hold rich latent capabilities that can be amplified without external supervision.

Highlights

  • Self-distillation driven entirely by self-generated QA pairs and reasoning traces
  • Only coarse topic descriptions guide curriculum synthesis
  • Gains on language benchmarks and VQA without forgetting other domains
  • Framed as evidence of amplifiable latent capabilities in foundation models
