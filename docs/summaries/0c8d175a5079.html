Pluralistic: JS Tan and Clarissa Redwine's "Against Tech Oligarchy" (07 Nov 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow's Pluralistic post covers 'Against Tech Oligarchy' by JS Tan and Clarissa Redwine, veterans of several tech-worker unionization drives that started well and then met fierce resistance in the second Trump term. He calls the book a superb analysis of the challenges and potential of tech labor organizing.

His framing: tech workers once held great power without unions, because each engineer added roughly $1m to a Silicon Valley firm's bottom line and was scarce. Many saw themselves as founders in waiting, an idea bosses encouraged by posing as peers and family. A cohort he calls 'Tron-pilled' also wanted to spread the benefits of digital networks to everyone. The post argues that scarcity-era leverage is fading, which is why organizing matters now.

Highlights

  • Book: Against Tech Oligarchy by JS Tan and Clarissa Redwine.
  • The authors ran union drives that hit heavy resistance.
  • Tech workers' past power came from value and scarcity, not unions.
  • Many saw themselves as temporarily embarrassed founders.
  • The 'Tron-pilled' cohort fought for the user.
