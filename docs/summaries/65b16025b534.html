Anthropic shares tank after IPO as the Nasdaq erases 2026 gains

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

NBC News reports Friday closing declines across major US stock indexes and a second-day fall in Anthropic shares. It places the trading session within a longer downturn involving interest rates, inflation, financing concerns and pressure on AI infrastructure expectations.

The article combines prices with attributed analyst explanations. The IPO is not established as the single cause of the wider decline, paper wealth is not realized loss for every holder, and these closing figures should not be mistaken for live quotations.
