AI models need moral support to make discoveries

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

seangoedecke

Summary

But generating all those moves manually is impossible… Giving LLMs a personality is just good engineering AI skeptics often argue that current AI systems shouldn’t be so human-like.

The idea — most recently expressed in this opinion piece by Nathan Beacom — is that language models should explicitly be tools, like calculators or search engines.

Although they can pretend to be people, they shouldn’t, because it encourages users to overestimate AI capabilities and (at worst) slip into AI psychosis .

  • Can you simply train the model on trajectories where AI solves hard problems?
