OpenAI shares mathematics research catalogue

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI's mathematics repository publishes manuscripts and supporting proof materials from evaluations of an internal model on open research problems. It contains 722 manuscripts grouped into 372 families, with families gathering a principal result and related arguments, consequences or alternative proofs. The model was given approximately 4,000 problems; most results used a common procedure averaging three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute.

OpenAI stresses that the catalogue mixes stages of verification. Some papers have Lean formalizations and others do not, so publication is not a guarantee that every claimed result is correct. It plans to add formalizations and preserve corrections as new versions while keeping earlier releases available. A few results used exceptional procedures, and one zeta-function writeup was edited by a human for readability.

Highlights

  • The source is the project's own repository description.
  • The model that produced most results is not released.
  • 372 families contain 722 manuscripts.
  • Not every manuscript has a Lean formalization.
  • Unformalized results may contain errors.
  • Corrections should remain traceable through version history.
