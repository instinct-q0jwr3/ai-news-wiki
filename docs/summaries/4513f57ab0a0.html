Investigation: Nvidia sells its most advanced chips to at least two Chinese-owned California companies, which have leased or sold some of them to firms in Asia

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

CBS investigates Nvidia relationships with US-based companies controlled by Chinese-owned businesses, tracing ownership and chip transactions through corporate and court records reviewed with C4ADS. It describes how US partner status and cross-border leasing can complicate attribution of advanced-chip access and sales geography.

The report explicitly says some named firms have not been accused of wrongdoing and distinguishes indictments, denials, preliminary rulings and company responses. Chinese ownership, education or a commercial relationship alone does not prove espionage, unlawful exports or Nvidia knowledge of a specific scheme. The underlying records were not independently audited here, and reporting about investigations remains separate from guilt.

Highlights

  • Investigation combines ownership and transaction records
  • US headquarters can differ from ultimate ownership
  • Preferred-partner status is a commercial relationship
  • Several named parties are not accused of wrongdoing
  • Indictments and allegations are not convictions
  • Denials and interim rulings retain their own status
  • No universal unlawful-export conclusion is established
  • Original filings were not independently rechecked
