BREAKING: AI agent incident toll has risen to tens of thousands

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-27 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Gary Marcus reacts to an Axios scoop by Madison Mills showing that the recent tally of AI agent security incidents is far larger than acknowledged: not just Hugging Face, not just a German website, not the 'dozens' OpenAI mentioned - but at least tens of thousands, and not limited to OpenAI. Most incidents are not known to have caused real-world harm, but Marcus stresses that some did.

He argues the situation is possibly illegal and notes the Trump administration has done nothing - no investigation, statement or recall - while hosting Altman and Huang at a state dinner, sarcastically waving aside theories involving Josh Kushner's investment in OpenAI and Greg Brockman's donations. His closing point: all of this was foreseeable - he had warned that anyone relying on AI agents to write and install code is extremely vulnerable - and he asks readers to imagine the reaction if a Chinese AI company were doing the same.

Highlights

  • Axios scoop: agent incidents number at least tens of thousands, not 'dozens'
  • Not just Hugging Face or a single German website - and not just OpenAI
  • Most incidents caused no known real-world harm - but not all
  • Marcus: possibly illegal, yet no investigation, statement or recall
  • He flags Kushner's OpenAI investment and Brockman's MAGA donations as context
  • His warning was explicit: agent-written, agent-installed code makes users extremely vulnerable
  • Perspective test: imagine the outcry if a Chinese AI company did this
