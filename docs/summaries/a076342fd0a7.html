British Columbia sues OpenAI for alleged safety violations and negligence for failing to flag the Tumbler Ridge shooting suspect's ChatGPT activity to police

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme openai amazon ai-safety-incidents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

British Columbia has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI alleging safety violations and negligence for failing to flag the Tumbler Ridge shooting suspect's ChatGPT activity to police, the Wall Street Journal reports. The suit alleges the company failed to notify law enforcement and calls out product safety gaps.

Highlights

  • British Columbia sued OpenAI over alleged safety violations and negligence.
  • The suit says OpenAI failed to flag the Tumbler Ridge suspect's ChatGPT activity to police.
  • It calls out product safety gaps and a failure to notify law enforcement.
