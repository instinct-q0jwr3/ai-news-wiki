Summary

A Techmeme excerpt of New York Times reporting attributes to Rhodium an estimate of large Chinese technology companies’ capital spending and compares it with AI-lab revenue. The primary article and underlying analysis were inaccessible.

Projected annual spending is not completed expenditure, and the compared populations and accounting periods were not verified. A revenue-to-capex gap alone does not establish insolvency, returns on investment or that all spending serves AI.

Highlights
