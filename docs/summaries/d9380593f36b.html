Publications How open-weight demand can support the useful life of NVIDIA GPU families Ornn Data · 7 September 2026 GPUs are commonly depreciated on the assumption that each new NVIDIA generation renders the previous one obsolete.

In this paper, we provide a counter to this thesis by examining the effect of open-weight demand on the economic usefulness of older GPU families.
