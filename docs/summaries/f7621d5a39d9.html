John Ternus says Apple doesn't "want to ship AI for AI's sake" and that Tim Cook is "available to help wherever I ask" on policy; Ternus has yet to meet Trump

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Steven Levy profiles Apple's new CEO John Ternus for Wired after two interviews around the low-key launch of the first touchscreen MacBook Pro. Ternus, 51, a 25-year Apple veteran and CEO for under two months, is positioned as the anti-drama choice: 'Apple doesn't need fixing, and it doesn't want drama. Its new leader is a product guy who bleeds six colors.' The loudest moment of his presentation was restoring the illuminated Apple logo - read as a signal that hardware still rules in the AI age.

On AI, Ternus says Apple doesn't 'want to ship AI for AI's sake'; on politics, he says Tim Cook is 'available to help wherever I ask' on policy, and notes he has yet to meet Trump. Levy first met him in 2015 at Apple's Input Design Lab, when the then-VP for Mac, iPad, Ecosystem and Audio Engineering struck him as affable and knowledgeable.
