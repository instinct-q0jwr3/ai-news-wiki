[AINews] OpenAI’s Patch the Planet program is 1 of 2 parties to discover PHP2Shell vulnerabilities in core PHP affecting 70% of the web, using GPT-5.6 Sol; coordinated response over 2 months

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Latent Space's AINews column digs into the PHP2Shell disclosure, noting that the critical PHP vulnerabilities were found independently by two parties leaning on frontier models: Calif researcher Ryan Austin, working under OpenAI's Patch the Planet project with GPT-5.6 Sol, and Wiz (now part of Google Cloud), using Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol alongside its own Wiz Atlas tooling. The column frames this as the first visible payoff of OpenAI's $1B Daybreak subsidy for frontline defenders, announced barely two months earlier.

The coverage is measured about the hype: all high-profile cyber disclosures carry some marketing, and whether PHP2Shell ranks with log4shell or Heartbleed is debatable. Still, the episode marks a shift - AI-assisted vulnerability discovery is now producing results independently at multiple organizations, and the labs are racing to fund and publicize defensive use of their models.
