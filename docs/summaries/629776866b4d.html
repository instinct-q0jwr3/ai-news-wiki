Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high

Summary

Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is an open, customizable multimodal model for always-on enterprise agents that read documents, check sources, follow policy and call tools. It is a 120B-parameter mixture-of-experts model.

Highlights

  • 120B MoE open multimodal model for enterprise agents.
  • Targets long document-and-tool workflows.
