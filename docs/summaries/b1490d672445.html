Gemini 4 Neon looks like an Opus 5.5 competitor

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high

Summary

An Agent Duel analysis projects Gemini 4 Neon preference performance by combining an assumed Carbon-like quality level with observed higher serving speed. It models the interaction of speed and early voting and gives an uncertainty interval around the projected comparison.
