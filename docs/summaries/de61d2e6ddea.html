Survey of 94 economists: 54% expect AI's gains to spread across the G20 over the next decade, not just the US and China; 32 see African countries falling behind

An FT survey of 94 economists finds 54% expect AI's economic gains to spread across the G20 over the next decade rather than concentrating in the US and China.
