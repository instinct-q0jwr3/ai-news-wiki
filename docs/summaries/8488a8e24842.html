Pluralistic: How to forfeit one of copyright's best labor policies (19 Nov 2026)

Summary

Cory Doctorow argues that expanded copyright alone cannot fix creators’ weak bargaining power and discusses termination of transfers as a structural counterweight. The examined passages explain why old profitable works can give creators leverage unavailable at the start of their careers.

This summary covers the examined opening and termination discussion, not the full later argument about loan-out companies. Legal descriptions are the essay’s account and were not checked against statutes, contracts or jurisdiction-specific advice.
