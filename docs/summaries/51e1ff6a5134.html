Qualcomm announces the Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2 for audio wearables, with up to 2x greater on-device AI performance and up to 40% lower power consumption

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme google small-specialist-models

Summary

Qualcomm announced the Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2, a chipset purpose-built for audio wearables - 'hearables' - with a heavy emphasis on on-device AI performance rather than simple audio quality.

The pitch is contextual audio: the chip is positioned to drive what audio wearables can do as AI features move onto the device itself, marking Qualcomm's push beyond pure audio silicon into AI-forward hearables.

Highlights

  • Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2 targets upcoming audio wearables ('hearables').
  • Major focus on AI performance, not just audio quality.
  • Positions contextual, on-device AI as the next job for audio chipsets.
