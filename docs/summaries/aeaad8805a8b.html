Show HN: Open-source early-reading reader with phonics help

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Yara Reads is a free, account-free reading app for kids who have just learned their phonics sounds and need something real to read. Every word in every story can be sounded out with the sounds the child already knows, and when they get stuck they tap the word and its sound buttons appear in place. The author built it for his six-year-old daughter Yara, who had finished Teach Your Monster to Read but had nothing to read next that was both a good story and fully decodable; her big brother Tom wrote the first story, and the app is structured so more can follow. Technically it's a Cloudflare Worker with static assets and D1 storage.
