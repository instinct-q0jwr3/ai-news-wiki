AI hardware startup Natura unveils Interface, a $99 smart ring that connects to AI agents and apps, including Claude, ChatGPT, Muse, Grok Bot, and Instinct

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Natura unveils Interface, a $99 smart ring meant as an interface to AI agents. It tracks heart rate, HRV, sleep and activity like a health ring, and at launch connects to agents and apps including Meta's Muse, Instinct, Grokbot, Claude and ChatGPT.

Founder Carlo Edoardo Ferraris, who earlier made HumanPods earbuds, says people want access to agents all day and that a ring is the right form. Pre-orders are expected next month, shipping in December or January.
