AHM Statement on OpenAI's October 6 Release of Mathematical Documents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Terence Tao reposts a statement from the Association for Human Mathematics (AHM) criticizing OpenAI's 6 October release of over 700 mathematical manuscripts. The statement notes OpenAI is defending lawsuits over plagiarism and copyright, and says mathematicians did not ask for this work.

It argues that OpenAI ignored the premise of the Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI, which said frontier labs should not test advanced problems on internal models, and calls the dump a demonstration of power, not scholarship. It urges mathematicians to stop working with OpenAI and return to science centered on human understanding. This is an advocacy group's position, posted as a guest post; Tao notes it was converted from another file format using AI.
