A Facebook co-founder proposes creating an "Office of AI Supervision" to embed teams inside frontier AI labs, with a three-year term to avoid regulatory capture

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

Summary

Facebook co-founder Chris Hughes proposes in the Financial Times an 'Office of AI Supervision': a regulator that would embed dedicated teams inside the frontier AI labs themselves, with staff serving a three-year term designed to avoid regulatory capture. The model borrows from resident-inspector regimes - supervision from inside the building rather than after-the-fact document requests.
