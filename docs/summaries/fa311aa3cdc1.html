How I (Accidentally) Started a Newsletter

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

works-on-my-machine

Summary

Scott Werner reflects on accidentally becoming a newsletter writer. The plan was demos: short videos proving the AI tools he was hacking together - a TDD pair-programming agent here, a prompt-driven whatever there - actually worked, shipped with runnable code. Somewhere in the middle of showing people, the newsletter became something else.

He is now exploring a paid series of short agentic-coding tutorial videos ($15/month, a 10-15 minute video every two weeks) and floats the idea to readers.

Highlights

  • The newsletter began as demo videos proving small AI tools actually worked
  • Werner describes the recurring amazement of wiring an AI tool together and seeing it work
  • He is considering a $15/month agentic-coding tutorial series with a video every two weeks
  • The piece is a meta note on how the project drifted from its original intent
