Tell HN: Notion AI chat sessions have been exposed via Fireworks AI hack

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

A Hacker News user reproduces a message presented as a Notion security notice about a Fireworks AI vendor incident. The quoted text says some chat transcripts may have been exposed without user or workspace labels and without tool-result content, while Notion stopped the affected connections.

The available source is a pasted email, not direct confirmation from the vendor. Lack of attached account labels does not guarantee anonymity if a conversation itself contains identifying information, and the account should not be generalized to all Notion data.

Highlights

  • Source is a user-pasted notice
  • Possible exposure concerns some AI chats
  • Quoted notice excludes tool results
  • Connections were reportedly shut down
  • Transcript content can still identify people
