Anthropic Expands Verified Access to Cyber Capabilities

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic combines Project Glasswing and its Cyber Verification Program into three access tiers for vetted security work. Defense Access covers defensive operations and eligible individual researchers; Red Team Access adds authorized penetration testing for organizations; Specialized Access is reserved for deeply reviewed organizations testing high-impact safety systems.

Each tier changes verification requirements and cyber safeguards rather than granting unrestricted use. Red-team access still blocks actions with physical-harm or mass-disruption potential. Anthropic describes required retention and a forthcoming Enterprise Frontier Safeguards option, with specified interim exceptions. Its vulnerability totals and effectiveness results are company-reported evidence, not independent validation in this summary.

Highlights

  • Three tiers replace separate trusted-access programs.
  • Defense Access can include qualified individuals.
  • Red Team Access is currently for organizations.
  • Specialized Access covers tightly reviewed high-impact systems.
  • Authorized scope remains a condition of testing.
  • Privacy and retention terms differ across arrangements.
