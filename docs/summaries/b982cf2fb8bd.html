The Individual Exception

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Ben Thompson connects personal agent-built software projects with a broader argument about exceptional individuals in an AI economy. Historical company revenue and staffing comparisons lead into Jensen Huang's reinstatement and the tension between equal corporate standards and investors' dependence on a particular leader.

The article is analysis, and its personal productivity examples are self-reported rather than a controlled comparison with team development. Its argument that AI increases individual economic power is not a measured forecast of employment or a legal finding about allegations discussed in the piece. Sponsor material and unrelated previews at the end are excluded from the analysis.
