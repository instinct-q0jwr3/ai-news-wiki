Gemini 4 Argon hints emerge as Google tests Carbon checkpoint

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high

Summary

Signs point to an approaching Gemini 4 Argon release: TestingCatalog spotted 'Gemini Argon' in Antigravity 2.0's model selector with three context configurations - 256K by default, 512K at roughly 1.3x quota per turn, and 900K at around 1.8x quota per turn - a pricing structure resembling Gemini Flash tiers. Google is also reportedly testing a 'Carbon' checkpoint.

In the same Antigravity update, the agent was renamed 'Chief of Stuff', feeding speculation that Antigravity could become the home for Google's bots/dots agents.

  • Context tiers: 256K default, 512K at ~1.3x quota, 900K at ~1.8x.
  • Google reportedly testing a 'Carbon' checkpoint.
  • Antigravity agent renamed 'Chief of Stuff'.
