Show HN: AI-built browser games, collected in one place

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Playground is a curated directory of independent browser games built with AI, collecting them in one place with genre tags, creator credits, and links to play. Every game opens in the browser, device icons show where each title works, and controller badges mark confirmed gamepad support. Games made with any AI model are welcome.

The site is free to browse and free to submit to, with every submission reviewed by hand - cover, details, and content - before it goes live. Approved games appear immediately, player ratings and reviews publish instantly, and creators get a private message channel with the site's operator. It is an early attempt at curation infrastructure for the wave of AI-built games: a human-reviewed shelf rather than an algorithmic feed, funded by reader tips.
