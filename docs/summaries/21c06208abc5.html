Canva adds a toggle to let free and paid users disable generative AI features like Magic Expand, following user frustration over the platform's AI push

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

The Verge reports Canva added controls to disable many generative features and prioritize human-created library content. The report explicitly distinguishes those controls from turning off every AI-assisted tool: background removal and object-moving features remain.

The controls affect product experience, not necessarily training-data policy or deletion of existing content. The article does not prove perfect detection of AI-generated assets, and account-specific rollout was not tested.

Highlights

  • Generative-feature toggle has explicit exceptions
  • Library preference is a separate control
  • Some assistive editing tools remain enabled
  • Interface choices differ from data-retention policy
  • Account-specific access was not checked
