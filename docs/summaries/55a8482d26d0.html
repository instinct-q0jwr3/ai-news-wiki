OpenAI fires three safety researchers for "mishandling research information"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak and Mikita Balesni, three safety researchers OpenAI fired the week before, published an open letter denying that they mishandled sensitive information outside company procedures. OpenAI says they shared confidential material with a third-party AI safety group.

The letter, addressed to OpenAI's safety and oversight bodies, says the firing and the communications around it have made former colleagues afraid to speak and to work with outside experts. They argue safety staff depend on that outside collaboration and that OpenAI is not a normal company.

Highlights

  • Three fired OpenAI safety researchers publish open letter
  • They deny mishandling sensitive information
  • OpenAI says they shared confidential data externally
  • Warn of a chilling effect on colleagues
