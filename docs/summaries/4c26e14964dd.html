NLUUG Award

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high

Summary

Bert Hubert received an NLUUG Lifetime Achievement Award, surprised on stage after his machine-learning-from-scratch talk at the NLUUG spring conference. He lists previous winners - Piet Beertema, Guido van Rossum, Wietse Venema, Bram Moolenaar, Andy Tanenbaum and others - and admits to a useful low-grade imposter syndrome that keeps him grounded, before deciding to embrace the award proudly.

The NLUUG cited PowerDNS, his hobby of explaining things, and his advocacy for open systems and privacy. Hubert notes the award is already proving useful beyond feelings: governments and institutions take his arguments more seriously when they come from a lifetime-achievement laureate.
