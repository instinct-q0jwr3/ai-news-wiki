Top-cited AI scientists at four US companies compared

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Topcitedscientists.com compares US-based authors in the AI and Image Processing subfield of the 2026 Stanford–Elsevier list at Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta. Its explanatory text says the panels plot lifetime paper counts against citations received in 2025 and reports substantial differences in listed-author counts.

This is a filtered bibliometric view, not a census of employees, a ranking of model capability or a measure of each company's total research output. The stated institutional assignments and field filters determine inclusion, and citations are an imperfect proxy for scientific contribution. The numeric explanation was read, but the plotted dots and source dataset were not independently audited.

Highlights

  • Only US-based listed authors are included
  • Subfield filter is AI and Image Processing
  • Citations are for 2025, papers span all years
  • Google includes DeepMind but excludes other Alphabet firms
  • List counts are not company headcounts
  • Citation differences do not establish model quality
  • Chart pixels and raw data were not independently checked
