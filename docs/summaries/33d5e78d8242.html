Can you tell which images are AI-generated?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Slop Sense is a small web game that tests whether players can tell real photographs from AI-generated images. A round lasts 60 seconds with up to 10 seconds per image; players answer 'Real photo' or 'AI-generated' by clicking or pressing 1 or 2.

Scoring rewards streaks: +100 points per correct answer, rising to +150 at a 3x streak, +200 at 5x and +300 at 10x or more, while a wrong answer resets the streak and a timeout scores zero. The game rides on the broader question of how convincing synthetic imagery has become.

Highlights

  • A 60-second quiz: real photo or AI-generated, up to 10 seconds per image.
  • Scoring: +100 per correct answer; streak bonuses of +150 at 3x, +200 at 5x, +300 at 10x+.
  • Wrong answer or timeout resets the streak; timeouts score zero.
  • Playable with mouse or keyboard (1 for real, 2 for AI), with an Enlarge option on mobile.
  • Positioned as a test of whether people can still spot AI-generated imagery.
