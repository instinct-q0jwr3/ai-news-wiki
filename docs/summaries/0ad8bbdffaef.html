US tech stocks see sharp falls over AI cost fears

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Tech stocks fell for a second straight day as investors balked at the scale of AI spending, the BBC reports. Intel plunged 28% on Friday after weak guidance, Alphabet extended its slide (another 1.4% after Thursday's 12% drop, which erased more than $500bn of market value when the company disclosed that AI answers in search are eating into its core advertising revenue), and the Nasdaq closed October with its worst monthly slide since March 2025.

The backdrop is the spending bill: Meta, Alphabet, Oracle, Amazon and Microsoft have committed roughly $800bn between them for 2026, almost all of it AI-related, while earnings are not consistently meeting expectations - reviving talk of an AI bubble. Intel alone shed more than $130bn in value on the day.
