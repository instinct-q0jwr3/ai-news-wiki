A new wave of Connected Apps is rolling out to Gemini

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

tldr-ai google gemini

Summary

Google is rolling out a new wave of Connected Apps for Gemini, letting users manage projects, design creative assets and plan workouts without switching tabs. Apps can be connected in Gemini settings or brought into a chat by typing an @ mention or asking directly. The September 23 announcement is by Gemini App group product manager Mai Lowe.

Highlights

  • New wave of Connected Apps rolling out to Gemini.
  • Manage projects, design assets and plan workouts inside Gemini.
  • Connect via Gemini settings or @ mention apps in chat.
  • Announced by GPM Mai Lowe on September 23.
