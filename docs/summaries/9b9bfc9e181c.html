DDoS Attack Breaks Beloved Video Game Wiki After AI Bro Was Banned

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

The Cutting Room Floor, a much-beloved wiki archiving unused content and unreleased builds from video games since 2002, is offline following a DDoS attack. The outage follows a public controversy: the site says an AI user pointed automated tools at it and got a warning about prompt injection from its anti-AI page, which 'successfully annoyed the operator and interrupted their work'.

The dispute spilled onto X, where one participant asked Grok about the 'proper legal response' to what he called false DDoS accusations posted in a GitHub gist and a live website.

Highlights

  • The Cutting Room Floor wiki is offline after a DDoS attack.
  • TCRF has archived unused video-game content with volunteers since 2002.
  • The site says an AI user's tools tripped its anti-AI page with a prompt-injection warning.
  • The feud escalated to legal threats over alleged false DDoS accusations.
