ChatGPT Targets Ads to You Based on Your Prompts, Phone Number, and Email

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

404 Media reports that OpenAI lets advertisers target ChatGPT users by email address, phone number or a unique phone identifier, based on its own tests of the ad system. Such targeting is standard in advertising.

The concern is that with about a billion monthly users, many may not realize their contact details can be used to place ads in their sessions.

Highlights

  • ChatGPT ads can target by email, phone number or device ID.
  • Standard ad practice, but users may not expect it.
