SAIR's Open Math Model initiative

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

tldr-ai

Summary

Terence Tao announced that SAIR, the Foundation for Science and AI Research he co-founded last year with private donors to support responsible AI use in mathematics and the sciences independent of major AI companies, is launching an Open Math Model initiative. SAIR has been negotiating with academic and industry partners to build open-weight models for science plus open-source tooling for using both open and closed LLMs in scientific applications.

The rollout was meant to be gradual, a few pilot projects over coming months, but given current events and high demand for open models, the announcement is being accelerated to today even though key planning is still underway. SAIR started small, podcasts, short events and competitions, recently received support from XTX Markets for its next competition round, and is now expanding in scale, ambition and logistical capacity.
