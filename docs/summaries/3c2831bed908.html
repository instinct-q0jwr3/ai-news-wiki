  • Where AI does produce value, that value is comparable to a plug-in, a new feature for your word processor or image/sound/video-editing package that might help you do your job somewhat better, or it might not.
  • That doesn't make AI useless, it just makes it a normal technology: useful for some, useless for others, capable of being abused and likely to waste a lot of time when used unwisely: https://www.normaltech.ai/ Normal technologies are fine.
  • That's why AI bosses are so committed to talking up AI's destructive potential: because destructive potential is nonetheless potential.
