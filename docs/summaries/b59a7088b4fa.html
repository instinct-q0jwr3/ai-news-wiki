Jensen Huang: AI leaders want to be 'relieved of the laws we do have'

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme amazon nvidia ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang says AI leaders who call for regulation are not seeking new legislation but want to be 'relieved of the laws we do have,' citing 'ulterior reasons,' Business Insider reports. Huang made the remarks in an interview with CBS News aired Sunday, pushing back on the idea that he is an AI doomer.

Highlights

  • Huang: AI leaders calling for regulation want relief from existing laws, not new ones.
  • He attributed 'ulterior reasons' to those calls.
  • The comments came in a CBS News interview aired Sunday.
