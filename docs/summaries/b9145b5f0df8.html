Chrome AI features wasting much bandwidth

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

A blogger attributes unusually high bandwidth use to Chrome’s AI features after observing a decline when those features were disabled. The account suggests comparing network activity before and after a settings change.

The post supplies a personal before-and-after observation, not controlled evidence of the cause or a representative monthly total. Downloads, versions and other background activity could affect the comparison. No browser settings were changed for this editorial pass.
