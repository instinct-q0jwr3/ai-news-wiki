Banks are now trying to offload the AI data center debt they just underwrote

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high

Summary

JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, SMBC and MUFG are exploring significant risk transfers on AI data center debt, per the Financial Times, shifting their riskiest slices to outside investors because they hit internal limits on AI-linked exposure. A bank group including JPMorgan and MUFG has spent over six months trying to distribute $38 billion of construction debt for an Oracle-leased Texas and Wisconsin project, and some lenders will sell pieces to non-bank buyers at a discount. A Man Group credit executive calls the deal sizes 'out of scale to anything we've thought about'.

Highlights

  • Banks are shopping AI data center debt they underwrote.
  • They are hitting internal exposure limits.
  • $38B Oracle-linked project debt has been stuck over six months.
  • Some pieces offered to non-bank buyers at a discount.
