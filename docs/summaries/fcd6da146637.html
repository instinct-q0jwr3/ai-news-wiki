The Waymo effect: how AI is quietly making research less collaborative

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

A researchagenda.news essay argues that AI is quietly making research less collaborative, using Waymo as a metaphor. Riding an empty car, the author notes, teaches us to prefer our own company, and frictionless technologies reduce the small interactions that collaboration depends on.

He warns research leaders to watch for it. It is an opinion piece without data.
