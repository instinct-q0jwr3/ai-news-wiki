OpenAI nears an up to $30B round led by investors from the UAE and Coatue at a $1.25T pre-money valuation, as an "opportunistic" bridge ahead of an IPO

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI is closing in on a funding round of up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation - over $1 trillion - ahead of a potential IPO, CNBC reports from people familiar with the deal. Investors from the United Arab Emirates are leading alongside Coatue, and the financing is positioned as an 'opportunistic' bridge rather than a needed raise, after investors approached OpenAI with proposals in September.

The company told investors on Thursday it aims to secure signatures by the end of next week, with the final size still being set. Alongside the round, OpenAI is negotiating a commercial deal for more computing capacity from the UAE - the capital and the compute arriving from the same place, as with the company's other recent megadeals.
