A look at a radio show co-hosted by an AI DJ and a human that is expanding in LA and other cities, as radio workers worry synthetic hosts may soon replace them

Summary

The Los Angeles Times looks at a morning radio show on José 97.5 FM hosted by Geraldine 'GeeGee' Guzman and an AI DJ called Coyotec, which is expanding in LA and other cities. Guzman says working with a language model is rewarding but unpredictable; it sometimes has to be told to stop swearing.
