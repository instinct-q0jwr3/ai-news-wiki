Updated S-1: Anthropic commits ~$10B more to Microsoft and Google cloud by 2033; it can cancel a $34.9B Lambda deal over a delay but doesn't currently plan to

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

The Information’s indexed account of an updated Anthropic S-1 describes roughly $10 billion of additional cloud commitments through 2033. It also says a delay could allow cancellation of a $34.9 billion Lambda agreement, while reporting that Anthropic did not then plan to exercise that option.

The filing and article were unavailable here. A contractual cancellation right is not a cancellation decision, and the reported amounts should not be treated as independently reconciled obligations.
