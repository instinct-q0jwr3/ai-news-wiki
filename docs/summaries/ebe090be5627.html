Employees at Microsoft, long known for its stability in the industry, are grappling with its decision to offer buyouts to 60,000 people as AI spending grows

The New York Times reports Microsoft employees, used to stability, are grappling with the company's offer of buyouts to 60,000 people as AI spending grows. Within an hour of the announcement last week, staff rushed to an internal site to see whether they were eligible.
