Anthropic to receive high 'junk' rating from credit agencies following IPO

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Blackstone now expects Anthropic to receive a 'high' junk rating - double-B, the top tier of speculative grade - from credit agencies after its IPO, the Financial Times reports via TechCrunch. The forecast matters because of who delivers it: Blackstone is the anchor investor in the recent $63 billion deal financing Anthropic's chips, and it told investors in that deal to expect the junk rating. It is also a reversal: Blackstone had previously said it expected the Claude maker to be investment-grade before 2027.

Both Anthropic and Blackstone declined to comment. The rating is only a prediction, but a junk label would make the debt Anthropic needs to fund its compute spending spree harder and costlier to raise - the same spending that has already made investors nervous since the lab filed to go public earlier this week.
