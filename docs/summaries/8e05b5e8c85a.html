Open-source version of Adobe Creative Suite, built with Rust, using AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

PetaPixel reports that software engineer Brandon Thomas released ArtCraft, a free open-source set of apps modelled on Adobe's Creative Cloud: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, Lightroom, Acrobat, After Effects and InDesign equivalents for macOS, Windows and Linux. He says he built them in Rust with heavy help from an Anthropic model, while stressing he is an experienced engineer and not a casual 'vibe coder'.

The suite is alpha, and Thomas targets full feature parity within a month, which the article calls very ambitious. He describes the work as a clean-room reimplementation, but PetaPixel notes patents could still be an open question, and that parity of features does not mean parity of performance, colour accuracy or plug-in support that professionals need. The piece also points out that AI has made reverse-engineering software cheap, weakening the old protection of expensive, undocumented code.

Highlights

  • Seven Adobe-style apps for macOS, Windows and Linux, free and open source, in alpha.
  • Built in Rust with an Anthropic model; author says he is an experienced engineer.
  • Goal: 100% feature parity within a month, called ambitious by the article.
  • Clean-room claim leaves patent questions open.
  • Professionals may hesitate over colour accuracy, plug-ins and support.
