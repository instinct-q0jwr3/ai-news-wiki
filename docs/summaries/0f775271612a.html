Mistral launches a preview of Mistral Large 4, or Le Chonk, a 1T model it claims tops any open model developed in the US or Europe; weights are due October 27

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

VentureBeat covers the preview of Mistral Large 4, code-named Le Chonk: a one-trillion-parameter multimodal model with 49B active parameters, trained from scratch in about two months on 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's European data centres, across more than 160 languages. Weights follow on October 27 under a custom Mistral license, after about three weeks of testing with developers, security leaders and authorities. Mistral pitches it for enterprises and governments to run on sovereign infrastructure, with a focus on coding, cybersecurity, finance and manufacturing. No API pricing was given.

Highlights

  • 1T parameters, 49B active; trained on 4,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs.
  • 160+ languages including all EU official languages.
  • Weights due October 27 under a custom license.
  • Sovereign-infrastructure pitch; no API pricing yet.
  • Basis: the opening of the article was read.
