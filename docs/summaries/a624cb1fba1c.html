An Interview With Intuit CEO Sasan Goodarzi About Accounting and AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

A Stratechery interview with Intuit CEO Sasan Goodarzi, who joined in 2004 from Siemens and Honeywell and became CEO in 2019. They discuss being an effective generalist, how Intuit has lasted 44 years, the shift from Quicken to QuickBooks, TurboTax and the consumer space. A main theme is accountants, Intuit's key acquisition channel, and how AI could turn the relationship from partners to customers. Ben Thompson argues Intuit looks like it is being disrupted, and Goodarzi makes the case that it is well placed to grow its market. Only the intro was read.
