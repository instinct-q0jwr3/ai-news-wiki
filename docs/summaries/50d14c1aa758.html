The (AI) Nature of the Firm

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Cameron Gordon applies Coase's 1937 'The Nature of the Firm' to AI: humans form firms because bounded individuals must cooperate for long-term planning, making the corporation a command-and-control architecture within market economies. The hook is Moltbook, a Reddit-style environment for decentralised AI agents interacting through message boards, which drew attention from Andrej Karpathy and an overview from Simon Willison. Gordon frames such agent collectives as a potential next step on the AI research frontier.

Highlights

  • Reads Coase's theory of the firm onto emerging AI-agent collectives.
  • Centered on Moltbook, a Reddit-style message-board environment for AI agents.
  • Notes Karpathy's attention and Simon Willison's technical overview.
  • Poses agent organizations as a research-frontier question, not just a curiosity.
