Hundreds of people attended a pro-AI party in DC on September 26, in what the organizers called a counterprogram to the "bipartisan psychosis" over data centers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

The Washington Sun reports from a pro-data-center party at PubKey, a cryptocurrency-themed bar in downtown DC: hundreds attended (a thousand RSVP'd) for a celebration of AI infrastructure, with themed cocktails - a peach-forward 'Loudoun County Iced Tea' nodding to Virginia's data center alley, an 'SMR-tini' espresso martini, and a 'Closed Loop Spritz' that sold out. The party was explicitly defensive, counterprogramming what the invite called a 'bipartisan psychosis' over data centers - at a moment when polls show three-quarters of Americans oppose local data center construction.

The organizers are three men from libertarian and conservative spaces: Jonas Du of Pirate Wires (Mike Solana's media company), Ed Tarnowski of the R Street Institute, and Casey Given of Young Voices. Industry speakers, including Build American AI's Nathan Leamer leading a pro-infrastructure chant, focused on beating China, conquering 'fear of the unknown,' and AI's world-transforming potential - while outside, communities fight data center construction tooth and nail.
