HiRO-ACEv2: Simulating extreme weather at 3 km resolution for a fraction of the cost

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high

Summary

Ai2's HiRO-ACEv2 emulates extreme weather at 3 km resolution. It reproduces rare rain, wind and pressure events with high accuracy, and the full system makes global 3 km climate data at about 1/300th the cost of the X-SHiELD physics model.

Highlights

  • Global 3 km climate data at ~1/300th the cost.
  • Reproduces rare extreme events accurately.
