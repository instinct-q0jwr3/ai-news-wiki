Google has acquired Crusoe's data center site in southeast Wyoming

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Crusoe announced that Google acquired its southeast Wyoming data-center site, ending Crusoe's pre-development work on site selection, approvals and a hybrid power strategy with Tallgrass Energy. CEO Chase Lochmiller says the pre-construction work set up the project for construction. Crusoe says it has more than 6 GW contracted and over 40 GW in its pipeline. The announcement is dated Nov. 6, 2026.

Highlights

  • Crusoe: 6 GW contracted, 40+ GW in pipeline
