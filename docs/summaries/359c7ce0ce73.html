ArXiv limits preprint submissions to two per month per submitter, as AI access fuels a record 40,363 submissions in September 2026, vs. 20,569 in September 2024

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

arXiv is limiting each submitter to two preprints per month, starting October 1, 2026. The blog says a small share of authors submit large numbers of low-quality papers, using up volunteer moderators' time and delaying good papers by days or weeks. September 2026 saw a record 40,363 submissions as AI tools made mass production easy.

arXiv calls the limit a stopgap while it works out new best practice for authors using advanced AI tools, and applies it across all submitters and categories.

Highlights

  • Two preprint submissions per month per submitter from Oct 1, 2026.
  • Record 40,363 submissions in September 2026.
  • Moderators are volunteers; a few authors consume a large share of their time.
  • Described as a stopgap policy, not a final answer.
