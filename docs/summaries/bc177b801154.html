Why AI resembles a charismatic religious leader

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

Many Americans are turning to AI for spiritual guidance.

Andrey Suslov/iStock via Getty Images Plus People are turning to artificial intelligence for a seemingly unending variety of reasons .

Many ask AI what to cook for dinner or how to develop a workout plan.

Highlights

  • Some even rely on AI to create school handouts or revise a work email.
  • Increasingly, however, they are asking it different kinds of questions .
  • According to recent research from the Pew Research Center , about 1 in 10 American adults now report using AI chatbots for emotional support or advice.
  • By helping people interpret their lives , AI is entering territory historically occupied by therapists, spiritual advisers and religious communities.
  • As a scholar of religion who studies spirituality and new religious movements, I think this shift raises a provocative question .
  • Could artificial intelligence represent the beginnings of a new kind of religious movement?
  • However, those factors are not necessarily what defines something as “religion.” In fact, scholars of new religious movements have long recognized the difficulty of deciding where religion begins and ends .
  • A charismatic religious leader typically gathers followers who collectively recognize that person’s extraordinary authority.
