Quoting Ben Hylak

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison quotes Ben Hylak skewering the industry's double standard on agent-native software: everyone wants every other product to shrink into a headless MCP server feeding their agent, while insisting their own product is the exception whose UI customers love.

The quip lands because it describes a real strategic tension: if agents mediate more software use, every vendor wants to be the agent's surface rather than its plumbing.

Highlights

  • Hylak: everyone wants other products reduced to headless MCP servers - but not their own
  • Captures the vendor dilemma of agent-mediated software: be the surface, not the plumbing
