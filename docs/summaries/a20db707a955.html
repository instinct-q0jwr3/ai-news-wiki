Highlights

  • Wang's premise: most people never say what they want, and unnamed dreams die in logistics
  • Muse is framed as a second mind whose job is to learn what you want and make it happen
  • It builds the plan, sends the email, makes the phone call, finds the funding, keeps you on schedule
  • The goal: leave humans only the wanting and the dreaming
  • Wang: history was shaped by the few fanatics who made their wants real
  • The open question he embraces: what 8 billion people with full agency will actually want
