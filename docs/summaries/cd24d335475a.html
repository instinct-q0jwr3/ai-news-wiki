Scrivendo Wohpe

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-30 · confidence: high

antirez amazon external-evaluation ai-coding-agents

Summary

In this Italian-language essay (an English translation exists as his previous post), Salvatore Sanfilippo marks the publication of Wohpe, his first science fiction novel and first long prose work, after two years of writing. Despite twenty years of blogging, he says writing the novel meant learning to write from zero. His analogy is the chess beginner who knows the rules and some strategy, yet to whom all moves look equivalent; a novice writer likewise has no taste for sentences. Real progress comes from writing itself and from rereading a few chosen model books deeply, not from the advice to simply read more.

He is blunt about the economics: a successful Italian SF novel from a small or mid-size publisher sells around 500 copies, and most books sell under 100 - his stupidest program, he notes, found ten times more users. Few activities have such a monstrous gap between the effort an work demands and the public's response, and would-be authors should know that from the start. He still closes by urging readers to write prose: writing is a search, and if you persist, you find things - there is a reason it was long considered the highest art.
