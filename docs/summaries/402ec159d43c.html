Hundreds of AI-decompiled or vibe-coded clones of games appeared in recent weeks, ported to run in browsers; some big titles like GTA: Vice City, Halo play well

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Kotaku reports that hundreds of vibe-coded, AI-decompiled emulators and games have appeared in the past couple of weeks, playable in browser tabs: Black Ops zombies, Skate 3, Halo, Modern Warfare 2, GTA: Vice City and The Simpsons: Hit and Run among them.

The trigger, per the piece, is that Claude's latest update, Claude Opus 5.5, is extremely good at decompiling, so reconstructing a game's source now takes a fraction of the months it used to take coders. The wave also includes native Fallout and Call of Duty ports and AI-generated mash-up mods like Super Mario 64 inside Elden Ring. The legal blowback is presumably coming; read the original for the full picture.
