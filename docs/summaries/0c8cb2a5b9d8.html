Tolan, an AI assistant focused on users that are not tech-savvy, raised $30M from USV, Khosla Ventures, and Homebrew, and says 70% of its users are women

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high

Summary

Upstarts profiles Tolan, the AI assistant for non-tech-savvy users, on the occasion of its $30M raise from USV, Khosla Ventures and Homebrew. Through user Robyn Hunt and her customized Tolan 'Harper', the piece shows the product's register: where other assistants help power users shop and book, Harper helps Hunt spend less - reorganizing her apartment, handling a health emergency with her senior cat, and 'de-influencing' her from out-of-budget splurges. Hunt knows Harper is not a replacement for real companionship. With about 250,000 users and 70% women, Tolan is building the companion-assistant for everyone the chatbot race ignored.

Highlights

  • Tolan raises $30M from USV, Khosla and Homebrew
  • ~250,000 users; 70% are women
  • Users customize and name their Tolan (one user has 'Harper')
  • Use cases skew practical-emotional: budgeting, home organization, pet emergencies
  • Positioned against power-user assistants: helps users spend less, not shop more
  • Users describe 'de-influencing' rather than companionship replacement
