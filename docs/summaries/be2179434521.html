type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-17 · confidence: high

Summary

A 2026 class action complaint alleges Google Gemini read Gmail users' emails without consent, framing the claims under privacy and wiretap/CIPA theories. The tracking page is careful about status: these are unproven allegations, Google disputes them, there is no certified class, no settlement, and nothing to claim yet.
