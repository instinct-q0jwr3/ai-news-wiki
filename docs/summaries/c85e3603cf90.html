[AINews] AMD buys World Labs for $8.2B, as Atlas solves sparse reconstruction problem for robotics, design and more

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-09-30 · confidence: high

latent.space

Summary

Latent Space's AINews digest leads with AMD buying World Labs for $8.2B (the official post is coy, but AMD being public reveals the price) and highlights what AMD is actually getting: Atlas, World Labs' first-of-its-kind omni model that predicts a new camera view from 2D images - essentially solving sparse reconstruction by combining generative models with multiview geometry, outperforming even specialized state-of-the-art models. Fei-Fei Li's reflection post traces the buildout: leading image/video/spatial-reconstruction training teams since 2024, plus the SceniX acquisition for robotics simulation. Atlas's predicted uses span RL environments for robotics, scene generation for therapy and entertainment, and real-world reconstruction for real estate, design, and construction. The digest notes it checked 12 subreddits and 544 Twitter accounts for the period 9/26-9/28.
