The Great Zipper of Capitalism

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high

works-on-my-machine

Summary

Scott Werner tells how he stumbled into what he thinks is a real unbundling opportunity. In May 2024 nobody was talking about building things with AI in Ruby, so he booked a New York bar for a low-stakes happy hour, hoping for ten or twenty people. Sixty showed up, and he accidentally became an organizer: that evening grew into Artificial Ruby, a monthly Ruby-and-AI meetup with speakers from around the world.

Running the community taught him how much invisible labor sits inside a seemingly simple event: RSVPs scattered across platforms, mailing lists to sync, promotion before and after, regulars to track, and pizza quantities to estimate from RSVP-to-attendance conversion rates that swing with weather, season and vibes - the 6:10 PM feeling of five arrivals, fifteen pizzas and forty-seven yeses. His 'great zipper' is the old bundling-unbundling cycle of capitalism: this unglamorous operational mess, still held together by CSVs, looks to him like a market that doesn't exist yet - one an ambitious builder could serve.
