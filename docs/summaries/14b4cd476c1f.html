CrowdStrike: China-linked hackers used AI tool ARTEX with a DeepSeek LLM and another model to target 200+ organizations in 11 days, compromising at least nine

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

NBC News, citing CrowdStrike, reports that Chinese state-linked hackers used AI tools - including the popular DeepSeek model and another model, via a tool called ARTEX - to launch cyberattacks against more than 200 organizations worldwide in just 11 days, compromising at least nine. CrowdStrike frames the campaign as an illustration of AI letting hackers operate at a scale that previously required far more human effort.
