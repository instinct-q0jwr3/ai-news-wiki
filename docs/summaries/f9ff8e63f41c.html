Introducing Pika H3 Flex

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Pika introduces H3 Flex, its post-trained version of MiniMax's H3 video model, aimed at professional iteration rather than first-shot generation. The headline claim is speed: a 15-second clip with synchronized audio at 768p in as little as seven seconds of transformer computation, achieved by post-training the model to generate in six steps instead of fifty and then refining the distilled model for quality and control. Flex takes up to 12 references - sketches, character sheets, product photos, moodboards, voice samples - exports five separate audio stems, finishes at up to 4K, and is being released as open weights.

At launch, Pika says Flex ranks #1 among open-weight models and #7 overall on DesignArena's text-to-video leaderboard, positioning it as the fast, steerable base creatives build variations on.

Highlights

  • Pika H3 Flex: post-trained MiniMax H3 built for professional iteration
  • 15s clip with synchronized audio at 768p in ~7 seconds of transformer compute
  • Generation distilled to 6 steps from 50, then refined for quality and control
  • Up to 12 references, five separate audio stems, up to 4K output
  • Open weights released; #1 open-weight and #7 overall on DesignArena text-to-video
