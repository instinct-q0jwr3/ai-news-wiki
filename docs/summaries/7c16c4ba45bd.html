AI-ready biological data: $1.8B global commitment

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Biohub announces an expansion of its Virtual Biology Initiative: $1.8 billion in funding, data, computation and measurement technology for AI-ready biological data, which it calls the largest coordinated commitment of its kind. The aim is an open resource for predictive models of biology and, eventually, treatment of disease.

The Department of Energy will invest more than $500 million over five years in lab measurement and computation, and NIH will coordinate datasets from more than $500 million in earlier federal spending, which Biohub will help standardize. Google DeepMind, Isomorphic Labs and Meta together add $300 million. These figures are from Biohub's announcement.
