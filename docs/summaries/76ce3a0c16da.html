Hoe wordt de overheid weer 'van de IT'?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

bert-hubert

Summary

Bert Hubert asks how the Dutch government can become 'of IT' again. Today it isn't: it lays its ear at a big-tech-dominated ecosystem for every decision, so the answer is always American platforms, run by third parties, under American (surveillance) law - now with AI baked in whether you want it or not.

His prescription is deliberately small: grand plans (like the new national cloud design) collide with a deep, learned helplessness - IT is 'the digital Bermuda triangle of your political career'. Instead, rebuild trust with small, important, visible projects: a government videoconferencing service (Teams fails too, and the Americans can read along) and bringing DigiD back in house. The promised NL Digitale Dienst should get a clear mandate and real clout - per the broadly supported Zwinkels motion - and become a hands-on builder, not another layer of ICT frameworks. (The page is in Dutch; the wiki keeps content in English.)
