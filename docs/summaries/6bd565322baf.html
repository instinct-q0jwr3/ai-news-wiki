OpenAI Reportedly Raises $11 Billion at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

TechCrunch, citing Bloomberg, says OpenAI has raised $11 billion of a round at a $1.25 trillion valuation. The round has not closed and could reach about $20 billion in the coming weeks, below an earlier $30 billion target. In the same news cycle Bloomberg reported that buyers of Anthropic's planned IPO shares want a lower valuation, possibly $1.1 trillion against $2 trillion expected a month ago.

TechCrunch links the jitters to the Democratic midterm win and voter hostility to data centers, which raise the chance of regulation. OpenAI had not commented.

Highlights

  • Reported: $11B raised at $1.25T valuation, round still open
  • Could total about $20B, short of a $30B goal
  • Anthropic IPO buyers reportedly eye as low as $1.1T
  • Midterms and data-center backlash are cited as market risks
  • Based on Bloomberg sources, unconfirmed by OpenAI
