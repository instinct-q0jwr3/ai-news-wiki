Pandex – Official European Law for AI Agents, over MCP

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-22 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

Pandex presents itself as a single API and MCP server for European law: national legislation with past versions and the decisions of the highest courts of the EU and seven countries, grounded in the institutions that publish them, with citations attached to every answer. The landing page lists document counts per jurisdiction and, unusually, publishes the gaps it knows about: French decisions served only through Judilibre need a registered account there and the official dumps run about two months behind, older Dutch decisions have no text at Rechtspraak, and several court or treaty sites answer its server with Cloudflare or proof-of-work challenges. Pricing is listed as free during the beta, one euro per thousand requests for products, and a corpus license from 1,000 euros a month, with a fixed two-year rate for the first ten customers.

All figures come from the vendor's own page, dated 17 November 2026 for its institution register, and were not audited against the source institutions. Coverage counts describe what the company holds, not the completeness of any national archive, and the beta pricing is an offer that can change rather than a stable quote. No API call or MCP integration was tested in this pass.

Highlights

  • One API and one MCP server across EU and seven countries' legislation and top courts
  • Answers are grounded in real law with citations, per the vendor
  • Each article carries past versions and the date each took effect
  • Known coverage gaps are published per institution, including Judilibre and Cloudflare blocks
  • Document counts are self-reported and dated 17 November 2026
  • Beta key is free; usage pricing starts at one euro per 1,000 requests
  • Vendor page was read directly; no endpoint was exercised
