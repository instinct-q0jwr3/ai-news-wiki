VFX company DNEG's AI unit Brahma, whose customers include Warner Bros. and the NBA, raised $150M led by Indian PE firm Multiples at a $2B post-money valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Brahma AI, the AI unit of DNEG - the UK-based visual-effects studio behind the Dune series and The Odyssey, owned by India's Prime Focus - raised $150M from Indian private equity firm Multiples Alternate Asset Management at a $2B valuation. Prime Focus said Brahma has a further $100M of investor demand and may upsize the round, and will hold a 66% stake through DNEG after the raise.

Brahma makes AI-generated audiovisual content for enterprise customers, aiming to take technology proven in high-end film and TV production to enterprises and creators across every industry - 'to build the AI-native technology platform through which the world's leading enterprises' create content.

Highlights

  • $150M raised at a $2B valuation from Multiples Alternate Asset Management.
  • Brahma is DNEG's AI unit; Prime Focus keeps a 66% stake post-raise.
  • Further $100M of investor demand may expand the round.
  • Productizes blockbuster VFX-grade AI audiovisual technology for enterprises.
  • DNEG's credits include the Dune series and The Odyssey.
