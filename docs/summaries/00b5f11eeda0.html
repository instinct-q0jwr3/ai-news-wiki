Summary

Brennan Kenneth Brown's third guide to the 'smallnets' makes the case for blogging on Gemini, a minimal internet protocol built in 2019 by the pseudonymous Solderpunk. Instead of HTML, every page is written in Gemtext, a stripped-down format with only headings, links and lists; every connection runs over TLS on port 1965, one request, one response, then the connection closes. Using it requires a dedicated browser rather than a web browser.

The pitch is partly practical and partly cultural. Practical: no JavaScript, CSS or even HTML to worry about, and hosting is cheap enough to run on low-spec hardware. Cultural: with no viable revenue model and general obscurity, Gemini space has no generative-AI swarms, no agents and no ads, just humans, an appealing answer to the anxiety that AI could take over the open web. The guide, published from Calgary as issue 288 of brennan.day, aims at creation, socialization and interactivity, and also touches Gopher and Spartan.
