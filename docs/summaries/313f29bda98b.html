China has told SoftBank it will not allow the $5.4B acquisition of ABB's robotics division to go ahead; China is the division's biggest market

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

The Financial Times reports, citing sources, that China has told SoftBank it will not allow its $5.4B acquisition of ABB's robotics division to proceed. China is the division's biggest market, and the unit has a mega-factory in Pudong.

Highlights

  • China tells SoftBank it will not clear the $5.4B ABB Robotics deal
