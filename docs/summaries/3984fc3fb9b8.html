Surprise, Meta's latest AI gimmick is just underpaid humans

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

hacker-news meta

Summary

404 Media reports that Meta's Muse - the AI agent chief AI officer Alexander Wang said was expanding to a phone beta for making outbound calls to US businesses - leans on 'a human agent layer' for the heavy lifting. An internal message board post reviewed by 404 describes Muse handing requests to a trained human agent who places the call and works it through, then reports back with a transcript and summary.

The report lands in a long tradition of AI products quietly powered by underpaid human labor, and the coverage is scathing: the product purportedly makes calls for people too anxious to call a barbershop, while the actual conversations are humans calling other humans, with Meta obfuscating the arrangement in its internal description of the feature.

Highlights

  • 404 Media: Muse's phone-call feature hands requests to trained human agents who place the calls.
  • Internal post describes Muse 'no longer working alone', routing calls to humans with transcript and summary.
  • Muse's phone beta was announced by chief AI officer Alexander Wang for calls to US businesses.
  • Framed as the latest case of an AI product revealed to be cover for cheap human labor.
