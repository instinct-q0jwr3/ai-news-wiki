Frontier Pre-Training at Microsoft AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Microsoft AI says it finished pre-training MAI-Base-2, a mixture-of-experts foundation model with 80B active and 2.8T total parameters, after June's MAI-Thinking-1 built on MAI-Base-1. It claims the model beats every open-weight model it tested on held-out evaluations.

The post is a company report on its pre-training effort, so the claims are unverified.

Highlights

  • MAI-Base-2: MoE with 80B active, 2.8T total parameters.
  • Claims to beat all tested open-weight models; self-reported.
