Apple unveils the 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6, with a 12-core CPU and a 12-core GPU, up to 32GB of unified memory, and Wi-Fi 7, from $1,999 on November 6

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple announced the 14-inch MacBook Pro with the M6 chip: a 12-core CPU with what it calls the world's fastest CPU core, a 12-core GPU delivering up to 30% faster graphics and 25% faster AI workloads than M5, up to 32GB of unified memory, Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6, and up to 24 hours of battery life.

Apple claims up to 11x faster graphics and 13x faster AI performance versus M1, leaning on Neural Accelerators in the GPU and a dual 16-core Neural Engine for running LLMs locally. Pre-orders open October 28 at $1,999, with availability November 6, in space black and silver.

Highlights
