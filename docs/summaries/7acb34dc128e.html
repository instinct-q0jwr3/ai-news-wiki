Oncology-trial AI startup Altis Labs raises a $25M Series A

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Altis Labs, an AI software company for drug development, has raised a $25 million Series A, CEO Felix Baldauf-Lenschen told Axios Pro. Its AI model analyzes CT scans taken during oncology trials and produces predictions tied to patient survival.

Highlights

  • Altis Labs raised a $25M Series A.
  • Its model analyzes CT scans from oncology trials to produce predictions tied to patient survival.
  • The raise was disclosed by CEO Felix Baldauf-Lenschen to Axios Pro.
