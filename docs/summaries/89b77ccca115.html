Software engineers don't get to be Luddites about AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Sean Goedecke argues software engineers don't get to be Luddites about AI. The violence framing is real - an Indianapolis council member's house was shot up over datacenter support, Sam Altman's home was firebombed and then shot at - and anti-AI writers like Brian Merchant and Gavin Mueller have explicitly reclaimed the Luddite label, arguing the 19th-century machine-breakers were right.

His counter: for engineers, whose profession is the automation machine, Luddism is self-negating. Being righteously angry about AI is understandable, but the weavers' cry against inevitable and irresistible laws is not a position available to the people who build the looms.
