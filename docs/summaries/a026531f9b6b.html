Cloudflare's Clairic and Clairic-flash

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high

Summary

Cloudflare releases two open-weight Clairic extraction models with a typed interface for fields, confidence and source spans. It describes a move from a single pass to parallel autoregressive field generation and a tradeoff between latency and accuracy.

The company explicitly labels its comparisons as self-reported internal evaluations, including synthetic receipts and customer-shaped cases. Perfect scores on those sets do not establish error-free extraction in general. A temporary price promotion and a change in which model occupies the existing endpoint also mean the old preview name is not a stable capability or price guarantee.

Highlights

  • Models target structured extraction
  • Field generation is parallel but autoregressive
  • Typed outputs include confidence and spans
  • Benchmarks are internal and directional
  • Preview endpoint changes its underlying model
  • Temporary price differs from later standard pricing
