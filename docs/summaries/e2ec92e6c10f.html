Biopharma world-model startup Mithrl raises a $20M Series A

Summary

Mithrl, an AI infrastructure company for biopharma, raised a $20 million Series A led by Obvious Ventures, CEO Vivek Adarsh told Axios exclusively. The company integrates its proprietary biomedical world model with the R&D systems of pharmaceutical companies.
