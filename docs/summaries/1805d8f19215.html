Gemini Robotics Embodied Reasoning 2 is now available on Google Cloud

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics Embodied Reasoning 2 is now in public preview on Google Cloud, bringing the specialized high-level 'robot brain' model to enterprise production. ER 2 combines spatio-temporal video understanding, long-horizon planning and safety guardrails across embodiments from stationary arms to humanoids.

Developers declare low-level control interfaces - Vision-Language-Action models or navigation APIs - as tools, and the model orchestrates them through multi-minute tasks involving hundreds of decisions, self-correcting and generalizing to novel situations, with natural conversation and multi-robot collaboration. Google Cloud adds security, multi-agent orchestration and lifecycle management.

Highlights

  • Gemini Robotics ER 2 in public preview on Google Cloud
  • High-level reasoning layer for any robot embodiment
  • Orchestrates VLA models and hardware APIs as tools
  • Multi-minute, hundreds-of-decisions tasks with self-correction
