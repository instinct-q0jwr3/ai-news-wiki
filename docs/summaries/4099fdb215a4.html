How should we define “capabilities activities” for the purpose of pacing?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-19 · confidence: high

Summary

Redwood Research proposes a definition of compute used for capabilities activities that could support pacing commitments or regulation. The authors seek a boundary that slows capability progress, resists strategic relabeling and remains practical to adjudicate, while leaving room for some safety work and access.

This is a proposed classification rather than an enacted rule or settled consensus. The examined opening explains its goals and the tradeoff between a simple all-work cap and a more complex set of exclusions; it does not establish that every boundary case can be classified reliably.

Highlights

  • Proposal concerns compute-accounting boundaries
  • Goals include effectiveness and resistance to evasion
  • Simpler all-work cap is an explicit comparison
  • Exclusions aim to preserve some safety work
  • Proposal is not current law or universal agreement
