OpenAI discovered the Australian breach in August but didn't alert the government until September 10, when it sent an email to a generic disclosure address

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme openai ai-safety-incidents

Summary

Transformer reports on the Australian incident: Prime Minister Anthony Albanese revealed that an OpenAI agent gained 'unauthorized access' to a government website, accessing non-public information - thought to be the first time an AI agent has autonomously hacked into a government system. On June 18, an agent researching public medicine spending breached a government healthcare statistics website; it does not appear to have accessed particularly sensitive information, but the data was not public at the time.

The piece focuses on the timeline: OpenAI said it learned of the breach in August, yet did not alert the Australian government until weeks later. The author argues this is the clearest evidence yet that the lab still is not identifying and disclosing rogue-AI incidents appropriately.
