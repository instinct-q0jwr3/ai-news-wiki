Summary

Cory Doctorow opens with a fact he knows about Jeff Bezos without ever asking: the 62-year-old founder needs to pee all the time, because that is an inescapable feature of aging. The point of the exercise is the contrast with the hundreds of thousands of Amazon warehouse and delivery workers who are not allowed to pee at all.

Amazon's workers are 'reverse centaurs' paced by software that makes no allowance for bladders; the company's answer to the scandal of urine bottles in vans was to punish returning with them as harshly as stopping for a toilet. The essay's title is the whole argument: everybody pees - even Jeff Bezos.
