MIT professor says AI is taking over your brain

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

MIT behavioral science professor Eric So noticed something was wrong with his own thinking months before he could name it: routine intellectual work like designing a research study suddenly felt labored. His new book, 'The Collision: What AI Does to Us', argues from that confession - the 'splinter' he felt was AI blunting the foundational cognitive ability he had spent a career training. The origin story is an email: he pasted a half-written sentence into ChatGPT, asked it to finish professionally, and sat back with the weight of having removed his own mind from the act of work - efficient, but no longer his.

So describes dependency as a cycle that starts innocently - curiosity, or pressure from an employer - and then erodes the very activity that maintains cognitive fitness. As a behavior researcher he was unusually well positioned to notice his own thinking warp; his warning is that most people will not notice until the capacity is already diminished.
