Artificial Analysis launches the Cyber Index Alliance with partners Collinear, IBM, Nvidia, and Vercel to evaluate how AI agents find and fix vulnerabilities

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high

Summary

Artificial Analysis is launching a Cyber Index - an independent benchmark of how AI models perform on enterprise cyber defense - alongside a Cyber Index Alliance of industry partners (Collinear, IBM and others) contributing expert input and datasets. The index measures the defensive loop: discovering vulnerabilities in a codebase, reproducing and validating them, and patching them without breaking existing functionality. Models work from source code, as a security engineer auditing an application would, and are explicitly not asked to build working exploits.

The framing is procurement: as attackers automate with AI, security teams need to know which models actually help defenders and what they cost to run. The index combines partner-contributed and open benchmarks, including two private held-out evaluations (CWE-bench and DeepsecBench), to keep the comparison like-for-like and uncontaminated. The Alliance is open to new members.
