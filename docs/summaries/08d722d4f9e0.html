Claude's Text Output Now Carries an Invisible Watermark

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Invisible watermarking is coming to frontier-model text output in the EU: as of August 2, the EU AI Act requires providers serving the European market to mark AI-generated content, and the major model developers that signed the Code of Practice are implementing watermarks to comply. Anthropic says future Claude models will generate text containing a watermark - a statistical signal for estimating the likelihood that Claude wrote a passage - and the cached coverage indicates OpenAI is adding an invisible watermark to eligible ChatGPT and Codex text output in the EU over the coming weeks.

The rollout marks the first large-scale deployment of text watermarking by major labs, driven by regulation rather than research. Watermarking won't be provider-specific: each major developer signing the Code of Practice implements its own scheme, which leaves open the interoperability question of who can detect whose marks - and how robust the signals prove against paraphrasing and translation.
