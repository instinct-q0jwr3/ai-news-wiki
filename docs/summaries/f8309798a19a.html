OpenAI urges the US to lead global frontier-safety standards

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high

techmeme openai ai-safety-incidents ai-policy-regulation

Summary

Ahead of Sam Altman's address at the UN, OpenAI released a set of international AI safety standards and urged the United States to lead a global effort to develop safety and security standards for building frontier AI systems, Axios reports. The move comes as world leaders, chiefly the US and China, weigh how to mitigate AI risk.

Highlights

  • OpenAI published international AI safety standards ahead of Altman's UN address.
  • It urges the US to lead development of global safety and security standards for frontier systems.
  • The appeal lands as the US and China weigh AI risk mitigation.
