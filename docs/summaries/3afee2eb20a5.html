Summary

Part One of Ed Zitron's premium 'Hater's Guide to Circular Financing' opens with a satirical set piece: an NVIDIA company meeting where Jensen Huang leads the crowd in celebrating that the AI labs can't actually afford his GPUs - 'Clammy Sammy and Wario Amodei's' labs lose money, while 'big strong men like Satya Nadella' beg for Vera Rubin shipments. The cartoon version makes the essay's point physical: everyone's money is everyone else's revenue.

Beneath the theater, the piece sets up the series' argument about the AI build-out's circular money flows - NVIDIA investing in the customers who buy its chips with cloud intermediaries in between - and Huang's trillion-dollar Grace Blackwell/Vera Rubin shipment claims as the hype layer on top.
