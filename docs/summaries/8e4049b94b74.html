Chip stocks slide after Broadcom boss likens AI investing to 'casino'

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Chip stocks fell on Tuesday after Broadcom's boss compared many AI investors to casino gamblers, and Chinese memory maker CXMT said it had made good progress on a new DRAM architecture that boosts density. South Korea's Kospi dropped 6.25%, Japan's Nikkei fell 1.8%, Taiwan about 2% with TSMC similar, and Samsung Electronics fell 7.4%. Basis: the opening of the Guardian's live report.

Highlights

  • Kospi down 6.25% on chip sell-off.
  • Broadcom's CEO likened AI investing to a casino.
  • CXMT claims progress on a denser DRAM architecture, pressuring Samsung.
  • Samsung down 7.4%, TSMC and Nikkei also lower.
