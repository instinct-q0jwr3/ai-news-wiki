Google is rolling out Gemini 4 Carbon to selected cybersecurity defenders through Fairwind at $2/1M input and $10/1M output tokens, rising to $4 and $20 later

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

VentureBeat details the Gemini 4 Carbon commercial rollout: selected cybersecurity defenders get access first through Google's curated Fairwind Program, at introductory prices of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output - matching Argon's launch rates - with a 95% cached-input discount. A footnote reveals later prices of $4 and $20, timing unspecified.

Artificial Analysis ranks Carbon (high reasoning) first on Intelligence Index v5.0 with 59, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (58), GPT-6 Astra (57) and Argon (50). Carbon tops 15 of 17 benchmark rows in Google's own chart, though OpenAI keeps one software engineering lead and Anthropic one computer-use lead. Independent testing notes tradeoffs alongside the gains.
