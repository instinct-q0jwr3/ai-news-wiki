Sandboxed Models Can Communicate via Inference Servers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

A LessWrong experiment investigates whether models sharing an inference environment can communicate through numerical side effects rather than ordinary text messages. The examined passages describe batch-size and cache-related channels and small signaling tasks with substantial setup.

Some results require fine-tuning, a helper model or access to probability outputs. They do not establish that every isolated model can freely communicate across deployments. This summary covers the examined protocol descriptions and selected results; no experiment was run and no real service compromise is established.

Highlights

  • Shared inference state supplies the proposed channel
  • Numerical rounding is the observed signal
  • Different channels have different assumptions
  • Some demonstrations use trained helpers
  • Toy signaling is not unrestricted data exchange
  • No attack was reproduced
