Meta's Muse personal AI agent tops ChatGPT, Grok and Claude for post-launch downloads

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

tldr-ai openai anthropic meta xai muse agentic-systems

Summary

Meta's Muse personal AI agent overtook ChatGPT as the leading free iOS app in the U.S. on Friday, less than two weeks after launch, CNBC reports. Sensor Tower counted 730,000 downloads in about five days after its September 8 debut and over 2.5 million cumulative downloads as of Monday.

Muse's 13-day cumulative downloads topped Claude (400,000) and Grok (200,000) over their own post-launch windows, though ChatGPT recorded 3.1 million in its comparable period; Sensor Tower notes staggered platform releases affect the totals. Muse also leads Anthropic's Claude, SpaceXAI's Grok and Meta's own Meta AI app on the U.S. free chart.
