Jay Clayton will likely be the White House's pick for AI czar, and he may remain in his current role as director of national intelligence

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

CBS News, citing sources, says Jay Clayton will likely be the White House pick for AI czar and may keep his job as director of national intelligence. Trump is expected to decide shortly, but a White House official calls the reporting baseless speculation until the President announces.

The role comes after David Sacks left earlier this year, and follows a White House meeting where top AI firms signed voluntary safety standards.

Highlights

  • Clayton likely AI czar, possibly while remaining DNI, per three sources.
  • White House says any report before an announcement is speculation.
  • Trump said he'd name an AI czar within days.
  • Top AI firms signed voluntary safety standards at the White House on Tuesday.
  • Clayton was confirmed DNI in July.
