F.02 Decommission

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high

Summary

Figure announces the decommission of its F.02 robot fleet as the F.03 fleet grows. F.02 had firsts including a BMW deployment, Helix and household chores. Disassembling robots full of proprietary actuators would delay the F.04 launch, so the company asked the internet what to do.

Highlights

  • F.02 fleet retired as F.03 grows.
  • Proprietary hardware must be protected on disposal.
  • Disassembly would delay F.04.
  • Company crowdsourced ideas.
