outis: Fight AI spam by generating and sending a fake "user unknown" bounce emails

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Outis is a tool that fights unwanted email by generating a fake 'user unknown' bounce for a message you received, so the sender believes your address does not exist and removes it from their list. The author targets AI-generated automated emails where the sender expects a reply. The name is Greek for 'nobody', the alias Odysseus gave the Cyclops.

Highlights

  • Sends fake 'user unknown' bounces
  • Aimed at AI-generated automated email
  • Goal: get removed from sender lists
  • Named after Odysseus's 'nobody'
