AI Data Centers Not Paying Their Costs, Will Keep Using NDAs and Seek Tax Breaks

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Senators Warren, Van Hollen and Blumenthal released a 27-page report, Power and Profits, on their nearly yearlong investigation into how AI data centers raise costs for communities, concluding that the industry is leaving ordinary citizens stuck with the bills.

The report finds the companies are not paying their fair share, keep using NDAs, and aggressively seek subsidies and tax breaks from residential ratepayers and state and local governments, while Big Tech reaps the benefits. The investigation began with letters sent in December 2025 to Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft, CoreWeave, Digital Realty and Equinix.

Highlights

  • 27-page Senate report on AI data center costs
  • Conclusion: companies are not paying their fair share
  • NDAs and subsidy-seeking documented
  • Ratepayers and local communities shoulder the costs
  • Seven developers investigated since December 2025
