DoorDash said it's only received 'several hundred' orders from AI agents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high

Summary

For all the noise about agents taking over commerce, DoorDash has a number: during its third-quarter earnings call, the company revealed it has seen 'several hundred' orders in total from third-party AI agents - against an average of 3 million deliveries a day. CEO Tony Xu's posture is neither denial nor hype: 'you never want to fight gravity. But at the same time, you don't want to be delusional.' Users still overwhelmingly order through the app.

The company is hedging constructively: it wants a healthy relationship with third-party agent makers, floats the idea of advertising inside agents someday, and has been shipping its own - a text-to-order agent in September, a CLI agent in July, the Ask DoorDash chatbot in June, and an MCP server for corporate ordering. The wider board is still being set: Amazon blocks personal agents while Walmart rolls out the red carpet, hoping to poach the agent-routed customer. The agent-commerce revolution, at least this quarter, is several hundred burritos wide.

  • DoorDash's own agents: text-to-order, a CLI agent, Ask DoorDash, and an MCP for corporate ordering
  • Open to advertising inside third-party agents someday
