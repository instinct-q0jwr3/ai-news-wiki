OpenAI Withdraws 3 Math Papers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI's math repository history file records that it withdrew three manuscripts: on algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds, on algebraicity of Kuga-Satake correspondences for K3 surfaces, and on the rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces. The withdrawn papers now carry notices explaining the gap and link to archived copies.

It also lists fixes to 14 other manuscripts, covering repaired arguments, corrected statements and clearer hypotheses, and updates to 13 more that cite revised companion papers. OpenAI added 6 formalizations, bringing top-line results formalized to 300 of 719, about 42%. This is OpenAI's own changelog, so it shows what the company admits, not an independent audit.

Highlights

  • Three manuscripts withdrawn after the 6 October release
  • Titles concern Weil classes, Kuga-Satake and the rational Hodge conjecture
  • 14 other manuscripts revised with proof repairs
  • 13 more updated to cite revised companions
