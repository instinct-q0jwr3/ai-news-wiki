Genie 4

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a new generation of its world-simulation model. Per The Verge's coverage, Genie 4 is powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash, generates interactive simulations of about two minutes (up from one), adds generated audio, allows real-time edits via natural language, and launches first as a research preview for trusted testers.
