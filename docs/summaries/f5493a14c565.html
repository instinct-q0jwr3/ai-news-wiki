Oakland-based Numa, which builds an AI platform to manage car dealership customer service, raised a $45M Series C, a source says at a $500M post-money valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

Fortune reports Numa raised a $45 million Series C led by Blue Cloud Ventures for dealership customer-service software. The article describes agents connecting to dealership systems for calls, appointments, repair updates, leads and warranty paperwork; a $500 million post-money valuation is attributed to an unnamed source.

Funding and described integrations do not independently show accuracy, labor savings or customer satisfaction. A separate executive survey cited in the article concerns broad reported agent adoption, not measured results at Numa dealerships.

Highlights

  • Funding is reported as a Series C
  • Valuation comes from an unnamed source
  • Product spans customer contact and paperwork
  • Integration claims are not performance measurements
  • Broader adoption survey is not Numa-specific evidence
