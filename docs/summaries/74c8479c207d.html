Why Connections Matter More in AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

This essay argues that professional relationships have become more important in AI as execution tools spread, frontier work requires more resources and closed research makes individual contributions harder to observe. Reputation and trusted introductions can therefore influence access to compute, jobs and funding.

The author does not claim technical ability has stopped mattering. Its numerical split between skills and connections is explicitly illustrative, not a measured statistic. The piece is a personal diagnosis of changing incentives and a case for building relationships, not proof of a fixed formula for success.

Highlights

  • Tools reduce some execution barriers
  • Resource-intensive research makes access consequential
  • Closed work makes contributions harder to verify
  • Reputation can substitute for direct evidence
  • Illustrative percentages are not empirical findings
