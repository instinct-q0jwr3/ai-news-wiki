Three LLM inflection points in twelve months

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison describes three perceived shifts in a year of LLM use: more dependable coding agents, persistent tool-using models able to pursue defined goals, and improved three-dimensional modeling and game design. Personal examples include debugging, locating a sound and repeated game playtesting.

This is a practitioner retrospective, not a controlled benchmark establishing uniform model reliability or universal development timelines. The category boundaries and judgments about entertaining games are the author's. Specific successful projects do not remove tool-access, review, cost or safety requirements for other tasks, and no example workflow was executed here.

Highlights

  • Retrospective identifies three personal inflection points
  • Coding usefulness is distinct from unrestricted autonomy
  • Tools and defined goals shape the reported results
  • Game quality judgments are subjective
  • Examples are not a representative benchmark
  • No universal model capability threshold is established
