Ask HN: CAD for wood working with LLM support?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high

Summary

A hobbyist woodworker asks the community whether AI can speed up 3D design of wood projects, since he models simple pieces by hand in Shapr3d. The thread is small, with about two dozen comments.

The top reply comes from a builder promoting ShopSpec, a parametric furniture tool that works out parts, joints and cut lists and has a beta MCP interface so agents can explore designs. It shows where LLM help for CAD is heading: a domain engine that the model drives, not free-form modelling.

Highlights

  • Woodworker asks for LLM-assisted CAD for furniture projects.
  • Reply points to ShopSpec, a parametric builder with a beta MCP interface.
