Anthropic tells investors quarterly revenue up nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of IPO

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison quotes the Financial Times: Anthropic told investors quarterly revenue rose nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of an IPO. It also said 'distillation' accounted for 5% to 7% of revenue and that it had cut it back materially.

Distillation means rivals training on outputs of stronger models, which creates heavy usage and spending.

Highlights

  • Q3 revenue $16.8bn, up nearly 50%.
  • Distillation 5-7% of revenue, reduced materially.
