Ask HN: If AI tools are so good, why isn't your bug backlog empty?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-17 · confidence: high

Summary

An Ask HN discussion asks why better coding agents have not emptied software teams’ bug backlogs. Replies describe different experiences: quick symptom patches, time spent negotiating acceptable tradeoffs, and workflows where agents first investigate causes before a human chooses a fix.

These accounts illustrate coordination and verification costs, but they are a self-selected discussion rather than a productivity study. A growing backlog can reflect new demand, prioritization or newly discovered problems as well as slow fixes; its size alone cannot establish how much work agents save.

Highlights

  • Discussion compares personal engineering workflows
  • Root-cause work differs from symptom repair
  • Task boundaries and tradeoffs consume time
  • Backlog size is not a controlled productivity measure
  • No representative adoption result follows
