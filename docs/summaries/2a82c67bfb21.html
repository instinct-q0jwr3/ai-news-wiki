Ask HN: Are the trades a safe career transition?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

An Ask HN poster worried about AI asks whether moving from software into a skilled trade would provide several decades of stability. Replies debate regulation, physical work, local markets and the costs of retraining, with incompatible forecasts about both software and robotics.

This is a discussion of personal uncertainty, not a representative labor-market study or individualized career advice. Votes and anecdotes do not establish which occupation is safe, and participants' predictions about decades of automation are unverified. No career recommendation for the reader follows from this thread alone.

Highlights

  • The original post asks about long-term career security
  • Replies include differing views on software and physical trades
  • Retraining costs and local demand matter to the discussion
  • Comment votes are not a representative survey
  • No occupation is established as automation-proof
