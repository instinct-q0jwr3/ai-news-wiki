a16z rebrands a16z Crypto as a16z Frontier and expands the fund's scope to back startups developing AI, robotics, and other emerging technologies

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Andreessen Horowitz is rebranding a16z Crypto as a16z Frontier, expanding the eight-year-old digital-assets fund's scope to AI, robotics, and other emerging technologies - a quiet admission that 'crypto' has fallen out of favor as a banner. Chris Dixon leads the fund alongside Marc Andreessen, Ben Horowitz, and general partners Eddy Lazzarin and Guy Wuollet, building on the $2.2 billion fund announced in May.

Dixon's rationale: as businesses use blockchain behind the scenes, the line between crypto companies and everything else blurs - 'the label matters less now.' The firm says it will keep backing crypto startups and advocating for crypto regulation, but general partner Ali Yahya has reportedly told investors he plans to leave to found a startup. The rebrand tracks where the industry's center of gravity has moved: the frontier label chases AI while keeping the crypto portfolio inside.
