The real reason Trump is standing behind AI — and the latest example of why that might turn out to be a bad idea

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

marcus-on-ai

Summary

Gary Marcus connects two simultaneous data points. First, the NYT reports Trump is downplaying AI fears - and presumably resisting regulation - for economic reasons, which Marcus calls the real reason Trump stands behind AI, and 'could be bad'.

Second, a CNN scoop from Katie Bo Lillis: an AI-assisted intelligence report sent the military scrambling to intercept a Chinese ship in the Middle East believed to be carrying nuclear-weapons-program components. It was a hallucination, and it 'almost started a war'. Marcus notes he warned the US Senate in 2023 of exactly this failure mode - inaccurate AI-generated information triggering accidental conflict - and closes: we may not get as lucky next time.

  • The hallucinated report 'almost started a war'

  • His warning: we may not get as lucky next time
