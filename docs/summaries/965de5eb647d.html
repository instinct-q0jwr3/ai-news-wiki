90% of US working hours are spent on tasks not covered well by AI benchmarks

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

A benchmark map compiled to show how well existing AI benchmarks cover American jobs. It lays out the 923 O*NET occupations as tiles and finds that about 90% of US working hours go to tasks the benchmarks cover poorly. It is a first step toward a continuously updated resource on AI capabilities across the economy.

Highlights

  • About 90% of US working hours poorly covered by benchmarks.
  • Maps benchmarks onto 923 O*NET occupations.
