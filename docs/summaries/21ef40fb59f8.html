[AINews] DeepSeek DeepInfer: Open Source SOTA Inference running 155T toks/day/cluster making $22m/day at ~80% margin for a 81 day payback

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Latent Space's AINews column devotes its lead to DeepSeek's open-sourcing of DeepInfer on day one of PyTorch Conf, calling it an intercontinental ballistic missile into the inference discussion and the open-source response to closed inference decacorns like Fireworks, Baseten, and Modal. DeepInfer is DeepSeek's customization of vLLM (with credit to SGLang and TensorRT-LLM) minus the crown jewels: the ultra-optimized KV cache, prefill/decode disaggregation, and load balancing. Using DeepSeek's disclosed production data, 1.5M cache-inclusive input tokens per second per prefill GPU, 96% cache hit rate, 14k output tokens per second per decode GPU across ~2,400 GPUs, the column estimates roughly $38 per card-hour of revenue at off-peak prices against $5-8 of all-in cost, an 80%+ inference margin, about $166B per GW annualized, and an 81-day payback period, against DeepSeek's own famous 10-month planning target. Latent Space has published a translated blog post with a research guide, explainers, and a codemap.

Highlights

  • DeepInfer open-sourced on day 1 of PyTorch Conf, timed for maximum effect
  • It's vLLM-based with SGLang/TensorRT-LLM credit, minus the crown jewels (KV cache, PD disaggregation, load balancing)
  • Claimed 155T tokens/day per cluster with 72-hour prompt caching
  • Latent Space math: ~$38/card-hour revenue vs $5-8 cost = 80%+ inference margin
  • Cluster math: ~$166B per GW, 81-day payback vs DeepSeek's 10-month planning norm
  • Production data: 1.5M input tok/s per prefill GPU, 96% cache hit, 14k output tok/s per decode GPU, ~2,400 GPUs
  • Translated blog post, research guide, and codemap published
