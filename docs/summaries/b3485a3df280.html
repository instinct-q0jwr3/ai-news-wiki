An interview with Goodfire researcher Nick Cammarata, whose viral blog post claimed LLMs are easier to understand than thought, as other researchers push back

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

MIT Technology Review interviews Goodfire researcher Nick Cammarata, whose 26,000-word blog post claiming LLMs may be far easier to understand, monitor, and control than believed has convulsed the field, landing just as a summer of agent containment failures left the industry desperate for interpretability. Some researchers called it the most important AI paper of the year and a five-year leap in understanding; Stanford's Chris Potts counters that it is little more than a hunch and changes nothing about the difficulty. Cammarata, who helped pioneer neuron-probing interpretability tools at OpenAI, says he was surprised by how much people updated, and frames the post as a hopeful story from someone who simply likes looking at neural networks.
