Anthropic rushing IPO before AI bubble bursts

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Wall Street is pressing both Anthropic and OpenAI to get their IPOs done fast, and the reason goes beyond bubble talk: big investors fear a left-wing takeover of Congress that would bury tech deals under years of hearings and investigations over AI job losses and deepfakes. One major deal lawyer describes advising clients to price their offerings before the end of the year, before things get crazy.

Anthropic, the report says, will likely publish its S-1 any day, aiming at an IPO before Thanksgiving. The bigger problem child is OpenAI: Sam Altman said last month the company was not planning to go public until next year, partly citing AI safety concerns, but bankers are now expected to urge a deal after Anthropic's and before January 3 - the date that closes the current window if Washington turns hostile.

Highlights

  • Wall Street is urging Anthropic and OpenAI to IPO sooner rather than later
  • The fear: a left-wing Congress bringing years of hearings on AI jobs and deepfakes
  • One deal lawyer's advice: get IPOs done before the end of the year
  • Anthropic was expected to publish its S-1 imminently, targeting a pre-Thanksgiving debut
  • OpenAI, publicly eyeing next year, is being pushed toward a deal before January 3
