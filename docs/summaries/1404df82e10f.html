Summary

Architectural Control at Agent Velocity Scott Werner Oct 13, 2025 7 2 1 Share A few weeks ago Justin and I went to a hackathon at Google.

A “we literally shipped a small language model with Chrome and you can use it locally” hackathon.

The same browser that you’re probably reading this post in is now shipping with a language model.

Highlights

  • They call it “Built-in AI” and they’re giving developers APIs to do inference locally.
  • Just you, the browser, and a surprisingly capable small model that can understand images and generate text right there on your machine.
  • Justin and I had a problem we wanted to explore: what if you could navigate the latent space of image generation without writing prompts?
  • What if instead of describing what you want, you just… adjusted sliders?
  • Basically, just let the AI decide what’s interesting about your image.
  • What changes is what breaks when you move fast.
  • Knowing what to specify and what to leave flexible.
  • Maybe this was just one weird hackathon where the stars aligned.
