Microsoft confirms that 2026 Surface PCs have dropped the Copilot+ PC branding, even though they meet all the requirements of Copilot+ devices

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme microsoft compute-buildout

Summary

Microsoft confirmed that 2026 Surface PCs drop the 'Copilot+ PC' brand, closing a category launched in 2024 with Qualcomm as the baseline for 'AI-first' Windows hardware with exclusive features gated behind new system requirements.

Windows Central's account of why: the launch never recovered from its debut disaster. Security researchers found Recall, the blockbuster Copilot+ feature, woefully insecure; Microsoft had to delay Recall and ship the new category without its leading AI experience, tarnishing the brand from day one. Dropping the name is the quiet acknowledgment. (Summary based on Windows Central's article via Techmeme.)
