Swarm (/swarm)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Documentation for Antigravity's /swarm: a team of named agents splits your task, works in parallel and coordinates on a shared message board, with a verifier agent reviewing changes. Your conversation becomes the coordinator; the board and graph views show workers' progress. Suggested when a task divides cleanly across files or questions.

Highlights

  • Named agents split tasks and coordinate on a board
  • A verifier reviews before you see changes
  • Works in the Antigravity app and CLI
