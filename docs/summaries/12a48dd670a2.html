Most Americans Say AI Is Bad for Society, Oppose More Data Centers

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

An ABC News/Washington Post/Ipsos poll finds 57% of Americans say AI is bad for society versus 42% good, with only 2% calling it 'extremely' good against 17% 'extremely' bad. About two-thirds oppose building new data centers in the US, and opposition rises further when the construction is in people's own communities.

The numbers land in a politically charged moment: tech leaders have publicly warned about AI risk while President Trump, who has called such warnings a 'hoax,' hosted tech CEOs at the White House last month for a self-policing 'constitution.' The poll quantifies the backdrop for the data-center fights already visible in local politics: the industry is building at historic speed while public consent, especially local consent, is thin.

Highlights

  • ABC/Washington Post/Ipsos poll: 57% of Americans say AI is bad for society, 42% good
  • Only 2% say 'extremely' good vs 17% 'extremely' bad
  • ~Two-thirds oppose new US data centers; opposition higher for local construction
  • Poll conducted on Ipsos' KnowledgePanel
  • Backdrop: Trump calls AI warnings a 'hoax'; CEOs signed a self-policing accord at the White House
