Mem Is Shutting Down

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high

Summary

Mem announces that its service will close on January 15, 2027, with notes becoming read-only on December 18. The notice says charging has already paused and previously paid features and limits remain available during the remaining service period.

The company directs users to export their libraries and check migrated attachments, collections and meeting transcripts. This is a product shutdown notice, not evidence that any particular user’s export is complete, that migration is lossless or that an automatic refund is promised.

Highlights

  • Shutdown date is January 15, 2027
  • Read-only period begins December 18
  • Notice says future charging has paused
  • Export and migration checks are separate steps
  • Refund and deletion terms are not inferred
