GPT-6 Cyber

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-07 · confidence: high

Summary

OpenAI's post introduces GPT-6 Cyber, which it says pairs stronger security-research capability with better alignment. It powered the Linux kernel research announced the day before, and teams at Mozilla, Palo Alto Networks, Calif and Cisco, plus OpenAI's own, are using it. It is available to select Daybreak Red customers with access to expand over time. The post had about 992K views.
