Anthropic and OpenEvidence partner to offer a specialized version of OpenEvidence's AI search tool for physicians in about 100 low- and middle-income countries

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic and the medical knowledge platform OpenEvidence are partnering to bring AI-powered search to physicians in about 100 low- and middle-income countries, Reuters reports. The collaboration will offer a specialized version of OpenEvidence's AI search tool built for those markets.

Highlights

  • Anthropic and OpenEvidence partner on medical AI search for physicians.
  • The offering targets about 100 low- and middle-income countries.
  • The product is a specialized version of OpenEvidence's AI search tool for doctors.
