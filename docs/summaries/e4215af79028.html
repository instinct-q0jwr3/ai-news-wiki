ASML reports Q3 net sales of €11.66B, vs. €11.72B est., a €4.96B net profit, vs. €4.1B est., and raises its 2026 net sales forecast from €43B-€45B to €44B-€45B

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high

Summary

ASML reported Q3 net sales of EUR 11.66B, slightly below estimates of EUR 11.72B, with net profit of EUR 4.96B against EUR 4.1B expected. CEO Christophe Fouquet said customers keep raising demand forecasts on AI-related investments and some want to place orders for 2029 and even 2030. Planned supply for 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet demand, and ASML is looking at ways to add capacity beyond the 30% increase for 2028 it forecast earlier. Samsung and TSMC committed to its High NA EUV machines this quarter (CNBC).
