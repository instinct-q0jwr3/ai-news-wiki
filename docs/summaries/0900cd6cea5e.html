Holyvolt, which develops AI tools for making and testing new battery materials, raised $45M from Zone II, CC.VC, and others, a source says at a $245M valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-18 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Holyvolt, a Stockholm startup using AI to discover and test new battery materials, has raised a $45M all-equity round from Zone II, CC.VC and angels from defense and finance, valuing it at $245M according to a person familiar with the company. COO Steven Prast, a former Tesla manufacturing leader, argues lithium-ion is at best 20% through its maturity curve - and that the West won't win the rest by fighting China on the part that's already finished: 'We will not be able to out-China China on the battlefield that China has defined.'

Prast's historical framing: what shrank the bag phone to a wrist device was the battery more than the transistor. Holyvolt's bet is using AI tools to hunt for battery chemistries China doesn't control.
