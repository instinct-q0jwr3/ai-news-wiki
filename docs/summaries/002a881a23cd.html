Ubisoft launches Motherbrain, a subsidiary focused on developing "smart NPCs with memory and AI game masters that manage large-scale systemic worlds"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-21 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

GamesIndustry.biz reports Ubisoft’s launch of Motherbrain, a subsidiary built around the Teammates generative-gameplay team. The stated work includes NPC memory and AI game masters for systemic worlds, with potential internal and external partnerships.

A subsidiary launch and product goals do not establish a shipped game, demonstrated reliability or creator adoption. The article also reports layoffs at Canadian studios, but their proximity in the story does not prove that AI caused those job cuts.
