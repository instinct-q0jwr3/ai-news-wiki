NYC’s venture capitalists need to love themselves

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Anil Dash argues that New York investors should back their own technology community rather than seek approval from dominant Silicon Valley firms. His case combines civic values with a business argument: acting as an undifferentiated source of deals for a few late-stage funds leaves local investors with little independent advantage.

Dash favors companies that can generate wealth while paying workers fairly and contributing taxes to the city. This is an opinion essay calling for local confidence and public accountability, not a neutral survey of every investor or a verified forecast of returns.

Highlights

  • Local investment is the core argument
  • Dash rejects dependence on dominant outside funds
  • Differentiation is presented as a business advantage
  • Worker pay and taxation are part of his proposed model
  • Claims about civic and investor behavior are the author's views
