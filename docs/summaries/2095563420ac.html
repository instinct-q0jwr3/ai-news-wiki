Credit Crunch

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Ed Zitron's long piece argues that financing the AI data-centre build-out is getting expensive and selective. It cites CleanSpark, building a data centre for Meta, offering investors big concessions to raise money, and banks such as Societe Generale, Sumitomo Mitsui and MUFG becoming choosier about data-centre loans. Blue Owl, which once agreed to invest up to $10 billion in 15 minutes, is cited as a fast, favoured partner. The thesis is that tighter credit could slow AI growth plans, with bond-market basis points explained along the way. It is an opinion piece. Basis: parts of a very long article were read.
