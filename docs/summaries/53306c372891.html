Europe's three biggest industrial companies are cashing in on the AI boom, as Schneider and Siemens expand further into software, while ABB focuses on equipment

The FT reports that Europe's three biggest industrial companies are cashing in on the AI boom, but with diverging strategies: Schneider Electric and Siemens are expanding further into software, while ABB is betting on physical equipment. The AI data-center build-out is lifting demand for all three - electrification, automation and power management - but the strategic split between software-led and hardware-led growth is becoming the defining contrast among Europe's industrial giants.
