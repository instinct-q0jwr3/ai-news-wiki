Introducing Natural Voice

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Natural announces an in-call payment service that transfers a customer away from a business's voice agent to a dedicated payment agent, then returns the call after the charge. Its design is intended to keep raw card details out of the merchant agent, model and logs while handling spoken or keypad input in Natural's environment.

Compliance, encrypted-call handling and conversion improvements are vendor claims rather than an independent PCI audit. The pizza-call transcript is a product demonstration, not a real order or permission to collect payment information. Deployment still requires correct call separation, authorization and handling of failures; the article does not make an unrelated agent's payment workflow compliant by association.

Highlights

  • Dedicated payment agent receives the transferred call
  • Merchant agent is intended to be absent during card collection
  • Spoken and keypad entry are described
  • Caller authorization precedes the charge in the demo
  • Vendor describes a checkout-link fallback after failures
  • Compliance and conversion claims are not independently audited
  • Example conversation does not authorize a payment
