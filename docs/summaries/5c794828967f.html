Inside-Out AI: Rebuilding Airbnb Behind the Scenes and Across the Guest Experience

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Latent Space interviews Airbnb CTO Ahmad Al-Dahle, who joined in January from Meta where he led generative AI and the Llama launches. Airbnb is being turned into an 'AI-native company' through what they call inside-out AI: use AI internally first to speed up product development, then use the same capabilities to change the guest experience.

An example is an internal tool called Everest that accelerated the launch of a new external service. Al-Dahle says he left Meta because the next hard frontier is deploying models at scale, and he wants to push people to work differently.

Highlights

  • Inside-out AI: internal speed-up first, then customer-facing change.
  • Everest, an internal tool, sped a new external launch.
  • Al-Dahle moved from Meta frontier models to deploying at scale.
  • Goal is changing how staff work and shipping production value.
