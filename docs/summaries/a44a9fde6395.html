Survival LLM: AI assistant and emergency library that runs on your own computer

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Survival LLM is a free, open-source AI assistant and emergency library that runs entirely on a user's own computer, answering questions like blackout food safety, burn treatment and water purification from trusted offline sources - FEMA and CDC guidance, medical references, survival manuals, iFixit and about 930,000 Wikipedia articles.

Every answer lists the articles it read with the exact passage highlighted, an explicit hedge against small-model mistakes. The app checks available memory and downloads the largest model that fits; the full library is a 9 GB download (19 GB installed), or 1.3 GB without Wikipedia and iFixit. It runs on Apple Silicon Macs and 64-bit Windows, needs no account, and works with the internet off after setup. Users can add their own PDFs and documents to the searchable library.

Highlights

  • Fully offline AI assistant plus emergency reference library
  • Sources: FEMA, CDC, medical references, iFixit, ~930k Wikipedia articles
  • Every answer shows its sources with highlighted passages
  • Model size adapts to available memory; no account, no tracking
