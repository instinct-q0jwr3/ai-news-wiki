OpenAI rolls out a version of GPT-6.2 Sol that is "optimized for everyday conversation" to the Chat tab for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

9to5Mac reports that OpenAI is extending GPT-6.2 Sol into ChatGPT's Chat tab with an everyday-conversation tuning. It attributes claims about photo detail and web-search instruction following to OpenAI, and describes a global rollout for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users.

The same article reports 23 December retirement for GPT-5.6 Sol in Chat, Work and Codex, and GPT-5.6 Terra in Work and Codex. These are reported product plans rather than a live account check or independently measured improvement. A staged rollout does not guarantee immediate availability to every eligible account. No subscription, model preference or alert was changed.

Highlights

  • Article describes conversation-tuned Sol in the Chat tab
  • Rollout is reported for paid and business tiers
  • Photo and search improvements are company claims
  • Older-model retirement is reported for 23 December
  • Staged availability was not checked in a live account
  • No account setting was changed
