United moves to block AI agents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

A Reuters report describes United restricting AI bots on its digital platforms, citing credentials, unintended purchases and mileage misuse. It contrasts that resistance with partnerships between agents, retailers and travel intermediaries. The stated concern is unauthorized actions and account exposure; the report does not establish that every form of AI-assisted travel discovery is barred.

Highlights

  • United cites security for platform restrictions
  • Credentials and unintended spending are concerns
  • Frequent-flyer miles are another risk
  • Other travel companies pursue partnerships
  • Discovery and account actions are distinct
