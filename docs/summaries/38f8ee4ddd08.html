Microsoft unveils Microsoft-Decision-1, a fast decision-scoring model trained on Qwen3.5-9B, and says it will soon rebase it on MAI, OpenAI, and other models

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high

Summary

Microsoft unveils Microsoft-Decision-1, a fast decision-scoring model trained on Qwen3.5-9B, available in Microsoft Foundry and through OpenRouter, and says it will soon rebase it on MAI, OpenAI and other models.

The pitch is that decision models are an emerging category distinct from LLMs: purpose-built to deliver structured outputs software can immediately act on, at low cost and high performance. Target tasks include routing, classification, prioritization and verification; Microsoft claims top performance in latency and quality on structured decision tasks.
