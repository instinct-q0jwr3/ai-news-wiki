YouTube Music rolls out labels for songs created with the help of AI; Spotify has AI labels, and Apple Music plans to add "Made With AI" labels later this year

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high

Summary

YouTube Music is rolling out labels for songs made with generative AI, shown on the player page and extending to the main YouTube app. Tapping the label explains the role AI played in the track.

Partners declare use through three designations: Fully Gen AI, Partly Gen AI or No Gen AI, and YouTube says it also runs its own detection tools. Spotify has comparable labels.

Highlights

  • YouTube Music adds AI labels with a detail view.
  • Partners self-declare Fully, Partly or No Gen AI.
