Google says users with a standard account can now build personal Android apps for free in AI Studio and will be able to share them with friends in coming months

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-16 · confidence: high

Summary

Google says anyone with a standard Google account can now build personal Android apps for free in AI Studio. The apps run on your own phone, launched from a private section of Google Play, and are not in the public Play Store (ZDNET).

Highlights

  • Free personal Android app building in AI Studio for standard accounts.
  • Apps stay private and run on your own device.
  • No coding experience required.
