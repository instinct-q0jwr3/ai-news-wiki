Apple HomeView

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high

Summary

Apple introduced HomeView, a smart home hub with a 7-inch Liquid Retina display powered by Apple Intelligence and Siri AI. It brings music, FaceTime, home monitoring and hands-free automation together, and recognizes users by their face so each person can reach their own content in Messages and other apps. Privacy and security are stressed throughout. Basis: the opening of Apple's newsroom post via Daring Fireball.

Highlights

  • HomeView: 7-inch display smart home hub with Siri AI.
  • Recognizes users by face to show personal content.
  • Covers music, FaceTime, monitoring and voice automation.
