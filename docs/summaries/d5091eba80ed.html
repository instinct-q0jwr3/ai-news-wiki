Nasdaq-listed neocloud Nebius acquires Inferize, whose tech helps optimize GPU utilization and reduce AI request costs, sources say for $100M to $150M

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Nebius acquired Inferize, a 10-month-old Israeli stealth startup, in a deal estimated at $100-150M, terms undisclosed. Inferize's technology reduces idle GPU capacity when AI models launch or demand shifts, so more customer demand is served from each GPU.

Founders Guy Bortnikov and Lior Gorbonos come from Granulate; the 17-person Tel Aviv team raised a seed round led by TLV Partners.

Highlights

  • Nebius buys Inferize for an estimated $100-150M.
  • Inferize cuts idle GPU cost when demand changes.
  • Founded early 2026 by ex-Granulate founders; 17 staff.
  • Deal value is a press estimate.
