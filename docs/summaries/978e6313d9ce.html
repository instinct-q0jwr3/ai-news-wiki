'That's so AI ' What gen Alpha's biggest insult tells us

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

The Guardian's language column dissects gen Alpha's favorite insult - 'That's so AI!' - used for anything inauthentic, unbelievable or rubbish: knock-off merchandise, exaggerated claims, excuses parents make. The slang has almost nothing to do with actual artificial intelligence and everything to do with the perceived qualities of AI slop.

The telling part is what kids have noticed: AI-generated content is superficially convincing but ultimately cheap and of dubious value, and that template maps neatly onto half the things in their world. The year's most popular slang, in other words, records a generation's instinctive downgrade of anything that feels mass-produced and hollow.

Highlights

  • 'That's so AI!' is gen Alpha slang for anything inauthentic, unbelievable or rubbish.
  • Applied to knock-off merch, exaggerated claims and parental excuses - not actual AI.
  • The insult encodes the slop template: superficially convincing, ultimately cheap.
  • The year's most popular slang is quietly negative on artificial intelligence.
