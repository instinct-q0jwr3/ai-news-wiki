Formalizing the Enormous Theorem

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

Axiom Math released what it calls the first complete computer-checked proof of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups - the 'enormous theorem' whose original proof spans roughly 15,000 pages across more than 500 articles by about 100 mathematicians, and earned John Thompson a Fields Medal and an Abel Prize.

Over 24 days, engineers and mathematicians directed swarms of dozens of AI agents that produced approximately 43.1 million lines of Lean code. The classification lists the finite simple groups - the 'atoms' of finite symmetry, indecomposable factors in the Jordan-Hölder sense - as a periodic table of finite symmetry.

Highlights

  • CFSG fully formalized in Lean, per Axiom Math
  • ~43.1M lines of Lean produced by AI agent swarms in 24 days
  • Original proof: ~15,000 pages, 500+ articles, ~100 mathematicians
  • The 'periodic table of finite symmetry', now machine-checked
