Proton to 'Re-Interview' Staff About AI to See Who Gets to Keep Their Job

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Proton, the company behind ProtonMail, told staff on Monday it will 're-interview' employees across the company about how they use AI in their jobs - with the implication that the interviews may decide who keeps 'continued employment', according to an internal email obtained by 404 Media.

A privacy company quizzing staff on AI usage as a condition of employment is a story with edges in both directions: it is an employer demanding workers justify themselves against the machine, run by a firm whose brand is distrust of exactly this kind of data extraction. The full article sits behind 404 Media's paywall; the internal email's existence and framing are confirmed.
