type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Hi Guys, like many of you I work with AI agents every day and across several clients.

The major time cost for me is to re-explaining context and past decisions, client rules, that we already do.

When you start a new session, how do you give context to the agent (context came from chat, mail, ticket ...) ?
