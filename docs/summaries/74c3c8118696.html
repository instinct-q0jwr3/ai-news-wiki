Muse Spark Open, the Most Capable US Open‑Weight Model to Date

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high

Summary

Meta released the weights of Muse Spark Open, calling it the most capable US open-weight model so far. It is natively multimodal, with 816B total parameters (42B active) and a 1M-token context, built for coding and agentic work.

Meta highlights running it locally on Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios.

Highlights

  • 816B total (42B active) open-weight multimodal model.
  • 1M context; runs locally on M5 Ultra Mac Studio.
