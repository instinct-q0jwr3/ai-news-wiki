Highlights

  • Khanna's 'Human Control Over AI Act' bans recursive self-improvement and autonomous modification of objectives or shutdown controls until guardrails exist
  • Creates a new federal agency for frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and xAI
  • The agency would run licensing, audits, and security standards for testing and human control
  • Independent auditors embedded at every frontier lab, reporting directly to the agency
  • Standards would cover sandboxes, air gaps, kill switches, anti-escape controls, and chip monitoring
  • Khanna: 'There's actually a civilizational extinction risk' - both loss-of-control and misuse risks
