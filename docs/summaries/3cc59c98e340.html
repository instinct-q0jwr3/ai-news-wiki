OpenAI launches Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, including Business Agents for companies to interact with users and a hub where companies can claim their profiles

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

The Verge reports OpenAI is launching Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, including verified 'Business Agents' that represent a brand, starting with eligible US e-commerce and online service businesses. Users can ask an agent about offerings and compare products inside the chat.

Highlights

  • Verified Business Agents represent brands
  • Beta, US e-commerce and services first
  • Comparison shopping inside chat
