General Intuition, which trains AI agents in spatial reasoning via gameplay footage, raised $220M at a $6.2B valuation, for $650M+ in total funding

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

General Intuition, Medal's sister company training spatial-reasoning agents on gameplay video, has raised another $220 million at a $6.2 billion valuation as it starts offering its models for training AI. CEO Pim de Witte calls them a new class of foundation models trained on billions of action-labeled videos, able to act in real time in never-before-seen environments; he claims leading robotics and world-model labs train on less than 1% of the action data Medal's upload pipeline makes available. Medal is on track for three billion uploaded gameplay videos per year, and de Witte notes the sim sees more accidents in a day than the entire USA - a data-density argument for game video as the substrate for embodied AI.

Highlights
