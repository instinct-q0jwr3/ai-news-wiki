AI Is a Boring Technology

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

A reflective essay argues that AI is a boring technology - infrastructure, like electricity or the internet - and that the sooner we treat it that way, the sooner we can stop being astonished and get back to making things. The author looks forward to the day 'AI' in a headline reads the way 'electric' does now: a word that survives only where it still distinguishes one thing from another.

The piece is careful about the analogy's limits: electricity never produced anything resembling judgment, but AI produces language, the thing we use to recognize a mind, so people reflexively reach for that category. That reflex, the author argues, comes from a human drive to connect rather than from technical illiteracy - and it is also where the danger lies, since a struggling mind can be handed its own thoughts back, slightly improved, at any hour, forever.

Highlights

  • Argues AI should be understood as boring infrastructure, like electricity or the internet.
  • Predicts 'AI' will fade from headlines the way 'electric' did.
  • Key difference from past infrastructure: AI produces language, which humans read as mind.
  • Frames anthropomorphization as a drive to connect, not a failure of intelligence.
  • Warns of the risk when a vulnerable person gets their own thoughts reflected back endlessly.
