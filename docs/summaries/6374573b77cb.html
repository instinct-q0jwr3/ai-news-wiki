Anthropic details early drug development work with six partners and says it has acquired Mithril, a one-person company working on muscular dystrophy

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic detailed early drug development partnerships with six organizations - BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines and The Column Group - and said it has begun drug discovery efforts of its own, including acquiring Mithril, a one-person company working on muscular dystrophy.

The strategy: rather than a single headline application like molecule design, get Claude involved across the whole drug development pipeline - target identification, molecule design and manufacturing optimization, regulatory filings, and clinical trial design and analysis. A partner quote describes Claude generating a clinical study plan and success assessment from a single prompt, work that would otherwise take days.
