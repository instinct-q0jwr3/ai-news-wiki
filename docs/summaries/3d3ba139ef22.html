Q&A with Mark Gurman on the iPhone Duo and Apple's AI era

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high

techmeme apple

Summary

Nilay Patel interviews Bloomberg chief Apple correspondent Mark Gurman about Apple's latest hardware event: the debut of the iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable smartphone, and the first major launch under new CEO John Ternus, who took over from Tim Cook less than two weeks before the event. Gurman - who reported virtually every detail of the event ahead of time - argues the timing of the foldable and the leadership change was not coincidental.

The conversation covers how Cook reshuffled Apple's leadership ranks over the past year, what that means for the company's culture, and how Ternus is expected to navigate Apple from the iPhone era into the AI era. Siri, Cook's legacy, and figures like Johny Srouji also come up, with Gurman drawing on his sourcing ahead of hosting the upcoming Power On podcast.

Highlights

  • iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable smartphone, launched at the annual hardware event.
  • John Ternus led his first major product launch as CEO, under two weeks after succeeding Tim Cook.
  • Gurman reported nearly every event detail in advance; he is Bloomberg's chief Apple correspondent.
  • Cook remains in the picture as executive chair while reshuffling of leadership ranks continues.
  • Core theme: Apple's transition from the iPhone era to the AI era, including Siri's role and AI-native devices.
