Show HN: I built a task system to handle the drudgery so I could focus on tasks

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high

Summary

3 & Done is a minimalist task app built on one constraint: it surfaces only three things at a time. You empty your head one line at a time - messy is fine - and the app chooses three tasks and says why. If you stop at one item, the email brings you your one thing; stop at two and it brings you both; stop at three or more and it picks three of them. Privacy is part of the pitch: what you type stays on the device unless you sign up, leaving only to be ranked, and voice input never leaves the browser. The design bets that a ruthless constraint beats an ever-growing list for people drowning in drudgery.

Highlights
