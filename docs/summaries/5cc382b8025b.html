Announcing d1 with vision

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Liquid AI announced d1 with vision: its first decision model now accepts images, text, or both. Instead of generating tokens, d1 outputs probabilities for yes/no, choice, or score questions in a single forward pass, returning text decisions in 200-300 ms via the Liquid API.

Liquid says it tested d1 against GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 on six real applications - from filtering support tickets to inspecting circuit boards - and claims d1 matches or beats GPT-6.1 Sol on four of them while costing 19x to 200x less and answering significantly faster on every task.

Highlights

  • Liquid AI's d1 decision model now handles images, text or both.
  • Outputs probabilities for yes/no, choice or score questions in one forward pass - no token generation.
  • Text decisions in 200-300 ms via the Liquid API.
  • Matches or beats GPT-6.1 Sol on four of six real-world tasks tested.
  • Claimed cost: 19x to 200x less than GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5.
