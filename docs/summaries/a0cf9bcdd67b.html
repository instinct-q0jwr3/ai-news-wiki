Reports of AI-related child sexual abuse material rise 8,400% in two years

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

The Guardian reports Childlight research showing technology-company reports of AI-related child sexual abuse material rising from about 4,700 in 2023 to more than 400,000 in 2025. The researchers call for better evidence, prevention and rapid removal of abusive material.

These are reports to NCMEC, not a count of unique victims or a direct estimate of prevalence. Improved detection and reporting may explain some of the increase. Separate IWF image assessments in the article use a different reporting system and should not be added to the Childlight figures.

Highlights

  • Research examines company reports to NCMEC
  • Counts rise sharply over two years
  • Reports differ from unique victims
  • Detection changes may affect the trend
  • Separate monitoring datasets are not interchangeable
