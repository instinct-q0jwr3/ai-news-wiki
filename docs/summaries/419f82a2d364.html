The Legion of PCAST

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-15 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high

Summary

Daring Fireball uses the membership of the President’s Council of Advisors on Science and Technology to criticize technology leaders’ proximity to the administration. The linked White House page confirms David Sacks and Michael Kratsios as co-chairs and lists executives including Jensen Huang, Lisa Su, Mark Zuckerberg and Sergey Brin.

The official page describes an unpaid advisory body with up to 24 members. The feed’s claims about reputational cost, political corruption and the significance of companies absent from the list are commentary, not findings of that government source. Membership establishes an advisory connection, not responsibility for every administration decision.

Highlights

  • Official page identifies council leadership
  • Technology executives appear on the roster
  • Council advises rather than legislates
  • Feed offers a critical political interpretation
  • Absence from the roster does not prove a political motive
