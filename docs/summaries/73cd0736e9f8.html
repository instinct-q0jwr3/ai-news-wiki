Anthropic S-1: it spent just $482M on capex in H1 2026, leasing most of its compute, and its capex is almost entirely outsourced via $782B in lease obligations

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Tim Culpan offers a skim-friendly tour of Anthropic's S-1, including a condensed income statement for the first half of 2026 and his pick of the most interesting data in the filing - a table he compiled at the end of the post. One figure he surfaces early: Anthropic spent just $482 million on capex in H1 2026, leasing most of its compute rather than owning it.

The analysis frames Anthropic as structurally different from the infrastructure-heavy AI narrative: its capital intensity sits with its landlords. Culpan and Asianometry's Jon Y had already discussed the earlier prospectus leak on their Supply-Chained podcast, and this post is the follow-up with the actual document in hand.
