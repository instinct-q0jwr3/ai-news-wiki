Summary

A short piece shared on Hacker News coins 'TAI-DR' - Too AI; Didn't Read: the reflexive dismissal of text that smells machine-generated, the AI-era counterpart to TL;DR. The captured text is only the coinage itself, so this summary reflects just that: a label for the growing habit of skipping content on suspicion that no human bothered to write it, and the implied problem for anyone whose real writing gets pattern-matched as synthetic.
