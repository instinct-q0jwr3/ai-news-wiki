Isomorphic Labs $5B Series C

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high

Summary

CNBC reports Isomorphic Labs raised a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia, with other investors including Alphabet. The company says the capital will support biological models and therapeutic programs moving toward clinical trials.

Financing confirms neither drug efficacy nor regulatory approval. The company’s stated ambitions and a sector-wide funding estimate describe business context, not independent evidence that its design engine improves patient outcomes.

Highlights

  • Funds target research and therapeutic development
  • Clinical progress is prospective
  • Capital raised is not clinical validation
  • Sector funding does not measure drug effectiveness
