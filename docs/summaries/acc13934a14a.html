President Trump says Scott Bessent is not "going to be Super Intelligence (SI) Czar", because "he doesn't want to" and Trump wants to keep him at the Treasury

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme

Summary

Donald Trump said Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent will not become his artificial intelligence czar, denying reports from Semafor and Reuters that Bessent was under consideration. On Truth Social, Trump said Bessent doesn't want the job and is doing too well at Treasury to move.

The post fills in pieces of Trump's announced AI governance plan: on September 19 he said he would create an 'AI Force, much like I did Space Force' and would soon name an AI czar, framing AI as 'the next Industrial Revolution' worth possibly 25% of US GDP, and vowing to stay ahead of China. The czar pick remains unannounced.

  • Trump says Bessent doesn't want the role and should stay at Treasury

  • He casts AI as the next Industrial Revolution, 'possibly as much as 25%' of US GDP
  • The czar announcement is still pending
