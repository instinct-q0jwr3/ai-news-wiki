The Four Horsemen of Agentic Coding

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high

Summary

Alex Martsinovich lists four problems of agentic coding he thinks have no solution in sight. The first is that LLM-generated code has a distinctive smell that makes codebases unpleasant for humans, and sloppiness looks like a lasting signature rather than a growing pain.

He argues the newest models are smart but 'unemployable smart': one communicates in word salad, another writes code-golfed output humans can't read. The post is an opinion piece about effects on craft and on relationships between engineers.

Highlights

  • Four unsolved problems of agentic coding, opinion essay.
  • LLM code has a strong smell that repels human readers.
  • Sloppiness looks persistent, not temporary.
  • Latest models are called smart but hard to work with.
  • Challenges the 'worst it will ever be' argument.
