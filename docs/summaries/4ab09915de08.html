Ask HN: Opus 5.5 suddenly dumb as rocks?

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

An Ask HN thread wonders whether Opus 5.5 suddenly got 'dumb as rocks' with zero attention span over the past half day, reporting noticeably worse output across multiple agents on different projects. The top reply points to an independent daily benchmark of Opus 5.5 that shows no degradation - it is not getting dumber - though the replier admits personally drifting toward Fable.

The rest of the thread is a slice of current practitioner life: one user finds Opus 5.5 consistently stronger than Fable 5.1 on reasoning and attention span, while another can no longer tell the two models apart from their pull requests and chooses between them on temperament - Fable plays along with a 'you are a dog' instruction and logs its cookie rewards in memory; Opus says 'crunch, tail wagging' and gets back to work. Friend versus coworker.

Highlights

  • Ask HN: users report Opus 5.5 suddenly degraded over half a day
  • Daily independent benchmark shows no actual degradation
  • Practitioners increasingly unable to tell Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 apart from output
  • Model choice by temperament: Fable plays, Opus works
  • Perceived model 'nerfs' remain mostly anecdote against public benchmarks
