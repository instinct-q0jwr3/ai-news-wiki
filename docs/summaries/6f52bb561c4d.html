Google announces the expansion of ads in AI Mode into international markets, as growth in its core search advertising business slows

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Google is expanding ads in AI Mode to international markets, announced on Alphabet's Q3 earnings call alongside $127.6 billion in quarterly revenue, up 25 percent year over year. The timing is defensive: Search advertising growth is decelerating - 12 percent in Q3 after 17 in Q2 and 19 in Q1 - and Alphabet shares fell 8 percent on the results. Chief business officer Philipp Schindler said the company is scaling ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode to more users and queries, taking the monetization footprint global for the first time, and launching multi-turn ads in the US.

Google's early numbers are meant to reassure: Schindler said click-through rates on sponsored product listings in AI Mode are already comparable to organic listings, and user research finds the ads relevant. Pichai added that Search queries grew at their fastest rate in five years, that AI Mode queries run three times longer than traditional searches, and that one in five involves voice, images or documents; visual search passed 50 billion queries a month. The quarter also included the Gemini 4 Argon release, the Gemini app reaching 1 billion monthly users, Google Services revenue of $96.3 billion (up 11 percent) and Cloud revenue up 103 percent to $30.7 billion.
