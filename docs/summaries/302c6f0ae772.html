I Resist Generative "AI"

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-08 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Esther Weidauer collects her arguments against generative 'AI' in one long article, rather than scattered social posts. She says she avoids using it, has been vocal for years, and writes for non-experts, with mostly independent sections and sources for further reading. She states her background: about 15 years in professional software development, around 25 of programming, work on search engines, her own neural networks, and a psychology degree with a statistics thesis.

She defines 'AI' for the article as generative systems that produce text, images or other media from prompts, and systems derived from them such as agents. The article opens with a long quoted anecdote about a startup founder. Only the introduction was readable here, so the individual arguments are not summarized.
