There's little that's "inevitable" about AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

A long essay argues that the claim AI is inevitable is a thought-terminating cliche worth dismantling rather than accepting. Nobody knows ahead of time which technologies a society will develop or adopt, so calling any specific future inevitable is unfalsifiable prophecy, not analysis.

In hindsight every successful technology looks inevitable, the piece notes, but that says nothing: the iPhone's exact shape was not preordained, and at most something with a vague family resemblance might have arrived in any number of forms. Treating inevitability as an argument, the author contends, is a way of telling people to stop thinking and get with the program.
