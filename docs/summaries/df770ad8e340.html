llm 0.37

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison's llm CLI hits 0.37 with about two months of accumulated work: support for gpt-6.1-sol and gpt-6.1-terra, a new 'llm openai endpoint add' command for OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and a new default model - gpt-6-luna, half the price of the previous default. Embeddings now handle mixed binary content types, needed for models like Gemini Embedding 2 that embed text, audio, images and video.

The most quotable part is the maintainer note on AI-assisted PRs. Where many open-source maintainers complain about a flood of low-quality machine-written pull requests, Willison reports the opposite experience on this project: he triages them with Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, treats them as 'bug reports with attached examples,' sometimes landing them and sometimes reimplementing the fix himself. This release credits 12 contributors, almost all AI-assisted, and he says the review burden was smaller than expected. A small data point in the maintainer-burnout debate: the flood is manageable if you fight model output with model review.

Highlights
