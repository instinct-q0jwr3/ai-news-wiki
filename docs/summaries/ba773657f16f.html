Which AI model is best for coding? A hands-on comparison

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

A hands-on coding-model comparison (September 23, 2026) crowns Claude Opus 5.5 as the best AI model for coding: it tops Cursor's coding leaderboard, is cheaper than Opus 5, and in the author's build test was the fastest model, getting every hidden test right in three runs out of four. GPT-5.6 Sol is a close second for careful, well-specified changes and used the fewest tokens of any model tried.

GPT-6 Sol, released September 22, is 'the one to watch' but was not in Cursor yet when the tests ran. The author's larger point: a leaderboard cannot tell you how a model does on your code - hence the hands-on test.

Highlights

  • Author's pick for coding: Claude Opus 5.5 (as of Sept 23, 2026).
  • Tops Cursor's leaderboard; cheaper than Opus 5; fastest in the test.
  • Got every hidden test right in 3 of 4 runs.
  • GPT-5.6 Sol: close second, fewest tokens used.
  • GPT-6 Sol (out Sept 22) is one to watch but was not yet in Cursor.
  • Lesson: test models on your own code, not just leaderboards.
