Wake up! America’s infrastructure is melting. And AI appears to be partly responsible.

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-19 · confidence: high

Summary

Gary Marcus argues that recent infrastructure outages and cyber incidents show growing risks from AI-written software, poorly constrained agents and malicious use. He connects public reports and personal experience to his prior warnings and supports California’s legal action against OpenAI.

The essay does not independently establish AI caused each listed outage. It mixes reported events, quoted allegations and the author’s causal hypotheses; neither their proximity in time nor a broad warning proves a shared technical cause.

Highlights

  • Author presents a critical risk argument
  • Different outages may have different causes
  • AI involvement is not established for every example
  • Legal allegations remain allegations
  • Prediction claims are the author’s assessment
