SemiAnalysis launches GDPx, an agentic capability benchmark measuring 11 domains, including chip design, cybersecurity, and RSI; Opus 5.5 tops with a 12% score

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

SemiAnalysis has built GDPx on a deliberately unfashionable premise: the first trillion dollars of AI revenue will concentrate in sectors that can deploy frontier intelligence at high ROI, so a benchmark weighted by GDP share or occupational taxonomies measures the wrong thing. Their alternative spans eleven chosen domains - chip design, cybersecurity, biology, finance, legal, quant trading, recursive self-improvement, hardware design, materials science, alignment and software engineering - while deliberately skipping government, real estate and insurance. It is brutal by design: the best model in the world scores 12 percent.

The rankings come with caveats the authors consider features. Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra lose points to their own safety classifiers; excluding cyber, bio and alignment reshuffles the board. Overall, Opus 5.5 is the best all-rounder (winning quant trading, hardware and chip design), Gemini 4 Carbon is Google's real step up - best in bio, edging software engineering, the only model in Opus/Astra's ballpark - and Astra wins RSI and materials science convincingly. Finance and legal tasks are so hard that essentially every model scores zero. On the cost-performance Pareto, Luna and DeepSeek fight the low end, OpenAI holds the middle, Opus takes the high end. One underrated practical note: safety classifiers should drive model choice per use case - Muse Wonder Full, they say, is meaningfully the best cyber model available.
