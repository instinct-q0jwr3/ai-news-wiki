Toezicht AIVD en MIVD, zonder toezichthouders, zonder kantoor

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high

Summary

Bert Hubert - former oversight commissioner for the Dutch intelligence services AIVD and MIVD, who resigned in 2022 over the then-new law - marks its entry into force on July 1. The law reshapes oversight of the services: less detailed advance procedure for hacking and tapping, more room for the agencies, and - his standing criticism - an oversight body left without adequate office or resources ('without supervisors, without an office').

He situates the fight in European law: the European Court of Human Rights has repeatedly held that secret surveillance needs adequate and effective guarantees against abuse, since it can undermine democracy 'under the cloak of defending it'. Dutch coverage (Follow The Money, NRC, AD) and sharp parliamentary questions from several parties are noted; other media showed no interest or capacity. The post is in Dutch.

Highlights

  • New Dutch intelligence law in force July 1; oversight weakened.
  • Hubert quit as AIVD/MIVD oversight commissioner over it in 2022.
  • Less prior procedure for hacking/tapping, more agency leeway.
  • ECtHR: secret surveillance needs effective guarantees against abuse.
  • Parliamentary questions filed by NSC, GL-PvdA/Volt, SP, BBB.
  • Post in Dutch.
