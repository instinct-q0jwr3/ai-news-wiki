Pluralistic: Thwarted (09 Oct 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow's lead essay argues that every billionaire is a policy failure, and that the corrosive part is not wealth itself but the power to live 'unthwarted': unelected, unaccountable, and able to dictate how millions of others must live. Having billions is no evidence of the wisdom to rule, he writes, yet that dictatorial power is the tangible effect of being a billionaire.

The essay develops the point through ideology: neoclassical 'revealed preferences' economics, he argues, exists to let billionaires call domination freedom, and Peter Thiel's claim that democracy is incompatible with freedom is the honest version of it. Having escaped taxes, the ultra-rich now chase immortality and fall for grifters, which Doctorow calls the strongest argument against billionaireism: they are fallible, yet only they get to impose their foolish ideas on everyone else. The rest of the post is a link roundup touching predictive policing, algorithmic price-fixing and older fights over anthrax and grey goo.

Highlights

  • Billionaireism defined as living 'unthwarted', above workers, neighbors and laws
  • Having billions is no predictor of wisdom to dictate how others live
  • 'Revealed preferences' economics framed as ideology that excuses power
  • Thiel's 'democracy is incompatible with freedom' quoted as the honest version
  • After defeating taxes, the ultra-rich fund immortality schemes and grifters
  • Fallible billionaires impose their ideas on people with no say
