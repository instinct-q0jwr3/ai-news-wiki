OpenAI announces ChatGPT Space, a collaborative hub for shared pages and files, available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans; it replaces Library

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-09-29 · confidence: high

techmeme openai microsoft labor-displacement

Summary

At DevDay, OpenAI unveiled ChatGPT Space, a collaborative hub where coworkers, ChatGPT, and the new Dots agents share pages and files. Spaces keep uploaded files and generated content organized for a team; the feature rolls out to Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans on desktop and web first, with mobile later. A companion meetings plugin takes notes, generates summaries with action items, files them into a space, and deletes the captured audio afterward (macOS desktop first). ChatGPT is also coming to Slack and Microsoft Teams.
