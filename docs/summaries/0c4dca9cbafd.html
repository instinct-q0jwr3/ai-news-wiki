ServiceNow and Nvidia launch Arc, a personal, long-running enterprise AI agent for productivity tasks across email and Teams, for RTX Spark and Nvidia OpenShell

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high

Summary

ZDNet reports ServiceNow and Nvidia have made Arc generally available: a personal, long-running enterprise AI agent for everyday work across email and Microsoft Teams, first teased in May. Arc is governed by ServiceNow's Super Intelligence Control Tower, which monitors agents, vets access, checks for prompt injection, and measures performance, and enforced by Nvidia's open-source OpenShell runtime, addressing the security concerns that followed a summer of agent-led breaches. It runs locally on Mac (Windows coming), is designed for Nvidia's RTX Spark devices, builds memory and skills over time, and hands complex tasks to frontier models only when necessary.
