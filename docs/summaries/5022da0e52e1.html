TrustedRouter, which raised $6M to offer E2EE and confidential computing for enterprise AI queries, says it processes 6B+ tokens per day, up from 1B in August

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

A Bloomberg newsletter opening introduces TrustedRouter as a company seeking to keep enterprise AI use private from model makers. Its Techmeme excerpt adds a reported six-million-dollar raise and a company claim of more than six billion tokens per day, up from one billion in August.

Only the newsletter opening and feed excerpt were accessible, not the technical implementation or full business report. Token volumes are company claims, not audited customers, revenue or profitability. End-to-end encryption and confidential computing have specific threat models; labels alone do not prove protection against every operator, endpoint, log or device exposure. No service or cryptographic attestation was tested.
