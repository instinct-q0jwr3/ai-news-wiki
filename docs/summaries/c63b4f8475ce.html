Perplexity Time Machine

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Perplexity introduces Time Machine for Search and Context APIs, serving stored page copies at or before a requested cutoff rather than the live web. The vendor describes up to a year of history at launch, intended to reduce hindsight leakage in research replay and forecasting evaluations.

The time boundary applies to its available crawl history, not a complete archive of every past webpage, and cannot remove knowledge already present in a model's weights. Vendor comparisons use automated leakage judgments, not proof of zero contamination; its own tests still flagged some returned material. Listed launch pricing is $5 per thousand searches and $1 per thousand context URLs, without historical surcharge, not a quote verified in an account. No API key or example request was used.

Highlights

  • Search and full-page retrieval share a cutoff
  • Results come from stored copies rather than live sites
  • Coverage depends on crawl history
  • Model-weight knowledge is a separate leakage channel
  • Vendor leakage judge found nonzero flagged pages
  • Reported forecast scores are setting-specific
  • No historical request or purchase was made
