Introducing 1Password for Agents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high

Summary

1Password announced 1Password for Agents, a platform for giving personal AI agents the credentials they need without handing over your digital life: agents work while credentials stay secure, and access becomes something every user can see, approve, and manage. The pitch pairs capability with control - an agent can plan an event, coordinate dates, and send invitations while the user watches what it touches.

The launch extends 1Password's bet that the agent era multiplies the number of non-human actors needing secrets - the same thesis behind its reported $500M ARR milestone - into a consumer-facing product. The hard problems it names: agents should operate with scoped, revocable access rather than copied passwords, and every grant should be visible and auditable by the human principal.

Highlights

  • 1Password for Agents: a platform for giving AI agents scoped, managed credential access
  • Agents work while credentials stay secure; access is visible, approvable, and manageable
  • Extends the non-human identity thesis behind 1Password's agent-driven growth
  • Key problems addressed: scoped/revocable access, no copied passwords, auditable grants
  • Consumer-facing complement to its enterprise agent-credential business
