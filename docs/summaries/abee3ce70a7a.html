If AI keeps evolving,would you worry about your next generation's survival

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

A Hacker News thread asks whether parents should worry about the next generation's survival if AI keeps evolving and eventually handles all work, and what to do today to prevent that outcome.

Responses are measured rather than alarmed: independence, persistence, empathy and thoughtfulness should stay useful in any future, commenters argue, and even skills that automation makes obsolete still train mental flexibility. Practical hedges mentioned are savings in global index funds and accepting that career prospects have always been uncertain.

Highlights

  • HN debates raising kids for an AI-does-everything future
  • Commenters lean mildly worried, not alarmed
  • Character skills seen as durable across futures
  • Obsolete skills still train mental flexibility
  • Index funds offered as a practical hedge
