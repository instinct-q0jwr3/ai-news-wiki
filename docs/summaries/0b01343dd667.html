An Open Letter to Steven Pinker on AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high

Summary

An open letter to Steven Pinker about AI, written in reply to arguments about AI risk. The excerpt lists four objections to 'doomer' scenarios: they treat intelligence as a single quantity, they conflate it with self-preservation and dominance, they assume one monomaniacal goal, and they assume humans would hand over unlimited, irreversible control of infrastructure. The author argues genuine intelligence trades off competing goals. Basis: the first part of a very long letter was read.

Highlights

  • Four objections to doomer scenarios.
  • Intelligence is not a single quantity.
  • Motives like self-preservation come from evolution, not intelligence.
  • Opinion piece.
  • Basis: first part of a long letter.
