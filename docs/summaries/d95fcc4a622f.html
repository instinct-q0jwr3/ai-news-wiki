Analysis: Huawei's Horinger data center, among China's largest for AI, has ~7% of the compute of SpaceX's Colossus 2 and may stay far behind until at least 2028

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-16 · confidence: high

Summary

Epoch AI estimates Huawei’s Horinger campus has about 81,000 H100-equivalents of operating compute, roughly 7% of its estimate for Colossus 2. It infers power capacity from visible cooling infrastructure, then estimates chip populations and performance from public records and deployment assumptions.

The estimate has a wide 90% interval of 36,000 to 141,000 H100-equivalents. Campus-wide compute is not necessarily one connected training cluster, and projections through 2028 depend on construction, chip availability and the comparison site’s expansion. Satellite clues and modeled equivalents are not a direct inventory or an observed training result.

Highlights

  • Cooling infrastructure anchors power estimates
  • Chip mix depends on assumed deployment lag
  • Compute estimate includes a wide interval
  • Campus capacity may span separate clusters
  • Future comparison depends on both sites expanding
