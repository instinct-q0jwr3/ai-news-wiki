Supercharging transformer residual connections

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Google Research revisits DeepCrossAttention, an ICML 2025 architecture that lets each token mix representations from earlier layers using learned routing and depth-wise cross-attention. It describes experiments in language modeling, synthetic tasks and image classification and relates the approach to attention residuals and hyper-connections.

The post reports up to threefold faster matching quality and 30-40% parameter reductions in its experimental settings, not universal savings for every transformer or deployment. Depth-wise routing differs from ordinary attention across tokens and introduces implementation and memory considerations. This is a November 2026 explanation of earlier research, not a newly replicated result or a drop-in improvement tested here.

Highlights

  • DCA routes information across depth on a per-token basis
  • Mechanism uses learned weights and split query/key/value mixing
  • Article describes ICML 2025 work
  • Reported gains depend on task and baseline
  • Depth attention differs from sequence attention
  • Related residual architectures have distinct stability tradeoffs
  • No universal speed or parameter reduction is established
