There's No "Going Back": On the receding horizons of the AI transition

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Johan Michalove argues the AI transition has receding horizons: there is no going back, and we may not even know what we lost. Newsletters, websites and ads across industries are converging on the same Claude-aesthetic and tone - idiosyncratic human markers processed out into a generic bleh where one company's output is indistinguishable from another's.

He borrows Raymond Williams' 'structure of feeling': nostalgia mourns subjectivities that have ceased to be possible, like urbanists mourning the pre-automotive city. The analogy: professions, knowledge work and consumer products are being rebuilt so they can no longer be performed without AI as middleware - and fifty years on, the pre-AI world may be as unreachable as the walkable city.
