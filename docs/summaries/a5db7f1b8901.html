Agentic AI Designs an Analog Synthesizer

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high

Summary

A hardware developer describes using Claude Code to design an analog synthesizer through specifications, SPICE simulations, PCB generation and firmware. After assembled boards arrived, software-controlled instruments and an audio loopback supported calibration and comparison with simulation.

The account includes substantial human direction: choosing the concept, reviewing the design, reorganizing PCB blocks, switching to four layers and fixing a hand-soldered bridge. Small touch keys remained awkward. The working board is evidence from one reported build, not proof that arbitrary mixed-signal circuits can be designed without expert oversight.

Highlights

  • Workflow starts with a complete specification
  • Simulation informs circuit revisions
  • PCB layout required human intervention
  • Physical boards were tested and calibrated
  • One build does not establish general autonomy
