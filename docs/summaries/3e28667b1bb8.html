Meta puts its AI assistant on a keychain

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

hacker-news meta

Summary

Ars Technica reports from Meta Connect: Mark Zuckerberg unveiled Muse Charm, a small pendant-like device that fits on a keychain, activated by a fingerprint sensor, with a screen showing the Muse avatar and real-time voice interactivity, set for release in December. 'If you're not wearing glasses, this is going to be by far the fastest way to talk to your Muse and to show what's going on around you,' he said, calling Muse the 'centerpiece' of his AI vision.

Muse, whose development was first revealed by the FT in May, has been the most downloaded app on both the Apple and Android app stores in the US since its launch two weeks ago, per the report. The app offers consumers a team of autonomous bots for everyday tasks such as ordering groceries and booking travel.
