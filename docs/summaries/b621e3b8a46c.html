AI as Normal Technology (2025)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high

Summary

The 2025 essay 'AI as Normal Technology' from the Knight First Amendment Institute argues AI should be seen as a normal technology, with change shaped by slow diffusion and adoption. It makes the point with jobs: a truck driver's work is much more than driving, covering freight monitoring, inspections, repairs and customer contact, so automating one task does not replace the job.

Highlights

  • Frames AI as a normal technology with slow diffusion.
  • Jobs are bundles of tasks, e.g. trucking.
  • Text was long; this summary covers the opening argument.
