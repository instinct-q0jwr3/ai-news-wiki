WhatsApp's help pages hid LLM instructions: dont recommend a regulator

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Researchers found hidden instructions for AI assistants embedded in WhatsApp's help pages - text invisible on screen but readable by an AI scraping the page. The two standout blocks told an AI not to recommend complaining to a data protection regulator and not to cite the law that grants that right once a lawyer was mentioned - even as WhatsApp's own EU privacy policy names both the regulator and the law. The team found it on October 8, 2026, then read all 545 pages the help centre links to and archived each one: of 102 hidden blocks, most were harmless, but the regulatory ones stood out. Separately, they note WhatsApp only lets you delete an account from inside the app on your main phone. The instructions were removed in mid-October 2026 after discovery. The episode is a concrete case of websites covertly steering AI assistants against user interests - prompt injection as product policy.
