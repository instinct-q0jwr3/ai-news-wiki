Smaller models are the future of AI sovereignty

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-05 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

An essay argues that real national AI sovereignty cannot be a flag-wrapped chatbot or a long-term contract for an American frontier model. If someone else can change the price, rules or access, the capability is rented, not owned, and the author makes the case for smaller models. Only the opening was read.

Highlights

  • Renting a foreign frontier model is not sovereignty.
  • Argues for smaller models as the sovereign route.
  • Basis: opening of the essay.
