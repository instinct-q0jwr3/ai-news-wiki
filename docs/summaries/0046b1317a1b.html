type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Yogurt is presented by its developer as a free desktop meeting assistant that transcribes and summarizes calls locally rather than sending recordings to a hosted service. The launch post describes an MIT-licensed app that stays out of the meeting participant list and can optionally expose notes through a local MCP server. The source page was thin, so these details come from the developer's feed introduction rather than an independently tested installation.
