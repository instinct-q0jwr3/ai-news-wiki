Reading Anthropic's S-1

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Tilo Mitra reads Anthropic's October 26 S-1 so you do not have to, in charts. The headline trajectory: quarterly revenue went from under $1 billion a year ago to $11.6 billion - 30.7x in five quarters - with $41 million in all of 2023, $386 million in 2024, $4.6 billion in 2025, and the first half of 2026 alone bringing in more than three times last year. The more interesting line is the crossover the whole industry has been waiting on: until this year, every dollar of revenue cost more than a dollar of compute; from Q1 2026, revenue per compute dollar moved above break-even, with revenue growing 14.7x against compute's 4.0x since Q2 2025 - real operating leverage, capped by Q2 2026's $974 million of operating profit, the company's first profitable quarter.

The famous $80.7 billion net loss is mostly an accounting artifact: about 90 percent is a fair-value remeasurement of early convertible notes and SAFEs whose paper value ballooned with the valuation - no cash moved. Operating cash flow is roughly $3.9 billion positive, and the company held $131.7 billion in cash and investments in June. The demand side: customers spending $100K+ a year rose 12.5x in twelve months, and about 250 customers now anchor the top of the book, with roughly 80 percent of revenue usage-based API tokens. Compute eats 53 percent of costs; the first-half gap of $693 million was paid from cash.
