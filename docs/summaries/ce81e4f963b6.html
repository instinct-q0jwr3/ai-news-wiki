Show HN: Hack – my Hacker News mobile client now lets you block AI posts

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

hacker-news apple ai-safety-incidents

Summary

I am the developer for HACK, a hacker news client that I have been building and maintaining for over 8 years now on iOS, iPadOS, MacOS and Android.

Now, you can block AI related posts in the app.

You can also disable infinite scrolling to prevent you from doomscrolling.

Highlights

  • To enable these, go to app's home screen, then go to settings (top right), then scroll all the way to the bottom and enable these settings.
  • You can see an example of the effectiveness of the AI blocking toggle below.
  • Currently, these features are available on Apple platforms.
  • I am adding them to the Android client at this moment too.
  • You can try the Apple versions here: https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/hack-for-hacker-news-yc-reader...
