Testing Thinking Mode

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

Midjourney is testing a Thinking Mode for image generation on its Alpha site. Users open a job's lightbox and press Rerun (Thinking) to have the model reason before drawing.

Early tests show better prompt accuracy, typography and coherence. The team asks testers for examples and feedback in its Discord ideas channel and may later offer it as a general thinking mode or as a way to add more thinking after a job.

Highlights

  • Thinking Mode in testing on Midjourney Alpha
  • Use Rerun (Thinking) from a job's lightbox
  • Improves prompt accuracy, typography, coherence
  • May become a general option later
