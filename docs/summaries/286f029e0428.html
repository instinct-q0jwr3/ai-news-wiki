Abby Care, which runs a clinically led, AI-powered platform to train and certify family caregivers, raised an $82M Series C led by Transformation Capital

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Fortune profiles Abby Care, which raised an $82M Series C led by Transformation Capital for a clinically led platform that trains and certifies family members to become paid caregivers. Founder Havi Nguyen's motivation is personal: as the eldest daughter of Vietnamese immigrants, she translated at doctor's appointments and managed Medicaid paperwork as a kid, and later, working at an autism-therapy startup during COVID, kept hitting the same staffing gap - therapists churned, but parents never turned away. Parents consistently asked whether they could learn the skills themselves, which became the company's premise: design a care model where the person most committed to the patient can deliver the care, with AI-powered training and clinical oversight making it safe and certifiable.
