EmbeddingGemma 2

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison welcomes EmbeddingGemma 2 being released under Apache 2.0. For embedding models, he argues, closed hosted-only models are a poor fit because applications store millions of vectors, and if the vendor retires the model you must pay to recompute them. He wants a hosted option with the fallback of running open weights himself or switching vendor.

Highlights

  • Apache 2.0 licence is the point for him.
  • Retired models force costly re-embedding.
  • Wants a hosted option with an open-weight fallback.
