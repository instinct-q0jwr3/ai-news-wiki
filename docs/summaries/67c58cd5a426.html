Let your AI agents paint big arrows, boxes and text on your screen

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Bigarrow is a small macOS command-line tool plus an agent skill that lets an AI agent point at things on your screen. When an agent needs you to click one button, such as an Allow dialog, it draws a large arrow with a short sign over the target instead of printing a request in a terminal you are not watching.

The overlay is click-through, never takes focus, works across displays and Spaces, and needs no permissions to draw. It is a single Swift binary under the MIT license with no daemon, account or telemetry.

Highlights

  • macOS CLI that draws arrows and text on screen
  • Lets agents point at the button a human must click
  • Click-through, no focus stealing, no permissions to draw
  • Single Swift binary, MIT license
