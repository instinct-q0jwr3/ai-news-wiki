Wall Street Expects Anthropic's Committed $900B Debt to Be Junk

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high

Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports Anthropic committing roughly $900 billion to computing over coming years, with its credit rating expected to influence financing plans after a large equity offering. The readable opening contrasts a very high equity valuation with uncertainty about the company's reception in the bond market.

Only the opening was accessible. The headline's junk-debt expectation is not an inspected rating-agency decision, a default or evidence that $900 billion of debt has already been issued. Spending commitments, financing requirements and outstanding borrowing are different measures; the full maturity schedule, guarantees and contractual conditions were not available here.
