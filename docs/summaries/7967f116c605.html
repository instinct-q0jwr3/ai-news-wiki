The Association for Human Mathematics says OpenAI's new math documents show power, not scholarship, and urges mathematicians to stop working with the company

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high

Summary

The Association for Human Mathematics says OpenAI's new math documents show power and not scholarship, and urges mathematicians to stop working with the company. This Techmeme entry points to the AHM site and the same statement reposted on Terence Tao's blog.

The AHM describes itself as defending mathematics as a human endeavor against AI, citing risks to education, journals and the independence of the community from corporate interests. Its stance is openly opposed to AI in mathematics, so read it as an advocacy position.

Highlights

  • AHM rejects OpenAI's math release as a show of power
  • Asks mathematicians to stop collaborating with OpenAI
  • AHM opposes corporate AI in mathematics by mission
  • Cites threats to education, journals and independence
  • Same statement as the Tao-hosted post
