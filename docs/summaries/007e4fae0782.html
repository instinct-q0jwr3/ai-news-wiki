Claude Cowork and chat come together for Enterprise

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic has unified Claude's chat and Cowork capabilities for enterprise customers, ending the split experience that forced users and administrators to pick a tool per task. Customers quoted in the announcement describe the merge as seamless - connectors, skills and MCPs carried over - and say it resolves the constant 'which Claude should I use' question.

One government customer describes Claude working through stacks of county, state and federal budgets and public meeting documents, mapping how funding streams connect, and producing a report that would have taken the team weeks - from under ten minutes of prompting. The strategic point: Anthropic is collapsing its product surface into one agentic Claude that adapts to how and where people work.
