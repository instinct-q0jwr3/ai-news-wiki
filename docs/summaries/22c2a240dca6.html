Meta Releases Faster K-Means Alternative

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high

Summary

Meta FAIR released e-means, a k-means replacement claiming about 10x faster epochs and wall-clock with lower loss than Lloyd's algorithm and no extra hyperparameters. It uses a mini-batch update rule with quality-focused batches, defaults that shift at low dimensions, and distributed variants. Includes a browser demo and reproduction scripts; claims are the authors' own.

Highlights

  • About 10x faster than Lloyd's k-means, lower loss claimed
  • No extra hyperparameters
  • Distributed and low-dimension modes
  • Code, browser demo and repro scripts
