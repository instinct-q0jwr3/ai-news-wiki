Pluralistic: Critique and writing (23 Oct 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-24 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow's Pluralistic links post for October 23 leads with 'Critique and writing': the argument that the most important part of a writing workshop is fixing other people's stories, not receiving notes on your own. Peer workshops work for counter-intuitive reasons - the obvious one is that they make you write at all, replacing impossible self-discipline with a group's expectation of regular new work.

The deeper mechanism: critiquing others' drafts trains the exact judgment your own blind spots resist - it is easier to see a story's flaws when your ego isn't attached to it, and that diagnostic skill transfers back to your own pages. The post rounds out with his regular 'Hey look at this' delights and an 'Object permanence' item on the USPS.
