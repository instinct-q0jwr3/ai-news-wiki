Euclidean geometry in the asymptotic spectrum of tensors [pdf]

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

An OpenAI preprint constructs compatible costs for tensors whose indices are placed in a real Hilbert space, over any field. The abstract and opening theorem assign a shared binary wire its Euclidean distance and a common label among several sites a normalized mean-width cost; for a triangle the per-bit cost is half the perimeter.

The PDF itself was downloaded and its abstract, introduction and main opening theorem read, rather than treating the GitHub viewer shell as paper content. This is a mathematical construction, not a quantum experiment, communication-speed result or independent verification of companion lower-bound proofs. Claims about arithmetic circuits and sunflower families are applications attributed to companion papers, whose full arguments were not checked here. No formal proof checker or model was run.

Highlights

  • Primary PDF opening and theorem were read
  • Construction is stated over arbitrary fields
  • Prices obey spectral monotonicity and product/sum rules
  • Shared and independent labels have different costs
  • Mean width is normalized to segment length
  • Companion applications were not independently verified
  • No experimental or practical speedup is established
