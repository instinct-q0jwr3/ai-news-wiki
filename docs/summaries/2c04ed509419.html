SessionGraph: Visualize all your coding agent sessions in one interactive graph

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high

Summary

SessionGraph's short landing page advertises a local desktop graph and search index spanning coding-agent session stores. It describes finding previous conversations and comparing session activity, with a terminal-based launch route and installer option.

Only the page text was inspected, not the application, source, installer or network behavior. Local and private are vendor claims; a comparison-with-friends pitch does not establish exactly what would be shared or how consent works. Indexing would expose sensitive transcript material to another program, and no such access was authorized or performed here. No command, download, session scan or sharing action occurred.
