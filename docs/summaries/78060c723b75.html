Pluralistic: America cannot undo Trumpism (09 Nov 2026)

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Cory Doctorow argues that countries cannot safely assume a return to the earlier US-led economic and technology order. He connects dependence on American finance, cloud services and devices to the risk of coercion, then criticizes political responses that acknowledge a rupture without rebuilding alternatives.

The essay presents a political and industrial-policy argument, including criticism of Canadian austerity. It is not a prediction that any named service will be disconnected or evidence that every form of international dependence has the same risk.

Highlights

  • Opinion centers on dependence and resilience
  • Cloud and devices are part of the argument
  • Finance and trade are linked to technology
  • Canadian policy is criticized
  • Specific future disruptions are not established
