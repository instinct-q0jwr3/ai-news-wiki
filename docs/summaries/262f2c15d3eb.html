AI R&D Is Accelerating Towards Potential RSI in 2027

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high

Summary

A thread digest on METR's deep-access report finds Anthropic is likely improving model capabilities on well-specified tasks at about twice the rate of March 2024 to October 2025, with high uncertainty (1.3x to 3x), mostly due to using AI for R&D. Commentators say the 'dramatic acceleration' threshold in Anthropic's policy may be crossed, and extrapolations put Claude leading internal research by mid-2027. Some key findings were redacted. The sources are social posts quoting the report, so numbers come second-hand.
