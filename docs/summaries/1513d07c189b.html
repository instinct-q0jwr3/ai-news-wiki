Qualiaphobic Artificial Neural Networks

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Erik Hoel proposes qualiaphobic neural architectures intended to minimize the chance of consciousness under competing theories. The accessible portion describes anchor systems, fragmentation of possible phenomenology and architecture changes designed to conflict with popular consciousness accounts.

This is a philosophical and technical research proposal, not a validated test proving that present or future AI lacks experience. Claims linking non-consciousness to moral status and alignment depend on disputed theoretical and ethical premises. The retrieved text is capped and the full article and all references were not audited; no architecture or behavioral experiment was implemented.
