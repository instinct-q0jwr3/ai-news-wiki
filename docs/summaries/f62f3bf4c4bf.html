AI lab offers free plumbing and HVAC repairs to train AI robots

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high

Summary

HUMXN, an AI lab specializing in real-world data, offers homeowners free or subsidized plumbing, electrical, HVAC, air-duct and carpet cleaning in exchange for letting workers record their jobs with 'wearable capture technology'. The goal is training data: founder Thomas Unise argues the next generation of AI needs more than internet text and images - robots must learn how skilled people interact with the physical world.

Unise says millions of technicians perform complex tasks daily whose expertise exists in no format AI can learn from, and frames the program as infrastructure connecting that knowledge with robotics companies while making sure the people creating the value get paid.

Highlights

  • HUMXN subsidizes home services in exchange for recording skilled labor
  • Covers plumbing, electrical, HVAC, air-duct and carpet cleaning
  • Workers wear capture technology; footage trains AI robots

  • Promises financial benefit for homeowners and service companies
