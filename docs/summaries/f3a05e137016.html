Amazon retires the Fire brand for tablets after 15 years, replacing it with Alexa Tablets that run Android and ship on October 14, including the $499 12 Pro

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high

Summary

The Verge reports that Amazon is retiring the Fire brand for tablets after 15 years and replacing it with Alexa Tablets that run Android and ship on October 14, including a $499 12 Pro. The article describes them as powerful, priced to move and full of AI ideas.

Basis: Techmeme excerpt; specs beyond price and date were not reviewed.
