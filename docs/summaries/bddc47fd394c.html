Rox, which develops AI agents to automate customer research and outreach, raised a $100M Series B co-led by General Catalyst and March at a $1.3B valuation

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Fortune reports Rox, whose AI agents automate customer research and outreach, raised a $100M Series B co-led by General Catalyst and March Capital at a $1.3B valuation, with Sequoia and GV returning and Prosperity7 new. Founder Ishan Mukherjee previously sold Pixie Labs to New Relic for about $150M and was its chief growth officer. The pitch: CRM should put information to work, not just store it.
