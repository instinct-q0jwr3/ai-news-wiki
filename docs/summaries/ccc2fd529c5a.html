AI-generated posters don’t have to be horrible

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-20 · confidence: high

hacker-news

Summary

A now famous Facebook post shows us the scourge of identikit posters generated by AI. Here’s an article on the subject from the Independent .

With apologies for picking on the Leamington Beer Festival - they are by no means unique The problem with these is not so much that they’re bad.

Highlights

  • The problem is that once you’ve seen that style 20 times it starts to irritate just from the sheer repetition.
  • I knew that even ChatGPT was capable of a broader variety of styles than this, so I set out to prove it.
  • I gave ChatGPT some invented event details and asked it to produce a poster.
  • Rather than go with the default look it would choose for a spring fayre, I made a point of specifying what I thought was a different style: Me: Produce a poster for a spring fayre.
  • Craft stalls Circus Skills workshop Go for a clean, unfussy, bright layout with a bold striking spring-themed graphic.
  • Avoid pastel/airbrush/oil style art or images of people.
  • To me it still feels like what I’m trying to avoid.
  • Me: What is this design style you’ve used called?
