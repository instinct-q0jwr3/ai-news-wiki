Show HN: HackerTV – HN's most-recommended Youtube videos as 24/7 TV channels

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high

Summary

HackerTV is a Show HN project that turns Hacker News' most-recommended YouTube videos into 24/7 TV channels with a retro TV interface: channel up and down, number keys, a program guide, captions and theater mode. The page warns that durations are estimated until YouTube metadata loads, so some programs may run short or get cut off.

Highlights

  • HN's most-recommended YouTube videos as always-on TV channels
  • Retro controls: channel surfing, guide, captions, fullscreen
