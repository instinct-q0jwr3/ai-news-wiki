US tech giants' hidden AI debt swells 90% to $3.2T in 6 months

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Reuters (via Nikkei) reports that US tech giants' off-balance-sheet obligations tied to AI have almost doubled in six months to about $3.2 trillion, a 90% rise. For Google, Meta and others, the commitments and obligations now equal roughly 80% of assets, with investors increasingly alarmed by seemingly unending infrastructure spending. Only the opening paragraph was captured, so the breakdown by company and by type of obligation is not available here.

The figure matters because it sits outside the headline capex numbers: leases, purchase commitments and similar obligations do not appear as ordinary debt. It adds to the week's other bubble signals, including hedge funds selling AI stocks and the compute commitments in Anthropic's S-1.

Highlights

  • Off-balance-sheet AI obligations at US tech giants up ~90% in six months to about $3.2T
  • Commitments reach roughly 80% of assets for Google, Meta and others
  • Investors increasingly alarmed by the spending pace
