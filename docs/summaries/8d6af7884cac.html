Show HN: Kairos – Open-source risk and fraud decisioning platform

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high

Summary

Kairos is an open-source risk and fraud decisioning platform that decides each sign-up, login, order, payment and payout as it happens (approve, step up, review or block) and keeps every decision with the signals behind it. It positions itself as an alternative to Sardine, Sift, Riskified and SEON, with two differences: a coding agent can tune it, and decision models can catch fraud before labels exist to train on.

The agent angle works over MCP. Connect Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or another agent, and it can do a fraud ML engineer's work. The README's example has an agent respond to rising chargebacks by designing six model variants, running an experiment, reading results and publishing a winning logistic-regression model (ROC-AUC 0.949 validation, 0.930 test) as v6. Classical ML and decision models run together. The performance numbers are the project's own example, not an independent test.

Highlights

  • Open-source alternative to Sardine, Sift, Riskified and SEON; decisions stored with their signals
  • Tuned by coding agents over MCP: variants designed, experiments run, winners published
  • Example run: logistic regression reached ROC-AUC 0.949 validation / 0.930 test
  • Numbers come from the README's own example
