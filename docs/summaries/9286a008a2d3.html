New York-based Hatz AI, which makes tools for managed services providers to help small businesses adopt AI, raised a $24M Series A

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

CRN reports that Hatz AI raised a $24 million Series A for its platform serving managed services providers and smaller businesses. Management says the funding will expand engineering, research and customer-facing technical support, with plans to double a 60-person workforce.

The strategy is to help providers build customized AI services rather than merely resell tools. Partner growth and demand are management-reported indicators; financing and hiring plans do not independently establish successful outcomes for customers.

Highlights

  • Reported Series A totals $24 million
  • Platform targets managed services providers
  • Management plans to double staffing
  • Custom implementation is a stated focus
  • Growth claims remain attributed to the company
