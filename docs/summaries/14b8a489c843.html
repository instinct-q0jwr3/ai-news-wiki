Agent-monitoring startup Raindrop raises a $35M Series A

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high

techmeme agentic-systems ai-safety-incidents

Summary

Raindrop, which builds monitoring technology for AI agents to catch failures such as hallucinations, raised a $35 million Series A led by CRV, co-founders Zubin Koticha and Ben Hylak told Axios Pro exclusively. The full terms are behind the Axios Pro paywall; the disclosed facts confirm the round size, the lead investor and Raindrop's focus on agent-monitoring infrastructure as enterprises deploy AI agents into production.
