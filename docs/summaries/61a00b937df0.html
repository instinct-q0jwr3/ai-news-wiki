500B Tokens Later: Letting AI Agents Decompile a First-Person Shooter

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high

Summary

A developer spent three months and some 500 billion tokens decompiling a popular first-person shooter, aiming not for a proof of concept but an accurate, stable and feature-complete recreation of the game. The author stays coy about which game: two earlier posts naming it were removed after, in their words, corporate America was here to ruin their fun.

The post is less about the game or decompilation itself and more about AI orchestration: how to optimize infrastructure, setup and harness so agents produce optimal results on a long-horizon project. The work was done with RektInator, Future and st0rm.

Highlights

  • Three months and ~500B tokens decompiling an FPS
  • Goal: accurate, stable, feature-complete recreation
  • Earlier posts naming the game were taken down
  • Focus on orchestration, infrastructure and harness
  • Done with RektInator, Future and st0rm
