Tencent Launches Hy4 flash

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-19 · confidence: high

Summary

Tencent introduces Hy4 flash as a lower-cost multimodal model with a stated million-token context and a smaller active parameter count than its total size. It reports favorable task cost and completion-time results from internal WorkIndex tests and a blind evaluation by 85 internal users.

Those comparisons depend on selected workloads, pricing tiers and internal samples. The company distinguishes current service availability from a future plan to release weights; an open-source plan is not proof the weights are already available. Promotional access terms are not permanent pricing.

Highlights

  • Architecture separates total and active parameters
  • Company reports internal workplace-task tests
  • User study is internal and limited in size
  • Weights are described as a future release
  • Promotional terms differ from lasting cost
