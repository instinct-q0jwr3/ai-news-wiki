AI isn't replacing SREs, it's deskilling them

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

A SigNoz newsletter essay asks a sharper question than the usual AI-and-jobs debate: not whether AI replaces SREs, but whether it quietly deskills them. If AI handles 95% of incident response, do engineers get worse at the 5% that actually matters - the novel outages? The piece anchors the concern in a 1983 research paper on automation that it calls eerily prescient, framing deskilling as a known failure mode of automation rather than a new AI anxiety.

The two popular prophecies it rejects: that SRE is the future of all software engineering (humans keep the systems alive while AI writes the code), and that AI devours every tech job including SRE. Neither, the author argues, helps an SRE planning their Tuesday in 2027; the useful work is watching whether capability erosion is already happening and whether anyone notices before the next novel outage.
