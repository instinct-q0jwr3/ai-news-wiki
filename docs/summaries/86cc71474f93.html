Why Normal People Still Laugh at AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Basis: the free preview - the full Westenberg post is subscriber-only. JA Westenberg argues that AI companies keep walking into a fifteen-year-old sitcom joke: someone asks their 'assistant' for something painfully simple and it goes absurdly wrong. Normal people still laugh at AI, the piece suggests, because the gap between the industry's promise of a competent assistant and the everyday experience of these tools was written into comedy years ago - and the labs keep delivering the punchline.
