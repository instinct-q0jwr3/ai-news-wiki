Token fraud is here and it’s BAD

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high

Summary

Simon Willison summarizes Claire Vo’s account of repeated free-trial abuse at ChatPRD, including alleged resale or diversion of model usage to unrelated businesses. The post says the service is winding down trials in response.

This is a link post recounting a product operator’s experience, not a comprehensive fraud study or independent audit. The proposed use of characteristic image prompts to identify underlying models is explicitly speculative. It illustrates how an agent’s open-ended capability can complicate the economics of a trial.
