Claude Fable/Mythos 5.1: new SOTA, 75% cache price cut

type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high

latent.space anthropic

Summary

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on September 1, billed as 'the world's most advanced models for coding and knowledge work', with the announcement drawing over 12 million views. List pricing holds at $10/$50/$12.5 per million tokens (input/output/cache write), but cache reads dropped 75% to $0.25 per million, a big win for long agentic sessions, partly offset by Artificial Analysis measuring 1.7x more output tokens, for a net per-task cost increase around 20%.

Anthropic's pitch is a 'usable autonomous worker' rather than a benchmark king: Fable 5.1 targets long-horizon delegated work, with staff highlighting honest failure reporting and Enterprise Frontier Safeguards ('ZDR++') with zero-data-retention support. Community analysis suggests Fable and Mythos 5.1 may share underlying weights with different safety/routing behavior. Reactions split between strong praise for coding, planning and tone, and complaints about rate limits, safeguard false positives and subscription UX, with Dan Shipper arguing 5.1 fixes the 'supergenius in a datacenter that was almost unusable' problem of Fable 5. Both models offer 1M-token contexts with text and image input.
