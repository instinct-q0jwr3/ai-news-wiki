Pro-AI PAC Leading the Future ended the midterms with $20M+ in its bank, from ~$100M, after focusing on safe bipartisan candidates; its riskier bets fell flat

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-05 · confidence: high

Summary

Wired reports that Leading the Future, the biggest pro-AI super PAC network, leaves the midterms with just over $20 million of the roughly $100 million it launched with in August 2025. Backers include Andreessen Horowitz, Joe Lonsdale and OpenAI's Greg Brockman. The network of 501(c)(4)s and super PACs, with American Mission giving mostly to Republicans and Think Big mostly to Democrats, was modeled on the pro-crypto Fairshake.

On the surface it looks like a win, with dozens of successful candidates from both parties. Wired's reading is that the money went overwhelmingly to safe candidates, while the few riskier races, including opposition to Democrat Alex Bores, fell flat. Critics such as the Tech Oversight Project say the intent was to intimidate lawmakers on AI safety and regulation; the PAC did not comment.

Highlights

  • Money went mostly to safe, bipartisan candidates

  • Critics say it aimed to intimidate lawmakers on AI regulation
