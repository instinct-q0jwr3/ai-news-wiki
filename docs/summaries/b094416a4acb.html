Goldman Sachs Is Pulling Claude Code from Its Bankers and Traders

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-08 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high

Summary

Goldman Sachs has restricted Anthropic's Claude Code and Microsoft's GitHub to its engineers, cutting off traders and bankers who used the tools to track client flows and model markets, per Bloomberg as relayed by The State of AI. October guidance followed a review of productivity and risk concerns. Strats, who span trading, quant research and software, keep access. The bank had barred Hong Kong bankers from Claude in April and is now a lead underwriter on Anthropic's IPO.

The change reverses a July message in which CIO Marco Argenti praised 'vibe coding' by non-technical staff, who had built tools and passed them to about 12,000 engineers to improve. The risk is that thousands of people without engineering training were running software that writes and executes code on machines handling client information. The Hong Kong limit rested on location and contract terms; the new one rests on role. Goldman's in-house multi-model assistant remains available firm-wide.

Highlights

  • Claude Code and GitHub now limited to Goldman's engineers.
  • Traders and bankers lose a tool they used for client-flow monitoring and modeling.
  • Strats keep access; guidance was updated in October.
  • Earlier, Hong Kong bankers lost Claude in April over contract terms.
  • Goldman is also a lead underwriter on Anthropic's IPO.
