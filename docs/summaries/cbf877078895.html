Anthropic opens its Australian retail IPO, giving individual investors access to Class A shares ahead of the company's planned Nasdaq listing

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high

Summary

Anthropic opened an Australian retail IPO on Wednesday so individual investors can buy Class A shares ahead of the Nasdaq listing. The offering runs 10 a.m. AEDT to 5 p.m. on Nov. 17 via brokers led by CommSec, alongside Bell Potter, LGT Wealth, Macquarie and Morgan Stanley among others. Anthropic aims to raise up to $64.4 billion in the IPO plus a $26 billion private placement, up to $90.4 billion combined, at $70 to $78 per share, with a prospectus to be lodged with ASIC.

Highlights

  • Australian retail tranche opened Wednesday, closes Nov. 17
  • Up to $64.4B IPO plus $26B private placement
  • $70 to $78 per share
  • CommSec leads; ASIC prospectus to come
