Erdosproblems.com Succumbs to the AI Onslaught

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high

Summary

The creator of erdosproblems.com reflects on how the site - built to promote Erdős problems to humans - became a barometer for AI capability as models went from useless to solving some of mathematics' hardest problems in under a year. The upside is real: many questions now have answers. But so are the costs: mathematicians dismissing Erdős-style mathematics as recreational, others giving up because they cannot compete with AI, and a wave of AI-produced solutions offered with no explanation - useful results that displace and discourage the people actually interested in the mathematics.

After soliciting public and private feedback on how the site should change, he found opinion strikingly divided. The essay is less a policy announcement than an honest reckoning with what it means when a human-curated corner of mathematics becomes an AI benchmark - a dynamic he notes has stopped being Erdős-specific as AI breakthroughs spread.

Highlights

  • erdosproblems.com became an unintended barometer of AI mathematical capability.
  • AI went from useless to solving some of the hardest problems in under a year.
  • Downsides: 'recreational math' dismissals, discouraged mathematicians, unexplained AI proofs.
  • Unexplained solutions still displace the humans who care about the mathematics.
  • Community feedback on the site's future is sharply divided.
