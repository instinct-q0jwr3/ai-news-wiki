Erdosproblems.com Succumbs to the AI Onslaught

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Thomas Bloom, who runs erdosproblems.com, writes that AI has gone from near useless to helping solve some of the hardest maths problems in under a year, and that his site has become a barometer for it. He notes real progress, but also harm: some dismiss Erdos-style problems as recreational, many stopped working on them believing they cannot compete, and a wave of AI-produced solutions without explanation displaces people who care about the mathematics. After asking users for feedback, he heard from a large silent audience who benefit from the site and says he is adjusting it with them in mind. Basis: the first part of the post was read.

Highlights

  • AI went from useless to helping with hard problems within a year.
  • Upside: many questions now answered.
  • Downside: unexplained AI solutions discourage human mathematicians.
  • Site changes follow user feedback, including a silent majority.
  • Basis: first part of the post.
