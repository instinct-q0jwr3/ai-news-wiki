AI-powered medical search engine Vera Health, which says it is used by 800,000+ clinicians in 190 countries, raised a $28.5M Series A led by Threshold Ventures

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-04 · confidence: high

Summary

Vera Health, a clinical AI search engine, has raised a $28.5 million Series A led by Threshold Ventures, with Optum Ventures, Mantis VC and SV Angel participating and existing backers Y Combinator and Gradient tripling down. The pitch is evidence-first: the platform scans more than 60 million peer-reviewed publications, clinical guidelines, consensus statements and drug labels, and - in Threshold's Emily Melton's words - starts with the evidence rather than the answer, making strengths and limitations visible at the point of care.

Co-founder Taieb Bennani frames the mission with a well-worn but damning statistic: new medical knowledge takes an estimated 17 years to reach clinical practice, and Vera wants that gap measured in seconds. The product philosophy runs on four principles - honesty, traceability, transparency and context - with honesty first: language models are built to sound certain and medicine rarely is, so Vera finds the evidence and only then writes the answer, 'a search engine that reasons like a clinician.' The company claims use by more than 800,000 clinicians across 190 countries.
