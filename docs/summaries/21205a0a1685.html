Launch HN: Maritime (YC F26) – A managed cloud for production AI agents

type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high

Summary

Maritime’s founders describe a managed cloud for persistent agents, combining isolated environments with scheduling, sleep and wake, recovery, networking and spending visibility. Their launch argues that lifecycle operations are a larger problem than creating a sandbox alone.

The pricing and security statements are founder claims, not an independent service audit or verified total-cost comparison. Commenters question patching maturity and latency. Application inference, resource needs and operational guarantees must be checked separately before choosing a provider.

Highlights

  • Launch emphasizes lifecycle management
  • Persistent isolated environments are described
  • Recovery and networking are part of the pitch
  • Founder pricing is not a complete cost audit
  • Discussion raises patching and latency questions
