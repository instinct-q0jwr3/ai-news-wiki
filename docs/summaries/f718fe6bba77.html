Amazon defines the role of the developer in the age of AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high

Summary

Amazon published a piece defining the software developer's role in the age of AI, framed around Bezos' observation that what will not change matters more than what will, and that good builders deeply understand their customers through anecdotes rather than survey averages. The argument: customer understanding and judgment are the durable core of the developer's job as AI takes over more of the mechanics.
