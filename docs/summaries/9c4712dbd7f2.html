Amazon rebrands its Kindle Unlimited subscription as Kindle+ and expands access to audiobooks, including titles narrated by AI

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high

Summary

Amazon rebranded Kindle Unlimited as Kindle+: still $12 a month, separate from Prime, with up to 20 titles borrowed at once - but the audiobook category now expands substantially, including titles narrated by AI through Amazon's Virtual Voice technology rather than human narrators. The pitch is continuity: readers can put down the Kindle and keep going with virtual narration as they move through their day.

On the supply side, Virtual Voice is free to authors in Kindle Direct Publishing, letting any KDP author add AI narration at no cost - which is how Amazon expects the audiobook catalog to scale. ZDNET's coverage frames the rebrand as Amazon folding AI narration into the mainstream subscription rather than a separate experiment, a distribution shift for synthetic voices in publishing.

Highlights

  • Kindle Unlimited rebrands to Kindle+ at $12/month, separate from Prime
  • Audiobook category expands to include AI-narrated titles via Virtual Voice
  • Readers can switch from ebook to virtual narration mid-book
  • Virtual Voice is free for KDP authors, aimed at scaling the audiobook catalog
  • Up to 20 titles borrowed at once, as before
