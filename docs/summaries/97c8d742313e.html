The Lucrative World of Literary Scams

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high

Summary

Snigdha Poonam investigates the literary scam economy after receiving an email addressed to 'Professor Snigdha Poonam' from a Seattle life-drawing community eager to discuss her seven-year-old book about young Indians, and to charge her a 'contribution fee' for the privilege. The red flags multiply: the follow-up came from a different organizer at a different community in a different city using the first man's email address, complete with a leftover AI prompt reading 'MAKE IT MORE PROFESSIONAL'. Genuine book clubs do not charge authors; the piece sets out to find who is behind the operation and how it works.

Highlights

  • Author investigates a book-club scam targeting writers
  • Pitch: fake community invites author, then requests a 'contribution fee'
  • Tells: wrong title ('Professor'), mismatched organizers and cities, shared email addresses
  • A leftover AI prompt ('MAKE IT MORE PROFESSIONAL') in the scam email
  • Genuine book clubs don't charge authors
