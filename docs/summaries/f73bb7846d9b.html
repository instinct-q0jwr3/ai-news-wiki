Reco, whose tech helps enterprises secure and govern AI agents across SaaS environments, raised a $55M Series B extension, taking its total funding to $140M

type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high

Summary

Reco raised a $55M Series B extension for tooling that secures and governs AI agents across SaaS. TechCrunch notes at least two dozen companies now sell agent security, with similar promises: knowledge graphs, monitoring, runtime security and MCP vetting.

Reco repositioned from mapping SaaS and AI platforms to a context graph linking agents to apps, people, accounts and permissions so teams can cut unneeded access. Its CEO says companies deploy agents faster than they can keep track.

Highlights

  • $55M Series B extension.
  • Crowded field: two dozen agent-security vendors.
  • Context graph shows what each agent can reach.
  • Driver: agents are deployed faster than they are inventoried.
