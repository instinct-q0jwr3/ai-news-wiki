Overview

Shopify powers a large share of independent e-commerce and is opening its checkout rails, including Shop Pay, to AI agents that complete purchases on behalf of users. This page follows its agentic-commerce strategy and platform policy.

Timeline

  • 2026-09-22 - Shopify plans to let Meta's personal AI agent, Muse, complete purchases on behalf of users at Shopify-powered stores, the Wall Street Journal reports. The purchases would run through Shop Pay, Shopify's one-tap checkout service.
