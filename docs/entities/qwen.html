Qwen

type: product · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: medium

Overview

Qwen is Alibaba's family of open-weight large language and image models. This page follows Qwen releases such as Qwen-Image, the project's leadership and organization inside Alibaba, and how the family competes with DeepSeek, MiMo and other open-weight lines.
