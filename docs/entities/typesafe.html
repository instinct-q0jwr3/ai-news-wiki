TypeSafe

type: organization · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: medium

Overview

TypeSafe is the company behind Jev, a typed decision-model spin on large language models that Vercel says was adopted faster than any other model in AI Gateway history. Its classifier.dev service wraps Jev with a confidence-based escalation tier, and the community-run JevBench ranks Jev as the leading typed decision model. The company's staying power is openly debated: analysts argue OpenAI could fast-follow by folding classification into its own models unless TypeSafe's training data and processes form a real moat.
