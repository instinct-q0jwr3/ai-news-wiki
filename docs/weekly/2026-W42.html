How to think about the transition

'AI is Not a Tool' picks apart the word everyone reaches for. A tool, the author argues, is something you wield: held in the hand, transmitting power, under continuous control - an implement with which you do something, not something that does things for you. Shirt pockets and drive-thru windows are useful but not tools; reserving 'tool' for 'anything useful' empties the word of meaning. (read more).

A paper argues that capability evaluations, which rely on being able to elicit hidden dangerous abilities, can be defeated.
