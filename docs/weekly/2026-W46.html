The bubble question

The workbench

Simon Willison, curious on a dog walk, asked Claude (Opus 5.5) to compare writing a SQLite parse_number() function, which strips commas, $, £ and % and handles a leading minus, as a C extension versus Rust. The binaries differ a lot: the Rust extension was 321 KB with rusqlite and 298 KB with sqlite-loadable-rs, against 14 KB for C. Yet the Rust version ran faster. He then asked for a C version that matches or beats Rust. Net cost per row over 2 million rows on mixed data was 67 ns for the first C, 19 ns for the new C, 46 ns for rusqlite and 41 ns for sqlite-loadable. On integers only: 25, 16, 36 and 32 ns.
