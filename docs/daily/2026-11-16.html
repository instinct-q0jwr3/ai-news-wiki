From circuits to serving constraints

A physical synthesizer build and inference engineering notes make the role of testing and workload choices visible.

A hardware developer describes using Claude Code to design an analog synthesizer through specifications, SPICE simulations, PCB generation and firmware. After assembled boards arrived, software-controlled instruments and an audio loopback supported calibration and comparison with simulation. The account includes substantial human direction: choosing the concept, reviewing the design, reorganizing PCB blocks, switching to four layers and fixing a hand-soldered bridge. Small touch keys remained awkward. The working board is evidence from one reported build, not proof that arbitrary mixed-signal circuits can be designed without expert oversight. (read more).
