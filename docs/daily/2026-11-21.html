Privacy boundaries depend on the platform

Signal’s own announcement explains why screenshot controls do not cover every route to decrypted application data.

Signal announces screenshot-related protections against operating-system AI capture on iOS 27 and supported Android environments, while explaining why these measures cannot cover every access route. It describes automatically enabling Android Screen security when assistant screen access is detected and acknowledges uneven device support. The post explicitly says filesystem access remains outside this remedy and that macOS screenshot protection is not available in the same way. Its recommended exclusions and its call for better platform controls are the publisher’s guidance, not settings changed here. Encryption in transit does not itself prevent an authorized operating system from observing decrypted content on a device. (read more).
