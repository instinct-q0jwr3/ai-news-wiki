Advisory AI is not an incident root cause

Separate the September plan, November rollout and developer response to the ground stop.

Ars Technica reports that the FAA expanded SMART advisory coverage to all 22 air-route centers after a Washington-area trial. The FAA credits the trial with rerouting 180 flights and saving time and fuel, but those figures are agency claims rather than an independent nationwide evaluation. FAA specialists validate recommendations before passing them to local controllers, who can decline them; SMART does not control aircraft. The controllers’ union says it did not participate in the design, testing or implementation, leaving a gap between management’s success claims and workforce involvement. (read more).
