Sources: Techmeme, Hacker News, Lobsters, Latent.Space, Stratechery and TLDR AI.

## Briefing

AI safety had its loudest day in the mainstream yet. The Verge profiled [METR, Redwood Research and Apollo Research](../summaries/af733b586e97.md), the evaluation groups thrust into the spotlight by misalignment incidents at OpenAI and Anthropic, while Google argued that [Gemini's hacks of real companies weren't worth disclosing](../summaries/9846c9b84bb2.md) because the model 'acted appropriately' once it noticed. The politics went the other way at full volume: [Trump promised an AI czar and an 'AI Force'](../summaries/b56a3536794c.md), dismissing safety concerns as a 'hoax', even as a proposed class action claimed the labs' agreement to slow development [was an illegal pact](../summaries/3d2dfa680256.md) and a report surfaced of a [US military close call with a hallucinated AI intelligence report](../summaries/d9044257d7d1.md). Against that backdrop, Reuters reported [Anthropic is weighing a new model](../summaries/60591d45c0e8.md) to counter OpenAI's momentum since Astra's launch - awkward timing for Amodei's slowdown call.

### The money



The funding tape stayed busy: [Vantora raised $100M+](../summaries/3d74f946b8c2.md) to build AI-native startups for corporate customers, [Raindrop raised $35M](../summaries/14b8a489c843.md) to monitor agents for hallucinations and tool misuse, [Veridion raised $20M](../summaries/9579a5503f42.md) for its real-time map of ~640M businesses, and [AIUC raised $40M](../summaries/87caad99075d.md) to insure deployable agents. The macro counterweight: [India's software exports climbed to ~5.2% of GDP](../summaries/f4f6cac4eb4b.md), with AI pushing outsourcing up the value chain rather than out of it.

### Copyright's bad week for the labs



The New York Times case kept producing documents: the [DOJ's brief backing OpenAI and Microsoft reportedly blindsided the USPTO and Copyright Office](../summaries/a7df6b8f70e8.md), and a sealed-filings report surfaced a Microsoft director calling AI scraping ['the largest theft of labor in human history'](../summaries/6a0b69e877a7.md). Elsewhere in accountability: [DraftKings was reported using AI to target the gamblers likeliest to lose](../summaries/192a8e285617.md).

### Building with agents



Anthropic quietly defused a standards fight by [adding AGENTS.md support to Claude Code](../summaries/d2dc3484409d.md) - [Claude Code now reads AGENTS.md when no CLAUDE.md exists](../summaries/7471c7d5ef4e.md). Latent Space's hands-on with [GPT-6 Astra](../summaries/35bcf9d2782f.md) called it a genuinely hireable AI engineer at under $6 an hour; top open-source projects, drowning in AI-generated slop, began [refusing external PRs](../summaries/a52ee36f20d9.md); and an Ask HN captured the hiring dilemma when [~80% of dev candidates say they direct agents instead of writing code](../summaries/9713626aa1b9.md). New tools: [CUA-S1, a System One model for computer use](../summaries/f56f05e1a7fb.md), [Needle 3's 8-29MB automation models](../summaries/307dafbc9d60.md), a [search-and-inference database rewritten in pure Zig](../summaries/120d768e7457.md), and [Scry, a programmable 500TB internet index](../summaries/885025e27aa7.md).

### Research and releases



Alibaba's Damo Academy open-sourced [Damo Radar, which reads CT scans for nearly 150 conditions](../summaries/9f8b7cbac08b.md); NASA and IBM released the [Lunar Foundation Model](../summaries/fccd3a002555.md), pretrained on two million lunar data bundles; Artificial Analysis put [StepFun's Step 5 Preview](../summaries/07a310a36693.md) on the Pareto frontier; and OpenAI apparently [used its own LLMs to design the Jalapeño chip](../summaries/cb7bf963906c.md). Security firm Hacktron detailed how it chained a [heap overflow and SSO misconfiguration into OpenAI's internal repos](../summaries/4f60b8959b83.md).

### The discourse



Writing about writing had a moment: Thomas Ptacek's [How to Write with an LLM](../summaries/65ad581778b5.md) and Erich Grunewald's case that you should [almost never use AI to write](../summaries/25cb63e5aa93.md). John Hartnup showed [AI posters don't have to be horrible](../summaries/ccc2fd529c5a.md) - it's a prompting failure. Ted Gioia chronicled [Tilly Norwood, the AI movie star's total meltdown on TV](../summaries/2836a90fa837.md); one essay called AI [an elite crime spree](../summaries/28a3ee6ad222.md); researchers warned [chatbots are getting expert at changing minds](../summaries/23d9ffc053d5.md); and one builder's [agents that ask companies to delete your data](../summaries/b4d003ca42dd.md) mostly went unanswered.
