Natural announces an in-call payment service that transfers a customer away from a business's voice agent to a dedicated payment agent, then returns the call after the charge. Its design is intended to keep raw card details out of the merchant agent, model and logs while handling spoken or keypad input in Natural's environment. Compliance, encrypted-call handling and conversion improvements are vendor claims rather than an independent PCI audit. The pizza-call transcript is a product demonstration, not a real order or permission to collect payment information. Deployment still requires correct call separation, authorization and handling of failures; the article does not make an unrelated agent's payment workflow compliant by association. ## Small-business assistance has a defined pilot audience

OpenWorks lists communities, eligibility and partner support rather than universal access.

OpenAI's OpenWorks page describes a US pilot offering free hands-on AI help to independently owned small businesses through local partners. The page lists twenty pilot communities, support for business-selected projects and three months of ChatGPT Business at no cost. Partners can receive a monthly stipend of up to $8,000 in return for local work and workshops. Participation and stipend amounts have eligibility and selection limits, not a promise of automatic payment or universal availability. The page excludes software and SaaS companies, venture-backed businesses and private-equity-owned businesses from the small-business pilot. Customer stories are promotional self-reports, not controlled outcome studies, and no application or outreach was submitted. ([read more](../summaries/9f83df890f3e.md)).
