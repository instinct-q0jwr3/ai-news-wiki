## Briefing

Tuesday belonged to Meta's Muse - as a security story. Shopify went the other way, planning to let Muse [complete purchases through Shop Pay](../summaries/69da42e0e50b.md), while Meta itself was spotted giving the agent [its own mailbox](../summaries/871e8f962980.md) and opening [developer connectors](../summaries/f1a4866d11c7.md). The accountability thread kept pace: British Columbia is [suing OpenAI for failing to flag the Tumbler Ridge suspect's ChatGPT activity](../summaries/a076342fd0a7.md), the antitrust suit over [agreeing to slow AI development](../summaries/de0f25c4f0ba.md) continued to reverberate, and OpenAI - urging the US to [lead global frontier-safety standards](../summaries/f8309798a19a.md) and insisting [fully autonomous RSI 'is not happening today'](../summaries/ffe9332a04f8.md) - was reported to have been [negotiating mutual stress-tests with Anthropic](../summaries/efbabf843cf6.md) before the hack. ### Agents and how to run them

The agent-infrastructure layer had a busy day. Google's [AX, an open agentic orchestrator](../summaries/07975f32cf7b.md), argues agents are neither microservices nor batch jobs and wraps state, isolation and cost controls around that reality; Foremerge tries to [catch intent conflicts between parallel coding agents](../summaries/76c1ddbc8498.md) that git's text diffs can't see; and an essay warned that [attackers can turn an agent's own tools against it](../summaries/1a7e4a633b30.md) as agents gain power faster than they gain security. On the personal-AI fringe: [Lossless-memory refuses to summarize](../summaries/ff78d6ac0015.md) your history, [Mini-AGI trains continually on 8 GB of VRAM](../summaries/458d8111da3b.md), and Latent Space's [Diogo Almeida interview on Jev](../summaries/5e34a38be50a.md) - 'System One models for Prod, not God' - drew a rebuttal claiming [you can use any LLM just like Jev](../summaries/9752ee4890ae.md).
