# Show HN: The Zelda timeline to catch up before the Ocarina of Time remake

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-18 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Ahead of the Ocarina of Time remake - 17 days out - a fan built an interactive Zelda timeline to catch up on the story before 'the Door of Time reopens.' Nintendo's own chronology (Hyrule Historia and its chronicle page) is the authority: after Ocarina it branches into the child, adult and fallen-hero eras.

The site walks the whole saga in short retellings, from Skyloft and the Goddess Sword through Vaati and the Picori, Ganondorf's grab for the Triforce, the Imprisoning War and beyond - the fun part, the author says, was rethinking how to visualize the branch.

## Highlights

- Interactive Zelda timeline built for the Ocarina remake, out in 17 days
- Based on Nintendo's official Hyrule Historia chronology
- Timeline branches after Ocarina: child, adult and fallen-hero eras
- Short readable retellings of each era and game
- The visualization of the branch is the project's centerpiece
