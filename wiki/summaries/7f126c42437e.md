# Introducing Natural Direct

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Natural brings Direct to general availability, giving agents a runtime choice of payment rail: ACH, wire, or realtime payments over RTP and FedNow, with mailed paper checks and international wire promised next. The argument is that agents making on-the-spot decisions cannot know in advance who they will need to pay - the counterparty's rail, due date and banking details surface at runtime - so the financial primitives have to be selectable programmatically, from the same Natural wallet an agent already uses for instant payments, requests, issued cards and checkout acceptance.

The operator keeps the reins: the controls and limits configured in Natural apply regardless of which rail the agent picks. The post walks through when each rail makes sense - ACH for payroll, invoices and rent with predictable next-day batch settlement, versus wires and realtime rails for immediacy.

## Highlights

- Natural Direct hits GA: agents pick ACH, wire or realtime (RTP/FedNow) programmatically
- Paper checks and international wire announced as coming soon
- All rails run from the same Natural wallet used for instant payments, requests and cards
- User-configured controls and limits apply across every rail
- Rationale: counterparties and rails surface at agent runtime, not in advance
