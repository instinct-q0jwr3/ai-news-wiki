## Summary

Cory Doctorow's Pluralistic issue argues that the label 'AI' has changed every 5 to 10 years since the 1950s, which hides what today's AI is. The current kind descends from older machine learning, which also used statistical methods but sat on explicit software models of the world, such as a coded chessboard and rules. Understanding that lineage is, he says, a precondition for judging the technology's capabilities, limits and above all its political economy. Only the opening of a long issue was read.
