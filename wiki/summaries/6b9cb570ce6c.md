# The Winning Essays for the Big Questions About AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`dwarkesh-podcast`

## Summary

Dwarkesh Patel announced the winners of his 'Big Questions About AI' essay contest - over 600 submissions. First place: Jassi Pannu (Assistant Professor at Johns Hopkins, biosecurity and pandemic preparedness, board member of Blueprint Biosecurity), answering what the OpenAI Foundation should do: she argues we can live in a post-disease world and gives concrete plans for dedicating tens of billions of dollars to it.

Second place: Ege Erdil, co-founder of Mechanize (environments and evals for frontier coding agents). The post publishes all three winning essays in full.

## Highlights

- 600+ essays submitted to Dwarkesh's Big Questions contest
- Winner Jassi Pannu: the OpenAI Foundation should fund a post-disease world
- Her proposal: concrete plans for tens of billions toward pandemic preparedness
- Second place: Ege Erdil, Mechanize co-founder (coding-agent evals)
- All three winning essays published in full in the post
