## Summary

Starting today, you can create, steer, resume, and watch your Devin Cloud sessions right from your favorite terminal.

Devin CLI is perfect for fast local iteration with your favorite models, but sometimes you want to hand off the implementation details to an agent.

Importantly, handing off a task doesn’t require switching your workflow: once you’ve delegated work to Devin, you can continue to observe and steer progress directly from your terminal as if Devin was working locally—while having the…

## Highlights

- With /handoff you can now instantly transfer any task to Devin to continue iterating on with its own cloud VM.
- Cloud sessions outlive the terminal that started them.
- When a terminal isn’t the right view, /open [web] opens the current session in the web app, and /open desktop opens it in Devin Desktop .
- We’re now launching full SSH support for Devin VMs, allowing you to use Devin’s development environment as your own.
- We’re making it even easier to try Devin Cloud from your terminal, with free SWE-2 sessions available until October 8.
- Just install the Devin CLI and run devin --cloud to start: curl -fsSL https://cli.devin.ai/install.sh | bash Read more in the docs .
