# Muse, Instagram, and VLC Lookalike Rip-Offs in the Mac App Store

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `meta` `muse`

## Summary

The Mac App Store is hosting lookalike rip-offs trading on Muse, Instagram, and VLC, and the receipts are user reviews from people who paid before realizing their mistake. One reviewer 'subscribed because I confused this service with Meta Muse'; others describe an Instagram-lookalike that seizes the screen with an unclosable free-trial popover that converts to a paid subscription and even ignores 'Quit App', forcing users to force-quit or delete the app to regain control. The post compares the two Muse listings side by side: Meta's is subtitled 'Your personal AI agent' while the copycat runs 'Your professional AI Agent', with near-identical screenshot headers ('Approve what gets sent or spent' vs 'Approve what gets sent or connect') and an opening description copied almost word for word.

## Highlights

- Mac App Store listings are imitating Muse, Instagram, and VLC, per user reviews and listing comparisons
- One user subscribed after confusing the copycat with Meta Muse, then asked for a refund
- The Instagram lookalike locks the screen behind an unclosable free-trial popover and ignores Quit
- Copycat Muse subtitles itself 'Your professional AI Agent' against Meta's 'Your personal AI agent'
- Screenshot headers and the app description are copied nearly word for word
- Users report having to force-quit or delete the app to escape the paywall
