## Summary

Latent Space's AINews leads with Mercor's $750M Series D at a $20B valuation. The data vendor reports $3B annualized revenue after crossing $1B in February and $2B in June, five million experts on its books, and says it already spends three times as much on inference as on employee salaries, a ratio it argues points to much faster GDP growth if it holds across the economy.
