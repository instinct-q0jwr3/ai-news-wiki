## Summary

Simon Willison discusses a Gemini plugin release and the confusion created by changing model names and temporary prices. He contrasts current promotional ordering with the later standard-price ordering and runs a few familiar drawing and animation examples.

Those examples are personal demonstrations, not a controlled ranking. The account’s promotion dates and prices are reported launch context, not a guarantee of current billing or access on another account.
