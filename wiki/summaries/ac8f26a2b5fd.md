# Amazon's Ring unveils the $249 Ring Drone Cam and the $999 Ring Rover Cam, utilizing its AI Pilot navigation software, under new subbrand Ring Robotics

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `amazon`

## Summary

Ring launched Ring Robotics with the $249 Ring Drone Cam, an indoor mobile camera drone with 2K video, and the $999 Ring Rover Cam, a business patrol robot. Both use 'AI Pilot' navigation, which maps interiors with cameras, sensors and cloud vision models and moves between patrol points.

## Highlights

- $249 indoor drone cam, $999 patrol rover
- AI Pilot maps interiors and patrols autonomously
- New Ring Robotics subbrand
