## Summary

Cory Doctorow's Free lunches essay argues that persistent technology-platform margins reflect barriers to competition rather than frictionless market efficiency. It links monopoly power to restrictive contracts, intellectual-property rules, arbitration, regulatory capture and limits on interoperability, using advertising, cloud services, ride-hailing and health care as examples.

This is a polemical economic argument supported by links and examples, not a new empirical audit of every margin, allegation or sector-wide causal claim. Its position is that removing legal and structural barriers would create opportunities for challengers. The dated archive links, event notices and book promotions elsewhere on the page are not part of the current argument.

## Highlights

- Essay challenges a frictionless-market account of high margins
