# The White House's AI task force begins its 120-day window to evaluate AI, including producing a risk report, implementing the White House Accord on SI, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

## Summary

The White House AI task force adopted its charter after its first meeting, starting a 120-day window to answer hard questions on how the government should handle AI. Semafor reports it will commission a risk report, among other work.

## Highlights

- 120-day clock starts for the White House AI task force.
- A risk report is among the first assignments.
