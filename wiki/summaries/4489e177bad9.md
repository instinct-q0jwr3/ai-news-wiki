## Summary

Tech.eu reports a financing round for Boltz, which develops models used to propose and assess molecules for drug discovery. The article describes spending plans for compute, data and hiring and distinguishes open models from its platform and partnerships.

Funding and binding predictions do not demonstrate clinical efficacy or eliminate laboratory testing. The startup’s long-term ambition is a goal rather than an achieved end-to-end drug-development system. The report also conflicts internally with its feed about the timing of an earlier seed round, so that date is not relied on here.
