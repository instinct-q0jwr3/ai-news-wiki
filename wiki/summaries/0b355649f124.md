# Numeral, a provider of AI-powered tech to automate sales tax compliance workflows in over 90 countries, raised a $100M Series C led by Insight Partners

## Summary

FinTech Global : Numeral, a provider of AI-powered tech to automate sales tax compliance workflows in over 90 countries, raised a $100M Series C led by Insight Partners — Numeral, the AI-driven sales tax compliance platform, has closed…

Numeral , the AI-driven sales tax compliance platform, has closed a $100m Series C round as businesses face a widening set of tax obligations across markets and product types.

It will also hire across engineering, sales, marketing and product.

## Highlights

- Salesforce Ventures, Geodesic, Benchmark, Mayfield, FCVC, Y Combinator and Uncork also took part.
- Numeral plans to spend the capital on faster product development and a broader offering for sectors such as software, manufacturing, distribution and wholesale.
- The raise reflects a regulatory environment that keeps moving.
- California has passed legislation that brings prewritten software into the scope of sales and use tax from 2027.
- Firms trading across multiple states and countries must track changes like this and adjust their systems each time.
- Numeral has grown quickly over the past year.
- The platform covers the full sales tax compliance lifecycle.
- Numeral has also introduced an accounting partner programme.
