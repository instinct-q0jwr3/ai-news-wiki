# Kimi for Science: More scientific ideas, made possible

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Kimi for Science invites natural-science, engineering and interdisciplinary researchers to apply for API credits and technical support. The official page advertises up to $20,000 in credits for selected projects and a December 8 first-cohort application deadline, with further cohorts planned.

The maximum is not a guaranteed award, cash grant or fixed token entitlement: amount and acceptance depend on review and research needs. Early model access and longer-term collaboration are offered opportunities rather than independently demonstrated scientific outcomes. Application details, data-sharing terms and eligibility edge cases were not separately audited, and no application or account was submitted.
