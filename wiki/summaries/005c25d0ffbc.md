# What Does a Positive AI Future Look Like?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Patrick Collison asks what the positive AI scenario actually looks like. Curing diseases and abundance are ingredients, he says, not an equilibrium: do people work, why, what is scarce, how are resources shared? He finds fiction like the Culture novels unconvincing as a goal, and notes that earlier eras changed slowly enough for error correction, which fast AI progress may not allow. He also calls the debate on AI consciousness high-stakes, because conclusions may drive moral-worth questions.

## Highlights

- Positive visions of AI are vague compared with risk talk
- Open questions: work, scarcity, resource allocation
- Fast change leaves less room for error correction
- AI consciousness may matter for moral status
