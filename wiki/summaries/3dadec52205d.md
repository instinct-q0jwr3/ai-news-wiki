# Yes, Claude can do Nine Loops

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Physicist and science writer Matt von Hippel challenged AI companies to impress him by tackling his old field: 'Show that an AI can take the kinds of computer resources an academic has access to, and solve one of the scattering amplitudes field's big outstanding problems... Give us N=8 supergravity to seven loops, or N=4 super Yang-Mills to nine loops.'

In this guest post he shares what happened when he issued that challenge - and, as he puts it, it is not often that you issue a challenge only to see it met. The result: yes, Claude can do nine loops.

## Highlights

- Von Hippel challenged AI labs to crack a scattering-amplitudes problem.
- The bar: N=8 supergravity to seven loops or N=4 SYM to nine loops.
- With only academic-level compute, not datacenters.
- Guest post reports the challenge was met.
- 'Yes, Claude can do Nine Loops.'
