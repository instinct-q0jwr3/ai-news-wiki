## Summary

Vespper (YC F24) launches what it calls a state-of-the-art Docx MCP, aiming at the surprisingly hard problem of agents editing Word documents. In legal, finance, and healthcare the .docx file is the deliverable, and engineers report spending weeks or months tuning harnesses to edit them reliably. The post explains why: a .docx is a ZIP of verbose OOXML XML parts where a 4-5 sentence paragraph can inflate into thousands of tokens, user-visible text is split across many XML nodes, and changes are not local the way they are in Markdown or simple HTML. Existing agent approaches fall into three categories, all of which break on complex scenarios.
