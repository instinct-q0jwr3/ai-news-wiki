## Highlights

- Launch HN for Vespper, a YC F24 startup building a Docx MCP for agents
- In legal, finance, and healthcare the Word document is the deliverable, but agents struggle to edit .docx well
- Engineers report weeks to months tuning harnesses for reliable Word editing
- A .docx is a ZIP of verbose OOXML: a short paragraph can become thousands of tokens
- Visible text is split across many XML nodes, so edits are not local like in Markdown or HTML
- Existing approaches fall into three categories that all fail on complex documents
