# Authority Instead of Substance: How AI Exposed the Emptiness of Expert Discourse

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Oleg Kholin argues that fluent but uninformative AI prose exposes an older problem in human expert discourse: reputation, caution and polished structure can substitute for specific claims and evidence. The essay compares the effect to a fictional decoder stripping pomp from official speech.

This is a critical essay, not a corpus study of experts, a controlled assessment of model training or a validated AI detector. Its proposed feedback loop between internet writing and preference training is an explanation offered by the author. Stylistic similarity alone cannot establish machine authorship or emptiness, and the piece does not demonstrate a cryptographic break.

## Highlights

- Essay distinguishes authority from substantive content
- Argument uses literary analogy
- Training-feedback explanation is interpretive
- No representative corpus measurement is supplied
- Polished prose alone does not identify AI authorship
- No specific cryptographic result is verified
