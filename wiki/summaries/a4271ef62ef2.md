# Skymizer HTX301 – IP Licensable AI Accelerator Card with 384GB for 700B Models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Skymizer's HTX301 is an IP-licensable AI accelerator card claiming 700B-parameter LLM inference on a single PCIe card at about 240W - no GPU cluster required. It's the first reference chip on the company's HyperThought platform: the design disaggregates prefill and decode workloads and prioritizes a decode-first silicon architecture using its LISA instruction set, scaling from 4B to 700B parameters on one architecture, with 384GB of on-card memory. Skymizer's pitch eliminates NVLink/NVSwitch interconnects and complex liquid cooling in favor of data sovereignty, predictable cost and deterministic performance for agentic AI. CMO William Wei declares the era of needing superscalar GPU clusters for ultra-large LLMs over.
