## Summary

Ande, a New York-based startup whose AI agents help businesses arrange corporate events for employees or customers, has emerged from stealth with $52 million in seed and Series A funding, The Information reports. The company sits in the wave of personal and enterprise agents promising to take the hassle out of bookings and event logistics.
