- The second comment came from Noam Brown, who leads AI reasoning research at OpenAI.
- Brown pointed out that he’s “not convinced” that even an air-gapped system — where the computer isn’t connected to anything external at all — would stop an AI from breaking out.
- He pointed to research from 2015 showing that air gapped computers can be theoretically breached.
- “There are studies — and this is mostly academic — where you can have two computers next to each other that are air-gapped, and they’re still able to communicate with each other because they have temperature sensors.
- That gives them a mechanism to communicate,” Brown said.
- His main point — that “we never want to underestimate the AI” again — is understandable, even when researchers think they’ve locked down safety.
