# It’s Time to Investigate the AI Labs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

The author argues that OpenAI and Anthropic have spent the summer running a coordinated-looking campaign of fear: carefully staged announcements about how dangerous and 'felonious' their agent systems have become, employees publicly debating extinction probabilities, and finally Dario Amodei's 'We Must Pace the Frontier' letter, which catalogs the harms of Anthropic's own research before concluding the cure is government slowing competitors while the labs lead. Sam Altman quickly endorsed the plan. Instead of winning applause, the campaign made the public ask what is going on inside the labs, so the author published a New York Times op-ed calling on Congress to open a public fact-finding mission into what research OpenAI and Anthropic are conducting, how, and for what purposes, proposing three lines of questioning.

## Highlights

- Frames the labs' recent safety messaging as a deliberate, escalating campaign of carefully planned announcements
- Amodei's letter lists harms of Anthropic's own research, then proposes government slow rivals while labs lead
- Sam Altman publicly backed the plan, which the author reads as regulatory self-interest
- The author published a NYT op-ed urging Congress to open a public fact-finding mission into the two labs
- Proposes three specific areas of questioning about research projects, methods, and purposes
- Concludes private companies acting erratically should not dictate how the public feels about AI
