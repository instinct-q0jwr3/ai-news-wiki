# Hitting a billion tokens per minute on one GPU by combining a query planner and an inference engine

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-09-29 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

TypeSafe AI introduces Quail, a query-aware inference layer that hits over a billion tokens per minute per H100 on a multi-join AI-SQL query - more than 10x its vLLM baseline, and under 6 cents per billion tokens on Modal. The thesis: while everyone builds coding agents, a quieter inference revolution is happening at the data layer, where LLM transformations of data (like an AI.IF prompt filter inside a SQL join) produce millions of long sequences that need far less than frontier intelligence. Serving those workloads through engines optimized for agentic, arbitrary-request traffic is massively inefficient, so Quail co-designs inference with the query planner; Karpathy is quoted framing the underlying model Jev as a point on the LLM pareto curve in an under-invested regime (no thinking, single token, low latency). On TypeSafe's new AI-SQL benchmark, Quail runs 1.84x faster than vLLM geometrically averaged, including two queries deliberately included to show headroom.

## Highlights

- Quail: a query-aware inference layer for AI-SQL workloads
- Over 1 billion tokens/minute per H100 on a heavy multi-join query, >10x a vLLM baseline
- Under 6 cents per billion tokens on Modal
- The workload: SQL queries with LLM predicates (AI.IF) generating millions of long, simple sequences
- Small open-weights models suffice; agentic-optimized engines waste that fact
- 1.84x geomean speedup over vLLM on their released AI-SQL benchmark
- Karpathy: this regime (no thinking, single-token, low-latency) had large latent demand and was under-invested
