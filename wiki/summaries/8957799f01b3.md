## Summary

The Information reports that DeepSeek and Moonshot AI relied heavily on distilling OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to train models including Kimi K3.1 Pro and V4.1-Flash 1017, after Anthropic's crackdown closed off its models as a distillation source. GPT-6 Astra, OpenAI's most powerful release, has seen strong demand since early September.
