# Stockholm-based legal AI startup Legora raised a $550M Series E at an $8.8B post-money valuation, up from $5.55B after raising a $550M Series D in March

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

City A.M. reports Stockholm-based legal AI startup Legora has raised a $550 million Series E at an $8.8 billion post-money valuation, up from $5.55 billion after a $550 million Series D in March, led by Atreides Management, with Nvidia's NVentures and Atlassian having joined in the earlier round. Legora, which builds drafting and review tools for law firms and in-house legal teams, will put the money into North American expansion across seven offices and into its agentic operating system, Legora aOS. CEO Max Junestrand says AI is changing the role of the lawyer toward judgment and advice, and the company had been eyeing a $10 billion valuation as recently as August.
