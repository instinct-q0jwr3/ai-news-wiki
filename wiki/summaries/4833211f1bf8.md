# OpenClaw creator says he spent $10 million in tokens last month

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw creator and member of technical staff at OpenAI, told Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht on stage at Cloudflare Connect 2026 that he spent around $10 million in tokens running AI workloads last month - on investors' money, as he freely admitted. Knecht had previously noted Steinberger's 'virtually unlimited token budget,' and Steinberger's follow-up - 'not all of that is useful' - drew more murmurs than reassurance from the audience.

The anecdote lands amid reports that OpenAI is trying to accelerate funding ahead of Anthropic's expected IPO, and it crystallizes a live debate about the economics of agentic coding: token spend at that scale is either R&D that compounds or burn that doesn't, and even the person spending it isn't sure how much was useful.
