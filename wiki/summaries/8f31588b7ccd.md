# An interview with YouTuber Benn Jordan, who recently testified in Congress against license plate readers, on Flock, privacy, the surveillance backlash, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Nilay Patel interviews Benn Jordan - musician, YouTuber and researcher - who has become the most visible investigator of Flock Safety, the automatic license plate reader company, and recently testified before the Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism at a hearing dedicated to Flock and other ALPR systems.

The backlash he helped build is bipartisan and now resembles the pushback against AI data centers: hundreds of cities, counties and local governments have canceled Flock contracts, Republican Sen. Josh Hawley has opened an investigation, and Reuters reported Flock plans to cut about 18 percent of its workforce. The conversation digs into how a creator ended up as the face of the anti-Flock movement and where the surveillance fight goes next.
