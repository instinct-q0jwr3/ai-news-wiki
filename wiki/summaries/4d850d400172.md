# Geoffrey Hinton: it might turn out we were better off without AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-06 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

In a Guardian interview, Geoffrey Hinton says it might turn out humanity would have been better off never developing AI, while insisting he is not a doomer and that AI could bring a 'wonderful future'. He warns of a possible large-scale incident this year or next, such as a temporary blackout of financial or power systems, and says that within about ten years we will know whether AI's risks can be managed, giving 'radical abundance' or 'massive unemployment' and a society torn apart.

He was speaking before a screening of 'AI: Probably Nothing to Worry About', a documentary featuring him and Demis Hassabis; he says he feels a duty to use his reputation and will 'burn it all' on the cause. He singles out leaders: he does not trust Sam Altman 'at all', and says Zuckerberg, Musk and Altman understand the risks but would rather have the money. Altman said in September the world should trust that OpenAI will do the right thing.

Hinton, 78, has worked on AI for about 44 years and left Google in 2023 to speak freely.
