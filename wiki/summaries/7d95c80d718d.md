# Show HN: Petal, open-source CoScreen/ScreenHero finally free

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Petal is an open-source screen collaboration tool, from a CoScreen founder, aiming to be the free replacement after Datadog's takeover of the original. It lets people click into a shared window to type, scroll and click as if it were their own.

## Highlights

- Open-source CoScreen/ScreenHero alternative.
- Remote control of shared windows.
- Written in Rust.
