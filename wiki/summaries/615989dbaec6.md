## Summary

A Nature news explainer reports rumors that AI-generated algorithms could break public-key cryptography, while noting the rumors could not be confirmed. The visible record, per an independent explainer tracking the story, is narrower: Scott Aaronson wrote on November 24 that he had heard rumors that one or more AI labs are 'sitting on' polynomial-time factoring, while saying he did not know how much to credit them, and Google Research posted a paper the next morning claiming a proven but still subexponential factoring bound, not a polynomial one. No published paper, code or statement claims factoring in P or an attack on RSA, and researchers quoted in coverage are calling for code, data and methods to be released so results can be reproduced.
