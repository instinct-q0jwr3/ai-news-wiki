# Beijing-based Sand.ai, an AI startup specializing in video generation, raised a $200M Series A+, bringing its total funding over the past 10 months to $300M

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

DealStreetAsia reports Beijing's Sand.ai, a video-generation startup, raised a $200M Series A+ at a valuation above $1B, making it a unicorn. CoStone Capital and Wangsu Science & Technology led, joined by state-owned Pudong Venture Capital and an arm of Shanghai International Group, with existing investors iMPC, CAS Star and HyT re-upping. It is the third round of 2026 and brings 10-month funding to $300M, less than five months after over $100M across two rounds.

## Highlights

- $200M Series A+, valuation over $1B
- Led by CoStone Capital and Wangsu
- Third round of 2026; $300M in ten months
- State-backed funds took part
