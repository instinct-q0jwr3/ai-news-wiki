# Micron reports Q4 revenue up 379% YoY to $54.23B, vs. $51.07B est., net income up 1,078% to $37.7B, and forecasts Q1 revenue above estimates

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Micron reported fiscal Q4 revenue up 379% year over year to $54.23 billion, ahead of the $51.07 billion estimate, with net income up 1,078% to $37.7 billion. It also forecast first-quarter revenue above estimates. CNBC ties the results to soaring demand for AI infrastructure memory. Based on the Techmeme headline and excerpt.

## Highlights

- Q4 revenue $54.23B, up 379%, vs $51.07B expected
- Net income $37.7B, up 1,078%
- Q1 revenue guidance above estimates
- Driver: AI infrastructure memory demand
