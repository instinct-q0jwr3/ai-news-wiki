# Training Text-to-Image Models Without a VAE

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Linum team proposes Pyramid-JiT (P-JiT), a decoder-only pixel-space architecture for text-to-image that drops the VAE entirely and predicts the target image at several resolutions along the DiT trunk. It matches Linum v2's FD-DINOv2 with 11.3x fewer training samples and trains in 4.3x fewer GPU-hours at 4x the pixels; code and weights are out under Apache 2.0 as a research artifact en route to Linum v3.

The motivation is video economics: the best video models are expensive because attention runs on huge token sequences - a 5-second 720p Linum v2 clip costs 110K tokens, versus the 8K contexts LLMs train on. Cutting tokens cuts attention cost, so the team is using text-to-image as a testbed for context-condensing ideas, aiming to make animation tools affordable enough for anyone to bring a story to screen.
