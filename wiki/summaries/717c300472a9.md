`hacker-news`

## Summary

Quili AI is a project from Quilicura, a community in Chile, where more than 40 residents answered over 25,000 prompts by hand on January 31 between 8 AM and 8 PM, posing as a chatbot to draw attention to data-center water use. Each chat averaged about 11 minutes and people from more than 68 countries took part. The organizers say the campaign reached over a billion people and thank supporters for helping 'save our water'. Press coverage in several languages is linked on the page.

## Highlights

- Quilicura residents answered 25,000+ prompts by hand as a human 'AI' on Jan 31
- Aim: highlight data-center water use in their community
- Claimed reach: users from 68+ countries, over 1 billion people through coverage
