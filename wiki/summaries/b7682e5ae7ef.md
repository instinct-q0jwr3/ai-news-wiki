# Meta quietly launched Waddle World, a Tamagotchi-like, AI pet-rearing game, in the Philippines in September, calling it a "daily journal disguised as a pet"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta`

## Summary

Engadget reports Meta quietly launched Waddle World, a Tamagotchi-like AI pet-rearing game, in the Philippines in late September. Meta describes it as a 'daily journal disguised as a pet': you hatch an egg, feed and play with the pet, and write or dictate journal entries that the AI pet answers with a reply and a doodle.

Mini-games - stacking groceries on the pet's head, catching falling stars, spelling words - earn points for food and toys. Engadget reads the cuteness as Meta's strategy to get people using and training its models and agents; the game is Philippines-only for now, on the App Store and Google Play.
