# Frontier AI models outperform analysts on earnings prediction

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Samaya AI reports that frontier models running in its finance-specific harness predicted earnings surprises better than expert analysts, which it calls a critical inflection point. The harness gives models point-in-time financial information on par with human experts.

The post presents a chart where recent models have lower revenue prediction error than analysts. This is a vendor's own research, selling the harness, so independent replication is not shown.

## Highlights

- Frontier models beat expert analysts on earnings surprise prediction
- Uses Samaya's finance harness with point-in-time data
- Measured by revenue prediction error
- Vendor research, not independently replicated
