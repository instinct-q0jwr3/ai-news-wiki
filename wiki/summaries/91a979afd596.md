# Publish fake stats, get cited in LLMs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A GEO researcher at Peec AI describes seeding a throwaway domain, stateglobe.com, with 50,000 entirely fabricated statistics pages generated in a single day with GPT-4.1, later adding 15,000 listicles, for a total cost of about $50. Seven months on, Google has deindexed the site, but ChatGPT has not: the fake numbers have driven more than 10,000 AI-referred sessions, attracted over 330 referring domains including Shopify, Bluehost, and DHL, and surfaced in an academic paper and a bank's investment research. His argument is that LLMs producing time-sensitive content hunt for reliable-looking data to fill knowledge gaps, and statistics pages are exactly what they reach for. Where most GEO advice targets the moment of the AI answer, this experiment maps what happens after, when people reuse the output in presentations, shopping lists, and their own sites.

## Highlights

- 50,000 fabricated stats pages generated in one day for about $30
- 15,000 listicles added later for roughly $20 more
- Google deindexed the site; ChatGPT kept citing it
- Over 10,000 AI-referred sessions and 330+ referring domains, including Shopify, Bluehost, DHL
- Invented numbers ended up in an academic paper and a bank's investment research
- Premise: LLMs writing breaking content grab reliable-looking statistics to fill gaps
- Builds on Seer Interactive's August 2025 study of the same tactic
