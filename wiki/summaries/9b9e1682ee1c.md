# Spending 35B Opus 5.5 Tokens to Write a Symphony

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Dan Price describes a month-long Opus 5.5 project producing a four-movement orchestral score and virtual-instrument rendering through thousands of actor–critic iterations. He reports roughly thirty-five billion tokens, about 2,400 score-review rounds, 184 recording rounds and a small number of video revisions, using textual scores and scripted production tools.

This is an author account, not independent artistic assessment or evidence that more iterations always improve quality. Price selected goals, supplied textbooks, listened and requested changes; the model judged written music and measured audio rather than listening itself. The recording is a software mockup, not a live orchestra, and neither the audio nor the token accounting was independently audited here.

## Highlights

- Score is written in LilyPond text
- Author supplies goals, books and listening feedback
- Critics specialize in limited review subjects
- Reported token use is not independently audited
- Virtual instruments supply the recording
- Model does not listen to the audio in this account
- Iteration is not a formal test of artistic merit
- No music-generation workflow was executed
