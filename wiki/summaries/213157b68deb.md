## Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports that institutional investors have told Anthropic its IPO valuation should land around $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion - down from higher figures floated earlier. Coming the same week as the amended S-1's growth-slowdown disclosure, the number suggests the roadshow conversation is now about how much to discount the deceleration rather than whether it exists.
