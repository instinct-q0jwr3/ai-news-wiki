# The CMA says Meta made changes to Muse's ToS in the UK to reflect the country's consumer law; the terms give users far more protections than in the US

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse`

## Summary

POLITICO compares Muse's UK and US terms and reports that the UK Competition and Markets Authority sought changes reflecting consumer law. Its account contrasts a US liability cap with UK wording about reasonable skill and care, while noting that practical redress for agent mistakes remains untested in court.

This is reporting on jurisdiction-specific terms, not a promise that every loss is recoverable, a legal opinion for a particular user or evidence of Spanish availability. Separate proposals to extend product liability to software remain proposals. Contract text, exceptions and regulatory sources were not independently legally audited, and no terms, account, payment or complaint was submitted.

## Highlights

- Report attributes UK changes to CMA engagement
- US and UK contractual positions differ
- Practical court outcomes remain untested
- Future product-liability reform is separate
- No universal compensation guarantee follows
- No account or legal action was taken
