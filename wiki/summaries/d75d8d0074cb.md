# Vitalik Buterin says "we should take the risks to cryptography from AI-accelerated math seriously", backing a "bunker mode" call for the blockchain industry

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Cointelegraph reports Vitalik Buterin saying the risks to cryptography from AI-accelerated math should be taken seriously, backing a 'bunker mode' call for the blockchain industry from Justin Drake. Ethereum researchers say users should consider moving funds to fresh addresses.

The warning is that AI could break wallet security within months, which is a claim from the researchers and is not confirmed. It follows reports that AI labs are testing models on cryptographic problems.

## Highlights

- Buterin backs a bunker-mode call for crypto
- Concern: AI-accelerated math could threaten wallet cryptography
- Researchers suggest moving funds to fresh addresses
- Timeline of months is a claim, not established
- Related to reports of labs probing crypto protocols
