# The Discovery Phase Is All There Is

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high_

`works-on-my-machine`

## Summary

A piece of satirical fiction presented as documents recovered from a decommissioned Slack workspace. The narrator wanders 'The Building', an office where floors are renamed, merged and deprecated faster than anyone can map them, and where the Department of Best Practices issues Memos that contradict and supersede each other within days - Memo #4301 mandates a framework that is redacted and replaced before the ink dries.

The centerpiece is an argument between a Typed Division developer and a Dynamic Corps developer over whether an instruction is clearer with every word annotated or written plainly - 'People will skim this and misunderstand it' versus 'People will never read yours!' Both are gone by the end of lunch, their badges invalid, replaced by a Memo disbanding all Corps and Divisions. Under the comedy sits a real thesis about AI-era engineering: practices churn too fast for doctrine to settle, so the discovery phase never ends - learn to pilot your agent in low-stakes environments, start lower than your fear, and treat wipeouts as the curriculum.

## Highlights

- Satire of AI-engineering culture: 'The Building' has floors for RAG, prompt engineering and system prompts, all perpetually deprecated
- Memos from the Department of Best Practices supersede each other within days; one Memo turns out to be a test that gets obedient readers reassigned
- A Typed Division and a Dynamic Corps developer argue annotation versus plain prose - and both vanish before dessert
- The final Memo disbands all Corps and Divisions: 'Memos are to be evaluated on results, not notation'
- The underlying advice: pilot agents in low-stakes environments before betting your week on them
- 'Start lower than your fear. Wipeouts are the curriculum.'
- The joke lands because the churn is real - the workspace was migrated six hours after these documents were created, and the new platform is already deprecated
