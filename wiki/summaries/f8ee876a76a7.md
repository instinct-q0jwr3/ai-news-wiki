# Introducing Kimi K3.1 Pro

## Summary

Simon Willison's link post on Kimi K3.1 Pro's launch: live on the Kimi API today, not yet open weights but with full weights promised by November 4, 2026, plus a Highspeed variant 2-3x faster at twice the price (input cache hits priced the same). US pricing is $2.00/M input and $10.00/M output - matching GPT-6.1 Sol and half the price of Claude Opus 5.5.

Moonshot's own benchmarks show K3.1 Pro approximately level with Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, while Artificial Analysis scores it 55 - ahead of Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 4 Argon (all 53) and behind only Opus 5.5 (58). Willison flags the milestone: this is the first time a soon-to-be open-weight model has scored this high against the frontier.

## Highlights
