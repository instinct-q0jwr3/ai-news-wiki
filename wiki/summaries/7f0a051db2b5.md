## Summary

George Hotz argues in a polemical essay that recursive AI improvement could concentrate power while excluding ordinary users and institutions. He predicts geopolitical conflict and criticizes the motives he attributes to AI-safety organizations.

These are the author’s political judgments and speculative forecasts, not verified descriptions of corporate plans, inevitable outcomes or quantified geopolitical evidence. The essay’s forceful language should not be converted into factual claims about named people or groups.

## Highlights

- Essay presents an adversarial political view
- Recursive improvement scenario is hypothetical
- Motives are attributed by the author
- War prediction is speculative
- No empirical probability estimate is established
