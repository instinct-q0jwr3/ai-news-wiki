# Alibaba open-sources RADAR, a CT-scan model for ~150 conditions

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `alibaba`

## Summary

Alibaba's Damo Academy has open-sourced Damo Radar, a vision-language model it calls 'the world's first expert-level generalist medical imaging model.' Trained on contrast-enhanced CT scans paired with clinical reports, it reads scans covering 18 abdominal organs and identifies nearly 150 conditions, including cancers. In nearly 40,000 real-world examinations it achieved an average AUC of 0.913 across 146 clinical findings.

In a comparative study with 26 radiologists from multiple hospitals, the model's average accuracy beat 23 of them, and working with the model the radiologists cut missed diagnoses by 10 percent while reducing reading time by more than 30 percent. The research was published in Science with participation from institutions including a Zhejiang University-affiliated hospital, and the team says the training method could extend to other imaging types - the latest in Alibaba's string of medical AI releases as Chinese tech giants push into healthcare.
