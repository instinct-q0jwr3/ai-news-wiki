# AI Came for the Coders. Understanding Why Tells Us Who's Next

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Robert Peake’s essay uses software work to argue that ease of verifying an output helps explain which tasks models learn well. The examined opening contrasts people’s sense of what feels human with the uneven distribution of machine capability.

The author illustrates the argument with a personal voice-cloning experience and analogies to mechanized craft. These are not controlled labor-market evidence or proof that any occupation has a fixed automation ceiling. This summary covers the opening and verification argument, not every later claim.

## Highlights

- Essay focuses on feedback and verification
- Human identity is distinguished from task mechanics
- Voice example is anecdotal
- Craft analogy is interpretive
- No job-loss forecast is measured
