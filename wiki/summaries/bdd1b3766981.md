# AI agents have a big problem: Nobody wants to let them in

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

A consumer-technology column argues that personal agents face a growing access problem as businesses restrict automated assistants. United's booking restrictions supply a concrete example, with account security, unintended purchases and regulatory requirements cited as reasons.

The larger claim is about institutional willingness, not just model competence. An assistant can be technically able to navigate a service while lacking permission to use it or cooperation from the business. One airline's decision does not establish that every industry has taken the same position.

## Highlights

- Business access is distinct from technical capability
- United provides the central example
- Credentials and unintended actions are cited concerns
- Regulatory duties can constrain automation
- Column does not establish universal rejection
