# Cuban: Facebook knowingly runs AI deepfake scam ads; docs say $16B fraud revenue

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-06 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Business Insider's Yahoo Finance piece from Oct. 6 reports Mark Cuban saying Facebook knowingly runs AI deepfake scam ads. He gets weekly emails from people who bought a $294 diabetes 'cure' he never sold; the ads use an AI copy of his face and voice. He says some ads even carry a disclaimer that the person is AI-generated and for entertainment, and that by the time anyone acts the pitch has worked. 'Facebook knows the little old ladies are getting ripped off,' he said on Pivot, calling it the culture of the organization. The headline cites Meta documents showing $16 billion in fraud-linked revenue.
