# "Who is Hiring?" postings stuck near 12-year lows as AI reaches half of all jobs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

HN Trends counts 244 postings in November’s Who Is Hiring thread as of November 14, down 37% from the same point a year earlier. AI appears in 51.6% of those postings, while agent-related terms are gaining prominence.

This measures one community’s job advertisements and keyword choices, not half of all jobs or a causal effect of AI on employment. The author also distinguishes November-only figures from smoothed role trends and notes changes to historical term matching.

## Highlights

- Sample is an HN hiring thread
- Comparison uses the same point in the month
- AI share counts keyword mentions
- Role figures use three-month averages
- No economy-wide causal result is established
