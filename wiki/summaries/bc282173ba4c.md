## Summary

9to5Mac reports GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol arriving together in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API, describing Astra as the demanding-work model and Sol as the lower-cost everyday choice. It attributes token-efficiency, coding and faster-generation claims to OpenAI.

Plan access and speed modes have eligibility and allowance tradeoffs. Lower unit prices do not guarantee a lower bill for every completed task, and a close score on one coding benchmark does not establish equal quality across projects.
