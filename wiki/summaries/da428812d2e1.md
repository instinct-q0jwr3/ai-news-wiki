# Oura agrees to acquire AI and on-demand doctor tool Counsel Health for $625M, and says Q4 revenue grew 153% YoY to $533M and FY 2026 revenue grew 92% to ~$1.75B

## Summary

Bloomberg reports via Techmeme that Oura agreed to acquire Counsel Health, an AI and on-demand doctor service, for $625 million, and said Q4 revenue grew 153% year over year to $533M with FY 2026 revenue up 92% to about $1.75B.

## Highlights

- Oura buys Counsel Health for $625M
- Q4 revenue $533M, up 153%; FY26 about $1.75B
