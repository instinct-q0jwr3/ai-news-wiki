# Meta's Interactive Simulator for Wearable AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `meta`

## Summary

Meta released InteractiveWorld, a simulator for wearable AI assistants: the assistant sees through a person's eyes and talks, the person decides and acts, and a stateful 3D world (Isaac Sim scenes with SMPL+H human models) responds with the next first-person view. 44 scenes and 36 human actions ship in the catalog.

InteractiveBench layers 120 frozen multi-turn tasks (6 categories x 20) on top, with one model playing the assistant and another playing the human, plus oracle videos, a scoring rubric and a one-command evaluation runner.

## Highlights

- InteractiveWorld: first-person simulator for wearable AI assistants
- 44 Isaac Sim scenes, 36 human actions, stateful worlds
- InteractiveBench: 120 frozen multi-turn tasks, model-vs-model
- Open repo with rubric, oracle videos and one-command evals
