# OpenAI GPT–6 Astra breaks Enigma message that has resisted solution since 2005

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

Crypto Cellar Research reports the break of the German Army Enigma message MVUEH of 10 July 1941, which had resisted all attempts since 2005. Carter Leffer submitted the break on 15 September 2026 for validation, and the key and plaintext checked out immediately: the recovered key even uses a different wheel order (253) than the other known keys from that day (512).

The page's author emphasizes how the break was achieved: GPT-6 Astra did it 'entirely on its own', with Leffer only directing the model to try the unbroken Enigma messages published on the site. The 82-letter plaintext is nearly identical to that of message Nr. 173 (SIPVX), broken in 2017 by Alex Shovkoplyas, differing because of an enciphering mistake (Bitte became Btte) and the sender's signature. The author, a self-described old cryptanalyst, says he spent weeks verifying the Bundesarchiv files the model cited and calls the result 'simply amazing'.
