# Nvidia tells its manufacturing partners to halt AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia, as the US steps up export controls

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `nvidia`

## Summary

The accessible Financial Times feed entry says sources report Nvidia telling manufacturing partners to halt AI-server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia as the United States tightens export controls aimed at Chinese access. It describes a reported company instruction to partners rather than providing the text of a rule.

The full article was unavailable in this pass, including on a clean version of the article URL. Which products, shipment dates, exceptions, legal provisions and company responses apply therefore remain unverified. The entry alone does not establish a worldwide server ban, that all shipments to those countries are illegal or that existing installations must stop operating. No regulatory or commercial action was taken.

## Highlights

- Source is an FT-attributed feed excerpt
- Four Southeast Asian destinations are named
- Instruction is attributed to unnamed sources
- Company action and legal requirements are distinct
- Affected product scope and exceptions remain unread
- No global ban or shutdown is established
