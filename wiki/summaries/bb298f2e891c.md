# Anthropic and London-based PE firm Hg agree to launch a consultancy by the end of 2026 to embed AI consultants and engineers into European businesses

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic and London-based private equity firm Hg have agreed to launch a consultancy by the end of 2026 that embeds AI consultants and engineers into European businesses, the Financial Times reports - a forward-deployed services arm advising companies on AI deployment, backed by Anthropic and the UK buyout group.

## Highlights

- Anthropic and PE firm Hg to launch an AI consultancy by end of 2026 (FT)
- Will embed AI consultants and engineers into European businesses
- A forward-deployed services arm for enterprise AI deployment
