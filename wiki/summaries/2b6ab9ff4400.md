# Qwen Intelligence Launches Three Mobile AI Agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `alibaba` `qwen` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Qwen launched Qwen Intelligence with three mobile agents it calls SOTA: a Mobile Planner Agent that plans, decomposes and orchestrates complex tasks (#1 on MobilePA-Bench, MobilePA-Bench Business and Memory); a Mobile-Use Agent that executes tasks API-first with GUI fallback (MobileWorld 82.1, MobileWorld-Real 92.2, AndroidDaily 97.2, 90% end-to-end success rate); and a Mobile Creative Agent that turns one sentence into ready-to-use creations, generating images in 3 seconds, about 2x faster than leading peers.

The team is also opening its benchmark suite - MobilePA-Bench, MobileWorld, MobileWorld-Real and MobileWorld-Safety - covering planning, cross-app execution, real-device performance and safety. Resources live at qwenintelligence.com, GitHub and arXiv.

## Highlights

- Qwen Intelligence launches with three mobile agents.
- Mobile Planner Agent: #1 on MobilePA-Bench and variants.
- Mobile-Use Agent: API-first with GUI fallback; 90% end-to-end success.
- Mobile Creative Agent: one sentence to creations; images in 3s.
- Open benchmark suite: MobilePA-Bench, MobileWorld, MobileWorld-Real, MobileWorld-Safety.
