# Apple HomeView review: fast home controls, helpful Siri AI, gorgeous customizable faces, great sound for its size, but Siri can be slow and pricey wall mount

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `apple`

## Summary

The Verge's HomeView review praises quick home controls, customizable photo-display functions and sound for the device's size. It finds Siri AI useful but sometimes slow, and argues that the product becomes less convincing when it tries to serve as a shared household phone rather than a focused smart display. These are one reviewer's practical judgments rather than universal performance guarantees.

## Highlights

- Fast smart-home controls earn praise
- Display customization and sound are strengths
- Siri latency remains a complaint
- Shared personal functions create friction
- Review separates core utility from broader ambitions
