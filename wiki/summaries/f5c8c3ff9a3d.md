## Summary

Simon Willison introduces an early Apache Arrow streaming-export plugin for Datasette. It exercises a recently added output-renderer hook and illustrates exporting incrementally instead of loading an entire result into memory.

The article distinguishes a screenshot from the development branch from the current release’s export grouping. AI-assisted implementation is the author’s account, not proof that review was unnecessary or that every export workload behaves correctly.
