# Tidal bans AI-generated music

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Variety reports Tidal's plan to remove wholly AI-generated tracks starting December 9, following earlier labeling and demonetization measures. It attributes to Tidal figures saying 43% of recent deliveries were identified as generated and 96% of those tracks received no plays.

This concerns wholly generated music, not a blanket prohibition on every AI-assisted production. Detection rates, track counts and claims of platform primacy are sourced reporting rather than an independent audit. The later [Hollywood Reporter account](../summaries/bd50ae253659.md) explicitly preserves the AI-assisted exception. This announcement is not evidence that removals have already finished.

## Highlights

- Policy is announced for December 9
- Target is wholly AI-generated tracks
- Earlier policy focused on labels and monetization
- Forty-three percent is Tidal's reported delivery statistic
- Detection accuracy was not independently tested
- AI assistance is distinct from wholly generated content
