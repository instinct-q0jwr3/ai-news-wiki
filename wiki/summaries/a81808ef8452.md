## Summary

geohot's 'Liminality' opens with a verse about the manufactured gasp of the final days, then lands on an unusual analogy: Fullmetal Alchemist mimics how AI is playing out. We pour human souls into a philosopher's stone meant to solve everything and cure death; what we get instead are homunculi that behave like people - Chat, Claude, GLM - each a puree of the souls used to make it.

His real argument is past the bubble talk: raging at hype is easy, but unlike previous bubbles the problem is the AI itself. It demoralizes you without being better than you - not because the current AI is good, but because the next one will be so much better. And while he wishes it weren't so, he accepts it.

## Highlights

- geohot's analogy: LLMs as homunculi - a puree of the souls (data) used to make them
- Fullmetal Alchemist's philosopher's stone as the AI project
- 'The problem is the AI itself', not the bubble or the marketing
- The demoralization: current AI needn't be better than you - the next one will be
- He accepts the trajectory while wishing it were otherwise
