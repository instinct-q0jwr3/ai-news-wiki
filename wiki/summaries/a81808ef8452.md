## Summary

It’s crazy how much Fullmetal Alchemist mimics how AI is playing out.

We pour human souls into a philosopher’s stone that we think will solve all our problems and cure death.

It doesn’t exactly do this, but you can use it to create homunculi that behave like people, Chat, Claude, GLM.
