# NADI, a new DARPA-like Dutch government agency, launches a €40M challenge to accelerate chip design with AI in collaboration with its German counterpart SPRIND

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Reuters' Toby Sterling reports that NADI, a new Dutch government agency set up in the style of DARPA to pursue strategic innovation, has launched a 40 million euro challenge to accelerate chip design with AI, in collaboration with its German counterpart SPRIND.

## Highlights

- NADI, a DARPA-like Dutch agency, launches a 40M euro AI chip-design challenge.
- The effort is run with Germany's SPRIND.
- Reported by Toby Sterling for Reuters.
