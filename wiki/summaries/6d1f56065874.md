## Summary

An interview with Ian Quigley of Leash Bio, which specializes in DNA-encoded libraries, a way to collect very high-throughput data on how proteins interact with small molecules. DEL data has a poor reputation for noise, but Leash says it extracts clean, reproducible data suited to large-scale modelling.

The host also points to Leash's past work, including a Kaggle competition showing protein-ligand binding is not solved, as background to the conversation on loops between lab and model.

## Highlights

- Leash Bio builds clean DEL datasets for training chemistry models.
- Interview covers the lab-in-the-loop approach.
