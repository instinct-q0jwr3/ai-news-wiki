# Crossing the Threshold: How OpenAI and Anthropic Approach Autonomy and Safety in Financial Agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Coverage of the AI Engineer New York conference centers on how to balance agent autonomy with safety. Presentations from a frontier lab and from financial firms reached a similar conclusion: safe autonomy depends on where controls and human judgment are placed, not on how many restrictions are piled on.

OpenAI's Joseph Kim talked about a threshold effect, and the article compares how OpenAI and Anthropic approach autonomy in finance settings.

## Highlights

- Safe agent autonomy depends on where controls sit, not how many there are.
- OpenAI and Anthropic approaches compared for financial use.
