# Product Managers aren't safe either

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

An essay argues that product managers should worry about AI at least as much as developers. The analogy: generative AI is to developers what Instagram was to professional photographers - 90% lost their moat to teenagers with phones, while the remaining 10% became more valuable because their talent could not be copied. 'Code spitters are cooked,' the author concedes, but architects are safer: reconciling needs with security, compliance, technical constraints and budget requires private data and interpersonal work that resists automation.

The PM-specific threat: when code is cheap, the developer prompting a feature has time left over to build product intuition - understanding the customer pain point and why a feature should exist at all. If the same person can code and reason about the product, the PM's separate role compresses.

- "When any PM can prompt a feature, what happens when a dev prompts one too?"
