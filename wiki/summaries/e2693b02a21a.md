## Summary

Cory Doctorow excoriates California AG Rob Bonta for settling with the Trump-aligned Ellison billionaires, clearing their takeover of Warner Bros and its merger with Paramount - leaving the Ellisons in control of Warners, Paramount and TikTok. Citing Matt Stoller's 'Happy Oligarch Day!' breakdown, Doctorow argues every 'concession' Bonta touted is meaningless: the commitments only matter if Bonta's office enforces them, and it never will.

He calls the settlement a surrender without a shot fired, handing the American movie and TV sector to 'two of the most odious creeps to draw breath.'

## Highlights

- Bonta's settlement clears the Ellison takeover of Warner Bros and merger with Paramount

- Doctorow calls the merger indefensible, illegal and dangerous
