# Het Nerdvote Effect

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high_

`bert-hubert` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Bert Hubert analyzes the Dutch 'NerdVote effect': Barbara Kathmann (GroenLinks-PvdA, list position 32) will be re-elected to parliament on preference votes with around 30,000 - triple her 2023 result, even as her party shrank 20%. The question is whether NerdVote.nl, the campaign Hubert helps run to elect candidates who think carefully about digital policy ('not TechVote - not the biggest fans of AI and internet'), deserves the credit, or the media's favored explanation, the Stem op een Vrouw (Vote for a Woman) initiative.

His reading of the numbers says NerdVote mattered more. Stem op een Vrouw's measurable boost on comparable female candidates was 2,000-3,500 votes, while Kathmann - who wasn't even visible in their advice without scrolling - gained 20,000. NerdVote logged 80,000+ real visitors, podcast appearances on nerd favorites and a Reddit AMA with 200,000 views. He is careful with the caveats: she won 10,000 preference votes on her own strength in 2023, has a broad network and a Rotterdam base (where her gain was oddly only 10%), and GroenLinks' own digital network campaigned hard. His conclusion: NerdVote couldn't boost just any candidate this way - but it could boost one with Kathmann's drive, network and background.

## Highlights

- Barbara Kathmann (GL-PvdA, #32) re-elected on ~30,000 preference votes - triple 2023, while her party shrank 20%
- NerdVote.nl's goal: elect MPs who think about what is wise in digital policy and can be effective in parliament - 'not TechVote'
- Context: nearly all talent on the Digital Affairs committee was leaving or in unelectable list positions
- Media credited Stem op een Vrouw, but its measured effect on comparable candidates was only ~2-3.5k votes
- Kathmann wasn't visible in that initiative's advice without scrolling, yet gained 20,000 votes
- NerdVote's footprint: 80k+ site visitors, 500 newsletter signups, popular nerd podcasts, a 200k-view Reddit AMA
- Hubert's honesty: she already won 10k on her own in 2023 and has a strong network - the campaign amplified a strong candidate
- 'Digital' contributions to election outcomes keep being under-credited, he argues - without diminishing Stem op een Vrouw
