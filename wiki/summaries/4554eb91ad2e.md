# Meta and Amazon are discussing a potential deal to connect Muse to Amazon, ending Amazon's block; any deal would require a sign-off from both CEOs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `amazon` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Fortune reports repeated talks between Amazon and Meta about connecting Muse to Amazon's store and potentially ending an earlier blockade. Unnamed people describe negotiations over search results, advertising and how access would work, against competing interests in shopping revenue and the customer relationship.

The report explicitly says discussions are fluid and an agreement may not happen; it is not evidence that access is already restored or that consumers can use a new integration today. Both companies declined comment, and expected CEO sign-off and future transaction fees are reported conditions or plans. Survey adoption and long-term spending projections supply context, not proof of this deal's outcome.

## Highlights

- Talks are attributed to unnamed sources
- Search quality and advertising are negotiation issues
- Access mechanics and timing are unresolved
- No completed agreement or restored access is reported
- Both companies declined comment
- Projected agentic spending is not current transaction volume
- No shopping connection or purchase was attempted
