## Summary

Keeping Large MoE Training Within Fixed GPU Memory.

Abstract: Training a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model at long context or large batch size fails as soon as any one component's peak allocation exceeds device memory, so the target is every peak at once, not the average footprint.
