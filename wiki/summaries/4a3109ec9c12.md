# MiMo-V2.6: Scaling Reinforcement Learning Towards Self-Improvement

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `xiaomi`

## Summary

Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6 report presents an omni-modal model family pushed forward by scaling reinforcement-learning compute: mid-training on a broad multimodal corpus first, then RL scaled along three dimensions - larger batches and throughput (1,568 samples and 2.7-3.7B tokens per step at up to 1M context, asynchronously), more diverse environments (code, general, visual, cyber) under a mixture of agent harnesses, and more grader compute via groupwise agentic grading that rewards shorter, token-efficient solutions on long-horizon tasks.

To keep training stable at scale, the team freezes the MoE router and builds a multi-layer defense against reward hacking, plus infrastructure for mixed-task agentic RL including a unified trajectory representation and high-concurrency multi-frame rollouts.
