## Highlights

- Ternary Bonsai 2 27B uses ternary {−1, 0, +1} weights with FP16 group-wise scaling, for 1.76 effective bits per weight and a total model footprint of 5.9GB .
- The low-bit representation is applied end to end across the language model.
- Against its full-precision counterpart, Ternary Bonsai 2 27B is more than 9x smaller while retaining 98.2% of aggregate benchmark performance .
- At this level of retention, compression becomes a deployment unlock: nearly the same capability, in a footprint that can run in far more places.
- Our first Bonsai 27B release was an important milestone, offering a practical way to run 27B-class intelligence on local devices.
