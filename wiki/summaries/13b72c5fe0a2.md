# Mumsnet denies using AI for content after prompt appears on message board

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Guardian reports that Mumsnet denied using AI to produce content after a detailed AI prompt appeared as a reply on one of its 'am I being unreasonable' posts. The prompt, titled AIBU Response Schema v.1.0, told a model to write a forum-style ruling with brief actionable advice, and defined forum abbreviations.

Users called it creepy and disappointing and rejected the site's explanations; one said they might as well do something else. The site, founded in 2000, had a 19% fall in monthly unique users in 2025 to 6.1 million. The article does not establish who posted the prompt.

## Highlights

- AI prompt appeared as a reply on a Mumsnet post
- Prompt asked for a forum-style ruling and advice
