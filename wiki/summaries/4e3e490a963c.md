# SpaceX plans to invest $50B in an Arizona data center campus and $24B in a Texas campus; in October, it said its AI business generated 50%+ of its revenue

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

The New York Times reports that SpaceX plans $74 billion in data-center campuses across Arizona and Texas: $50 billion in Cochise County and $24 billion near Starbase. The report describes the projects as proposed investment rather than completed construction.

The Arizona project still needs county approval, and the Texas project is described as generating its own power, with further approval needs unclear in the report. Announced spending and job counts are company plans; they do not establish final cost, completion or the absence of local constraints.

## Highlights

- Reported investment plan totals $74 billion
- Arizona and Texas have separate campuses
- Arizona approval is still needed
- Texas plan includes its own power generation
- Announced costs and jobs are not completed outcomes
