# Introducing &Tutor

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Humans& has released Ampersand Tutor (&Tutor), a model trained to teach, available via API - and the training recipe is the interesting part: &Tutor learns by teaching against Persimmon, the company's large-scale user model, which plays the learner. It claims state of the art on TutorMoments, a benchmark built from real tutoring moments, and the company's thesis is on display: models understand people better by learning from long human interactions, and teaching is the sharpest case - you have to model what the learner knows and build on it.

The sample dialogue shows the target behavior: not answering, but eliciting - checking the student's conjecture (n = 10), celebrating the realization, then steering back to the original question with a hint about why it matters (cubes grow fast, and 10 sits between 8 and 27). A tutor model trained against a simulated learner is also a quiet milestone for synthetic-interaction training: the student in the loop is itself a model.
