## Summary

The public Harvard CS 2881R page describes a graduate AI-safety course taught by Boaz Barak that joins technical alignment questions with societal effects. It expects mathematical and machine-learning foundations plus Python experience, and combines lectures, readings, student experiments, notes and a final project. The listed experiments span reasoning, cyberattack detection, evaluation awareness and other safety topics, offering a map of questions being taught rather than a set of established research findings.

The page encourages use of generative and agentic AI in coursework and discloses Barak's concurrent OpenAI role as a potential conflict. It describes an in-person course with mandatory attendance, not an open-enrollment online credential. Plans to publish recordings are conditional on technical feasibility and speakers' preferences. The schedule and admission details are source-page information, not a checked offer of current availability, and no enrollment or contact was initiated here.
