# Hackers abuse Google Ads, Bing redirects to push Claude ClickFix attacks

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-11 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic` `google` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

BleepingComputer reports that hackers are abusing legitimate Bing search-result redirects as click URLs in Google search ads to send users to fake Claude installers delivering ClickFix attacks. Push Security researchers dubbed the technique 'Adception': using Bing's trusted domain as the ad destination appears designed to slip past advertising security checks before victims are bounced through a compromised site to the malicious download page.

The campaign surfaced after researchers spotted a malicious Google ad targeting searches for 'claude mac'. The attack layers cloaking so security scanners and direct visitors never see the payload, a more evasive pattern than typical malvertising.

## Highlights

- Fake Claude installers pushed through Google ads
- Bing redirects abused as trusted click URLs ('Adception')
- Victims bounced through compromised sites to the payload
- Campaign found via ads targeting 'claude mac' searches
- Layered cloaking hides the payload from scanners
