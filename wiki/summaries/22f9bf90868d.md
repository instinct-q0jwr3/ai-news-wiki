# Data Processing Incidents on Inference Providers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-22 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

OpenRouter's 22 November announcement describes its response to researchers' findings that some inference providers failed to uphold data policies or had data compromised. The company says it off-boarded three providers, removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility, including precautionary removals, and notified potentially affected customers. It lists statements from Atlas Cloud, Novita, Phala and Together, while noting that not all affected providers have posted publicly. It says work continues on recurring checks, verification of forwarding paths and validation before removed endpoints can regain ZDR eligibility.

This is the routing layer's own account of its response to the Dragos canary investigation already covered from Dragos and Ars Technica, not a new independent experiment. OpenRouter says the findings did not show a breach or unauthorized access of OpenRouter itself. Exposure through an inference provider and compromise of the router's systems are different claims. The reported removals, notifications and safeguards were not independently audited here; precautionary removal also does not establish that every affected endpoint leaked data.
