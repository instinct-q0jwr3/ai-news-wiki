# AI can clone your indie game, but not its soul

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-04 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Robert Varadan's post argues that building in public is not dead and that agentic AI can now handle every part of making a video game. He points to posts where models produced whole clones, such as a World of Warcraft or Minecraft look-alike, from a few reference images and a short text prompt. Many are slop, he says, but this is the worst these models will be and they could not do it six months ago.

He then turns to the backlash against one account that asks top models, including Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, to clone indie game demos and posts the videos as a benchmark. Detractors call it theft. Varadan avoids the ethics but says he understands why indie developers and gamers are upset. Only the first part of the post was read.

## Highlights

- Agents can now generate whole game clones from short prompts.
- The quality is often poor but improving fast.
- One account's clone-the-indie-demo benchmark draws theft accusations.
- Basis: opening of a long post.
