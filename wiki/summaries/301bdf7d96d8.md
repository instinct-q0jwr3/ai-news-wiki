# An analysis of the tech job market in late 2026: product managers seem to be benefiting most in the AI era, demand for engineers is plateauing, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Lenny Rachitsky's late-2026 tech job market report, his second of the year, finds product managers benefiting most in the AI era while engineering demand stalls and design hits a record low. AI skills are now needed across roles, jobs concentrate in the Bay Area, remote work is at a record low, and the market is growing fast but mostly outside software. Based on two proprietary datasets.

## Highlights

- PMs winning; engineering stalled; design at a record low
- AI skills now expected in every role
- Bay Area concentration up, remote at record low
- Growth is mostly outside software
- Two proprietary datasets, paid newsletter
