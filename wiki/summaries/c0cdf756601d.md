# AI Model Groupthink

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Magic Numbers built a small web tool that asks a panel of 12 AI models for their top three answers to any ranking question and scores each model against the consensus of the other eleven. Full credit goes to the right item in the right place, half credit to the right item in the wrong place.

The author found Chinese models the most conformist: DeepSeek (0.59), Kimi K2, Tencent's Hy3 and GLM take the top four spots, and the five Chinese models average 0.47 against 0.38 for six American models and 0.36 for Mistral. Qwen is the exception at 0.38. The most contrarian were Meta's Llama 4 Scout (0.34) and Anthropic's Claude Haiku (0.32). He cautions that consensus is not quality, and outliers can be more interesting than correct.

## Highlights

- 12 models asked for top-three picks on any question
- Each scored against the other eleven's consensus
- DeepSeek the most conformist at 0.59
- Chinese models average 0.47, American 0.38, Mistral 0.36
- Most contrarian: Llama 4 Scout and Claude Haiku
- Agreement is not the same as being right
