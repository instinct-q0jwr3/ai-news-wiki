# Can AI automate Epoch?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Epoch AI tested whether frontier models can automate its own research work. It gave six models real tasks taken from its daily work, with context and the highest reasoning settings, and no human help.

The result is mixed: models such as Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra are reliable on well-defined tasks but fail on the open-ended parts that block full automation. Epoch argues that mixing benchmark-style rigor with the impressions from real work exposes gaps that benchmarks miss, and notes that models now use large token budgets better, so fixed-effort tests understate recent progress.

## Highlights

- Six models given real Epoch research tasks
- Reliable on well-defined work
- Fail on open-ended parts of the job
- Real-work tests expose gaps benchmarks miss
- Fixed token budgets understate recent progress
