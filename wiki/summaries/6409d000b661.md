## Summary

Cory Doctorow: beyond the bubble, the emissions and the wage erosion, AI is an epistemic disaster. Photoshopped images, impersonators and VFX existed for years, but deepfakes democratized reality-bending images, sound and video - and the result is not just that fakes seem real.

It's the corrosive second-order effect: because we know some of what we see is fake, we can no longer be sure that any of it isn't. Politicians, battlefields and influencers all arrive equally plausible, and the shared floor of what counts as evidence gives way - the actual epistemic crisis.

## Highlights

- Doctorow: AI is an epistemic disaster on top of everything else
- Deepfakes democratized reality-bending media that used to need specialists
- The deeper damage: nothing can be presumed real anymore
- Plausibility is now uniform across the true and the fabricated
