## Summary

Basis: feed metadata only - Daring Fireball quoting Mark Gurman's Bloomberg Power On column; the Bloomberg source fetch failed. Gurman reports that finished units of Apple's HomeView home hub have been sitting boxed in warehouses 'for the better part of a year' while the software lagged. The holdup was the new assistant: without a serviceable version of the revamped Siri, Apple could not deliver the experience it envisioned, so the hardware was ready but the software was not. Apple postponed HomeView repeatedly - spring 2025, then summer, then fall, then through 2026. The story underlines how Apple's AI software delays, not hardware, are dictating its smart-home roadmap.
