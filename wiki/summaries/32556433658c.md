## Summary

Stanford Hazy Research reports that a fitted rotation and global scale can transfer factual-recall hidden states between frozen base models. Its post describes twelve models, twenty pairs and subject-disjoint training, validation and test facts, with layer choice selected on validation data.

Evaluation uses facts both models already recall, so it is not unrestricted knowledge transfer or proof of a universal representation for all reasoning. Reported chained-map and compression percentages are relative transfer measures, not general question-answer accuracy. The dark-memory examples are proof-of-concept recoveries, and neither plots nor experiments were independently reproduced here. No model weights or states were accessed.

## Highlights

- Maps are fit on fewer than five thousand shared facts
- Layer selection uses validation rather than test results
- Recall experiments span twelve base models
- Selection requires both models to know evaluated facts
- Relative transfer does not imply all-task interchangeability
- Shared-geometry interpretation remains scoped evidence
- No experiment or model download occurred
