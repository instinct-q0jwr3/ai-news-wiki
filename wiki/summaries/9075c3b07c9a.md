# Indie Game Development with AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Sandro Maglione built two full games with AI without writing or reading code, working only through chat, a playtest link and a preview page for 3D models. His conclusion is that AI made writing a game faster but not easy: the hard part moved from implementation to choices only the developer can make, such as what game, what style, what is fun and what is broken.

Any step the agent cannot do becomes the developer's full-time job, so genre and style choices decide where your time goes. Detailed anime characters failed every time after two days of attempts, while simple stylized models worked. He built a TypeScript ECS runtime, Ritmo, to collect automated checks, and found three quarters of one game's commits came after it was already playable, with math-based tests catching economy holes. He predicts AI-made games will ship faster and sell better.

## Highlights
