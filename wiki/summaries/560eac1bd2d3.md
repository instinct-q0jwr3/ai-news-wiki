# AI and Irontown

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high_

`armin-ronacher` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Armin Ronacher uses Princess Mononoke as a lens on AI. Lady Eboshi's Irontown gives outcasts work and dignity yet its mine and weapons destroy the forest, and the film takes no side. He sees the same in AI: it empowers people previously shut out, but has externalities for others, and the right stance is to understand it rather than pick a camp.

## Highlights

- Irontown: real good and real harm together
- AI empowers outsiders and also imposes costs
- Argues for understanding over taking sides
