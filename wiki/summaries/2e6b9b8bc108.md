# Ravical, whose AI-powered platform helps accounting and tax firms manage their operations, raised a $17M seed led by MMC, bringing its total funding to $25M

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

Ravical, a 2025-founded startup building an AI platform for accounting and tax firms, has raised a $17M seed round led by MMC Ventures with Lakestar, Pitchdrive and Shapers participating, taking its total funding to $25M, tech.eu reports. The product targets a structural problem: when every accountant uses their own assistant their own way, the individual improves but the firm does not - so Ravical turns how a firm's best people work into shared, firm-wide processes that AI then carries out across every client.

The timing argument is pressure from both sides: firms struggle to hire and retain skilled professionals while clients change habits - Ravical's own research says 70% of UK SMEs now act on AI-generated advice without consulting their accountants. The new funds go to product development and international expansion.
