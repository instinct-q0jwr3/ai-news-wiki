## Summary

Human.md is a personal manifesto about human judgment, deliberate rule-breaking, iteration and responsibility in AI-assisted creative work. It argues that accepting the first generated result is not the same as active authorship.

The text’s claims about what models cannot do are rhetorical positions, not measured capability limits. Its advice describes the author’s creative philosophy rather than an authoritative workflow or instructions to change any reader’s tools.

## Highlights

- Essay values intentional creative choices
- Iteration is central to its argument
- Responsibility stays with the person publishing
- Model limitations are asserted rather than tested
- Manifesto is opinion, not an evaluation
