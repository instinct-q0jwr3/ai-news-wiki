# Anthropic raises an additional $12.2B as underwriters exercise their "greenshoe" overallotment of shares, bringing the total raised in its IPO to $93.5B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-21 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

CNBC reports that Anthropic’s underwriters exercised the overallotment option, increasing the reported public offering total. It separately identifies a concurrent private placement and a larger combined gross-proceeds figure.

Those totals describe different scopes, and gross proceeds are not net funds after expenses or necessarily proceeds retained by the company alone. The reported exercise is newer than earlier accounts describing only an option; the underlying release was not independently inspected here.

## Highlights

- Report says the option was exercised
- Public offering and private placement are separate
- Combined gross total has broader scope
- Gross is not net of expenses
- Earlier optional terms are superseded in this account
- Primary release was not independently checked
