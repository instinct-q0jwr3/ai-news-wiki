# FAA tees up $875M AI tool to help manage air traffic congestion

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

This September Ars Technica report describes the FAA’s initial plan to use SMART to advise air-traffic flow management around Washington before a wider rollout. It explains that the system combines operational data to propose alternative routes through existing processes rather than directly taking control of aircraft.

The $875 million figure covers a twelve-year contract that also includes a broader flow-management replacement, not simply the cost of one AI model. The report raises questions about model transparency and rollout scope; the newer November expansion is a separate development.

## Highlights

- September article covers the initial deployment plan
- SMART provides operational recommendations
- Humans retain air-traffic decisions
- Contract includes more than the AI advisory layer
- Later nationwide expansion needs its own account
