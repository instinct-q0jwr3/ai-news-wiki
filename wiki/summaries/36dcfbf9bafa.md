## Summary

Basis: the Launch HN post text from the feed. Laminar (YC S24) has been building open-source observability for AI agents since 2024. Alongside the launch they released flow-1, a model trained with reinforcement learning to find errors in agent traces; on Laminar's own benchmark it reportedly matches GPT-6-sol at the task. The pitch: as agents run longer and more autonomously, tracing and automatically detecting where runs go wrong becomes the bottleneck, and a dedicated small model makes review scalable.
