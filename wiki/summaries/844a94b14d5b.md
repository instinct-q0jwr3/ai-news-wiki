# I'm not paying $20 for ChatGPT or Claude because a free local LLM does

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `anthropic` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

An XDA Developers writer says he stopped paying $20 a month each for ChatGPT and Claude because a free local model, Qwen run through llama.cpp on a Windows 11 PC, covers what he needs. He had believed no single model could replace the strengths of both cloud services.

The article argues from personal use: for everyday drafting and menial tasks the local model is enough, costs nothing per month and keeps data on his machine. It is an opinion piece from one user's workflow and does not benchmark the model against the cloud services.

## Highlights

- Writer replaces two $20 subscriptions with a local Qwen model
- Runs through llama.cpp on a Windows 11 PC
- Covers his daily drafting and routine tasks
- Claim rests on personal experience, not benchmarks
- Savings come with hardware and setup costs
