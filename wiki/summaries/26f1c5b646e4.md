## Summary

Construct22 presents a playful prompt that makes a chatbot invent a group conversation in which models comment on their user. The page labels one example fictional and presents another as a user-submitted, translated and partly anonymized generated response. Its entertainment value comes from recombining personal details into a roast.

This is generated roleplay, not evidence of an actual hidden group, private model opinions or communication between providers. Permission and anonymization of the submitted example are publisher claims, not independently verified provenance. The invitation to run and share a personalized version was read as article content only: no private history was retrieved, no prompt was run and no personal response was posted.
