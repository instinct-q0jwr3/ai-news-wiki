# VC isn’t VC anymore — understanding the rise of Cancer Capital

## Summary

Anil Dash argues that what is called 'venture capital' today isn't venture capital anymore. The popular picture - an investor with a fund cutting checks to help founders build companies - has been replaced by a handful of billionaire extremists using the cover of VC to advance an agenda where they're accountable to no one.

He writes from the inside, and says so explicitly: he has raised tens of millions as a startup CEO and been involved, as board member or advisor, in raising hundreds of millions more; he has sat across the table from the people he's describing. This isn't sour grapes from someone who didn't get a check - it's a first-hand account of how the industry's center of gravity moved. The piece is the foundation essay for his 'Cancer Capital' series.
