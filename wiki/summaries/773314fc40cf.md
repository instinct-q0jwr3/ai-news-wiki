## Summary

Chinese optical transceiver maker Ligent Technologies jumped 19% in its Hong Kong debut, giving it a market cap of about $4.6 billion after raising roughly $727 million, Bloomberg reports. It is the latest AI-linked IPO on the Hong Kong exchange.

## Highlights

- Ligent rose 19% in its Hong Kong debut.
- The IPO raised ~$727M at a ~$4.6B market cap.
- It is the latest Hong Kong listing riding AI infrastructure demand.
