# Anthropic just launched tools to show companies what their AI spending is buying

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `anthropic`

## Summary

TechCrunch reports the financials Anthropic shared with investors this week: an operating profit in Q2 and 900 percent net revenue retention - the same customers spent nine times more in Q3 2026 than in Q3 2025, with Claude Code the engine. To keep enterprise buyers confident, Anthropic launched a suite of reporting tools that use internal analytics and employee surveys to show managers what a team produced over a date range, what it cost in tokens, and where workflows broke down, plus recommendations for improving results. Anthropic says it will not train on this data by default, and it is adding pooled group budgets for managing token spend.

The context is an ROI reckoning: most of the Fortune 500 are not yet seeing major gains from AI deployments, while venture-backed firms drive token spend. A Jellyfish study of 276,000 developers using agentic coding tools found Anthropic-model tools responsible for nearly a third of all proposed code changes, with spending on them up 10x since the start of the year to about 2 percent of total engineering cost - growth, Jellyfish's chief scientist notes, that most CFOs did not plan for.

## Highlights

- Anthropic posted a Q2 operating profit; 900% net revenue retention year over year
- New reporting shows teams' output, token cost and workflow breakdowns, with recommendations
- No training on the analytics data by default; pooled group budgets added
- Jellyfish: Anthropic-model tools drive nearly a third of proposed code changes across 276k developers
- Agentic coding spend up 10x this year to ~2% of total engineering cost
- Most of the Fortune 500 still not seeing major gains from AI deployments
