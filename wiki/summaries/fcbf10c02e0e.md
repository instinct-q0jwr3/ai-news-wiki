# When Do We Need On-Policy Distillation? Distilling on Offline Student Rollouts Is Often Better

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

An NVIDIA research paper asks when on-policy distillation, which trains a student on its own generated rollouts, is actually needed, and finds that distilling on offline student rollouts is often better. Basis: the title and the opening of the abstract.

## Highlights

- Questions the assumption that on-policy distillation is needed.
- Finds offline student rollouts often work better.
