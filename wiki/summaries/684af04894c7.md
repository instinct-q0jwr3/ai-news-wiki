# Disney and AWS partner to develop AI-powered audio-animatronics of three Star Wars characters, debuting later in November at Amazon re:Invent in Las Vegas

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `amazon`

## Summary

The Hollywood Reporter describes Disney and AWS developing interactive Anzellan audio-animatronics for a planned public demonstration. Cameras, microphones and AI help choose responses to visitors and other characters.

The report says Imagineers authored the available words and movements, so the example is constrained selection rather than unrestricted generated dialogue. A demonstration plan does not establish a theme-park rollout or independently verified reliability in open public settings.

## Highlights

- Characters are planned for a public demonstration
- Sensors inform response selection
- Words and movements remain authored by Imagineers
- AI does not imply unrestricted dialogue
- Wider deployment and reliability were not verified
