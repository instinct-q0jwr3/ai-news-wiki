# Classification of Finite Simple Groups Formalized in Lean

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Classification of Finite Simple Groups - one of mathematics' largest proofs - is being formalized in Lean, and the project has already produced new mathematics: while formalizing the first-generation proof, the team found counterexamples to a closure assertion used in a published route to the Unbalanced Group Theorem.

The 35-page paper explains the obstruction and gives an alternative argument for the simple-group case, built on Solomon's 1976 theory of maximal 2-components, with the preservation of simple tops as the key step.
