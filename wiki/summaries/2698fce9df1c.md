## Summary

Tamserve presents a shared serving engine for SAM 2-family video trackers, batching work across live camera feeds instead of loading a model copy per camera. Its application supplies frames and initial objects; optional text-based discovery adds another route to selecting targets.

The repository reports throughput and memory comparisons on specified GPU and model configurations. These are author-run results, not independent guarantees across camera counts, resolutions and object densities. Serving a tracker more efficiently does not eliminate occlusion failures, incorrect detections or the consent and privacy obligations of a real deployment.

## Highlights

- One model serves several streams
- Application owns video input and timing
- Pointing or text can initialize tracking
- Benchmark hardware and settings matter
- Tracking accuracy and deployment privacy remain separate
