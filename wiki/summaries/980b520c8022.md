# SpaceX revenue soars 161% as AI business grows

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

ABC News covers SpaceX's second-ever quarterly earnings report since going public in June: revenue grew 161% year over year for the three months ending in September, driven largely by the AI business and Starlink, and the company swung to a $4.40 billion profit from a $1.05 billion loss a year earlier, and from a loss in the prior quarter. Shares jumped nearly 4% after hours to about $153.30, valuing the company near $1.95 trillion. The connectivity segment, which includes Starlink, brought in $5.2 billion, up 69%, and investors are watching Starlink's acquisition of nationwide low-band spectrum as a sign it will compete with US mobile carriers. The first earnings release in August had shown $7.8 billion in quarterly revenue, up from $4 billion a year earlier, but costs outran it.

## Highlights

- SpaceX Q3 revenue up 161% YoY, driven by AI and Starlink
- Profit of $4.40B vs a $1.05B loss a year earlier; first profitable quarter
- Shares up ~4% after hours to ~$153.30; valuation near $1.95T
- Connectivity segment (incl. Starlink): $5.2B, up 69%
- Starlink's nationwide low-band spectrum buy signals carrier competition
- Second earnings report since the June IPO; August showed $7.8B revenue but no profit
