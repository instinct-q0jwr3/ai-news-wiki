# Meta unveils mobile app Horizon Create and web app Horizon Studio for building games with AI prompts; the games will run on Facebook, Instagram, and Horizon

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta`

## Summary

Meta announced two new tools for building games on its Horizon social platform with AI prompts: Horizon Create, a mobile app, and Horizon Studio, a browser app with more granular controls. Both are heading to early access with a waitlist.

The bigger play is distribution: published games made with these tools will be recommended on Facebook and Instagram, where they will also be playable - someone scrolling Instagram can tap a clip and jump in. Horizon has struggled as a platform, and Meta is betting AI-generated games plus its social reach can change that.

## Highlights

- Horizon Create (mobile) and Horizon Studio (browser) build games from AI prompts.
- Both enter early access via waitlist.
- Published games will be recommended and playable on Facebook and Instagram.
- Horizon has struggled; Meta bets AI creation plus social distribution can fix it.
