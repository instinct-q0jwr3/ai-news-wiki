# News: Microsoft Disclosures Suggest OpenAI Sales Account For Around 70% Of FY26 AI Revenue, More Than 7% of FY26 Revenue

## Summary

Ed Zitron on new Microsoft disclosures: for fiscal year 2026, Microsoft recorded $24.1 billion of revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI (including revenue-sharing payments), with $6.0 billion of accounts receivable from OpenAI at June 30, 2026.

Why it matters: Bloomberg's analysis - assuming Microsoft's AI run rate kept growing at the 123% reported in March - puts Microsoft's AI business at roughly $34 billion for the fiscal year. Set the two numbers side by side and OpenAI appears to account for more than half, and likely about 70%, of Microsoft's AI revenue. Combined with analyst estimates that OpenAI and Anthropic make up over 70% of all AI revenue across Microsoft, Google and Amazon, the AI revenue story starts to look like a two-customer story.

## Highlights

- Microsoft FY2026: $24.1B revenue from OpenAI arrangements, $6.0B receivable
- Bloomberg's estimate: Microsoft's total AI run rate ~$34B
- Implication: OpenAI is more than half, likely ~70%, of Microsoft's AI revenue
- Analysts: OpenAI + Anthropic are 70%+ of AI revenue across MSFT, Google and Amazon
- Zitron's framing: the AI revenue boom may be a two-customer story
