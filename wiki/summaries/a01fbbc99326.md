# Black high school students sue after AI mistakes Doritos bag for gun

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Lawyers’ Committee announces a lawsuit on behalf of students detained after a weapon-detection system allegedly misidentified a snack bag. Its account attributes the escalation to both a false alert and failures in human review and communication.

The source represents the plaintiffs and supplies their allegations, not a final court finding. The announcement does not independently establish each defendant’s liability, the system’s overall error rate or all facts of the incident.

## Highlights

- Source is plaintiffs’ legal representative
- Case alleges both technical and human failures
- Detention followed an alleged false alert
- Liability has not been adjudicated here
- Single case does not establish overall error rate
