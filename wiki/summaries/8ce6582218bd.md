# AI retail trading agent startup Catalyst raised a $30M seed led by Sequoia and says a pilot generated hundreds of millions in trading volume over two weeks

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Catalyst, a retail trading agent startup founded in 2025 by Justin Zheng (25) and Dylan Iskandar (21), raised a $30 million seed led by Sequoia, with Jump Trading, PeakXV, Lux Capital, AntiFund, Coinbase and Premji Invest participating. The founders met as teenagers playing poker at a Tyler Cowen conference; Zheng built a Village Global-backed biometrics startup in high school and joined Worldcoin in 2019, while Iskandar published cybersecurity papers for the US Department of Defense as a teen.

The product turns natural-language intent into trading strategy and executes it, with a human in the loop to accept - 'people will simply state an intent, and we have our agents to figure out everything end to end'. Catalyst says a pilot generated hundreds of millions in trading volume over two weeks. Sequoia's George Robson frames the agents in superhero terms: tireless, quantitative guardians of a retail investor's capital in a financial world too complex for any single human.
