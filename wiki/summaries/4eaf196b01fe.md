## Summary

Data-security startup Cyera raised $400 million from Goldman Sachs in a Series G extension, the Wall Street Journal reports, bringing its total funding since June 2025 to $1.94 billion. The raise comes as Wall Street anxiety about AI drives an investor surge into security.

## Highlights

- Cyera raised $400M from Goldman Sachs in a Series G extension.
- Total funding since June 2025 reaches $1.94B.
- Investor interest is tied to Wall Street's AI anxiety.
