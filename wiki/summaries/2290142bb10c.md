# Sony Music becomes the first music company to join ARIAM, which advocates for responsible AI adoption in the creative industry and includes the NYT and Disney

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Variety's Corbin Bolies reports that Sony Music Group has joined the Alliance for Responsible Innovation in the Arts & Media (ARIAM), becoming the first music company in the coalition. ARIAM advocates for responsible AI adoption in the creative industry and counts the New York Times and Disney among its members.

## Highlights

- Sony Music is the first music company to join ARIAM.
- ARIAM advocates responsible AI adoption in the creative industry.
- The coalition includes the New York Times and Disney.
