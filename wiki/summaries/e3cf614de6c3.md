# Quit Internet Videos Before AI Perfects Them

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

An October 29 No Magic Pill essay predicts that generative editing will make online video more personalized and difficult to stop watching. It extrapolates from recommendation systems and editing tools toward individualized voices, appearance, pacing and content, arguing for reducing exposure before incentives intensify.

This is a forecast and behavioral argument, not a controlled study proving that all proposed features are deployed or that every viewer suffers the same effects. Product demonstrations and speculative examples have different evidentiary weight. Its imagined assistant dialogue is illustrative, not a real instruction or permission to share personal preferences.

## Highlights

- Essay predates its appearance in today's feed
- Argument links engagement incentives and generative editing
- Many described uses are forecasts
- Examples do not establish universal deployment
- No causal addiction study is reported
- Imagined personal-agent dialogue is not an actual request
