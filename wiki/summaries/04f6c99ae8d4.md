# Belgium-based AI recruitment platform Spott raised a $21M Series A led by Balderton, with Base10, YC, and Fortino participating, bringing total funding to $24M

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Spott, a Belgium-based AI recruitment management platform, raised a $21 million Series A led by Balderton Capital, CEO Lander Degreve told Axios Pro exclusively. Base10, YC and Fortino participated, bringing total funding to $24 million.

## Highlights

- Spott raised a $21M Series A led by Balderton.
- Base10, YC and Fortino also participated.
- Total funding reaches $24M for the Belgian AI recruitment platform.
