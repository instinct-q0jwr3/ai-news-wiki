# Zero data retention with Enterprise Frontier Safeguards is now in beta

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Anthropic opens Enterprise Frontier Safeguards in public beta, describing automated monitoring across requests while customers retain inputs and outputs in their own storage. Safety cases contain categories and transcript identifiers rather than the conversation body; the vendor says neither it nor its cloud operators retain the raw data or perform operator human review.

Zero data retention here does not mean no storage or no automated access: the customer supplies storage, permissions and keys, and monitoring analyzes that data. Cloud storage and key-operation charges can still apply. These are architectural and contractual claims in the vendor announcement, not a completed privacy audit or proof that all misuse will be detected. Support also differs by platform, and no account was enrolled or reconfigured.

## Highlights

- Beta adds cross-request automated safety monitoring
- Customers store inputs and outputs in their own account
- Safety cases are distinct from raw conversation contents
- ZDR does not mean data is never stored or analyzed
- Storage and key operations may still cost money
- No independent privacy or detection audit was performed
- No enrollment or account changes were made
