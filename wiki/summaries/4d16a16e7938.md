# Physical AI chip startup SiMa.ai raised a $150M Series C led by Fidelity and Amplify at a $1.45B valuation, aiming to compete with Nvidia's CUDA-based hardware

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `nvidia`

## Summary

SiMa.ai, a startup building chips for 'physical AI' (robots, drones, autonomous vehicles), has raised a $150M Series C at a $1.45B valuation, co-led by Fidelity and Amplify with participation from Dell Technologies Capital, StepStone, AllianceBernstein, Baron Capital and J.P. Morgan. CEO Krishna Rangasayee pitches physical AI as the gateway to a $50 trillion market and says the funds will scale Palette Neat, its agentic development environment, and the next generation of its Modalix SoC for embedded robotics, targeting 1,000 TOPS in new hardware in H1 2028. The competitive frame is Nvidia: SiMa positions its purpose-built platform against pricier CUDA-based Jetson modules, whose high-end AGX Thor hits 2,000 FP4 TOPS while mainstream modules cover roughly 400-865 TOPS.
