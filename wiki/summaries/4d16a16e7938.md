## Highlights

- SiMa.ai raised $150M Series C at a $1.45B valuation, co-led by Fidelity and Amplify
- Physical AI covers robots, drones, and autonomous vehicles that sense and act in the real world
- CEO Rangasayee calls it the gateway to a $50 trillion market
- Funds scale Palette Neat (agentic dev environment) and the next-gen Modalix SoC
- Targeting 1,000 TOPS in new hardware in the first half of 2028
- Positions against Nvidia's Jetson line: 2,000 FP4 TOPS at the high end, 400-865 TOPS mainstream
