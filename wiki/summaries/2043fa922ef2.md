# Court denies motion to dismiss DRAM price fixing case

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-15 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Litnews reports that a federal judge allowed a proposed DRAM price-fixing class action against Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron to proceed. The account describes allegations of coordinated conventional-memory supply cuts, shifts toward HBM and other conduct that the court found sufficient to make an agreement plausible at the pleading stage.

Denial of dismissal is not a finding that a conspiracy occurred. The reported market shares and price increases come from the complaint, and the court was assessing legal sufficiency rather than resolving disputed evidence or awarding damages.

## Highlights

- Motion to dismiss was reportedly denied
- Case concerns conventional DRAM supply
- Shift toward HBM forms part of the allegations
- Pleading plausibility is not proof
- Complaint figures are not adjudicated findings
