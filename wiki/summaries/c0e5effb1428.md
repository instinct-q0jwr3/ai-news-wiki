# Ben Affleck is an AI nerd

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Ben Affleck, it turns out, fine-tunes open video models: he describes unfreezing weights and training only the last cinematic layer so a film crew can hit real production standards. The context: Affleck founded InterPositive in 2022, a 16-person AI shop for film post-production, which Netflix bought in March 2026 for $587 million in cash.

His reasoning was that public video models were trained on his peers' films - not a real business, in his view - so InterPositive raised money, spent eight months shooting its own dataset on a controlled stage, and used it only for late-stage training. Each new production then trains a private model on its own dailies, keeping both the footage and the learning in-house. That product is what Netflix paid for.
