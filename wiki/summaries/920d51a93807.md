## Summary

Simon Willison links to historian Mark Humphries' account of GPT-6.2 Astra identifying a possible creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook. The account says the model connected a diary entry with a nearby dated visit and proposed checking a paper journal, which Humphries reports confirmed the hypothesis.

This is a secondary link post describing a particular case, not independent inspection of the archive or a benchmark of historical reasoning. The model's twenty-two-minute lead-finding phase differs from later human access to the paper source. The interpretation and significance remain attributed, and one successful attribution does not settle the future role of historians.
