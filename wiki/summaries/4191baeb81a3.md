## Summary

AINews frames November 11 as a convergence around decision models: Google launches Gemini Decision 1, while conference demonstrations show classifiers taking over narrow routing and extraction tasks from larger reasoning models. The newsletter contrasts Google's favorable launch benchmarks with a more mixed independent evaluation, where task cost, latency and document-level accuracy differ across competitors.

The architectural lesson is to assign fast bounded choices to a classifier and reserve a generative model for work that needs it. A Cloudflare demonstration reportedly reduced six LLM calls to one, but that result belongs to its workload rather than all agent designs. The edition also surveys frontier-model evaluations; scores and practitioner impressions are task-specific, not a universal ordering.

## Highlights

- Decision models return bounded choices rather than prose
- Google launch results differ from independent comparisons
- Per-task cost is distinct from input-token price
- Cloudflare demo combines classifiers with a reasoning model
- Reported call and token savings are workload-specific
- Benchmark rankings do not settle every use case
