# AI isn't replacing your job. It's turning it into low-paid gig work

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-19 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Rest of World essay argues that AI data work extends platform labor’s insecurity into credentialed professions. The examined passages connect interviews with Venezuelan workers, reported project shutdowns and platform payment terms to newer recruiting for physicians and other specialists.

Advertised maximum rates are not typical realized earnings, and different language tasks are not automatically identical jobs. The essay’s broader labor argument is not a measured prediction that every professional occupation becomes gig work. This summary covers the examined opening and terms discussion.

## Highlights

- Essay draws on worker interviews
- Advertised rates differ from realized pay
- Project continuity is a central concern
- Platform terms give the buyer substantial control
- Different tasks complicate rate comparisons
- No occupation-wide forecast is proven
