# OpenAI launches two ChatGPT shopping features: a virtual try-on tool for clothing and accessories, and a Favorites feature to save products to a user's Library

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai`

## Summary

OpenAI launched two ChatGPT shopping features worldwide: a virtual try-on for clothes and accessories, and a Favorites list that saves products to a Library. Try On appears in shopping results; users upload a selfie or full-body photo, or a screenshot of an item, and see it on themselves. Both use the new ChatGPT Images 2.5 model.

TechCrunch places it in OpenAI's repeated shopping experiments: its earlier instant checkout did not perform well. It can also build outfits from a described style or find items from celebrity photos, which moves ChatGPT toward Pinterest and Google territory for fashion discovery.

## Highlights

- Try On and Favorites launched globally on Thursday.
- Try-on takes a selfie, full-body photo or item screenshot.
- Favorites saves products to a Library alongside try-on images.
- Built on ChatGPT Images 2.5, which OpenAI says gives better lighting and lower latency.
- OpenAI's earlier instant checkout feature underperformed.
