# AI and the Destruction of the Creative Commons

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Chester Wisniewski argues that AI has shattered a 40-year equilibrium in software copyright. The balance between protection and openness was always tedious detail, but decades of debate had settled it - until now. His personal stake: he learned computing by typing BASIC listings from magazines into a Commodore 64 and teaching himself REXX to write BBS games; without those 'open' examples he'd never have been empowered to learn.

The essay's warning: the culture of open examples that created generations of developers is being destroyed as AI absorbs open work while the outputs and platforms close off, breaking the commons that made skills transferable. (Full text fetchable; summary based on the article's opening sections.)

## Highlights

- Wisniewski: AI threw out a 40-year equilibrium between copyright protection and openness
