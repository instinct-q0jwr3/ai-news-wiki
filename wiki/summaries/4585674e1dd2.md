# Historic UN Security Council Briefing on AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`marcus-on-ai` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

A historic UN Security Council session on AI: Yoshua Bengio, Sam Altman, Dario Amodei and Hugging Face's Clement Delangue gave back-to-back speeches, with the AP headline being the AI leaders' own warning that without intervention 'AI could be a risk to humanity as a whole.'

Gary Marcus reprints Bengio's remarks in full (with permission), saying he agreed with virtually every word - and, more surprisingly to him, with almost everything the other three said as well. The convergence, he notes, landed exactly on his long-running themes: preflight safety testing and transparency, echoed by many other speakers from around the world.

## Highlights

- Bengio, Altman, Amodei and Delangue addressed the UN Security Council back-to-back
- AP: AI firm heads warned the UN that without intervention AI could risk humanity as a whole

- He also agreed with most of what Altman, Amodei and Delangue said
