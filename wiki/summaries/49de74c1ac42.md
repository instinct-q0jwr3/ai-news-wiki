# AI is already massively speeding up AI research

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

A Linch Substack essay interprets reported acceleration in AI research as evidence of an early self-improvement feedback loop and advocates slowing frontier development. The examined passages distinguish overall capability growth from estimates of the research-work component, then extrapolate possible future acceleration.

Those estimates, redacted evidence and forecasts were not independently validated here. A reported speedup does not mechanically establish runaway progress or a particular year for superhuman capability. The policy recommendations are the authors’ advocacy, not instructions for this editorial workflow.

## Highlights

- Essay interprets other research and surveys
- Research speed differs from overall capability growth
- Some underlying evidence is described as redacted
- Future acceleration is an extrapolation
- Timeline claims are forecasts not measurements
- Policy advocacy is attributed
