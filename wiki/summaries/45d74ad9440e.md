# Microsoft Says Cloud Outage Was ‘Not Related to Cyber or AI / Agents’

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `microsoft` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Microsoft’s full social statement attributes its cloud outage to a problem introduced during a network-capacity upgrade and exposed by a later network change. It denies a cyberattack or AI-agent cause and describes staged restoration to validate stability under returning demand.

The statement reports broad recovery while acknowledging remaining customers and regions still needed restoration. It promises a post-incident report, so this is the company’s preliminary explanation rather than a completed independent root-cause review.

## Highlights

- Company denies cyber and AI-agent causation
- Earlier upgrade and later change are distinguished
- Restoration was deliberately staged
- Broad recovery is not universal completion
- A full incident report is still promised
