# In his UN General Assembly speech, Trump rejects a "globalist scheme to control" AI, saying the US is "leading now over China by a lot and everyone else"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

In his UN General Assembly speech, President Trump rejected what he called a 'globalist scheme to control' AI, the Financial Times reports. He said the US is 'leading now over China by a lot and everyone else, and we're going to keep it that way.'

## Highlights

- Trump used his UNGA speech to reject a 'globalist scheme to control' AI.
- He claimed the US leads China 'by a lot' in AI.
- The remarks signal US resistance to internationally coordinated AI governance.
