# New York bans AI toys, including those using internet to talk to children

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Guardian reports New York adopting a five-year restriction on manufacturing and selling chatbot-enabled toys, motivated by safety and privacy concerns involving children. It cites official statements and advocacy testing of connected dolls, animals and robots, and describes similar action in California.

The article calls the signing Wednesday, whereas NBC and the earlier official-source record place it Tuesday; the signing-day discrepancy is not silently resolved in favor of this story. Earlier coverage gives a delayed effective date rather than an immediate ban, and the measure should not be widened to every toy containing any AI. Reported unsafe examples are attributed investigations, not tests reproduced here.

## Highlights

- Restriction concerns chatbot-enabled toys
- Term is reported as five years
- Safety and child-data risks motivate the measure
- Signing weekday differs across reports
- Delayed effectiveness is distinct from signing
- Not every use of AI in toys is automatically covered
- No product testing or full statutory audit was performed
