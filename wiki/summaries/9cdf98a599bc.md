# Xiaomi open-sources MiMo-V2.6 Pro and Flash models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `xiaomi`

## Summary

Xiaomi released and open-sourced the MiMo-V2.6 series: two natively omnimodal models for coding, visual tasks and computer use, advanced through scaled reinforcement learning. MiMo-V2.6-Pro is the most capable, Flash targets a lower-cost balance, and a Pro-UltraSpeed variant offers up to 20x faster output at the same quality for latency-sensitive work.

Xiaomi says Pro performs on par with Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol across most agent benchmarks, scoring 46.32 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3 - ahead of Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8 Max as the highest-scoring open-source model in the comparison. API prices hold at V2.5 levels: Flash at $0.14/$0.28 per million uncached input/output tokens, Pro at $0.435 input.
