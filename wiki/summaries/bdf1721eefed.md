## Summary

Patrick McGee argues that Apple hardware chief Johny Srouji would be a strong candidate to lead Nvidia and says he believes Srouji would accept. The post quotes separate reporting that Srouji was under consideration, then adds McGee’s assessment of his competence, ambition and position at Apple.

This is a commentator’s candidate appraisal, not confirmation of an offer, acceptance or appointment. Belief about someone’s willingness should not be converted into a reported personal decision.
