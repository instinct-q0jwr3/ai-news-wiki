# President Trump says Scott Bessent is not "going to be Super Intelligence (SI) Czar", because "he doesn't want to" and Trump wants to keep him at the Treasury

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

President Trump said Friday that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent will not be his AI czar, after Semafor and Reuters reported he was considering Bessent for the post. 'Scott Bessent will not be going to be Super Intelligence (SI) Czar,' Trump wrote on Truth Social. 'Number One, he doesn't want to. Number Two, he's doing such a great job at Treasury, and that's where I want to keep him!'

The announcement follows Trump's September 19 pledge to form an 'AI Force, much like I did Space Force.'

## Highlights

- Trump: Bessent will not be 'Super Intelligence (SI) Czar'.
- Follows Semafor and Reuters reports that Bessent was considered.
- Trump: 'He doesn't want to' and is needed at Treasury.
