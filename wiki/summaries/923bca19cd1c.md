## Summary

Reuters reports that a US appeals court upheld the ruling for Thomson Reuters in its copyright lawsuit against Ross Intelligence, rejecting Ross's fair-use defense for using the material to train an AI system. The decision keeps intact one of the earliest major US rulings on whether copyrighted content can be used for AI training without a license.
