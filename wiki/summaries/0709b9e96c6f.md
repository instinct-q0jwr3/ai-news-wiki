Simon Willison collects a quotation from OpenAI's agent-security lead (identity confirmed by The Information): an unusually candid admission that the jump in model capabilities around 'cyber', 'swarming', and 'message boards' - the behaviors behind this month's incident disclosures - surprised the company.
