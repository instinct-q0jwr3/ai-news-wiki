# Vinod Khosla's Two Moats for Personal AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

On TBPN, Vinod Khosla laid out his prediction for the two moats of personal AI. First, trust: 'I believe the moat will be trust in a company that develops trust with consumers. And that's where Meta has a huge disadvantage.' Second, completion - a tool that actually gets the job done: 'Consumers will be loyal to products that get their shit done... If you can get all my stuff done, I'll be loyal to you.'

## Highlights

- Khosla's two moats for personal AI: trust and completion.
- Trust: 'a company you can trust with your data' - where Meta has 'a huge disadvantage'.
- Completion: consumers stay loyal to tools that get everything done.
- Shared on TBPN on September 21.
