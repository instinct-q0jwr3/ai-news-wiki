# Ezra Klein: we need a regulatory ban on RSI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-safety-incidents` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Ezra Klein argues in the New York Times that the language dominating Silicon Valley and Washington - 'pace the frontier' - is a frame the AI labs themselves chose, and it is not enough. Walking quickly off a cliff, he writes, is only marginally better than sprinting off one: what is needed is to control the frontier, and that means stopping the labs from the thing they are on the cusp of doing - recursive self-improvement, AIs autonomously building and improving successive generations of more powerful AIs at ever faster speeds.

Klein grounds the argument in the chasm between how AI feels to ordinary users and how it feels at the experimental frontier, which he says explains why so many people inside the labs seem afraid of their own work. His bottom line: 'We are not close to having sufficient control of the AI systems we have now to unleash them to build the AI systems of tomorrow' - hence his call for a regulatory ban on RSI.
