# Japanese used bookstores see 5x sales surge as books are being bought by the ton

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Japanese used bookstores are seeing a fivefold sales surge as books are bought in bulk, reportedly to feed AI - a trend sparking debate about large-scale book destruction. In the Hacker News discussion, commenters draw a line between mass-printed titles, where hundreds of thousands of copies remain, and rare one-of-a-kind volumes that could be lost for good if caught up in the fray.

## Highlights

- Japanese used bookstores report a 5x sales surge from bulk buyers.
- Books are reportedly being bought for AI, raising destruction concerns.
- HN commenters separate mass-printed stock from rare, irreplaceable volumes.
- Older and rare books risk getting 'caught up in the fray'.
