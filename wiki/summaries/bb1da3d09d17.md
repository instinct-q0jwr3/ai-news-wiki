# Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) is now Open Weight

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `mistral`

## Summary

Mistral has released the weights of Mistral Large 4, the trillion-parameter, natively multimodal flagship it calls Le Chonk, a few weeks after its public preview debut. The lab says three additional weeks of scaled-up reinforcement learning produced massive improvements in coding and defensive cybersecurity, with gains still accelerating and no signs of saturation. The result, in Mistral's telling, is the best open-weight model for coding and one of the most capable open models overall: it takes the lead on FrontierCode and Terminal-Bench, matches the best open-weight models on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index with substantially better token efficiency, and tops Cognition's private FrontierCode 2.0 benchmark - which judges whether a maintainer would merge the agent's change - at 19.7 percent.

The release leans hard on sovereignty: Le Chonk was trained from scratch on European infrastructure with what Mistral calls clear lineage from inception to release, pitched as a trusted foundation for organizations that want to own, customize and self-host their AI. Three reasoning modes (low, medium, high) let developers trade cost against performance, and the improved reasoning efficiency is meant to make sophisticated coding agents cheaper to run.
