# Amazon uses AI to monitor union risk in UK warehouses

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `amazon`

## Summary

Internal Amazon documents made public in a court battle with a Canadian union reveal Atlas, an in-house AI forecasting tool that predicts how likely warehouse staff in the US, Canada and the UK are to attempt unionizing, staging a sit-in or other 'disruptive labour events'. The documents, apparently training resources for Canadian managers, say Atlas amasses hundreds of data points - Reddit posts, internal survey responses, workplace data and previous industrial action - to give each warehouse a union-activity risk score between 100 and 900, where a lower score means higher risk.

The reporting, anchored in Coventry worker Garfield Hylton's experience, notes Amazon already logs every scan and idle second of a worker's day, but the AI surveillance of 'dissent' on social media and the company's internal bulletin board was unknown to employees.

## Highlights

- Surveils social media and internal bulletin boards for 'dissent'
- Surfaced via Amazon's court fight with a Canadian union
