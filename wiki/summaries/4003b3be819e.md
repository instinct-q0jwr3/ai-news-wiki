## Summary

Cory Doctorow recommends Jennifer Jenkins' book 'Music Copyright, Creativity, and Culture' through the lens of her career: Jenkins directs Duke's Center for the Public Domain and produces the annual New Year's roundup of works entering the public domain - a series born in the decades when the Sonny Bono Copyright Act froze the public domain entirely.

Doctorow's pitch is that Jenkins has a rare gift for making copyright - one of the most complicated, worst-understood and most consequential areas of law - not just comprehensible but fascinating, citing her late-2023 explainer on what 'Mickey Mouse's copyright is expiring' actually meant as the best in a crowded field. She's the go-to expert for outlets like Planet Money for a reason.

## Highlights

- Jennifer Jenkins runs Duke's Center for the Public Domain and its annual public-domain roundup
