# RIAA Flags Anna's Archive and AI Voice Cloner to U.S. Government

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The RIAA told the US Trade Representative, in its annual 'notorious markets' submission, about Anna's Archive and an AI voice cloner. The review makes no legal findings and only encourages listed services and their host countries to act on piracy and counterfeiting.

## Highlights

- RIAA flags Anna's Archive and an AI voice cloner to USTR.
- The notorious-markets review makes no legal findings.
