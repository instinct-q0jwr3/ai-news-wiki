# Google Tabletop Creates Shared Interactive Planning Spaces

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `google`

## Summary

Google Labs introduces Tabletop as a shared space where groups add links, files and ideas, then generate interactive planning tools. Its examples include itineraries, polls, expense splitters and tournament brackets that participants can use at the same time. The announcement describes a US waitlist rollout for adults, not general global availability.

## Highlights

- Shared real-time planning space
- Inputs include links, files and ideas
- Examples include polls and expense splitters
- Invite links bring groups into one workspace
- US adult waitlist limits the announced rollout
