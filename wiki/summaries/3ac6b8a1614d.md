# [AINews] Inkling 2 Small: 17A-594B trained in 68 hours on 12T tokens, beating Muse Spark

## Summary

Latent Space's AINews for Nov. 5 centers on Inkling 2 Small: a 594B-total, 17B-active model pretrained from scratch in under three days on under 12T tokens in native NVFP4, with a single joint RL run and a very good vision encoder. The authors say it is competitive with and often beats same-size Chinese models, and people compare it with Mistral, Reflection and Meta Muse, some calling it the smartest Western open-weight model by a margin. The notable part, they say, is intentional customizability, matching Thinking Machines' Tinker message. The issue also covers METR's study of AI research at Anthropic and John Schulman's take on alignment when scaling training. The post frames Thinking Machines as now a high-functioning lab after a shaky start.

## Highlights

- Inkling 2 Small trained in under 3 days on <12T tokens, native NVFP4
- Single joint RL run; strong vision encoder
- Seen as competitive with same-size Chinese models and top Western open weights
- Emphasis on customizability; METR's Anthropic study also covered
