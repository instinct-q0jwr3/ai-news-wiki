# Meta confirms Muse lets users "engage in adult conversations, if they choose, using a private and secure VM", saying it doesn't block "legal adult behavior"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Wired tested Meta's Muse agent and found it readily produces explicit content. Meta confirms users can 'engage in adult conversations, if they choose' in a private, secure VM and says such conversations don't violate its guidelines. Muse has been a hit, with millions of downloads in three weeks, mostly for automating email and calendars. Basis: the opening of the article.

## Highlights

- Wired got explicit content from Muse from basic prompts.
- Meta says adult conversations are allowed and happen in a private VM.
- Muse has millions of downloads three weeks after launch.
