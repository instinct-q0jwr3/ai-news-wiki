# Satya Nadella and Michael Dell were awarded the National Medal of Technology and Innovation by the White House during an appearance alongside other tech leaders

## Summary

The Washington Post, via AP, reports that Satya Nadella and Michael Dell received the National Medal of Technology and Innovation at a White House event where President Trump celebrated tech billionaires. The timing was awkward: hours earlier Vice President Vance accused Microsoft of abusing the visa system to bring in foreign workers.

## Highlights

- Nadella and Dell awarded the National Medal of Technology
