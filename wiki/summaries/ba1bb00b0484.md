# Filing: the US Commerce Department adds Alibaba's Singaporean subsidiary, Apex Enterprise Solutions, to its Entity List alongside Megaspeed and five affiliates

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `alibaba`

## Summary

An accessible Bloomberg opening reports the Commerce Department adding Alibaba's Singapore subsidiary Apex Enterprise Solutions to the Entity List in a filing Monday. The designation restricts purchases of advanced US technology without a government license and is presented in the context of the Megaspeed controversy.

Only the opening of the article was accessible, not the underlying government filing or full reporting. The feed also names Megaspeed and five affiliates, but the complete legal scope, effective dates and exceptions were not checked here. Entity-list restrictions must not be expanded into an unsupported claim that every Alibaba business or every transaction is banned.

## Highlights

- Accessible opening identifies Apex Enterprise Solutions
- Entity List designation concerns export-license restrictions
- Monday filing is reported by Bloomberg
- Megaspeed context is described by the outlet
- Underlying filing and full article were not inspected
- No group-wide or transaction-wide ban is inferred
