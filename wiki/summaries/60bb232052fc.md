# Bites, whose AI tools deliver personalized training to frontline workers via WhatsApp, SMS, Slack, and other channels, raised a $40M Series A led by PeakSpan

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Bites, an Israeli startup building AI-generated training for employees who do not work at desks, has raised a $40 million Series A led by PeakSpan Capital - $55 million total - with Vertex, Horizon Capital, Rittenhouse and Lyra participating and PeakSpan's Phil Dur and Charlie Reinkemeyer joining the board. The product turns company procedures into short, branded videos delivered through the channels frontline workers already live in: WhatsApp, SMS, Slack and Teams.

The plan for the money: accelerate US expansion, develop the AI platform, grow sales and customer success, build partner and reseller channels, and widen from hospitality, retail, consumer goods and manufacturing into banking, insurance and businesses that run on external workers. The technology bet is linking training to business outcomes rather than completion rates. It is the same agent-economy pattern as Broccoli AI's trades play, aimed inward at the workforce instead of outward at the phone lines.
