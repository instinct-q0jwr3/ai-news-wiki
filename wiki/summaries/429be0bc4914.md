# What Anthropic left out of their draft S-1

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`marcus-on-ai` `anthropic`

## Summary

Gary Marcus reads Anthropic's draft S-1 with Sherlock Holmes' Silver Blaze in mind: the curious incident is the dog that did nothing in the night-time. Earlier the same day he had told readers to watch for what the filing reveals - or not - about demand after the decline of 'tokenmaxxing' and the rise of low-cost alternative models; minutes later the document dropped.

His focus is the omission: what the S-1 leaves out about recent demand trends matters as much as what it discloses. The post sits partly behind his paywall, but the frame is clear - the absence of certain data in a document this exhaustive is itself information, and investors should read the silence as carefully as the numbers.
