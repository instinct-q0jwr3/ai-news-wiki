# Visit Iceland Will Send a Real Knitter to Your Dumb Meeting So You Can Skip It

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Visit Iceland's answer to the AI notetaker is a person with needles. The tourism board's 'AI Knittakers' campaign - where AI stands for 'Authentic Icelandic' - lets you book a real knitter to attend your online meeting in your place. Where an AI notetaker captures what is said, a Knittaker 'will read the room, notice the loaded pauses, reactions, ideas and disagreements before choosing the meeting's one-word summary and knit it in Icelandic wool' - PIVOT, ALIGN, RESET, UNMUTE, BLA BLA or WTF - and mails it to the organizer.

The campaign, launched October 29 and bookable free through November 20, is built on a Visit Iceland survey finding that US and UK residents feel they lose about eight hours a month to unnecessary meetings. Knitters range from a 30-year-old Kópavogur native to a 68-year-old Seyðisfjörður resident who learned from her grandmother at 11; the chief Knittaker is singer Salka Sól Eyfeld. It is a tourism campaign, but also a neat satire of the meeting-surveillance defaults the industry has normalized.
