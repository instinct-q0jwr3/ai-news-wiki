# Memo: Amazon says measuring AI adoption solely by token usage or code generation is "deeply flawed," as it discourages staff from tokenmaxxing

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `amazon`

## Summary

CNBC reports on an Amazon memo to software engineers that calls measuring AI adoption by token usage or code generation 'deeply flawed', and discourages 'tokenmaxxing' - using AI as much as possible regardless of output. The memo, circulated internally in September and posted publicly Tuesday, redefines the role of software engineers and managers in the AI age.

Context: companies are capping token usage or switching to cheaper models as AI bills rise, and Amazon itself shut down an internal leaderboard in May after employees tokenmaxxed to climb it. Amazon says it has not instituted token caps, and the memo stops short of telling staff to ignore the metrics entirely - it warns they drive higher costs and complexity.
