# Tiny Computers for iPhone Duo

## Summary

Fountain Interactive built Tiny Computers for the iPhone Duo: tiny emulated machines, including a working Commodore 64 command line, running on the dual-screen device. John Gruber links it as a must-try for Duo owners, while noting Cabel Sasser's observation that the ahistorical details betray a young, Claude-aided developer who never used the real machines.

## Highlights

- Tiny emulated computers (Commodore 64 and more) for iPhone Duo
