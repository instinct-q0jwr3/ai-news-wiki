# Introducing Eleven v4, our most emotive model

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-09-29 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

ElevenLabs launches Eleven v4, its most emotive text-to-speech model, plus the low-latency Eleven v4 Turbo. The pitch is prosody with intent: 'I need you to stay calm' should sound different from a doctor to a frightened patient than from a squad leader dropping into battle, and v4 interprets tone, pacing, emotion, character, and context while preserving speaker identity. It ranks #1 on Artificial Analysis and was preferred by ~75% of listeners in blind head-to-head tests. A new architecture improves multi-speaker dynamics so dialogue responds to conversational context rather than sounding assembled line by line.

## Highlights

- Eleven v4: ElevenLabs' most emotive TTS model, plus a low-latency Turbo variant
- Interprets tone, pacing, emotion, character, and context while keeping speaker identity
- #1 on Artificial Analysis; preferred by ~75% in blind head-to-head tests
- Built on a new architecture with more natural multi-speaker dialogue dynamics
- Example: 'I need you to stay calm' rendered differently for a doctor vs a game character
