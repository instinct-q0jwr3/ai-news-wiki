# Meta says it will allow its AI glasses users to opt out of having their "visual data" used to train its AI or shown to third-party contractors outside the US

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta`

## Summary

Meta will let AI-glasses users opt out of having their 'visual data' used for AI training, its latest concession to the privacy backlash against the glasses. Opted-out camera interactions with Meta AI will not train models and will not be shown to third-party contractors overseas: 'If you've opted out and you ask your glasses to translate a menu, we'll use your visual data to answer your question and then it's gone.'

The confusion Meta is trying to fix: photos you deliberately capture are not used for training, but images and video captured while using Meta AI - which never hit your camera roll - have been used for that purpose, something many users likely never knew. Critics note the company has done a poor job explaining the distinction.

## Highlights

- Meta glasses users can now opt out of visual data being used for AI training.
- Opt-out also blocks review by third-party contractors in other countries.
- Meta AI camera interactions previously trained models even though they skip the camera roll.
- Latest concession in the ongoing privacy backlash against the AI glasses.
