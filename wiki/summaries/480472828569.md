# Konversacia kontrolo 2025 (mallonge)

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Bert Hubert's Chatcontrol 2025 brief, in its Esperanto translation (one of a dozen community translations he links and hosts). Same content as the English original: led by Denmark, many EU states want WhatsApp/Signal to scan all photos and links with AI, reporting 'doubtful' images plus location and phone number to a new EU Centre, Europol and local police - with Signal threatening to leave the EU and governments exempting their own police and military. (The page is in Esperanto; the wiki keeps content in English.)

## Highlights

- Esperanto edition of Hubert's Chatcontrol 2025 brief
- AI scans of all private photos/links, doubts reported to EU Centre, Europol, local police
- Signal would rather leave the EU than implement it
- Police, military and national security are exempt from the law
- Hubert crowdsources translations and hosts them on his site
