# PostgreSQL 19 Running Behind Schedule Due to AI/LLM Burden

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

PostgreSQL 19 is running behind schedule - November at the earliest, missing the usual September-October window - and developer Robert Haas says AI is at fault, Phoronix reports. The project has been flooded with bug reports filed by AI/LLM agents. More bugs found means more fixes needed; LLMs can draft those fixes, but they are not always correct or compliant with PostgreSQL standards, so humans still review and commit them - 'and those humans have not gotten any smarter between 2025 and 2026.'

The second factor: time that should have gone into reviewing what made the release got diverted to security issues and back-ports on older branches, which is also why there were so many late reverts. The episode is a concrete case of AI output increasing total system throughput while decreasing the net capacity of the humans who gate it.

## Highlights

- PostgreSQL 19 delayed past its usual Sept-Oct window, November at earliest
- Cause: a surge of bug reports from AI/LLM agents overwhelming developers
- LLM-drafted fixes still need human review; "those humans have not gotten any smarter"
- Review time diverted to security back-ports, causing late reverts
- A concrete case of AI raising report volume while shrinking maintainer capacity
