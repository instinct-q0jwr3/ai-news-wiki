# Amazon Enters the Decision Model Race With Strands Decider 2B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `amazon`

## Summary

Amazon's Strands Labs released Strands Decider 2B, a small decision model for fast experimentation and local development. Decision models, or 'system one' models, pick between sets of options rather than generating free text, a category that took off after TypeSafe's Jev launched earlier this month.

Examples include yes/no questions and choosing among categories.

## Highlights

- Strands Decider 2B: small open decision model.
- Picks among options; does not generate arbitrary text.
- Follows Jev from TypeSafe two weeks ago.
- Aimed at local development.
