# NVIDIA Releases Nemotron-Labs-Sigma Continuous Diffusion Language Models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `nvidia`

## Summary

NVIDIA Research presents Sigma, 3B and 8B language models generating blocks by denoising continuous 16-dimensional token embeddings. A shared backbone supports autoregressive verification of diffusion-drafted blocks, while guidance and temperature control the quality/diversity tradeoff. The page reports few-step generation and a 3.46-times average 8B self-speculative throughput gain on GB200 hardware.

The speedup is against its specified autoregressive decoding baseline on that hardware, not a universal comparison with deployed services. Quality, sample-budget gains and negligible-loss claims are research-team results, not reproduced here. More samples can improve pass@k without improving a single answer's reliability. No model was downloaded, served or benchmarked.

## Highlights

- Research family has 3B and 8B scales
- Generation denoises continuous token embeddings in blocks
- Shared backbone supports self-speculative decoding
- Reported 3.46x result is an 8B GB200-specific average
- Pass@k depends on sample budget
- No inference or independent speed test was run
