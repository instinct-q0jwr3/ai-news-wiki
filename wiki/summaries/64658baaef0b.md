# Amazon plans to invest $100M to build a robotics manufacturing facility in Indiana, set to open by 2028; its robots at 300+ facilities help with 75% of orders

## Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports that Amazon plans to invest $100M in a robotics manufacturing facility in Indiana, slated to open by 2028. Context: Amazon's robots already operate at more than 300 facilities and touch roughly 75% of customer orders, so the plant expands in-house production of the machines its logistics network increasingly depends on. The article is paywalled, so this summary is based on the headline and lead captured by the feed.
