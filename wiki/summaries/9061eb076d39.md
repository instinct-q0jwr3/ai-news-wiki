# Danish Supreme Court Finds That AI CSAM Is CSAM

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Denmark's Supreme Court ruled that when sentencing for distributing AI-generated sexual material depicting people under 18, courts should not take into account whether the people depicted are presumed to be fictitious. The defendant had been found guilty under the then-applicable criminal code section on such material.

The headline reads this as treating AI-generated child sexual abuse material the same as other CSAM. The source is in Danish and only the start was read.

## Highlights

- Danish Supreme Court: fictional depiction is no mitigating factor.
- Basis: Danish text, opening only.
