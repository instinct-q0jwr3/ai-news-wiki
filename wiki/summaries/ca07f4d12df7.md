# More than 35,000 Grok-built apps were open to anyone with the link, including SpaceXSI's own

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

## Summary

A security write-up says over 35,000 apps built with Grok's Build mode were open to anyone with the link under grok.me. On November 8 the authors paged through public addresses signed in to nothing and found, among others, a confidential onboarding plan for a new SpaceXSI enterprise sales hire: internal system addresses, tools, 19 internal chat channels and a 30-day plan. The exposure was the share-by-link default; names were covered in the published figures.

## Highlights

- 35,000+ Grok-built apps public by link
- Found a confidential SpaceXSI onboarding plan unauthenticated

- Researchers redacted names
