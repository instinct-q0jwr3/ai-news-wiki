## Summary

Latent Space's 'You are the Team' episode with Moshi CEO Anekha Sokhal: the former JPMorgan energy-derivatives trader is building the tool she wished she'd had - a recommendation engine connecting market information to what matters for a trader's book, pitched as Bloomberg, Claude, and Netflix having a baby.

Talking with swyx at AI Engineer New York, Sokhal covers trusted sources, document extraction, and build-versus-buy decisions for models, arguing useful AI must fit the way people actually work. She also addresses the pressure on AI engineers told 'you are the team,' and shares what customer conversations at the conference taught her - meeting users comes before hiring.
