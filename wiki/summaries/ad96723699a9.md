# Why We're Banning AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A magazine announces it will stay handmade and ban AI. It cites falling opportunities and pay for artists as clients embrace generative AI, more touch-up work and fewer sustainable jobs, and growing despair in the field.

It urges readers to ensure every publication they support does the same.

## Highlights

- Magazine commits to handmade, no AI.
- Cites falling pay and work for artists.
