# The Only Skill That Matters Now

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high_

`works-on-my-machine`

## Summary

Scott Werner tells a fable: a Tokyo restaurant where the owner, tired of explaining his grandmother's miso soup, built machines that cook everything perfectly. The chefs revolted - where's the art, the soul, the training? - so he turned them into inventors instead: flavor architects and experience designers creating dishes the machines can't make yet.

The restaurant now has a three-year waiting list, and the owner's line is the thesis: 'We forgot how to cook, so we could remember how to create.' Applied to the AI moment, the only skill that matters is the one machines can't supply - imagining what doesn't exist yet.
