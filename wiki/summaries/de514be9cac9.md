# Introducing Ember-1

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Fireworks Research introduced Ember-1, a specialized model that delivers Kimi K3's quality with 40% fewer tokens. Built on Kimi K3, it learned to cut unnecessary reasoning while keeping the thinking that matters - addressing user feedback that K3's long reasoning traces made automated coding expensive at scale.

Fireworks says quality held up on external benchmarks, in live customer A/B tests, and on its own coding and agent workloads. Ember-1 is available today and kicks off an ongoing series of specialized models shaped by what developers ask for; the launch came alongside the announcement of the company's inaugural Forge 2026 conference.

## Highlights

- Ember-1: Kimi K3 quality with 40% fewer tokens.
- Trained to cut unnecessary reasoning while keeping the useful thinking.
- Motivation: K3's long traces made automated coding expensive at scale.
- Quality held on external benchmarks, live A/B tests and Fireworks' own workloads.
- First of an ongoing series of specialized Fireworks models.
