# Xbox CEO Asha Sharma announces Xbox Labs, a small team to test emerging ideas, citing recent game ports and mashups, many of them built with AI tools

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

Xbox CEO Asha Sharma announces Xbox Labs, a small group of game makers, engineers and scientists within Xbox that will build and test emerging ideas in the open and measure success by whether more people play and create. She says it was spurred by recent AI-built game mashups and code ports, calling them good outcomes for players even when they challenge existing business models (GamesIndustry.biz).

## Highlights

- Xbox Labs: a small team to prototype emerging ideas.
- Prompted by AI-assisted game mashups and ports.
- Success measured by more people playing and creating.
