# Ask HN: How do you manage your AI agents' context across clients and projects?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `anthropic` `agentic-systems` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

A Hacker News poster asks how people preserve prior decisions and client rules across AI work sessions without mixing unrelated projects. Replies describe repository-local decision records, status documents and specification-driven workflows.

The thread is a small set of practices rather than a validated solution. Its central problem is continuity with separation: retaining useful project context while avoiding the accidental reuse of one client’s assumptions in another’s work.

## Highlights

- Question concerns cross-session context
- Client separation is a distinct requirement
- Replies suggest decision and status records
- Repository-local files are one approach
- No measured effectiveness is supplied
