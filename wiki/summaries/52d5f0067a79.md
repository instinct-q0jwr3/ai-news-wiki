# Neural Networks Are Already Clean

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Goodfire's Nick Cammarata argues that polysemanticity in early model organisms was not caused by superposition but by ordinary, mostly fixable issues. His team can now understand most neurons in models up to trillions of parameters, and he thinks the outlook for mechanistic interpretability is better than thought. A working hypothesis, in a long post.

## Highlights

- Polysemanticity blamed on fixable issues, not superposition.
- Most neurons understood in models up to trillions of parameters.
