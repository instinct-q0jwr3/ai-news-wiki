# Shopify plans to allow Meta's Muse to complete purchases on behalf of users at Shopify-powered stores via its one-tap checkout service Shop Pay

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `shopify` `muse` `agentic-systems` `agentic-commerce`

## Summary

Shopify plans to let Meta's personal AI agent, Muse, complete purchases on behalf of users at Shopify-powered stores, the Wall Street Journal reports. The purchases would run through Shop Pay, Shopify's one-tap checkout service.

## Highlights

- Shopify plans agentic checkout via Meta's Muse.
- Muse would complete purchases for users at Shopify-powered stores.
- Transactions would flow through the Shop Pay one-tap checkout.
