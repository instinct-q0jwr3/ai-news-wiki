# Ask HN: How do you evaluate the skills your AI agents use?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems` `external-evaluation`

## Summary

This Hacker News discussion asks how to evaluate reusable instructions used by AI coding assistants. A respondent contrasts quick self-evaluation with building a benchmark from real work, noting that the value of testing grows with deployment scale and that results can change between models.

The thread offers evaluation ideas rather than a proven universal test. Checking the final artifact and checking a change in an assistant’s behavior are different measurement problems.

## Highlights

- Discussion concerns reusable task instructions
- Real-work benchmarks are proposed
- Quick self-evaluation is described as limited
- Model changes can alter behavior
- Artifact quality and behavior need different tests
