# Reading the Anthropic S-1, with Zvi Mowshowitz

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Patrick McKenzie and Zvi Mowshowitz read Anthropic's S-1 on the Complex Systems podcast. Points covered: a founder-controlled structure with a single Class F share holding 50.1% of votes, an uncapped 3:1 employee donation match that may be the most expensive perk ever, and about $900 billion in forward compute commitments. They discuss softer Q3 revenue as a sign of a GPU shortage rather than a demand problem, the bubble and circular-financing bear cases, how the S-1 discusses existential risk, and who is to blame when AI-assisted hackers took $5 billion from Binance.
