# University libraries are being swarmed by AI bots, putting public access at risk

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

CBC reports that university libraries worldwide are being swarmed by AI data-scraping bots that slow or crash sites meant for students, researchers and the public, sometimes knocking whole collections offline. Reports began in late 2024 across North America, Europe and Oceania and keep getting worse. San Jose State's Jill Strykowski says the bots are 'bombarding us', and Victoria's Dean Seeman says traffic sometimes takes sites down completely.

Libraries and museums are prime targets because their vetted content sits mostly outside paywalls and logins. Ontario Tech's Peter Lewis says ordinary users now compete with bot farms, an extractive use of public-interest institutions to build commercial models.
