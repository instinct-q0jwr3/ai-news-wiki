## Summary

Historian Mark Humphries describes a prerelease GPT-6.2 Astra session identifying the vicomte de Chabot as a candidate creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook. A diary clue led to a paper travel journal, and the author reports that later examination of archival copies supported the attribution.
