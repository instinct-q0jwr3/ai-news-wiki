The article version of Latent Space's interview with Anthropic's Katelyn Lesse (Engineering) and Angela Jiang (Product), covering the Claude Platform: launching Opus 5.5, separating the model's 'brain' from the agent's 'hands', scaling the API, and building reliable managed agents.
