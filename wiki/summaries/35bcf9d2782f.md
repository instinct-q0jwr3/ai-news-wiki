## Summary

We spent 20B+ tokens of GPT-6 Astra to explore everything.

GPT-6 Astra , the first Stargate and lightly looped supermodel from OpenAI, launched today , cleanly beating Fable 5.1 on many metrics including completely saturating the hardest versions of FrontierMath (97.6%) and ARC-AGI-3 (99.9%).

After burning over 20B tokens of Astra , we can confirm the most surprising finding: GPT-6 Astra is one of a new class of models 1 that are fully capable AI Engineers in their own right .

- You could hire someone at $200-$1000 a day, or you can hire GPT-6 for $100 over 2 days to do this.
