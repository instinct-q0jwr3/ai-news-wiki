# AI safety is mostly a sex cult in Berkeley

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

A piece on verysane.ai examines allegations about the Berkeley AI-safety scene, starting from a sealed police report obtained by Rolling Stone. The report was filed by onetime MIRI employee Louie Helm, who claimed a teenager was 'being exploited' by a group of men, including Eliezer Yudkowsky, who ran a local youth camp - the Summer Program for Applied Rationality and Cognition, hosted by CFAR. The article notes this first set of allegations was investigated and deemed baseless.

According to the piece, officers dispatched to Yudkowsky's home wrote that the 'alleged victim', then 18, stressed that they 'lived in a happy and safe environment'. That person later transitioned and today lives with his wife, whom he met in Berkeley's Rationalist scene, and their two children; he told the author he rejects the notion that he was exploited by anyone other than Helm, whom he felt used him as ammunition against Yudkowsky.
