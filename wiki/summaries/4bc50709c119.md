# S-1: as of September 30, Anthropic had 11,900+ customers spending $100,000+ annually, 1,900+ customers spending $1M+, and ~250 customers spending $10M+

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `amazon`

## Summary

Anthropic's S-1 shows a customer base that is large, concentrated, and expanding fast: as of September 30, the company counts more than 11,900 customers spending at least $100,000 annually, more than 1,900 spending $1 million or more, and about 250 spending $10 million or more a year.

The stickiness metric stands out: more than half of customers spending at least $100,000 now use both the Claude platform and a first-party app like Claude Code or Cowork, up from about 30% at the end of last year - the cross-product ecosystem strategy that made Amazon and Uber durable. Anthropic attributes the momentum to significant usage expansion as customers move from pilots to production.
