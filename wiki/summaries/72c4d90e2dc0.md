# ArtCraft – Controllable AI for Artists

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

ArtCraft is an app for artists who want more control than text prompts give. The site pitches composing a shot yourself, with images, backdrops, props and 3D elements, and then letting a model render it.

Features listed include placing virtual actors in physical environments with consistent locations across shots, 3D and 2D compositing, image-to-3D mesh, character posing and background removal. It lists models such as Seedance 2.5 and Nano Banana 2, and a subscription is required. This summary comes from the product site, so the claims are marketing.

## Highlights

- Controllable AI tool aimed at artists and filmmakers
- Compose scenes with layers, 3D meshes and posed characters
