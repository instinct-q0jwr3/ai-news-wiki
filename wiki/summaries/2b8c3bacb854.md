# Town, which develops enterprise AI assistants, raised a $108M Series B at a $1B valuation, after announcing a $55M Series A in June

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

TechCrunch reports Town's $108M Series B at a $1B valuation, co-led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures. The two-year-old San Francisco startup, founded by former Plaid CTO Jean-Denis Greze, launched publicly just four months ago, says it reached millions in ARR within weeks, and has now raised at least $160M including June's $55M Series A. Town builds enterprise AI assistants and was also named this week among the work-focused agents transacting through Stripe Link - the agent-commerce rails and the agent vendors are raising in the same news cycle.
