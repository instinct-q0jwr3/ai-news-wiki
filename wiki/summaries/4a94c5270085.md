## Summary

Gary Marcus notes the timing: Anthropic is reportedly about to file publicly for what could be a record-breaking IPO, and in some ways they could not have picked a worse week. The post is paywalled past its opening, but its premise - that the market debut lands amid a pile-up of hostile news cycles - captures the mood around the filing. Marcus has been tracking the gap between AI valuations and AI economics, and an S-1 dropped into that debate gives him, and everyone else, actual numbers to argue with.
