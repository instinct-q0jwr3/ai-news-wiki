# Open d1: Edge decision models for text, vision, and audio

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

Liquid AI releases d1, open decision models that give an answer in a single forward pass instead of generating tokens, for fast decisions on small edge hardware. d1-3B takes text and images, and d1-omni-600M, an experimental checkpoint, takes text with either image or audio.

d1-3B was built by merging weights of LFM2.5 models and fine-tuning with different seeds; Liquid says training on long inputs, shuffling answer options and fixing data shortcuts mattered more than advanced techniques. Both models are on Hugging Face. The omni model scores 15.95 on the company's index.

## Highlights

- Decision models answer in one forward pass, no tokens
- d1-3B handles text and images
- d1-omni-600M handles text plus image or audio, experimental
- Data fixes mattered more than fancy methods
- Open on Hugging Face
