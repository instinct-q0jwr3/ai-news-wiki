# When talking about AI safety, President Trump says "I don't mind guardrails. But I don't wanna put us in a deep second position", and "China has no guardrails"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

In a CBS/NYT interview with Ross Douthat after the midterms, Trump discussed AI alongside other topics. He said China's Xi is 'all in' on what he prefers to call super intelligence, and that whoever wins the race with China wins. Asked why he is not worried by the doom warnings of AI builders, he said the risk is not nearly as real as they claim and can be managed, and that China will get the technology anyway.

On guardrails and working with Democrats on AI safety he said he does not mind them but does not want to be put in a deep second position, adding that China has none. The transcript is long and mostly about other subjects; this summary covers only the AI exchange.

## Highlights

- Trump: race with China decides the winner
- Says doom risk is overstated and manageable
- 'I don't mind guardrails' but not at the cost of second place
- Open to working with Democrats on AI safety
- Interview mostly covers midterm loss and other topics
