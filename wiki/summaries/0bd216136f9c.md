# Show HN: JevBench, a reproducible benchmark for typed decision models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `typesafe` `external-evaluation`

## Summary

JevBench scores Jev 1.13.0 at 83.6, keeping it at #1 on the typed-decision-model leaderboard. The benchmark maintainers explain why classifier.dev is listed as an honorable mention rather than ranked from v1.2.4: its own pages and API identify the fast tier as Jev itself ('the fast tier is Jev, TypeSafe's decision model', model 'jev-1.13.0'), so ranking it would rank Jev against Jev.

The smart tier - Jev plus a reasoning model (gemini-3.8-flash) that re-asks only answers under 0.7 confidence - was never run in the benchmark, so nothing scores it. Only the free fast tier was measured; the page estimates the paid flat rate at $0.0033 per 1,000 decisions at full use of the $20/month Pro plan (200,000 fast classifications a day).

## Highlights

- Jev 1.13.0 scores 83.6 on JevBench, ranked #1
- classifier.dev demoted to honorable mention: its fast tier is Jev itself
- Smart tier is escalation on sub-0.7 confidence answers, not best-of-N or a committee
- Escalation model: gemini-3.8-flash; never benchmarked, so unscored
- Measured run used the free tier (20,000 classifications/day)
- Estimated $0.0033 per 1,000 decisions at full use of the $20/month Pro plan
