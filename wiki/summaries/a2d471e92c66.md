# Using LLMs to trace alchemical knowledge and decode 17th century letters

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

A follow-up post on the GPT-6 Astra code-breaking story reports that the team is still analyzing the model's logs to see exactly how it executed the break. It also documents a July 2026 find in the German Bundesarchiv: several collections of radio messages, both enciphered and in cleartext, from the SS-Totenkopf Division's logistics command (Nachschubfuehrer).

Many of those messages were sent to the Ib (Quartiermeister) radio station and are identical to ones on the site's 1941 Message List; others are new but most likely related. The new messages are being added to the list in bold with an NF indicator - all NF messages are outgoing.

## Highlights

- Researchers are still analyzing the GPT-6 Astra logs of the break.
- July 2026: Bundesarchiv yielded new SS-Totenkopf logistics radio-message collections.
- Many messages match the 1941 Message List; others are new but related.

- All NF messages are outgoing, sent to the Ib radio station.
