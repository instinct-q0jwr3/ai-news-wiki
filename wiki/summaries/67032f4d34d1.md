# Flai, which makes AI tools for car dealerships to manage phone calls, emails, and texts, raised a $27M Series A led by Base10 Partners

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Flai, which builds AI tools for car dealerships, raised a $27 million Series A led by Base10 Partners, with dealers Friedkin Group and Findlay Automotive, Toyota's venture arm, Y Combinator and First Round Capital participating. A year ago it was three people pitching dealers; now it works with more than 10 of the top 50 US dealer groups, and its AI answers customers, runs outbound campaigns and schedules 50,000 appointments a month across sales and service - driving a claimed 20x revenue increase.

CEO Ari Polakof frames Flai as an AI-native CRM built from the ground up for each dealership rather than a phone-answering tool: it follows up with customers, alerts leadership when someone is angry or a promise is due, and handles bilingual flows - a luxury dealer in Puerto Rico has customers switching between Spanish and English mid-conversation.
