# Ed Zitron’s AI Prediction Track Record

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball`

## Summary

A long fact-check at danluu.com takes on Ed Zitron's AI writing and argues that, behind the flamboyant style, 'basically everything is made up or misrepresented.' The aggression and expletives, the author writes, hype up people who already agree rather than change minds; Zitron found a niche in anti-tech grift and exploits it. The piece links plenty of Zitron's sources but says that following them to the primary material shows something very different from what Zitron implies.

The concrete charges are about arithmetic. In one Zitron spreadsheet, the author says, February 1-10 is not counted, March 1-10 is counted twice, August 21 to October 21 is treated as one month instead of two, October 21 to November 1 is dropped, and a commenter notes the sheet even contains a February 30. Zitron also claimed he computed Anthropic's 2025 revenue at $3.6 billion, suggesting funny business - but the numbers work out once the errors are fixed. The broader framing compares this prediction style to Paul Ehrlich, whose forecasts of imminent catastrophe ('even money that England will not exist in the year 2000') routinely failed, and argues that crisp, strong, doubt-free positions are simply the surest way to build a following.
