# World Labs Is Joining AMD

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

World Labs announces it has signed a definitive agreement to join AMD, with the transaction expected to close by end of 2026 subject to regulatory approval. The spatial-AI startup, founded in 2024, frames the move as the next step after a deep technical partnership with AMD on model training and inference optimization: scaling its reach means getting closer to the hardware. Fei-Fei Li joins AMD as Executive Vice President and Chief Scientist, reporting directly to CEO Lisa Su, while Justin Johnson and Ben Mildenhall continue leading the World Labs team inside AMD as a frontier research organization. The stated joint ambition is an end-to-end open AI ecosystem spanning hardware, software, platforms, and open models.

## Highlights

- World Labs signs a definitive agreement to join AMD; close expected by end of 2026
- Follows a year of technical partnership on training and inference optimization on AMD GPUs
- Fei-Fei Li becomes AMD Executive VP and Chief Scientist, working directly with Lisa Su
- Justin Johnson and Ben Mildenhall keep leading the World Labs team inside AMD
- Stated goal: an end-to-end open AI ecosystem across hardware, software, platforms, and open models
