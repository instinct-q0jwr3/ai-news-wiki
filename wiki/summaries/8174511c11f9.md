# M5 Ultra Mac Studio Review

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Federico Viticci's MacStories review of the top-of-the-line M5 Ultra Mac Studio with 256 GB of RAM delivers a blunt verdict: it is a dream machine for local AI agents. Over four days of testing against an M3 Ultra with 512 GB of RAM and a desktop PC with an RTX 5090, he concludes that for size, price, thermals and Apple's unified memory, the M5 Ultra changed how he thinks about local models. The 5090 keeps an edge in memory bandwidth, but given the PC's bulk, heat and noise, he would take the Mac Studio any day. Running Qwen3.8-Flash-Next locally has been fast enough that he made it his default in Open Minis for iOS and Hermes Agent: the personal assistants he uses most, more than Siri AI, now run entirely on a local model. Agents respond faster, stay quick at larger context windows, and hold speed through long multi-turn loops; he also runs local models in the Codex app as main threads or subagents orchestrated by GPT-6 Astra.

On why local AI at all, he is honest about the economics: by the time the hardware is repaid you could have paid for the most expensive Anthropic subscription for years. His own answer mixes privacy, cost and the tinkerer's 'cool, why not'. The concrete proof is his summer project: Desk, an internal app organizing 310 documents for his iOS and iPadOS 27 review, where a team of agents built on DeepSeek V4 Flash plus olmOCR ran 24/7 for 99 days, transcribing WWDC sessions, extracting features from webpages, PDFs, notes and screenshots, cross-referencing them across review chapters, and organizing everything through the Notion API.
