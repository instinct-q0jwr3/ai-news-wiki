# Trump says he will appoint an AI czar and form an 'AI Force'

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high_

## Summary

President Trump said he would appoint an AI czar and form an 'AI Force,' Bloomberg reports, in a Truth Social post that rejected AI safety concerns as a 'hoax.' He continued to push tech companies to race ahead with development despite growing safety fears.

## Highlights

- Trump says he will appoint an AI czar and form an 'AI Force'.
- The Truth Social post rejected AI safety concerns as a 'hoax'.
- He urged tech companies to keep racing ahead on development.
