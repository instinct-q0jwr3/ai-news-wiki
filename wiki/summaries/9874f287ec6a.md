# Three AI agents, two countries, and one uneven world wide web

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Roya Pakzad, a technology and human rights researcher, tests three AI agents across two countries and languages, moving beyond whether models answer differently in English versus Farsi to how language shapes what agents can do on the web. Her aim follows a RightsCon panel on human rights impact assessment of agentic AI.

The post argues that the web is uneven by language, so agents can behave differently depending on the language and country they work in. She draws on her open-source platform for language-pair analysis of LLM responses.

## Highlights

- Compares three agents across two countries and languages.
- Looks at web access and quality by language, not just model answers.
- Draws on RightsCon panel on agentic AI and human rights.
- Researcher with an open-source language-pair evaluation platform.
