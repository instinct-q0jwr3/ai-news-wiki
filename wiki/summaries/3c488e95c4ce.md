## Summary

A curated September 2026 benchmark list of open-weight 'uncensored' and offensive-security models for authorized red-team work, penetration testing, and security research, with data sourced from HuggingFace model cards and official publications. The list links downloads for around twenty models, including WhiteRabbitNeo, BugTraceAI CORE Apex/Ultra, CYBER-FROST 3.8, CyberPal2.0-20B, several Cyber-* fine-tunes, RavenX-CyberAgent (a Qwen3.6-35B franken-merge), REDCELL-26B, VEXT-Pentest-7B, and Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored from OrcaRouter. The cached text is mostly the download list itself, so benchmark scores and methodology are not captured here.
