# Cisco gives engineer unlimited AI tokens to rewrite legacy code

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Fortune's own account of the Cisco story adds the context Jeetu Patel's bet is pushing against: entry-level jobs are vanishing, some 40% of bosses say they plan to hire fewer graduates because AI is cheaper, and young workers get blamed for not doing free overtime. Patel's counter-move was to hand one of the company's biggest jobs to a 27-year-old he found 'five levels' down the org chart: Padmanabhan 'Paddy' Krishnamurthy, who joined Cisco in 2025 when the $429 billion giant acquired EzDubs, the real-time translation startup he cofounded in 2023.

Krishnamurthy told Patel he could rewrite Cisco's legacy code with a two-person team - beyond what the most AI-forward people on Patel's own staff were proposing. He now has four weeks to prove the approach, aiming to refactor 98% of the legacy codebase within 24 months, with unlimited AI tokens behind him. Patel is openly hunting for more Paddys: young hires who 'challenge the system' and act as an 'energizer bunny' for everyone around them.
