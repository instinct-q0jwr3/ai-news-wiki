# Valve Responds to Developers Fearing AI Slop and Clones

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

At the Steam Indie Connect event in Korea, Valve's Steam Business Team told developers worried about AI clone games that trust built with players is the strongest barrier: make the genuine version the best version through community building and consistent updates, since features and identity added over time can't be copied like a single mechanic.

Valve also shared Steam figures: a recent peak of 43.5 million concurrent users, South Korea revenue up 126% over five years, with in-game purchase revenue up 388%. The team walked through launch preparation - Coming Soon pages, demos, Next Fest - with the message that launch is the starting line, not the finish.

## Highlights

- Valve's answer to AI clones: community plus consistent updates
- 'The genuine version must be the best version'
- Steam hit 43.5M peak concurrent users; Korea revenue +126% in 5 years
- Guidance delivered at Steam Indie Connect in Busan
