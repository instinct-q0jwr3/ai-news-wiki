# The gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is widening

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Andrej Karpathy revisits an April tweet to argue that the gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is widening into a sharp funnel. Roughly 6 billion people (~75%) have barely touched LLMs; another 1-2 billion (~20%) are casual free-tier users who see derpy chatbots - a better Google search, a writing aid - and reasonably conclude they have little use for it, a tier that includes many professionals whose work is mostly talking to other people.

Then comes the thin neck: about 20 million people (0.2%) using frontier models for math and code see first-hand that projects that took weeks now finish from a prompt - building, copying, translating and decompiling apps. Karpathy notes how recent this is: less than a year ago he was still writing code by hand with tab-completion. The funnel explains why public discourse about AI keeps talking past itself.
