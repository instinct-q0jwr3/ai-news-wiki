# MiMo-v2.6-Pro: Intelligence, Performance and Price Analysis

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `xiaomi`

## Summary

Artificial Analysis' model page for Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro places it among the leading open-weight models on intelligence at a reasonable price for its class. The model scores 46 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v4.3.2), well above the comparable-model median of 18, supports text, image, speech and video input with text output, and offers a 1M-token context window.

Trade-offs: it is notably fast at 125 tokens per second (median 75) but somewhat verbose, generating 140M tokens across the Intelligence Index evaluations. Pricing is $0.43 per 1M input tokens (median $0.30) and $0.87 per 1M output (median $1.13); the full Intelligence Index run cost $206.66. The page covers the reasoning variant of the model and breaks results across agentic knowledge work, coding, physics reasoning, hallucination rate, long-context and more.

## Highlights

- MiMo-V2.6-Pro scores 46 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2; comparable-model median is 18.
- Open weights, released September 2026; reasoning variant benchmarked.
- Multimodal input: text, image, speech and video; text output; 1M-token context window.
- Speed: 125 tokens/second, well above the 75 median; verbosity on the high side (140M tokens across evals).
- Pricing: $0.43 per 1M input tokens, $0.87 per 1M output; full index evaluation cost $206.66.
- Index components include Terminal-Bench 4.0, SciCode, Humanity's Last Exam, GDPval-AA and AA-Omniscience.
