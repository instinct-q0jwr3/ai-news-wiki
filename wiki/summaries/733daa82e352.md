## Summary

Laminar (YC S24) launches on HN with open-source observability for AI agents and its headline model, flow-1, trained with RL to find errors in agent traces. The claim: flow-1 matches GPT-6-sol in detection quality on Laminar's benchmark while being 23x cheaper, making it affordable to analyze every production agent trace rather than sampling a fraction. The thread (48 points, 23 comments in the first hours) is the community's second look after the earlier Launch HN - same product, sharper framing around the error-detection model.
