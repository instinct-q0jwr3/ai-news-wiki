## Highlights

- New claude-api skill commands: build-eval and hillclimb
- build-eval constructs an evaluation inside your codebase
- hillclimb improves the app one change at a time against the eval
- A held-out example set catches overfitting to the eval
- Principles: model capability is jagged; case selection by current failure can mislead
