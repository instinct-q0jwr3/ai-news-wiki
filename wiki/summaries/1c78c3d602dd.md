# the Commerce Department is finalizing a semiconductor control update that would prevent remote access and chip smuggling into China via data centers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

## Summary

The Information reports, according to the Techmeme feed, that the Commerce Department is finalizing semiconductor controls aimed at remote access to advanced chips and diversion through data centers into China. The wording describes a rule in preparation, not an enacted restriction. The original article was unavailable, leaving its exact scope, exceptions and effective date unverified.

## Highlights

- Reported update targets remote compute access
- Data-center diversion is another stated concern
- Rule is described as being finalized
- Scope and effective date were not accessible
