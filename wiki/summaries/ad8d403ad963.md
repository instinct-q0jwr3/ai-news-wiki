# Startups embrace open-weight models to cut reliance on frontier labs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai`

## Summary

Bloomberg reports that a growing set of startups - including Harvey, Abridge, Ramp and Rogo - are embracing open-weight models or training their own models to cut their expensive reliance on frontier labs like OpenAI and Anthropic. Harvey, the $15.6 billion legal startup, built its business on fine-tuning models like GPT-4 for lawyers, but after a March update to its AI agents, customer usage spiked while gross margins dropped sharply from about 50 percent, pushing it to diversify its model stack.

The shift reflects a broader repricing of the application layer: as open-weight models close the quality gap, startups see training or customizing their own models as the way to protect margins and reduce vendor risk, rather than paying frontier-lab API prices that scale with their own growth.
