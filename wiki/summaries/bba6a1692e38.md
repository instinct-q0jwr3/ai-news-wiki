# Meta previews Muse Charm, a palm-sized Tamagotchi-like device for using Muse, with a ~2-inch OLED screen, front and rear cameras, and 5G, on sale by year's end

## Summary

A Bloomberg report that Meta previewed Muse Charm: a palm-sized, Tamagotchi-like device for using its Muse assistant, with a roughly 2-inch OLED display. The body is paywalled and was not captured, so this summary rests on the headline.

On that basis: Meta is experimenting with a dedicated hardware form factor for its assistant - a pocket companion rather than a phone app or glasses. Price, ship date, and what it does beyond the tiny screen are unknown from the capture.
