# OpenAI says it will begin testing a visual ad format in ChatGPT later in October in the US, displaying clearly labeled visual ads while users generate images

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-05 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

OpenAI says it will test visual ads in ChatGPT later in October in the US with an initial group of advertisers. They will be clearly labelled and appear while users generate images, using pictures to show product inspiration or how a product is used.

## Highlights

- Visual ad test starts later in October in the US.
- Ads appear during image generation and are labelled.
- Initial group of advertisers only.
