# OpenAI's projected compute bill climbed to $856B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `openai` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

The Financial Times reported that OpenAI expects negative free cash flow of $278 billion between 2026 and 2030, citing a July presentation prepared for a computing deal. This analysis notes the framing oddity: the same materials put revenue at $350 billion in 2030 against roughly $36 billion this year, and compute and infrastructure spending at about $856 billion across the period, up from the roughly $600 billion target given to investors publicly in February. Spending can rise while burn falls because much of the build is financed by partners rather than by OpenAI itself.

The $278 billion figure is actually an improvement of about $27 billion on the roughly $305 billion negative free cash flow OpenAI projected for the same period in May, which the author argues makes the alarm framing odd: the number getting headlines is the smallest of the three, and it moved in the right direction. Samantha Oltman wrote up the figures for Bloomberg, which corrected its story shortly after publication.

## Highlights
