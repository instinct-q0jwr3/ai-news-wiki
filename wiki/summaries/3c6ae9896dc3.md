# Ask HN: Am I the only one whose AI hype is cooling down a lot?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `anthropic` `google` `agentic-systems` `ai-coding-agents` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

A small Hacker News discussion asks whether enthusiasm for AI coding and research tools is fading after initial excitement. The poster still uses generated code but describes extra work checking research and prefers treating the model as a narrower implementation tool. Replies distinguish coding usefulness from poor organizational data and infrastructure.

These are a handful of self-selected experiences, not adoption statistics or a measured decline in model quality. Reduced spending could reflect price changes, changing tasks or a higher standard as well as lower perceived value. The discussion cannot establish a broad industry trend or the best workflow for every developer.

## Highlights

- Poster separates coding speed from research reliability
- Verification and repeated refinement are described as costs
- Replies offer different explanations
- Sample is small and self-selected
- Spending changes have several possible causes
- No industry-wide adoption trend is established
