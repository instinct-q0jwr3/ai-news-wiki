# Show HN: An API for word timestamps from speech, 75% within 20ms

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `gemini` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Yarn introduces Quebec, a speech-alignment API combining constrained decoding with a small boundary-refinement model. Its report separates missing timestamps, word-edge timing error and the practical result of deleting a word in an editor.

The page reports seventy-seven percent of boundaries within twenty milliseconds of human labels, newer than the feed headline’s seventy-five percent. Results are vendor-run and focused on English speech; agreement with human labels and performance on one editing rule do not establish universal accuracy.

## Highlights

- Decoder search is bounded by rough transcript timing
- Small model refines local word boundaries
- Training uses public and privately labeled recordings
- Page figure differs from the feed headline
- Timing and bad-cut metrics measure different outcomes
- English evaluation does not prove multilingual quality
