# Show HN: We compressed a 31B video+audio diffusion model to 14B, 6 steps, INT8

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Video Rebirth released HyperFlow H3 Flash, a compressed few-step version of MiniMax-H3 that generates video with synchronized stereo audio. The Show HN title says the 31B model was cut to 14B, run in 6 steps, with INT8 weights.

The repo gives a decision rule: use GGUF on PCs with 64 GB of RAM or less; use INT8 on data-center GPUs or 24 GB+ RTX cards with at least 69 GiB of system RAM. It needs an Ampere or newer NVIDIA GPU and diffusers 0.40+, and the first run downloads about 90 GB (about 68 GB with fp8 text encoder). Modes include text-to-video, keyframe and reference-image inputs, with multi-GPU on Linux or WSL2.

## Highlights

- Few-step, compressed version of MiniMax-H3 with joint video and audio.
- Title claims 31B to 14B parameters, 6 steps, INT8.
- GGUF for 64 GB RAM or less; INT8 for bigger systems.
- Needs an Ampere or newer NVIDIA GPU; first-run download is about 90 GB.
- Supports text, keyframe and reference-based generation.
