# A deep dive on Amazon's robotics efforts, resulting in 1M+ active warehouse robots; Amazon is still the world's 2nd-largest corporate employer with 1.5M workers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `amazon` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Ars Technica's deep dive on Amazon robotics says no organization runs more robots: over a million in warehouses, supporting tens of millions of same-day and next-day deliveries, with the fleet set to grow as Amazon builds factories for more capable robots. It is still the world's second-largest corporate employer, with more than 1.5 million workers.

Executives say robots take on the hardest, most fatiguing tasks. Researchers and labor groups are less sure: George Mason's Brian Greenwood notes that collaborative robots work physically close to people and can set the pace of work, a break from past automation that happened away from workers. Basis: the first part of the article.

## Highlights

- More than 1 million robots in Amazon warehouses, and the fleet is growing.
- Amazon still employs over 1.5 million people.
- Amazon says robots absorb the riskiest tasks.
- Researchers warn collaborative robots can dictate the pace of human work.
