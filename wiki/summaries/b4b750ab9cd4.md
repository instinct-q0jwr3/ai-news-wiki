## Summary

Dario Amodei responded publicly to Nathan Calvin's critical investigation of Anthropic, thanking him for scrutiny he calls healthy for a free society and agreeing that some cited examples were mistakes - while rejecting the post's thrust. His core defense: most of what Calvin criticizes follows from Anthropic's long-held 'race to the top' theory of change, showing responsible frontier development to raise the industry's bar.

Amodei argues the strategy only works from the frontier, and claims Anthropic's pro-safety, commercially costly actions over the past year far outweigh the items on Calvin's list - which he also disputes in part.
