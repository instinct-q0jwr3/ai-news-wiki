# Big Tech uses residual value guarantees for off-balance-sheet AI spend

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `nvidia`

## Summary

Big Tech is increasingly using residual value guarantees to keep AI spending off balance sheets, the Financial Times reports, giving Nvidia and Broadcom a way to support their customers' purchases. Wall Street has found a new way to turn tech giants' credit strength into cheaper funding for the AI build-out.

## Highlights

- Residual value guarantees are increasingly used for off-balance-sheet AI spend.
- The structures let Nvidia and Broadcom support customer purchases.
- Wall Street is converting Big Tech credit strength into cheaper AI funding.
