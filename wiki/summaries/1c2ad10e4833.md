## Summary

Gary Marcus comments on reports of Jensen Huang's resignation and Colette Kress taking over temporarily. He credits Huang's role in shifting Nvidia from games toward AI, while arguing that conflicts, political exposure and financing dependencies made the situation dangerous. His judgments about blame and future industry effects are opinion; underlying allegations should not be treated as established merely because the commentary repeats them.
