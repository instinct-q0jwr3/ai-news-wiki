# New AI Is More Than Twice as Good at Diagnosing Diseases as Doctors

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Microsoft's AI Diagnostic Orchestrator (MAI-DxO) diagnoses difficult cases more than twice as accurately as practicing generalist physicians while ordering fewer tests, per the paper 'Sequential Diagnosis with Language Models' - first posted as an arXiv preprint last year and now published in peer-reviewed Science. Suleyman cautioned The Verge that real-world deployment still requires clinical testing, and Microsoft says that testing begins soon under AI health lead Dr. Dominic King.

The peer-reviewed publication is the milestone: the 2x claim moves from preprint to journal scrutiny, making MAI-DxO the strongest published evidence yet for AI diagnosis surpassing doctors on hard cases - with the important caveat that benchmark cases and clinical reality differ, which the planned trials are meant to bridge.

## Highlights

- MAI-DxO diagnoses difficult cases 2x+ more accurately than practicing doctors, per Science-published paper
- The system also ordered fewer tests and procedures

- Strongest peer-reviewed evidence yet for AI diagnosis exceeding physicians on hard cases
