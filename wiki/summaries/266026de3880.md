# Salesforce announces plans to acquire Cresta, a provider of AI-powered contact center automation software; source: Salesforce agreed to pay $2.7B for Cresta

## Summary

The Information reports Salesforce is acquiring Cresta, a provider of AI-powered contact-center automation, for $2.7 billion. The deal extends Salesforce's recent AI acquisition spree and is another consolidation move in enterprise customer-service AI, where incumbent suites are buying agent capabilities rather than building them.

## Highlights

- Salesforce to acquire contact-center AI vendor Cresta for a reported $2.7B, per The Information
- Enterprise suite vendors keep buying agentic customer-service capabilities
