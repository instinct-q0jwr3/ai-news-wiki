# Power your agents: Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar is now generally available

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `gemini` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar is now generally available in Gemini Enterprise, a week after the Gemini 3.8 Live and Extended Thinking announcements. First previewed at Google Cloud Next 2026, the interactive visual persona is, Google says, officially ready for enterprise production.

Google frames the launch around quality: Gemini 3.8 Live provides a native speech-to-speech foundation for fluid, responsive dialogue, and Live Avatar adds an interactive visual presence on top, as enterprise voice AI evolves beyond basic speed and cost metrics.

## Highlights

- Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar is GA in Gemini Enterprise.
- First previewed at Google Cloud Next 2026.
- Native speech-to-speech foundation for fluid dialogue.
- Live Avatar adds an interactive visual persona.
- Google pitches interaction quality over speed and cost metrics.
