# Google DeepMind's AI hurricane forecasting model outperformed conventional models by accurately predicting that Hurricane Isaias would make landfall in Florida

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `google` `gemini`

## Summary

When Hurricane Isaias formed in early October, conventional forecast models initially sent it toward southeastern Louisiana. Google DeepMind's AI model called the actual landfall - the western Florida Panhandle near Destin, where it arrived as a Category 2 - right from the start. Veteran Miami meteorologist Michael Lowry now calls DeepMind's model the gold standard of the last few years.

Forecasters interviewed say AI is rapidly transforming hurricane prediction, with machine-learning models increasingly used alongside the physics-based suites. The Isaias call matters because track errors of that size are what evacuation decisions are made on - and because the AI model outperformed on a live, consequential storm rather than a retrospective benchmark.
