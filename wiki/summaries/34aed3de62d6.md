# Tell agents the why, not just the how

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`seangoedecke` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Sean Goedecke's advice piece: tell agents the why, not just the how. He illustrates with his own real prompt to a coding agent: it opens with the long-term goal (a local tool that hides AI content from pages he loads) and the short-term goal (the best detection model that runs on a MacBook without killing battery), his current guess (Pangram's EditLens 3B or a smaller Roberta, quantized), the infrastructure available (Runpod via MCP for experiments too heavy for local), and an explicit invitation to propose a better path.

The argument: agents make better decisions when they know what success looks like and why, because goal-level context lets them correct your plan instead of just executing it - the same reason senior engineers want context, not tickets.

## Highlights

- Goedecke: give agents goals and rationale, not just instructions
- His example prompt states long-term goal, short-term goal, current guess, and available infra
- Explicit invitation: 'if you can see a better way to achieve my goals, let me know'
- The why lets agents correct the plan, not just execute it
- Same principle as briefing senior engineers: context, not tickets
