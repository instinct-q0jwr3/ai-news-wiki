# CERN and Mistral sign a statement of intent to establish a strategic partnership

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `mistral`

## Summary

CERN and Mistral have signed a statement of intent to establish a long-term strategic partnership focused on developing foundation models for scientific discovery. The partnership combines CERN's expertise in fundamental physics, scientific data and large-scale computing with Mistral's model-training capabilities, with an initial focus on high-energy physics and ambitions to serve the broader scientific community. For Mistral it is a flagship European institutional anchor; for CERN, a path to frontier-model capability tuned to its data.
