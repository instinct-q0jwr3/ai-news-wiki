# DietPi v10.8 has been released

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

DietPi v10.8 was released, announced on Hacker News. The lightweight Debian-based distribution adds a WireGuard management tool, name-based software installation, an optimized HAProxy package, CD/DVD/Blu-ray drive support on Quartz64, ARM and UEFI support for UTM virtual machines, and fixes across its drive manager, cloudshell, and several self-hosted apps including Immich's machine learning component.

## Highlights

- DietPi v10.8: new WireGuard server/client management tool
- Software installed by name instead of numeric IDs
- UTM VMs gain ARM and UEFI support
- Marginal AI relevance; surfaced via the HN feed
