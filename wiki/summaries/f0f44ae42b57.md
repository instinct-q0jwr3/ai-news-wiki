# Ask HN: How to deal with shipping AI-written code you don't understand?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `meta`

## Summary

An Ask HN thread sparked by a LinkedIn post from a Meta engineer who says he has never felt so uncomfortable doing his job: AI now writes the code, and it all feels like a black box. Checking every line would take forever at the speed teams are expected to ship, so engineers end up owning systems they understand at a high level but not in detail - and he suspects this is becoming the new standard, which cuts against every instinct of the profession. The question to HN is how others deal with shipping AI-written code they don't fully understand: where reviewers draw the line, what practices (tests, staged rollouts, ownership norms) keep the risk acceptable, and whether the discomfort is a signal or just the cost of the new workflow.
