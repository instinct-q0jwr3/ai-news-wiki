## Summary

Simon Willison quotes Ted Sanders on model naming and scaling: LLM performance is multidimensional - the best math model may not tell the best jokes - yet a single variable, model size, explains much of the variation. Bigger models are more performant and reliable, cost more per token, and recall more esoteric facts.

Sanders confirms the ordering of OpenAI's model line (Luna < Terra < Sol < Astra) and explains modern version numbers as encoding release order, relationship to sibling releases, and magnitude of improvement - a 6.1 follows 5.6, shares training data and personality with other 6.1s, and isn't a big enough leap to merit a 7.

## Highlights

- Sanders: size explains much of performance variation
- Bigger = more reliable, pricier per token, better esoteric recall
