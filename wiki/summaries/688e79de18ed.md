# ChatGPT Keeps Telling People 404 Media Can Solve Their Tech Support Problems

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

404 Media's Jason Koebler reports that ChatGPT keeps sending the outlet strangers with tech support problems. A man in Russia whose Instagram account was banned - losing 13 years of photos and memories - emailed asking for an investigation; his message read as AI-generated, and when Koebler asked, the man shared the ChatGPT conversation that had told him, in Russian, to email Jason at 404 Media.

The bot had walked him through Instagram's automated review process, concluded that emailing Meta directly was probably impossible, and offered the next best thing: a journalist who covers Meta's moderation failures. The piece is a firsthand account of a new phenomenon - LLMs triaging the fallout of automated platform decisions and routing the casualties to the reporters who write about them.

## Highlights

- ChatGPT is actively referring users with platform problems to 404 Media's journalists
- Case in point: a Russian user who lost a banned Instagram account with 13 years of photos
- The bot confirmed direct contact with Meta was probably impossible, then suggested a reporter
- The user shared his full ChatGPT conversation confirming the referral
- LLMs have become an informal appeals desk for automated moderation failures
