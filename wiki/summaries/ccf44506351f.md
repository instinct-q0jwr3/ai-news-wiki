# Center for Technology & Statecraft: Chinese fabs had acquired ~343 DUVi tools by early 2026, with ~270 from ASML; DUVi can be adapted to make 7nm chips and HBM

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

SCMP reports on a Center for Technology & Statecraft study: Chinese-owned fabs had acquired about 343 immersion DUV lithography systems by early 2026, around 270 from ASML. DUV is less advanced than EUV but can be adapted for 7-nm logic and advanced memory used in AI chips.

Former US export control officials Nicholas Brown and Saif Khan urge Washington and allies to stop further exports, feeding the debate over a total blockade.

## Highlights

- ~343 immersion DUV tools in Chinese fabs by early 2026, ~270 from ASML.
- DUV can be adapted to 7-nm logic and advanced memory.
- Authors want further exports shut down.
- Report published late September by a new Washington group.
