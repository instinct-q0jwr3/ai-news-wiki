# Introducing Our Smallest, Most Efficient Model: Muse Ember

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`simon-willison` `openai` `anthropic` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems` `ai-price-war`

## Summary

Simon Willison covers Meta's Muse Ember, its smallest model, which Meta says sits on the Artificial Analysis efficiency frontier: no model scores higher for less per task. For short prompts it costs $0.10 per million input and $0.50 output tokens, the same as GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5, though longer prompts cost more. He rendered pelican SVGs at six reasoning levels; the 'high' level failed to return a full image and quality did not track level.

## Highlights

- Priced like Luna and Haiku 5.5 for short prompts
- Meta claims the efficiency frontier on Artificial Analysis
- Six reasoning levels, pelican test shows uneven results
- Fable 5.2 is about 80x the price
