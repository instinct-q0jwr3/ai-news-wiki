# Apple updates the 24-inch iMac with an M6 chip, with up to a 12-core CPU, up to a 12-core GPU, and a dual 16-core Neural Engine, from $1,499 on November 6

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `apple` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Apple updated the 24-inch iMac with the M6 chip: up to a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU with Neural Accelerators per core, plus a dual 16-core Neural Engine delivering up to 5x faster on-device AI performance than the M4 model. Apple claims up to 18x faster performance than the most popular Intel-based iMac.

The update adds Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, optional 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet, up to 2x faster storage, the 4.5K Retina display, up to four Thunderbolt 4 ports and seven colors. From $1,499, pre-orders October 28, available November 6, running macOS 27 with Siri AI and Apple Intelligence.

## Highlights

- 24-inch iMac gets M6 with per-core Neural Accelerators
- Up to 5x faster on-device AI than M4 iMac
- Wi-Fi 7, optional 2.5Gb Ethernet, seven colors
- From $1,499; pre-order Oct 28, available Nov 6
