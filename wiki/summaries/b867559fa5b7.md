# Claude Fable 5.2 launch offers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic's support page says launch offers give eligible subscribers extra Fable 5.2 usage for a limited time: one to three 'Fable resets' by subscription length, and one week included plus one reset for plans on usage credits.

## Highlights

- Extra Fable usage at launch for eligible plans
- 1-3 resets depending on tenure
- Credit-based plans get a week plus one reset
