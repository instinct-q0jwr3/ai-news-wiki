# AI has made it 'close to impossible' to assess university coursework

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

England's Office for Students warns that widespread AI use is weakening the reliability of coursework as evidence of learning. The Guardian reports that the regulator wants universities to use more supervised exams, oral presentations and other assessments that can establish a student's own knowledge.

The issue is assessment design rather than simply catching individual cheating. The regulator says unreliable measures can threaten registration conditions and lead to sanctions. Reported alternatives also include critiquing AI output and assessing the process of producing work, alongside AI-free formats.

## Highlights

- Regulator questions validity of unsupervised coursework
- More in-person and oral assessment encouraged
- Degree credibility is the stated concern
- Registration sanctions are possible
- Some institutions assess process or critique of AI output
