# Scale Labs and CAIS' Remote Labor Index: Fable 5.2 completes 41.3% of freelance projects at professional quality, above Opus 5.5's 21.3% and GPT-6 Astra's 20.8%

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `agentic-systems` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

On the Remote Labor Index from Scale Labs and CAIS, Claude Fable 5.2 completes 41.3% of real freelance projects at professional quality, far ahead of its predecessor and competitors. When the benchmark launched about a year ago the top system, Manus, scored 2.5%, so the leader is now over 16 times higher.

## Highlights

- Fable 5.2: 41.3% on RLI
- Launch-era leader scored 2.5%
- Measures client-acceptable freelance work
