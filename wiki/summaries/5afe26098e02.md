## Highlights

- Runs a sliced 0.5B 1.58-bit (BitNet) LLM across 7 ESP32-S3 boards
- Master node: BPE tokenizer plus INT4 embeddings (~14MB in flash)
- Six compute nodes each run 4 transformer blocks (1.58-bit attention + MLP, PSRAM KV cache)
- Nodes communicate over a high-speed SPI daisy-chain
- Master finishes with final RMSNorm, tied LM head, and greedy sampling
- Repo ships firmware, workflow guide, and architecture diagrams
