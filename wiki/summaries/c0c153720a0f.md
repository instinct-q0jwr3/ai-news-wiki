# New York Times adds more AI features to its Cooking app. Users aren't thrilled

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The New York Times added an AI recipe-modification tool to its Cooking app - ingredient substitutions and other tweaks, rolling out gradually on iOS for subscribers - and a slice of the readership is unhappy. NYT Cooking senior editor Genevieve Ko explains the features came from product and engineering colleagues seeing AI's potential for requests users already had.

The coverage frames the backlash with a cooking metaphor: one new ingredient can ruin the stew. Users' objections center on AI touching a product they valued for human editorial craft, even when the modification feature is opt-in.

## Highlights

- NYT Cooking adds AI recipe modifications, iOS subscribers first
- Early usage dominated by ingredient substitution requests
- Reader backlash: AI seen as spoiling a human-crafted product
- Gradual rollout to monitor performance and usage
