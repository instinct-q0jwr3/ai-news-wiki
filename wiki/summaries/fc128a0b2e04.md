# World Bank says 'small AI' could transform developing countries

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-18 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The World Bank is urging developing countries to build 'small AI' - specialized models trained on narrower datasets for specific purposes that can run on a mobile phone with low connectivity. Chief knowledge officer Paschal Donohoe argues AI could accelerate development for emerging economies, but only if adapted to local needs.

His claim is sweeping: economic development that could have taken centuries now has the potential to happen in decades, if countries invest in models suited to their infrastructure rather than waiting for Silicon Valley's ever more sophisticated consumer products.

## Highlights

- World Bank pushes 'small AI' for developing countries
- Small models run on phones with low connectivity
- Trained on narrow datasets for specific local purposes
- Donohoe: development could compress from centuries to decades
- Condition: AI must be adapted to local needs, not imported wholesale
