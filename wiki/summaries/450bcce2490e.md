# Investing in the public benefit

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-18 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

Anthropic is committing 5% of its shares to a new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, subject to stockholder approval, with the stake growing automatically: an additional 2% at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline. Together with co-founder and staff pledges, the total commitment reaches roughly 20% of shares.

The company explains the split: safety research, interpretability work and nonprofit discounts stay inside the company because they mix public benefit with for-profit value, while the Fund gains flexibility for bigger moves - large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations. The first named area is labor-market impacts, where Anthropic has already committed $200M.

## Highlights

- 5% of Anthropic shares committed to a new Public Benefit Fund
- Stake grows 2% per 50% valuation increase above a baseline
- Roughly 20% total with co-founder and staff pledges
- Fund targets large donations and direct distributions, e.g. labor-market impacts
- $200M already committed to studying worker displacement
- In-company mission work (safety research, nonprofit discounts) stays in-house
