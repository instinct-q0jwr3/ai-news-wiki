# Vitalize, an AI platform that helps hospitals manage labor and capacity in real time, raised a $31M Series A led by Oak HC/FT, with YC and others participating

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Vitalize raised a $31 million Series A led by Oak HC/FT, with Norwest Ventures, .406 Ventures, Constellation Ventures, Y Combinator and the MemorialCare Innovation Fund participating, for an AI platform that unifies hospital staffing, scheduling, flow and capacity in real time. Co-founder and CEO Veeraj Shah calls hospital staffing the most valuable healthcare task to tackle: labor is 50-60% of hospital operating costs and approaches $1 trillion in annual US spend.

Shah saw the problem firsthand as a medical student and inside staffing offices and command centers, where nurses and managers burn hours daily on spreadsheets, group texts and phone calls - inefficiencies that ripple into admissions, throughput and operations. Vitalize aims to rewire that manual work into a real-time operations layer.
