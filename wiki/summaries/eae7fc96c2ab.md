# PicoJool, which is developing AI data center interconnects based on vertical cavity surface emitting lasers, raised a $27.5M Series A led by Socratic Partners

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-27 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

SiliconANGLE reports that PicoJool, a startup backed by former Intel CEO Pat Gelsinger, raised $27.5M in a Series A led by Socratic Partners with participation from Hudson River Trading, bringing its total funding to $39.5M after a $12M seed led by Gelsinger's Playground Global last November.

PicoJool targets the strain AI data centers put on infrastructure, positioning its technology to ease the growing power and connectivity demands of AI build-outs.

## Highlights

- PicoJool raises $27.5M Series A led by Socratic Partners
- Backed by former Intel CEO Pat Gelsinger (Playground Global led the seed)
- Total funding now $39.5M
- Mission: ease the infrastructure strain of AI data centers
