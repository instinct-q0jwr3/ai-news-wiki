# Let Gemini tackle repetitive chores on your Mac

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `google` `gemini`

## Summary

Google announces background computer use in Gemini’s Mac app, with a live preview, chosen application access and interruption controls. Its main description says users approve key actions rather than establishing that every individual click requires approval.

Rollout is described as English-language access in the United States. The announcement is not a reliability audit or proof of access for other regions, and no application permissions were changed in this pass.

## Highlights

- Feature spans local apps files and websites
- Background window preserves foreground work
- User controls include pause and selected access
- Main text refers to key-action approval
- Region and language restrict initial rollout
- No account or device was configured
