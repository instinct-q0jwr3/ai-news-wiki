# Cyanogen's co-founder and two others debut Kora, a Linux desktop OS that integrates AI models at a system level, set for a 2027 launch on Snapdragon and x86 PCs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Cyanogen co-founder Kirt McMaster and two others launched Kora, a Linux desktop OS for 'the AI native', aimed at creators and developers. Its idea is to bring AI models together at the system level and share context across tools and providers.

## Highlights

- Linux desktop OS for AI-native power users
- System-level integration of multiple AI models
- Not aimed at a mass market
