# Apple releases iOS 27.2, with Siri AI enhancements for recalling past conversations, understanding more personal context, supporting five new languages, more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `apple`

## Summary

Apple released iOS 27.2 with a significant Siri AI expansion on iPhone 16 and later (plus iPhone 15 Pro models): Siri can now recall past conversations - ask it about the restaurants it recommended last week - and draw on more personal sources for answers, including photos attached in Messages, Calendar event attendees, voicemails, and better Safari history and Reading List results.

The update adds an Excluded Apps control to choose which apps Siri, Search and suggestions can access from one setting, granular text selection inside Siri responses, and new language support: French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. Siri AI remains unavailable in the EU and mainland China. Messages gains context-aware suggestions, and Shared Albums in Photos get caption editing and duplicate detection.

## Highlights

- iOS 27.2 lets Siri recall past conversations, like restaurant recommendations from last week
- Siri AI now draws on Messages photos, Calendar attendees, voicemails, Safari history and Reading List
- New Excluded Apps setting controls which apps Siri, Search and suggestions can see
- Language support added: French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish; still not in the EU or mainland China
- Also: granular text selection in Siri responses and context suggestions in Messages
