# Jev handed our LLM the wrong page a tenth as often as our embeddings

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `typesafe`

## Summary

A team building an AI receptionist for a maintenance company describes retrieval failures: a caller asking about a leaking plumber's repair was handed the wrong web page by embedding search. They report that Jev, TypeSafe's judge, handed the model the wrong page about a tenth as often as embeddings did. Basis: the opening of the post.

## Highlights

- Embedding retrieval gave wrong pages for real caller questions.
- Reports a roughly 10x lower wrong-page rate using Jev.
- Use case: AI receptionist for a multi-trade company.
