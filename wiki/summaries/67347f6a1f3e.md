# The IMF lifts its 2026 global GDP growth forecast to 3.1%, up from 3% in July, citing an AI boom that offsets Middle East war disruptions and high energy costs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

The IMF raised its 2026 global growth forecast to 3.1% from 3% in July, saying the AI investment boom offsets energy-cost damage tied to the Middle East war, while warning that resilience is reaching its limits. Growth was 3.5% last year; the 2027 forecast stays at 3.4%, with 2027 inflation at 4.1%. Counselor Silvana Tenreyro said the economy has been resilient but that resilience is reaching its limits (CNBC).

## Highlights

- 2026 global growth forecast 3.1%, up from 3%.
- AI-driven investment boom cited as an offset to energy shocks.
- 2027 growth held at 3.4%; inflation 4.1%.
- IMF warns resilience is nearing its limits.
