## Summary

Nvidia's board announcement says Huang has resigned as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress will retain her finance role while acting as interim chief executive, and the board will search internal and external candidates for a permanent successor.

The statement also names Harvey C. Jones as board chair. It establishes the company's announced governance actions, not the truth of every allegation discussed elsewhere or the eventual strategic direction under new leadership.

## Highlights

- Company announces immediate resignation
- Kress combines interim chief executive and CFO roles
- Permanent successor search is planned
- Jones is named board chair
- Statement does not settle surrounding allegations
