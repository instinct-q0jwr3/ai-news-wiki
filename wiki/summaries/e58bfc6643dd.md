# Instinct in group chats

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-21 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Instinct can now be added to group chats: a new Instinct joins the group and works for everyone in it, including friends who don't use the product. Noah Shinn's announcement lists the use cases - planning trips, coordinating roommate logistics, buying tickets the moment they go on sale and splitting costs, running a fantasy league with standings and lineup reminders, sorting carpools from everyone's schedules, and organizing holiday meals with who's-bringing-what tracking.

The post stresses permissions design: your personal Instinct asks before connecting to a group's Instinct, you choose which groups to trust and can revoke trust anytime; the group Instinct has no direct access to personal accounts - your own Instinct gates what gets shared; and if someone new joins a group, pending replies are held until you approve sharing with the expanded group. Group chats are live for early access users, with a broader rollout coming.

## Highlights

- Instinct can now join group chats and work for the whole group, no account needed for friends.
- Use cases: trip planning, roommate logistics, ticket drops, fantasy leagues, carpools, holiday dinners.
- Your personal Instinct asks before connecting to a group; trust is per-group and revocable.
- The group Instinct cannot touch personal accounts directly - your Instinct gates sharing.
- New member joins a group: pending replies are held until you approve the wider audience.
- Live for early access users; general rollout coming.
