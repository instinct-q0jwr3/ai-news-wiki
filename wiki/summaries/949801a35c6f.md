## Summary

A one-line note from Simon Willison: a computer scientist who refuses to find anything interesting about LLMs right now is like a geneticist who refuses to find anything interesting about the newly opened Jurassic Park. He gives the skeptic a voice - 'pfft, it's just frog DNA. And they deliberately let them eat people for the marketing.' - which makes the joke carry both readings at once: the technology is genuinely astonishing, and the skeptics' objections may be entirely accurate while completely missing the scale of the thing in front of them.

## Highlights

- Willison's analogy: ignoring LLMs as a computer scientist is like a geneticist ignoring Jurassic Park
- The imagined skeptic concedes the dangers ('they deliberately let them eat people for the marketing') and still sounds absurd
- The note captures both the validity of AI skepticism and the futility of refusing to engage with the technology
