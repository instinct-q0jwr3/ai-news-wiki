# Tencent launches Hy Image 3.5 Preview, saying during internal testing the image model achieved results on par with ByteDance's Seedream 5.0 Pro

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `alibaba`

## Summary

Tencent has launched Hy Image 3.5 Preview, its latest image-generation model, saying that in internal testing it achieved results on par with ByteDance's Seedream 5.0 Pro. Bloomberg notes the release landed the same day rival Alibaba kicked off its AI conference, underlining the pace of competition among Chinese AI labs.

## Highlights

- Tencent launched Hy Image 3.5 Preview, described as its latest and best image-generation model.
- Tencent claims internal-test results on par with ByteDance's Seedream 5.0 Pro.
- The launch coincided with the start of Alibaba's AI conference.
