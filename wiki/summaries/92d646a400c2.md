# An Anthropic hiring manager says he stopped giving coding interviews because Claude is better at them and tells junior engineers that "hunger beats tenure"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

Felix Rieseberg, a hiring manager at Anthropic, writes that he has stopped giving traditional coding interviews because Claude now solves them better than candidates - small coding challenges, once a weak but useful predictor of team success, no longer measure anything meaningful. Their most impactful builders succeed regardless of test performance.

His advice to junior engineers is that 'hunger beats tenure': impact is less bounded by accumulated framework knowledge, and he sees newer engineers with AI flying past better coders with more experience. He leans on the newcomer's advantage - fresh eyes spotting friction veterans stopped noticing, citing Patrick Collison at Stripe and Orson Welles on ignorance as confidence.
