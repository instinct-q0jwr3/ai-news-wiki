# Genie 4 is now available

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Google announces an updated Project Genie experience with Genie 4, live prompting, native audio and a gallery for sharing and remixing generated worlds. An accompanying agent coordinates models to help construct scenes, while Genie 3 remains an option with different capabilities.

Access is rolling out to eligible Ultra subscribers, with a separate Pro waitlist. Google explicitly identifies latency, physical interaction, audio precision and scene permanence as ongoing research problems. The announcement is not evidence of a generally reliable simulator or universal availability.

## Highlights

- Genie 4 adds live world edits and audio
- Gallery supports sharing and remixing
- Agent helps coordinate scene creation
- Access depends on plan and eligibility
- Company acknowledges persistence and physics limits
