# US data center operators and power developers are repurposing jet engines to secure power in as little as two years, bypassing multi-year grid-connection delays

## Summary

US datacenter operators and power developers are repurposing jet engines - pulled from the aircraft boneyards of the Sonoran desert - to secure power in as little as two years, bypassing multi-year grid-connection delays, per the New York Times. The power crunch's improvisation era continues: when turbines are sold out through 2028, yesterday's engines become tomorrow's baseload.

## Highlights

- Datacenter power developers repurpose retired jet engines for power in as little as two years, skipping grid queues, per NYT
