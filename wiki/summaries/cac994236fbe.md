# Introducing CoreWeave ARIA: AI Research and Iteration Agent

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-09-29 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

CoreWeave introduces ARIA (AI Research and Iteration Agent), a coding agent built into Weights & Biases with deep platform knowledge. ARIA reads your experiments, builds live visualizations to support its analysis, and starts with full project context. The pitch is a full autoresearch loop: you describe what you want to learn ('does dropout after attention help my overfitting?'), ARIA forms a hypothesis, writes the config, launches the experiment via W&B Launch on your compute, evaluates results against baseline, and updates the workspace with comparisons - running the whole research cycle rather than just analyzing it.

## Highlights

- CoreWeave ARIA: a research agent built into Weights & Biases
- Reads experiments, builds live visualizations, starts with full project context
- Runs the full loop: hypothesis, config, launch, evaluation, comparison
- Launches experiments through W&B Launch on the team's own compute
- Example prompt: does dropout after attention layers help with overfitting?
