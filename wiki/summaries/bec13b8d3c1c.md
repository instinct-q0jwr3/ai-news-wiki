# Aided by AI, young Chinese launched 7M+ one-person startups in 2025

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Young people in China launched more than 7 million one-person startups in 2025, up roughly 42% from 2024, as they turned to AI tools to escape a bleak job market, the Wall Street Journal reports. Fed up with the rat race, many are trying their luck with solo AI companies despite brutal competition.

## Highlights

- 7M+ one-person startups were launched in China in 2025, up ~42% year over year.
- AI tools enable the solo-founder trend amid a bleak job market.
- Founders face fierce competition despite the low barrier to entry.
