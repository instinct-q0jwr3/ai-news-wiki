# Intelligence Density

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

A post argues the industry measures AI cost wrong: a cheaper token does not necessarily make a cheaper task, because a model can use more tokens, make more tool calls, or take longer to reach the same result. Tibo, who leads Codex at OpenAI, puts it in pizza terms: each slice can get cheaper while the whole pizza gets more expensive.

The authors say they often find open source models cost more than closed ones over time - because everyone is measuring cost per token. What we actually care about is closer to cost per task, cost per unit of intelligence. They call this Intelligence Density.

## Highlights

- A cheaper token is not necessarily a cheaper task.
- OpenAI's Codex lead: slices get cheaper while the pizza gets pricier.
- Open source models often cost more over time than closed ones.
- The wrong metric: cost per token.
- Proposal: measure cost per task - 'Intelligence Density'.
