# Chaos ensues when AI interviewer meets AI interviewee

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

A YouTuber known for trolling AI job-interview bots, Graham Dot Zip, sent his own AI agent to an interview with him. Earlier videos, some near 2 million views, showed bots accepting gibberish answers as if they were sound.

The result was chaos and the article's moral is not to let a bot negotiate for you. It is mostly entertainment but reflects job seekers' dislike of automated screening.

## Highlights

- A comedian sent his own AI agent to meet an AI interviewer.
- Earlier videos showed interview bots accepting nonsense answers.
