# Abolish AI! Manifesto

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

A manifesto arguing that AI is not a neutral tool but 'a tool of a society that has given up' - designed, by its developers' own admission, to enable population control that subverts existing political apparatuses and to substitute people's capacity to make their own judgements. Optimists who think they can plug into the system without being dominated by it are, the authors say, the real deluded ones.

The text borrows the framework of abolition as a political project: identify an oppressive institution, work to eradicate it, and pair the negation with a positive vision - here, redirecting the faith placed in AI toward a revolutionary project of liberation.

## Highlights

- Positions AI as an instrument of control, not a neutral tool
- Argues AI is designed to substitute human judgement by design
- Frames abolition on the model of slavery abolition: eradicate plus a positive vision
- Calls for redirecting faith in AI into a revolutionary project
- Published on Lobsters; polemic rather than reporting
