Historical company revenue and staffing comparisons lead into Jensen Huang's reinstatement and the tension between equal corporate standards and investors' dependence on a particular leader.

Its argument that AI increases individual economic power is not a measured forecast of employment or a legal finding about allegations discussed in the piece. Sponsor material and unrelated previews at the end are excluded from the analysis.
