# Meta has a new AI pet app called Waddle World

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `meta`

## Summary

Meta has soft-launched Waddle World, a Tamagotchi-style virtual pet app with AI under the hood, currently available only in the Philippines on iOS and Android. Spotted by social media consultant Matt Navarra, the app lets users hatch a pixel pet, feed it and play with it in a deliberately low-pressure 16-bit world: Meta's own description insists it is not a chatbot, not an assistant and not a productivity tool, with no streaks to protect and no scores to chase - and it is flagged 18+.

The launch reads as another data point in Meta's fast iteration on AI companions, and possibly a testbed for something physical: reporting suggests it could be a precursor to a Tamagotchi-like gadget powered by Meta's Muse model.

## Highlights

- Meta launches Waddle World, a 16-bit AI virtual pet, in the Philippines on iOS and Android

- Spotted by Matt Navarra; wider availability unconfirmed
