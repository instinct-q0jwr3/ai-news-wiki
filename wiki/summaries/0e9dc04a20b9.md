# Scuba, which is building a "context engine" designed to serve as a shared memory hub for human workers and AI agents, raised $34M across pre-seed and seed

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Scuba, founded by two former Slack leaders, is building a 'context engine' that acts as a shared memory hub for human workers and AI agents. Their argument: assistants and coding agents make individuals faster but teams still lack shared context and taste (Upstarts Media). Basis: the opening of the article.

## Highlights

- Scuba is a shared-memory 'context engine' for people and agents.
- Founders Noah Desai Weiss and Dolapo Falola came from Slack.
- Bet: team context is the missing layer for AI tools.
