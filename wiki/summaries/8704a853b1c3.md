## Summary

Daring Fireball collects reviews of the iPad Mini with the A20 Pro chip. David Pierce at The Verge says the new Acoustic Display fires sound out of the front panel rather than speaker holes, as some TVs do. He finds it sounds no better, maybe slightly bassier and not worse, and slightly better pointed at the listener, calling it mostly a wash in service of the design.
