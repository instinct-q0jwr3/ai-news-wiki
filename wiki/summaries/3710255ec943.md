# AMD says it will invest $1B in Meta's equity offering, its first Helios rackscale systems are installed at Meta, and its EPYC CPUs power Meta's Muse AI agent

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `nvidia` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Nvidia and AMD committed a combined $4 billion to Meta's equity offering: $3 billion from Nvidia and $1 billion from AMD, following Meta's announcement of a $95 billion raise for AI infrastructure. AMD added that its first Helios rackscale systems are installed and running at Meta and that EPYC CPUs power Meta's Muse AI agent.

Nvidia said Meta will expand Vera Rubin and Vera CPU deployments and deepen partnership on Muse, with Jensen Huang framing agents as 'a new computing paradigm'. The stakes are part of a broader pattern: Nvidia has joined 121 investment rounds this year (up from 13 in 2022), and AMD committed up to $5 billion to Anthropic in July. AMD rose 3% on the news while Meta slipped 1%.
