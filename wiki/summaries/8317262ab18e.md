# Walmart CEO John Furner says the company won't use its AI shopping assistant or electronic shelf labels to change product prices based on a shopper's identity

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

The Financial Times reports that Walmart US CEO John Furner says the company will not use its AI shopping assistant or its electronic shelf labels to change prices based on an individual shopper's identity. The statement draws a public line against personalized/surveillance pricing just as AI shopping agents and digital shelf labels make it technically trivial - a commitment made, notably, before any scandal forced it. The article is paywalled, so this summary is based on the headline and lead captured by the feed.

## Highlights

- Walmart CEO: no identity-based pricing via AI assistant or shelf labels.
- Addresses fears of personalized surveillance pricing.
- Electronic shelf labels plus AI made the practice feasible.
- A pre-emptive public commitment, not a response to scandal.
- FT piece paywalled; summary from headline/lead.
