# How Anthropic ships Claude with Claude: Separating the Brain from the Hands

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`latent.space` `anthropic` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Latent Space interviews Anthropic's heads of Engineering and Product, Katelyn Lesse and Angela Jiang, on how Anthropic ships Claude with Claude. Against the backdrop of the S-1's revealed economics - a swing from -126% losses to 19% GAAP profit in a year - they discuss the Opus 5.5 launch: lower cost than its predecessor, less verbosity, and stronger intelligence per dollar.

The episode's central mental model separates an agent's 'brain' from its 'hands': the model's reasoning versus the harness and sandbox that act. They cover protecting thinking blocks against distillation extraction (and the breaking changes that can impose on customer harnesses), what early-access customers actually contribute, and why good evals remain golden.

## Highlights

- Anthropic eng/product leads on shipping Claude with Claude
- Opus 5.5: cheaper to run, less verbose, more intelligence per dollar
- 'Brain vs hands': separating model reasoning from harness and sandbox
- Preserved thinking blocks fight distillation but can break customer harnesses
