AI chip startup Tenstorrent has raised a $1.5 billion Series E led by Trace Capital and Innovation X at a $6.2 billion valuation - up sharply from the $2.6 billion valuation at which it raised $700 million in 2024. Bloomberg notes the raise follows takeover interest in the company earlier this year. Tenstorrent, led by veteran chip designer Jim Keller, remains one of the best-funded independent challengers to Nvidia's accelerator dominance.
