## Summary

Daring Fireball quotes Benjamin Mayo on X, commenting on Apple promoting behind-the-scenes footage showing that the cross-stitch 'Welcome Home' logo for its October 13 event was stitched by hand: there needs to be a name for the phenomenon where every published piece of art by a company now comes with a proof-of-human video.

Gruber agrees. In the AI era, evidence of human craft is becoming a marketing genre of its own.
