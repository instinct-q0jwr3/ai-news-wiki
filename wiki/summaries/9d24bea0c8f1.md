# Changes to Gemini model access and limits

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `gemini`

## Summary

Google's help page says Gemini Apps will change model availability for personal accounts in October 2026, starting October 9 for users without an AI subscription. AI Plus subscribers will get an email with their date.

Gemini is moving to compute-based usage limits that refresh every 5 hours until a weekly limit, with usage depending on prompt complexity, features and chat length. Paid users get higher limits, and premium models and features use up quota faster. This fits the Reddit claim that free access is shrinking.

## Highlights

- Changes start Oct 9 for users without an AI plan.
- Compute-based limits refresh every 5 hours with a weekly cap.
- Heavier prompts and premium features use more quota.
- Model availability table not captured.
