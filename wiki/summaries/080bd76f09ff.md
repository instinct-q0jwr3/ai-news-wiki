## Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports via Techmeme that leaked documents show OpenAI's adjusted operating margin improved from -150% to -109% between Q2 and Q3, with revenue up about 50% to $10 billion, still far from profitability.

- Revenue about $10B, up 50%
