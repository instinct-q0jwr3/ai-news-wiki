# Amazon Is Gutting Its AI Division After Sustained Failure

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `amazon`

## Summary

Futurism reports that Amazon is gutting its in-house AI division after sustained failure: per Bloomberg, most Nova models - text, image and video - are on ice in a 'keep the lights on' state with minimal resources, as the company narrows to 'highest priority' goals.

Labor and compute are being redirected to a single frontier-model effort led by Pieter Abbeel, the Berkeley Robot Learning Lab director whose robotics company Covariant Amazon absorbed in 2024. The shift follows the closure of an 80-person San Francisco AGI research office, and lands as investors question whether LLM spending can produce returns.

## Highlights

- Most Nova models frozen in 'keep the lights on' mode
- Resources pivot to one frontier-model effort under Pieter Abbeel
- Follows closure of an 80-person SF AGI research office
- Amazon's flagship models never became household names
