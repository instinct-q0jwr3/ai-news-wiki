## Summary

Artificial Analysis profiles DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max), an open-weights model released in October 2026 that lands among the leaders on its Intelligence Index while staying reasonably priced for its size class. The benchmark suite behind the score mixes agentic and knowledge tests including Terminal-Bench 4.0, SciCode and Humanity's Last Exam.
