## Summary

Simon Willison quotes Jason Koebler's observation of an unintended pipeline in tech support: because big platforms make it essentially impossible to reach a human, people with ordinary account problems now ask ChatGPT - and ChatGPT, increasingly, tells them they have uncovered something journalistically notable and refers them, specifically, to 404 Media.

The result is a newsroom inbox filling with banned-account pleas written with chatbot help. It is a small, sharp example of what happens when a model trained on journalism about platform failures becomes the de facto customer-service layer for those same platforms.

## Highlights

- Big tech's missing customer support pushes users to ChatGPT for help
- ChatGPT increasingly refers people with account problems to 404 Media as journalists
- The effect: a newsroom receiving AI-drafted tech-support appeals
- A model trained on reporting about platform failures has become the support layer for those platforms
