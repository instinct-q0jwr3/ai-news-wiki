# Running RAG with a (small) language model in the browser

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

A post tests running RAG with a small language model in the browser and shows why small models hallucinate: SmolLM2-360M, Gemma 3 270M and others confidently suggest absurd GPUs for running Llama 70B. It argues retrieval grounding matters for small local models. Basis: the opening of the post.

## Highlights

- Tiny models give confident but wrong hardware advice.
- Explores browser-based RAG with a small model.
- Retrieval is used to ground the answers.
