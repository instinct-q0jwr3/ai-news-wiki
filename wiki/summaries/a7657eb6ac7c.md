# Analysis: the hacker who targeted South Korean banks is likely Chinese-speaking, financially motivated, and used LLMs and open-source Chinese agentic tool ARTEX

## Summary

CrowdStrike Intelligence analyzes a campaign against South Korean financial organizations that led to data exfiltration. It says the attacker is likely Chinese-speaking and financially motivated, and used LLMs and ARTEX, an open-source Chinese agentic tool.

This is a vendor threat report, so attribution is an assessment. Only the Techmeme excerpt was reviewed.
