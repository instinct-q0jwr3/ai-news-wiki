## Summary

Part one of Ed Zitron's Hater's Guide to AI Debt covers the form of the debt: who is raising it and against what. The hyperscalers - Oracle, Google and Amazon already cashflow negative, Meta close behind - have let AI data-center capex eat nearly every dollar of operating cashflow, and the trillion-plus dollars of revenue backlog added from Anthropic and OpenAI contracts isn't easing the burn. The satirical frame (a CEO screaming at his CFO to just raise debt) captures how little the demand side is questioned.

Alongside them sit the neoclouds: companies conjured to buy AI chips and build data centers, often crypto miners converting their operations, sometimes renting colocation from Core Scientific or Applied Digital, which raise their own debt for buildings and power. Their capex rivals the biggest companies on earth while their revenues are a footnote. Zitron's simplification: hundreds of billions are being invested to maybe, someday, make single-digit billions - if the data centers actually get built and the demand materializes.
