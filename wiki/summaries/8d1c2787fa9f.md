# AI is killing All About Berlin

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-21 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Nicolas Bouliane describes a steep decline in traffic to All About Berlin and the effect on his ability to maintain the site as a livelihood. He attributes the loss to Google AI Overviews and argues that an answer assembled from his work removes both visits and the direct relationship with readers.

This is a publisher’s first-person account, not a controlled estimate of the cause of every lost visit. The extracted text contains both seventy and seventy-five percent, apparently reflecting an update, so the direction and scale are clearer than a single stable figure. Broader claims about the future of search remain the author’s interpretation.

## Highlights

- Publisher describes a major audience loss
- Revenue and maintenance time are linked
- Author attributes the decline to AI answers
- Extract includes an apparent revised percentage
- Personal account does not isolate every causal factor
