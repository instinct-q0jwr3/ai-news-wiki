# NY Gov. Kathy Hochul signs bills banning AI chatbots from using "unsafe features" for minors and requiring security features in internet-connected baby monitors

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed two child-safety tech bills a week before the gubernatorial election: one imposes some of the country's strictest restrictions on AI chatbots interacting with children on sensitive topics including mental health - banning 'unsafe features' - and the other requires internet-connected baby monitors to include security features blocking outside watchers.

The chatbot measure takes effect in the coming months; the baby monitor measure in 90 days. Hochul, leading Republican Bruce Blakeman by 14 points in the latest Siena poll, has made tech regulation - social media restrictions, school phone bans, last year's AI risk disclosure law - a core campaign theme, as states fill the federal void on children's online safety.
