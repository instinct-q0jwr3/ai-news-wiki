# Q&A with Mark Zuckerberg and Alexandr Wang on the Muse app, Muse Wonder, the risks of committing $100B+ to AI, rebooting Meta's AI team, agentic commerce, more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

## Summary

In a Cheeky Pint conversation with John Collison, Mark Zuckerberg and Alexandr Wang discuss Muse Wonder, Meta's new model, and the Muse app as Meta's AI infrastructure passes five gigawatts. They describe the complete talent reboot behind Meta Superintelligence Labs and the strain of committing $100B+ in capex before knowing the model would work.

Other topics: Wonder's architecture (active memory, multimodal perception, frontier coding), why the model is the product, agentic commerce, hardware such as smart glasses, and why AI won't be winner-take-all. Basis: the episode description and opening of the transcript.

## Highlights

- Meta's AI infrastructure is scaling past five gigawatts.
- Zuckerberg describes writing a $100B-$200B check before seeing the models.
- Covers Muse Wonder's memory, multimodal and coding design.
- Also discusses agentic commerce, smart glasses and an AI market that is not winner-take-all.
