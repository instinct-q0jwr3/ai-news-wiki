# Reflection AI releases Apache 2.0-licensed weights for Beam, a 501B-parameter model, trained with RL that penalized unnecessary tokens

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-16 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Reflection AI released Apache 2.0 weights for Beam, its first open model at 501B parameters trained with reinforcement learning. It pitches Beam against Chinese open models, claiming it finishes tasks in fewer tokens and so lowers cost per task.

## Highlights

- 501B open model, Apache 2.0.
- Claims fewer tokens per finished task.
