# Do not fear AI. Fear AI companies

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A widely shared essay argues that the public's exhaustion with AI is being deliberately misdirected: the threat is not the technology but the companies building it. The author contends that AI labs conflate 'AI technology' with 'AI company,' framing self-improvement, containment failures and inevitability as properties of the tech while omitting that these require compute, tools and harvested data the companies themselves provide - and objectives they assign. 'AI is not the real threat to humanity. AI companies are.'

The piece frames the endgame as 'Artificial Employees as a Service': a walled-garden race in which the winner rents out replacements for human workers. It calls for instinctive, organized rejection under the banner 'Reject artificial, find authentic, be human' - stop using the tools, boycott their customers, support open alternatives, and resist through protest and creation. The essay closes on the claim that companies are 'terrified' of a public rejecting their regime at 'this crucial inflection point.'

## Highlights

- Essay thesis: AI companies, not AI technology, are the real threat to humanity.

- Frames the business model as 'Artificial Employees as a Service' - renting out replacements for human workers.
- Accuses labs of lobbying for legislation written in their interest and restrictive to competitors.
- Call to action: 'Reject artificial, find authentic, be human' - boycott tools, favor open alternatives, protest.
