# When Does the US Become a Net Importer of Tokens?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

A short model asks when the US becomes a net importer of tokens. With US AI demand starting at 66 GW in 2027 and tokens per watt improving 2.9x a year, the grid binds only if token demand grows faster than about 2.5x a year. With slowing construction and cancellations, the model puts the switch between 2028 and 2030.

## Highlights

- Break-even token growth about 2.5x a year
- Efficiency gains of 2.9x a year in tokens per watt
- Net importer of tokens between 2028 and 2030 in the model
