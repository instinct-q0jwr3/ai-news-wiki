Today, we’re releasing Timestral, our first family of time-series foundation models.

Timestral delivers probabilistic, multivariate forecasts and supports past-only and known-future covariates.

## Highlights

- Available in four sizes (5M to 209M parameters), it balances accuracy and efficiency, running efficiently on CPUs and excelling in zero-shot forecasting for diverse domains.
- The model combines synthetic pre-training, covariate-aware attention, and long-context learning, with fine-tuning options for domain adaptation via Mistral Forge.
- Our largest model, at just 209M parameters, ranks first on all three of the field's main public benchmarks, GIFT-Eval, TIME, and fev-bench, outperforming the previous state of the art with only two-thirds of the model parameters.
- Our family of models sits on the Pareto front of forecasting accuracy versus model size, delivering leading performance at each size.
- Time series foundation models bring the pre-training approach to time series.
- We evaluated Timestral on the three main public forecasting benchmarks: GIFT-Eval, TIME and fev-bench.
- Timestral has been optimised as a family of four models: 5M, 28M, 123M and 209M parameters.
