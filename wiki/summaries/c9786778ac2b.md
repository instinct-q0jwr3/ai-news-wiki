# LanceDB, which makes a multimodal lakehouse for AI training, built on the open format Lance, raised a $57.5M Series B led by Bessemer and Innovation Endeavors

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

SiliconANGLE reports LanceDB raised a $57.5 million Series B to expand a multimodal data platform built on the open Lance format. Its account emphasizes preparing and retrieving large training datasets while leaving customers’ underlying data in existing storage.

The article describes company and customer claims about reducing data preparation and infrastructure work, not an independent benchmark. The financing supports development plans; it does not itself establish faster training or better model quality for every workload.

## Highlights

- Report identifies a Series B funding round
- Platform combines structured and large media data
- Lance format targets record-level access
- Company describes data remaining in customer storage
- Customer examples are attributed rather than reproduced
- Financing is not technical validation
