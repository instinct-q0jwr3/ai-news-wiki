# Meta says it worked with Apple to make Muse Spark Open run locally on M5 Ultra Mac Studios, letting users pair two to four Studios to code or do agentic work

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `apple` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Meta and Apple announced a collaboration so developers can run Muse Spark Open locally on Apple's top Mac Studios with M5 Ultra. The article frames it as a thaw between Tim Cook's and Mark Zuckerberg's companies now that John Ternus leads Apple, with AI as common ground.

## Highlights

- Meta and Apple tuned Muse Spark Open for local use on M5 Ultra Macs.
- Seen as a sign of cooperation under Apple's new CEO.
