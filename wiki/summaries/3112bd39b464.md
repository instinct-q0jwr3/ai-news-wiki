# Anthropic launches Personal Finance in Claude, letting users connect bank and credit card accounts via Plaid with read-only access to analyze spending trends

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `anthropic`

## Summary

ZDNET reports Anthropic launched Personal Finance in Claude. Users link any number of bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid with read-only access and ask things like where they spent money this month or whether they can afford a vacation in six months. It mirrors OpenAI's ChatGPT Finances, launched in May. Anthropic says its April usage report showed financial advice among top topics.

For connected accounts only, Claude sees the bank name, account name, type and last four digits, balances, credit limits and up to two years of transactions. Conversations in the Personal Finance hub are not used for training; it is its own mode with its own protections.

## Highlights

- Read-only Plaid connection to banks and cards
- Similar to ChatGPT Finances (May)
- Claude sees balances, limits and up to two years of transactions
- Hub chats are not used for model training
- Response to existing use: finance is a top topic
