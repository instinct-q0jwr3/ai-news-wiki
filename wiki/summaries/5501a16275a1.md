# Mercury 3: Diffusion at scale

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Inception Labs introduces Mercury 3, which it calls the most capable diffusion-based LLM released to date. The claims: performance on par with or better than GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on a range of benchmarks, while generating at 1,068 tokens per second - 4-8x faster than those speed-optimized frontier models. Mercury 3 is more than 40x larger than Mercury 1 yet just as fast, which the company reads as proof that text diffusion scales. Context grows to 1M tokens (from 260K in Mercury 2.5), priced at $0.20 per million input and $0.70 per million output tokens. Early-access partners already run it for voice and chat support, real-time tutoring, data engineering and web navigation.
