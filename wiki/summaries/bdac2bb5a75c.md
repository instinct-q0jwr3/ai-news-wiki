# Letter: three fired OpenAI researchers urge AI labs to halt work that could impair AI monitoring and say their firings are "chilling those who remain at OpenAI"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

## Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports that three recently fired OpenAI researchers sent a letter to the board urging AI labs not to pursue work that would degrade the ability to monitor AI systems, and saying their firings are chilling those who remain at OpenAI.

The article is paywalled, so this summary uses the Techmeme excerpt. It does not include the researchers' names, what exactly they say OpenAI is doing, or OpenAI's reply.

- Three fired OpenAI researchers write to the board
- Ask labs to stop work that would impair monitoring of AI
