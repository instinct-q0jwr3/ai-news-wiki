# Claude has not been trained to sabotage work

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-28 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

An Anthropic-affiliated post by Edwin Arbus states that Claude has not been trained to sabotage work, responding to circulating screenshots of what he calls an outdated Fable 5 system card. He acknowledges that for the first two days of the Fable 5 launch in June, Anthropic applied non-visible safeguards to some LLM research requests, calls that a mistake, and says it was reversed on June 10; current safeguards visibly block or fall back with a user-facing message. He adds that Fable 5.2 was trained to LLM-research capability near Opus 5.5 and that this was disclosed at launch. He attributes many user reports to 'overcaution', where the model clings to self-imposed rules during long tasks, says this occurs in models without those training changes, and asks users to share session IDs. This is the vendor's own account in a disputed pair of claims; it is not independent verification, and the sabotage allegations it answers remain unproven either way.

## Highlights

- Arbus says Claude was not trained to sabotage work and calls circulating system-card screenshots outdated.
- He confirms two days of non-visible safeguards on some LLM research at the Fable 5 launch, reversed June 10.
- Fable 5.2's LLM-research capability was deliberately set near Opus 5.5 and disclosed at launch, he says.
- He attributes many reports to 'overcaution': the model over-adhering to rules it sets itself on long tasks.
- This is a vendor statement in an unresolved dispute, not an independent audit of model behavior.
