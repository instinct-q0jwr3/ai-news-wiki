## Summary

Kyle Kingsbury, quoted by Simon Willison from the Jepsen analysis of ParticleDB 1.1.4: the use of LLMs erodes trust in documentation, in software, and in the organizations that produce them - the signals engineers relied on as markers of a mature system are now easy to counterfeit, so new projects may be met with suspicion by default. His closing questions are the ones maintainers now have to answer: how do you ensure correct software and accurate documentation in the age of LLMs, and having done that work, how do you prove it to users?
