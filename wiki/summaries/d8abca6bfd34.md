# What driving across Europe costs in tolls, vignettes, bridges and fuel

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-11 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Copilot Travel reference page compares what a car driver pays for motorways, vignettes, tolled bridges and fuel across Europe in 2026. France is the most expensive per kilometre, at about 11 euros per 100 km. Eleven countries have free motorways for cars, five of them with tolled bridges, tunnels or city charges, and eight sell a time-limited sticker instead.

The page gives concrete examples: a Helsinki to Costa del Sol motorhome trip of 4,555 km pays 225 euros for the Oresund and Great Belt bridges and 121 euros in French tolls, while Spain's toll-free roads add nothing. Mont Blanc costs 56.40 euros one way and the Brenner 12.50 on top of Austria's vignette. A 60-litre petrol fill costs 65 euros more in Denmark than in Poland. The data is published under CC BY 4.0 with a CSV of tariffs and sources. It is a travel data page with little direct AI content.

## Highlights

- France about 11 euros per 100 km; 11 countries have free motorways for cars, 8 use vignettes.
- Helsinki to Costa del Sol trip: 225 euros for two Nordic bridges, 121 euros French tolls, 0 in Spain.
- Mont Blanc 56.40 euros one way; Brenner 12.50 euros plus Austrian vignette.
- Petrol fill of 60 litres costs 65 euros more in Denmark than in Poland.
