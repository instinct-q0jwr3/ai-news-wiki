# Watermarking in vLLM

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

A technical post explains watermarking for text generated in vLLM. Provenance matters for trust and accountability, and while images and audio can embed watermarks as subtle perturbations, text's discrete nature rules that out - so instead of perturbing the text, the method influences the generation process in a way that is detectable but does not modify the expected output distribution.

The authors frame text watermarking as both a scientific and engineering challenge with requirements to balance: the watermark must not systematically favor certain words or writing styles, and must leave the model's expected output distribution unchanged.

## Highlights

- Text watermarking in vLLM, explained.
- Images and audio take perturbation watermarks; text's discrete nature rules that out.
- Method: influence generation detectably without changing the output distribution.
- A watermark must not favor certain words or writing styles.
- Framed as both a scientific and an engineering challenge.
