# Ask HN: Is it impossible to disable Siri on macOS 27?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `microsoft` `apple`

## Summary

An Ask HN thread documents the poster's failure to fully disable Siri on macOS 27: after turning Siri off in Settings, disabling it and its extensions via Screen Time, and stripping Siri- and Apple Intelligence-related services using the mac-os-debloat guide, a 'Siri' process, the 'Siri AI.app', still runs in Activity Monitor after restart. The poster compares the residue, combined with Apple's Personal Context feature, to Microsoft's Recall.

The comments split between workarounds, commiseration and criticism of Apple's local AI push. One reply invokes Cunningham's Law: claim it is impossible in a Show HN and people will flock to correct you. Others note the local AI stack costs around 10% of storage on base Macs, and that the local/remote handoff has made previously instant Siri tasks sluggish, a two-minute timer taking almost 30 seconds to set.
