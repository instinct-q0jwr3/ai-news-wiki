# OpenAI says it will give its AI cyber defense system Daybreak and GPT-5.6 Sol to the Ukrainian government for free to help it protect civilian infrastructure

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

OpenAI will share its AI cyber defence system Daybreak free with the Ukrainian government to help protect civilian infrastructure such as hospitals and power plants, according to CERT-UA, which recorded nearly 6,000 cyber-attacks in 2025. Under the deal Ukraine also gets access to OpenAI's advanced GPT 5.6 Sol model. Daybreak can quickly identify weaknesses in digital systems and help develop fixes.

George Osborne, former UK chancellor and now Head of OpenAI for Countries, said protecting civilian infrastructure means defending it against both physical and digital attacks. Sophos threat intelligence director Rafe Pilling called the initiative a welcome force multiplier given the volume of Russian offensive operations since 2014, while RUSI's Jamie MacColl noted western tech firms gain valuable data from an active conflict and that Ukraine already has AI tools from OpenAI competitors like Google.
