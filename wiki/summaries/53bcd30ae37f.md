- This has led to internal discussions on the best way to inform the community of the rapid progress to prepare and adapt the field.
- Mathematics is a fundamental science, and novel discoveries may result in a wide range of applications.
- That makes the promise of these capabilities substantial, and their responsible development and deployment important beyond mathematics itself.
- We recognize this and are working through how broader deployment of math-related AI capabilities can be done responsibly.
- To that end, we’re working with mathematicians who have established an independent mathematics advisory group.
- This group will serve as a bridge to the mathematical community and broader public, giving mathematicians a voice in how we move forward.
