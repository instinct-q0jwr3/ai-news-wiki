## Summary

KradleBench evaluates models in dynamic Minecraft tasks across collaboration, economy, conflict, logic and creativity. Its November 20 leaderboard places GPT-6.2 Astra slightly above a skilled human on the aggregate, while the page says the human remains stronger at strategy and conflict. Replays and per-run cost comparisons make different failure modes visible.

This is a benchmark publisher's account, not a universal ranking of intelligence or real-world teamwork. One skilled player is not a population-level human baseline, aggregate scores can hide dimension differences, and models receive structured observations and code-based actions rather than the same interaction mode as a typical player. No runs or grading were independently reproduced.

## Highlights

- Minecraft environment continues while models reason
- Tasks last roughly two to fifteen minutes
- Five dimensions contribute to the aggregate
- Human baseline is a named skilled player
- Human retains advantages in strategy and conflict
- Structured observations and code actions shape the task
- Leaderboard is not a universal intelligence ranking
