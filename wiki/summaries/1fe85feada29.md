# OpenAI, the Partition Principle, and Mathematics

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

Asaf Karagila, a mathematician, explains why he will not send Sam Altman the bottle of whisky he promised on his Problems page, and says readers should be angry at OpenAI over its math release and the partition principle result. He says he is not against AI as a tool and uses it for summaries or proofreading, but avoids it for mathematics until a framework exists.

He argues the field should decide how to judge contributions, for example whether chat logs must be shared, noting arXiv's rate limits and AI statements. The excerpt sets up the complaint but the full argument about the partition principle was not reviewed.
