# Muse boosts Meta's ad business by prompting Instagram users to organize saved posts in Muse, reducing shopping friction and increasing advertisers' ROI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

CNBC reports that Meta is using Instagram prompts to move saved posts into Muse and make later shopping easier, indirectly helping its advertising business even while Muse is ad-free. Unnamed sources describe conversion benefits, while public executive comments give shopping-use and average-purchase figures.

The mechanism is reported rather than demonstrated in a controlled causal study, and the article supplies no audited incremental revenue amount. Forter's 51% share for Muse concerns agentic orders it measured in the week ending November 22, not all commerce or necessarily every agent transaction. A future transaction fee and possible in-Muse advertising are plans or expectations, not currently observed implementations here.

## Highlights

- Saved Instagram posts are the reported entry point
- Ad-free Muse can still support Instagram advertising
- Conversion benefit is attributed, not causally measured here
- Executive purchase and usage figures remain company reports
- Forter percentage applies to its measured order sample and week
- Potential future ads are not a confirmed launch
- No consumer account or ad settings were inspected
