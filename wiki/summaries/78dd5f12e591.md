# Medium launches TK, an AI-powered, minimalist note-taking, journaling, and private social networking app with daily prompts, its first major launch since 2017

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Medium has launched TK, a minimal note-taking and journaling app with a private social layer. CEO Tony Stubblebine says it responds to 'Google Zero', where search and chatbots send less traffic and AI firms scrape content, so Medium wants writing to move to private circles of friends and fans that need no Google referrals. The article frames it as the company's biggest launch in almost a decade.

## Highlights

- TK: note-taking plus private sharing with friends and fans
- Reaction to falling search traffic and AI scraping
- Bet: blogging moves toward group-chat style privacy
- Medium's biggest launch in about ten years
