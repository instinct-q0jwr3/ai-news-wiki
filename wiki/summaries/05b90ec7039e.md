# The UK NCSC says AI is "transforming cyber operations" and expects China and Russia to "routinely use AI cyber to achieve their geopolitical goals" by 2028

## Summary

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre says AI is 'transforming cyber operations' and expects China and Russia to 'routinely use AI cyber to achieve their geopolitical goals' by 2028, per the Financial Times - with the NCSC head set to warn that defenses, particularly for critical infrastructure, are inadequate.

## Highlights

- UK NCSC: AI 'transforming cyber operations'; China and Russia expected to routinely use AI cyber by 2028, per FT
- NCSC head to warn critical-infrastructure defenses are inadequate
