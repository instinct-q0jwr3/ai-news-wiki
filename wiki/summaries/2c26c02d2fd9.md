# Elon Musk says "I will give one of my SpaceX shares to every kid in America" in 2027, a gift worth ~$10.5B, after Anthropic committed 5% of its shares to APBF

## Summary

CNBC reports that Elon Musk said Monday he will give one of his SpaceX shares to every child in America in 2027. With 72 million children under 18 in the US and SpaceX stock closing at $146.03 on Friday, the gift would be worth about $10.5 billion; Musk owns about 4.8 billion SpaceX shares (6.4 billion including unvested awards and options, a 48.4% beneficial stake). 'This will be the first of many', he posted on X, without details on mechanics. The pledge lands days after Anthropic committed 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund, and CNBC notes Musk's giving has lately been dominated by political donations - more than $276 million to Republican groups this cycle.
