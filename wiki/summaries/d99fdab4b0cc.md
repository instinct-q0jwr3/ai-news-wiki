# As AI models commoditize, lasting wealth will accrue to those controlling chokepoints like distribution, payment rails, and a stablecoin dollar, not to the labs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

A long essay, 'The Commanding Heights', argues that as AI models commoditize, durable wealth will accrue not to the labs but to whoever controls the chokepoints: distribution, payment rails and a stablecoin dollar. It divides the economy into Genesis - the industry that makes and serves models, which will keep spending on itself even as returns fall, since rational capital allocation at each lab can destroy capital in aggregate - and the far larger economy where the bigger fortunes will be captured by those controlling how models reach it.

The macro claims are sweeping: America's future depends on AI spending, stock prices and model capability rising as one line, with Washington using increasingly central backstops - retirement savings, the Treasury - to guarantee it; training costs rise ~3.5x a year; only America and China can sustain frontier models, so intelligence splinters into a commercial frontier and a sovereign one locked behind SCIFs; and America's greatest power in the age of intelligence will be the Dollar, as abundant models manufacture near-unlimited demand for a global stablecoin 'Neo-Dollar'.
