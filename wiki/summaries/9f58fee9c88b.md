## Summary

An essay on post-training for robotics opens with a rambling GPT-2 sample about roasting a chicken to mark where language models stood - and argues frontier robotics models (Physical Intelligence, Generalist, DeepMind) now look like language models in the GPT-2 days, with pretrained models capable of genuinely complex tasks.

The catch: complex behavior does not equal reliability. A robot that loads dishes correctly 95% of the time will break something every week in a home full of glass, pets and kids, so a pretrained generalist policy cannot be deployed as-is - hence the case for universal post-training.

## Highlights
