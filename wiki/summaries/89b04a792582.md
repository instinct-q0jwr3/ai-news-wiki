# Nevis, which uses AI to automate paperwork for financial advisors, raised a $70M Series B led by EQT Ventures, a source says at a $500M valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Nevis, a New York startup that uses AI to take paperwork off financial advisors' desks, has raised a $70 million Series B, valuing the 70-person company at $500 million. Founded in October 2024 by former Revolut executives - CEO Mark Swan with Philipp Burda and Ivan Chalov - Nevis sells AI agents that do advisors' routine manual work so they can serve more clients.

The bet rides on a demographic squeeze: Americans want more financial advisors while the supply shrinks, and startups are racing to multiply the ones who remain. Swan frames the product as giving advisors agents that actually do the manual work - saving time, growing books of business, and expanding service.
