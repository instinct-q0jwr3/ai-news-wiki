# Databento, which offers market data APIs and says it has contracts with five of the six largest frontier AI labs, raised a $100M Series C at a $1.1B valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

FinTech Global reports Databento’s $100 million Series C at a $1.1 billion valuation, led by Fifth Down Capital. It describes a live and historical market-data API business and repeats company claims of profitability, rapid revenue growth and agreements with five of the six largest frontier AI labs.

The financing report connects demand for financial data with AI development, but does not disclose those contracts or identify a common definition of the largest labs. Commercial claims and operational-performance statements remain attributed rather than independently audited.

## Highlights

- Reported Series C totals $100 million
- Fifth Down Capital is the named lead
- Product combines live and historical data
- AI-lab agreements are company claims
- Contract terms are not disclosed
