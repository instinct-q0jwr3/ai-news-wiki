# Minneapolis-based Slingshot, which uses AI to help businesses offer embedded insurance, emerges from stealth with a $27M seed co-led by Rally and Skyward Group

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Slingshot Insurance, an AI-native embedded insurance startup based in Minneapolis, emerged from stealth with a $27 million seed round co-led by Rally Ventures and Skyward Group, with insurance veteran Jim Hays joining the board. Rally also co-founded the company, part of a venture-studio approach it has applied to roughly eight portfolio companies.

Slingshot focuses on small policies sold at the point of another purchase - coverage added when booking a flight, for instance. Its software connects businesses that want to offer such policies with the insurers that underwrite them, using AI to process and share data between the two sides. First-time founder and CEO Ethan Genteman leads the company.
