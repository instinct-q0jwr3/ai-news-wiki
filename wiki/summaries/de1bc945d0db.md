# Tests: chatbots rely on three obscure AI-built voter sites for US midterm answers; Copilot cited the error-prone Ballot Brief in 75% of tests and ChatGPT in 22%

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-01 · updated: 2026-11-05 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Short note from feed metadata only: the Washington Post tested chatbots on US midterm voter questions and found they lean heavily on three obscure, AI-built voter-information sites - Copilot cited the error-prone Ballot Brief in 75% of its test answers, ChatGPT in 22%. The sites, including one built by Ben Torres Ezrick as a one-stop shop for voters, are themselves AI-generated. The Post's page is paywalled, so the test methodology and error examples are unverified here, but the shape of the finding - chatbots laundering AI-generated civic content into authoritative answers - matches the tests described.
