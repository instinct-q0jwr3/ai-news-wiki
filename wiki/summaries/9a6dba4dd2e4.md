## Summary

Simon Willison comments that educators now must change how they teach and assess, yet it is unclear what works with AI tools, and results take 3 to 6 years to see because they only show when a cohort graduates. Students effectively become guinea pigs for untested methods.

## Highlights

- Teaching must change, effective methods unclear
- Feedback loop takes years
- Students are the test group
