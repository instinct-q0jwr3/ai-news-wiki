## Highlights

- Muse for Small Business connects to Asana, Zoom, Intuit, Box, Canva, and Slack
- Also links Meta ad accounts and professional Instagram/Facebook profiles
- Same pricing as consumer Muse: free with limits, subscription beyond
- Follows Monday's enterprise platform announcement with MongoDB CEO CJ Desai hired to run it
- Meta stock +25% in September; Muse hit #1 on the App Store, passing ChatGPT
- Evercore's Mahaney projects 100M Muse users within 6-12 months
