# Meta launches Muse for Small Business, integrating the AI agent with Asana, Zoom, Intuit, Box, Canva, Slack, and other apps, alongside its own ad accounts

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Meta launches Muse for Small Business, connecting its AI agent to Asana, Zoom, Intuit, Box, Canva, and Slack, plus Meta ad accounts and professional Instagram/Facebook profiles, at the same free-with-limits/subscription pricing as consumer Muse. It lands a day after Meta announced a new enterprise platform (with MongoDB CEO CJ Desai poached to run it) spanning a Muse agent, business agent, and coding tool. Context: Meta stock is up 25% in September after the Sept. 8 Muse launch hit #1 on the App Store ahead of ChatGPT, Evercore's Mahaney expects 100M users within 6-12 months, and Zuckerberg - who paid billions for talent including Alexandr Wang, whose group ships the Muse Spark models - calls Muse the centerpiece of Meta's AI business pivot.

## Highlights

- Muse for Small Business connects to Asana, Zoom, Intuit, Box, Canva, and Slack
- Also links Meta ad accounts and professional Instagram/Facebook profiles
- Same pricing as consumer Muse: free with limits, subscription beyond
- Follows Monday's enterprise platform announcement with MongoDB CEO CJ Desai hired to run it
- Meta stock +25% in September; Muse hit #1 on the App Store, passing ChatGPT
- Evercore's Mahaney projects 100M Muse users within 6-12 months
