# The OpenClaw Foundation launches ShellBench to test 13 frontier AI models across five harnesses on 100 tasks; top AI models hit only a ~60% completion rate

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

The Deep View reports the OpenClaw Foundation's ShellBench launch, testing thirteen models across five harnesses on one hundred messy workplace tasks. The coverage says top configurations complete roughly six in ten tasks and emphasizes omissions, run-to-run variation and price differences between near-leading systems.

The article reports benchmark results and an organizer interview, not an independent reproduction or a universal reliability rate. It attributes 51% of completion failures to missing information and says relevant information had often already been collected. Model, harness, task set, grading and sampling all shape those figures; similarly scored systems can have different per-run prices without establishing the best total cost for every workload.

## Highlights

- Benchmark combines thirteen models and five harnesses
- One hundred tasks use realistic workplace files
- Top completion rates are reported around sixty percent
- Omissions are a major reported failure mode
- Repeated runs can vary substantially
- Reported cost per run differs across leading models
- No universal agent reliability or cost ranking is established
