## Summary

Jack Clark publishes the text of his Per Jacobsson lecture, delivered 17 October at the IMF meeting in Bangkok, in which he tells assembled economists, bankers, and policymakers that we are in the early stages of the singularity and should expect significant, rapid change. He argues AI is already changing national security, altering the world's economic trajectory, and contributing to its own development, and that it is on course to bring even more change than expected. The lecture focuses on how humanity can share in AI's benefits through foresight and restraint; he flags his own dual position as narrator and participant, and promises future Import AI issues on the stakes and risks.
