# CrowdStrike agrees to buy Fiddler AI, which helps monitor AI agents, for ~$400M, and Dreadnode, an automated security AI testing tool, for ~$95M, mostly in cash

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

## Summary

CrowdStrike agreed to buy Fiddler AI - which monitors AI agents - for roughly $400M, and Dreadnode, an automated AI security-testing tool, for about $95M, mostly in cash, per the Wall Street Journal. The deals land as companies deploy AI agents faster than security teams can establish controls: agent observability and adversarial testing are becoming security-stack line items.

## Highlights

- CrowdStrike buys Fiddler AI (~$400M) and Dreadnode (~$95M), mostly cash, per WSJ
- Agent monitoring + automated AI red-teaming consolidated into a major security vendor
- Context: enterprises deploying agents faster than security teams can govern them
