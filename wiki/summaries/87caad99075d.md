# Underwriting Superintelligence: Backing Agents you can Sue — Rune Kvist, AIUC

`latent.space` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

AIUC, cofounded by Anthropic's first product hire Rune Kvist, announced a $40M Series A to build the standards, testing and insurance infrastructure for deploying frontier AI. Its thesis: the binding constraint on AI adoption will be trust, not capability, and companies like Cursor, Harvey, Lovable and ElevenLabs increasingly face the question of who is responsible when autonomous systems fail.

The episode centers on AIUC-1, a standard for agent security, safety and reliability backed by real insurance, with agents stress-tested for jailbreaks, hallucinations and data leaks. Kvist argues most AI companies optimize the happy path without serious adversarial testing, that standards may need quarterly updates rather than decadal ones, and that insurance (via markets like Lloyd's of London) and standards must evolve together, especially as cases like the Air Canada chatbot clarify liability and a $20 coding agent could plausibly contribute to $200M of damage.

## Highlights
