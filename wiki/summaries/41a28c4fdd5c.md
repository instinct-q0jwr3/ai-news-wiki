# Fujifilm plans to increase magnetic tape production capacity fivefold to meet AI-driven storage demand, its biggest production expansion in ~20 years

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Nikkei reports Fujifilm will raise magnetic tape production capacity fivefold to meet AI-driven storage demand - its largest investment in the medium in roughly 20 years. Magnetic tape remains the offline backup standard for data center operators, financial institutions and research organizations, and the AI boom's data appetite is reviving it.

## Highlights

- Fujifilm quintupling magnetic tape capacity
- Biggest tape investment in ~20 years
- AI-driven storage demand revives the offline backup medium
- Tape remains standard for data center, finance and research backup
