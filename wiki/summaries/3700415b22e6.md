# Nat Friedman says Meta built Muse "from scratch" but was "heavily inspired" by OpenClaw, as users compare the two, including calling Muse "OpenClaw for normies"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse`

## Summary

Meta's Muse was 'definitely heavily inspired as a product by OpenClaw' though 'built from scratch,' according to an X post by Nat Friedman, head of product at Meta's Superintelligence Labs. The clarification came after early adopters speculated Muse worked so well because it was OpenClaw underneath, with one viral post calling it 'LITERALLY OpenClaw for normies.' Friedman said the team 'fell in love' with OpenClaw and wanted something like it that could scale to billions of people. TechCrunch notes the episode fits Meta's playbook of absorbing rivals' best features, and that OpenClaw's creator was acquired by OpenAI earlier this year after its breakout success.

- Prompted by viral comparisons, including 'OpenClaw for normies'.
- Friedman joined Meta last year with Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang.
