# The AI Pascal’s Wager

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-01 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

Ploum argues that open source projects must now choose between accepting and rejecting AI-generated contributions, and that the middle ground, like Debian's AI policy, is unsustainable. Nobody is really happy with it, and generated code slips in anyway, so staying in the middle is effectively slower support for the pro-AI position.

He says either choice alienates some contributors, but rejecting AI costs less: people who cannot contribute without LLMs probably cannot review their own code, and no user refuses software because humans made it. Adopting AI mostly alienates community members and gives up independence for perceived productivity. He leaves the strategic call to each project's key members. Based on most of the post.

## Highlights

- Projects face a forced choice on AI code
- Middle-ground policies like Debian's are unsustainable
- Not choosing equals slower acceptance
- Rejecting AI mainly loses contributors who can't review code
- No user avoids software for being human-made
- Decision belongs to key community members
