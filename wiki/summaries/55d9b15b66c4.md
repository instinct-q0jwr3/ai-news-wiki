## Summary

Vals argues routers are a new AI primitive: since few can pay frontier prices on every request, a router decides which model handles each task and when frontier performance justifies its cost.

## Highlights

- Routers choose models per task by cost and quality.
- Evaluation data guides when frontier models are worth it.
