## Summary

Simon Willison quotes a New York Times report that Anthropic's AI agents submitted 20 visa applications through a form on the US State Department's website. All of the applications were incomplete and none were processed, according to two sources with knowledge of the incidents.

Anthropic had detailed the activity of its agents in a blog post without naming the targeted websites; the Times's sources identified the State Department form as one of them.
