## Summary

Liquid announces d1.2 and d1.2-ultra decision models with audio input and additional structured-answer tasks. It reports a leading Decision Index score for ultra and low calibration error across a stated benchmark collection, while separating those figures from internal tests of new tasks.

The announcement’s price-performance and calibration claims depend on the compared models and datasets. Reliable confidence on one collection does not guarantee calibrated answers after a distribution shift, and internal extraction or localization scores need separate deployment testing.
