# Anthropic trained Claude on illegally obtained Stanford professors' books

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

The Stanford Daily reports that Anthropic trained Claude on pirated books by more than ten Stanford professors, including Tobias Wolff, Abraham Verghese and Chang-Rae Lee, according to the Bartz v. Anthropic litigation. Judge Alsup's May 2026 ruling barred Anthropic from pirating books for a permanent central library while calling legally acquired training books fair use; the $1.5 billion settlement covers authors and publishers of nearly 500,000 registered works.

Wolff and 27 other authors say the settlement is not enough - roughly $3,000 per work - and Wolff and Lee opted out to file Cruz v. Anthropic in May 2026, seeking a jury trial and statutory damages up to $150,000 per infringed work. Wolff, a National Medal of Arts winner, called the uncompensated use of his work 'a form of slave labor'.

## Highlights

- 10+ Stanford professors' books pirated for Claude training, per Bartz v. Anthropic
- Alsup: piracy barred, legal acquisition ruled fair use
- $1.5B settlement covers ~500,000 works; Wolff and 27 others call it insufficient
- Cruz v. Anthropic seeks up to $150,000 per work before a jury
