# DishwasherBench – Can Frontier Models Stack a Dishwasher?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

DishwasherBench is a game and language-model benchmark in which a player loads a dishwasher inside a physical simulation of one real machine, a Bosch SMV4HAX40G. The simulation uses rigid-body physics for loading and then simulates an Eco 50 wash, with jets, droplets, run-off, soil and detergent chemistry, scoring items that come out clean. After level one not everything fits, so choices matter.

It is version 0.1.0 and calibrated on a single real wash. A score currently shows whether items were packed well and cups, glasses and pots were placed mouth down, but not yet whether plates and cutlery would actually wash clean. The page has a section on what a score can be trusted for.

## Highlights

- Benchmark and browser game on a physics simulation of a real Bosch dishwasher.
- Simulates a wash with water jets, soil and detergent chemistry.
- Early 0.1.0 release calibrated on one real wash.
- Scores reliably reflect packing, not yet plate and cutlery cleanliness.
