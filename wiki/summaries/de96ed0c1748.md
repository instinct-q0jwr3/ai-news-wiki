# Relay, which develops cloud-hosted, AI-powered smart radio communicators for frontline workers, raised $36M to help businesses capture "frontline intelligence"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Relay, which makes cloud-hosted AI smart radios for frontline workers, raised $36M, bringing its total above $90M. International Paper led, with Cerity Partners Ventures and G2 Ventures. It wants to capture 'frontline intelligence' from workers on factory lines, hotels, hospitals and emergency response who aren't at desks.

## Highlights

- $36M raise led by International Paper; $90M+ total.
- Cloud smart radios for frontline workers.
- Aims to capture 'frontline intelligence' with AI.
