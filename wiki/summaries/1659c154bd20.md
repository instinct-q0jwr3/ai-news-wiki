# Jensen Huang says AI model distillation is "competition"; Scott Bessent described it as "theft" in July and threatened sanctions against overseas companies

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `nvidia`

## Summary

Jensen Huang publicly broke with the White House line on model distillation, telling CNBC's Squawk Box that training models on other models' outputs is 'competition', not robbery. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent had called distillation 'theft' in July and threatened sanctions against overseas companies using it to extract capability from U.S. models, and CISA recently accused Chinese AI companies of 'industrial-scale knowledge distillation campaigns'. Huang countered that testing others' products is normal - Nvidia's own hardware gets stripped 'down to bones' by companies learning how it works - and that while he would prefer nobody learned from Nvidia's products, 'competition makes everything better.' His suggested remedy for companies that dislike it: know your customers and disable the service. Anthropic says it found Alibaba and DeepSeek engaged in 'illicit distillation'; China rejects the claims.
