# The FAA announced on November 10 that it has expanded the use of SMART, an AI tool for advising air traffic controllers to manage congestion, across the US

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Ars Technica reports that the FAA expanded SMART advisory coverage to all 22 air-route centers after a Washington-area trial. The FAA credits the trial with rerouting 180 flights and saving time and fuel, but those figures are agency claims rather than an independent nationwide evaluation.

FAA specialists validate recommendations before passing them to local controllers, who can decline them; SMART does not control aircraft. The controllers’ union says it did not participate in the design, testing or implementation, leaving a gap between management’s success claims and workforce involvement.

## Highlights

- Nationwide expansion follows a regional trial
- Agency reports time and fuel savings
- Specialists validate recommendations
- Local controllers may reject advice
- Union says it was not involved in testing
