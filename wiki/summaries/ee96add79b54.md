# Manus' parent Butterfly Effect says it raised $500M+, led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, in its first funding since Meta was forced to unwind its Manus buyout

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

## Summary

CNBC reports that Butterfly Effect, parent of AI agent startup Manus, raised more than $500 million, led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital. It is the first funding round since Meta was forced to unwind its purchase of Manus.

Only the Techmeme excerpt was read, so valuation and investor details beyond the lead names are not available.
