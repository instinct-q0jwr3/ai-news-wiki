The r/opensource story has jumped from The Verge to the wider tech press: the subreddit's moderators banned projects that use AI, are made by AI, or are intended for AI, and - in the same rules refresh - told non-English speakers to find another community because AI-written and AI-translated text cannot be told apart. The revolt in the community and the rival r/trueopensource subreddit continue to grow.
