## Summary

Cory Doctorow argues the EU's legitimacy depends on visible competence: fixing everyday things rather than splashy megaprojects, which in a public-private model become vehicles for overcharging and underdelivery. His case is border IT: Schengen's open internal borders depend on shared systems, and failures there hand arguments to those who say government cannot help. He wants the EU to go 'big and hard' at making border IT work.

## Highlights

- EU legitimacy rests on visible competence, not megaprojects
- Public-private megaprojects invite overruns and banditry
- Border IT failures undermine Schengen
- Fix the boring systems well
