# On using AI as a writing assistant

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

Nina Shamsi writes about using AI as a writing assistant. She thinks of herself as a bad writer because her pieces wander, and says AI helps her turn a fluid thought process into tidy pieces she can share.

She is unsure the experiment works: she finds AI writing stilted, and says editing it to carry her voice can leave the piece less cohesive than the original draft. She shares her workflow and says she learns the pitfalls by using the tool in different ways. The Lobsters summary adds that AI may amplify weaknesses instead of fixing them.
