# Palo Alto Networks is in talks to acquire Israeli startup Clutch Security, which develops software for tracking passwords used by AI agents, for ~$500M

## Summary

The Information reports, citing sources, that Palo Alto Networks is in talks to buy three-year-old Israeli startup Clutch Security for around $500M. Clutch makes software for tracking passwords and credentials used by AI agents.
