# KDE turns 30 and someone's brought an AI-native desktop proposal

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

KDE turns 30 this year, and its Akademy conference in Graz (starting September 19) features a likely divisive proposal: an 'AI-native' desktop. Longtime contributors Eva Brucherseifer and Jan Muehlig sketch 'Kadai', an encrypted, vendor-agnostic personal kernel from which Plasma would assemble each Activity, a desktop 'compiled per user, per device, per moment' from a small portable model of the user.

The proposal argues Plasma is uniquely suited, thanks to Activities, Plasmoids and its scripting surface, but would need upstream changes like declarative reconciliation, per-widget capabilities and richer Activity metadata, aiming to make KDE 'the first shell that treats AI as infrastructure rather than as a chat box bolted to the side'. Critics note its resemblance to last December's Resonant Computing Manifesto, and even the Trinity Desktop fork (a KDE 3 continuation) has begun dabbling in AI-generated code, leaving few AI-free corners.

## Highlights

- KDE celebrates its 30th anniversary; Akademy opens September 19 at Graz University of Technology.
- Proposal talk: 'What would it take? A lovable, sovereign, AI-native KDE' by Eva Brucherseifer and Jan Muehlig.
- Core idea: 'Kadai', an encrypted, vendor-agnostic personal kernel from which Plasma assembles each Activity per user, device and moment.
- Required upstream changes: declarative reconciliation, per-widget capabilities, richer Activity metadata.
- Ambition: make KDE the first shell treating AI as infrastructure rather than a bolted-on chat box.
- The talk's first part revisits a 2003 usability study of KDE 3.1 where 87% of 60 Linux-naive office workers enjoyed KDE.
- David Gerard links the concept to the Resonant Computing Manifesto launched at Wired's Big Interview in December 2025.
- Even the AI-skeptical Trinity Desktop (KDE 3 fork) has contributors experimenting with AI-generated code.
