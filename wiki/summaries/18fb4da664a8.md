## Highlights

- Gems become 'skills' starting Nov 17, 2026, with automatic migration
- Skills launched with Gemini Spark and cover the same custom-instruction use case
- Skills are invoked with / in the prompt box and multiple can be combined
- The help article and Create-skills button did not work yet at announcement time
- Skills creation is currently limited to AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers
- The author criticizes Gemini's habit of promoting cutesy curated prompts over users' own Gems
