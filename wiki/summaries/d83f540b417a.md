# AI promised less work. I've never worked more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A full-stack engineer at Bold.org writes that AI agents have made him work more, not less. He now runs several agents in parallel on implementation, production investigations, reviews and research, and finds the constant context-switching between them more draining than the two or three deep-focus tasks a week he used to handle.

He pulls his GitHub contribution calendar from 2019 onward and maps it against his roles at Bold.org (full-stack engineer, principal engineer, and now VP of Technology), showing activity climbing steadily through the agent era. He cautions the counts mix commits, PRs, reviews and issues and are a rough activity signal, not a measure of value.

## Highlights

- Agent parallelism means more tasks at once, each needing intermittent human attention
- Context-switching between agents is the new source of fatigue
- GitHub contribution data shows his activity rising year over year since 2019
- Author uses agents for all engineering work and still reads the code
