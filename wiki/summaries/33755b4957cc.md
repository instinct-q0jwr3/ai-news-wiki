# Codeberg Divides

## Summary

Armin Ronacher on Codeberg's new terms excluding projects largely written with generative AI - a decision he disagrees with while defending Codeberg's right to make it. His framework is infrastructure neutrality: he wants GitHub to have competition, but what he needs from a code host isn't democracy, it's predictability and reasonable neutrality toward the open-source software it hosts.

The catch he names: a democratic provider without a clear constitution can be worse at both than a corporation. Codeberg is a German association and the vote was legitimate - but a majority vote says nothing about whether a decision is good, especially for the people already depending on the platform. A majority can still decide certain projects and people no longer belong.

## Highlights

- Codeberg's new terms exclude projects largely written with generative AI

- He wants GitHub competition - but with a constitution, not just votes
