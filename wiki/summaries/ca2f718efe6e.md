# Google rebrands its Gemini Spark AI agent as Tasks and makes it free in six countries; in the US it can call businesses and buy items with Google Pay

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `google` `gemini` `agentic-systems` `ai-price-war`

## Summary

The Verge reports Google is renaming Spark to Tasks and broadening free access in six countries, with additional calling, payment and account-sign-in features in the United States. The article describes sensitive-action approval and account-specific isolated execution as Google’s stated controls.

Country rollout and feature eligibility differ. Company assurances are not an independent security evaluation, and free access to the assistant does not make purchases or external services free. No accounts or payment methods were connected.

## Highlights

- Spark is reported as renamed Tasks
- Core rollout spans specified countries
- US receives additional action capabilities
- Sensitive-action controls are attributed to Google
- Assistant access and transaction cost are distinct
