# Cleanview: ~40% of pending US data center capacity is planned to be built in states where newly elected governors have pledged to stop or pause construction

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Bloomberg reports, citing Cleanview, that about 40% of pending US data-center capacity is planned in states where newly elected governors pledged to stop or pause construction. A new crop of Democratic governors-elect campaigned on putting the brakes on AI development.

## Highlights

- ~40% of pending US data-center capacity sits in states with governors pledging pauses
- Democratic governors-elect campaigned on slowing AI buildout
