# OpenAI's DevDay 2026 announcements to turn ChatGPT into a place to discover, launch, and use software could potentially disrupt the traditional app store model

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

TechCrunch reads OpenAI's DevDay announcements as a plan to disrupt the app store model by turning ChatGPT into the place where software is discovered, launched and used by people and agents. ChatGPT, with 1.2 billion weekly users, will suggest apps in the flow of conversation.

OpenAI also introduced a way to bring your ChatGPT identity to other apps and use your existing AI allowance in third-party apps. The focus of the event was agentic assistants called Dots and new models, but the combined message was about distribution.

## Highlights

- ChatGPT becomes a surface for finding and launching apps.
- 1.2 billion weekly users, per OpenAI.
- ChatGPT identity and AI allowance usable in third-party apps.
- TechCrunch reads it as a challenge to app stores.
