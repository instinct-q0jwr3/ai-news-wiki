# A profile of OpenAI VP Charles Porch, an ex-Instagram executive who is rehabilitating AI's image across Hollywood and the media, including via celebrity dinners

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai`

## Summary

New York Magazine profiles Charles Porch, the ex-Instagram executive Sam Altman hired in 2025 to make OpenAI culturally inevitable - the man whose job is persuading Hollywood to embrace the technology much of Hollywood believes exists to replace it. The profile opens with the Gwyneth Paltrow Hamptons dinner that leaked in August, drew a vicious backlash ('tone-deaf dumbass'), was postponed, and eventually happened quietly in New York in September. 'I know those dinners are effective because I see it,' Porch says.

Porch spent two decades building one of the most formidable contact lists in entertainment, arriving in LA at 20 in his grandmother's Honda Accord wagon and, per Erin Foster, 'never burned a single bridge.' His 2025 Paris wedding to billionaire scion Robert Denning - Seinfelds, Harry Styles, Antony Blinken, European aristocrats - made him a public character in his own right. 'He was someone who understood the room before he was part of the Establishment,' says a former colleague. 'Now he is of the room.'

The profile's real subject is how much harder the assignment is than the Instagram version. When Anna Wintour hosted dinners for Kevin Systrom, tech was fashionable; now 'the full-throated endorsement of AI is probably the biggest third-rail issue in Hollywood,' one media executive says, between job-displacement panic, copyright anger, and rogue-agent headlines. 'Everyone wants to hate OpenAI and everyone wants to love Charles Porch,' says podcaster Nayeema Raza. 'That's probably the value for Sam.' The piece is a useful map of how frontier labs now fight for legitimacy: not with policy papers but with Arnold Palmers at Sant Ambroeus.
