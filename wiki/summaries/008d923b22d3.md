# An Interview with OpenAI's Richard Ho on Why Jalapeño Is a Groundbreaking AI Chip

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `openai`

## Summary

An interview with OpenAI's Richard Ho on Jalapeño, the company's first AI inference chip, presented at Hot Chips in late August to widespread acclaim. SemiAnalysis InferenceX benchmarks showed the design beating rivals including Nvidia's Blackwell on performance per watt across open-source models such as GPT-OSS, DeepSeek R1, and Kimi K2.5 - though rankings may shift once Nvidia's Vera Rubin systems arrive. Ho argues building a chip this competitive on the first attempt is a major achievement and calls Jalapeño the most promising program in the new wave of AI silicon.

The conversation covers what a custom inference chip buys a frontier lab: control over the cost curve of serving models, hardware-software co-design for specific workloads, and insulation from the GPU supply queue. Jalapeño positions OpenAI alongside Google (TPU) and Amazon (Trainium) as a vertically integrated model-and-silicon player rather than a pure Nvidia customer.
