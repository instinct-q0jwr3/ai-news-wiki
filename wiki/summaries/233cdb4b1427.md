## Summary

Breadcrumb is a Mac app that records your screen activity and serves it as context to your AI. History is encrypted with your own key and stays on the Mac. Transcription, speaker naming and day organisation use local models, and speakers are named from on-screen text without building voice profiles.

Answers go to whichever AI asks, so a local AI keeps everything on the machine while Claude or ChatGPT fall under their own privacy policy. You can exclude apps, sites and words, and incognito sites are never recorded. It makes one daily update check.

## Highlights

- Records your Mac activity as context for AI assistants.
- Encrypted with your key; processing is local.
- No voice profiles; speakers named from the screen.
- Exclusions for apps, sites and phrases; incognito never recorded.
- Privacy depends on which AI you connect.
