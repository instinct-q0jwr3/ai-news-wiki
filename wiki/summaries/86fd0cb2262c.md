# Show HN: We had AI write a wiki from 45k Enron emails, every fact cited

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Show HN gave an AI 45,047 Enron emails and let it write a 214-page 'company brain': wiki pages about the companies, regulators, and banks Enron dealt with, where every fact is a sentence carrying a date and a citation back to the email it came from. When later mail shows a fact stopped being true, the page expires it on that date - so each page has a slider that replays what was known at any point in time.

No human edited a page or a fact; accuracy figures come from a held-out audit by a second AI, documented in the methodology. The design thesis is that facts-as-sentences-with-dates-and-sources capture what CRM-style fields cannot: knowledge that changes too often or doesn't fit a schema. It is one of the cleaner public demonstrations of cited, time-aware document synthesis over a large real corpus.

## Highlights

- AI wrote a 214-page wiki from 45,047 Enron emails; every fact cites its source email
- Facts carry dates and expire when later emails contradict them
- Each page has a time slider replaying what was known on a given date
- No human edited any page or fact; a second AI ran a held-out audit
- Thesis: sentence-facts with dates and sources capture what CRM fields cannot
