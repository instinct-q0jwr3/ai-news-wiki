# Nscale and Anthropic are reportedly in talks for yet another $45B compute deal

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `anthropic` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Bloomberg sources say Anthropic and Nscale are close to announcing a second $45 billion compute deal, barely a month after the pair announced their first one, though the sources caution talks are still underway and details could change. If signed, it would extend Anthropic's year-long string of giant compute commitments, struck on the belief the spending will pay off even as some investors warn of too much money chasing too little profit. Both companies are pursuing IPOs: Anthropic is expected to go public this year, while Nscale is reportedly facing investor pushback over its proposed $30 billion valuation.
