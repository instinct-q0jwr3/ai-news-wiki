# Previous-Token Prediction Based LLM Inversion for Prompt Reconstruction

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

An arXiv abstract proposes reconstructing prompts from model responses using an inverse model trained on synthetic target-model data and previous-token prediction. The authors report reconstruction and transfer results on their chosen token-based metrics.

Only the abstract was examined. Multiple prompts can produce similar outputs, so reconstruction does not necessarily recover the unique original instruction or a hidden secret. Full methods, data and privacy implications need separate review.

## Highlights

- Method trains an inverse model on synthetic data
- Prompt-response mapping can be many-to-many
- Claims concern chosen reconstruction metrics
- Transfer claims come from the authors
- Abstract does not prove unique original recovery
