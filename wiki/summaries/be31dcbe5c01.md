# Nebuly, whose tech helps enterprises measure the business value of AI agents by analyzing their impact on productivity and revenue, raised a $21M Series A

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-15 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Tech.eu reports Nebuly’s $21 million Series A led by Planven for measuring the business impact of enterprise AI agents. The platform aims to connect agent activity with time savings, work capacity and customer-facing revenue rather than treating token counts or adoption as outcomes.

The report says finance and leadership teams can change assumptions behind the calculations, and describes private-cloud or on-premises operation. Those capabilities do not by themselves establish causal productivity gains: the quality of the inputs, assumptions and comparison remains central to any return-on-investment estimate.

## Highlights

- Reported round totals $21 million
- Planven is the named lead
- Usage and business outcomes are distinguished
- Calculation assumptions can be reviewed
- Measured association is not necessarily causal impact
