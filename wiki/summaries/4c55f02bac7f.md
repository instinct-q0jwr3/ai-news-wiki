# Unlocking Stable On-Policy RL on TPU with Bitwise-Zero Train–Inference Mismatch

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-17 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Google’s engineering account explains how different numerical paths in an RL sampler and trainer can assign different probabilities despite identical model weights. Its Tunix Zero-TIM work aligns decode, prefill and training computations to produce bitwise-identical log probabilities in the reported multi-host TPU setup.

This addresses a defined numerical mismatch rather than every source of RL instability. The reported result depends on aligned kernels, reduction order, precision and execution conditions; it is not proof that arbitrary hardware or future changes preserve identical behavior without checks.

## Highlights

- Same weights need not imply identical floating-point results
- Decode, prefill and trainer are compared separately
- Reported tests span 64 TPU v5p chips
- Bitwise equality is stronger than approximate agreement
- Numerical alignment does not solve all RL failure modes
