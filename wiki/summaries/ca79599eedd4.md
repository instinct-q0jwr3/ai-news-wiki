# Google DeepMind launches EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740M-parameter model to map code, images, video, and audio in a shared embedding space, under an Apache 2.0 license

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `google` `gemini` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

Google DeepMind launched EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740M-parameter model that natively maps text, code, images, video and audio into one shared embedding space - enabling queries like finding a video clip from a voice memo or searching hours of audio with text, all on-device. It builds on the Gemma 4 architecture, ships under Apache 2.0, and follows the original EmbeddingGemma, which passed 20 million downloads as a lightweight engine for on-device search and privacy-first RAG.

DeepMind claims EmbeddingGemma 2 matches its predecessor's multilingual text performance while jumping 9.92 points on MTEB Code (68.76 to 78.68), making it suitable for local codebase indexing and coding-agent retrieval. Across image, video, documents and audio it sets a new quality-per-parameter standard for sub-1B models, outperforming some specialist models twice its size.
