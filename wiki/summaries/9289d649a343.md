# Why Didn’t Google Build Muse?

`daring-fireball` `google` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

John Gruber answers the second-most-common response to Meta's Muse assistant - 'Why didn't Google build this?' - with reporting about CC, Google's AI agent for email, calendar, chats and tasks. A new version of CC now targets family coordination: planning the day ahead, signing permission slips, creating shopping lists, crafting weekly meal plans.

His short answer is 'they're working on it'; the longer answer is more interesting. Google runs the world's biggest email service - the most valuable trove of personal information and communication context there is - which is both its advantage and its constraint in shipping a Muse-style personal agent. (The most common response to Muse, he notes, was some variation of 'I will never trust Meta with my data'.)
