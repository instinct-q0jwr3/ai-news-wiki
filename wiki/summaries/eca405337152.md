## Summary

UC San Diego describes an independent community review of OpenAI and Anthropic claims concerning the Exponential Time Hypothesis. The announcement names researchers facilitating discussion, a public review forum and planned meetings.

The institution’s wording makes consequences conditional on the findings holding up. Organizing review is not the same as endorsing a proof, and this page does not establish that review has concluded or that a particular algorithmic consequence has been verified.

## Highlights

- Announcement concerns independent scrutiny
- ETH claim originates with the labs
- Discussion and meetings support verification
- Implications are explicitly conditional
- No completed validation is announced
