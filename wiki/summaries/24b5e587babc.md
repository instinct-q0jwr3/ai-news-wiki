## Summary

Claude Meetings documentation describes a beta that joins Google Meet visibly, takes notes and creates artifacts during calls. Rollout is for Team and Enterprise, with exclusions for some configurations including zero-data-retention and certain security or compliance options.

The documentation distinguishes organization enablement from each person's auto-join choice and stresses participant agreement to transcription. A meeting assistant's availability does not grant consent to record other people; the specific organization settings and call context still matter.

## Highlights

- Visible Google Meet participant
- Notes and live artifacts are described
- Team and Enterprise beta has exclusions
- Organization enablement differs from personal auto-join
- Participant consent remains necessary
