# Gemini 4 Argon: The Model and Safety Cards

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `google` `gemini` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Zvi walks through Google's Gemini 4 Argon model and safety cards. Google reports a significant step up in frontier safety domains versus previous Gemini models and says it cannot rule out the unmitigated model approaching several critical capability thresholds: Cyber Uplift Level 1, CBRN (biological) Uplift Level 1, and the Stealth and Situational Awareness tracked level.

The card describes a comprehensive agent monitoring and response platform, expanded reporting on chain-of-thought transparency and ML R&D automation risks, and a stated policy of not training directly against monitors in order to preserve reasoning transparency - with an explicit call for the rest of the industry to keep chain-of-thought monitoring viable. Argon significantly outperforms Gemini 3.8 Flash on all tests.

## Highlights

- Unmitigated Argon may approach cyber, bio and stealth capability thresholds
- Google built an agent monitoring and response platform for deployment
- Policy: no training against monitors, to preserve CoT transparency
- Expanded public reporting on ML R&D automation and loss-of-control risks
