# Pluralistic: Google is a scammer's paradise (05 Aug 2026)

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Cory Doctorow argues that Google - 'a thrice-convicted monopolist' with over 90% search share, attained by buying competitors and paying Apple $20B+/year not to build a rival engine - has become the internet's absentee landlord, and that its negligence has made it a scammer's paradise.

Google's self-image as the internet's wise steward ('organize the world's information...') rests on the tacit claim that the internet needs a monopolist protector. Doctorow rejects the premise, but adds that even by its own standard Google fails: the power is real while the responsibility is not - 'with great power comes no responsibility.'

## Highlights

- Doctorow: Google's true motto is 'with great power comes no responsibility'
- Cites 90%+ search share, competitor buyouts, and $20B+/year payments to Apple
- Calls Google an absentee landlord whose neglect enables scammers
- Rejects the 'the internet needs a monopolist' defense while noting Google fails even that standard
