# SignSplit, which offers infrastructure to let people digitally sign datasets, likenesses, and creative works, raised $400M from W Group at a $1B valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

SignSplit, which builds infrastructure for digitally signing datasets, likenesses and creative works, launched with $400 million from W Group at a $1 billion valuation. Founded in 2024 and led by CEO Alessandro Monterosso, it argues that AI and robotics need high-quality human data, that scraped internet data has opaque origins, and that developers will have to pay for data with clear provenance, permissions and usage rights. W Group is a collective of 11 fintech firms focused on blockchain.
