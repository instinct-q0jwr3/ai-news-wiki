# How Accurate Have AI Progress Forecasts Been So Far?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

The Forecasting Research Institute opens a series on the accuracy of AI progress forecasts it has collected since mid-2022, spanning benchmarks and non-benchmark capability tests, adoption and diffusion metrics, social impacts, and geopolitics and governance.

The topline: across their studies, forecasters dramatically underestimate AI progress on benchmarks; the record on adoption and diffusion is mixed though it leans toward underestimation; and there is not yet enough evidence to judge forecasts of macro-scale economic and societal impacts. The post is authored by Josh Rosenberg, Matt Reynolds, Nadja Flechner and Zack Devlin-Foltz.

## Highlights

- FRI reviews how accurate AI progress forecasts have been since mid-2022.
- Forecasters dramatically underestimate benchmark progress.
- Adoption and diffusion: mixed record, leaning toward underestimation.
- Macro economic and societal impacts: not enough evidence yet.
- First post in a short series on improving forecast accuracy.
