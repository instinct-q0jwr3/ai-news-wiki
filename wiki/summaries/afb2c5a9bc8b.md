# SpaceXAI releases Grok 4.7 at $2/1M input, $6/1M output tokens

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `xai` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

xAI released Grok 4.7, positioned as its most capable model for coding and knowledge work, at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output - the same price and higher speed positioning as Grok 4.6. The release claims a new, larger base model trained with a longer reinforcement-learning run weighted toward tasks that take many hours, yielding better self-verification and longer-context management, plus native understanding of the Grok Bot harness for conversational work.

xAI emphasizes safeguards: it calls Grok 4.7 the strongest model it has tested on refusals and jailbreak resistance, topping LatchBio's biosafety benchmark at 62.4% while keeping low refusal rates on legitimate cyber work. On professional-task benchmarks GDPval and AA Briefcase - which simulate work done by lawyers, nurses and financial analysts - Grok 4.7 improves on 4.6 and performs comparably to other frontier models, and on CursorBench 4.0, which stresses longer-running coding tasks, it is claimed at the frontier in price-performance.

## Highlights

- Pricing: $2 per 1M input tokens, $6 per 1M output - pitched as twice as fast at half the price of comparable models.
- New larger base model, longer RL run weighted toward multi-hour tasks; better self-verification and long-context handling.
- Trained to natively understand the Grok Bot harness for conversational and general knowledge work.
- Safety: tops LatchBio's biosafety benchmark at 62.4%; claimed best-in-class refusals and jailbreak resistance.
- Benchmarks cited: improvements on GDPval and AA Briefcase (professional knowledge work); frontier price-performance on CursorBench 4.0.
- Better document and presentation creation at high effort settings.
