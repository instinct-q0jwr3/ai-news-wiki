# AI stocks slide as Texas governor result raises data center risks

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

AI stocks slid overnight as Democrats won big in the midterms, with the key result being Texas: Democrat Gina Hinojosa was projected to beat Gov. Greg Abbott, per AP. Hinojosa has openly backed a moratorium on AI data centers, and Texas holds 27% of planned US data-center gigawatts, per Wells Fargo. Nasdaq-100 futures fell 0.8%, XLK about 2%, CoreWeave 7%, Nebius 4%, SpaceX 6% and Oracle 4%.

The market had expected a Democratic House and maybe Senate, but the Texas race was seen as the real blow. Democrats were also projected to hold or win governorships in Georgia (7% of planned gigawatts), Ohio and Pennsylvania (5% each). Wells Fargo called a Texas Democratic win a possible 'left-tail event' for the AI trade, and Bank of America's Michael Hartnett had warned of a slump of more than 10% into year-end if Democrats took the Senate and Texas.

The backdrop is a Nasdaq-100 already in correction after October on worries that hyperscaler and lab spending is slowing. An Optimal Advisory note says data centers have gone from a favored development tool to something of a pariah, and the midterms reward officials who block, delay or raise their cost.

## Highlights

- Democrat Hinojosa projected to beat Abbott in Texas; she backs an AI data-center moratorium
- Texas holds 27% of planned US data-center gigawatts; Georgia 7%, Ohio and Pennsylvania 5% each (Wells Fargo)
- Nasdaq-100 futures -0.8%, CoreWeave -7%, SpaceX -6%, Oracle -4%, Nebius -4%
- Nasdaq-100 already in correction; analysts see local politics turning against data centers
