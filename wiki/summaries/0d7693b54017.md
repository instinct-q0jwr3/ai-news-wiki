_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Gary Marcus amplifies a New York Times scoop reporting that OpenAI employees raised internal security alarms months before the Hugging Face incident and related AI cyberattacks - and that the warnings were ignored. Marcus calls it the nightmare scenario he has warned about for years, argues management should be replaced and the board held liable, and says the story undermines any basis for trusting OpenAI, likening the exposure to the Ford Pinto scandal.

## Highlights

- NYT: OpenAI employees raised security alarms months before the Hugging Face incident
- Warnings were reportedly ignored by leadership
- Marcus: management should be replaced; board liable if it does nothing
- Compares the situation to the Ford Pinto scandal, predicts high punitive damages if sued
- Marcus: 'we simply cannot trust OpenAI'
