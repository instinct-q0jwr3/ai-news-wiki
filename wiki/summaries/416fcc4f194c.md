# Toronto-based Blue J, which provides AI-powered tax research software, raised a $176M Series E round led by JMI Equity at a $700M+ valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

BetaKit reports Blue J raised a $176 million Series E at a valuation above $700 million to expand AI tax research and advisory workflows. The round includes undisclosed secondary sales, so its headline size is not all new operating capital.

The company plans broader professional deliverables and a UK presence. Customer and revenue growth figures are company claims, while planned workflows are a product roadmap rather than demonstrated accuracy or a substitute for professional tax judgment.

## Highlights

- JMI Equity leads the reported round
- Secondary capital forms an undisclosed share
- Expansion targets workflows and the UK
- Growth metrics are company-reported
- Roadmap does not establish tax-advice accuracy
