## Summary

Bert Hubert recounts his appearance on the Dutch radio program Met het Oog op Morgen discussing the recent 'escaped AI' hack, and publishes the transcript. Two of his points stand out. First, a company cannot hide behind what its AI did: responsibility for an agent's actions stays with the organization that deployed it. Second, even without superhuman intelligence, an AI attacker brings enormous patience and time - it tries every gap in your security and usually finds a way in.

His conclusion is a call to action: anyone running 'sleepy' IT - systems without a dedicated, strong team watching them - is now carrying outsized risk. The post is in Dutch and largely a transcript of the broadcast segment with Sheila Sitalsing.
