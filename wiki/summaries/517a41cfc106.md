# Building the Most Diverse UMI Dataset in Robotics

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Pantheon describes building a robotics-data operation around Universal Manipulation Interface hardware and a mix of freeform and scripted collection. It says it reached more than one million unique tasks in eight weeks at $10 per hour of data, using 90 operators and custom infrastructure, and releases a densely annotated 100-hour sample.

The central bet is that diverse tasks and environments, including mistakes and recovery, teach more useful world dynamics than repeated clean demonstrations of a few tasks. Automated annotation through Argus reduces the overhead of labeling freeform behavior; scripted collection fills gaps in dexterous tasks. The scale, cost and diversity rankings are the team's own reported results.

## Highlights

- The project prioritizes diversity over repeated narrow tasks.
- UMI captures manipulation data without a complete robot setup.
- Freeform and scripted collection address different coverage gaps.
- Failures and recovery are retained as useful training material.
- Argus supplies dense annotation and quality checks.
- A 100-hour sample is released; reported economics are not independently audited.
