# Nvidia is requiring Southeast Asian customers seeking Nvidia Cloud Partner status to enroll in a program that tracks the locations of their AI chips

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Bloomberg reports, citing sources, that Nvidia requires Southeast Asian customers seeking Nvidia Cloud Partner status to enroll in a program that tracks the locations of their AI chips, stepping up monitoring of accelerators sold in the region.
