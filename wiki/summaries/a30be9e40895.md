# OpenAI Responds to Departed Safety Researchers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `openai` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

OpenAI's research leaders responded publicly to the three departed safety researchers - Jasmine, Mikita and Tomek - saying a thorough investigation found they 'violated clear policies on handling sensitive information' and uncovered 'a significant breach of trust beyond what's outlined in the letter they published'. OpenAI says it stands by the decision not to continue their employment.

The statement insists the firings were not about raising safety concerns: 'Safety and research debates happen every day at OpenAI, often spirited and highly critical. We actively encourage these discussions.' It adds the company is extremely forgiving of good-faith mistakes but cannot work without a high degree of trust - while noting it usually keeps employment matters private and won't litigate a back and forth.

## Highlights

- OpenAI: the three researchers violated policies on handling sensitive information.
- Claims a 'significant breach of trust' beyond their public letter.
- Insists the decision was not about safety advocacy - debate is encouraged daily.
- Says it keeps employment matters private but answered the letter's points directly.
