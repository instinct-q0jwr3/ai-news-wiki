# Microsoft makes Word, Excel, and PowerPoint generally available inside Copilot, which Satya Nadella calls "the new OS for work" in his Ignite keynote speech

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-19 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `microsoft`

## Summary

Ars Technica reports Microsoft made Word, Excel and PowerPoint available inside Copilot, with editable files created through the assistant interface. The Ignite coverage places that release alongside autonomous-agent plans, organizational context systems and identity and permission controls.

Availability differs across the announced components: Workspaces remains early access, and final Autopilot pricing was not announced in the report. Claimed governance effectiveness still needs demonstration. Microsoft also acknowledges that estimated assisted hours are model-generated, not direct measurements of time saved.

## Highlights

- Office file creation is described as generally available
- Generated files remain editable in Office apps
- Other announced components have different release stages
- Agent controls include identity permissions and activity logs
- Autopilot final pricing was not reported
- Estimated saved work is not directly measured productivity
