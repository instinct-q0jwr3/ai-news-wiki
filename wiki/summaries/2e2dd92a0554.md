# GM says it plans to debut OnStar AI, an in-car assistant that can execute vehicle functions and run diagnostics via voice commands, in the 2027 Cadillac Lyriq

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

General Motors used its annual GM Forward event in New York to lay out an AI-centric vision for its vehicles, headlined by OnStar AI, an in-car assistant debuting in the 2027 Cadillac Lyriq. Borrowing the name of the 30-year-old roadside-assistance service with 12 million subscribers, OnStar AI will initially handle voice commands for vehicle functions like climate control without menu-diving, plus diagnostics. Chief Product Officer Sterling Anderson frames the ambition as making the roughly 95 billion hours Americans spend driving each year more productive, relaxing, and engaging.

Longer term, GM describes the car as a personal assistant that could coordinate family logistics, such as arranging kid pickups based on which spouse's car is closer, and eventually run errands autonomously. The announcements are part of a broader raft of AI-enabled capabilities the automaker says will make the vehicle a more central and useful part of daily life, signaling that legacy carmakers now see conversational AI agents as a core differentiator rather than an add-on feature.

## Highlights

- GM will debut OnStar AI, a voice-first in-car assistant, in the 2027 Cadillac Lyriq
- Initial features: voice control of vehicle functions like air conditioning, plus diagnostics
- The OnStar brand carries 12 million subscribers from 30 years of roadside assistance
- CPO Sterling Anderson wants car time to be more productive, relaxing, and engaging
- Future scenarios include coordinating family pickups between spouses' cars
- Part of a broader GM Forward push positioning AI agents as central to future vehicles
