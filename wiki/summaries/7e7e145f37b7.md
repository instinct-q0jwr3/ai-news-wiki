# Meta's Vision Encoders in Multimodal Models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `meta`

## Summary

A controlled multimodal-model study separates three features usually bundled in a vision encoder: separate pretraining, dedicated visual capacity and placement before fusion with language. Under matched data and lifetime compute, it finds dedicated early visual processing valuable, while moving that capacity inside early shared-backbone layers can match a separate encoder.

The study also measures a time-to-first-token advantage in a specific serving setup. Its encoders see less data than major released encoders, and its results do not rule out benefits from the extra data those encoders import. The findings concern tested model scales, recipes and tasks, not every multimodal architecture.

## Highlights

- Study trains its own comparison recipes
- Lifetime compute includes earlier training stages
- Dedicated visual capacity matters in these tests
- Early in-fusion modules can match a separate encoder
- Released encoders may benefit from additional data
