# Anthropic's S-1 excludes Labs co-lead Ben Mann from its "Continuing Founders" and Founder LLC; Anthropic says "it's still fair to refer to him as a co-founder"

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

The San Francisco Standard reports that Anthropic's S-1 excludes Ben Mann - billed as 'cofounder Ben Mann' at the company's Singapore office opening two weeks earlier - from its 'Continuing Founders' and the Founder LLC, per a footnote on page 248. That means he is not among the seven founders who will jointly control more than 50.1% of shareholder voting power after the IPO.

Anthropic confirmed there has been no change to Mann's role and said 'it's still fair to refer to him as a cofounder'; his LinkedIn lists him as one. Mann, 37, co-leads Anthropic Labs with Instagram's Mike Krieger, helped develop GPT-3 at OpenAI, and left in 2021 with the seven others who founded Anthropic, citing safety priorities.
