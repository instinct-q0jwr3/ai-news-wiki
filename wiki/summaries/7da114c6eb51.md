# The Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Terence Tao hosts a guest post announcing the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence (AGMAI), hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton and online at agmai.org. The group frames the present as a historic moment for mathematics: rapid AI advances create both opportunities and urgent questions about preserving deep human understanding of the discipline.

AGMAI's purpose is to advise AI companies on their interactions with mathematical research and the mathematical community, including responsible presentation and release of mathematical results, and to serve as a channel of communication between mathematicians and the AI industry. It pledges independence (no payments from AI companies), transparency (recommendations published publicly) and accountability.

## Highlights

- New body: Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence, hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study (Princeton), agmai.org.
- Mission: advise AI companies on interactions with mathematical research and on responsible release of mathematical results.
- Independence pledge: members accept no payment from AI companies; recommendations will be published openly.
- Tags on Tao's post name Camilo De Lellis, Edward Witten, Martin Hairer, Timothy Gowers, Melanie Matchett Wood and others - a signal of the group's mathematical weight.
- Motivation: 'recent events raise urgent questions' about long-term prospects for deep human understanding of mathematics.
