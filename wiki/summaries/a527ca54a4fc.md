# Moody's: major US life insurers like Athene held $85.2B in AI debt as of June 30, up 24% from December 31, and the figure could rise to $185B by the end of 2027

## Summary

The accessible opening of a New York Times report cites Moody’s estimates that US life insurers held $85.2 billion in AI-related debt at June 30, up 24% over six months. It illustrates the exposure with a chip-purchase financing vehicle and a reported Athene investment.

The opening says the exposure is 2.1% of life insurers’ total debt and could grow to $185 billion by the end of 2027. That projection is not a committed allocation or a loss forecast. The rest of the article and underlying Moody’s analysis were unavailable, limiting assessment of definitions and risk concentrations.
