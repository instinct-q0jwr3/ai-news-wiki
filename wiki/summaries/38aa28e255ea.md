# Indeed: US data center roles pay a ~42% premium

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Data-center roles in the US offer roughly a 42% pay premium for hourly maintenance and installation workers compared with similar jobs at other businesses, according to Indeed data reported by the Wall Street Journal. Powering the AI build-out requires physical work: walking sites, checking for burning smells and monitoring for bird damage.

## Highlights

- Data-center maintenance and installation roles pay ~42% more than comparable jobs.
- The figures come from Indeed data.
- The work behind the AI build-out is hands-on: walking, smelling for burning, watching for bird damage.
