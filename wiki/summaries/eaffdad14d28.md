## Summary

The AI News Report read all 663 comments of a Nov. 4 'Ask HN: What's your current AI agentic setup?' thread (534 points, 530 people, 392 direct answers) and counted the tools 424 people said they use. It distills the most detailed setups into seven patterns. Questions covered paid plans, other models, coding harnesses, multi-agent tools and always-on platforms. Only the method and intro were read, not the tallies.
