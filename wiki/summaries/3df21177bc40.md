# Typesafe AI raises $870M at $7.5B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `typesafe`

## Summary

TypeSafe AI announced an $870 million Series A at a $7.5 billion valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz with Sequoia Capital, existing investor DCVC and a group of angel investors. The announcement is deliberately informal - 'TL;DR we raised $$$' - but makes concrete claims: a third of the Fortune 500 already use its Jev product, and it says it has saved customers millions of dollars in production.

The company says the money will go toward more 'machine-native' models, infrastructure for building smart software, and the enterprise features customers have been requesting.
