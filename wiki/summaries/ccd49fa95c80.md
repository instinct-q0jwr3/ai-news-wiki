## Summary

Cory Doctorow applies his 2001 'Metacrap' critique of the Semantic Web to agentic commerce: any plan that begins by requiring everyone else to set aside their own needs in favor of your priorities is doomed. The original agentic vision - one chatbot venturing onto the unstructured web to comparison-shop and book for you - foundered on complexity.

The fix became 'swarms' of specialized bots for searching, evaluating and transacting, which buys accuracy at the price of compute and compounding reliability failures. His deeper point: ecommerce opacity is a feature, not a bug, and merchants have no reason to make themselves legible to your agents.

## Highlights

- Doctorow: agentic commerce repeats the Semantic Web's 'Metacrap' mistake
- Single chatbot shopping gave way to specialized bot 'swarms'
- Swarms trade compute cost and reliability for accuracy
- Ecommerce opacity is deliberate; merchants won't cooperate with your agent
