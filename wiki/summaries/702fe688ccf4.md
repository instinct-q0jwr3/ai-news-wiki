# Show HN: OnKey – I'm a bad singer, so I built an app to show where I'm off-key

## Summary

OnKey is a free, open-source singing trainer for Apple Silicon Macs, built by a self-described bad singer after his previous app flooded its catalog with AI-generated songs. Play a song in Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube in the browser and sing along: the first play gives lyrics while OnKey learns the singer's melody, and afterward notes scroll by with your pitch drawn over them, ending in a per-line score report.

The pipeline runs locally: a model separates the lead vocal from the music, CREPE tracks the singer's pitch, and the GAME note model converts it to one bar per syllable. OnKey never streams or downloads music - your own app plays it - and setup asks only for microphone, system audio, and optional listen-only following of Spotify/Apple Music. Requires macOS 14.2+ on M1 or later.
