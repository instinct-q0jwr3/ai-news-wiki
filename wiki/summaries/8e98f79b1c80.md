# Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `nvidia` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

NVIDIA announces that its board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. The board says it received input from senior US government levels and is confident his return poses no national-security risk. Harvey Jones remains board chair, and Colette Kress returns from interim CEO duties while continuing as CFO.

Reinstatement is not closure of the underlying investigation. The company says its independent-director committee and counsel will continue their work, government inquiries remain ongoing, and a new compliance chief is being sought. The board's security judgment is its attributed statement, not a published independent clearance. Daring Fireball's reaction is commentary on the announcement, not another investigation.
