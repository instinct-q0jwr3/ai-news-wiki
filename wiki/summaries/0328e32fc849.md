# Ripple-65B Preview: U.S. Open-Weight SOTA on Agentic Coding

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-04 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Ricursive Intelligence, a lab building self-improving systems by co-optimizing hardware, software and model for chip design, released Ripple-65B Preview, a general-purpose variant of the proprietary model behind its chip-design platform. It is a dense 65B model pretrained on 12 trillion tokens with up to 1M context, and the lab claims a new US open-weight state of the art on agentic coding: 80.52 on TerminalBench 2.1, ahead of larger US open models such as Reflection Beam 501B (80.1), Meta Muse Spark Open 816B (76.4), Thinking Machines Inkling 975B (63.8) and NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B (56.4).

The cost claim is the headline: $32M of total R&D from scratch, including data mixing, evals, debugging, synthetic data and recipe development, which the lab says is one-tenth the cost of the previous open-weight leader. It contrasts that with Altman's $100M+ for GPT-4 and Amodei's projections of $10-100B. Rather than brute-force RL, it says it iterated on SFT and RL recipes for compute efficiency. The small dense design runs on local devices such as NVIDIA DGX Spark. Weights are on Hugging Face, Together AI, GMI Cloud and OpenRouter. All benchmark and cost figures are the lab's own.

## Highlights

- Ripple-65B Preview: dense 65B, 12T tokens, 1M context, from chip-design lab Ricursive
- Claims US open-weight SOTA on agentic coding: 80.52 on TerminalBench 2.1
- Claims $32M total R&D, about a tenth of the prior open-weight leader
- Runs locally on DGX Spark; available on Hugging Face, Together, GMI Cloud and OpenRouter
- All figures are self-reported
