# Why Texas Is Making Data Centers Wait

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

An a16z post explains why Texas is pausing new data centers. It points to the upcoming election, but the main issues are the grid interconnection queue and community trust.

Developers spam projects across several sites, many are speculative builds with no customer, and some have never plugged in a GPU, so ERCOT cannot tell which are real and does not want ratepayers funding upgrades for projects that never appear. On the community side, only 28 of 377 companies had answered a state survey on resource use, and a Hood County tax waiver request for 'Project Patriot' came without naming the company. a16z is an AI investor, so read the framing with that in mind.

## Highlights

- Texas pauses data centers, partly for election reasons
- Interconnection queue clogged with speculative projects
- ERCOT cannot tell real projects from speculative ones
- Only 28 of 377 companies answered a resource-use survey
- Code-named projects frustrate local officials
- Written by an AI investor
