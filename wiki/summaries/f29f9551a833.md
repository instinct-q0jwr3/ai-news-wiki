# Inside Inner Mongolia, China's fastest-growing AI data center region, where construction is accelerating with low building cost and no public backlash

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-08 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

SemiAnalysis visited a gigawatt-scale AI datacenter region in Inner Mongolia, which it calls China's fastest-growing and 'West Texas of China'. Unlike Texas, which faces its highest anti-datacenter sentiment, Inner Mongolia faces no such backlash. The main stop was Galaxy, a campus in Ulanqab planned for up to 800MW, similar to Stargate Abilene's core campus. Its first 120MW phase was delivered within a year at a fifth of US costs, and all four phases are built to suit two internet giants.

The zone had nearly 30,000 construction workers across 22 datacenter projects, so builders skip fully modular designs yet costs keep falling. Owner Envision, a renewable systems integrator, is highly vertically integrated: its own wind farms and dedicated power line, a blade factory across the road, and in-house 800V power modules, battery storage and substations. SemiAnalysis says the speed, scale and cost were astonishing.

## Highlights

- Galaxy campus in Ulanqab: up to 800MW, first 120MW built in under a year.
- Cost reported at about a fifth of US levels.
- Nearly 30,000 workers on 22 datacenter projects in the zone.
- Envision integrates wind farms, power line, 800V systems and batteries.
- No local anti-datacenter backlash, in contrast to Texas.
