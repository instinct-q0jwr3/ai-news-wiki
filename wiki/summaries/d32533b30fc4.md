# Switching LLMs mid-conversation is a ski-rental problem

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Edgee frames switching models during a long session as an online decision with a one-time cache-rebuilding cost and recurring savings. Its gateway combines difficulty routing with a switching policy, then replays past traffic to compare candidate settings.

The examined opening describes a commercial architecture, not a proof that the cheapest replay policy will preserve future answer quality. Capability labels, cache behavior and workload drift matter. This summary does not cover or validate every later result.

## Highlights

- Per-turn routing can waste cached history
- Switching has a one-time cost
- Past-session replay informs settings
- Capability labels constrain the optimization
- Future workload changes can break assumptions
