# Liquid AI Launches LFM3 Models for On Device AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Coverage of Liquid AI's LFM3 launch for on-device AI: a natively multimodal family (text, vision, audio) in a compact footprint with open weights, backed by AMD for Ryzen AI processors and Qualcomm for Snapdragon, with enablement on Ambarella silicon underway.

The headline benchmark: LFM3-1.7B beat Qwen3.5-2B on 65 everyday agent tasks in real macOS environments, 64% to 48% task success, using 58% fewer tokens.

## Highlights

- Second-source coverage of the LFM3 on-device launch
- Multimodal open weights for AI PCs and edge devices
- 1.7B model beats Qwen3.5-2B 64/48 on real Mac agent tasks
- AMD, Qualcomm and Ambarella platform support
