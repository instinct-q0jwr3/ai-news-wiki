# Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, and other Chinese AI companies ordered 6M Nvidia RTX Pro 5500 chips worth $70B, but expect only ~1% within four months

## Summary

Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance and other Chinese AI companies ordered 6 million Nvidia RTX Pro 5500 chips worth $70 billion but expect to receive only about 1% within four months, per Bloomberg sources. Developers expect only a small fraction of the ordered chips over the next several months.
