# Alibaba says it plans to build its first cloud regions in Turkey, Finland, and the Netherlands over the next 12 months, amid escalating US-China AI tensions

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `alibaba` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Alibaba says it plans to build its first cloud regions in Turkey, Finland and the Netherlands over the next 12 months, Bloomberg reports. The move accelerates its data-center expansion in Europe and the Middle East amid escalating US-China AI tensions.

## Highlights

- Alibaba plans first cloud regions in Turkey, Finland and the Netherlands within 12 months.
- The build-out accelerates its Europe and Middle East data-center expansion.
- It comes amid escalating US-China AI tensions.
