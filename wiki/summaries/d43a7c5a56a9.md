# An Interview with Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar About the AI Religious War

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`stratechery` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Ben Thompson interviews Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar - twenty years at the company, author of 'Mobilize', and increasingly a public intellectual - in a wide-ranging Stratechery conversation about what Sankar calls the industry's religious war over AI. His central criticism: the industry is fixated on model capability and insufficiently focused on diffusion, the unglamorous work of getting AI into production. Companies that frame AI around the top line, he argues, adopt it far more successfully than those treating it as a cost exercise, and he is frustrated that the labs refuse a systems-engineering approach to safety.

Sankar also explains what Palantir has learned deploying AI for customers: how organizations can preserve their alpha instead of regressing to the model's median, what a forward-deployed engineer actually is and does, and whether the world needs more models at all. The interview closes with his retrospective on Anthropic's battle with the US government - a case study in the politics of AI supply - and opens with the family history that shaped him: born in India, raised in Lagos until an armed robbery drove the family out.
