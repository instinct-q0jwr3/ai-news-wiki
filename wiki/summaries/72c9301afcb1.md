## Summary

The Information reports on a letter in which the White House argues it does not have to share the full draft of its voluntary AI testing framework, citing national security and commercial exemptions. The administration's 'voluntary' evaluation regime is becoming a transparency fight: the framework that companies point to as evidence of oversight is itself being withheld from public view.

## Highlights

- White House refuses to share the full draft of its voluntary AI testing framework, per a letter reported by The Information
- Cited exemptions: national security and commercial confidentiality
