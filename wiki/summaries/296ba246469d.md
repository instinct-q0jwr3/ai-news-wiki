## Summary

SWE-Bench Pro V2 is a refreshed public split of the software-engineering agent benchmark with a modified benchmark and a locked evaluation protocol, co-developed with Reflection; probe scripts, re-grade agents, per-task grades in both modes and trajectories ship with the release. The benchmark targets long-horizon tasks in public open-source repositories.

SWE-Bench Pro was built to fix predecessor weaknesses: tasks come from diverse, complex codebases (consumer apps, B2B services, developer tools), public and held-out subsets use strong copyleft licenses to reduce contamination, and a private subset uses proprietary startup codebases. It is deliberately much harder than earlier benchmarks - top models score around 23% on the public set versus 70%+ on SWE-Bench Verified - with Resolve Rate as the primary metric.

## Highlights
