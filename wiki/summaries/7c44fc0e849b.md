# German search engine Ecosia, used by government services, ditches Mistral for open-source models, including Chinese ones, citing Mistral's disappointing quality

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `mistral`

## Summary

Politico reports that Ecosia, the Berlin search engine used by government services including Germany's environment ministry and the UK's NHS, is dropping Mistral for open-source models, including Chinese ones. Founder Christian Kroll says Mistral's models are about a year behind.

Ecosia had switched from OpenAI to Mistral in May but had recurring problems, including overloaded servers; Kroll says it was too large a customer. It also doubts Mistral's environmental fit, given France's nuclear power share, and says reliance on international investors is not truly sovereign. Mistral's chief scientist Guillaume Lample invited Ecosia to test the new model with early access. It is one customer's account.
