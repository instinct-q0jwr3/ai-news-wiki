## Summary

Artificial Analysis scores GPT-6 Sol (max) at 48 on its Intelligence Index - well above the 25 median for comparable models - while rating it moderately priced at $2.00 per 1M input and $10.00 per 1M output tokens. The reasoning model was also fairly concise in evaluation, generating 77M tokens against an 88M median.

GPT-6 Sol supports text and image input with an 872k-token context window, and at 104 tokens per second it runs faster than the 75 t/s average for its class.
