# Anthropic launches the Claude Frontier Academy with a $100M commitment to train 10K Frontier Deployed Engineers by 2028, starting with Accenture, Bain, others

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic launched the Claude Frontier Academy with a $100M commitment to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by 2028. It includes a residency built around a simulated enterprise deployment, testing judgment rather than isolated technical skills.

Consulting firms such as Accenture say they will send engineers through it, describing forward-deployed engineers as people who work inside client workflows to put AI into governed production.

## Highlights

- $100M to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by 2028.
- Residency uses a simulated enterprise deployment.
- Accenture and others quote support.
- Aimed at the gap between AI pilots and production.
