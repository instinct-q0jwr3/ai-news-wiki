# Supabase raised $150M led by Singapore's GIC and agrees to acquire Turso, which offers a database optimized for AI agents, for an undisclosed sum

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Supabase raised $150M led by Singapore's GIC with CapitalG, IronArc and SquarePeg, and agreed to buy Turso, a database optimised for AI agents, for an undisclosed sum. Supabase sells a managed PostgreSQL with auth, backups and scheduled jobs, and offers features for AI workloads.

## Highlights

- $150M raise led by GIC.
- Acquiring Turso, an agent-oriented database; price undisclosed.
- Participants include CapitalG, IronArc, SquarePeg.
