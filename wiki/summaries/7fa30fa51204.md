## Summary

The Financial Times reports, citing sources, that SoftBank is seeking up to $100 billion from Gulf investors for a fund to buy companies and improve their operations with AI and other advanced technology. Masayoshi Son has held talks with senior figures. The article is paywalled, so only the excerpt was available.
