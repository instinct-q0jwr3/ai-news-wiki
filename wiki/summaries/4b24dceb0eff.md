## Summary

Simon Willison describes using Claude Fable 5.2 to generate a specimen page for comparing ways to style HTML select controls. His practical motivation is that browser defaults look different; after experimentation, he favors custom appearance, spacing, borders and a small embedded arrow image. The post includes examples described as comparisons across three browsers.

This is a personal design note and an account of an AI-assisted prototype, not a general browser-compatibility or accessibility test. Its aesthetic preference is the author's, and the plan to collect screenshots in desktop and mobile browsers should not be mistaken for evidence of exhaustive testing. No CSS was deployed or visual behavior independently tested in this editorial pass.
