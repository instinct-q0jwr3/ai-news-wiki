# Nscale's S-1: Microsoft and Anthropic are 85% of its $103B contract value

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `microsoft`

## Summary

Nscale's S-1 filing shows Microsoft and Anthropic account for 85% of its $103 billion in total contract value, Bloomberg reports, and only $2.6 billion of that contract value was active as of late August. The company's 1,000-fold increase in contracts over three years comes mainly from two clients hungry for AI capacity.

## Highlights

- Microsoft and Anthropic make up 85% of Nscale's $103B total contract value.
- Only $2.6B of contract value was active as of late August.
- Nscale's contract base grew roughly 1,000-fold in three years, driven by two clients.
