# Colorado-based Enveda, which uses AI to discover new drugs in the natural world, raised a $311M Series E at a $2B valuation, double its valuation a year ago

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Colorado-based Enveda, which uses AI to discover drugs in natural sources, raised a new round led by Baillie Gifford with participation from Iconiq and others, doubling the valuation it achieved 12 months earlier. Founded in 2019 by Recursion Pharmaceuticals early employee Viswa Colluru, Enveda's thesis is that powerful medicines can be harnessed from plants and microbes rather than synthesized from scratch.

While no AI-discovered drug has yet won FDA approval, Enveda is among the wave of companies pushing AI-discovered candidates into human trials: it is currently testing several drugs in patients, including one for severe skin conditions and another designed to help maintain weight loss after patients stop GLP-1s.

## Highlights

- New funding round led by Baillie Gifford with Iconiq participating; valuation doubled in 12 months.
- Discovers drugs from plants and microbes using AI rather than synthesizing from scratch.
- Founded 2019 by Recursion early employee Viswa Colluru.
- Multiple candidates in human trials, including severe skin conditions and post-GLP-1 weight maintenance.
- Part of the wave testing whether AI-discovered drugs can reach FDA approval.
