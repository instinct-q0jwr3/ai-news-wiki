# Dayjob: Tax – evaluating AI agents on 50 professional tax assignments

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems` `external-evaluation`

## Summary

Surge AI extended its DAYJOB benchmark family to tax: 50 professional assignments across preparation, compliance and advisory work, testing whether agents show professional judgment on underspecified problems. The flagship example: an agent correctly told 'Anna' that two proposed condo sale dates both qualified for the $250,000 home-sale exclusion, but missed that moving out in August 2024 meant she would stop satisfying the two-of-five-years residence rule around August 2027.

Across five attempts, Astra never surfaced that deadline; Opus caught it every time. The point of the benchmark is exactly that gap - a tax adviser should flag consequential issues the client didn't know to ask about. Earlier DAYJOB releases covered healthcare and finance.
