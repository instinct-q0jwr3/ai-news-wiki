# New NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Open Models for Interactive World Simulation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `nvidia`

## Summary

NVIDIA added four open-weight checkpoints to Cosmos 3 for interactive world simulation and multi-sensor driving scenes. All are post-trained from Cosmos 3 Nano with 16B trainable parameters under OpenMDW 1.1 (commercial and non-commercial use). For robotics, Sim-Bimanual predicts the visual results of actions and Sim-Transfer turns simulator controls into generated observations. For driving, synchronized camera and LiDAR generation, plus scene and traffic-signal controls, let researchers vary layouts and study a driving policy. Streaming generation links video prediction to ongoing interaction.

## Highlights

- Four open checkpoints, 16B parameters, post-trained from Cosmos 3 Nano
- Robotics: Sim-Bimanual and Sim-Transfer
- Driving: synchronized camera and LiDAR, scene and signal controls
- OpenMDW 1.1 license; streaming generation
