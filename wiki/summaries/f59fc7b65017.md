# Imprint, which helps companies offer co-branded credit cards and manage loyalty programs, raised $130M led by Khosla Ventures at a ~$1.9B valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

## Summary

The accessible opening of an Upstarts report says Imprint raised $130 million at a $1.88 billion valuation for its co-branded credit-card business. It reports more than two million cardholders and expected annual GAAP revenue of about $400 million, with management emphasizing growth faster than staffing. The article's deeper discussion of loyalty and agentic commerce is subscriber-only and was not available in the fetched text.
