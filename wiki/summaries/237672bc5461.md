# AI agents ask better questions than KPMG accountants in audit trial

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

European Business Magazine relays reporting about a KPMG trial in which more than a hundred AI agents examined an unnamed company’s accounts alongside auditors. KPMG says some agents pursued questions more deeply, but a human also had to correct a regulatory-document matching failure.

The exercise was not the basis for a statutory audit opinion. It does not establish independent audit quality, lower client fees or the ability to replace accountants; responsibility and review remained with people.

## Highlights

- Report describes an experimental parallel audit
- Positive assessment is attributed to KPMG
- A regulatory matching error required intervention
- Exercise did not approve the accounts
- Cost and quality gains remain unproven
