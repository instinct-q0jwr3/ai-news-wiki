# Anthropic reveals $900B AI spending commitments ahead of IPO

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-02 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

The Financial Times reports Anthropic has disclosed roughly $900 billion in AI spending commitments ahead of its IPO - the purchase commitments and lease agreements detailed in the amended S-1, substantially all tied to compute and infrastructure. The figure reframes the listing: investors are being asked to price not just the fastest-growing software business ever, but one of the largest infrastructure obligations in corporate history.

## Highlights

- FT puts Anthropic's spending commitments at ~$900B ahead of the IPO
- The commitments are nearly all compute and infrastructure, per the amended S-1
