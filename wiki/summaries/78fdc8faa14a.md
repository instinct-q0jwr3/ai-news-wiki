# Training a Text-to-Image Model for $21K

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Linum, a two-person lab training generative video models from scratch, shares a $21K text-to-image training run as a proof of concept toward its next video model. It uses a 2B-parameter 'Pyramid-JiT' model and the team was surprised by the quality for its size; a gallery of 249 samples is linked.

The motivation is that today's video models are too slow and expensive and lack proper controls, which makes iterating on a story impractical. Linum v3's main goal was to cut inference cost sharply, because a fast and cheap model is the first step toward creative tools that democratize animation. Only the introduction was read; training details are not covered here.

## Highlights

- Linum trained a 2B Pyramid-JiT text-to-image model for about $21K
- A proof of concept toward a cheaper, controllable video model
- Two-person lab training from scratch; 249 sample gallery published
- Training details not read
