# Google adds computer use to Gemini for macOS, letting it work across apps, files, and the web in the background, available to US AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra users

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `google` `gemini` `agentic-systems` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

TechCrunch reports computer use in Gemini’s Mac application, with a preview of cross-application work and controls to pause, stop and approve actions. The report distinguishes this from the separate Tasks and family-organization offerings.

Initial access is described as English-language use in the United States with selected paid plans. That is not worldwide availability or evidence that every proposed task works reliably. No account was connected and no computer permissions were granted.

## Highlights

- Computer use spans apps files and websites
- Preview and interruption controls are described
- Other Gemini offerings are separate
- Initial rollout has region language and plan limits
- No reliability test was performed
- No account or permissions were changed
