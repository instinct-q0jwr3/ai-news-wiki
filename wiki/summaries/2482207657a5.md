## Summary

Simon Willison quotes John Gruber's take on Meta's Muse: technically groundbreaking - every user gets their own persistent Linux VM in Meta's cloud - and packaged disarmingly, down to a cute mascot. It is the first consumer-accessible agentic AI system, and Meta has done an amazing job on the packaging.

But Gruber's power-saw analogy frames the worry: someone who buys a saw that can sever fingers knows what they bought, whereas consumers installing a cute agent have no real understanding of what it can do - especially, he adds, if it's running on your Mac. How powerful Muse is, and therefore how dangerous, is a genuinely open question most users are not equipped to evaluate.
