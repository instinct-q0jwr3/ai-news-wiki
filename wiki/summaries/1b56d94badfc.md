# One Month Without AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A developer explains why he banned AI contributions from his FOSS project LibreWeddingPlanner - pre-empting future drama more than reacting to actual AI submissions - and then, with some shame, examines his own heavy AI use at work, which he compares to an addiction: the friend who says 'you feel so powerful', the candy at the school gate, the autocomplete that starts it and the agents that follow.

His account of a month without AI describes what the habit had been doing to him: feeling dumber, lazier and a worse developer as tasks that took days collapsed into hours with his judgment quietly delegated away.

## Highlights

- He banned AI contributions from his FOSS project LibreWeddingPlanner
- Meanwhile kept using AI heavily at work - and compares it to addiction
- The spiral: enhanced autocomplete first, code-generation agents after
- One month without AI as a deliberate reset
- His verdict on the habit: it was making him dumber, lazier, a worse developer
