## Summary

Google Research and DeepMind leaders summarize AI work on disease: mapping the human brain, identifying genetic diseases in under eight hours, helping repurpose drugs, designing custom enzymes and improving cancer detection while reducing administrative burden on doctors. Many tools are open-access. The post is an overview of existing programs rather than a new result.

## Highlights

- Google overview of AI for medical research, published October 12.
- Examples: brain mapping, genetic diagnosis in under eight hours, enzyme design.
- Aims include better cancer detection and less admin for doctors.
- Several platforms are open-access.
