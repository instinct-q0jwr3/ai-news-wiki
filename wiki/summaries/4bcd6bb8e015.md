## Summary

Simon Willison quotes Amazon engineering leadership's update on the developer role in the age of AI: if your work does not benefit from current AI tooling, 'that is a finding, not a failing' - document why, share it with peers, bring it to your manager. An engineer who can explain where AI does not yet help is demonstrating exactly the judgment Amazon is asking for.
