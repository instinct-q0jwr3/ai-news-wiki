# I love LLMs, I hate hype

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-28 · confidence: high_

`geohot` `agentic-systems` `ai-safety-incidents` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

George Hotz sets the record straight: he loves LLMs. After a hacking career (2007-2014) his whole working life has been AI, and he's giddy about the new models, self-driving cars, video generation and coding agents - a Linux box running opencode on a local GLM-5.2 that can 'install tmux with the geohot configuration' from a sentence is, for him, the long-promised Year of the Linux Desktop.

What he hates is two flavors of hype. First, negative-valence hype: the constant claim that a window is closing, that you'll fall hopelessly behind - mostly designed to make you feel bad and move to San Francisco. Second, the strawman leap from 'fancy autocomplete, smart compiler, better search engine' to light-cone-owning superintelligence where everything Changes in a flash. He'll bet everything that neither is true.

## Highlights
