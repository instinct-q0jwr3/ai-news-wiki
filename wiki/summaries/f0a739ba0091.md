# Sunnny

## Summary

Sunnny is a new B2B product that consolidates a company's scattered customer data into one cleaned, identity-resolved view designed to be read by people and AI models alike. The pitch: valuable customer information sits fragmented across CRMs and other systems, and Sunnny ingests it, cleans it, resolves identities and relationships, and presents the result neatly.

Rather than a slide deck, the founders - Mikkel and Mike, a small mostly-remote Bay Area team - are launching with a live demo populated with simulated data that visitors can explore directly. Prospective customers who want to try their own data are onboarded by hand for now, with a wider release planned before the end of the year. The company emphasizes that customer data stays the customer's: it is not read, trained on, or shared, and users can manage their own encryption keys. Standard connectors cover popular CRMs, plus a universal connector, and the team is developing skills and instructions so AI models can connect to systems and populate Sunnny directly.

## Highlights
