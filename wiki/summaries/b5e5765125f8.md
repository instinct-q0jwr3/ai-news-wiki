## Summary

Gwern's January 2021 newsletter, from the month DALL·E and CLIP dropped: the roundup covers OpenAI's DALL·E (GPT-3-12B generating 1280 tokens into VQ-VAE pixels, producing illustrations and photos from text) and CLIP, plus Danbooru2020 (a large crowdsourced tagged anime-illustration dataset) and 'This Anime Does Not Exist.ai'.

Site notes: a return-to-top floating button, disableable popups, and a final popup reimplementation - the dynamic-JS version, after the clever recursive-inlining approach hit painful edge cases and proved too inefficient for browsers. A time capsule from the week image generation went public, via the OPML backfill.
