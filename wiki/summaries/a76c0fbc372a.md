# Meta launches an outbound calling feature for Muse, such as to businesses, available for paid US subscribers; Gemini and startup Instinct offer a similar option

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `google` `meta` `muse` `gemini` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

TechCrunch reports that Meta has expanded Muse's outbound calls to US businesses to paid subscribers after an earlier limited test. Examples include customer-service calls and restaurants, but the article notes that businesses may reject automated callers.

Technical ability to place a call is therefore distinct from successful service on the other end. The report describes a US paid rollout, not worldwide availability or a guarantee that an assistant can complete every request.

## Highlights

- Paid US subscribers get broader calling access
- Earlier access was a limited test
- Businesses may refuse automated callers
- Availability does not guarantee task completion
- Reported launch scope is geographic and plan-specific
