## Summary

Black Forest Labs releases FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6B-parameter open-weights model for text-to-image generation, editing and multi-reference work with up to 10 reference images. It keeps native 4K generation, precise editing and bounding boxes that place or confine edits.

## Highlights

- 5.6B open-weights image model.
- Up to 10 reference images, native 4K.
