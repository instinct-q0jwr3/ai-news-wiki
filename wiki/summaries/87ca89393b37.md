# Meta says it is letting go of employees it hired from AI safety startup Virtue AI four months after they joined the company, citing clashing work styles

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-03 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Meta told Semafor it is letting go of employees it hired from AI safety startup Virtue AI, four months after they joined in June, citing clashing work styles. Spokesperson Andy Stone said Meta Superintelligence Labs remains focused on safety, alignment and frontier risk.

Virtue had worked with Anthropic, OpenAI and NIST, and its co-founders Bo Li, Dawn Song and Sanmi Koyejo joined Meta per Axios in June.

## Highlights

- Ex-Virtue AI hires dismissed after four months.
- Meta says work styles clashed.
- Virtue's founders had joined in June.
- Meta says safety work continues.
