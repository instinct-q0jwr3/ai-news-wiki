# Don't fall into the anti-AI hype

## Summary

antirez's most-read AI essay (500K+ views) is a plea against anti-AI hype, written from explicitly sympathetic ground: he loves writing software line by line, believes in software with a human touch, hopes for a society that doesn't forget the last, and says he doesn't even want AI to succeed economically. But respecting his own intelligence means facing facts: AI is going to change programming forever.

He notes he saw it early - he left his job in 2020 to write a novel about AI, UBI and automation, and opened an AI-focused YouTube channel at the end of 2024 - yet still believed there were a few years before programming would be reshaped. He no longer believes that: recent state-of-the-art LLMs complete large subtasks, and the timeline collapsed.

## Highlights

- antirez: 'facts are facts, and AI is going to change programming forever'

- He predicted the shift early (2020 novel on AI/UBI; 2024 AI YouTube channel)
- What changed: recent SOTA LLMs completing large subtasks collapsed his timeline
- His ask: don't let your idea of software or society impair your vision
