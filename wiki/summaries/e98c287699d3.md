# Digitale autonomie in het kort bij KIVI, STT en NAE

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`bert-hubert`

## Summary

Bert Hubert, writing in Dutch, summarises his talk on digital autonomy at KIVI, STT and NAE. His main point is that European alternatives for email and word processing exist, and Europe has its own clouds, but entrenched American products have features for everything and people are used to them, so switching means missing things at first.

Some listeners took this as a call to go back to the Stone Age. He says it is not; the question is whether Europe should give up as a software supplier over a few misplaced buttons. The day ended with the view that experts must do more than consult to get better decisions in The Hague and Brussels.
