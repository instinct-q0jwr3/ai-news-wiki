# Ga niet kapot aan je eigen overheid

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`bert-hubert`

## Summary

In this Dutch-language piece ('Don't break yourself on your own government'), Bert Hubert argues that the Dutch government has written rules for its own use of cloud, AI and other algorithms - and almost never follows them. The Algemene Rekenkamer, the national audit office, found that for two-thirds of government cloud IT projects the mandatory risk assessment was not merely inadequate but entirely absent. A report by ABDTOPconsult, an internal investigation body, adds that recent tests of the obligatory fallback plans all failed.

Hubert also raises the unresolved legal question of whether GDPR article 9 special-category personal data - including health data - may be stored in American clouds at all. Government-commissioned research indicates this is permitted only with an extra protective encryption layer, a requirement everyone quietly ignores because it is too difficult. His warning: citizens should not let their own government's habitual non-compliance become their problem.
