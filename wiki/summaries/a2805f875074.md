## Summary

A report from The Information, per its sources: Anthropic is asking shareholders to approve a Palantir-style governance structure granting its seven co-founders outsized voting control ahead of a possible IPO. The body is paywalled and was not captured, so this summary rests on the headline.

On that basis: the move would concentrate control with the founding team as Anthropic approaches public markets - the same playbook Palantir used to keep founders in charge after listing. The share classes, voting ratios, and timing are unknown from the capture.
