# Apple unveils the $149 HomePod mini 2, with an S10 chip, "Sound Glow" interactive lighting, and Siri AI via a future update, in five colors, shipping October 30

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `apple`

## Summary

Apple announced the $149 HomePod mini 2 with an S10 chip, 'Sound Glow' lighting and five colors; Siri AI, described as a more capable, conversational assistant with personal context, arrives in a future software update. Paired with the new Apple TV 4K it supports four-speaker surround sound.

## Highlights

- HomePod mini 2: $149, S10 chip, five colors.
- Siri AI arrives later via software update.
- Works with the new Apple TV 4K for four-speaker surround.
