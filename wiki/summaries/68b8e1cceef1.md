## Highlights

- Dayton students chat with 'Jordan', a SchoolAI bot designed to be angsty rather than sycophantic
- Jordan asks students for advice, then grades their responses for engagement and authenticity
- The district paid SchoolAI $72,600 last year; logs are visible to staff, an evaluator, and the company
- Students at the alternative school would often otherwise be suspended or expelled
- A student: helping Jordan 'helped me kind of solve my own problems'
- The experiment illustrates schools adopting AI with little evidence or policy guidance
