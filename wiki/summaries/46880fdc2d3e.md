## Summary

Cory Doctorow: the most reliable way to lose a political battle is to let your adversary define its terms. If you're an artist and your boss frames the AI fight as being about 'IP' rather than labor, you'll be conscripted into a victory that makes him richer and you poorer.

He draws the parallel to antitrust: before the Carter era, fighting monopoly was about power - 'autocrats of trade' beyond the reach of regulators, unions or competitors - not about prices or efficiency. Letting neoclassical economists redefine the fight 50 years ago was the original blunder. The essay's title says the payload: inefficiency is bad, actually - but only if you first accept the efficiency frame, which is precisely the trap.
