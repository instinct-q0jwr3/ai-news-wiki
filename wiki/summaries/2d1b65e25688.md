# OpenAI says it plans to let third-party groups conduct technical safety evaluations of its AI models during the training, evaluation, and deployment phases

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `external-evaluation` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

OpenAI says it plans to let third-party groups conduct technical safety evaluations of its AI models during the training, evaluation and deployment phases, Bloomberg reports. The change moves outside vetting of safety risks to earlier phases of the development cycle rather than only after release.

## Highlights

- OpenAI will allow third-party technical safety evaluations of its models.
- The evaluations cover training, evaluation and deployment phases.
- The shift moves external vetting earlier in the development cycle.
