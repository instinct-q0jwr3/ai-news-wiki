# Generalization Dynamics of LM Pre-training

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-01 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

A paper on generalization dynamics during LM pre-training challenges the idea that models steadily mature from pattern-matching parrots to generalizing intelligence. Using a toy eval suite, the authors show models frequently and suddenly hop between parrot-like and intelligence-like computations, which they call mode-hopping, latching onto memorized or in-context patterns. Based on the abstract.

## Highlights

- Pre-training is not a smooth path from parrot to intelligence
- Models hop suddenly between modes
- Called mode-hopping
- Shown on a toy eval suite
- Basis: abstract
