## Summary

The New York Times details Anthropic's compute-deal spree: more than 3.5 gigawatts of expected capacity coming online in 2026, sourced well beyond Nvidia's chips to Amazon, Google and AMD among others. The reporting underpins the $895 billion in purchase commitments and lease agreements disclosed in the amended S-1 - Anthropic is locking up power and silicon across every available supplier at once.
