# Meta rolls out Muse to UK adults to help with everyday tasks, the first Big Tech company to launch an AI agent in the UK; UK watchdogs have warned about agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

POLITICO reports Meta's UK rollout of Muse to adults for everyday tasks, alongside a previous warning from the Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum about consumer risks from agents. It relays Meta's privacy assurances and says use of interactions to improve its AI requires users to switch the relevant setting off if they do not want it.

The article also attributes reports of unwanted actions in the US to earlier Reuters coverage and preserves Meta's response describing them as isolated. These are reported incidents and company statements, not tests of the UK release or a regulatory finding that it is safe. Rollout does not guarantee availability for a particular person, and no app, private-message connection, shopping task or privacy setting was activated.

## Highlights

- POLITICO reports UK adult availability
- Article places rollout alongside a regulator warning
- Training-use control is described as opt-out
- Earlier unwanted-action reports retain source attribution
- Meta characterizes the incidents as isolated
- No independent UK product or privacy audit was performed
- No account or agent permissions were changed
