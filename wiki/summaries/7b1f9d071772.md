# Oracle has scrapped a ~$7B, five-year deal to lease ~100K advanced AI chips to Tencent after yielding to pressure from the Trump administration

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-06 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Oracle has scrapped a roughly $7 billion, five-year deal to lease about 100,000 advanced AI chips to Tencent after yielding to pressure from the Trump administration, per Financial Times sources. It reflects tension for US AI infrastructure groups doing business with Chinese tech giants.

## Highlights

- Oracle cancelled a ~$7B, five-year lease of ~100K AI chips to Tencent
