# Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-20 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Architect Labs describes Frostbite, an interaction-net CPU design developed by iterating software, instruction sets and hardware together. It reports a 4.59-fold performance-per-watt gain across 25 workloads against an undisclosed dual-issue in-order core, with smaller gains on held-out tasks.

The results use pre-silicon power estimates and a particular area-matched comparison, not measured production chips or a win over every modern CPU. The post describes formal contracts and recalibration after early power estimates diverged sharply. Wider industry benchmarks and larger out-of-order comparisons are future work.

## Highlights

- Interaction nets expose locality and ownership
- Compiler and hardware are co-designed
- Ten search workloads differ from fifteen held-out tasks
- Comparison core identity is undisclosed
- Power is estimated before silicon
- Broader benchmarks remain future work
