# Teachers Using AI Say It Frees Up Time for What Matters Most

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Walton Family Foundation and Gallup survey finds about three in five US K-12 educators used AI tools for work last school year, and nearly two-thirds of them say it freed time for the parts of the job that matter most.

The wider study looks at 'mattering', the sense of being valued, and finds teachers get it most from students.

## Highlights

- About 60% of K-12 teachers used AI last year.
- Nearly two-thirds of users say it freed up time.
