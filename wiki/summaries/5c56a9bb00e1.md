# Jensen's Bizarre Adventure

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`ed-zitron`

## Summary

Ed Zitron covers the Jensen Huang crisis: reports that the Nvidia CEO had a relationship with a Chinese businesswoman described as a Ministry of State Security asset, and Huang's apology memo to his wife. The piece connects this to Nvidia's long lobbying to loosen chip export rules for China.

It also digs into Megaspeed: Bloomberg found that most of Megaspeed's $2.4 billion of Bianca boards, which carry Nvidia's GB200 and GB300 chips, were unaccounted for at the sites Nvidia described to Washington. After questions, Nvidia visited separate warehouses and said the boards were there and not diverted to China, but declined to say whether the stored volume matches Malaysian customs records. Megaspeed did not answer queries. A polemic resting on Bloomberg and other reporting.

## Highlights

- Huang apologized to his wife and family in a memo
- Zitron links the scandal to Nvidia's China lobbying
- Megaspeed: most of $2.4B in Bianca boards unaccounted for
- Nvidia says it found the hardware in other warehouses, not diverted
- Open questions on matching customs records
