# Brown student maps the hidden global labor powering AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Brown University undergraduate has built an interactive map of the hidden human labor behind AI: the low-paid data workers, mostly in the Global South, whose annotation and moderation make curated algorithmic life possible. Cunyan Ma, working with Brown's Center for Technological Responsibility, Reimagination and Redesign, aggregates existing data-worker databases, her own collection and news reports on working conditions into a single freely available map linking tech companies and their intermediaries to workers around the world.

Her stated motivation is to visualize 'AI colonialism' in a way that is impactful and immediately accessible - putting the supply chain of human judgment back into a picture that usually shows only models and machines.

## Highlights

- A Brown student built an interactive map linking tech companies to data workers worldwide
- The map aggregates worker databases, original collection and news reports on conditions
- It focuses on low-paid Global South labor behind AI annotation and moderation
- Creator Cunyan Ma frames the goal as visualizing 'AI colonialism' accessibly
- The project is freely available online
