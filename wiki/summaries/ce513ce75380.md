# Open-Weight AI Models Seize Token Lead, but Proprietary Still Make the Money

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Open-weight models processed 56% of production tokens through Vercel's AI Gateway in August, up from 36% in July and under 10% in December 2025 - the first time they have led token volume. Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch said August 22 set a record with 62% of traffic.

Volume is not revenue: open-weight models captured only about 14% of estimated spending, since closed-weight tokens cost roughly 7.8 times as much on average. The figures come from Vercel's usage and estimated-spending data, reported by Techstrong.ai.

## Highlights

- Open-weight models hit 56% of AI Gateway production tokens in August (36% in July, <10% in Dec 2025)
- Record single day: 62% open-weight share on August 22, per Guillermo Rauch
- Only ~14% of estimated spending despite the volume lead
- Closed-weight tokens cost ~7.8x open-weight tokens on average
- Source: Vercel AI Gateway usage and spending data via Techstrong.ai
