# Note-taking tool Otter.ai raised $320M in non-dilutive capital from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund; PitchBook says it raised $50M prior to this round

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Otter.ai has raised $320 million in non-dilutive capital from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, a vehicle that finances marketing spend and takes returns on its results rather than equity, earmarked for enterprise growth, go-to-market, international expansion, and product innovation. The nearly decade-old company crossed $100 million in ARR last year and says 40 million users have used the product, up from 25 million, but shared no monthly-active or churn figures, a pointed omission in a crowded meeting-notetaker market that includes Fireflies.ai, Granola, Read AI, Calendly, Wispr, and Superhuman's Fathom. The category's race is to connect meeting data to enterprise systems and automate workflows; new CMO Alex Gay, from Superhuman, says Otter is targeting people in back-to-back meetings whose transcripts can become actionable roadmaps.

## Highlights

- $320M non-dilutive round from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund
- CVF finances marketing and is repaid from its results, not equity
- Only ~$50M of prior equity funding, last raised in 2021 (PitchBook)
- Crossed $100M ARR last year; 40M cumulative users, up from 25M
- No MAU or churn disclosed in a crowded notetaker market
- Rivals: Fireflies, Granola, Read AI, Calendly, Wispr, Superhuman/Fathom, and more
- Strategy: turn meeting transcripts into enterprise workflow automation
