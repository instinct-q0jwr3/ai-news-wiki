# Claude Status – Elevated errors for multiple models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic's status page reports an incident of elevated errors across multiple Claude models, now resolved. Impact ran from 5:50pm PT / 00:50 UTC to 7:10pm PT / 02:10 UTC on September 21-22, affecting claude.ai, the Claude API, Claude Code and Claude Cowork.

The timeline: investigation opened at 00:57 UTC into errors on Claude Mythos 5.1, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5; the cause was identified by 01:35; by 02:11 Fable 5 and 5.1 and Mythos 5 and 5.1 had returned to normal success rates with Opus 5 still being fixed; at 02:35 UTC the incident was marked resolved with monitoring in place.
