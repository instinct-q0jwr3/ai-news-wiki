# AI Users have workdays up to 90 min longer than non-users

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

ActivTrak's Productivity Lab analyzed digital work patterns of 9,140 knowledge workers at 467 enterprises from April to September 2026. It found AI users have workdays up to 90 minutes longer than non-users, and heavy users switch apps more often with shorter stretches of uninterrupted focus. The data is behavioral, not survey-based; the framing separates adopting AI tools from integrating them well.

## Highlights

- AI users: workdays up to 90 minutes longer
- Heavy users switch apps more, focus less
- 9,140 workers at 467 enterprises, Apr-Sep 2026
- Behavioral data rather than self-reports
