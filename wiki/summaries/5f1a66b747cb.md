# Port of the TypeScript compiler, checker and lsp to Rust, by LLM

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The ts-rust repository ports Microsoft's native TypeScript compiler, checker and language server from Go to Rust, written almost entirely by LLMs. The author says he never read the code and that it is an early release, while also claiming full compatibility on every real-world project he tested, a claim the README itself does not back with evidence.

The account of the process is the interesting part. OpenAI models spent over $400,000 of API-priced tokens and wrote 1.3 million lines of Rust over months without getting past about 84% compatibility. Claude Opus 5.5 then started from scratch, had a working version in 10 hours and went further in about a tenth of the time, at roughly $24,000 of API-priced spend over two weeks, which used 925% to 983% of his $200 plan's weekly limits. These figures are the author's own.
