## Summary

A Hacker News poster reproduces a message presented as a GitBook incident update. The quoted notice describes compromised infrastructure credentials, service isolation, credential replacement and possible access to integration secrets and assistant questions.

The available source is a customer’s pasted message, not a directly authenticated company bulletin. Its account of an AI-assisted attack and the affected data should remain attributed; it does not establish that every GitBook customer or document was affected.

## Highlights

- Source is a pasted customer notice
- Credential exposure is alleged
- Services were reportedly isolated and restored
- Integration tokens and questions are named
- Universal customer impact is not established
