# CoreWeave stock drops 15%+ and its bond yields near 15%, over concerns that some tech companies are borrowing too heavily for the AI build-out; ORCL drops 8%+

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-18 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

CoreWeave shares fell about 16% and bond yields jumped, with its 2032 notes near 14.8% and 2031 notes about 14.9%, on investor fear that tech companies are borrowing too heavily for the AI build-out. CEO Mike Intrator told staff the market is acting irrationally and that the backlog more than covers its debt. A Wells Fargo strategist says the era of easy money is fading and investors now demand more risk compensation (CNBC).

## Highlights

- CoreWeave stock down about 16%; bond yields near 15%.
- CEO says backlog more than supports debt obligations.
- Strategist: investors now demand more compensation for AI risk.
