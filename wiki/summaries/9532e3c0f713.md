# At its Accelerate conference, Amazon says it is opening its seller tools to third-party AI agents, starting with Claude, in beta for US merchants

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `amazon` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

At its Accelerate seller conference in Seattle, Amazon announced new AI tools for independent sellers, led by a plugin that lets merchants run their Amazon businesses from Anthropic's Claude or Amazon's own Quick assistant - checking inventory, adjusting prices and updating listings without opening Seller Central. Amazon says about 90% of sellers already use some form of outside AI, and VP Mary Beth Westmoreland described the vision as sellers never having to log into Seller Central at all.

Amazon is also releasing background automations that monitor a seller's business and act with approval, plus an upgraded Seller Assistant that remembers pricing patterns, inventory cycles and growth goals. The stakes are large: independent sellers account for over 60% of units sold on Amazon, and seller fees brought in $46.8B in Q2 - more revenue than AWS - while those fees sit at the center of the FTC's antitrust case, set for trial in March 2027.

## Highlights

- Plugin lets sellers manage their business from Anthropic's Claude or Amazon's Quick assistant.
- Amazon says ~90% of sellers already use outside AI for parts of their operations.
- New background automations act on the business with seller approval; Seller Assistant gains memory.
- Independent sellers are over 60% of units sold; seller fees were $46.8B in Q2, above AWS revenue.
- Seller fees are central to the FTC antitrust case, trial set for March 2027.
