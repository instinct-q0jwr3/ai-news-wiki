## Summary

Simon Willison quotes a Hacker News comment by Jake Boggan reacting to the news that Barnette's Conjecture - a graph theory problem he has worked on, off and on, for 24 years - appears to have been proven by an AI, listed as problem 180 on openai/math.

Boggan describes moving to Budapest to study graph theory 'among the greats', spending thousands of hours on the conjecture, and briefly believing last summer that he had solved it himself. Hearing that it is solved leaves him oddly sad, 'like hearing an ex-girlfriend died suddenly in a car crash' - a glimpse of the mixed emotions researchers may feel as AI systems close out problems that defined parts of their lives.
