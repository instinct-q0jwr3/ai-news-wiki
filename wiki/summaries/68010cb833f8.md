# Nano Banana Pro is the best AI image generator, with caveats

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`max-woolf`

## Summary

Max Woolf follows up his exhaustive Nano Banana prompt-engineering analysis with a review of Nano Banana Pro, Google's image model with five touted additions: high-resolution output, better text rendering, grounding with Google Search, thinking/reasoning, and better use of image inputs. His first relief: Pro is not Nano Banana 2 and does not obsolete the base model - it costs far more per generation (AI Studio requires payment, though the Gemini chat app offers free watermarked generations) and may not even be the best option depending on your intended style.

So he reruns his test suite of cases tuned to the base model's strengths and weaknesses. The infamous 'make me into Studio Ghibli' style transfer, which objectively failed on his mirror selfie in base Nano Banana, now passes - 'you can nit on whether the style is truly Ghibli or just something animesque,' but Pro understands the intent, and beats what ChatGPT managed. Code-in-image generation is next on the docket (the excerpt cuts off at a recursive Fibonacci test). His earlier work showed the base model could handle a comical number of simultaneous constraints - three precisely specified kittens, heterochromatic eyes matching fur colors, rule-of-thirds composition, Victorian mansion, no text or watermarks - so the bar for 'better' is genuinely high.
