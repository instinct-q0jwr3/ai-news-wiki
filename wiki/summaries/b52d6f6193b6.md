# SK Telecom unit SK Hyper is in talks with tech firms and investors to finance 15GW of data center capacity in South Korea by 2035; the first 5GW will cost $350B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

SK Telecom unit SK Hyper is in talks with big tech firms and financial investors to fund an envisioned 15GW of data-center capacity in South Korea by 2035, with the first 5GW expected to cost $350 billion, Bloomberg reports - one of the largest single-country AI infrastructure ambitions yet disclosed.

## Highlights

- SK Hyper in talks to finance 15GW of South Korean data-center capacity by 2035 (Bloomberg)
- First 5GW phase projected to cost $350B
- Talks involve big tech firms and financial investors
