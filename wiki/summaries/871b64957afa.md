# Anthropic blocks users for anything touching training larger models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-31 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

A single tweet, worth noting as a data point in the usage-policy wars: researcher Jiahao Shao says Anthropic has tagged his account for training a competitor and now blocks or degrades anything touching RFT, SFT or training larger models - Opus 5.5 sessions downgraded to Opus 5 or killed outright. Shao insists he never distilled Claude: the rollouts came from a strong open-source model, and Claude was only used to submit jobs and run analysis. His closing jab - that Anthropic built its models on the world's data and now treats paying users doing research automation as the enemy - gave the post 106K views in its first hours.

There is no way to verify either side from the outside: Anthropic has not commented, and enforcement telemetry is private. But the complaint fits a widening pattern of labs policing how their models' outputs are used, and of users discovering that enforcement is opaque, automated and hard to appeal.
