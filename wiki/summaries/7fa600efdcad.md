# OpenAI's B2B Marketplace: The Hyperscaler Of AI Apps

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `openai`

## Summary

OpenAI's DevDay brought Dots, new models and team environments, but the under-the-radar announcement is its B2B Marketplace: launched September 29 with 32 partners, it lets enterprise customers put part of an existing OpenAI commitment toward partner products. The most notable partner is Baseten - OpenAI customers can now spend their commitments on open models served by Baseten inside Codex or via the Responses API, routing each agentic task to the best-fit model across open and closed.

The piece frames this as OpenAI becoming the 'hyperscaler of AI apps' and a response to the summer's open-model surge: open-weight token volumes have passed closed ones, though Ramp's AI Index puts only 5% of business spend on open weights, partly because aggressive price cuts from OpenAI and Anthropic defend share. The Marketplace is another lever in that fight.

## Highlights

- OpenAI Marketplace launched at DevDay (Sep 29) with 32 partners.
- Enterprise customers can spend existing OpenAI commitments on partner products.
- Baseten partnership: open models usable inside Codex and the Responses API under OpenAI commitments.
- Context: open-weight token volumes surpassed closed, but only ~5% of business AI spend (Ramp).
- Framing: OpenAI positioning itself as the hyperscaler of AI apps.
