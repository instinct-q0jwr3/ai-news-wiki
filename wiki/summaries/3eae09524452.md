# New York Ban AI Toys for 5 Years

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

New York's governor announces signing S.9408A/A.11144B, establishing a five-year moratorium on manufacturing, distribution and sale in New York of internet-connected toys with integrated conversational AI. The announcement says the law takes effect ninety days after signing and provides civil penalties up to $15,000 per day for each violation.

The official release is a primary source for the announcement, but it is not the full statutory text or an exhaustive treatment of definitions and exceptions. It concerns a specified category of connected chatbot toys, not every AI product, all electronic toys or immediate prohibition on the signing date. Consumer-test claims in the release retain their attribution.

## Highlights

- Governor announces a signed five-year moratorium
- Scope is internet-connected conversational AI toys
- Manufacturing, distribution and sale are named activities
- Effective date is ninety days after signing
- Release states penalties up to $15,000 per day per violation
- Full legal definitions and exceptions were not independently parsed
- Announcement does not ban every use of AI
