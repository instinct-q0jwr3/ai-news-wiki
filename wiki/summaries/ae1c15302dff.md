## Summary

Gwern's February 2021 newsletter: a monthly roundup of gwern.net revision-history changes and notable machine-learning reading. Site updates include movable, stickied, full-screenable popups ('another step towards Windows-95-in-the-browser').

The ML picks cover controllable neural text generation (Lilian Weng), a review of language-model fine-tuning advances (Sebastian Ruder), Reynolds & McDonell's prompt-programming work (a good zero-shot prompt beating the original 10-shot Fr→En translation), Zhao et al. on calibrating unstable few-shot prompts, and TransGAN's two-transformer GAN. A time capsule from the prompt-engineering era, surfaced via the OPML blog batch.
