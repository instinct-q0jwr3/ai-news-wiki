## Summary

A Hacker News discussion asks which concrete AI-related skills a moderately technical person can learn independently for economic benefit. Replies range from practical experimentation and business applications to communication, problem definition and reviewing generated code.

The thread does not settle on a validated curriculum or show that any skill guarantees employment or income. Its useful distinction is between knowing a tool’s commands and being able to define a worthwhile task, assess the result and apply it in a real setting.

## Highlights

- Question concerns self-directed learning
- Replies emphasize practice and task definition
- Communication is proposed as a transferable skill
- Suggestions are anecdotal
- No career outcome is established
