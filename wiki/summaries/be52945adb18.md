# Analysis of open weights: six models from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax, and Ant Group released since April carry fingerprints of export-controlled Blackwell GPUs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `deepseek` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

An analysis by Unemployment Labs, highlighted by Techmeme, finds numerical initialization patterns in six public models consistent with Blackwell hardware. The authors say this supports a training-hardware inference but does not prove the chips' location, access route or any export-control violation.

## Highlights

- Public weights provide the evidence
- Fingerprint concerns initialization
- Training hardware is an inference
- No legal violation is established by the method
