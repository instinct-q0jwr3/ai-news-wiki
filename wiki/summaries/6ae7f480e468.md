# A Series of Unfortunate Events for OpenAI Users

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

A critical post on how the past month went for Codex users. After heavy usage resets and the arrival of Opus 5.5, which the author says matches Astra at a tenth of the cost, OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol, which he calls its worst model ever and weaker than the older Luna. Just before DevDay, OpenAI reopened its $200 plan but cut usage from 20x to 10x at the same price, which set off an immediate backlash. It is one writer's opinionated account, not measured data.

## Highlights

- Author says Opus 5.5 matches Astra at about a tenth of the cost.
- Calls GPT-6 Sol the worst model in OpenAI's history.
- $200 plan reopened with usage cut from 20x to 10x.
- Opinion piece; claims not independently tested.
- Basis: first part of the article.
