# A profile of White House OSTP Director Michael Kratsios, an ex-Peter Thiel aide who is among a handful of players steering the Trump administration's AI policy

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-01 · updated: 2026-11-04 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Harry Lambert's Colossus profile of Michael Kratsios opens with a scene money cannot usually buy: six men who run or own companies worth around $18 trillion - Jensen Huang, Elon Musk, Michael Dell, Satya Nadella, Lisa Su and Sergey Brin - locked in an auditorium by the Secret Service at the Donald J. Trump Institute of Peace, waiting to receive national medals, with Kratsios, the man who assembled them, called away to see the president. Left alone, the circle talks shop: Huang quizzes Musk on functional safety ('By any means necessary,' Musk laughs), and the conversation turns to compute scarcity - Brockman hounding Musk for it, Brin being asked too, and Musk marveling at Micron's 87 percent gross margin and $85 billion in net profit, which he calls a supply-chain price shock like nothing in his life.

The profile's subject is the connective tissue: Kratsios, an ex-Peter Thiel aide now serving as Trump's chief scientific advisor and OSTP director, is among the handful of players steering the administration's AI policy - the fight to regulate AI and reform American science runs through his office. His own barb for the safety crowd captures the posture: the people 'sounding the alarm' are the only ones who can solve the problem, and rather than solving it they dump it on others - 'the solution to all their problems is not Chuck Schumer.'
