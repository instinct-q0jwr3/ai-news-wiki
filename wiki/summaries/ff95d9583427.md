# Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon says AI companies are "starting to do phones" and asking Qualcomm to support running 100B-parameter local LLMs continuously by 2028

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `small-specialist-models` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon tells Alex Heath that AI companies are 'starting to do phones' - and are asking Qualcomm to support running 100B-parameter local LLMs continuously by 2028. He expects an AI-driven smartphone supercycle as agents start using our apps and acting on our behalf, with phones staying central and smart glasses becoming the biggest AI wearable category.

Qualcomm has stakes in several competing visions: chips for Android phones, Meta's smart glasses, and coming devices from AI companies like OpenAI. The conversation also covers China-US consumer AI adoption differences, Qualcomm's Modular acquisition as a developer alternative to Nvidia's software stack, and what 6G could change.
