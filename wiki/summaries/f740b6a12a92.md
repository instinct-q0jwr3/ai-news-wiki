# Anthropic launches roadshow for proposed initial public offering

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its IPO, offering 825 million Class A shares: 801.4 million from the company and 23.6 million from existing holders, who will keep their proceeds. Underwriters get a 30-day option on 123.75 million more shares. The expected price is $70 to $78 and the stock is applied for on Nasdaq under 'ANT'.

## Highlights

- 825M shares: 801.4M new, 23.6M from existing holders
- Price range $70 to $78
- Nasdaq listing applied for as ANT
- 30-day option for 123.75M extra shares
