# Muse Voice

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `muse`

## Summary

Meta's announcement rolls out real-time voice conversation with Muse, previewed at Connect 2026, in the Muse app on mobile, web and Mac; tap the phone icon to start. Meta pitches thinking out loud, planning trips while walking or cooking, and rehearsing interviews or pitches. An avatar reacts with expressions and gestures; users choose voice only, side by side or full screen, and pick from hundreds of voices (a New Yorker, a pirate). It uses Muse Realtime Avatar and Realtime Voice technology.

## Highlights

- Real-time voice with Muse on mobile, web and Mac
- Expressive avatar: voice only, alongside or full screen
- Hundreds of voices to choose from
- Use cases: trip planning, interview and pitch rehearsal
