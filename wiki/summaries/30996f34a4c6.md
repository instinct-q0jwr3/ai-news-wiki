# Analysis: Anthropic's subscriptions offer ~5x more API-equivalent value per month than OpenAI's for agentic workloads with Claude Opus 5.5 vs. GPT-6.1 Sol

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `anthropic` `meta` `xai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

SemiAnalysis argues that consumer subscription plans are heavily subsidised yet matter far more than their revenue share suggests. They are about 10% of Anthropic's revenue but can take over 40% of inference compute, cutting blended revenue per megawatt by roughly $36M. A monthly payment buys credits, and each model and token type burns credits at ratios that differ sharply from API prices, so the value of a plan depends on those limits. The piece frames resets and limit changes as marketing and customer-acquisition tools, citing OpenAI's generous resets and Codex growth, and says modelling lab finances requires understanding them.

## Highlights

- Subscriptions: about 10% of revenue, 40%+ of inference compute.
- Roughly $36M lower blended revenue per MW.
- Plans grant credits; credit ratios differ from API price ratios.
- Resets work as customer-acquisition marketing.
