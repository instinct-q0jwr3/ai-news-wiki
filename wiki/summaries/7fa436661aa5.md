## Summary

JA Westenberg's essay 'The Art of Agency Laundering' - subtitled 'On committees, coins and Claude' - examines how responsibility gets diffused when decisions are delegated: to committees, to chance, and now to AI systems. The full text sits behind the publication's free-post paywall, so this summary is based on the title and framing: 'agency laundering' names the move of passing a decision through an impersonal process so that no one remains answerable for it, with Claude-era AI as the newest rinse cycle.
