## Summary

Simon Willison's llm-gemini 0.38.1 release note fixes a missing extra-high reasoning setting for Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Neon. He distinguishes those models from Carbon, which he says supports only low, medium and high reasoning.

His accompanying pelican illustration compares Hydrogen at high and extra-high settings: output rose from 5,311 to 31,477 tokens and the stated cost from 3.99 to 23.61 cents. The example makes a larger reasoning budget's cost visible, but one visual prompt is not a general model-quality evaluation or a current price quote for every workload. The plugin and model calls were not run here.

## Highlights

- Dot release exposes an omitted reasoning option
