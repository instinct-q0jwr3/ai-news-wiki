# Introducing Replit Missions

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-23 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Replit introduces Missions as outcome-based work carried out by agent teams within agreed budgets and approval boundaries. Its DataCat case study describes building a self-hostable monitoring platform, while separate NumPy and simulated-commerce examples illustrate reported search and optimization gains.

The article explicitly limits its comparisons: some builds use one run per setup, hosted production services were not the benchmark, and an AI judge checking completion is not an independent human audit. Budget exhaustion can stop a mission with the outcome unresolved. The examples remain company-reported rather than general guarantees.

## Highlights

- Missions specify outcomes budgets and approval boundaries
- Teams explore and test alternatives in parallel
- DataCat is an internal company case study
- Comparisons include single-run limitations
- AI completion judgment is not independent assurance
- An exhausted budget can leave the task incomplete
