# How Musk and Benioff pledged to rename SpaceXAI and AIForce, while Nadella and Nvidia adopted "super intelligence", as Silicon Valley falls in line with Trump

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

## Summary

The Verge documents Silicon Valley falling in line with President Trump's rebrand of artificial intelligence as 'super intelligence'. Musk promised to rename SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI; hours after Trump declared that the White House considers anyone using 'Artificial Intelligence' instead of the new term 'THE ENEMY!', Marc Benioff said Salesforce would rename AIForce to SIForce; Jensen Huang attended the summit where an executive order required government agencies to say 'super intelligence', and Nvidia's $1 billion commitment to scientific advancement included 'super intelligence research'.

Satya Nadella and Microsoft are the latest to adopt the phrasing. Most of the broader population still says AI, but the industry's most powerful figures - each with something to lose - have capitulated to the rebrand.

## Highlights

- Trump rebranded AI as 'super intelligence'; an executive order mandates the term in government.
- Musk: SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI. Benioff: AIForce to SIForce.
- Nvidia's $1B science commitment includes 'super intelligence research'.
- Nadella and Microsoft are the latest adopters.
- Trump called users of 'Artificial Intelligence' THE ENEMY!
