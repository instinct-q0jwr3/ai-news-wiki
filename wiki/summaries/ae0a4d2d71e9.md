# Delaware-based Causa Prima, which develops an agent-to-agent AI platform for managing B2B payments, resolving billing disputes, and more, raised a $20M seed

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-16 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Delaware-based Causa Prima announced a $20 million seed round, bringing total funding to $30 million, for an agent-to-agent platform that has AI agents act for companies to resolve billing disputes and speed up payments between businesses and suppliers. Cofounder Maex Ament says today's accounts payable and receivable work is still manual and the answer is a network of agents (Fortune).

## Highlights

- Causa Prima raised a $20M seed, $30M total.
- Agents for two companies negotiate payments and disputes.
- Targets manual accounts payable and receivable processes.
