## Summary

Gary Marcus says the market's mayhem proves his Nov. 4 post that tech stocks would crash once AI firms no longer get a free ride. He notes the previous day was mixed: chipmakers and neoclouds like CoreWeave were hit while Amazon, Apple and Google rose. Theories included rotation into stronger balance sheets and investors wanting hyperscalers to cut capex after the election showed public resistance to data centers. The rest is paywalled.
