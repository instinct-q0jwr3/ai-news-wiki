# Elon Musk's SpaceXAI to add another 660,000 AI GPUs this year

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `xai` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Elon Musk says SpaceXAI will bring another 660,000 Nvidia GPUs online this year, pushing the Colossus buildout past the million-GPU goal he announced nearly two years ago. Per his X post, 220,000 GB300s will be operational next week, another 220,000 in November, and a further 220,000 by late December 'if we get lucky'. Added to the existing fleet (Colossus 1: 150k H100, 50k H200, 30k GB200; Colossus 2: 110k GB200, 440k GB300), that would put SpaceXAI at 1.1 million GB300s and 1.44 million GPUs total. The piece notes Colossus 1's mixed Hopper/Blackwell setup was inefficient for training Grok, so Musk rented it to Anthropic for inference, while Colossus 2 is all-Blackwell to avoid bottlenecks.
