## Highlights

- Musk: 220k GB300s next week, 220k in November, 220k more by late December 'if we get lucky'
- Would take SpaceXAI to 1.1M GB300s and 1.44M total GPUs in operation
- Colossus 1: 150k H100, 50k H200, 30k GB200; Colossus 2: 110k GB200, 440k GB300
- Colossus 1 was inefficient for training Grok, so it is rented to Anthropic for inference
- Colossus 2 is all-Blackwell to avoid training bottlenecks
- SpaceXAI is three years old, against rivals six (Anthropic) and ten (OpenAI) years old
