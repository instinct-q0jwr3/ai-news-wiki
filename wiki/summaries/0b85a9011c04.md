# Ricursive releases Ripple-65B Preview, a general-purpose variant of its AI model for advanced chip design, under Apache 2.0, costing $0.60 per 1M output tokens

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-06 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

VentureBeat reports Ricursive released Ripple-65B Preview, a general-purpose variant of the model behind its chip-design platform, under Apache 2.0. It is on Hugging Face and via Together AI, GMI Cloud and OpenRouter, at $0.20 per million input and $0.60 per million output tokens. Ricursive claims a lead over selected US open models on terminal and software engineering benchmarks, but its results trail other open models elsewhere, and the advertised million-token context exceeds the 16,384-token limit listed by OpenRouter.

## Highlights

- Apache 2.0 release of Ripple-65B Preview
- $0.20/$0.60 per million tokens on OpenRouter's GMICloud
- Lead claimed only against selected US models
- Context limit mismatch: 1M advertised vs 16,384 listed
