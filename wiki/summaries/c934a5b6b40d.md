## Summary

Death Plan Switch is a self-hosted dead man's switch built by a solo web-studio owner in regional New South Wales, born from a real fragility: his clients' sites, domains and hosting all run through him, and if he died nobody would know where anything was. His plan: everything the business runs on lives in one encrypted document, his backup developer holds the password, his lawyer a second copy - and this tool is the missing middle that notices he has gone quiet and tells the right people in the right order. It emails a weekly check-in; if he stops responding, a staged escalation runs - first a next-of-kin confirmation, then client notifications, at most 13 days after the last check-in. Design details show care: opening the email never counts (scanners open links), only pressing the button does, and a false alarm can be cancelled at any stage, with everyone already contacted told it was a mistake.
