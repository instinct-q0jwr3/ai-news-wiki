# SoftBank looks to issue $10B+ in debt for its OpenAI investment

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `softbank`

## Summary

SoftBank is looking to issue $10 billion and €1 billion in debt to fund its OpenAI investment, Bloomberg reports, in what would be one of the biggest junk bond sales ever. The package seeks the equivalent of more than $11 billion.

## Highlights

- SoftBank seeks to issue $10B plus €1B in debt for its OpenAI investment.
- The sale would rank among the biggest junk bond deals ever.
- Total sought: the equivalent of more than $11B.
