# Power approval set to delay Oracle's Wisconsin AI datacenter

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

The Register reports Oracle's Wisconsin AI datacenter, Project Lighthouse by Vantage in Port Washington, may miss its 2027 start because the transmission infrastructure awaits regulatory approval, per research firm Aterio. Oracle targets delivering capacity in the second half of next year.

Separately Oracle reportedly sent a force majeure notice to a Blue Owl subsidiary developing Project Jupiter in New Mexico, per Bloomberg.

## Highlights

- Project Lighthouse could slip past 2027 over power approval.
- Aterio warns of real delay risk.
- Oracle reportedly sent a force majeure notice on Project Jupiter, New Mexico.
