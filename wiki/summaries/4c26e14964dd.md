- Earlier this year I was lured to the NLUUG spring conference, and after my presentation ( on machine learning from scratch ), the two large presentation rooms were joined for some additional announcements.
- Now, I have a useful low-grade case of imposter syndrome , where I always wonder if what I do is in any way special or good enough .
- But still, have I done enough to win such an award?
- The NLUUG sure thinks so, and they were kind enough to write it up .
- So after pondering it a bit, I’ve decided to embrace the NLUUG Award, and am very proud to have received it!
- Finally it may be good to know that this award is not only useful to make me feel good.
- So thank you NLUUG, please keep up the great work, and know that I will forever be grateful!
