# South Korea targets ' highest performance' sovereign AI model

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

South Korean President Lee Jae Myung unveiled the country's largest-ever budget on Monday, telling the National Assembly the government will 'boldly invest in AI and future growth engines.' Total 2027 spending rises 12.8% to 820.9 trillion won (~$610 billion), with 21.3 trillion won for semiconductors, physical AI and AI data centers - nearly double the 2026 level - plus a separate 4.7 trillion won toward a sovereign AI model Lee says will be the 'world's highest performance.'

The budget doubles as an industrial-workforce plan: 2,000 specialists trained for semiconductor design, expanded AI education for students and job seekers, and university 'contract departments' linked to Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix that co-design courses and hire graduates before they finish. Defense spending rises 8.2% to 73.3 trillion won, with Lee promising a 'smart, elite, and powerful military' built on AI, robots and drones. He claims the spending requires 'virtually no net increase in government debt.'

The timing is the export boom. October exports surged 93.7% year on year to a record $115.3 billion - semiconductors riding AI datacenter demand - putting ten-month shipments at $930 billion, past $900 billion for the first time, with a $42.5 billion October trade surplus. The IMF forecasts 3.3% growth in 2026, Korea's fastest since the 2021 rebound. Lee says $1 trillion in annual exports is within reach. The sovereign-model commitment makes South Korea the latest country to treat frontier AI as strategic infrastructure on par with chips themselves.
