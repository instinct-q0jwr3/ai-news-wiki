# Becoming Skilled at Making Documents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`anil-dash` `anthropic` `agentic-systems` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

Anil Dash observes that most business documents are genuinely poor - presentations that glaze eyes, memos that obscure - and that his earlier 'Make Better Documents' post, a list of advice he'd given for years, got a huge response.

So he has packaged that advice as a 'Better Documents' skills.md file: one of the simple markdown skill files that give LLM tools like Claude new capabilities, so that AI-assisted document drafting starts from good defaults instead of reproducing the average - which is to say, the mediocrity - of the documents in its training data.

## Highlights

- Most business documents are poor; Dash's advice list struck a nerve
- He shipped a 'Better Documents' skills.md for LLM tools
- Agent skills: plain markdown files that add capabilities - more proof markdown is taking over
- Point: make AI tools produce better documents by default, not average ones
