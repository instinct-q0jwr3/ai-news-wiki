# Councilmember, residents push back on AI 'blight scores' given to homes

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

An NBC 5 investigation found that AI-powered cameras mounted on Dallas trash trucks have photographed and assigned 'blight scores' to more than 21,000 properties in four months, flagging possible code violations. A city council member and residents are now pushing back, questioning privacy implications and whether camera-driven enforcement could bring fines to lower-income neighbourhoods least able to pay.

## Highlights

- AI cameras on Dallas trash trucks scored 21,000+ properties in four months
- System assigns 'blight scores' to detect possible code violations
- City councilmember calls for deeper review of camera-driven enforcement
- Residents raise privacy concerns and risk of fines in lower-income areas
- NBC 5 obtained the city records through an investigation
