## Summary

Cory Doctorow asks the uncomfortable version of the post-Trump question: what if post-Trump America is worse? His argument: Trump is an effect, not a cause. His exceptional talent is holding together a coalition of America's most corrupt, reckless and cruel people - people who hate each other (Fuentes fantasizing about Miller; Loomer getting ICE to deport a personal enemy over a Twitter feud). They steal, inform on each other and self-medicate on podcast supplements.

The internet-policy consequence: planning for a better post-Trump internet requires reckoning with the coalition that survives him - the grifters, grievance merchants and platform barons who will still be there, competing to inherit the machine. Reforms that assume the aberration simply leaves will be designed for a country that does not exist.
