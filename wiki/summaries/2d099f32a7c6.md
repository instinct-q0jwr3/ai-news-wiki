## Summary

Simon Willison is co-hosting (with Jesse Vincent) a San Francisco evening event on October 14th for people building weird and interesting things with and on top of coding agents - an 'agentic show-and-tell' rather than a conference.

The ask: compare notes on what you're trying, what you're learning, and what you haven't figured out yet. They're especially keen on work that hasn't been discussed publicly, odd experiments, and unfinished projects with no obvious market. One flowing conversation with informal show-and-tell; sharing is encouraged, no presentation required.
