# Global stocks tumble as AI panic grips markets after Democrats' midterm success

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-07 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Guardian's live business blog reports global stocks tumbling as fears that the Democrats' midterm wave will sink the AI investment boom roil markets after heavy Wall Street losses. Asia-Pacific markets were 'a sea of red'. A trader quoted says clients 'got carried out', with margin calls, and describes the shift as a policy change that pauses and cools AI, data-center approvals and equity capital markets. The AI trade was already shakier: Magnificent Seven earnings undershot growth expectations, with signs AI spending is slowing.

## Highlights

- Global stocks fall again on fears of tech regulation and data-center curbs
- Asia-Pacific markets deeply in the red
- Trader: margin calls and a 'six-year structural shift' to cool AI
- Mag Seven earnings undershot; AI spending pace slowing
