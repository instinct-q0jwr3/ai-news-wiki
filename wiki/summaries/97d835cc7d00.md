# German publisher C.H.BECK takes a majority stake in Noxtua, which develops AI tech to research, analyze, and draft legal documents and raised a $100M+ Series C

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

German legal publisher C.H.BECK has taken a qualified majority stake in Noxtua, whose AI researches, analyzes and drafts legal documents, after the startup raised a $100M+ Series C. C.H.BECK plans to tie its product strategy closely to Noxtua's platform, bringing its beck-online database of more than 60 million legal documents to the AI market. Noxtua says it has quadrupled its team in 12 months, quintupled revenue in four months, serves more than 30,000 legal professionals, and recently launched its Legal AI Workspace with leading local publishers in Poland, Sweden and a third country. C.H.BECK emphasizes Noxtua will keep its independence, praising its rejection of 'move fast and break things'.

## Highlights

- C.H.BECK takes a majority stake in legal-AI startup Noxtua.
- beck-online brings 60M+ legal documents to the partnership.
- Noxtua: 30,000+ legal professionals, revenue quintupled in four months.
- Legal AI Workspace launched with publishers in three countries including Poland and Sweden.
- Noxtua raised a $100M+ Series C.
