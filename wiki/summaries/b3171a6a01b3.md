# A new feature for my blog, built using my voice

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`simon-willison` `openai` `agentic-systems` `ai-coding-agents` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Simon Willison describes shipping a new Newsletters page on his blog almost entirely by voice. He talked to the ChatGPT desktop app's Codex tab in voice mode against a local dev server while cooking dinner.

The page indexes his free weekly Substack issues and his monthly sponsors-only updates. His dictated requirements include keeping them off tag and blog index pages, showing them on date pages, and making the monthly ones searchable once they are public a month later. The post includes the transcript and the resulting design.

## Highlights

- Blog feature built mostly by voice
- Used Codex voice mode in ChatGPT desktop
- New Newsletters index page
- Rules for tag, date and search pages dictated aloud
