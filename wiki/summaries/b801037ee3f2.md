# Mistral CEO says U.S. AI safety debate masks competitors' 'negligence'

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `mistral` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch told CNBC that the US AI safety debate is 'a cover for the negligence of some of our competitors' - arguing the loudest safety talkers are deflecting from their own containment failures, and that the real task is building systems that can contain AI agents. He joins a US chorus from the other direction: former crypto czar and current PCAST co-chair David Sacks told tech giants to 'stop pretending the motivation to slow down is purely altruistic', and Defense undersecretary Emil Michael warned of a 'coordinated campaign' of fearmongering. CNBC sought comment from OpenAI and Anthropic. The backdrop is the global rise in rogue-agent incidents.
