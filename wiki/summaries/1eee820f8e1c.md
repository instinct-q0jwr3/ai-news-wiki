# Avante, which builds an AI platform to help companies manage employee healthcare and other benefits, raised a $30M Series A, following a $10M seed in 2024

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

GeekWire reports Avante, a Seattle startup building an AI platform for corporate benefits management, raised a $30 million Series A led by Frist Cressey Ventures, bringing its total to $40 million after a $10 million seed in 2024. Its agents compare vendor contracts against actual claims, review plan documents for errors and prepare renewals; employees can query their own coverage.

Avante claims more than 70 customers including Samsung Semiconductor, Zscaler and News Corp, nearly fivefold bookings growth over the past year, and reach across 385,000 employees and family members. Founders Rohan D'Souza and Carly Eckert are veterans of Olive AI, the health care automation company that shut down in 2023; the raise comes as large employers project a median 9.2% health cost increase for 2027.
