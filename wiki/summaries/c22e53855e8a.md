## Summary

An Ask HN thread speculates that a cluster of service disruptions may reflect coordinated AI-assisted attacks. Replies challenge both the shared-cause assumption and the evidence offered for it.

The discussion provides no forensic link between the incidents and does not establish every disruption was a hack. Temporal clustering, competing intuitions and claims of Bayesian reasoning are not substitutes for incident reports or attribution evidence.

## Highlights

- Thread contains competing speculation
- Outage does not necessarily mean compromise
- No common forensic cause is supplied
- Timing alone cannot attribute an attacker
- No coordinated cyberwar is established
