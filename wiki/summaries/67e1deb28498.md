## Summary

TechCrunch reports Reflection AI, a 2024 startup of former DeepMind researchers billing itself as America's answer to open Chinese AI, is releasing its first model, Beam. It positions Beam as a workhorse that beats other Western open models, strong at coding and agentic tasks, using 3x to 4x less compute than comparable models, per CEO Misha Laskin.
