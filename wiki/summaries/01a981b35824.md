## Summary

Latent Space surveys companies combining AI proof generation with formal verification and contrasts machine-checked guarantees with testing or another model’s judgment. Interviews and company examples emphasize requirements precise enough for a proof checker to evaluate.

The guarantee applies to the encoded specification and assumptions, not automatically to every user intention or deployed environment. Claims about future workflow simplicity and broad security benefits remain vendor expectations. The survey mentions a claimed Riemann result, but that mention is not independent mathematical validation.
