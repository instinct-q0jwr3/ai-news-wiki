# Ask HN: How to get the most intelligence out of your agents?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `agentic-systems` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

An Ask HN on a gap many agent users feel: the poster finds chatgpt.com's top-tier model consistently more insightful than Codex's agent models, but with far weaker tool calling - so they shuttle context between the two by hand and want that intelligence living in their local agent instead. The early answers sketch the community's workarounds: build specialised subagents that tease out more thinking from reasoning-budget-limited models (one commenter details the approach while flagging it as unproven), effectively using orchestration to buy back the intelligence the agent harness leaves on the table.

## Highlights

- Ask HN: how to get the most intelligence out of your agents
- Poster: chatgpt.com's top tier outsmarts Codex agents but can't call tools
- Current workaround: manually copying context between the two
- Community suggestion: specialised subagents to extract more reasoning from budget-limited models
- The intelligence-vs-tool-use trade-off between chat and agent surfaces remains unsolved
