# ElevenLabs is in early talks for a 2027 funding round at a $33B target valuation, up from $22B in September; its annual recurring revenue is now $700M

## Summary

Bloomberg's readable opening reports ElevenLabs in preliminary discussions for a funding round targeting a $33 billion valuation, with efforts expected in the first quarter of 2027. The account attributes the timing and target to people familiar with private discussions, some of whom want an earlier process.

This is not a completed financing, binding valuation or confirmed investor commitment. Only the opening was accessible; the feed's comparison with an earlier $22 billion round was not independently checked against original financing documents. Proposed valuation and timing can change, and the report does not establish new revenue or profitability.
