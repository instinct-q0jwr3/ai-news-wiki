## Summary

Google DeepMind's page for Gemini 4 Argon for robotics says the model is a step change in physical understanding, code generation and visual-semantic reasoning. On the ERQA embodied-reasoning benchmark it scores 87% with pure reasoning, which Google says beats every other frontier model, and it pitches Argon as general-purpose physical reasoning toward 'physical AGI'. Basis: the opening of the page.
