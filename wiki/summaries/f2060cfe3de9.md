# Replit Agent can now buy for your business and help you reach customers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-13 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Replit expands its agent beyond software creation into business purchases and Google Ads. The announcement describes preparing a merchant checkout and requiring the owner to approve the final total, with Link and Visa supporting the payment flow.

The launch specifies US one-time purchases for Core and Pro users and says payment-time restrictions are enforced in code. These are product claims about scope and controls, not open-ended spending authority. Advertising is another workflow with its own account and budget decisions.

## Highlights

- Agent prepares checkout rather than receiving blanket spending permission
- Owner approves final purchase total
- Link and Visa support payment handling
- Launch is limited by geography and plan
- Ads involve separate budget and account choices
