George Hotz argues that electricity supply is a long-term constraint on computing power and favors trade and domestic energy investment over a zero-sum AI race. He then expresses concern that strategic competition could encourage dangerous ideas about decisive military advantage.

The post is speculative geopolitical commentary. Its energy extrapolations and conflict predictions are the author's views, not demonstrated forecasts, and its characterization of Nvidia's situation should not be treated as an independently established legal fact.
