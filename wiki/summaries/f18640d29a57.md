# Recursive self-improvement through collective intelligence

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

MASS, from researchers at UC Berkeley and Sakana AI, tackles the supervision bottleneck at the frontier: once AI produces work even experts struggle to evaluate, the model being improved may also be the best available optimizer and evaluator. The method repeatedly optimizes multi-agent workflows, then distills the collective intelligence of multi-agent systems into a self-supervision signal for recursive self-improvement - homogeneous RSI, where the optimizee is its own optimizer and evaluator.

The motivation: at the frontier, verifying a discovery can demand scarcer expertise and costlier experiments than generating it, so production will outpace supervision. MASS reports improved score per output token over the base model on research benchmarks, and the authors frame it as a step toward AI's 'Odyssean moment' of continual self-learning.

## Highlights
