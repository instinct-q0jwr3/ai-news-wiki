# Simons Foundation launches $15M effort to help mathematicians navigate AI era

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Simons Foundation, with Anthropic and OpenAI, is committing $15 million in combined funding and resources to help mathematicians deal with AI. The foundation will manage and award the money, partly through an open call for proposals.

It will pay for workshops and initiatives to evaluate recent AI-assisted results, teach tools, share views and shape shared standards for AI use in mathematics.

## Highlights

- $15M from Simons, Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Open call for proposals on evaluating AI-assisted math results.
