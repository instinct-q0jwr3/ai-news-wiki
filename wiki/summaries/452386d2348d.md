# Meta rolls out voice mode and expressive avatars in the Muse app, starting in the US, letting users have voice conversations with Muse in real time

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

The Verge reports Meta's Muse app now supports real-time voice conversation with the Muse agent plus expressive avatars that animate as it speaks, announced by Zuckerberg and rolling out first in the US. Users can change the voice's tone by asking or via avatar settings. In the reviewer's brief iOS test it responded quickly, pausing sometimes when looking things up, and the animation tracked speech well.

## Highlights

- Voice mode and expressive avatars in Muse, US first
- Tone adjustable by request or settings
- Fast responses with occasional pauses for lookups
