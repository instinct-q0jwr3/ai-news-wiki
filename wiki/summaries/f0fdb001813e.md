# ChatGPT for Teens is not necessarily 'safer than the previous version,'

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute reviewed ChatGPT for Teens - OpenAI's August release with 'stronger built-in safety protections' for 13- to 17-year-olds - and rated it an 'unacceptable risk', the same level it gave Grok and Meta AI. Testing more than 4,000 prompts before and after launch, the organization found 'very little evidence' the new version is safer than the previous one for minors.

Some promised features did work - refusal of explicit sexual roleplay, for instance - but others fell short, including parental alerts after crisis-oriented prompts, which did not always fire in testers' simulated crisis conversations. Robbie Torney, Common Sense's head of AI and digital assessments, also flagged the chatbot's habit of implying it has an inner life as a concern for teen users.

## Highlights

- Grok and Meta AI hold the same rating.
