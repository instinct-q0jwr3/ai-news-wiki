# Q&A with OpenAI VP of Hardware Richard Ho on its Jalapeño inference chip co-designed with Broadcom, using internal OpenAI models to design the chip, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-04 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai`

## Summary

More Than Moore interviews OpenAI's Richard Ho about Jalapeño, OpenAI's own inference chip, designed with Broadcom as partner and Celestica on board and rack integration. It carries 216 GiB of HBM4 at 15.4 TB/s, draws about 550 W sustained (700 W peak), and a full system is 2,048 accelerators reaching 27 EFLOP/s at four-bit precision.

At HotChips 2026 OpenAI showed first measured results from working silicon and claimed, against NVIDIA GB200/GB300, 1.5x to 1.9x better performance per watt, 1.7x to 3.6x lower latency, and much larger gains at operating points where NVIDIA struggles. These are OpenAI's own claims. Only the opening of the interview was read.

## Highlights

- Jalapeño is an inference accelerator, not a training chip.
- 216 GiB HBM4; 2,048 accelerators per system.
- Claims 1.5-1.9x perf per watt over GB200/GB300.
- Vendor-reported numbers; basis: opening of interview.
