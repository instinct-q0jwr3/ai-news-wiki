## Summary

The Information reports that Replit recently shifted most of its AI spending from Anthropic to OpenAI after Anthropic discontinued its discount. OpenAI reportedly offered more than 30% off in a deal worth over $100 million per year, part of a broader push to win AI workloads on price.

- Replit moved most of its AI spend from Anthropic to OpenAI, per The Information.
- OpenAI offered 30%+ discounts in a deal reportedly worth $100M+ per year.
- The switch followed Anthropic discontinuing its own discount to Replit.
- The story illustrates price becoming a competitive lever between frontier model providers.
