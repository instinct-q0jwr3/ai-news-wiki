# Our Smallest, Most Efficient Model: Muse Ember

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `meta` `muse`

## Summary

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Ember, its smallest and cheapest model, aimed at subagent work such as coding and document analysis. Partner quotes cite speed: up to 30% faster at the tail than a comparable model, and a p95 latency of 2.2 seconds, 4x to 13x faster than leading open-weight models with 36-48% fewer output tokens. These are testimonials on Meta's page.

## Highlights

- Smallest, cheapest Muse model
- Built for agent subagents: coding, document analysis
