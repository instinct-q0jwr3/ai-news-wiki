# How Pew Research Center is – and is not – using AI in our work

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Pew Research Center explains how it is, and is not, using AI. The post (an update of an August 2024 note) frames Pew's position along three axes: as social scientists exploring what AI adds to the toolkit, as opinion researchers studying public reaction to AI, and as information providers whose values of accuracy and methodological rigor demand deliberation. The through-line is a commitment to keeping the work people-centered. The cached source is brief, so this summary reflects only the framing Pew published.

## Highlights

- Pew describes its AI posture across research, public-opinion study, and publishing roles
- Accuracy and methodological rigor are cited as the constraints on AI adoption
- Pew says its primary commitment is that work remains people-centered
- The post updates an earlier note from August 2024
