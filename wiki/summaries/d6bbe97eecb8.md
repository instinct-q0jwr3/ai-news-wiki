# Amazon and Google will together own nearly 30% of Anthropic after its IPO

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

## Summary

TechCrunch reports projected Amazon and Google ownership in Anthropic around its IPO, explaining how convertible notes and nonvoting shares shape the percentages. Some notes remain unconverted, so the reported ownership differs from a hypothetical full conversion.

The article attributes details to disclosures that were not independently inspected here. Equity value is not realized profit, nonvoting ownership is not identical to voting control, and financing relationships are separate from future cloud-spending commitments.

## Highlights

- Convertible notes affect reported ownership
- Not all notes convert at the offering
- Some shares are nonvoting
- Potential equity value is not realized proceeds
- Cloud revenue and share ownership are distinct
