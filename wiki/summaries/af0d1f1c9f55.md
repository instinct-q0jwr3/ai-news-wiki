# Bluesky reply bot checker

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`simon-willison`

## Summary

A Simon Willison beat: infuriated by automated reply bots - a swarm on Twitter, now arriving on Bluesky - he had Opus 5.5 vibe-code a tool that examines any Bluesky profile for likely bot behavior. Bluesky's free, useful API makes this kind of investigation practical in a way Twitter's closed API no longer does.

The signals it checks: replies posted within seconds of the target's posts, accounts that never post original content (no posts, images or links) but consistently reply to higher-follower users, and - his personal rage trigger - question marks, because reply bots keep baiting him into answering questions no human ever posed.

## Highlights

- Willison vibe-coded a Bluesky reply-bot checker with Opus 5.5
- Signals: seconds-fast replies, reply-only accounts targeting big accounts, bait questions
- Bluesky's open API makes bot investigation feasible; Twitter's closed one killed it
- Motivation: his posts attract dozens of mindless automated replies - and the bots are migrating
