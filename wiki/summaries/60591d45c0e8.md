# Anthropic considers a new model to counter OpenAI's Astra momentum

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Anthropic is considering releasing a new AI model to counter OpenAI's momentum since the launch of GPT-6 Astra, Reuters reports. The deliberations come ahead of a possible IPO and after CEO Dario Amodei's public call for a slowdown in the AI race.
