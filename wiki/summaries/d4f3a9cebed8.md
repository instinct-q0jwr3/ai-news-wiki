# Apple Only Started Suggesting iOS 26 Users Upgrade to 27 Three Days Ago

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-16 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `apple`

## Summary

John Gruber points to MacRumors' report that Apple began promoting iOS 27.0.1 as the main update for iPhones still on iOS 26 only on Thursday, three days before this post. Until then, iOS 27 (released September 14) sat in the smaller 'Also Available' section while iOS 26.7.1 held the prominent slot.

Swapping the order nudges iOS 26 users toward 27 without forcing it. Apple still recommends at least installing iOS 26.7.1 for security fixes, and it has not published iOS 27 adoption numbers. Gruber's angle is that most people only update when Apple pushes them.

## Highlights

- Apple now shows iOS 27.0.1 as the prominent update on iOS 26 devices.
- iOS 27 came out September 14 but was hidden under 'Also Available' for weeks.
- Nothing is forced; iOS 26.7.1 remains available for security fixes.
- No iOS 27 adoption figures published yet.
