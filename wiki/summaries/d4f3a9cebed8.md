# Apple Only Started Suggesting iOS 26 Users Upgrade to 27 Three Days Ago

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-11 · updated: 2026-10-12 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `apple`

## Summary

Joe Rossignol, on Thursday: Starting today, iPhone users still running iOS 26 will see iOS 27.0.1 as the prominent software update in the Settings app under General → Software Update, with iOS 26.7.1 now relegated to a smaller “Also…

Just the other day I posted about how most users only get OS updates when Apple pushes them through automatic updates.

But even for users who update manually, until Thursday, the default suggested update for users on iOS 26.x was to remain on the latest version of iOS 26.

## Highlights

- To get iOS 27 you had to scroll down “below the fold” and most users don’t do that. iOS 27 adoption amongst normals is very slow, but that’s only because Apple wants it slow.
- That’s not because iOS 27 is buggy — it speaks to its Snow-Leopard-like focus on bug fixes and performance updates that it’s actually remarkably stable.
- By reversing the order in which the updates are shown today, Apple is more actively encouraging iPhone users who are still on iOS 26 to install iOS 27.
- Apple is not forcing iPhone users to install iOS 27, but at least installing iOS 26.7.1 is recommended in order to receive the latest security fixes.
- Apple has yet to share iOS 27 adoption numbers.
