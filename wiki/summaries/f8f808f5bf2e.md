# Olmo-core 3 for MoE Training

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Ai2 released Olmo-core 3, an upgrade to its LLM training framework with a redesigned open mixture-of-experts system. It is designed to scale MoE training to the trillion-parameter range while keeping compute efficiency, and underpins the next generation of Olmo. It continues Ai2's habit of opening its training tools, aiming to put large-model development within reach of academics.

## Highlights

- Olmo-core 3 with a redesigned open MoE training system.
- Targets trillion-parameter scale with efficiency.
- Part of next-generation Olmo.
- Open tooling for academic researchers.
