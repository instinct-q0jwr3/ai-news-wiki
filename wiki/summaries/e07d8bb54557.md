# Berlin-based Pliant raised an $80M Series C led by Seaya Nova and Speedinvest, as it opens its B2B payments infrastructure to AI agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `anthropic` `agentic-systems` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Berlin fintech Pliant raised an $80M Series C led by Seaya Nova and Speedinvest, with Illuminate Financial, Motive Partners and Molten Ventures. The money funds expansion in the US and Canada and its AI roadmap. Pliant passed $100M in annual recurring revenue after doubling each year for three years.

The product angle: Pliant combines card issuing, payment accounts, FX and spend management, and now lets outside agents, Claude, ChatGPT or a company's own automations, run payment workflows through the Model Context Protocol with permissions, controls and audit trails. Its pitch, echoed by Seaya Nova, is that when agents move money someone must be licensed, auditable and liable.

## Highlights

- $80M Series C led by Seaya Nova and Speedinvest
- Past $100M ARR; expanding to the US and Canada
- Opens B2B payments to agents (Claude, ChatGPT) via MCP with permissions and audit trails
- Pitch: agents moving money need a licensed, liable layer
