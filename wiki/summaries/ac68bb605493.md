# Enact, which develops full-stack tech for general-purpose robots, emerges from stealth with a $30M pre-seed from Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch, Yann LeCun, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `mistral`

## Summary

Swiss robotics startup Enact Intelligence emerged from stealth with a $30 million pre-seed round - unusually large for that stage - backed by Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch and former Meta AI chief scientist Yann LeCun, among others. Enact is building full-stack technology for general-purpose robots, with a bet on manipulation through force and touch sensing rather than vision alone.

CEO and co-founder Manuel Yves Galliker, previously at Physical Intelligence, says Enact's robots sense force and touch through their own actuators, feeling what is happening in real time and learning more data-efficient behaviors as a result. The company is targeting industrial and real-world work where camera-based approaches struggle with contact-rich tasks.
