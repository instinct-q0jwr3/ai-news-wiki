## Summary

A quoted passage from Addy Osmani and co-authors argues that 'did a human read the code?' is a poor proxy for 'what could go wrong with this change, and which check is right to catch it?'. It adds that review now runs continuously instead of as a gate at the end.

## Highlights

- Ask what could go wrong, not whether a human read the code.
- Review becomes continuous instead of a final gate.
