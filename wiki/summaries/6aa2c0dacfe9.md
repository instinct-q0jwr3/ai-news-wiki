# Plurai, founded by former Nexar staffers to use synthetic data and digital twins of corporate IT environments to assess AI agents, raised a $13M seed

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Plurai, founded by Nexar alumni, uses synthetic data and digital twins of a company's IT environment to train and test AI agents, adapting self-driving simulation techniques. The article cites benchmarks: LiveBench 2.0 has Claude Fable 5.2 at 82.9/100 and ChatGPT-6 Astra at 75.6, and an EON simulated-company test put Claude Opus 4.8 at 76.8% accuracy, so agents still need supervision.

## Highlights

- Digital twins of corporate IT for agent training
- Founders from Nexar, worked with Waymo
- Benchmarks show top agents under 83%
- Simulation approach from autonomous driving
