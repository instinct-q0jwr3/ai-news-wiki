Poynter reports via Techmeme on a Wesleyan Media Project finding: political campaigns spent about $187M on roughly 300 AI-generated or AI-enhanced ads in the midterms, and 90% of tracked AI ads were for GOP candidates. Campaigns stayed wary of voter backlash.
