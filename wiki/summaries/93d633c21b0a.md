# Vesta, which uses AI agents to automate much of the loan origination process, raised $30M led by Conversion, bringing its total funding to $85M

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Vesta, an AI-native mortgage-origination startup, raised $30 million led by Conversion Capital, with customers Pennymac and New American Funding investing alongside Citi Ventures and Andreessen Horowitz, bringing total funding to $85 million. CEO Mike Yu says demand exploded in the past year, with revenue up 12x year over year - and with under 5% market share, the plan is to staff up and expand product lines, including a personal assistant for mortgage issuers.

The pitch is concrete economics: closing a US mortgage takes about 40 days and costs around $11,000 per loan, mostly human labor, with reviewers as the bottleneck. Vesta's customers deploy swarms of agents against that work, typically starting with a human approving each agent's output before letting it handle a share of loans autonomously - some lenders even let Vesta agents make underwriting decisions, though companies remain legally responsible for them.
