# Pangram releases Pangram Image 1.0, which it says is state-of-the-art in AI image and AI video detection, and the smaller Image Flash 1.0 for AI video detection

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Pangram released Pangram Image 1.0, which it calls state of the art in AI image and video detection, plus a smaller faster variant. It tested against commercial and academic detectors using 1,200 synthetic mirrors of 100 real photos made with 12 frontier image generators, and reports 99.96% accuracy on that set. It says the model is trained to resist social-media compression and downscaling, blur and noise, and lowers the minimum input to 256x256 with a reduced-confidence note below 512. A heatmap shows where AI content sits in a mixed image. It cites a study that humans are barely better than chance at spotting fakes. Accuracy figures are the vendor's own.

## Highlights

- Pangram Image 1.0 for AI image and video detection, plus a smaller fast variant
- Claims 99.96% accuracy on 1,200 synthetic images from 12 generators
- Trained for compression, blur and noise; minimum size lowered to 256x256
- Self-reported benchmark; humans reportedly near chance
