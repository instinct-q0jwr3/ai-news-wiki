# COO Jen Wong says 80%+ of Reddit's monetization comes from logged-in users whose time on the site rises from 5 min/day in year one to 40 min/day by year five

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-28 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `external-evaluation`

## Summary

Reddit COO Jen Wong tells Fortune that over 80% of Reddit's monetization comes from logged-in users, whose daily time on the site rises from five minutes in their first year to forty by their fifth, while the communities they join grow from ten to over a hundred. The interview lands as Reddit's share price sits 62% down year to date after CEO Steve Huffman said Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode were cutting search referrals, knocking the stock from near $130 to under $91 overnight. Wong frames the shift as requiring a different kind of partnership with search engines rather than a separation, and points to the early-November launch of Reddit Reviews, which aggregates product feedback across communities into ratings. All engagement figures are company-reported in an executive interview, not independently audited, and the retention claims serve Reddit's case to investors amid the AI-search selloff.

## Highlights

- Wong says 80%+ of monetization comes from logged-in users who season from 5 to 40 minutes daily over five years.
- Reddit stock is down 62% YTD after Huffman tied declining search referrals to Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode.
- Wong casts the search relationship as a changed partnership, not a break.
- Reddit Reviews, launched in early November, organizes product feedback across communities into ratings.
- All metrics are company-reported in a promotional interview; none are independently audited.
