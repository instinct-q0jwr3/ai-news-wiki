# Show HN: Open-Source Version of Cyclon: Phone where AI is the interface?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-14 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

OpenCyclone presents itself as an independent open-source Android project that lets an agent operate existing apps on a hidden display. Its page claims approval gates before sending, paying or deleting, and offers a choice of model providers rather than requiring new phone hardware.

The page also admits that the software was built quickly, tested on a Pixel 7 and is less reliable on long multi-app tasks; some promised sections are unfinished. These are project claims, not a security audit, proof that every consequential action is intercepted or confirmation of affiliation with the similarly named commercial phone.

## Highlights

- Existing Android hardware is the intended target
- Project claims code-enforced approval gates
- Model-provider costs remain separate
- Long multi-app tasks are acknowledged as unreliable
- Security claims were not independently tested
