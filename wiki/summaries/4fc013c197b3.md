# OpenAI says its internal model produced 372 math results, nearly all from a single prompt handed to a single AI agent; some might have taken multiple attempts

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Scientific American reports OpenAI released 372 new results from an internal model that has not been made public, each claimed to resolve or substantially advance an open problem in mathematics or theoretical computer science, a month after a major result the company says was one of the biggest open problems. Claims include the four-dimensional Kakeya conjecture, improvements to key algorithms and progress toward the Riemann hypothesis. Many are already verified in Lean, but mathematicians will need months to judge novelty. OpenAI says nearly all came from a single prompt to a single agent, and some may have needed several attempts.

## Highlights
