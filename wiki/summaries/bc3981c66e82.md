# the Cyberspace Administration of China is investigating DeepSeek and Moonshot over alleged data leaks to Anthropic and has questioned employees

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `deepseek` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

The Cyberspace Administration of China is investigating DeepSeek and Moonshot AI over alleged leaks of sensitive user data to Anthropic, and has questioned employees, The Information reports. The probe follows Anthropic's allegation that both companies had been routing sensitive user data.

## Highlights

- China's internet regulator is investigating DeepSeek and Moonshot.
- The probe concerns alleged data leaks to Anthropic; employees have been questioned.
- It follows Anthropic's claim that both companies routed sensitive user data.
