# NetBSD may have found a new role: The AI-free FOSS OS

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-14 · updated: 2026-10-17 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The Register reports NetBSD is thriving as an explicitly AI-free project: it got a record 120 donations last month totaling $120,282 against a $50,000 target. The piece ties the interest to the Linux world embracing AI, citing Torvalds saying Linux isn't an anti-AI project, Debian permitting AI-generated contributions, an AI-heavy Ubuntu Summit and a Red Hat memo on an agentic development lifecycle.

## Highlights

- NetBSD raised $120,282 last month against a $50,000 goal, a record 120 donations.
- Interest rises among people who avoid AI-written code.
- Context: Debian allowed AI contributions; Torvalds called Linux not anti-AI.
