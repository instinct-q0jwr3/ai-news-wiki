## Summary

Carl Handellen's rant argues AI extinction fears are overblown noise. He quotes the 2023 extinction-risk statement and Yudkowsky, mocks listing AI beside pandemics and nuclear war, and says that as a software engineer he no longer writes code but AI will almost certainly not take over the world or kill all life. He says those who pushed the narrative should be held to account for wrong predictions, addressing readers scared into hoarding groceries, and cites an Anthropic alignment lead saying AI could kill all humans. Opinion piece.
