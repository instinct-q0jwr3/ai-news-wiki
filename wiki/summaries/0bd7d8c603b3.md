# OpenAI Begins Testing Private Inference

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `openai`

## Summary

A post attributed to Sam Altman announces testing of OpenAI Private Inference with a small customer group as part of Private Intelligence. It also describes automated safety review with zero-data-retention and private safety processing without personnel access to underlying content.

The post is a brief announcement, not technical documentation proving the threat model, attestation flow, retention guarantees or broad availability. A pilot does not establish that every consumer chat, account or existing endpoint has the same protections. No customer enrollment, sales contact or account setting was changed.

## Highlights

- Testing is limited to a small group
- Private Inference and Private Safety Processing are named
- Personnel-access claim is attributed to the announcement
- Threat model was not independently audited
- No general consumer rollout is established
- No enrollment or account action occurred
