# ShinyHunters claims it used an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day to hack FBI-related services and steal employee and applicant data; it also defaced the FBI jobs site

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems` `ai-safety-incidents` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

ShinyHunters claims it breached multiple FBI-related services via an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day and stole data 'on all FBI employees and applicants'. A representative told 404 Media the haul includes agents' names, home addresses, phone numbers and information on their spouses.

If genuine, the breach carries national-security and counterintelligence implications: criminals from the same ecosystem have previously used hacked phone records to track, intimidate and harass the FBI agents investigating them, and the data would be a boon to foreign intelligence services mapping a key US law-enforcement agency - while exposing agents and their families to serious safety threats.

## Highlights

- ShinyHunters claims data on all FBI employees and applicants via an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day.
- Alleged haul: names, home addresses, phone numbers, spouse information.

- Data would also serve foreign intelligence mapping of the FBI.
- Claims reported by 404 Media; significance hinges on verification.
