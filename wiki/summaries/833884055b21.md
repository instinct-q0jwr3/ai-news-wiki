# Pilgrim, whose device combines air sampling and genomic sequencing to detect biological threats, raised a $25M seed led by Buckley at a $150M valuation

## Summary

Kate Clark / Wall Street Journal : Pilgrim, whose device combines air sampling and genomic sequencing to detect biological threats, raised a $25M seed led by Buckley at a $150M valuation — Pilgrim has raised $25 million and has…

Two Anthropic leaders are investing in a small startup backed by Peter Thiel that is developing hardware to detect viruses in the air, as fears grow that artificial-intelligence systems might cause novel biological threats.

Logan Graham, head of Anthropic’s frontier red team and Sholto Douglas, a member of its technical staff, invested in a $25 million seed round for Redwood City-Calif.-based Pilgrim that valued it at $150 million, the company said.
