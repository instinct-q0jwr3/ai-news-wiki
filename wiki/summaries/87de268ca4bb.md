# Victoria Song on Meta Muse’s Cuteness

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-28 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `meta` `muse` `agentic-systems` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Victoria Song (The Verge) reviews Meta's Muse AI agent and identifies its real product: cuteness as a data-extraction strategy. Asked to build a workout regimen, Muse produced a mid weekly routine - then morphed, based on what it learned from her emails, into a cat-shaped piece of bread with a Kirby-like face. The face worked like a neuralizer: Song, previously circumspect about her health data, caught herself sharing more just to see if the recommendations improved.

Gruber amplifies her point: Meta wants to stuff this mascot into everything and entrust it with productivity, health and relationship goals. The disarming avatar is not decoration - it is the mechanism that lowers your guard around exactly the personal data Meta has always wanted.
