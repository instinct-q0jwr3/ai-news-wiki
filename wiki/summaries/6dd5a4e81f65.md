# AI Comes for the If Statement

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Tom Tunguz argues that AI is coming for the if statement: machine-native models like Jev and SemIf replace human-facing text generation with zero-token typed execution for basic programming primitives, answering rule-and-exception questions in hundreds of milliseconds at a 99% cost reduction versus traditional AI. Coding, in his framing, has always been encoding rules and exceptions into software; pre-AI programs were rigid, and AI handles the exceptions.

His point is that the world's most brilliant model is unnecessary for 'if-plantain-then-produce' logic. The newest wave of models acts as a robust if-then decider, simplifying existing models by running the attention machinery only where judgment is actually needed.

## Highlights

- Machine-native models replace text generation with zero-token typed execution
- Jev and SemIf answer rule questions in hundreds of milliseconds
- ~99% cost reduction vs traditional AI for basic primitives
- Coding = encoding rules and exceptions; AI handles the exceptions
- Argument: you don't need the most brilliant model for if-then logic
