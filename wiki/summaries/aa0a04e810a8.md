# Maxwell: A new kind of model that sits between code and hardware

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `anthropic` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

doubleAI's Maxwell announcement opens with Alan Turing's 1947 vision of a machine that learns from experience by altering its own instructions - and his 1952 experiment hand-simulating a chess program, half an hour per move, losing the game but proving the concept. The company positions Maxwell, its 'Code Execution Model', as the same idea at modern scale: a model that sits between code and hardware and improves programs as they run, without changing a line of source and without humans in the loop. Where compilers optimize statically before execution, Maxwell observes execution itself and adapts - Turing's self-modifying machine aimed at GPU and CPU performance.
