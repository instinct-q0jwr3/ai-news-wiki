# Sensor Tower: Muse had 10.9M downloads since its September debut, 10.2M of those in the US, becoming the 12th app to hit 10M US downloads in its first 40 days

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `muse`

## Summary

TechCrunch, citing Sensor Tower, reports Meta's AI companion Muse reached 10.9 million downloads in its first 40 days, 10.2 million of them in the US, making it only the 12th app ever to hit 10 million US downloads that fast, a club otherwise limited to Threads, Disney+, and mobile games like Fortnite, PUBG Mobile, and Pokemon GO. Meta is buying the velocity as much as earning it: 39% of its total ad spend went to Muse in October (up from 23%), making Muse the 21st-largest US brand by ad impressions, up from 73rd. The interesting number is retention: Muse holds 30% of users at day 7 and 23% at day 14, versus 19% and 16% averages for ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok.

## Highlights

- Muse: 10.9M downloads in 40 days, 10.2M in the US (Sensor Tower)

- Retention beats AI rivals: 30% day-7, 23% day-14 vs 19%/16% for ChatGPT, Claude, Grok
- Meta's cross-app distribution to 3.6B daily users underpins the launch
