# AI devours a fifth of companies' IT budgets

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-24 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Forrester research reported by The Register: AI will account for 20% of corporate technology spending in 2027 - a fifth of IT budgets - even as more than half of IT executives are expected to miss their return-on-investment targets. In the survey, 45% of AI decision-makers say investments must pay back within a year to count as successful, yet only 22% saw a positive impact on operating profit over the past year.

Forrester's diagnosis: companies buy AI technology without changing the organizations and work processes around it, so gains stay trapped in isolated use cases. The report expects cost pressure to push enterprises toward cheaper open models, with expensive frontier models reserved for where they clearly earn their keep.

## Highlights

- Forrester: AI will consume 20% of corporate IT spending in 2027
- More than half of IT executives expected to miss AI ROI targets
- 45% of AI decision-makers demand payback within a year; only 22% saw operating-profit impact last year
- Root cause: technology investment without organizational and process change
- Expects a shift toward cheaper open models to control costs
