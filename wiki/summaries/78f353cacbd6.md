# Jensen Huang Agrees to Testify Before Congress, But Not Publicly

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `nvidia` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

TechCrunch reports that former Nvidia chief Jensen Huang has agreed to testify before Congress before Thanksgiving, according to committee chairman Brian Mast and other reporting. The proposed initial session is behind closed doors, with a recording and transcript expected. Agreement to appear is different from completed testimony; the report does not establish what Huang will say or what the committees will conclude.

## Highlights

- Report cites committee chairman
- Initial appearance is described as private
- Recording and transcript are expected
- Agreement is not completed testimony
- Investigative conclusions remain open
