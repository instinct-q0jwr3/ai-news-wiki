## Summary

Siri AI is now open to all developers.

November 3, 2026 Starting today with the iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, macOS 27.3, and visionOS 27.3 betas, a new Developer Preview enables any developer to integrate their app with Siri AI, giving businesses and developers new ways to reach…

## Highlights

- Thousands of developers have already built integrations with Siri to let users ask for directions, manage a to-do list, send a message, or catch up on an audiobook.
- Siri AI is available in many languages, including English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish and will soon expand to support Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish, and Vietnamese.
- People around the world are turning to Siri AI to get things done every day, which means more customers can discover and use apps just by asking.
- Today’s preview allows any app to bring its content and actions directly into Siri AI by using App Intents, making it easier for all apps to tap into Siri AI’s newest capabilities, including personal context understanding, onscreen…
- So people can just ask for help in their own words, and Siri AI carries out any custom action you’ve built, from checking a delivery to booking a reservation to placing an order.
- For complex requests, we’re also introducing skills to let you teach Siri AI how to combine several actions into a single experience.
- And Siri AI can now surface any entity in the Spotlight semantic index, not just the ones that fit an existing schema.
- This developer beta offers a sneak peek at Siri AI app intents for developers.
