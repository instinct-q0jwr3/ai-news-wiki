# Meta expands its AI-powered "Your Algorithm" feature from Instagram to Facebook and Facebook Reels and says it is using LLMs more in its ranking systems

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta`

## Summary

Meta brings Instagram's 'Your Algorithm' to Facebook and Facebook Reels, letting users add and manage topics and mark them interested or not, with recommendations responding immediately. Meta says it is also using LLMs more to rank content (The Verge).

## Highlights

- 'Your Algorithm' expands from Instagram to Facebook and Reels.
- Users manage topics with interested / not interested.
- Meta is using LLMs more to rank content.
