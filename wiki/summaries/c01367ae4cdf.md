# Gemini 4 Carbon is now available

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `gemini`

## Summary

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to everyone: developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers. Google calls it its strongest model, with frontier cybersecurity capability and long-horizon agentic work across knowledge work, software engineering and scientific reasoning. Two weeks earlier it went to cybersecurity defenders through the Fairwind Program. Gemini 4 Argon remains the default choice for most engineering and knowledge work.

## Highlights

- Gemini 4 Carbon generally available
- Frontier claims in cybersecurity and long-horizon agentic work
- Argon stays the mainstream option
- Earlier limited release to cyber defenders
