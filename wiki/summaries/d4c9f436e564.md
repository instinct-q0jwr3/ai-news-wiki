# No, Anderson Cooper, AI is not going to kill all humans by 2030

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`marcus-on-ai`

## Summary

Gary Marcus responds to the viral Jacob Coxon moment - the ex-OpenAI/ex-Anthropic employee telling Anderson Cooper the human species might not be here in five years. First, fairness: Marcus calls out the character assassination around Coxon, including lies about his Anthropic tenure (possibly derived from an inaccurate AI-generated profile), a weaponized thread casting him as a Democratic operative, and the absurd 'entry-level' label for someone with two-plus years on an OpenAI technical team. The nuance: some of what Coxon says is true, some speculative; some within his expertise, some not.

What Marcus valued in the thread is the window into frontier-lab state of mind - corroborated by Anthropic's Evan Hubinger ('we really do earnestly believe AI could kill all humans... >10% within the next decade... not clearly on track' to solve alignment) and by OpenAI's chief scientist saying something similar. But Marcus's core argument is about the Doomers' real-world track record: fabulous at publicity (the Yudkowsky/Soares bestseller, the Pause letter, Coxon's thread) and at raising close to a billion dollars - yet the net effect of a decade of doomer talk, in his view, has been to make frontier companies wealthier and more powerful. 'Everytime they scream that AI is going to kill us all, VCs toss in billions more.' None of the sponsored safety work has produced a demonstrated, implemented solution to the core problem.
