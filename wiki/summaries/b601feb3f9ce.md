# OpenAI reveals they solved VP ≠ VNP before Anthropic

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `anthropic`

## Summary

OpenAI's November 23 VP and VNP manuscript claims a superpolynomial border arithmetic-circuit lower bound over every field. The abstract and first theorem state exp(Omega((log n)^(5/4))) gates for an explicit VNP family, transferring to the directed Hamiltonian cycle polynomial over every field and to the permanent outside characteristic two.

The current repository says November 24 papers subsume these results and advertises a Lean proof, but neither that formalization nor the mathematical proof was independently checked here. The title's priority claim about which lab solved the problem first is not established by a repository edition date. Algebraic VP versus VNP is not Boolean P versus NP, and the characteristic restriction must not be dropped when discussing the permanent.
