# Delhi-based Bharat Semi, which is developing programmable image sensors combining capture, processing, and AI capabilities on a single chip, raised a $24M seed

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

TechCrunch reports Bharat Semi raised $24 million to develop programmable image sensors combining capture, processing and AI on one chip. The pre-revenue company is prototyping its first generation and targets initial commercial production in 2027, with more capable sensors planned later.

Design wins and potential cost savings are company claims, not evidence of shipped volume or an independently measured 70% cost reduction. The company intends to remain fabless; a separately discussed fabrication project is not what this funding finances.

## Highlights

- Sensors integrate several visual-computing functions
- Products remain under development
- Commercial production is a target
- Cost savings are a founder estimate
- Funding is separate from a proposed fabrication facility
