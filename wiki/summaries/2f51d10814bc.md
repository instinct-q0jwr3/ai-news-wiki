# AI Had Better Work Out

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`ed-zitron`

## Summary

Ed Zitron argues the AI trade is dangerously simple: hundreds of billions are being invested to maybe, someday, produce single-digit billions in revenue, contingent on data centers actually getting built and OpenAI and Anthropic being able to pay for their compute. He layers on the market-structure risk: over 50% of Americans now hold stocks, equities exceed 60% of liquid net worth, and Goldman data shows retail volumes up 28% since mid-April with retail-favorite stocks up 29%. Unlike 2000, retail traders have frictionless phone access to exotic, loss-amplifying products like event contracts, futures, shorts, and margin. The piece closes by walking through the recent correction that, analysts warned, was taking no prisoners.

## Highlights

- Apollo's Torsten Slok: if AI doesn't work out, 'we're going to have a problem'
- Zitron's frame: hundreds of billions invested for maybe single-digit billions someday
- Over 50% of Americans hold stocks; equities top 60% of liquid net worth
- Goldman: retail volumes up 28% since mid-April, retail-favorite basket up 29%
- Contrast with 2000: Microsoft+Cisco+Intel peaked at ~$1.36T combined
- New risk: phone-tap access to event contracts, futures, margin for retail
- Recent correction described as 'taking no prisoners', with more declines expected
