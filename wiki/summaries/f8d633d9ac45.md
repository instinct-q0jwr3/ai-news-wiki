# Closed AI models dominate the price-performance frontier

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Village's LLM Atlas study finds closed models now dominate the price-performance frontier. Open models win on value in bursts, usually a strong, cheap DeepSeek release that holds a wide band of budgets until a closed model undercuts it. Recent closed releases are cheaper and stronger than any open one, so open's share has shrunk to the very cheapest end. Method: capability estimated from public benchmarks and cost from evaluators' runs, relative to GPT-5 at high effort, across 201 log-spaced budgets (scores as of Oct. 31, 2026). Caveat: open models may be cheaper self-hosted, and widening the budget range lifts open's share to 16%.

## Highlights

- Open models win only in bursts, then closed releases undercut them
- Open's winning band is now confined to the lowest prices
- Costs from hosted API runs; self-hosting may be cheaper
- Widening budgets to 0.3%-10x GPT-5 lifts open share to 16%
