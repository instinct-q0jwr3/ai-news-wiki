# Filing: Microsoft recorded $10B in revenue from OpenAI in Q1, nearly 11% of its total quarterly revenue, and has $7.2B in outstanding receivables from OpenAI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `microsoft`

## Summary

A Microsoft regulatory filing reveals the scale of the OpenAI relationship: $10 billion in revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI in the quarter ended September 30 - nearly 11% of Microsoft's $92.3 billion quarterly total - and $24.1 billion for the full 2026 fiscal year. At the current pace, OpenAI-related arrangements would bring in $40 billion annually.

Microsoft also holds $7.2 billion in outstanding receivables from OpenAI, up from $6 billion in June. The split between revenue-sharing payments and Azure cloud services isn't disclosed; leaked documents previously showed $865.8 million in net revenue-sharing over the first nine months of 2025 under a 20% revenue-share deal.

## Highlights

- $10B from OpenAI in one quarter - ~11% of Microsoft's revenue
- $24.1B from OpenAI across FY2026; $40B annual run rate
- $7.2B in outstanding OpenAI receivables
- Split between revenue share and Azure spend undisclosed
