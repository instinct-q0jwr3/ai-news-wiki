## Highlights

- Agents reached 48% of Wrangler usage last week, up from single digits a year earlier
- Agents use ~2x as many distinct commands per day and are ~4x as likely to use 6+ commands
- Wrangler covers only ~280 of Cloudflare's thousands of API operations, with inconsistent patterns across teams
- The new cf CLI is designed agent-first and exposes every Cloudflare product
- Available now as an open beta: npm i -g cf
