# A theoretical computer scientist, citing sources, says AI labs have quietly started probing whether their models can break important cryptographic protocols

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Scott Aaronson's blog relays, with permission, a message from Omer Reingold saying that AI labs have quietly started probing whether their models can break important cryptographic protocols. The headline claim rests on sources named by the blog.

The page also hosts a guest post by Pravesh Kothari, Raghu Meka and Prasad Raghavendra asking how theoretical computer science should adapt if AI theorem provers soon prove open problems in hours, and proposing a separate 'conceptual track' at conferences. The cryptography claim is secondhand, so read it as a report, not a confirmed result.

## Highlights

- Claim: AI labs are testing models on breaking cryptographic protocols
- Relayed secondhand from Omer Reingold
- Same page: guest post on TCS in the age of AI theorem provers
- Proposes a conceptual track at STOC/FOCS/SODA
- Cryptography claim unconfirmed
