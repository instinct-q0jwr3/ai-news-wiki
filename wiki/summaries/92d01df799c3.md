# Show HN: HN.watch – Videos of all Hacker News posts

## Summary

HN.watch is a Show HN project that attaches a short AI-generated explainer video to every story on the Hacker News front page. The site renders the current front page with a 'video' link beside each title so readers can watch a quick summary instead of, or before, opening the article. The cached snapshot is essentially the front-page listing itself, which shows the concept in action across that day's mix of stories.

## Highlights

- Show HN project that generates a short explainer video for every HN front-page story
- The front page is rendered with a video link next to each story title
- The cached snapshot is mostly the live story listing, demonstrating the format
- Aimed at readers who want a quick gist before deciding to open an article
