# Scrimshaw Jukebox

## Summary

Simon Willison asked Claude Opus 5.5 to design a simple text music format, build an artifact that plays it, and compose game music with the quality of the original Secret of Monkey Island. He says it leaned harder into the Monkey Island theme than intended but the results are surprisingly good. He wonders whether competent music composition is a new capability for text models, like 3D graphics a few months ago, and notes it would take careful tests on other models to confirm.
