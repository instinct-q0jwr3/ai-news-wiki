# China's AI travel curbs now reach executives' spouses and children

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Bloomberg reports that China now requires the families of some top AI and chip executives - spouses and children included - to obtain Beijing's approval before travelling abroad, even for short trips. The rule covers prominent startup founders and heads of strategically important AI companies whose work Beijing considers vital to state security, and agencies have begun notifying affected people; more names will be added over time. It widens the travel curbs imposed earlier this year on top staff at private firms like Alibaba and DeepSeek, aimed at stopping key know-how flowing to the US. TNW notes it has not independently confirmed the report.

## Highlights

- China extends AI travel curbs to executives' spouses and children, per Bloomberg
- Family members need Beijing's approval even for short trips abroad
- Covers founders and heads of strategically important AI and chip companies
- Agencies have started notifying affected people; the list will grow
- Widens earlier curbs on staff at Alibaba, DeepSeek, and similar firms
- Aim: stop key know-how and information flowing to the US
