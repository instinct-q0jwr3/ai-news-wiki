# Artificial Analysis: Gemini 4 Neon trails only Claude Fable 5.2 and GPT-6.2 Astra on the Intelligence Index and rivals Opus 5.5 on coding at a much lower cost

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Artificial Analysis reports Gemini 4 Neon results on its intelligence, coding and document-work evaluations. The report distinguishes high and extra-high effort, includes completed-task cost and time, and notes that output-token use can offset part of a lower unit price.

These results describe the evaluator’s tasks and execution settings, not a universal ranking. In one visual test higher effort scores lower, and coding comparisons include different model-and-harness combinations. Some cost comparisons use Carbon’s non-discounted price.

## Highlights

- Evaluator reports task cost as well as token price
- Effort settings are not interchangeable
- Coding results depend on execution software
- Higher effort is not uniformly better
- Comparison uses specified non-discounted pricing
