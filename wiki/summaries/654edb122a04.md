## Summary

Mirendil introduced Fern, its first model, pretrained from scratch with a context window of up to 10M tokens. The first version, Fern 0.1, received a short round of post-training, and the company says new versions will ship frequently. It positions Fern as its most efficient model, built for focused, high-volume agentic tasks.
