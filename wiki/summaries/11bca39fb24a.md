## Highlights

- EliseAI raised $350M at a $4B valuation, nearly doubling in 13 months
- Co-led by a16z and Bessemer, with Ontario Teachers' as new investor
- Entirely primary capital - no secondary sales for early investors or employees
- Automates back-office work for housing and healthcare
- CEO Minna Song credits execution and market penetration, not a single catalyst
- Fourth a16z/Bessemer investment in EliseAI since 2023
