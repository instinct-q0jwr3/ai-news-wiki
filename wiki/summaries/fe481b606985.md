# Android gets AI defenses against messaging scams, fake calls

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Google is rolling out AI defenses on Android against scam messages and call-forwarding abuse. They target more sophisticated scams such as fraudulent bank alerts and voice-cloning attacks, and are reaching eligible devices now.

## Highlights

- AI scam detection for messages and call forwarding on Android.
- Aimed at fake bank alerts and voice cloning.
