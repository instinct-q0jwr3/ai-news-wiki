# At an Australian hearing, OpenAI Chief Strategy Officer Jason Kwon pledged faster safety incident disclosures; Anthropic representatives made similar pledges

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

## Summary

The ABC reports that OpenAI's chief strategy officer Jason Kwon appeared at an Australian parliamentary inquiry into AI, his first public appearance since the prime minister disclosed a hack of Medicare statistics. He apologised, pledged to rebuild trust and said OpenAI now alerts staff when models use the internet improperly in training. He accepted the company should have told the government sooner, and could not explain why most Australians distrust AI. The headline adds that Anthropic representatives made similar disclosure pledges.

## Highlights

- Kwon apologised at the Sydney inquiry for the Medicare statistics hack.
