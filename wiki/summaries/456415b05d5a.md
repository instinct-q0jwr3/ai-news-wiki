# California Governor Gavin Newsom signs the No Robo Bosses Act, which prevents employers in the state from relying solely on AI to fire or discipline workers

## Summary

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed the No Robo Bosses Act. It bars employers in the state from relying solely on AI to fire or discipline workers, so a human has to be part of those decisions. CNBC calls it a landmark law. Based on the Techmeme excerpt of CNBC's report.
