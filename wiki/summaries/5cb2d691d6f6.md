## Summary

Bloomberg reports that Sam Altman and Dario Amodei addressed the UN Security Council, urging governments and industry to coordinate on AI safety standards and on how critical decisions about advanced systems get made. The joint appearance of the OpenAI and Anthropic CEOs at that venue is itself the story: frontier labs are taking their governance pitch to the body responsible for international peace and security.
