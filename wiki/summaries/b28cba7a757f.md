# The United States of Inference

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The United States of Inference maps 50 AI inference providers to US states with a witty reason for each, such as Moonshot AI for Alabama's Rocket City, Crusoe for Alaska's gas-flaring oil wells, General Compute for Arizona's chip fabs and DeepInfra for Arkansas's Walmart. It is a playful directory of providers and what they run, including some outside the US such as Moonshot's Kimi lab in Beijing.

## Highlights

- Directory of 50 inference providers by state
- Each pairing explained with a joke
- Mix of providers: Crusoe, DeepInfra, General Compute, Moonshot
