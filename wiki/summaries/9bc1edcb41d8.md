# Chicago-based data analytics company Ocient raised a $100M Series C co-led by Boka Capital and OCA Ventures to grow its agentic AI analytics business

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Chicago data analytics company Ocient raised a $100M Series C, bringing its total funding to $260M, to expand its agentic AI analytics business, per SiliconANGLE. It says revenue should more than triple in the fiscal year ending in January after doubling the year before.

Growth comes from national security and telecom customers consolidating cybersecurity data without sampling. CEO John Morris says AI agents, not human analysts, increasingly query the system, raising utilization. Ocient competes with Snowflake and Databricks but says it often complements them; its design avoids moving data and uses forward error correction instead of replication.

## Highlights

- $100M Series C, total funding $260M.
- Revenue expected to more than triple this fiscal year.
- Demand from national security and telecom customers.
- Agents increasingly query the platform instead of human analysts.
- Competes with Snowflake and Databricks, often alongside them.
