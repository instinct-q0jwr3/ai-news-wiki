# App Store 2026 in numbers: AI, pricing, abandoned apps

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-price-war`

## Summary

Obelisk Club analyzed 178,637 apps on App Store top charts across 173 countries since Oct. 9, 2026. 9.8% mention AI, GPT, machine learning, generative or chatbot, clustered in Graphics & Design, Photo & Video and Developer Tools; ratings barely differ (4.49 vs 4.4). 26.7% of free apps advertise purchases or subscriptions; 67.3% come from independent developers with the same ratings as larger publishers. 70.9% were updated in six months, but 20.3% have not been for a year and 9.8% for three. Of 1-2 star US reviews, 12.7% cite subscriptions and price and 10.9% bugs. Data is CC BY 4.0, refreshed quarterly.

## Highlights

- 9.8% of charting apps mention AI; ratings about the same
- 26.7% of free apps push in-app purchases or subscriptions
- 67.3% independent developers, ratings equal to publishers
- 20.3% not updated for a year or more
