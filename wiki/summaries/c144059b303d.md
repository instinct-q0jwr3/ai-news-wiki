## Summary

ResolveAI announces SRE-1 and an on-call product, separating model-level incident evaluations from complete-system comparisons. It describes task-specific reinforcement learning, tools pinned to incident time and escalation when an engineer is needed.

Reported results come from company-selected production cases and repeated attempts. An ensemble result differs from one investigation, and a system comparison includes more than model weights. Customer statements and claimed alert closure rates were not independently audited.
