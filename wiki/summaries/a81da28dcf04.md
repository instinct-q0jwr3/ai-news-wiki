# Google Publishes RRSI for Self-Improving AI Agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `google` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Google's RRSI (a self-improving agent harness) reports gains on every out-of-distribution benchmark without overfitting the split it evolves on - the inverse of prior methods, whose large evolve-set gains shrink or vanish on new benchmarks, two ending below the harness they started from.

The method constrains its own edit loop: caps on how much one proposal may change and which measured gains may stick; early rounds may bundle coordinated edits to find a mechanism, late rounds get one attributable change. Every candidate is logged with hypothesis, diff, score and cost; when progress stalls inside the noise band, budget redirects to untouched components; task names, entities, answers and benchmark-specific logic are rejected before scoring; and a gain must clear the variance of the unchanged base harness, with extra inference tokens paid for.

## Highlights

- Improves every out-of-distribution benchmark without overfitting the evolve split.
- Prior self-improvement methods lose their gains - or regress - when the benchmark changes.
- Constrains proposal size and which measured gains are allowed to stick.
- Logs every candidate with hypothesis, diff, score and cost; redirects budget to untouched components when stalled.
- Rejects benchmark-specific edits pre-scoring; gains must beat base-harness variance.
