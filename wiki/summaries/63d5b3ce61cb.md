# Tell HN: GitHub refuses to remove cracked copies of my software after a month

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-08 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Hacker News poster describing himself as Photopea's developer says people are using AI to modify the editor's browser-delivered JavaScript, strip advertising, and redistribute it on GitHub. His concern is both distribution control and reputation: he says users of unofficial copies have contacted him about problems as though they were using the original service.

The post reproduces a GitHub response declining to confirm an anti-circumvention violation under section 1201 after a month-long wait. The discussion distinguishes that question from ordinary copyright infringement and debates whether a different legal complaint would be more effective. This is a dispute reported by one participant, not a court finding or independently confirmed account of GitHub's conduct.

## Highlights

- The poster reports dozens of unofficial repositories.
- AI-assisted code modification is the alleged copying mechanism.
- Advertising removal threatens the original distribution model.
- Users may confuse unofficial versions with the original.
- Section 1201 and copyright infringement are different legal questions.
