# Anthropic says new measures like preserved thinking in Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 have reduced data harvesting through distillation attacks by about 90% to 99%

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic says new countermeasures, including preserved thinking in Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5, have cut data harvested by distillation attacks by roughly 90 to 99 percent, based on early evidence. Distillation attacks are covert, industrial-scale campaigns, often run through networks of fraudulent accounts, that copy one model's capabilities into another.

Anthropic's concern is that distilled models approach the frontier without proportionate safeguards, raising biology and cyber risk. It cites Zhipu, which it says distilled US models' cyber abilities for GLM-5.3 and switched to an older Claude and another lab's model after Anthropic's cyber safeguards degraded its attacks on Fable 5. The US CAISSI assessed GLM-5.3 as the most cyber-capable open-weights model so far, and Anthropic says its safeguards are easy to bypass.

## Highlights

- Distillation harvesting reduced by about 90 to 99 percent, per early evidence.
- Anthropic credits measures such as preserved thinking in Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5.
- Accuses Zhipu of distilling cyber capabilities for GLM-5.3.
- CAISSI rates GLM-5.3 the most cyber-capable open-weights model released so far.
