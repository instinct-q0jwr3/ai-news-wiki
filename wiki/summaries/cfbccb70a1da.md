# Nuros – AI study tools from notes, PDFs, videos, and links

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Nuros is an AI study-tools product that turns notes, PDFs, videos and links into study guides, practice tests and topic explanations. Student testimonials describe a shift from last-minute rereading to slower, steadier spaced sessions: 'It brings back the things I forget so I don't feel like I am starting over every time', with material still feeling familiar weeks later.

Incoming college students credit it with compiling reliable sources across articles, YouTube and textbooks into clean, comprehensive study guides, and with replacing ineffective high-school habits with practice tests and in-depth explanations ahead of the school year.
