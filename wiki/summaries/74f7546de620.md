# Apple updates App Review Guidelines to crack down on AI nudify apps and apps that reveal undocumented capabilities, like nudification, after app review

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-17 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `apple`

## Summary

Apple's new App Review Guidelines crack down on AI 'nudify' apps and on apps that reveal undocumented features. Apps that let users create or share content face particular scrutiny over IP, nonconsensual intimate imagery and bullying, and hidden or dormant features are barred.

## Highlights

- AI nudify apps targeted on the App Store.
- Hidden or undocumented features prohibited.
