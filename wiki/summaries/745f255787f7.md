## Summary

AINews covers the launch of Meta's Muse Wonder and Wonder Full, which completes Meta Superintelligence Labs' 14-month climb to a frontier lab and gives Meta a model family alongside OpenAI's Sol, Terra and Luna and Anthropic's Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. Wonder Full beats Fable and Astra on some benchmarks, but with differing post-training it is unclear whether Wonder is truly in that class.

The issue then rounds up reactions and independent benchmark numbers from Artificial Analysis and others across Reddit and Twitter. Basis: the opening and lead item of a long newsletter.
