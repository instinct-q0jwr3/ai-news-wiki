# Cognex acquires Intel spinout RealSense for ~$600M

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Cognex, the Nasdaq-listed machine vision company, is acquiring RealSense, the robotics and 3D computer vision company spun out of Intel just 14 months ago, in a deal valued at approximately $600 million. The acquisition turns what was once an uncertain Intel research initiative into a substantial exit in the fast-growing market for robotic perception and Physical AI.

Cognex will pay approximately $500 million in cash, financed entirely from its existing balance sheet, plus a three-year cash retention program worth $56.5 million at target for RealSense employees, subject to performance modifiers, and restricted stock units valued at approximately $50 million. RealSense CEO Nadav Orbach, 46, says most of the Haifa-based company's Israeli employees will receive at least NIS 1 million ($330,000) from the transaction, and credits independence from Intel for letting the company pursue opportunities that would have been difficult to develop inside the chipmaker.
