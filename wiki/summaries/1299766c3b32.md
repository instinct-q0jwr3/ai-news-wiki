# The Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps — 7th Edition

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-07 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Andreessen Horowitz publishes the seventh edition of its Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps, with just 11 debuts, the fewest so far. For the first time it adds a ranking by observed spending on US consumer cards, using data from YipitData, which captures vendors missing from web and mobile lists such as desktop apps and agents inside messaging platforms.

The main finding is that consumer AI use is wide but shallow. Nearly half of US consumers report using AI, but only 25% use it daily, and as of August just 4.5% had an active paid personal subscription to ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. Heavy spenders matter: the top 10% account for roughly half of observed spending.

## Highlights

- Seventh edition, only 11 new entrants

- Nearly half of US consumers use AI, 25% daily
- 4.5% paid for ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude as of August
- Top 10% of spenders are about half of spending
