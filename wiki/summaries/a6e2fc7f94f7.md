## Summary

The Guardian estimates Donald Trump's Nvidia holdings gained up to $15 million after the rally accompanying Huang's reinstatement. It bases the upper estimate on financial-disclosure ranges and possible holdings as of September 30, and reports Trump saying he helped persuade the board.

The headline describes an estimated mark-to-market gain, not evidence of a newly realized sale or an exact current position. Holdings may have changed after the disclosure date, and price movements are volatile. The article's reporting of potential conflicts and allegations is distinct from a legal finding of insider trading or proof of a specific financial motive.
