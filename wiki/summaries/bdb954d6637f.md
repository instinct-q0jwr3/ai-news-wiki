# OpenAI launches Images 2.6 with GIFs and image reference

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-05 · confidence: high_

## Summary

TestingCatalog covers OpenAI's Images 2.6 launch: global rollout in ChatGPT and Codex on all tiers, with GIF creation and reusable reference images, so you can save yourself or a pet once and reuse them. GPT-Image-2.6 Flare and Sunburst arrive in the API, generating 50% faster, with Sunburst keeping edits more consistent at medium or high quality. OpenAI says usage has reached 4 billion images a week.

## Highlights

- Images 2.6 on all ChatGPT tiers and Codex
- GIFs and saved reference images
- API: Flare and Sunburst, 50% faster
- 4B images per week
