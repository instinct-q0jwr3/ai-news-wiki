# Introducing Flint

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-27 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Recursive introduced Flint, a mixture-of-experts model built for fast agentic coding on local hardware. Flint has 40 billion total parameters but activates just 2.5 billion per token, and the small team - people and agents working together - built it from scratch in under four months.

The company claims Flint leads all MoE models under 50 billion total parameters on agentic coding benchmarks including SWE-Bench Pro v2, Terminal-Bench 2.1, and SWE-Bench Verified, while rivaling the coding performance of dense models in the 25-40 billion parameter range. The pitch is local-first: frontier-ish coding assistance that runs on a laptop, with the speed that a tiny active-parameter count buys.

## Highlights

- Recursive's Flint is a 40B-total / 2.5B-active MoE model for local agentic coding
- Built from scratch in under four months by a small team of people and agents
- Claims benchmark lead among sub-50B MoE models on SWE-Bench Pro v2, Terminal-Bench 2.1, SWE-Bench Verified
- Rivals dense 25-40B models on coding while running on a laptop
- Local-first pitch: fast coding assistance without a datacenter
