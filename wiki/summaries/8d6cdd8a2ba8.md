# Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which it describes as the same underlying model. Fable carries strong cybersecurity and biology safeguards so it can go to all users; Mythos goes to vetted organizations through its Life Sciences and Cyber Verification Programs. The company says they improve coding, reasoning and vision and write outputs up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions.

Prerelease claims include completing software migrations that were otherwise infeasible, designing three working electronic devices, solving two open black-hole physics problems and formalizing the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours. Pricing falls to $8 per million input and $40 per million output tokens, with a fast mode at 2.5x speed for $16 and $80. Anthropic says high-stakes misalignment risk remains low and adds its strongest protections against distillation. All results are Anthropic's own.

## Highlights

- Fable and Mythos 5.2 are one model; Fable is the safeguarded public version
- Up to 30% faster output than 5.1
- $8 / $40 per million tokens, fast mode $16 / $80
- Claims: FLT formalization in 30 hours, two black-hole physics problems
- Mythos limited to vetted cyber and life-science organizations
