# Zus Health, which is building an AI-native healthcare data platform, raised a $33M Series B led by Drive Capital, bringing its total funding to $100M+

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-16 · updated: 2026-11-22 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `microsoft` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Fierce Healthcare reports Zus Health raised $33 million to expand connected patient records and AI-supported clinical workflows. Its platform combines a longitudinal record, a network for collecting outside clinical data and applications such as visit preparation and care management.

Transaction and citation volumes are company claims. A more connected record may help reduce fragmented information, but this funding report does not independently establish clinical accuracy, patient benefit or permission to use every available record for every workflow.

## Highlights

- Funding supports data and workflow infrastructure
- Platform aggregates longitudinal patient information
- Clinical applications use the shared data layer
- Usage metrics are company-reported
- Clinical benefit and access controls need separate evaluation
