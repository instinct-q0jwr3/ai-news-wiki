# Growth of AI Through a Cloud Lens

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`mitchell-hashimoto`

## Summary

17 years ago, Amazon kicked off a platform shift with the release of S3 and EC2 under the Amazon Web Services (AWS) umbrella.

This isn't a shift from cloud , this is a platform shift within a different category, but it has the same potential to fundamentally change the way we build and deliver software.

I will look at the rise of AI through the historical lens of the rise of cloud.

## Highlights

- This resulted in a shift best summarized as "cloud-native," fundamentally changing the way we build and deliver software worldwide from small individuals to the largest global companies.
- Today, AI -- in particular the advancements in large language models (LLMs) -- is starting to feel like the beginning of another platform shift.
- Rather than recount a full history of cloud (boring!), I will highlight a few categories of thought and use those categories to make comparisons from cloud to AI and predictions about the future.
- The early success of cloud was marked by providing immediate value to early adopters.
- Recent AI developments have the same immediate value feeling.
- At the same time, immediate value is often mistaken for durable value .
- Early cloud computing was raw and impractical for a large tract of problems.
- This led to excitement and growth in the context where cloud computing was practical.
