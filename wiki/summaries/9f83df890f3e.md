## Summary

OpenAI's OpenWorks page describes a US pilot offering free hands-on AI help to independently owned small businesses through local partners. The page lists twenty pilot communities, support for business-selected projects and three months of ChatGPT Business at no cost. Partners can receive a monthly stipend of up to $8,000 in return for local work and workshops.

Participation and stipend amounts have eligibility and selection limits, not a promise of automatic payment or universal availability. The page excludes software and SaaS companies, venture-backed businesses and private-equity-owned businesses from the small-business pilot. Customer stories are promotional self-reports, not controlled outcome studies, and no application or outreach was submitted.

## Highlights

- Pilot is for eligible independently owned US small businesses
- Twenty communities are listed on the page
- Business chooses the project to work on
- Three months of ChatGPT Business are included at no cost
- Partner stipend is up to $8,000, not guaranteed
- Several ownership and business categories are excluded
- Customer outcomes are testimonials rather than controlled evidence
