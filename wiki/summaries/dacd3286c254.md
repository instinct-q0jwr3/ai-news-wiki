## Summary

Dean Ball’s essay argues that reactive AI policy needs a clearer long-term picture of desirable outcomes. Its opening treats recent security developments as context and says urgent decisions alone may not add up to a good overall future.

The author explicitly speaks personally rather than for his employer. This summary covers the examined opening, not the full lecture, and does not independently validate its descriptions of cyber incidents or its account of industry beliefs.

## Highlights

- Essay is adapted from a lecture
- Author distinguishes personal and employer views
- Opening contrasts urgency and long-term goals
- Historical incident framing was not audited
- No industry-wide consensus is established
