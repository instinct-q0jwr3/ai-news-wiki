# Anthropic says Opus 5.5 matches Fable 5.1 "on most tasks" while costing about 40% less to run than Opus 5; Opus 5.5 costs $4/1M input and $20/1M output tokens

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in a new family, saying it matches Claude Fable 5.1 'on most tasks' while costing about 40% less to run than Opus 5 and generating output more than 30% faster. Pricing drops to $4 per million input and $20 per million output tokens (from $5/$25), with cache read costs cut 60%; five-hour usage limits rise 20% and stretch ~25% further overall. Anthropic's benchmarks put Opus 5.5 ahead of Fable 5.1 and OpenAI's much pricier GPT-6 Astra on most tasks, and Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are expected in the coming weeks.

## Highlights

- Opus 5.5 matches Fable 5.1 'on most tasks' at ~40% lower operating cost than Opus 5.
- Priced at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens; cache reads down 60%.
- Output generation over 30% faster; five-hour subscriber limits up 20%.
- Anthropic benchmarks claim leads over Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra on most tasks.
- Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 expected in coming weeks.
