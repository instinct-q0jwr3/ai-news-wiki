## Summary

Business Insider reports Larridin raised a $30 million Series A led by Pelion Ventures. The startup sells measurement for the AI hangover: companies went all in on AI this year and are now reckoning with heavy bills, and Larridin tracks how AI actually affects employee output and where adoption should be improved - the analytics layer for the ROI question Apollo's Slok keeps answering with 'zero.'
