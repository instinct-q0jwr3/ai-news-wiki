# Ask HN: Do you want a router that has DD-WRT pre-installed?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-12 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-safety-incidents` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

An Ask HN ponders whether routers with DD-WRT pre-installed could support a small company. The poster hesitates: options already exist (GL.iNet Flint, OpenWrt One), and the hackers who want open firmware would likely just flash their own - as the poster has done for years across an Archer C9, C8 and R7000, often bought used for under $30.

The comment sketches the market: Ubiquiti and MikroTik sell quality proprietary-OS routers around $65, Protectli and Netgate pfSense boxes start at $259, the Flint 2 is $169 and the OpenWrt One $89. The dream setup - a low-power SFF PC with dual NICs running OPNsense as the router, wireless gear reduced to access points - waits for 'fun money'.
