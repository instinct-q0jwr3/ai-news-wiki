# Faster Quantum Algorithms with Agents and Formal Verification

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Google DeepMind reports using Gemini 4 agent teams and Lean verification to improve selected quantum circuits. Reported reductions include an 11.8-fold computing-cost improvement for a fusion-related calculation, eightfold for a molecular simulation, 2.5-fold for a P450 application and large improvements to specific Grover and arcsin implementations.

The gains refer to defined algorithmic resource measures, not measured end-to-end speed on a practical fault-tolerant quantum computer. The authors explicitly say the scientific simulations still exceed likely early error-corrected hardware resources. Lean checks mathematical properties of specified results; they do not demonstrate fusion energy or drug-discovery outcomes. The library and solutions are announced as open source, without independent reproduction here.

## Highlights

- Agent teams search within researcher-specified problems
- Lean is used to verify proposed mathematical results
- Fusion example reports 11.8-fold lower computing cost
- P450 example reduces required error-corrected qubits from 481 to 333
- Grover and arcsin numbers apply to specific implementations
- Practical scientific runs still require more hardware
- No independent performance reproduction is claimed
