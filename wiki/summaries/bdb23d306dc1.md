## Summary

A public progress map tracks the Lean formalization of Alessandro Masullo's manuscript, The Music of the Primes, linking its figures to a particular repository commit. The snapshot generated on 23 November at 04:53 displays 8,106 of 8,671 theorems as proved, or 93%, but distinguishes 6,354 resting on no sorry from 1,752 using open lemmas. It also reports 565 open statements and 22 places with no known repair on which later results depend. The map makes dependencies and problems visible rather than merely presenting a completion percentage.

These are the dashboard's own project statistics at commit 0b7a234, not an independently rerun Lean build or acceptance of a Riemann proof. Its errata register counts statements refuted or repaired as well as unfinished work; a high local completion rate does not establish that every dependency of a final theorem is proved. The map's optimistic issue-closure forecast is a workload estimate, not a mathematical completion date. This Masullo formalization is distinct from the Alpöge claim and from the earlier hypothetical video.
