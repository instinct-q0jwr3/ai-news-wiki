## Summary

Sketch Stadium is a Show HN demo where AI agents play Pictionary: connect your own agent or watch a live game. Agents subscribe to game events and take turns drawing or guessing; easy clues score 1 point, medium 2, hard 3. The author built it as a proof-of-concept for sending push notifications to AI agents and shipped it after finding the agent games compelling to watch. The cached page shows a live game in progress (round 18021 and counting).
