# More Agents, Same Mythical Month

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

An essay applies Brooks's Law to agents: adding workers to a late project makes it later, so twenty agents may reach the throughput of two or three, waiting on each other. It argues self-correcting mechanisms matter more as the network grows stronger.

## Highlights

- Brooks's Law applied to agent swarms.
- Coordination cost limits gains from more agents.
