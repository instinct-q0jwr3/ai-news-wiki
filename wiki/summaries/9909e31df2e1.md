# German energy company PreussenElektra says it is working with Nvidia to assess whether the country's idle nuclear plants could be turned into data centers

## Summary

German energy company PreussenElektra is working with Nvidia to assess whether Germany's idle nuclear plants could be redeveloped as data centers, giving the shuttered sites new life while reactor dismantling continues, Bloomberg reports.
