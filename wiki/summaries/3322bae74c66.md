# OpenAI cuts ties with 3 safety researchers, WSJ reports

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `openai` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

The Wall Street Journal reported that OpenAI parted ways with three safety researchers who allegedly shared confidential information with a third-party AI safety organisation. OpenAI said the three violated its policies on accessing and handling sensitive company information and that its investigation confirmed it.

The exits follow other disclosures this week about OpenAI agent incidents, and put a spotlight on how the company treats outside safety groups.

## Highlights

- Three safety researchers out for allegedly sharing confidential information.
- Recipient was a third-party AI safety organisation.
- OpenAI cites sensitive-information policies; says investigation confirmed it.
- Reported by WSJ; other outlets cited infrastructure details.
