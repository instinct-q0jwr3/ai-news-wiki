## Summary

The Wall Street Journal reports that Broadcom has been working to arrange more than $50 billion in financing for OpenAI's custom AI chip, and that Oracle is in talks on financing for a large chip purchase. Lenders in talks include Apollo, Blackstone and Goldman Sachs, for megadeals worth tens of billions each.

The article is paywalled, so only the Techmeme excerpt was read. It shows how AI chip spending is being financed with large debt packages rather than cash alone.
