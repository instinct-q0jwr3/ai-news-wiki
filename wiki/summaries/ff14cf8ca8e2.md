# The AI Superintelligence Debate Has Serious Flaws

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-19 · updated: 2026-11-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `amazon`

## Summary

An IEEE Spectrum essay questions engineering assumptions behind recursive self-improvement and superintelligence forecasts. The examined opening argues that progress in tasks with verifiable training signals need not transfer uniformly to messier domains, while warning that pursuing superintelligence can still cause harm.

This is a technical and cultural argument, not a proof that superintelligence is impossible or a measured probability of catastrophe. The summary covers the examined opening and data-bottleneck discussion rather than the full essay.

## Highlights

- Essay distinguishes AGI from more ambitious ASI
- Verifiable feedback is central to its critique
- Uneven progress complicates extrapolation
- Pursuit can carry risks even if the goal fails
- Argument is not an impossibility theorem
