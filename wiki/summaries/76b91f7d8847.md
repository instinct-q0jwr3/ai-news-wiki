## Summary

antirez's end-of-2025 stocktake on AI, in bullet form. His first note: after years of holdouts calling LLMs stochastic parrots - machines with no representation of prompt meaning or of what they were about to say - 2025 is the year almost everyone stopped saying so; the functional evidence and scientific hints accumulated past the point of denial.

He goes on to chain-of-thought and the year's other shifts (the post continues the enumeration). The piece is the companion essay to 'Don't fall into the anti-AI hype': a builder with deep credibility registering that the ground has genuinely moved.
