# Zenzap, a private messenger with AI agents that aims to replace apps like WhatsApp in the workplace, raised $44M from Bessemer, Lightspeed, and others

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Zenzap is a private work messenger with built-in AI agents, aimed at companies whose staff coordinate over WhatsApp, Telegram or iMessage. It promises company context for the AI while keeping to IT and security rules.

It has raised $44 million from investors including Bessemer and Lightspeed. Founder Guy Weiss is raising for the first time after earlier bootstrapping a marketing platform.

## Highlights

- Zenzap pitches a private work messenger with AI agents.
- Raised $44 million led by Bessemer and Lightspeed.
