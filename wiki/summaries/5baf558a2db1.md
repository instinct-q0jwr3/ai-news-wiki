# I struggle to believe that AI R&D is ingenious

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A LessWrong shortform post by Arjun Panickssery says he struggles to believe autonomous AI R&D is ingenious. In his side projects the model's judgment is basic-level poor: eight hackathon papers he judged scored 60-95% on Pangram, were similar and methodologically incomplete in the same way. The exception is hill-climbing tasks with fast feedback: he improved the SOTA for listing legal Scrabble moves 10x by prompting Astra repeatedly, though key leaps came from his own suggestions. Astra also writes hard-to-follow reports. A reply agrees that AIs need human direction. A single person's anecdote.

## Highlights

- AI judgment seen as basic-level poor in his projects
- Works best on fast-feedback hill-climbing, still needing human hints
- 10x Scrabble-move SOTA came with human-suggested leaps
- Anecdotal, not a benchmark
