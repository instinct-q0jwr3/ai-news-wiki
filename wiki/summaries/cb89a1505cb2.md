# OpenAI launches the Grid Protectors Program, a US effort for its future data center campuses to reduce electricity demand when the grid is under strain

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

## Summary

OpenAI announced the Grid Protectors Program, a US initiative under which its future data center campuses will reduce electricity demand when the grid is under strain. It's a grid-relations move as much as an energy one: siting opposition increasingly runs through local power prices and reliability fears, and curtailment commitments give utilities and communities something concrete.
