# Anti-Patterns in Software Blogging

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`simon-willison` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

Simon Willison passes on Michael Lynch's writing advice for software bloggers. Lynch warns against meandering intros, misjudging what readers already know, assuming they read earlier posts, and excessive formality.

Willison adds that with so many developers delegating writing to AI, software blogging is getting bland and samey. The feed excerpt also notes a warning against leaning on links instead of explaining terms.

## Highlights

- Anti-patterns in software blogging
- Avoid meandering intros and excess formality
- Do not assume readers read earlier posts
- AI delegation makes blogs bland
