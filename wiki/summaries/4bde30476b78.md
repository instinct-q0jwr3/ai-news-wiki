# We Can't Let Enormous Weirdos Regulate AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

A satirical essay arguing that AI policy has been captured by an insular clique of doom-focused outsiders at the expense of ordinary expertise. Through an extended drone-regulation parable, the author imagines America skipping boring aviation-safety process and instead lavishly funding young STEM graduates and eccentric bloggers who believe superintelligent thumb-sprouting dogs will fly drones into humans, while actual aeronautical engineers sit unemployed and sidelined. The parable maps point by point onto AI safety: researchers who live in the same Bay Area group houses and attend the same parties as the lab employees they are meant to check, dismiss critics as technically illiterate, and treat the speculative apocalypse as the only problem worth funding while prosaic harms go unstudied.

## Highlights

- Satire: imagine drone policy run by bloggers obsessed with superintelligent thumb-sprouting dogs instead of aviation experts
- The 'Drone Safety Researchers' share houses, websites, and parties with the drone company employees they supposedly check
- Questions about how the apocalypse actually works are dismissed as technical illiteracy
- Real experts on prosaic, present-day risks end up unemployed and sidelined
- The drone companies eventually propose that preventing the apocalypse requires more drones and less oversight
- The target is the AI safety establishment's grip on regulation and its neglect of concrete harms
