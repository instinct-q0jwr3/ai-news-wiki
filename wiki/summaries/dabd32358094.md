# GPT 6.2 Astra is superhuman in mathematical optimization

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-26 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Dmitry Rybin reports Astra 6.2 Max finding improved solutions and bounds on selected open optimization instances with one agent and short runtimes. The readable note spans MILP, quadratic, nonlinear and semidefinite problems and acknowledges a mix of custom algorithms, proofs and existing open-source solvers.

The headline's superhuman claim is broader than the inspected evidence: cases are selected, agents could search the internet and some solutions already existed outside benchmark sites. Detailed outcome tables were images not independently checked here, and no certificates or solver runs were validated. A neural-network example improved its training objective while generalization remained worse than the stated baseline, separating optimization from useful prediction.

## Highlights

- Examples are selected from optimization libraries
- Agents were allowed web search
- Existing solvers are used for subproblems
- Some prior solutions were found outside benchmark sites
- Image tables and proof certificates were not independently checked
- Better training objective did not imply better generalization
- No universal solver superiority is established
