## Summary

antirez argues that whatever their flaws, AI systems keep replicating human skills that were science fiction a few years ago - and that experts have failed to calibrate expectations in both directions, so prediction is unreliable. A plateau is credible, he grants, but at this point it would likely just trigger massive research into the next architecture.

His sharper point is economic: if AI keeps improving long enough to become significantly more independent of humans, this revolution will be very unlike past ones - yet markets are reacting as if governed by stochastic parrots, pattern-matching previous tech booms into the assumption that AI will likewise create more business opportunities than it destroys. That, he warns, is not the only possible outcome.
