## Summary

Regardless of their flaws, AI systems continue to impress with their ability to replicate certain human skills.

Even if imperfect, such systems were a few years ago science fiction.

It was not even clear that we were so near to create machines that could understand the human language, write programs, and find bugs in a complex code base: bugs that escaped the code review of a competent programmer.
