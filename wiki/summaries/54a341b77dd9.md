# AI Company Lets Cops Pinpoint Specific Houses Based on a Single Interior Photo

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

404 Media reports that Raven, the police geolocation tool from Graylark Technologies (formerly GeoSpy), can now locate the interior of a home from a single photo. Graylark's CEO announced the ability to geolocate interior images across millions of short-term rental units. In a demo, a washer-dryer photo from social media was matched to a Charlotte Airbnb listing, and a bathroom photo to a Vrbo beach house in Montecito, with results quoting the listings' own text.

The tool apparently draws on listing data from sites like Zillow and Airbnb. The Los Angeles Police Department and Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office have previously bought access, and the report treats it as an example of AI power now available to local law enforcement.

## Highlights

- Raven can match a single interior photo to a specific short-term rental listing.
- Demo results quoted real Airbnb and Vrbo listings in Charlotte and Montecito.
- Graylark, formerly GeoSpy, apparently scrapes Zillow and Airbnb data.
- LAPD and Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office previously bought access.
