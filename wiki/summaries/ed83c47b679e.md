## Summary

A quotation collected by Simon Willison from TikTok creator @therealcornpop: audiences can tell when creators use AI to write their video scripts, and the giveaway runs deeper than the usual tells.

It's not just the familiar AI-isms - 'it's not X, it's Y', the rule of three, the weird broken staccato that sounds deep but isn't. The real tell is absence: no spear of a voice, and no detectable opinions about the thing being discussed.
