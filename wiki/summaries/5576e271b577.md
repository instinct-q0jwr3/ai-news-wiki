# Ahead of Anthropic's IPO, CAIS and others sign the MisAnthropic Statement, challenging the company's "safety" branding and its "reckless attempt" to build RSI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-18 · updated: 2026-11-20 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

The Center for AI Safety announces a coalition statement criticizing Anthropic’s safety positioning and ambitions for recursively improving AI. It frames company commitments, policy activity and concentration of power as reasons for opposition.

This is advocacy from a participating organization. Its allegations and interpretation of motives were not independently adjudicated, and a coalition’s disagreement with a company does not itself establish misconduct or the probability of a technical catastrophe.

## Highlights

- Source announces an advocacy statement
- Criticism concerns safety branding and power
- Allegations remain attributed to the coalition
- Motive claims were not independently verified
- Statement is not a technical risk measurement
