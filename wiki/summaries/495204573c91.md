## Summary

Cory Doctorow's advice to Canada starts from a single observation about Trump's dealmaking: the art is one word, renege. Trump tore up NAFTA - a deal already tilted to America's advantage at Canada's expense - and replaced it with CUSMA, which was even better for the US and even worse for Canada. Then, reelected in 2024, he attacked CUSMA in exactly the language he had used against NAFTA, branding his own deal 'a very bad deal' that must be shredded.

The lesson Doctorow draws: no agreement with the United States binds, so appeasement only buys the next shakedown. Canada's real option is to build leverage - rallying other countries, retaliating where it hurts, and cutting its dependence on American platforms and supply chains. Done right, he argues, Canada could both defend itself and genuinely help Americans undercut the oligarchy that abuses both countries; his quip is that true Carneyism has never been tried.

## Highlights

- Trump's dealmaking summed up in one word: renege
