# The US AI Labs, Mapped: Founders, Revenue and the Money Behind Them

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

With Anthropic's S-1 public, this analysis maps the nine major US AI labs - founders, revenue, and the money behind them - and starts with the arithmetic of the moment. Five months before filing, Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation against $4.59 billion of 2025 revenue: 210 times trailing sales. Measured against its own stated run-rate above $47 billion, the same round is 21 times. Two honest multiples for one company, tenfold apart.

The filing shows the run-rate is no one-month spike: H1 2026 revenue was $16.29 billion, 14 times the same half a year earlier and more than three times all of 2025. The author's conclusion frames the whole sector: last year's accounts are not what anyone is paying for. The US labs are priced on the slope of the revenue line and on a bet about how long it stays that steep - and the map asks what rate the market pays for slope, and whose money pays it.
