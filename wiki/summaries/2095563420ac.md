## Summary

Ed Zitron's 'Credit Crunch' argues that the financing behind the AI data-center boom is tightening. Bond markets are turning hostile to lower-rated borrowers - CleanSpark, developing a data center for Meta, had to offer investors big concessions to land financing - and banks including Société Générale, Sumitomo Mitsui and Mitsubishi UFJ are becoming more selective about data-center lending.

The piece traces how dependent the boom has become on a small set of aggressive lenders like Blue Owl, whose executives once agreed in 15 minutes to invest up to $10 billion alongside Primary Digital Infrastructure, and on the creditworthiness of players like CoreWeave, which Zitron calls 'a massive stinker of a company'. The through-line: as borrowing gets expensive and investors picky, planned data centers - and the AI growth plans resting on them - are at risk.

## Highlights
