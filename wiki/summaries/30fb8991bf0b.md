# How Much Does Reasoning Effort Affect Score and Cost?

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Vals AI tested how much reasoning effort changes score and cost, running the top model from seven labs at lowest, middle and leaderboard (usually highest) settings across the eight benchmarks of its Vals Index. At the lowest setting four of seven models kept at least 80% of their score for half the cost or less: Claude Opus 5.5 kept 80% at 16% of the cost, Grok 4.7 kept 86% at 16%, and Gemini 4 Argon kept 99% at 84%. Gemini 4 Argon's middle setting even scored slightly above leaderboard at 78% of the cost.

But cheaper is not free. On the whole index, 0 of 7 models matched leaderboard score at the lowest setting. In 22 of 56 model-and-benchmark pairs a cheaper setting matched the leaderboard score, none significantly different, while 33 were significantly lower. With hindsight selection, models gained a median 6.0 points over their leaderboard setting at 64% of the cost, but a real system must choose before seeing results, so real gains would be smaller. The practical lesson is per-task routing of effort, since some tasks need more reasoning than others.

## Highlights

- Lowest effort kept 80%+ of score at half the cost or less for 4 of 7 models; Opus 5.5 kept 80% at 16% of cost
- 0 of 7 models matched the leaderboard score at the lowest setting on the full index
- Cheaper settings matched leaderboard in 22 of 56 pairs; 33 were significantly lower
- Hindsight per-task choice would gain a median 6 points at 64% cost; real systems would gain less
