# Anthropic says it is giving employee-like access to METR as part of its pledge to partner with evaluators for assessment of its models and safety practices

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `external-evaluation` `small-specialist-models` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Anthropic says it has begun giving a team from METR, a nonprofit that evaluates frontier AI models, employee-like access. This follows last month's commitment to stronger third-party assessment and the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, where companies pledged to partner with outside auditors.

METR is expected to publish its findings.

## Highlights

- METR gets employee-like access at Anthropic.
- Follows a pledge to use independent evaluators.
