# AI agents now control part of DeepMind's compute for Gemini experiments

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `gemini` `agentic-systems` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

The Decoder summarizes a DeepMind podcast in which Koray Kavukcuoglu says agents now allocate part of the lab's compute to experiments for Gemini. He describes a shift toward general-purpose models for science and products, with each generation still assembled through substantial human research rather than a fixed recipe.

The available account is secondary and gives no fraction of compute, controlled productivity comparison or independent audit. Kavukcuoglu explicitly distinguishes this from full recursive self-improvement. His stated practice of not directly rewarding internal thought text is a claimed monitoring strategy, not proof that all model intentions are faithful, transparent or safe. No experiment, compute allocation or model operation was performed.
