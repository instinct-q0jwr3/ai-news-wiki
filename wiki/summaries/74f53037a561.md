# Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows sweeping AI vision, surging costs

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `anthropic`

## Summary

Reuters got Anthropic's IPO prospectus: the company is betting AI will transform the economy more profoundly than industrialization, electricity, and the internet - at staggering cost. The filing shows a $42 billion net loss in 2025 and plans for $518 billion in cloud, compute, and infrastructure obligations in coming years. Revenue grew 12-fold in 2025 to nearly $4.6 billion while operating losses exceeded $8 billion (excluding writedowns mostly tied to prior fundraising). The offering, which could value Anthropic above $2 trillion, would benchmark how Wall Street prices frontier AI labs - a five-year-old company with explosive growth and losses to match.
