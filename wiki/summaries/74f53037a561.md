## Highlights

- Anthropic IPO prospectus: AI as a bigger transformation than industrialization, electricity, or the internet
- $42B net loss in 2025; $518B of cloud/compute/infrastructure obligations planned
- Revenue grew 12x in 2025 to ~$4.6B; operating loss over $8B
- IPO could value the five-year-old lab above $2 trillion
- Would set the benchmark for how Wall Street values frontier AI companies
