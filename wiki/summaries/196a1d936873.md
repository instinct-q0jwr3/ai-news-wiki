# Meta Introduces Custom Spaces in Muse

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `meta` `muse`

## Summary

Meta is adding 'spaces' to Muse: small custom experiences you create by describing an idea, such as a holiday gift tracker, a daily word game or a DIY planning board. Each space becomes part of Muse and can be refined as you use it, so the product shifts from chat toward personal apps built by prompt.

## Highlights

- Spaces are custom mini-apps built by describing them
- Examples: gift tracker, word game, planning board
- Spaces persist inside Muse and can be adjusted over time
