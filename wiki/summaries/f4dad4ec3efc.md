## Highlights

- Qwen-3.8-Flash-Next reverse-engineered and ported War of the Lance (1989) to browsers in about a week
- Faithful reproduction of rendering and rules in a new Three.js engine, then modernized into a Total War-style UX
- Fable 5.1 + Blender headless API: ~80-100 usable 3D models per week of subscription
- GPT-6 Astra is a clear step up - 'mid-career professional' 3D modelling where Fable was junior
- Thesis: knowledge acquisition time is what priced skill; AI collapses that moat
- Synthesizing exact features is now easier than total-conversion modding of an existing AAA game
