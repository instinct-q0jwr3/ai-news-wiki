## Summary

1Password has passed $500 million in ARR, up from $400 million in November 2025, with the Wall Street Journal attributing the growth to enterprise adoption of AI coding assistants and agents. The company also announced 1Password for Agents, extending its credential management to non-human actors, and reports double-digit free cash flow margins.
