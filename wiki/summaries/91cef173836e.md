# Cat Wu joins OpenAI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Cat Wu announced on X that she is joining OpenAI to lead product management for Core Products. She cites OpenAI showing scaling works, bringing AI to over a billion people and believing in products as core to the mission. She will work with Tibo Sottiaux and the PM team to build agents that 'have your back' while keeping users in control. The post had 2.9M views.

- Will work with Tibo Sottiaux
