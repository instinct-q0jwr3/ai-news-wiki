# Gemini 4 Argon is now available for everyone

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `gemini`

## Summary

Google makes Gemini 4 Argon generally available: via the API in AI Studio and the Gemini platform for developers and enterprises, in Antigravity for AI Ultra subscribers, and in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search for Ultra and Pro subscribers. Google positions Argon for deep, multi-step work across coding, finance, legal, cybersecurity, and science, and says it ranks #1 on the Vals Index of economically critical sector performance, launching at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output with cached input at 95% off. Internally, Google says Argon in Antigravity lifted agentic code submissions by more than 35% while cutting rollbacks by nearly a third; demos show it autonomously sculpting a multi-building temple in Blender via multi-agent visual critique loops and turning photos of a real desk into a playable miniature racing game through Blender MCP, Nano Banana texturing, and Three.js.
