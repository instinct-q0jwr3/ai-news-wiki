## Summary

Push Square reports rapid PS5 jailbreak and emulator development, with modders using AI tools to speed work. It describes support for firmware from 7.00 through 13.60 and patchy emulator performance, while noting that newer games requiring newer firmware remain inaccessible.

The article sees a continuing contest between firmware fixes and exploitation of older installations, with enthusiasm affecting demand for compatible consoles. Its ideas about future updates or a faster PS6 transition are speculation, not Sony announcements. No exploit instructions or repositories are provided here.

## Highlights

- Firmware version limits determine reported applicability.
- Newer game requirements still restrict use.
- Emulator performance is described as uneven.
- AI tools are reported as helping modders move faster.
- Predictions about Sony's next steps are speculative.
