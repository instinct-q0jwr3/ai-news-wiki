# What is Speculative Decoding?

## Summary

A first-principles derivation of speculative decoding. LLMs spend most decoding time waiting, not computing: every token requires reading all model weights from memory to multiply them by a single vector. The core trick - check several tokens for the price of one - is built up from scratch into a general framework covering every modern drafter architecture.

The walkthrough covers EAGLE-3 and multi-token prediction through block-diffusion drafters like DFlash and DSpark, then closes on training: why minimizing KL divergence is not quite what you want from a drafter.

## Highlights

- Speculative decoding derived from first principles.
- Bottleneck: each token re-reads all weights to multiply by one vector.
- Trick: verify several tokens for the price of one forward pass.
- Covers EAGLE-3, multi-token prediction, and block-diffusion drafters (DFlash, DSpark).
- Training insight: minimizing KL divergence is not quite the right objective.
