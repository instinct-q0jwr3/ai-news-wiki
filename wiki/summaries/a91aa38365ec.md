# Two Kinds of Test-Time Scaling

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

James Tillman separates two kinds of test-time scaling. The ordinary kind trades more compute for better results per task up to the compute used in training. 'Compute extrapolation' goes beyond training-level compute, and he thinks it likely speeds frontier progress, makes dangerous-capability evaluation harder and changes race dynamics. He sees it as newer and still being mastered through context management and multi-agent scaling, and argues it should be measured.

## Highlights

- Distinction: scaling within vs beyond training compute
- Extrapolation may speed progress and hinder safety evals
- Improvements likely via context management, multi-agent setups
- Worth measuring
