# NTT Data is in advanced talks to build a data center for Anthropic in Osaka of up to 500 MW, among Japan's largest, potentially costing $6.3B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-13 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Nikkei Asia reports, citing multiple sources, that NTT Data Group is in final-stage talks to build Anthropic's first dedicated data center in Japan, in Osaka. The facility could reach 500 MW, among the largest in the country, cost about $6.3 billion and partially open by 2028. Basis: the article's opening paragraphs.

## Highlights

- NTT Data in advanced talks to build an Osaka data center for Anthropic.
- Up to 500 MW, potentially $6.3B, partial opening by 2028.
- Would be Anthropic's first dedicated data center in Japan.
