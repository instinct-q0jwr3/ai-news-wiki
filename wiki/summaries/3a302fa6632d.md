# the NSA told lawmakers it is spending billions this year to test AI models; proposals for a US AI regulatory body estimated costs of $20M-$40M per year

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-safety-incidents` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

The Washington Sun reports, citing two sources familiar with classified intelligence estimates, that the NSA told lawmakers it is spending billions of dollars this year evaluating and testing advanced AI models. The NSA's Artificial Intelligence Security Center has been testing frontier models to identify potential national security vulnerabilities.

The price tag - significantly greater than previously known - has led lawmakers to believe a more comprehensive AI regulatory system could cost the government tens of billions of dollars per year, the sources said. The report notes President Trump has mostly resisted calls for greater federal oversight of AI, and that the disclosures follow a string of high-profile hacking and security incidents.
