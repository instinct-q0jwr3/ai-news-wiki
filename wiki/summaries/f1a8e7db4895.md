# The doom justifies the valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`geohot`

## Summary

geohot (George Hotz) argues that AI doom messaging is 'designed to cause panic' - optimized, in fact, so that no framing of the actual products could induce more psychological spiraling. The 'AI Apocalypse' news cycle, he writes, has served mainly to contextualize the industry's valuation around hypothetical future value: 'a baseless assertion of infinite growth between the lines of a public-facing eschatology.'

After two weeks back in Berkeley he turns the essay on the doom culture itself, calling it a cult that needs the apocalypse (or acceleration) to justify life choices, and extends the critique to SF, LA and New York's respective demands on attention and meaning.
