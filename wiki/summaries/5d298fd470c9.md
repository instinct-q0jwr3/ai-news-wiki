# Samsung's Android XR smart glasses hands-on: stylish with good privacy protections like local face redaction and nice Gemini features like real-time translation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-12 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `google` `meta` `gemini`

## Summary

The Verge's hands-on account finds Android XR glasses from Samsung and partner brands more useful than expected in translation and other Gemini demonstrations. It also praises proactive privacy features such as local face redaction.

These are impressions from a demonstration rather than a long-term test of battery life, reliability or every privacy implication. Local redaction can reduce one kind of exposure without proving that all data collection risks are removed. The review separates product promise from the broader social unease around camera-equipped glasses.

## Highlights

- Hands-on demos emphasize translation and AI assistance
- Multiple eyewear brands use Samsung hardware
- Reviewer praises local privacy measures
- Demo impressions are not long-term reliability results
- One privacy feature does not settle all data risks
