# Inception launches Mercury 3, a diffusion-based LLM it claims is "comparable to Luna and Haiku models" but 4x to 8x faster, generating ~1,000 tokens per second

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `anthropic` `agentic-systems` `external-evaluation` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

The Deep View covers Inception's Mercury 3 launch with an eye on the contrarian bet: while the industry scaled autoregressive LLMs and their data centers, Inception's diffusion LLM generates text much faster at lower cost and now matches low- and mid-tier Anthropic and OpenAI models on quality. Inception also reports running swarms of Mercury 3 agents for recursive self-improvement, which sped the model up by a further 16% on top of the team's own upgrades. Mercury 3 is described as the most capable diffusion LLM to date, claimed comparable to Luna and Haiku models while generating around 1,000 tokens per second - 4x to 8x faster.

## Highlights

- Mercury 3 covered as the contrarian diffusion bet against autoregressive scaling
- Claimed comparable to Luna/Haiku-tier models at ~1,000 tokens/sec (4-8x faster)
- Inception used swarms of Mercury 3 agents for recursive self-improvement
- The agent swarm added 16% speed on top of the team's own upgrades
- Diffusion LLMs generate text faster at lower cost than traditional LLMs
