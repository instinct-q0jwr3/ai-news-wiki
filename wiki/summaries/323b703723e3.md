## Summary

Bert Hubert closes his Hello Deep Learning series - a 20,000-word from-scratch introduction - with guidance on where to go next. His warning: much of what is online about deep learning is sloppy or plain wrong, so he keeps a curated short list of books and sites he has found genuinely useful, and invites readers to send their own favorites by pull request or email.

## Highlights

- Wrap-up page of the Hello Deep Learning series
- A 20k-word introduction leaves much to learn - and much nonsense to avoid
- Curated list of useful books and sites
- Reader contributions welcome via GitHub or email
