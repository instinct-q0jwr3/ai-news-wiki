# Google’s Project Suncatcher to put ML infrastructure in space

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `google` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Google unveiled Project Suncatcher, an early test to scale AI compute in space. The team is launching a prototype satellite to evaluate whether Google's AI hardware can survive the harsh conditions of orbit: extreme radiation, intense vibrations and the unique cooling challenges of a vacuum.

A new video series from the Project Suncatcher team explores the science behind the moonshot, what they have found so far and the engineering challenges that remain. The September 24 announcement is by Travis Beals, Senior Director of Paradigms of Intelligence.

## Highlights

- Project Suncatcher: Google's early test to scale AI compute in space.
- A prototype satellite will test whether AI chips survive orbit.
- Challenges: extreme radiation, intense vibration, cooling in a vacuum.
- Findings shared in a new video series on the mission's science.
- Announced September 24 by Travis Beals, Paradigms of Intelligence.
