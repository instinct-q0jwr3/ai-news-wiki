# Q&A with Brian Chesky on Airbnb's plans for agent-to-agent interactions, why chatbots fail at travel e-commerce, the need for an AI-native OS, and more

## Summary

In a TechCrunch interview, Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky says a chatbot is the wrong interface for travel and shopping because it shows a few options at a time and needs several turns. Airbnb has just launched AI-powered search, but he says it is not the endgame either and expects something between a chatbot and the current version.

The talk covers where interfaces are headed, why consumer AI still is not working well, and the role of voice. Only the opening of the interview was read.

## Highlights

- Chesky still rejects chatbot-only interfaces for travel.
- Airbnb's new AI search is a step, not the final form.
- Topics include voice and why consumer AI underdelivers.
- Basis: opening of the interview.
