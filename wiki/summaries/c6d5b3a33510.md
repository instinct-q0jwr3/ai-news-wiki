# A look at a 1,700-member Slack run by Medicare agency CMS where Microsoft, OpenAI, and other companies help shape policy on AI apps and medical records access

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-10 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

## Summary

CBS News reports that a cadre of tech executives, financiers and high-level Trump administration officials has spent a year quietly charting a campaign to persuade Americans to use AI-powered apps to manage their healthcare and medical records. As the campaign unfolded, officials granted tech companies an unusual opportunity to nudge the nation's health department toward a loosely regulated future in which the government reimburses AI health app services as it does medical professionals.

In February, federal health officials invited Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI, Apple and Google, among others, to a listening session on conversational AI products for patients; representatives of at least 35 industry organizations attended. A 1,700-member Slack run by CMS, the Medicare agency, serves as the venue where these companies help shape policy on AI apps and medical records access.
