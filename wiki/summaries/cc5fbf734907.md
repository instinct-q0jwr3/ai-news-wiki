# Siri AI Class-Action Lawsuit Settlement Website Is Now Live

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-10-02 · confidence: high_

`daring-fireball` `apple` `amazon` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

The settlement website for Apple's $250 million Siri AI class action is now live. The suit alleged false advertising after Apple previewed a personalized Siri powered by Apple Intelligence in June 2024 - personal context, on-screen awareness, per-app controls - promoted it in presentations, on its site and in a Bella Ramsey TV commercial, and then delayed the launch in March 2025. Apple denies the allegations and says it settled to avoid the costs and risks of a trial; Siri AI finally shipped at WWDC 2026 and remains a beta feature on iPhone 15 Pro and newer running iOS 27.

Eligibility covers US residents who bought one of the listed iPhone models in the US (not for resale) between June 10, 2024 and March 29, 2025. Claims run from September 21 to December 21; the estimated payment is $25 per eligible iPhone, varying with claim volume up to a $95 maximum. Eligible customers are being emailed a claim ID and PIN, but claims can also be filed without them using the device serial number or Apple Account details. A final approval hearing is set for February 24, 2027, so payments won't begin until next year.

## Highlights

- Apple is paying $250M to settle the class action over the delayed personalized Siri
- The claim: Siri's Apple Intelligence features were advertised in 2024 (including a Bella Ramsey TV ad) and delayed in March 2025
- Estimated $25 per eligible iPhone, adjustable up to $95 depending on total valid claims
- Eligibility: US purchase of a listed iPhone between June 10, 2024 and March 29, 2025, for personal use
- Claim window: September 21 to December 21; notification emails carry a claim ID and PIN but aren't required
- Apple denies all allegations; settled 'to stay focused' on products
- Siri AI shipped at WWDC 2026, still labelled beta on iPhone 15 Pro+ with iOS 27
- Final approval hearing February 24, 2027 - payments start sometime next year
