# London-based Alecto, which uses AI and legal experts to help consumers understand UK property law, raised a $20M seed led by Insight Partners

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

SiliconANGLE reports that Alecto Conveyancing raised $20 million in seed funding led by Insight Partners. Founded by Will Rice, the London firm describes a service combining AI and legal experts to explain property transactions and help consumers understand the process. Conveyancing is its first market, with broader geographic and legal expansion presented as a later ambition.

The article reports the company's financing announcement and its case against opaque, hourly legal services. It does not demonstrate lower costs, better legal outcomes or error-free advice, and its goal of leadership in property legal services by 2027 is not an achieved market position. No regulated-status register, service terms or customer result was checked here; the presence of human legal experts should not be collapsed into a fully autonomous legal service.
