# Show HN: AI search for every photo and every frame of video on macOS

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-04 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

SCM is a local-first macOS app that searches every photo and video frame in a folder with AI, with no accounts, cloud or uploads. All inference runs on the Mac, on Apple Silicon with macOS 12 or later.

Typing first runs a fast filename keyword pass, then a vision model takes over, scoring results by cosine similarity against image embeddings. It installs through a Homebrew cask that clears the quarantine flag, or can be built with bun as an Electron app.

## Highlights

- Private, on-device search over photos and video frames.
- Apple Silicon, macOS 12+, installs through Homebrew.
- Keyword pre-pass, then vision-model embedding search.
- Electron app built with bun.
