# Some Thing is Happening A note from the AI companies

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A satirical open letter purports to be 'a note from the AI companies' to creative people, cheerfully explaining that the industry scanned the internet, books, magazines and archives to assemble collections of creative work, and intends to make a lot of money selling 'distillations of your creativity' - unlimited, uncredited and uncompensated imitations, for years to come.

The FAQ format does the satirical work: Is generative AI plagiarism? 'Plagiarism is a gray area... We only do that sometimes.' Will you open-source your model in return, since information wants to be free? 'That's a great question! Unfortunately, no.' The piece lands in the long-running argument over training data, consent and compensation - its bite comes from stating the industry's position plainly and letting it indict itself.

## Highlights
