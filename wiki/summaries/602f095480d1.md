# Anthropic renames its $100 per month Max 5x plan as Max and the $200 per month Max 20x plan as Ultra, and removes the 5-hour limit for verified Ultra accounts

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `anthropic`

## Summary

Anthropic is renaming its Claude subscription tiers: the $100/month Max 5x plan becomes simply Max, and the $200/month Max 20x becomes Ultra. Prices stay put and current subscribers need do nothing. The substantive change comes with the rename: Anthropic is removing the 5-hour usage limit for verified Ultra accounts, starting with most existing subscribers; newer accounts keep the limit for now.

## Highlights

- Claude Max 5x becomes Max ($100/month); Max 20x becomes Ultra ($200/month)
- Prices unchanged; no action needed from subscribers
- The 5-hour usage limit is removed for verified Ultra accounts
- Rollout starts with most existing subscribers; newer accounts keep the limit for now
