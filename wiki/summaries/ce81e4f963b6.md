# Show HN: Hack – my Hacker News mobile client now lets you block AI posts

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-22 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `apple` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

The developer of Hack, an eight-year-old Hacker News client for iOS, iPadOS, macOS and Android, added a toggle to block AI-related posts. In his test, 32 of 90 front-page titles were filtered across three pages.

The update also adds a switch to disable infinite scrolling against doomscrolling. Both settings live at the bottom of the app's settings screen, blocked titles are reviewable via 'See Blocked Posts', and the features are currently Apple-only with Android to follow.

## Highlights

- HN client Hack adds an AI-post blocking toggle
- 32 of 90 front-page titles filtered in the developer's three-page test
- Also new: infinite-scroll kill switch against doomscrolling
- Blocked posts remain inspectable via 'See Blocked Posts'
- Apple platforms first; Android support in progress
