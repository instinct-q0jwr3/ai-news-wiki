# Autonomous strike drone uses Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano to pick and bomb targets

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `nvidia`

## Summary

Swedish startup Scaleout Systems demonstrated an autonomous strike drone that identifies, ranks and attacks targets using small computer-vision models running entirely onboard, no external comms required. At BAE Systems Bofors' Winter Demo 2026 under the ALMA (Affordable Loitering Modular Ammunition) program, a low-cost loitering munition autonomously ranked an armored engineering vehicle highest among detected targets, flew to it and dropped an explosive, with about 200 seconds of recon and a full mission under 320 seconds.

An earlier February 'arctic strike demonstration' at BTC Karlskoga ran YOLOv8 Nano on an Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano aboard an Airolit S1 airframe at -18C, claiming 30 fps at around 20 m/s with ~30 ms latency. Scaleout's FEDAIR project, part of NATO's DIANA accelerator, received 100,000 euros in development funding; its Tactical Computer Vision Network uses federated learning, and a Swedish Air Force ISR test kept inference running at full frame rate on a node whose link was degraded and then cut. No procurement order or combat deployment exists yet, and a UN expert group recently concluded human judgment and control are required under the laws of war.

## Highlights

- BAE Winter Demo 2026: loitering munition autonomously detected, ranked and struck an armored engineering vehicle.
- Fully onboard processing; mission completed without communications, supporting electronic-warfare resilience claims.
- Mission profile: ~200 seconds of recon, full mission under 320 seconds; pilot retained as failsafe.
- February test: YOLOv8 Nano on a hobbyist-available Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano, 30 fps at ~20 m/s, ~30 ms latency.
- FEDAIR is part of NATO's DIANA accelerator with 100,000 euros in development funding.
- Tactical Computer Vision Network combines Scaleout Edge with federated learning for local training and shared updates.
- Swedish Air Force ISR test: offline node kept inference and active learning at full frame rate, then backfilled on reconnect.
- A UN expert group says human judgment and control are required to comply with the laws of war; review in Geneva in November.
