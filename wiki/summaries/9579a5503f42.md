# Business map startup Veridion raises a $20M Series A

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-19 · updated: 2026-09-21 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

The round was led by [Hoxton Ventures](https://hoxtonventures.com/), with participation from existing investors Underline Ventures, OTB Ventures, Gapminder, Day One Capital and Launchub.

Founded in 2019, Veridion has developed what it describes as a live business graph, a digital representation of around 640 million businesses worldwide that is continuously updated to reflect changes in their operations.

The technology is designed to complement existing market intelligence platforms by providing more frequently updated operational information.

## Highlights

- The platform analyses billions of digital signals from sources including company websites, public registries, regulatory filings, online product catalogues, social profiles and trusted news sources.
- Veridion says its technology covers more businesses and delivers market intelligence more quickly than traditional sources, which often rely on company information updated quarterly or annually.
- Information updated quarterly or annually is no longer intelligence.
- It's history. > Our system creates a continuously updated digital replica of the world's businesses, giving organisations the intelligence they need to understand changing conditions, identify new opportunities and make better decisions…
- Veridion currently provides services to more than 100 organisations and employs more than 60 people across Europe and North America.
- North America is already the company's largest market.
- The new funding will support the expansion of Veridion's business intelligence platform and its capabilities as the company grows internationally.
