# Laser-armed AI submarine robots patrol Norwegian salmon farms, killing sea lice

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Tom's Hardware reports that Stingray Marine Solutions has deployed more than 3,500 laser-equipped systems in Norwegian salmon pens, citing the supplier's annual report. The systems use AI to identify sea lice and target them with lasers while fish continue swimming normally. The reported footprint covers more than 112 million fish.

The approach aims to reduce reliance on chemical treatments and the handling stress associated with delousing. It is not a complete substitute for other controls: equipment has an upfront cost, and murky or poorly covered areas can leave parasites outside its reach. Deployment totals and welfare benefits are attributed to the reporting and supplier material.

## Highlights

- AI identifies sea lice for targeted laser treatment
- Reported fleet exceeds 3,500 systems
- Supplier reports coverage of 112 million fish
- Continuous treatment reduces need for handling
- Upfront cost and limited coverage remain constraints
