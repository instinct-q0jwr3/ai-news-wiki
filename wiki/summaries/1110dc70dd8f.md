# Email: OpenAI plans to merge ChatGPT's Chat and Work tabs on desktop, rolling out to select users before a wider release; Codex remains a separate experience

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-02 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `ai-coding-agents`

## Summary

OpenAI is merging ChatGPT's Chat and Work tabs on desktop, per an email to users - an admission that the app's current split personality (ChatGPT vs Codex sections, Chat vs Work tabs inside the former) confuses people about where their project actually lives. The merged experience rolls out to select users for feedback first: bring in files and plugins, go from exploring an idea to having ChatGPT build the website, the presentation or the web task, reviewing as it works. Codex stays a separate experience.

The economically loaded line: ChatGPT and Codex will now draw tokens from the same allowance pool. OpenAI says most users will not notice, but heavy users may hit resets more often - the polite version of the unlimited-chat retirement that this week's MCP-harness confession was mourning. Anthropic did the equivalent merge in September, folding Claude's chat and Cowork into one screen. Both labs are converging on the same shape: one surface where conversation becomes execution.
