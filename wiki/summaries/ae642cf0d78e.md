## Summary

John Gruber links DIELINE's piece on Jessica Hische designing the icon and logotype for Meta's agentic AI, Muse - a gig she announced on Threads in an oddly defensive register (her newsletter subject: 'Don't Be Mad at Me'), pre-answering the 'how could you' from her own industry.

DIELINE's Bill McCool reads it as the design world's ethical landmine era: Hische cited complicated feelings about AI, good past experiences at Meta, staff salaries and a pregnant employee's health plan. McCool's own stance - pro-designer, anti-brand, no ethical consumption under capitalism - is that everyone's portfolio has doozies, including his (Amazon warehouse stock that paid his tuition in 1998), but taking the work means owning the fallout.
