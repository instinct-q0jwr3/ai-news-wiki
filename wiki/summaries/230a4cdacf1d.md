# EA uses AI and archival audio to revive John Motson in EA Sports FC 27

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-25 · updated: 2026-11-27 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

The BBC reports EA combining archival recordings and generated speech to bring John Motson's voice to FC 27 on November 26. EA says it obtained permission from his widow and son and paid for likeness and voice rights; financial terms are undisclosed. New player names are one reason given for generating speech rather than only replaying old audio.

Family approval is distinct from Motson's own explicit lifetime consent to synthetic speech, a concern raised by a voice-cloning campaigner in the report. EA says this is not intended to replace commentators, but that statement is a company position, not a binding industry-wide employment guarantee. The announced availability was still prospective at this pass, and the game's generated output was not tested.

## Highlights

- Release is announced for November 26
- Archival lines and generated speech are combined
- Family permission and payment are reported
- Deal terms are not disclosed
- Posthumous family permission differs from lifetime consent
- Critic raises a personal-consent objection
- No game output or future employment guarantee was verified
