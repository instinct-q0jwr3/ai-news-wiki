# Walmart unveils Agent Gateway, an MCP server for AI agents to shop its site, and Agent Profiles to authorize agents and track their purchases separately

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems` `agentic-commerce`

## Summary

Walmart is leaning into agentic commerce with Agent Gateway, an MCP server that lets AI agents shop its site, plus Agent Profiles for customers' assistants, announced by CEO John Furner at the Masters of Scale Summit. The technology will support agentic shopping across both Walmart and Sam's Club, extending existing integrations with ChatGPT, Gemini, and a partnership with Meta's Muse.

The strategy is a deliberate counter-position to Amazon, which recently began blocking AI agents from purchasing on behalf of shoppers, and to travel and booking sites that have locked agents out entirely. Walmart is betting that agent-mediated shopping becomes mainstream and that being the most agent-accessible major retailer is an advantage rather than a threat - building first-party rails for agents instead of scraping defenses against them.

- Walmart launched Agent Gateway, an MCP server letting AI agents transact on its site

- Announced by CEO John Furner at the Masters of Scale Summit in San Francisco
