# Step 5 Preview, a 1M-context MoE from StepFun, shows up on OpenRouter

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

StepFun's Step 5 Preview, a mixture-of-experts model with a one million token context, appeared on OpenRouter on 8 October. It is priced at $1 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output tokens, and is described as built for working with codebases and documents using tools over multiple steps.

It is a preview, so no independent evaluations were reviewed.

## Highlights

- Step 5 Preview from StepFun on OpenRouter
- 1M-token context, mixture of experts
- $1 input and $2.70 output per million tokens
- Aimed at multistep tool use on codebases and documents
- Preview, no independent benchmarks seen
