# AI is not “just a tool”

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`lobsters`

## Summary

A September 19 essay pushes back on the phrase 'AI is just a tool': framing LLMs that way is meaningless and justifies nothing, the author argues - a pithy line used either to trivialize something much more complicated or to excuse the use of dangerous products made by irresponsible companies.

The author says the rote argument shows up everywhere: tech organizing spaces, justifications of LLM-generated code in the Linux kernel, and journalism. Their counter: 'AI is not just a tool. AI is an industry led by psychos. AI is LLMs.'
