# ChatGPT Space: a dystopia of centralised context

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

Richard Ng argues that OpenAI's new ChatGPT Space, a shared document space where people tag in ChatGPT or Codex, could become a context-centralisation flywheel giving one company dystopian power over how organisations learn and build IP. He sees real product and commercial promise, citing Microsoft 365's roughly $100bn revenue, but his essay is a polemic against any one firm owning everyone's context, followed by a two-minute open-source proof of concept as an alternative. Basis: the first part of a long essay was read.
