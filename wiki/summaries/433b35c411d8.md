# Can AI replace mathematicians? Vienna researchers weigh in

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-09 · updated: 2026-10-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

The University of Vienna's magazine asks whether AI can replace mathematicians after OpenAI released over 700 manuscripts across about 370 problem families. Researchers say AI may change the field but argue mathematics is far more than producing proofs quickly.

The piece recounts OpenAI's 8 September claim of progress on parts of the Navier-Stokes equations, one of the Millennium Prize Problems, as the most notable AI math result so far. One quoted researcher, Nathanael Berestycki, says a tipping point has been reached. The excerpt does not cover all of the Vienna researchers' views.
