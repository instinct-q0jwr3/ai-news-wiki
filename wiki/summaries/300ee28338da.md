## Highlights

- For the latest on the Fireworks AI incident, see their official updates here.
- What is Fireworks AI and how we used them: Fireworks is one of the most used global AI infrastructure platforms powering tens of thousands of customers and used by hundreds of thousands of developers.
- We used Fireworks for classification jobs to help customers understand their own AI usage through a feature in Notion called Insights, and we used them as a general inference provider to serve open-weight models inside our product.
- The Insights feature has been disabled as we investigate this incident, and we are no longer serving open-weight models through Fireworks.
- On November 6, Fireworks notified us that malicious actors may have accessed potential customer data.
- On November 8, Fireworks confirmed this new dataset for us.
- Upon analysis of the new data, we discovered that it included unattributable Notion AI chat transcripts used for the Insights feature.
- No other Notion data from your workspace is affected, and we continue to monitor this incident.
