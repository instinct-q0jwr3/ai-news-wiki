## Summary

Simon Willison updated llm-meta-ai for Meta's Muse Wonder and Wonder Full. He notes they may be Meta's first Fable-class models, with Artificial Analysis scoring Muse Wonder (Max) as equivalent to GPT-6.1 Sol, and compares pelican SVGs across six reasoning levels with Muse 1.3 and 1.2. Meta is shipping about a model a month.
