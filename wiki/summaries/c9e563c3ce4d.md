# Drama 3 just leveled up

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-30 · updated: 2026-10-31 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Fish Audio has released upgraded weights for Drama 3, its voice model, and Artificial Analysis' independent benchmarks put it at the top of the open-weight field. On the Speech Arena leaderboards, Drama 3 takes the #1 open-weight spot on both Provider Voice (where it ranks #6 overall, +38 Elo over the Preview) and Controlled Voice (+22 Elo over Preview), and it is #1 of all models on the Pronunciation Robustness benchmark.

## Highlights

- Fish Audio ships upgraded Drama 3 weights
- #1 open-weight on both Artificial Analysis Speech Arenas
- Provider Voice: #6 overall, +38 Elo over Preview; Controlled Voice: +22 Elo
- #1 of all models on Pronunciation Robustness
