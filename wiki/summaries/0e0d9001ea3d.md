# What Is Reasoning

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Armin Ronacher demystifies 'reasoning' in LLMs: reasoning traces are not exotic internals, just text the model is trained to emit into a scratchpad before its final answer. GPT-OSS's Harmony format makes the mechanics visible - special channel tokens route analysis text into a separate stream, while closed models presumably redact and summarize it.

More surprises: 'reasoning effort' is not a sampler setting but a line baked into the system prompt (which is why changing it invalidates the KV cache); some models can be tricked into leaking reasoning into the 'final' channel or even into tool calls; and disabling 'thinking' can be as mechanical as prefilling a closing token. Punchline: GPT 5.6 terra's safety filters refused to spell-check the post itself, so he switched to Kimi.

## Highlights

- Reasoning traces are just text routed to a scratchpad channel, not exotic machinery
- GPT-OSS Harmony shows the plumbing: channel tokens split analysis from final
- 'Reasoning effort' is a system-prompt line, not a sampling property
- Models can be tricked into leaking traces into final answers or tool calls
