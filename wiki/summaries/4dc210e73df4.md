## Summary

CLiF is presented as continual learning from full-stack trajectories: agents learn unwritten team practice, such as issuing an account credit rather than a refund for a duplicate charge on an annual plan, from users' interactions instead of docs or prompts. Basis: the opening of the post.

## Highlights

- Agents learn tacit rules from interaction traces.
- Example: credit instead of refund on annual plans.
