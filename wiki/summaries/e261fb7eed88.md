# An interview with Atlassian CEO Mike Cannon-Brookes on the SaaSpocalypse, enterprise AI, Atlassian's reorg, why chatbots aren't the future of work, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-29 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

The Verge's Decoder interviews Atlassian cofounder and CEO Mike Cannon-Brookes on the 'SaaSpocalypse' thesis and why he thinks chatbots are not the future of work. The bear case put to him: AI agents could read across all a company's tools directly, making migration to Atlassian's platform less enticing, or AI could simply build Jira/Trello-style tools on demand and destroy the category. Cannon-Brookes pushes back at length, arguing for the value of design and human users and claiming AI is increasing usage of Atlassian's tools. The conversation also covers Atlassian's new org chart - all products as expressions of a single core platform - and this year's layoffs, which he frames as needing a different 'mix of skills' as AI changes what work means.
