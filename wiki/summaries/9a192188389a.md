## Summary

The Verge reports on an Apple engineering listing for agents that can work across applications, websites and services. The article says the listing has since been removed and separates that hiring signal from existing and proposed Siri capabilities.

A removed job listing is evidence of stated development intent as reported, not a product launch, guaranteed roadmap or demonstrated autonomy. Account-specific access and future releases were not verified.

- Listing describes cross-tool work

- No release date or tested capability follows
