## Highlights

- Argues current AI products do not take their own problem-solving premises seriously
- The universal 'AI can make mistakes' fine print is legalese that shifts verification burden onto users
- No chatbot explains how a user is supposed to know which info is 'important' to check
- Proposes concrete features a serious research/coding AI tool would need, starting with provenance
- Says the critique applies to Ollama and local models as much as to frontier labs
