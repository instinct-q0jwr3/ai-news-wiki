# How Primary Compute Markets Work

## Summary

Ornn's first article in a compute-markets series explains the primary market, where providers sell capacity directly to customers. It uses a thought experiment: you get a quote for 1,024 NVIDIA B300s but need a loan, and the lender wants proof you can cover operating costs and repay. Three ways to sell: reservations (customers commit for an agreed period, which can help secure financing), on-demand and spot. The piece shows how the sales structure drives how a cluster gets financed.

## Highlights

- Primary market: providers sell compute directly to users
- Three structures: reserved, on-demand, spot
- Long reservations can secure financing for GPU purchases
- First of a series on how compute is bought, sold and financed
