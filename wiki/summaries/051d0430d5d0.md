## Summary

An informal experiment asks each model fifty times to imagine an animal persona through minimal OpenRouter API calls, then uses another model to label the answers. The author reports recurring motifs across model families and describes separate image-generation and SVG loops for a multimodal subset.

The results characterize sampled outputs under a particular framing, not stable identities, consciousness or evidence of a specific training corpus. The author acknowledges strong context and harness sensitivity and labels explanations about Google training data as speculation. The reported pictures and frequencies were not independently reproduced or visually scored here; labeling error, API routing and limited samples constrain interpretation. No API, image generation or persona prompt was executed.
