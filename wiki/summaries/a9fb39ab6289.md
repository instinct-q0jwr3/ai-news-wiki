## Summary

Simon Willison's llm-gemini 0.36 adds Gemini 4 Carbon. He notes it costs $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens at launch, and demos from Google's Antigravity: with /swarm, Carbon built an open-world 3D game set on Google's Mountain View campus with a pelican on a GBike, coordinating subagents to model assets in Blender and build it in Three.js with procedural audio.

## Highlights

- llm-gemini 0.36 supports Gemini 4 Carbon
- Priced $2/$10 per million tokens
- Antigravity swarm demo: subagents build a 3D campus game
