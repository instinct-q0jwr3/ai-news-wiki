# Nvidia launches Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a 120B-parameter open model for enterprise agents, and says it outperforms frontier models on specialized benchmarks

## Summary

Nvidia launched Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a 120B-parameter open model for enterprise agents, part of a three-model family combining language and vision for tasks like reading tables and charts in documents.

## Highlights

- 120B open vision-language model for enterprise agents.
- Part of a three-model Nemotron release.
