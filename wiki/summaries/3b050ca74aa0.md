# Reddit launches Reddit Reviews, a built-in hub using AI to turn subreddit comments into product pages with pros, cons, and sentiment, starting with 10K items

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-10 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Reddit launched Reddit Reviews, a hub that uses AI to turn subreddit comments into product pages, starting with 10,000 items, as a crowdsourced alternative to Wirecutter-style reviews.

## Highlights

- AI-summarized product pages from Reddit threads
- 10,000 products at launch
