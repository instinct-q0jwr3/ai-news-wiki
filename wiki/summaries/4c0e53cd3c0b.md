# An interview with Intel's leadership, including CTO Pushkar Ranade, on AI chip demand, lessons from 10nm, whether Intel can catch up to TSMC, Terafab, and more

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-23 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Dwarkesh Patel spent two days touring Intel's most advanced fab and interviewing its technical leadership, including CTO Pushkar Ranade, about the chips of the future - gate-all-around transistors, backside power, CFET, hybrid bonding - with animated explainers built for the occasion. The headline tension: AI compute demand has grown 3x per year for a decade, but Intel's CTO says the company could only double leading-edge wafer production with today's global supply chain.

The interview digs into what AI's demand shock means for a foundry whose roadmap is measured in node years: advanced packaging and new transistor geometries raise per-wafer value but not wafer counts, and the supply chain's capacity to expand leading-edge output is the binding constraint. Intel's leadership frames both the technology path and the industrial-policy stakes of a demand curve that fabs physically cannot chase.
