# The AI-collapse pre-mortem

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`bert-hubert`

## Summary

Bert Hubert writes the AI collapse's pre-mortem with a careful disclaimer: he's not claiming AI is good or ethical - he dislikes what people do with LLMs, the energy burn, and the copyright issues. His tl;dr: even if the bubble bursts, LLMs can do amazing things (while not being overly useful in a business sense and often being very wrong), and the rest of AI has already earned a Nobel prize and does astounding work in translation, transcription and image analysis.

So a bubble collapse would change nothing about the technology's impressiveness: AI is not going away, and dismissing it because everyone overhyped it - while ruining the economy around it - would be foolish.

## Highlights

- Hubert: even if the AI bubble bursts, the technology's achievements stand
- LLMs: amazing capabilities, but not generally business-useful yet and often wrong
- Non-LLM AI already has a Nobel prize and excels at translation, transcription, image analysis
- He disclaims defending AI's ethics, energy use or copyright practices
- 'AI is not going away, and we'd be fools to disregard it'
