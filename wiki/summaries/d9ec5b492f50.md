# New AI research suggests AI bubble bursting and to expect major market collapse

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-27 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

London Business School describes research using language models to score almost eight hundred thousand Economist articles for speculative narratives and related signals, constructing a monthly financial-bubble index. The researchers argue that unusual changes relative to the publication's own history matter more than its persistently alarmed baseline.

The reported probability of up to 97 percent concerns classification of an AI equity bubble in September 2026, not a 97 percent probability of an imminent crash or a dated trading signal. The article itself notes frequent false alarms and makes usefulness depend on assigning greater cost to a missed crisis. The underlying paper, labels, temporal validation and out-of-sample calibration were not independently audited. Speculation and useful technology can coexist; this does not establish that AI is economically worthless.

## Highlights

- Index analyzes financial journalism rather than company fundamentals directly
- Historical false alarms are central to the account
- Up to 97 percent is a bubble-state estimate
- It is not a crash probability or timing guarantee
- Policy value depends on asymmetric error costs
- Underlying methodology was not independently reproduced
- No investment or portfolio action was taken
