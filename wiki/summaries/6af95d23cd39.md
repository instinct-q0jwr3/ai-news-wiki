## Summary

Aircad is a browser-based professional 2D drafting and 3D solid modeling tool with multiplayer editing and an AI agent that drafts, models and renders alongside you, driven from a command line with familiar shortcuts or plain words. One shared file is the source of truth, with unlimited version history and attribution per edit.

## Highlights

- 2D drafting and 3D modeling in the browser
- AI agent edits and renders in the drawing
- Multiplayer: one shared file, no stale copies
- Full version history with per-edit attribution
