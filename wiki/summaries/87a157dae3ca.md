# Asian tech stocks plunge amid fears US Democrats may delay data centers; Taiwan's Taiex sees a record intraday point drop, Kospi falls 8.7%, and Nikkei 6.7%

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-06 · updated: 2026-11-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Nikkei Asia reports AI-linked Asian markets plunged Friday after the Wall Street tech selloff, as investors feared Democrats could delay US data-center construction after their midterm sweep. Taiwan's Taiex had a record intraday point drop, Kospi fell as much as 8.7% (Korea Exchange imposed a circuit breaker) and the Nikkei fell 6.7%.

## Highlights

- Kospi down as much as 8.7%; circuit breaker triggered
- Nikkei down 6.7%; Taiex record intraday point drop
- Cause: fear of data-center delays after the Democratic win
