# Nvidia reportedly halts GeForce RTX 5090 production

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-16 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `nvidia`

## Summary

A leak says Nvidia has stopped supplying GB202 chips to board partners for consumer GeForce RTX 5090 cards, including the 'D' versions for China. It comes from a notice attributed to an unknown factory and a leaker with a record on the card market, and a second leaker backed it up.

Nvidia has not confirmed it, so treat it as a report. Commenters mostly grumbled about price and availability of top cards.

## Highlights

- Reported halt of RTX 5090 GB202 supply to board partners.
- Based on a leaked factory notice, not an Nvidia statement.
