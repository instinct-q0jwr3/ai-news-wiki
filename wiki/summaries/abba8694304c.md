# Why AI Compute Comes in Rectangles — Erik Bernhardsson, Modal CEO

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`latent.space` `agentic-systems` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Latent Space interviews Modal CEO Erik Bernhardsson on the occasion of a monster Series D: after a $5B Series C, Modal reports crossing $1 billion in annualized run rate, raising $750 million at a $16B valuation, and spinning up nearly three billion sandboxes. The conversation covers the infrastructure and economics behind that growth - and Bernhardsson's title claim that demand for compute rarely looks like the contracts used to buy it, hence rectangles: capacity is sold in fixed shapes while agent workloads arrive in bursts.

The discussion spans elastic GPUs, the explosion of sandboxed agent environments, and the economics of the emerging agent cloud - a continuation of the serverless-AI vision Bernhardsson laid out two years earlier, where developers run code without provisioning clusters, predicting load, or paying for idle resources. The interview doubles as the launch of new cohost Basil Chatha from the forward-deployed side of AI engineering.
