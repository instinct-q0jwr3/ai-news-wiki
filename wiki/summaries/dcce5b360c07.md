# They really do think AI might kill everyone

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-26 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`seangoedecke`

## Summary

The people building AI earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade.

Help peer One of the most influential 20th century pieces of writing about AI is Isaac Asimov’s The Last Question .

Many people found it hard to believe that AI researchers think this way.

## Highlights

- Although there are many humans in the story, the protagonist is the computer Multivac, who evolves over the course of ten trillion years from a single datacenter to a universe-spanning mind in hyperspace.
- Multivac (now called “AC”) ends the story like this: Continue reading...
- A recent resignation tweet from an Anthropic researcher has everyone talking about the AI apocalypse again.
- Among other things, he said: The people building AI earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade.
- In fact, not only do many AI researchers 1 seriously believe this, they’ve been thinking and writing about it since the mid-2000s.
- It’s been such a common idea that the AI research community has abbreviated “how likely you think AI is to kill everyone” to “p(doom)” (i.e. the probability 2 of doomsday) since around 2010.
- They truly do believe that there is a reasonable chance that superintelligent AI will kill everyone.
- It could kill everyone in passing, e.g. like we might pave over an anthill to build a road.
