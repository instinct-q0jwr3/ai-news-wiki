# Will AI kill us all

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A Virev blog post asks whether AI will kill us all and answers: probably not, but nobody can rule it out, and the people building AI worry more than they did. It stresses that every number is someone's judgment and depends on who is asked.

It cites a December 2024 survey of 1,580 AI researchers with a median 10% chance of extinction-level outcomes, up from 5%; mid-2026 superforecasters at 2.4% and experts at 5% for a catastrophe killing over 10% of people by 2100; and 50% of US adults telling YouGov in September 2026 they are concerned. It adds that Anthropic and OpenAI have both held back work over safety but keep releasing stronger models, and that expected arrival of human-level AI moved from 2061 to 2042.

## Highlights

- Risk cannot be measured; every figure is a judgment
- Researchers median 10% for extinction-level outcome (2024 survey)
- Superforecasters 2.4%, experts 5% for 10%+ deaths by 2100
- 50% of US adults concerned, per YouGov, September 2026
- Labs both pause work and keep releasing stronger models
- Expected human-level AI date moved from 2061 to 2042
