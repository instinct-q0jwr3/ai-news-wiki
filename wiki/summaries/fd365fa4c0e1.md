## Summary

The current balance of power in open models.

I was recently invited to brief a group of Congressional members and staff on the state of open-weight models in the lens of U.S.-China competition.

I’m sharing my prepared remarks as a state of the union on open models that is accessible to a broader audience.

## Highlights

- Open language models are AI models where their weights are publicly available for inspection or downstream use.
- These are most often contrasted to so-called “closed” AI models.
- Open language models primarily are bucketed into two categories, open-weight and open-source models.
- Open-weight models are the most common form, such as popular models like Meta’s Llama, Alibaba’s Qwen, Google’s Gemma, or DeepSeek’s models.
- Since about April 2025, Chinese AI companies have been the clear leader in open-weight models.
- The other prominent open-source models are also built by American non-profit organizations, including OpenAthena’s Marin models and EleutherAI’s Pythia models.
- Chinese open-weight models surpassed American open-weight models in these two key areas about 18 months ago.
- The top American open models on the Artificial Analysis Index are behind 15 other Chinese made models.
