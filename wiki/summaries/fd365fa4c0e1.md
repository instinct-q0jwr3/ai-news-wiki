# The current balance of power in open models

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Prepared remarks from a Congressional briefing lay out a state of the union on open models through the lens of US-China competition. The author distinguishes open-weight models (public weights governed by licenses - Llama, Qwen, Gemma, DeepSeek) from true open-source models, which also ship training code and data. Since about April 2025, Chinese companies have been the clear leaders in open-weight models, while the most prominent fully open-source models have been built in the United States.

## Highlights

- Written as a briefing for Congressional members and staff.
- Open-weight (Llama, Qwen, Gemma, DeepSeek) vs. open-source (weights plus training code and data).
- Chinese AI companies have led open-weight models since about April 2025.
- The most prominent open-source models are US-built.
