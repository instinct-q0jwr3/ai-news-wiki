# Amazon says it blocked Meta's Muse AI assistant from accessing Amazon.com to shop on users' behalf, citing ToS violations, security risks, and merchant consent (Todd Bishop/GeekWire)

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-21 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `agentic-systems` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Amazon says it has cut off Meta's new Muse personal AI agent from shopping on Amazon.com on behalf of customers, after failing to get Meta to voluntarily exclude the site. Amazon's account: Meta never told it Muse would access the store, the agent does not identify itself when it browses, and it appears to capture and store customer credentials, which Amazon calls a privacy and security risk. Since Sunday night, Muse users hitting Amazon see a popup: 'Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use.'

Meta counters that Muse 'has no visibility into people's passwords or payment methods', with credentials going into secure storage the agent can use without seeing. The standoff is notable because the two are partners: Amazon products have been purchasable inside Facebook and Instagram since 2023, and Meta signed a multibillion-dollar deal in April to run agentic AI workloads on Amazon's cloud. It fits Amazon's year-long campaign to keep outside agents off its site, suing Perplexity over Comet and blocking shopping agents from Google and OpenAI, and it lands on a business with more than $68 billion in ad revenue at stake. Amazon says it is in direct conversation with Meta and declined to comment on legal action.
