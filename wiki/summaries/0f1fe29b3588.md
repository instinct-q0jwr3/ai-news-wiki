# The Case for Boycotting Generative AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-03 · confidence: high_

`marcus-on-ai`

## Summary

Gary Marcus makes the case for a public boycott of generative AI. His premise: the frontier labs are knowingly - by their own admission - doing something irreversible and incredibly dangerous without public consent, are already causing harm, and have no serious plan to mitigate the risks; the government, too close to the industry, doesn't either.

His exhibits are the labs' own words: Anthropic's Evan Hubinger publicly estimating a greater-than-10% chance AI kills all humans within the decade while admitting alignment is unsolved, and OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki writing with similar candor days earlier. Even allowing for marketing exaggeration, Marcus argues, an industry that says this about its own product should not get the public's business.

## Highlights

- Marcus calls for a public boycott of generative AI
- Premise: labs admit the danger, lack a mitigation plan, and government won't supply one
