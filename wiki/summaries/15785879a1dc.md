## Summary

Stefan Torges outlines routes by which aligned superintelligence could enable extreme concentration of human power, emphasizing state leaders and coalitions rather than assuming only a company takeover. The essay assigns an overall probability around 23% to fewer than twenty-five people controlling civilization's future.

The number is explicitly conditional on superintelligence arriving this century and alignment being solved in time, and combines subjective judgments with a simple model. It is not an observed frequency, unconditional forecast or evidence of a current conspiracy. Routes, institutional obstacles and broad uncertainty matter more than a falsely precise reading of the total. No scenario is treated as a recommended course of action.
