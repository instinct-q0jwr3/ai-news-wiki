# Job listings show Amazon is building a "new-to-world" home robot to operate "alongside family members", after its $1,450 Astro reached only a few thousand homes

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-09 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `amazon`

## Summary

CNBC reports, from an analysis of job listings, that Amazon is building a 'new-to-world' home robot meant to operate 'alongside family members' in dynamic home environments. It follows Astro, the $1,450 Alexa-equipped rolling tablet revealed in 2021, which never left invite-only status and reached only a few thousand customers; a business version was sunset within a year of its 2023 launch.

The push traces to Jeff Bezos's 2017 bet that consumers would have a robot at home. Amazon has assembled a new team over several months, and a listing said 'Robots are about to step out of the movies and into our homes'; it has since been removed. CNBC links it to CEO Andy Jassy's shareholder letter calling AI and robotics among the biggest inflections of our lifetime.

## Highlights

- Job posts point to a new Amazon home robot team.
- Astro cost $1,450 and shipped to only a few thousand customers.
- Business Astro was shut down within a year of 2023 launch.
- Listing described operation 'alongside family members'.
- Source is CNBC's analysis of job posts, not an announcement.
