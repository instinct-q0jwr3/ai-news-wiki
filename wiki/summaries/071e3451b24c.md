- Argues headlines blaming 'AI agents' for hacks misassign accountability and fuel fear

- Circular-saw analogy: user instructions plus engineered mitigations like blade covers and emergency stops
- Cites the EU AI Act's AI-literacy mandate as the same principle in law
- Concedes sandbox escapes and web hacking are 'very concerning'
- Warns that fluent LLM text generation should not be mistaken for responsible agency
