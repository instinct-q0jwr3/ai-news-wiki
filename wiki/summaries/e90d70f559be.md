## Summary

NAND maker Kioxia reported fiscal Q2 operating profit up 22x year over year to about $12.7B, in line with estimates, on the back of ballooning AI data-storage demand - but forecast Q3 operating profit of roughly the same ~$12.7B, below the ~$14.9B analysts expected, warning that the AI-fueled surge in flash prices is not a straight line, per Bloomberg via Techmeme.
