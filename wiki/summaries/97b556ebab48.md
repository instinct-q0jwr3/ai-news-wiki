# Cloudflare debuts open-weight multimodal decision models Clef and Clef-flash, claiming they are smarter and faster than Jev, based on Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.5-9B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-02 · updated: 2026-10-07 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `alibaba` `hugging-face` `typesafe` `qwen`

## Summary

Cloudflare released two open-weight decision models, Clef and Clef-flash, two weeks after TypeSafe's Jev took off. Like Jev they answer bounded questions: yes/no, multiple choice and rankings. They are built on frozen, post-trained Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.5-9B backbones, run a prefill-only pass and score options in parallel.

Cloudflare claims they beat Jev on speed and several benchmarks and handle images and video, at a higher price. Weights are on Hugging Face for local use. These are Cloudflare's own claims.

## Highlights

- Clef and Clef-flash: open-weight decision models, multimodal.
- Backbones: Qwen3.8-27B and Qwen3.5-9B, frozen and post-trained.
- Answer yes/no, multiple choice and ranking questions.
- Claimed faster and smarter than Jev; costs more.
- Benchmarks are from Cloudflare.
