## Summary

Latent.Space's AINews walks its engineer audience through one of the most unusual S-1s ever filed - 350 pages - and surfaces the numbers that matter: Anthropic growing at a 1400% rate, 48% of revenue flowing through cloud channels, and, adjusted for $1 billion a quarter of stock-based compensation, the first profitable quarter in company history thanks to significant operating leverage.

The piece highlights the efficiency story told in two loops and the forward pitch: 'virtual collaborators' - explicitly not 'agents' - for over a billion knowledge workers by 2028. Anthropic's own S-1 language argues AI systems have reached an inflection point where they function as genuine collaborators in professional work, completing substantive tasks and surfacing original analyses.
