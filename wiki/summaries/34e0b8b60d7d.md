# AI inference startup Fal raised $1.3B led by Bessemer at a $16B valuation, up from $8B in April; a source says it hit $800M in annualized revenue this year

## Summary

AI inference startup Fal raised $1.3 billion led by Bessemer at a $16 billion valuation, up from $8 billion in April, per Bloomberg; a source says it hit $800 million in annualized revenue this year. Fal hosts image, video and audio models for creators and developers, doubling its valuation in about six months.
