# OpenAI: 'fully autonomous RSI is not happening today'

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

OpenAI lays out its position on the next phase of AI development: as automated AI research takes over more of the work of building successive model generations, recursive self-improvement (RSI) could accelerate progress rapidly even while people remain involved. The company frames its mission around three goals - building an automated AI researcher while iterating on the alignment problem, delivering the economic benefits of very intelligent machines, and empowering individuals with a personal AGI - and argues alignment research must keep pace with capabilities.

The post stresses that capable, aligned AI is itself part of the safety answer, strengthening defenses and advancing alignment research, and calls for shared standards to guide development across labs and countries. Notably, OpenAI states that fully autonomous RSI is 'not happening today', positioning gradual, supervised automation of research as the manageable path rather than an abrupt intelligence explosion.

## Highlights

- OpenAI: fully autonomous recursive self-improvement (RSI) 'is not happening today'
- Three stated goals: build an automated AI researcher and iterate on alignment; deliver scientific and economic benefits; empower everyone with a personal AGI
- Warns that as AI drives more of its own R&D, the pace of progress could accelerate rapidly even with humans in the loop
- Argues alignment research must keep pace with capabilities as systems grow more autonomous
- Calls for shared standards to guide development across labs and countries
- Frames capable, aligned AI as part of the defense against the risks of the transition itself
