## Summary

An Austin Patrick Substack essay recounts a person’s claimed police contact after threatening chatbot messages and uses it to discuss reporting, context and privacy. In the examined passages, the writer explicitly says he cannot attest that the reported events are true because he did not experience them directly.

That caveat is central: the story is not an independently verified account of a particular company’s reporting system, police procedure or legal duty. The author’s broader proposal about disclosure to law enforcement is a policy position, not established law or permission to disclose anyone’s information. This summary covers the examined narrative and opening argument, not all later claims.

## Highlights
