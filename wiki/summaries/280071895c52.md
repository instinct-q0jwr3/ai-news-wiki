## Summary

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg said on the company's Q3 2026 earnings call that Muse, Meta's AI agent app, has attracted more than 10 million users since launch, and that over a quarter of them are making purchases through the app with an average purchase size above $200. The figures are an early signal that Meta's push into agentic commerce is converting engagement into transactions at meaningful ticket sizes.
