# I built a native Apple app for 4 platforms in one afternoon with my AI workflow

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-28 · updated: 2026-09-30 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `apple`

## Summary

A developer with ADHD built ClearTime, a visual timer, as a native app across macOS, iOS, watchOS, and tvOS in about five hours from first commit to TestFlight builds, using an AI workflow. The motivation is personal: a clock tells the time but does not help him feel how much is left. The post also explains why this matters more now: at 48 he developed SVT, spent a week in the ICU with a heart rate pinned at 220 bpm, and underwent an ablation; recovery and a healthier, lower-stress life became the backdrop for building tools that reduce friction. The writeup, he jokes, took longer than the build.

## Highlights

- Native visual timer shipped to Mac, iPhone, Apple Watch, and Apple TV in ~5 hours via an AI workflow
- Built for ADHD: clocks show the time but do not convey how much is left
- The author developed SVT at 48, spent a week in ICU at 220 bpm, and had an ablation
- Physical visual timers inspired the idea; he wanted one that follows him across screens
- TestFlight builds worked on all four platforms the same afternoon
- Jokes the writeup took more time than the design and build
