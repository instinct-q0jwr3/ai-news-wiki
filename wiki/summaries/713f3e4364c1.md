## Summary

Simon Willison added Gemini 4 Argon support to llm-gemini and tested it with his pelican SVG at low, medium and high settings, all excellent; the high one cost 17.166 cents. Argon launches at $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens, the same as GPT-6 Sol, half Claude Opus 5.5 and a fifth of Claude Fable 5.1. It is available via the Gemini API and the $100 per month plan.
