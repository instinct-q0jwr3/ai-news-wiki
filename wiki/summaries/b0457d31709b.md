# Fake OpenAI, SpaceX events used to steal Microsoft, Google accounts

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-12 · updated: 2026-11-13 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `google` `microsoft`

## Summary

BleepingComputer reports a phishing campaign using fake invitations to events branded as OpenAI, SpaceX and Anthropic. Citing Push Security, it describes attackers combining device-code deception and sign-in interception to capture Microsoft and Google account access. The lure is an apparently valuable event or free service, not a legitimate request from the named companies.

The report illustrates how a convincing registration page can turn a routine sign-in into account compromise. Branding and a familiar login flow are not proof of authenticity. This is reporting on an external campaign, not evidence that the user received one of these invitations.

## Highlights

- Fake event invitations borrow AI-company branding
- Report cites credential and session theft
- Different account providers face different attack paths
- Registration pages are the lure
- No user-specific exposure is established
