# Every Mac comes with an (undocumented) family member classifier

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-07 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A write-up on 8rank.ai says every Mac since macOS 13 (2022) runs a pair of background daemons that build an on-device knowledge graph about its owner: who they know, how those people relate to each other, how they communicate, where they go and when. The graph is assembled by cross-referencing Contacts, Calendar, Photos, Messages, Mail, location, Screen Time and other behavioral signals.

The author's twist is that this graph is held as a queryable store that LLMs may retrieve from, yet documentation is thin: the closest thing is the man page for knowledgeconstructiond. The post explains how the system works and offers a tool to inspect your own graph. The claim about LLM retrieval is hedged in the text itself ('allegedly').

## Highlights

- Daemons shipped since macOS 13 build a personal knowledge graph on device.
- Inputs include Contacts, Calendar, Photos, Messages, Mail, location and Screen Time.
- The graph is described as queryable by LLMs, though the post hedges that point.
- Official documentation is limited to one man page.
- The post ships a tool to peek into your own graph.
