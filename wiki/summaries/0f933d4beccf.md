# China's biggest memory maker says it has caught up with Samsung and Micron

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-23 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), China's largest DRAM maker, announced its fifth-generation technology platform has entered mass production, with vice president Luo Xiaodong claiming its process capability is now on par with the most advanced mass-produced nodes in the industry and that the memory shortage cannot last until 2030.

The platform specs an 11.95nm active area half-pitch and a 45:1 storage capacitor aspect ratio using four-exposure patterning, yielding at least 50% more dies per wafer than the fourth generation on an 8Gb baseline. Two 24Gb LPDDR5X products already run on it - in 496-ball and 245-ball packages for mid-range and high-end handsets - and Reuters reports CXMT is pitching the platform to electronics makers as an additional source of supply, aiming squarely at buyers currently choosing between Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron.
