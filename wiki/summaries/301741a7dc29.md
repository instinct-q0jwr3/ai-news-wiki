# MiniMax launches M3.1 Flash, saying it delivers "a generational leap" in agentic tasks, coding, computer use, and cybersecurity, with weights due October 23

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-19 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems` `ai-safety-incidents` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

MiniMax officially rolls out M3.1 Flash and confirms that Space Bunny - the anonymous model that processed more than 200 trillion tokens on public platforms - was an early version of it. The company claims a generational leap in agentic tasks, coding, computer use and cybersecurity, with online inference nearly 3x faster and usage costs cut by 70% versus its predecessor. MiniMax says M3.1 Flash reaches the level of leading models on complex agentic 'cowork' tasks, computer use, software development and cybersecurity benchmarks, and open weights are due October 23.
