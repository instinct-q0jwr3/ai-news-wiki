# Developer uses AI to build free Adobe clones after frustration with cancellation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-23 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Brandon Thomas, a 15-year software veteran with a decade of Rust experience, used Claude Opus 5.5 to build Artcraft - free, open-source Rust clones of seven Adobe applications: Photocraft (Photoshop), Vectorcraft (Illustrator), Filmcraft (Premiere), Lightcraft (Lightroom), Effectcraft (After Effects), Designcraft (InDesign) and Printcraft (Acrobat Pro). The trigger was frustration with Adobe, a company once sued by the US government over its cancellation fees; Thomas declared 'software is over'.

He claims 100% parity within a month - tempered on Hacker News to 99% parity in months rather than years - and insists the project has team support and won't be abandoned. Early users report the apps look like Adobe's but struggle with basic functionality, so for now they are not replacements for the established tools.
