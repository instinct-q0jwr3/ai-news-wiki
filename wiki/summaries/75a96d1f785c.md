# Shares of US tech giants jump after Democrats' midterm sweep as investors bet that a Democrat-controlled Congress will slow AI capex that's draining cash flow

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-04 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Shares of US tech giants led the market higher Wednesday after the Democratic midterm sweep, as investors bet a Democrat-controlled Congress will slow AI capex that is draining cash flow, per Bloomberg. It contrasts with the AI-infrastructure names that fell on data-center risk.

## Highlights

- Big Tech shares rose after the sweep on bets AI capex will slow
- Investors treat slower spending as good for cash flow, per Bloomberg
