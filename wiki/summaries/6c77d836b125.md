# Compiling web applications into verified operations for AI agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-24 · updated: 2026-11-28 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Protocolis describes a compiler that turns webpage controls into typed operations, executes a selected operation and rereads the page to report changes. Its author copy reports 3,443 counted runs and, for a matched long-task subset, 80 completions out of 105 compared with 46% for the best alternative in each comparison.

The accessible page is an abstract and headline results, with the arXiv version described as forthcoming. These are author-reported comparisons, not independently reproduced guarantees. Verified operations in this framing do not prove that every task is correct, authorized or secure, and the reported 22% disagreement over claimed long-task completion itself limits the language of certainty.

## Highlights

- Compiler derives operations from page controls
- Execution includes a post-action read
- Abstract reports 3,443 counted runs
- Long-task subset is 80 of 105 completions
- Judge disagreement is not eliminated
- ArXiv publication is described as forthcoming
- No general task-correctness or authorization guarantee follows
