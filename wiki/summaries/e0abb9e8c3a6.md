Anthropic announced the donation of the Agent Skills open standard to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation, saying 70+ platforms have adopted it - the formal announcement of the move Simon Willison reported yesterday. Anthropic's own page frames it alongside its public-benefit mission and the Haiku 5.5 launch.
