# a16z invests $35M in Horowitz Andreessen Academy, an independent, unaccredited two-year alternative to college; Anthropic, Meta, and others will provide compute

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-22 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Andreessen Horowitz is investing $35 million in the Horowitz Andreessen Academy, an independent, unaccredited two-year alternative to college, the Wall Street Journal reports. Anthropic, Meta and other Bay Area tech companies are on board and will provide compute for the program.

## Highlights

- a16z is spending $35M to back the two-year Horowitz Andreessen Academy.
- The academy is an independent, unaccredited alternative to college.
- Anthropic, Meta and other tech giants will provide compute.
