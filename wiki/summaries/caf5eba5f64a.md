## Summary

Tech Policy Press reports a protest outside Nasdaq during Anthropic’s stock-market debut. Interviewed demonstrators raise concerns about financial exposure, environmental costs, public services, surveillance and control over AI development.

The examined report records activists’ views and observed demonstration activity. Predictions of a bubble collapse or broad claims about healthcare harms are attributed arguments, not findings established by the gathering. This summary covers the examined report rather than an independent assessment of every allegation.

## Highlights

- Reporting describes a specific demonstration
- Participants raise several different concerns
- Financial-loss predictions are opinions
- Policy demands differ from enacted rules
- Protest does not validate every underlying claim
