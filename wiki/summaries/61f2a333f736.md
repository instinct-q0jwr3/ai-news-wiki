## Summary

OpenAI unveiled a $500/month Pro plan at DevDay - its highest usage allowance, bundled with the new Ultrafast speed tier that pushes Codex and Work apps to up to 300 tokens per second (also available to API users). The $200 plan OpenAI pioneered at the end of 2024 (later matched by Anthropic and Perplexity) is simultaneously made less appealing: $200 Pro subscribers will see reduced something in Codex and Work (the cached text truncates), a classic upsell ladder. A new version of the Sol model was announced at the same event.
