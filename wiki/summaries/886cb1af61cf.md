# California startups have raised a record $385B across seed to growth rounds so far in 2026, or 75%+ of all US startup funding; early and seed stage raised ~$62B

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-26 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

California startups have raised $385 billion across seed- through growth-stage rounds so far in 2026 - more than all US startups raised in any prior full year, with two months still left, and more than double last year's tally. Per Crunchbase data, the state captured over three-quarters of all US startup funding this year, an apparent record share, and its slice has been trending up for three years.

Most of the concentration traces to a handful of megarounds for San Francisco AI pioneers, with OpenAI and Anthropic's financings doing the heaviest lifting, though the broader Silicon Valley and Southern California ecosystems are also showing strength. The numbers describe an AI boom that is geographically narrow even as it is historically large.
