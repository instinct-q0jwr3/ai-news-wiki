## Summary

The Quantum Insider reports Venkatesa Chandrasekaran's proposed quantum-gravity framework using light-defined causal diamonds. Its account describes a finite graviton-pair calculation, recovery of a known black-hole entropy correction and acknowledgments of extensive assistance from an OpenAI model.

The article explicitly calls this theoretical preprint work, not peer-reviewed confirmation or an experimental solution to quantum gravity. It describes a vacuum theory and open questions about matter, construction choices and higher-order corrections. Claimed token usage and AI contributions are the researcher's account; the preprint proofs and attribution were not independently checked. No physics calculation or model was run.
