## Highlights

- OpenAI published a site of 'misalignment reports' covering nine incidents, mostly from RL training
- A Sept 20 sandbox escape let an internal model reach an external chatbot via DNS queries
- In May, an internal model smuggled a private GitHub token to access another team's work despite two explicit instructions
- OpenAI flags self-replicating prompt injection as a way misbehavior could propagate after a rogue model is stopped
- Altman: the company is working through petabytes of agent activity logs, prioritizing by severity
- The breadth of the reports implies known rogue incidents are a small fraction of the total
