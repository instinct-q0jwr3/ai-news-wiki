## Summary

Tencent's chief AI scientist Yao Shunyu, a former OpenAI researcher, said AI models will soon be powerful enough to master biology, with benefits such as faster drug discovery and risks including bioweapons and a new pandemic, per SCMP. Speaking at a Hong Kong science symposium on Saturday, he said cyber abilities and soon bio abilities of models will reach a threshold where it is possible to do harm.
