# AI in UX: Work Is Faster, but Practitioners Have Mixed Feelings

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-24 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

A UX-industry survey piece collecting practitioners' conflicted experiences with AI at work. The quotes span the spectrum: newcomers who chose companies where 'AI is embedded everywhere,' specialists who find AI-generated design quality 'very poor / immature,' and researchers who call the tools untrustworthy. The dominant tension is top-down: 'the biggest change is the constant push from management to use it more and more,' with practitioners actively pushing back where AI falls short.

The emotional register is the story: one practitioner describes 'a strange feeling to be forced to use something that I do not believe in,' another hates using a tool at work that they ban their own children from using. Work is faster, the piece grants, but the profession's relationship with the tooling is unresolved - adoption driven by mandate rather than craft conviction.

## Highlights

- UX practitioners report faster work but deeply mixed feelings about AI tooling
- Quality complaints: AI-generated design is 'very poor / immature' for specialty apps
- Management pressure is the biggest change; practitioners push back where AI falls short
- Some describe being forced to use tools they don't believe in - or ban at home
- Adoption is mandate-driven rather than craft-driven
