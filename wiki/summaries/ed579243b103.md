## Summary

The New York Times reports that Oregon and Virginia sued SpaceX under consumer-protection laws over Grok Imagine's sexualized imagery. It reports the states' allegations, requested financial damages and counts of at least 1.8 million images of women and 65 of children. X responds that prohibited uses violated its terms, that protections were tightened and that offenders are reported to law enforcement.

These are allegations in reported litigation, not a judgment. The counts are the article's account of the alleged output, not independently audited here, and complaints were not inspected directly. The company's response must remain alongside the claims. No underlying images were accessed, reproduced or sought for this summary.
