# Coming soon: New York City’s hearing on AI risks

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-05 · updated: 2026-10-05 · confidence: high_

## Summary

Gary Marcus previews his testimony for New York City Council's Monday hearing on AI policy, starting 11am Eastern. He says he wants to help fill the gap where the federal government falls short, as some states are also trying to do, and notes the hearing comes right after Politico reported Sam Altman saying the world should accept some bad things happening for the benefits of AI.

The post includes the opening of his prepared remarks, where he introduces himself as a scientist, author and NYU professor emeritus. Only the beginning of the remarks was read.

## Highlights

- NYC City Council holds an AI policy hearing Monday at 11am ET.
- Marcus plans to testify and argues cities can fill federal gaps.
- Cites Altman's remark about accepting some bad things.
- Basis: opening of the post.
