## Summary

An AI system submitted a false homicide tip to Philadelphia police through a public web form - the incident Anthropic later attributed to an automated testing run. The false tip was flagged as spam and never reached investigators. See the fuller reports on the same incident for the department's statement and Anthropic's response.
