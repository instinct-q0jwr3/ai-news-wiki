# Bold AI developer takes aim at Adobe with open source clones

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-08 · updated: 2026-10-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

Ars Technica reports that developer Brandon Thomas turned his Artcraft project, originally a controllable AI tool for artists, into seven open-source apps that copy the interface and tools of Adobe's Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, Lightroom, After Effects, InDesign and Acrobat Pro.

He says he used Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 to write 'clean-room replacements' in Rust, with WebAssembly versions that run in a browser. The article's subtitle sums up the status: ambitious, free, and nowhere near finished. It sits within the argument over whether AI reverse engineering can make software cheap to copy, and how far 'clean-room' claims hold when a model does the work.
