## Summary

Mastra’s authors introduce an open-source software-factory interface for coordinating maintenance work through a board and Slack. They describe durable sessions, company knowledge with citations, repository-event triggers and configurable development environments, alongside internal pull-request counts.

Those counts measure the authors’ activity, not independently verified quality or net productivity. Closing outside contributions and asking for detailed issue reports is their project choice, not a general recommendation. No software was installed or repository access changed for this summary.

## Highlights

- Author post describes a coordination product
- Slack is an important interface in their usage
- Internal PR counts are not quality measurements
- Durability and event triggers support longer work
- Project contribution policy remains context-specific
