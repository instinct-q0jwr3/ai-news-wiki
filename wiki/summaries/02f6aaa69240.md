# The sleep framework: A new way for LLMs to retain knowledge in continual learning

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-28 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `small-specialist-models`

## Summary

Google Research's Ali Behrouz and Vahab Mirrokni introduce a 'sleep' framework for continual learning: LLMs get a periodic lifecycle alternating active interaction with offline memory consolidation, converting fragile short-term context into stable long-term parameters - the model's version of how the brain stabilizes memory during rest.

The goal is to escape the static knowledge cutoff without catastrophic forgetting. The authors report that the alternating cycle lets models continuously acquire new facts, retain long-context knowledge up to 10M tokens, and improve complex reasoning.

## Highlights

- 'Sleep' phase consolidates short-term context into long-term weights
- Targets static knowledge cutoffs without catastrophic forgetting
- Retains long-context knowledge up to 10M tokens in tests
- From Google Research (Behrouz and Mirrokni)
