# Anthropic's IPO Filing Warns It May Be 'Imminently' Close to RSI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-03 · updated: 2026-11-10 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `anthropic`

## Summary

A second, more careful reading of the RSI passage in Anthropic's S-1A - this one putting the filing's language next to the company's own May 2026 essay 'When AI builds itself.' The essay is notably more measured than the risk factor: 'We are not there yet, and recursive self-improvement is not inevitable,' it says, drawing the line between AI that speeds up research (writing code, running experiments) and AI that decides what to research - choosing problems and recognizing dead ends. Only the second closes the recursive loop, and Anthropic's own view is that humans still hold the comparative advantage in seeing the bigger picture.

The filing's actual claims, restated precisely: internal estimates about future models - not current ones - indicate Anthropic may imminently meet its RSP definition of 'dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI'; no current model is designated past the threshold; and the company admits it has previously failed to identify accelerations until after they happened. It is also considering RSP updates 'to keep humans in control of frontier model development.'

The piece adds one useful measurement: as of August 2026, Claude 'leads' - does work end-to-end while an engineer decides whether it ships - on 26% of internal tasks. That is the empirical grounding the RSI debate usually lacks: a named metric on the spectrum between assistant and autonomous researcher, sitting far from the loop-closing case the essay describes.

## Highlights

- Second look at the RSI disclosure: internal estimates say Anthropic may imminently meet its RSP threshold; no current model is designated past it
- Contrast with Anthropic's May essay: 'We are not there yet' - the loop only closes when AI decides what to research, not when it speeds up research
- As of August 2026, Claude 'leads' (end-to-end with engineer sign-off) 26% of internal tasks - a rare concrete metric on the autonomy spectrum
- Anthropic says it is considering RSP updates 'to keep humans in control of frontier model development'
