# Nvidia and Meta expand their Muse partnership, including Meta deploying more Vera Rubin and Vera CPUs; Nvidia will invest $3B in Meta's planned equity fundraise

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-27 · updated: 2026-10-30 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `meta` `nvidia` `muse`

## Summary

Nvidia and Meta are expanding their partnership around Muse, Meta's AI assistant, in a deal both companies frame as evidence of demand for 'useful' AI agents. As part of the deepened collaboration, Meta intends to deploy more Nvidia infrastructure - including Vera Rubin systems and Vera CPUs - to power its next-generation AI agents.

The announcement lands days after Meta's $95 billion fundraising plans surfaced, and ties two of the build-out's central players closer: Meta buying more of the hardware stack, Nvidia locking in its largest customers for the Rubin generation. On Meta's last earnings call, Zuckerberg said shopping was Muse's most common use case, averaging over $200 per purchase - the 'useful agents' demand both companies are citing.
