# North 2: Enterprise AI Without Compromises

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-06 · updated: 2026-10-20 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Cohere's North 2 is pitched as the end of the enterprise agentic-AI tradeoff: until now, the secure platform wasn't smart enough, the smart one wasn't controllable, and the governable one cost too much at scale. North 2 is a full-stack agentic platform shaped by a year of production deployments across finance, healthcare, telecom, manufacturing, energy and the public sector.

The release leans on enterprise references - LG CNS deploying it across Korea, Bell Cyber using it for security operations with sovereignty requirements built in - and on NVIDIA's Kari Briski noting Blackwell and Hopper acceleration delivers 'more tokens for less'. Themes: working with enterprise knowledge, predictable and transparent paths to AI ROI, and full enterprise ownership of the deployment.

## Highlights

- Cohere launches North 2, a full-stack agentic AI platform for enterprises.
- Pitch: no compromise between security, intelligence, cost and control.
- Shaped by production deployments in finance, healthcare, telecom, energy and government.
- LG CNS and Bell Cyber cited as reference customers (scale in Korea; sovereign security ops in Canada).
- NVIDIA Blackwell and Hopper accelerate inference for Cohere models in North 2.0.
- Emphasis on data sovereignty, predictable ROI and enterprise ownership.
