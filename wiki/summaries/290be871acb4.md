# Glue work is still harmful in the age of AI

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-11 · confidence: high_

`seangoedecke`

## Summary

Sean Goedecke argues glue work stays harmful in the AI age. Software is in a centaur era, where human engineers plus AI beat either alone, and that era may last decades as it did in chess. LLMs writing most code does not free engineers for more glue work; he gives four reasons, starting with AI reducing coordination needs, and holds that processes sustained by unrewarded sacrifice ought to die.

## Highlights

- Centaur era of software may last decades
- Glue work matters less to companies now, not more
- Four reasons; first: AI cuts coordination needs
- Processes kept alive by unrewarded work should die
