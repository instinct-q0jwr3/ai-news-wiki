## Summary

An OpenAI-hosted mathematical preprint claims a separation of VP and VNP over every field, including a stronger lower bound against border arithmetic circuits. Its abstract gives a superpolynomial bound of exp(Omega((log n)^(5/4))) and says the result applies to the permanent outside characteristic two. The paper defines its setting in terms of nonuniform arithmetic circuits computing formal polynomials.

These are the manuscript's claims, not an independently verified theorem in this summary. Reading its abstract and opening definitions does not validate the proof or establish expert acceptance, and no formal proof-checking result was inspected for this paper. Algebraic VP versus VNP must not be silently substituted for the Boolean P versus NP problem.
