# Filings: Nvidia, Anthropic, and OpenAI had their highest-spending lobbying quarter between July and September, spending $2.2M, $2.1M, and $1.5M, respectively

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-22 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `anthropic` `nvidia` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Federal disclosures show Nvidia, Anthropic, and OpenAI each had their highest-spending lobbying quarter between July and September: Nvidia $2.2 million, Anthropic $2.1 million, OpenAI $1.5 million - nearly $6 million combined, around cybersecurity, child safety, and more. The sums aren't unusual next to Microsoft ($2M+ quarterly since 2021) or Google (over $3M this quarter), but for the three AI firms they mark a step-change in how seriously they take Washington.

The spending lands amid visible courtship: tech CEOs attended a White House lunch with President Trump in late September that produced a 'morally binding' AI safety accord. The Verge reads the filings as a clear signal that the AI labs now see shaping policy as core business, with regulation moving from hypothetical to imminent on multiple fronts at once.

## Highlights

- Record lobbying quarter: Nvidia $2.2M, Anthropic $2.1M, OpenAI $1.5M (July-September filings)
- Nearly $6M combined, covering cybersecurity, child safety, and more
- Still below Google's $3M+/quarter, but a step-change for the AI labs
- Follows a September White House lunch that produced a 'morally binding' AI safety accord
- Signal that shaping AI policy is now core business for the labs
