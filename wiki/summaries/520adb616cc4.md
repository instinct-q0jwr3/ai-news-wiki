# Anthropic's Scion is heavily inspired by Mononoke

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-15 · confidence: high_

`lobsters` `anthropic`

## Summary

An engineer who helped build Meta's Mononoke source control server argues that Anthropic's new version-control system Scion copies the 'derived data' idea Mononoke used to speed up operations such as blame. He compares Anthropic's description with his own 2022 write-up.

His complaint is about credit: Anthropic mentioned Sapling's lessons but not Mononoke, and the post reads as if Claude designed it all. He says it borders on plagiarism. This is one engineer's view.

## Highlights

- Ex-Meta engineer says Scion's derived-data design mirrors Mononoke.
- He says Anthropic's credit is inadequate.
