# Gemini 3.8 TTS Playground

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-10-04 · confidence: high_

`simon-willison` `openai` `anthropic` `google` `gemini` `ai-policy-regulation`

## Summary

Simon Willison tries Google's two new Gemini text-to-speech models - gemini-3.8-flash-tts and gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts - which ship with a library of over 2,000 voices and can clone a custom voice from a 30-second sample (of your own voice or one you have rights to). Because the underlying API allows open CORS, he vibe-coded a bring-your-own-key playground with GPT-6 Astra to try it from the browser.

The API's standout feature for him is scripted multi-character conversation: each speaker gets its own voice and style instructions. His demo - two pelicans debating a move to Pacifica Pier, script by Claude 4.5 Opus - cost 2.74 cents for 1m18s of audio on the full Flash TTS model, generated in about 20 seconds.

## Highlights

- Google ships gemini-3.8-flash-tts and flash-lite-tts.
- 2,000+ built-in voices; custom voice from a 30s sample.
- Willison vibe-coded a BYOK playground (GPT-6 Astra) via open CORS.
- Multi-speaker scripts: per-character voices and style instructions.
- Demo cost: 78 seconds of audio for 2.74 cents in ~20 seconds.
