## Highlights

- Sonnet 5.5 (max) ranks #2 on the Intelligence Index, +18 over Sonnet 5, behind only Opus 5.5 (max)
- Reaches parity with Opus 5.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, AA-Briefcase, GDPval-AA, and AutomationBench-AA
- Heaviest token use ever measured: ~193k output tokens per task, ~7x GPT-6 Astra (max)
- Same $2/$10 pricing as Sonnet 5, but ~$7.60 per task, ~50% higher than Sonnet 5
- Off the cost Pareto frontier except at high effort, where it sits narrowly behind GPT-6 Sol
- Lags Opus 5.5 on factual knowledge (54% vs 66%) but hallucinates less (47% vs 59%)
- Evaluations ran on a pre-release deployment with a known bug that can degrade results
