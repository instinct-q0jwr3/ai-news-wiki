# OpenAI Raises $11B at a $1.25T Valuation

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-08 · updated: 2026-11-09 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai`

## Summary

A 24/7 Wall St. piece says OpenAI has received about $11B in new money by Friday, November 6, the first part of a round priced at a $1.25T pre-money valuation, which CFO Sarah Friar confirmed on TV. It is smaller and cheaper than the reported first ask of $30B or more at $1.4T (two investors said $1.5T, which Friar disputes), after AI stocks sold off. The closed amount is about 37% of the original target; the total could reach $20B, with a separate report of up to $9B more not yet closed. SoftBank and MGX took part.

Friar said the price is almost 50% above March's raise ($122B at $852B), an implied step-up of roughly 46.7% that the article calls approximate. She cited 70% quarter-over-quarter growth in Q3, doubled enterprise business, a roughly even consumer/enterprise revenue split, 1.2B weekly ChatGPT users and a $1B ads run rate by August, with no revenue figures. The article notes Microsoft and NVIDIA shareholders depend on OpenAI's ability to fund its plans.

## Highlights

- About $11B received at a $1.25T pre-money valuation.
- First ask reportedly $30B+ at $1.4T; Friar disputes the higher figure's origin.
- Total could reach $20B; the follow-on has not closed.
- Friar: Q3 growth 70% QoQ, 1.2B weekly ChatGPT users, ads at $1B run rate.
- Lead investors, terms and use of proceeds were not disclosed.
