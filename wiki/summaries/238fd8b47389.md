# In a report, Railway says it believes the October hack targeted AI provider keys for resale, and unauthorized spend on the stolen keys was estimated at ~$740K

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `ai-safety-incidents` `compute-buildout`

## Summary

Railway's incident report says the October hack exploited a previously unknown build-infrastructure vulnerability to escape filesystem isolation and steal an internal credential, then customer authentication secrets, generating about $740,000 in fraudulent AI usage. Decrypted customer environment variables for at most 1.85% of workspaces (about 100,000) were accessed, a maximum estimate rather than confirmed thefts. Railway believes the attackers wanted AI provider keys for resale and told customers on Oct. 22 to rotate secrets.

## Highlights

- About $740K of fraudulent AI usage
- Up to 100,000 workspaces' env vars accessed (max estimate)
- Entry: zero-day in build infrastructure
- Motive believed to be AI key resale
