# GPT-6.2 Astra vs. Claude Fable 5.2 hands-on: nearly tied on three custom accounting, coding, and data tasks, but Fable used far more tokens and cost 5x+ as much

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-23 · updated: 2026-11-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `anthropic`

## Summary

Jessica Wachtel's New Stack comparison tests GPT-6.2 Astra and Claude Fable 5.2 once each on three custom tasks: accounting close, a billing-code repair and completion task, and messy laboratory-data analysis. Both score full marks on accounting and data analysis; Fable scores 110 of 110 on code and Astra 108. The reported total estimated cost is $7.60 for Fable versus $1.45 for Astra, with corresponding run times of 917 and 232 seconds. The coding difference is one missed billing rule rather than a broad failure across the suite.

These are one-run results at default API settings against the author's answer keys, not a repeated benchmark or general ranking. The article reports list prices, but those figures and estimated spend were not independently checked here. Its tests were not designed to measure a separate safety regression mentioned in the article. Lower cost and a near-tied total do not erase the practical importance of the single billing error, nor does a perfect run establish reliability on new tasks.
