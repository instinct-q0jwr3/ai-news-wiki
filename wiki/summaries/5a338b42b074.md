# South Korea reopens corporate-lab research as military service

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-21 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

South Korea will let AI researchers fulfill their military obligations at large corporate laboratories from 2027, reopening a route closed for 14 years, according to Ministry of Science and ICT data obtained by ruling-party lawmaker Hwang Jung-a. LG Electronics, Samsung Electronics, Samsung Medison and HD Hyundai Robotics have been recommended to participate, requesting 81 placements across nine research institutes - short of the 120 reserved for large-company labs.

The alternative-service program lets science and engineering researchers with master's or doctoral degrees serve through three years of research. New placements at large businesses were halted in 2013 to prioritize small and mid-sized firms; the government is restoring access for AI labs to strengthen the domestic industry and retain specialists who might otherwise move overseas. The plan sets aside 240 positions for master's-level AI researchers in 2027, split evenly between large-company laboratories and government-funded or defense-industry institutes, with large-company allocations running through 2029.
