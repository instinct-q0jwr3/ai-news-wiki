## Summary

A Reddit headline says Z.ai denies a GLM-5.5 delay and expects a December launch. A separately read [Neural Wire report](https://neuralwire.co/articles/z.ai-issues-clarification-on-glm-5.5-release-schedule/) attributes the same denial to company leadership via WeChat, conflicting with [the earlier Reuters-attributed delay report](../summaries/25f2480c5106.md).

The original company statement and screenshot were not independently authenticated in this pass; the readable Reddit page exposed only its headline. The December timing is therefore a reported company position, not a verified release commitment, and Reuters' anonymous-source account remains a conflicting report rather than silently becoming established fact. Neither proves the model is currently available or establishes its final capabilities.
