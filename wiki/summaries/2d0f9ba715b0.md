# Getting good at AI takes time and practice

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-28 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`seangoedecke`

## Summary

An essay argues that getting good at AI takes real time and practice, against the popular claim - especially among AI skeptics - that current models need no learned skill and any tricks will be obsolete next generation. Like most things, the author writes, AI proficiency is a long-term skill, not a bag of tricks.

The piece also takes on the 'programming is actually art' argument against LLMs in technical fields: true or not, it will never persuade the general population, which supports technical fields for their technology, not their beauty.
