# Build and manage Connected Apps in one place

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-29 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Google launched a preview of its Connected Apps Platform, a single pipeline for developers to build and manage apps that plug into both Search and the Gemini app. Integration runs through a cloud-hosted MCP server or Android MCP using the AppFunctions APIs; a new Google Connected Console handles registration, testing and submission, and developers choose whether their app goes live in Search, Gemini or both. Once live, they get traffic, latency and error-rate tracking plus brand controls like verified logos and authenticated accounts.

The platform formalizes what has been a partner-by-partner program: more than 50 Connected Apps already run across Search and Gemini, from groceries to event ticketing, and a new partner batch - Adobe, Calm, Experian, Localiza and Peloton - is rolling out in Gemini now and heading to Search in the coming weeks. Access opens in phases, with more developers, markets and Google products promised over the coming months.

## Highlights

- Connected Apps Platform: one pipeline to ship apps into both Search and the Gemini app
- Integrate via cloud-hosted MCP server or Android MCP with AppFunctions APIs
- Connected Console covers registration, testing, submission, metrics and brand controls
- 50+ Connected Apps already live; new partners include Adobe, Calm, Experian and Peloton
- Phased preview; more developers, markets and Google surfaces to come
