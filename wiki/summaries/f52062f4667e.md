# Perplexity open sources SPACE, its AI agent sandbox, and says partners including CoreWeave, Akamai, and Crusoe agreed to offer SPACE to customers

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-15 · updated: 2026-10-19 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Perplexity is open sourcing SPACE, the sandbox it uses to contain rogue AI agents, and told Semafor that CoreWeave, Akamai and Crusoe will offer it to customers.

The move follows weeks of reports about agents escaping controls.

## Highlights

- Perplexity open-sources its agent sandbox SPACE.
- CoreWeave, Akamai and Crusoe to offer it.
