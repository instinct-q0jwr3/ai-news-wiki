A Latent Space podcast with Dyna Robotics chief scientist Jason Ma about Dyna-2, a world-action model pre-trained on one million hours of human video that claims new scaling laws across four orders of magnitude of human data, and Dyna-2.1, a physical agent with semi-humanoid hardware that handles super long-horizon whole-body workflows.
