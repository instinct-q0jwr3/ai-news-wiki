## Summary

Bert Hubert's summary of the 2025 Chat Control fight: led by Denmark, many EU member states want to force WhatsApp, Signal and others to inspect every photo and link with AI. If the AI has any 'doubt' about child abuse material, the photo, location, phone number and other details go to a new 'EU Centre', then Europol and local police - with no suspicion required and a reporting bar of anything not 'manifestly unfounded'.

His warnings: the scanning AI would be built by Meta itself, the EU Centre keeps 'legal' photos anyway, WhatsApp would demand consent or block image sharing, Signal says it would leave the EU, police say they're already overwhelmed - and governments exempted their own police, military and security services. With Germany close to supporting it, he urges everyone, especially Germans, to contact their politicians and scream loudly.

## Highlights

- Chat Control: AI scanning of all photos and links, no suspicion needed
- 'Doubtful' content plus location and phone number reported to EU Centre, Europol, police
- The scanning AI would be built by Meta on the EU's behalf
- Signal would leave the EU; even 'legal' photos stay in the database
- Governments exempted their own police, military and security services
- Hubert: contact your politicians, especially in Germany, and scream loudly
