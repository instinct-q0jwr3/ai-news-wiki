# HypeAuditor: OpenAI doubled its sponsored Instagram posts promoting ChatGPT from 61 in June to 122 in July, rising again to 141 in August, as it seeks new users

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-23 · updated: 2026-09-24 · confidence: high_

`techmeme` `openai` `ai-safety-incidents`

## Summary

Business Insider, citing data from HypeAuditor, reports that OpenAI more than doubled its sponsored Instagram posts promoting ChatGPT, from 61 in June to 122 in July and 141 in August, as the company seeks new users. The ramp-up in creator marketing comes as AI safety concerns take center stage and, in the publication's words, AI's reputation is 'in hot water'.

## Highlights

- OpenAI sponsored Instagram posts rose from 61 in June to 141 in August, per HypeAuditor data cited by Business Insider.
- The influencer push aims at winning new users for ChatGPT.
- The spending grows while AI safety concerns take center stage.
