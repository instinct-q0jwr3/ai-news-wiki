## Summary

Cory Doctorow compares the sales pitch for workplace AI with scientific-management consulting: selling managers a sense of control over workers can matter more than demonstrated productivity. He distinguishes worker-led use of automation from imposed deployment that accelerates work beyond people's capacity to catch errors.

This is polemical labor commentary using a historical analogy, not a representative survey of managers or a causal estimate of AI productivity. Broad statements about motives, management and technology are the author's judgments, and the historical claims were not separately audited.
