## Summary

Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 adds diarization, configurable transcription styles and word-level timestamps. Microsoft says it beats Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, GPT-Transcribe, Whisper V3-Large and ScribeV2, ranks first on FLEURS across 60 languages with 5.2% average word error rate, and sits on the accuracy-latency Pareto frontier per Artificial Analysis. Vendor claims.
