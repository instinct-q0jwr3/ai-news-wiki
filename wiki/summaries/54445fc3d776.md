# Tutoring company tells parents to save their money and 'use AI instead'

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-25 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

An Australian tutoring company has told parents to invest in Gemini or ChatGPT rather than pay 'overpriced' human tutors, as students increasingly turn to AI for exam preparation and immediate study feedback. Dymocks Tutoring and Talent 100, which runs five centres across Sydney, will close at the end of the week after telling customers that technology has rendered its service obsolete.

The story was reported by education correspondent Maani Truu on September 23.
