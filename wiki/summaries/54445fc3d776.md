# Tutoring company tells parents to save their money and 'use AI instead'

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-25 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news`

## Summary

An Australian tutoring business told parents to stop paying for human tutors and put the money into Gemini or ChatGPT subscriptions instead - then closed down. Dymocks Tutoring and Talent 100, which runs five centres across Sydney, shuts at the end of the week after telling customers that AI has rendered its service obsolete.

The company is pointing at a real shift: students increasingly use AI for exam preparation and instant study feedback. A tutoring company declaring its own human service obsolete - rather than being displaced quietly - makes this an unusually blunt data point in the AI-and-education debate. (The Sydney Morning Herald original sits behind its paywall; this summary is based on the article's freely available first section.)
