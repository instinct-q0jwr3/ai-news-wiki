# Reprogramming Dormant Neurons to Backdoor AI Agents

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-11 · updated: 2026-11-14 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

The Lazarus research describes how editing a small number of dormant neurons can implant a trigger-dependent backdoor in an open-weight model without retraining. The model can behave normally on ordinary evaluations yet redirect an agent when a chosen condition appears. This exposes a gap between benchmark performance and the integrity of the deployed weights.

The authors emphasize provenance, comparison with trusted originals, constrained permissions and monitoring. They say their construction can be detected with access to the original model, but scanning alone cannot prove safety against a careful attacker. Model supply-chain controls therefore need the same attention as software dependencies.

## Highlights

- Small weight edits can create conditional behavior
- Ordinary evaluations may miss dormant triggers
- Attack requires altered model weights
- Trusted originals enable integrity comparisons
- Scanning is a signal, not proof of safety
- Limited permissions reduce downstream damage
