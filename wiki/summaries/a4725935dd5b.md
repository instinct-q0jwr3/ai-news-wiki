# Benchmarking Bot Detection Systems Against Modern AI Agents in 2026

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-20 · updated: 2026-10-26 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems` `external-evaluation`

## Summary

Researchers revisit their bot-detection benchmark with modern adversaries: conventional scripted browsers, stealth browsers that hide automation signals, and AI agents that navigate pages autonomously. A year ago the five leading systems detected 33-87% of automated browsers; now, across Proof of Human, Google reCAPTCHA v3, hCaptcha, Cloudflare Turnstile, and Cloudflare Bot Management, detection ranges from 9% to 99%. The test used a five-question survey mixing multiple-choice and open responses, with each of five adversaries attempting it 20 times per system, yielding 89-100 evaluable sessions each. Proof of Human led at 99% aggregated detection; behavioral signals alongside device signals again outperformed device-only approaches.

## Highlights

- Bot detection vs modern AI agents: detection rates from 9% to 99%
- Systems tested: Proof of Human, reCAPTCHA v3, hCaptcha, Turnstile, Cloudflare Bot Management
- Proof of Human led at 99% aggregate detection
- Adversaries: scripted browsers, stealth browsers, autonomous AI agents
- Method: five-question survey, 20 attempts per adversary, ~100 sessions per system
- Year-ago baseline was 33-87%; behavioral plus device signals still win
