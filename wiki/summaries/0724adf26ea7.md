# Baba, which develops AI tools to help Medicare and Medicaid patients connect to care teams and handle advocacy tasks, raised a $40M Series A led by FirstMark

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-25 · updated: 2026-10-29 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Baba, a 2025-founded care platform that connects Medicare and Medicaid patients to dedicated care teams (care coordinators and licensed therapists), raised a $40M Series A led by FirstMark Capital, with CVS Health Ventures and Valtruis participating and General Catalyst, Genius Ventures and Ground Up Ventures returning. FirstMark's Rick Heitzmann frames it as a bet on the consumerization of healthcare for rural, older and complex-need patients. Baba says it supports more than 10,000 patients with 100,000 families on its waiting list, and CMS selected it for the 10-year ACCESS model.

The money goes to research, reaching more health plans and accelerating value-based arrangements; CEO Connor Sweeney says the goal is a model where Baba takes on risk for coordinated, accountable care. The company also launched BabaBench, a benchmark measuring frontier AI agents on long-horizon patient advocacy tasks like paperwork, billing and insurer calls.
