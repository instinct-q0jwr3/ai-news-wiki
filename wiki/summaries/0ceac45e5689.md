# RRSI: Regularized Recursive Self-Improvement of Agent Harnesses

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-24 · updated: 2026-09-26 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

RRSI (Regularized Recursive Self-Improvement) applies self-improvement loops to agent harnesses while constraining how the loop edits them. The authors report it improves every out-of-distribution benchmark without overfitting the split it evolves on, where prior methods show large evolve-set gains that shrink or vanish once the benchmark changes - two of them ending below the harness they started from.

The regularization: every harness component stays editable, but the loop limits how much one proposal may change and which measured gains are allowed to stick. Early rounds may bundle a few coordinated edits to find a mechanism; late rounds get one attributable change. Every candidate is logged with its hypothesis, diff, score and cost change, so the proposer builds on what worked and stops re-testing what failed.
