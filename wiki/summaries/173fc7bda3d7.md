# GPT-6.1 Sol replaces GPT-6 Sol after just 7 days, with near-Astra intelligence

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-30 · updated: 2026-10-06 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `openai` `labor-displacement`

## Summary

Artificial Analysis reports that GPT-6.1 Sol replaces GPT-6 Sol just seven days after launch: it scores one point below GPT-6 Astra on the Intelligence Index at less than a quarter of Astra's cost per task. Pricing matches GPT-6 Sol at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, but the cache-read discount rises from 90% to 95%, making the blended agentic-workload price slightly lower - an additional cut on top of Sol's original 50% discount from GPT-5.6 Sol.

The model gains 4 points on the Intelligence Index versus GPT-6 Sol, with notable jumps in agentic knowledge work (AA-Briefcase, GDPval-AA), a 12-point Terminal-Bench 4.0 jump, and a hallucination-rate fall from 60% to 54%.

- Second effective price cut after Sol's original 50% discount
