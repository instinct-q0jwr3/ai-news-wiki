# Watchtower, which uses AI and simulation software to help the US Armed Forces make logistical decisions, emerges from stealth with a $35M seed

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-10-21 · updated: 2026-10-22 · confidence: high_

`techmeme`

## Summary

Alex Konrad at Upstarts Media profiles Watchtower Labs, decloaking with a $35 million seed to fix military logistics with AI and simulation software. The origin story: Admiral Samuel Paparo of US Pacific Command, responsible for 200 ships across 14 time zones, told Shield Capital's Raj Shah and Akhil Iyer that troop and ship movements were still being decided in Excel spreadsheets and emailed around. When they could not find a startup to back, they incubated one: Iyer, a Marine special-operations combat veteran, is CEO, with Suren Aghajanyan as CTO and John Morman heading engineering. The pitch is optimization under scarcity: defend across the Pacific's space with fewer resources than wanted, so plan better and faster.
