# Instinct Concierge Can Now Handle Phone Calls

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-09-20 · updated: 2026-09-21 · confidence: high_

`tldr-ai`

## Summary

Instinct has introduced Instinct Concierge, a white-glove tier that lets its assistant handle phone calls and other high-touch tasks. Announced by Noah Shinn on X, the service targets cases that resist self-serve flows: booking restaurants with no online reservations, getting on a dentist's cancellation list, or sorting out a cable bill.

The feature is rolling out gradually to Instinct's early access group first, with broader access promised soon. It moves consumer AI assistance from chat-only errands into synchronous, voice-based real-world tasks.

## Highlights

- Instinct Concierge can make phone calls on the user's behalf.
- Positioned as a white-glove service for high-touch cases: offline restaurant bookings, cancellation lists, billing disputes.
- Announced by Noah Shinn on X on September 16, 2026.
- Rolling out first to Instinct's early access group, with expansion promised soon.
- Framed around tasks that cannot be completed through online self-service flows.
