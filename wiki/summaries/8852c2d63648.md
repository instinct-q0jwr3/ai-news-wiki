# Hungary: Communist-era files released, up to 60k former agents revealed

_type: news-summary · created: 2026-11-05 · updated: 2026-11-08 · confidence: high_

`hacker-news` `agentic-systems`

## Summary

Euronews reports Hungary has opened communist-era secret police files, revealing up to 60,000 former agents. PM Peter Magyar's September bill gave the public unrestricted access, ending decades of stalled efforts he says enabled blackmail and pressure. Politicians, clergy and TV personalities appear, but the archive head says many informants were ordinary people. The government will provide hotlines for emotional support as families face revelations. Only loosely AI-related, included via HN.

## Highlights

- Files opened to the public after 36 years
- Up to 60,000 former agents named
- Many informants were ordinary people
- Government sets up counseling hotlines
