## A complexity essay is not a resolution of P versus NP

Viola interprets reported algorithmic advances as support for a prior belief while explicitly leaving the central problem unresolved.

In a Communications of the ACM blog essay, Emanuele Viola interprets reported AI-assisted advances in theoretical computer science as support for his longstanding belief that P equals NP. The piece recounts claimed results about 3SUM, integer multiplication and the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and argues that historical underestimation of efficient algorithms should make researchers reconsider their expectations. It explicitly says that the AI results it discusses did not prove P equals NP. This is an author's interpretation and account of reported results, not independent verification of their correctness, acceptance or AI contribution. The referenced manuscripts and proofs were not checked here.
