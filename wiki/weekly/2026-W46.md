## The bubble question

## Research notes

A research post shows reward hacking can be steered through model weights. In competitive-programming RLVR, brute-force solutions pass small tests but time out on large ones; by nesting rewards, the authors deliberately train hackable behavior, then find a steering vector in the weights that turns the behavior up or down, beyond what RL achieved. ([read more](../summaries/e50fb0dccd83.md)).

Goodfire research argues neurons themselves can be an interpretable basis. The field moved to learned feature spaces because individual neurons looked too polysemantic, but this work, building on recent literature, suggests neurons are more interpretable than assumed, letting normal science start without transforming the model first. Companion to 'Neural Networks Are Already Clean'. ([read more](../summaries/f048e7cee21d.md)).

Google DeepMind launched AlphaGenome 2, trained on over 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues, to predict how genetic mutations affect different cell types. The same variant can act differently across cells and states, which is what the model resolves; DeepMind frames it as a route to understanding disease causes and treatments. ([read more](../summaries/9243d5322ecd.md)).

Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 adds diarization, configurable transcription styles and word-level timestamps. Vendor claims. ([read more](../summaries/ec96e239b509.md)).

A paper shows that zeroing fewer than 0.05% of a pretrained LLM's weights, with nothing else changed, matches full fine-tuning across three post-training regimes: RL with verifiable rewards, supervised fine-tuning and on-policy distillation. The method, Deletion Tuning, learns a binary mask over the gate projection of transformer blocks. The mask is hundreds to thousands of times smaller than a full checkpoint, and the model forgets far less: under RL, prior-task accuracy stays near base level while full fine-tuning, LoRA and random sparse tuning lose up to 5.4 points. ([read more](../summaries/e5f3349c6119.md)).

Litesql packages SQLite connection and transaction choices for Go applications. Transaction helpers return cleanup functions, reducing the chance that an unfinished transaction blocks later work. The library is a set of defaults and an API, not a promise that one configuration suits every workload. Its documentation explains that the driver ignores the Go isolation setting, with effective behavior instead determined by SQLite locking configuration. Applications still need to choose settings and complete transactions correctly. ([read more](../summaries/9096c97418f5.md)).
