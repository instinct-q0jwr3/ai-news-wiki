## Overview

Google's Gemini family spans the consumer app, the API, and a growing set of agentic and media features: Call for Me phone calls on Pixel, Connected Apps, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS voice models, and Enterprise and Notebook integrations. This page follows Gemini's product rollouts and its positioning against Muse, ChatGPT and other personal agents.
