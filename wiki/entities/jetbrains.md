## Overview

JetBrains is expanding beyond the IDE into agentic software development with JetBrains Air, an open, multi-vendor system spanning the Junie coding agent, in-IDE agent direction, team coordination and governance. This page follows its product launches and its bid to own the organizational layer of agent-driven development.
