## Overview

Mistral is Europe's flagship foundation-model lab, pairing frontier model releases with applied partnerships such as Apollo, an Ancient Greek LLM built with the Austrian Academy of Science and Sail Reply. This page follows its models, partnerships and positioning in the multi-vendor AI landscape.
