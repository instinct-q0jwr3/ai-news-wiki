## Overview

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent for consumers, built by Meta Superintelligence Labs and heavily inspired by OpenClaw. This page follows its chart-topping launch, expansion into phone and video calls, computer use and smart glasses, security incidents such as the macOS token zero-day, agentic-commerce integrations like Shop Pay, reporting on its human-agent fallback layer, and rival responses from OpenAI and others.
