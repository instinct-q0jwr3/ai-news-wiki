# Trends: initial baseline (2026-09-19)

First analysis; only one day of history in `wiki/daily/`, so this sets the baseline rather than confirming trends.

- **Agentic era**: the highest story density is around agents: the AGENTS.md standard adopted by Claude Code, agent monitoring (Raindrop), ~10,000-agent swarms (Navier-Stokes), personal agents (Meta) and agents as UI (Salesforce). The "agent that acts" pattern dominates over the "model that chats".
- **OpenAI vs Anthropic race**: Astra (GPT-6) sets the pace and Anthropic answers with Fable/Mythos 5.1 plus rumors of a counter-launch before its IPO. Competition on pricing too (cache -75%).
- **Safety institutionalized**: METR/Redwood/Apollo move to the foreground; the AEF-1 standard is born co-signed by xAI, OpenAI and Anthropic. In parallel, concrete incidents (Gemini hacking companies, OpenAI repos compromised, hallucinated military intel).
- **Politics and courts as a main front**: Trump's AI czar and AI Force, DOJ backing OpenAI/Microsoft against the NYT, antitrust suit over an alleged slowdown pact.
- **Capital flows to agent infra and data**: $100M+ (Vantora), $35M (Raindrop), $20M (Veridion) in a single day.
- **Cultural debate over writing with AI**: two viral opposing pieces on HN the same day (for and against writing with LLMs).

## Update 2026-09-20

Second day of history.
